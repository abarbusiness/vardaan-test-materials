Detailed Solutions: 20 Min Rapid MCQ Test (Current Electricity)
1. What is the SI unit of electric charge?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Coulomb
The SI unit of electric charge is Coulomb (C).
2. How many electrons constitute one coulomb of charge?
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) 6.25 × 1018
Using quantization of charge, \( Q = ne \)
\( 1 = n \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \)
\( n = \frac{1}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}} = 6.25 \times 10^{18} \) electrons.
3. An instrument used for measuring electric potential difference is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Voltmeter
A voltmeter is specifically designed to measure the potential difference between two points in a circuit.
4. A voltmeter is always connected in ________ in a circuit.
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Parallel
Because a voltmeter has very high resistance and measures the potential drop across two points, it must be connected in parallel.
5. How much work is done in moving a charge of 2C across two points having a potential difference of 12V?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) 24 J
Potential difference \( V = \frac{W}{Q} \)
\( W = V \times Q = 12 \, \text{V} \times 2 \, \text{C} = 24 \, \text{J} \).
6. If the current through a resistor is doubled, the heat produced will become:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Four times
According to Joule's law of heating, \( H = I^2Rt \).
If \( I \) becomes \( 2I \), then new heat \( H' = (2I)^2Rt = 4I^2Rt = 4H \).
7. Which of the following represents Ohm's law?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) V = IR
Ohm's law states that potential difference is directly proportional to current, hence \( V = IR \).
8. The slope of a V-I graph for an ohmic conductor represents:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Resistance
Slope \( = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I} \). By Ohm's law, \( \frac{V}{I} = R \). Thus, the slope gives the resistance.
9. What is the SI unit of resistivity?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Ohm-meter
\( R = \rho \frac{L}{A} \Rightarrow \rho = \frac{RA}{L} \).
Units: \( \frac{\Omega \cdot \text{m}^2}{\text{m}} = \Omega \cdot \text{m} \) (Ohm-meter).
10. Resistivity of a metallic wire depends on:
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) Material & Temperature
Resistivity is an intrinsic property of the material and varies with temperature. It does not depend on physical dimensions (length or area).
11. If a wire of resistance R is stretched to double its original length, its new resistance will be:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) 4R
When stretched, volume remains constant. If length becomes \( 2L \), area becomes \( \frac{A}{2} \).
\( R' = \rho \frac{2L}{A/2} = 4 \left( \rho \frac{L}{A} \right) = 4R \)
12. Which material has the lowest electrical resistivity at room temperature?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Silver
Silver is the best conductor of electricity among metals, meaning it has the lowest resistivity.
13. When three resistors of 2Ω, 3Ω, and 6Ω are connected in parallel, their equivalent resistance is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) 1 Ω
\( \frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3+2+1}{6} = \frac{6}{6} = 1 \)
Therefore, \( R_p = 1 \, \Omega \).
14. In a series circuit, which physical quantity remains constant across all resistors?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Current
In a series connection, there is only one path for the flow of charge, so the current remains the same throughout the circuit.
15. Two resistors of 10Ω and 20Ω are joined in series across a 6V battery. The current in the circuit is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) 0.2 A
Total resistance \( R_s = 10 + 20 = 30 \, \Omega \).
Current \( I = \frac{V}{R_s} = \frac{6}{30} = 0.2 \, \text{A} \).
16. What is the maximum resistance which can be made using five resistors each of 1/5 Ω?
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) 1 Ω
Maximum resistance is obtained in series.
\( R_{max} = 5 \times \frac{1}{5} = 1 \, \Omega \).
17. What is the minimum resistance which can be made using five resistors each of 1/5 Ω?
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) 1/25 Ω
Minimum resistance is obtained in parallel.
\( \frac{1}{R_{min}} = 5 \times \left(\frac{1}{1/5}\right) = 5 \times 5 = 25 \Rightarrow R_{min} = \frac{1}{25} \, \Omega \)
18. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Kilowatt-hour
Electrical energy for commercial purposes is measured in Board of Trade Units, which is Kilowatt-hour (kWh).
19. 1 kWh is numerically equal to:
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) Both B and C
\( 1 \, \text{kWh} = 1000 \, \text{W} \times 3600 \, \text{s} = 3,600,000 \, \text{J} \)
This can be written as \( 3.6 \times 10^6 \, \text{J} \) or \( 36 \times 10^5 \, \text{J} \).
20. Electric power can be represented by which formula?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) P = V2/R
Power \( P = VI \). Substituting \( I = \frac{V}{R} \), we get \( P = \frac{V^2}{R} \). (A, C, D are incorrect formulas).
21. A bulb is rated 220V and 100W. When operated on 110V, the power consumed will be:
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) 25 W
Resistance of bulb is constant. \( R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{(220)^2}{100} = 484 \, \Omega \).
New power \( P' = \frac{(V')^2}{R} = \frac{(110)^2}{484} = \frac{12100}{484} = 25 \, \text{W} \).
22. The heating element of an electric iron is made of:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Nichrome
Nichrome (an alloy of nickel, chromium, manganese, and iron) is used because it has high resistivity and does not oxidize easily at high temperatures.
23. Alloys are usually used in electrical heating devices because:
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) Both B and C
Alloys have higher resistivity than pure metals, do not oxidize (burn) readily at high temperatures, and have high melting points.
24. An electric fuse is based on the principle of:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Heating effect of current
When excessive current flows, the fuse wire heats up according to Joule's law and melts, breaking the circuit.
25. A fuse wire should have:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) High resistance, low melting point
High resistance ensures it generates enough heat to melt quickly, and a low melting point ensures it breaks the circuit before damage occurs to appliances.
26. Which of the following does NOT represent electrical power in a circuit?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) IR2
Power formulas are \( P = VI \), \( P = I^2R \), and \( P = \frac{V^2}{R} \). \( IR^2 \) is incorrect.
27. Two identical resistors are first connected in series and then in parallel to a battery. The ratio of heat produced in series to parallel is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) 1:4
Let resistance be \( R \). In series, \( R_s = 2R \). In parallel, \( R_p = \frac{R}{2} \).
For constant voltage, \( H = \frac{V^2}{R_{eq}} t \).
\( \frac{H_s}{H_p} = \frac{R_p}{R_s} = \frac{R/2}{2R} = \frac{1}{4} \). Ratio is 1:4.
28. A current of 0.5A is drawn by a filament of an electric bulb for 10 mins. Find the amount of electric charge that flows.
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) 300 C
\( t = 10 \, \text{mins} = 600 \, \text{s} \).
\( Q = I \times t = 0.5 \, \text{A} \times 600 \, \text{s} = 300 \, \text{C} \).
29. What is the resistance of an ideal ammeter?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Zero
An ideal ammeter is connected in series and should not alter the circuit current; thus, it must have zero resistance.
30. What is the resistance of an ideal voltmeter?
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) Infinite
An ideal voltmeter is connected in parallel and should not draw any current from the circuit; thus, it must have infinite resistance.
31. If the radius of a conducting wire is doubled, its resistance becomes:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) One-fourth
Area \( A = \pi r^2 \). If radius is doubled, new area \( A' = \pi (2r)^2 = 4\pi r^2 = 4A \).
Since \( R \propto \frac{1}{A} \), new resistance becomes \( \frac{1}{4} \)th.
32. Kilowatt is a unit of:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Electric power
Kilowatt (kW) measures the rate of doing work or consuming energy, which is Electric Power.
33. Tungsten is used for making bulb filaments because:
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) It has a very high melting point
Tungsten has an extremely high melting point (approx 3380°C) allowing it to become white hot and emit light without melting.
34. In parallel combination of electrical appliances, what happens if one appliance stops working?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Others continue to work
In a parallel circuit, each appliance has its own path for current. If one path breaks, current continues to flow through the other branches.
35. Three resistors 1Ω, 2Ω, and 3Ω are connected in series. The potential difference across the 3Ω resistor is 3V. What is the battery voltage?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) 6 V
Current in series circuit \( I = \frac{V_3}{R_3} = \frac{3}{3} = 1 \, \text{A} \).
Total resistance \( R_{eq} = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 \, \Omega \).
Battery voltage \( V = I \times R_{eq} = 1 \times 6 = 6 \, \text{V} \).
36. A wire of resistance 10Ω is bent to form a closed circle. The resistance across any two diametrically opposite points is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) 2.5 Ω
The circle splits the wire into two semi-circles of resistance \( 5 \, \Omega \) each, connected in parallel.
\( \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{2}{5} \Rightarrow R_{eq} = 2.5 \, \Omega \).
37. The physical quantity having the unit J/C is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: D) Potential Difference
Potential difference \( V = \frac{W}{Q} \), where Work is in Joules (J) and Charge is in Coulombs (C). 1 J/C = 1 Volt.
38. Which effect of electric current is utilized in an electric toaster?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Heating effect
Toasters convert electrical energy into heat energy through high-resistance heating elements.
39. Calculate the current if 500C of charge passes through a circuit in 100 seconds.
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) 5 A
\( I = \frac{Q}{t} = \frac{500}{100} = 5 \, \text{A} \).
40. Resistivity of a wire depends on its length. (True or False)
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) False
Resistivity is a material property. It depends on the nature of the material and temperature, not on dimensions like length or area.
41. The direction of conventional electric current is taken as:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Opposite to flow of electrons
By convention, current flows from the positive terminal to the negative terminal, which is opposite to the movement of negatively charged electrons.
42. The equivalent resistance of n identical resistors connected in parallel is x. If they are connected in series, the resistance will be:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) n2x
Let individual resistance be \( R \).
In parallel: \( x = \frac{R}{n} \Rightarrow R = nx \).
In series: \( R_s = n \times R = n(nx) = n^2x \).
43. Device used to maintain a potential difference across a conductor is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Cell/Battery
A cell or battery provides the chemical energy needed to maintain a constant potential difference across a circuit.
44. What is the resistance of a 100W, 220V bulb?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) 484 Ω
Using \( P = \frac{V^2}{R} \),
\( R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{(220)^2}{100} = \frac{48400}{100} = 484 \, \Omega \).
45. If area of cross-section of a wire is doubled, while keeping length constant, resistance will:
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) Halve
Resistance is inversely proportional to cross-sectional area (\( R \propto \frac{1}{A} \)). If area becomes \( 2A \), resistance becomes \( \frac{R}{2} \).
46. Which gas is typically filled in electric bulbs to prolong the life of the filament?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Argon or Nitrogen
Chemically inactive gases like Argon and Nitrogen are used to prevent the hot tungsten filament from oxidizing.
47. A circuit has a resistance of 12Ω. A 24V battery is connected to it. What is the current?
[1 Mark]
Answer: B) 2 A
Using Ohm's Law, \( I = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{24}{12} = 2 \, \text{A} \).
48. Ohm's law is valid only when:
[1 Mark]
Answer: A) Temperature remains constant
Ohm's law strictly applies providing the physical conditions like temperature of the conductor remain constant, because resistance changes with temperature.
49. The work done in moving a unit positive charge from one point to another is called:
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) Potential difference
By definition, potential difference between two points is the work done per unit charge (\( V = \frac{W}{Q} \)).
50. How many 176 Ω resistors (in parallel) are required to carry 5 A on a 220 V line?
[1 Mark]
Answer: C) 4
Required total resistance \( R_{eq} = \frac{V}{I} = \frac{220}{5} = 44 \, \Omega \).
For \( n \) resistors of \( 176 \, \Omega \) in parallel, \( R_{eq} = \frac{176}{n} \).
\( \frac{176}{n} = 44 \Rightarrow n = \frac{176}{44} = 4 \).
