Minor Test 16 (Ratio, Proportion & Unitary Method)
Class: 6 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working clearly. Direct answers will not be awarded full marks.
  • Ensure quantities are converted to the same unit before calculating a ratio.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the ratio of \( 45 \text{ minutes} \) to \( 1.5 \text{ hours} \) in its simplest form.
2.
Check if the numbers \( 12, 16, 18, \) and \( 24 \) are in proportion.
3.
If \( 8 \) notebooks cost \( \text{Rs. } 120 \), what is the cost of \( 5 \) such notebooks?
4.
Divide \( \text{Rs. } 500 \) between Aman and Bina in the ratio \( 3 : 2 \).
5.
Find the value of \( x \) if \( x : 4 :: 9 : 12 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
The ratio of boys to girls in a school is \( 5 : 3 \). If there are \( 450 \) boys, find the number of girls and the total number of students in the school.
7.
Which ratio is greater: \( 5 : 8 \) or \( 3 : 4 \)? Show your working.
8.
Two numbers are in the ratio \( 4 : 7 \). If their sum is \( 132 \), find the two numbers.
9.
A worker earns \( \text{Rs. } 1800 \) in \( 6 \) days.
(a) How much will he earn in \( 25 \) days?
(b) If he earned \( \text{Rs. } 4200 \), for how many days did he work?
10.
A car travels \( 165 \text{ km} \) in \( 3 \text{ hours} \) at a uniform speed. How much time will it take to travel \( 275 \text{ km} \)?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 4 \).
(a) Find the measure of each angle of the triangle.
(b) If the smallest angle is halved and the largest angle is doubled, what will be the new ratio of the three angles?
12.
A printing machine prints \( 1500 \text{ pages} \) in \( 45 \text{ minutes} \).
(a) How many pages will it print in \( 2 \text{ hours} \)?
(b) How much time (in hours and minutes) will it take to print \( 6000 \text{ pages} \)?
13.
The ratio of the monthly income to the savings of a family is \( 11 : 2 \). If their monthly savings are \( \text{Rs. } 2500 \), find:
(a) The total monthly income of the family.
(b) The monthly expenditure of the family.
(c) The ratio of their monthly expenditure to their monthly income in simplest form.
