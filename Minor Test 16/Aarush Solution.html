SECTION A

Q1. Find the HCF of \(36\) and \(84\) using the prime factorization method.
[2 Marks]
First, find the prime factors of each number:
\(36 = 2 \times 18 = 2 \times 2 \times 9 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^2\)
\(84 = 2 \times 42 = 2 \times 2 \times 21 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 2^2 \times 3^1 \times 7^1\)
The HCF is the product of the smallest powers of common prime factors.
Common factors are \(2\) and \(3\). The smallest powers are \(2^2\) and \(3^1\).
\[ \text{HCF} = 2^2 \times 3 = 4 \times 3 = 12 \]
Final Answer: \(12\)
Q2. The HCF of two numbers is \(6\) and their LCM is \(36\). If one of the numbers is \(12\), find the other number.
[2 Marks]
We know the relationship: \(\text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = \text{Product of the two numbers}\)
Let the other number be \(x\).
\[ 6 \times 36 = 12 \times x \]
\[ 216 = 12x \]
\[ x = \frac{216}{12} = 18 \]
Final Answer: The other number is \(18\)
Q3. Define conduction. Give one example of a good conductor of heat.
[2 Marks]
Conduction: The process by which heat is transferred from the hotter end to the colder end of an object (or between two objects in contact) without the actual movement of the particles of the medium.
Example: Metals like Copper, Iron, or Aluminum.
Q4. Why do we prefer to wear light-colored clothes in summer?
[2 Marks]
We prefer light-colored clothes in summer because they are poor absorbers (and good reflectors) of radiant heat from the sun. They reflect most of the heat that falls on them, keeping our bodies cool.
Q5. Name the mode of heat transfer from the sun to the earth. Why cannot convection occur in space?
[2 Marks]
Mode of transfer: Radiation.
Reason: Convection requires a material medium (like air or water) for the actual physical movement of heated particles. Since most of space is a vacuum (no material medium), convection cannot occur.

SECTION B

Q6. Find the least number which when divided by \(12\), \(16\), and \(24\) leaves a remainder of \(5\) in each case.
[3 Marks]
First, find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of \(12\), \(16\), and \(24\), which will be exactly divisible by them.
Prime factorizations:
\(12 = 2^2 \times 3\)
\(16 = 2^4\)
\(24 = 2^3 \times 3\)
\(\text{LCM} = 2^4 \times 3 = 16 \times 3 = 48\)
The required number leaves a remainder of \(5\), so we add \(5\) to the LCM:
\[ \text{Required Number} = 48 + 5 = 53 \]
Final Answer: \(53\)
Q7. Three bells toll at intervals of \(10\), \(15\), and \(20\) minutes respectively. If they all toll together at 8:00 AM, at what time will they next toll together?
[3 Marks]
The time after which they will toll together again is the LCM of their tolling intervals.
Find LCM of \(10\), \(15\), and \(20\):
\(10 = 2 \times 5\)
\(15 = 3 \times 5\)
\(20 = 2^2 \times 5\)
\(\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5 = 4 \times 3 \times 5 = 60 \text{ minutes}\)
Since \(60 \text{ minutes} = 1 \text{ hour}\), they will toll together \(1 \text{ hour}\) after 8:00 AM.
\[ 8:00 \text{ AM} + 1 \text{ Hour} = 9:00 \text{ AM} \]
Final Answer: \(9:00 \text{ AM}\)
Q8. Determine the greatest \(3\)-digit number that is exactly divisible by \(8\), \(10\), and \(12\).
[3 Marks]
First, find the LCM of \(8\), \(10\), and \(12\):
\(8 = 2^3\)
\(10 = 2 \times 5\)
\(12 = 2^2 \times 3\)
\(\text{LCM} = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5 = 8 \times 3 \times 5 = 120\)
The greatest \(3\)-digit number is \(999\). We divide \(999\) by \(120\) to find the largest multiple.
\(999 \div 120 = 8\) with a remainder of \(39\).
Subtract the remainder from \(999\) to get the exact multiple:
\[ 999 - 39 = 960 \]
(Alternatively, \(120 \times 8 = 960\))
Final Answer: \(960\)

[3 Marks]
Sea Breeze Land Breeze
1. It blows from the sea towards the land. 1. It blows from the land towards the sea.
2. It occurs during the daytime. 2. It occurs during the night time.
3. Caused because land heats up faster than the sea during the day. 3. Caused because land cools down faster than the sea at night.
Q10. Explain how heat is transferred in a pan of boiling water. Name the specific process and describe its mechanism.
[3 Marks]
Process Name: Convection.
Mechanism: When a pan of water is heated on a flame, the water at the bottom (near the heat source) gets hot first.
As water heats up, it expands, becomes lighter (less dense), and rises towards the top.
The colder, denser water from the top and sides moves down to take its place near the heat source. This water then gets heated and rises, creating a continuous cyclic movement called convection currents. This continues until the entire volume of water is heated.

SECTION C

Q11. Find the greatest number that will divide \(445\), \(572\), and \(699\) leaving remainders \(4\), \(5\), and \(6\) respectively.
[5 Marks]
If the required number leaves remainders \(4, 5,\) and \(6\), then it must exactly divide the numbers minus their respective remainders.
Let's find the numbers that are exactly divisible:
\(445 - 4 = 441\)
\(572 - 5 = 567\)
\(699 - 6 = 693\)
The required greatest number will be the HCF of \(441\), \(567\), and \(693\).
Prime factorization:
\(441 = 3 \times 147 = 3 \times 3 \times 49 = 3^2 \times 7^2\)
\(567 = 3 \times 189 = 3 \times 3 \times 63 = 3^4 \times 7\)
\(693 = 3 \times 231 = 3 \times 3 \times 77 = 3^2 \times 7 \times 11\)
To find HCF, take the lowest powers of common prime factors (\(3\) and \(7\)):
\[ \text{HCF} = 3^2 \times 7^1 = 9 \times 7 = 63 \]
Final Answer: The greatest dividing number is \(63\).
Q12. (a) What is radiation? Give one daily life example.
(b) Why bases of utensils are black while handles are plastic/wood?
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [2 Marks]
Radiation: It is the process of heat transfer from a hot body to a cold body directly, without heating the space in between, and it does not require any material medium.
Example: Feeling the heat from a campfire when sitting at a distance, or getting warmth from a room heater.
Part (b) [3 Marks]
Bases painted black: Black surfaces are excellent absorbers of radiant heat. Painting the base black ensures the utensil rapidly absorbs maximum heat from the flame, cooking food faster and saving fuel.
Handles made of plastic/wood: Plastic and wood are insulators (bad conductors of heat). They do not allow the heat from the hot cooking pan to transfer to our hands, allowing us to safely lift the hot utensil without getting burnt.
Q13. (a) Find LCM of \(24\), \(36\), \(40\) by division method.
(b) Why are room heaters typically placed near the floor?
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [3 Marks]
Using the common division method:
  2 | 24, 36, 40
  ----------------
  2 | 12, 18, 20
  ----------------
  2 |  6,  9, 10
  ----------------
  3 |  3,  9,  5
  ----------------
  3 |  1,  3,  5
  ----------------
  5 |  1,  1,  5
  ----------------
    |  1,  1,  1
Multiply all divisors: \(\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5\)
\[ \text{LCM} = 8 \times 9 \times 5 = 72 \times 5 = 360 \]
Ans (a): \(360\)
Part (b) [2 Marks]
Room heaters are placed near the floor because of convection. The heater warms the air near it. Warm air is lighter (less dense) and rises to the ceiling. The cold, heavier air from above then sinks down to the floor to take its place. This cold air then gets heated.
This continuous cycle sets up convection currents that efficiently heat the entire room from bottom to top.
