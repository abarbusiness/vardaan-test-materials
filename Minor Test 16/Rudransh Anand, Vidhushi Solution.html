Minor Test 16 - DETAILED SOLUTIONS
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Express the ratio \( 45 \text{ minutes} : 1.5 \text{ hours} \) in its simplest form. [2 Marks]
Concept: Convert both quantities to the same unit before finding the ratio.
1.5 hours = \( 1.5 \times 60 \) minutes = 90 minutes. (1 Mark)
Ratio = 45 : 90 = \( \frac{45}{90} = \frac{1}{2} = 1 : 2 \). (1 Mark)
Answer: \( 1 : 2 \)
Q2. If \( x : y = 3 : 5 \), find the ratio \( (3x + 4y) : (8x - y) \). [2 Marks]
Let \( x = 3k \) and \( y = 5k \), where k is a constant. (0.5 Mark)
Substitute in the expression:
\[ \frac{3(3k) + 4(5k)}{8(3k) - (5k)} \] \[ \frac{9k + 20k}{24k - 5k} \] (1 Mark) \[ \frac{29k}{19k} = \frac{29}{19} \] (0.5 Mark)
Answer: \( 29 : 19 \)
Q3. Check whether the numbers 15, 20, 30, and 40 are in proportion. [2 Marks]
Condition for proportion: Product of extremes = Product of means.
Extremes = 15, 40. Product = \( 15 \times 40 = 600 \). (1 Mark)
Means = 20, 30. Product = \( 20 \times 30 = 600 \). (0.5 Mark)
Since 600 = 600, they are in proportion. (0.5 Mark)
Answer: Yes, they are in proportion.
Q4. Find the fourth proportional to \( 4, 9, 12 \). [2 Marks]
Let the fourth proportional be x.
Then, \( 4, 9, 12, x \) are in proportion.
\( 4 : 9 :: 12 : x \)
\( 4 \times x = 9 \times 12 \) (Product of extremes = Product of means) (1 Mark)
\[ 4x = 108 \] \[ x = \frac{108}{4} = 27 \] (1 Mark)
The fourth proportional is 27.
Q5. Are the ratios \( 25\text{g} : 30\text{g} \) and \( 40\text{kg} : 48\text{kg} \) in proportion? Justify. [2 Marks]
First ratio: \( \frac{25}{30} = \frac{5}{6} = 5 : 6 \) (1 Mark)
Second ratio: \( \frac{40}{48} = \frac{5}{6} = 5 : 6 \) (Dividing by 8) (0.5 Mark)
Since both ratios are equal, they form a proportion. (0.5 Mark)
Answer: Yes, they are in proportion.
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Divide ₹ 3450 among A, B, and C in the ratio 3 : 5 : 7. Find the share of each. [3 Marks]
Sum of the terms of the ratio = \( 3 + 5 + 7 = 15 \). (0.5 Mark)
Total amount = ₹ 3450.
A's share = \( \frac{3}{15} \times 3450 = 3 \times 230 = \text{₹ } 690 \) (1 Mark)
B's share = \( \frac{5}{15} \times 3450 = 5 \times 230 = \text{₹ } 1150 \) (1 Mark)
C's share = \( \frac{7}{15} \times 3450 = 7 \times 230 = \text{₹ } 1610 \) (0.5 Mark)
A: ₹ 690, B: ₹ 1150, C: ₹ 1610
Q7. The ratio of copper and zinc in an alloy is 9 : 5. If the weight of copper in the alloy is 48.6 grams, find the weight of zinc in the alloy. [3 Marks]
Let the weight of copper be \( 9x \) and zinc be \( 5x \).
Given, weight of copper = 48.6 g
\[ 9x = 48.6 \] (1 Mark) \[ x = \frac{48.6}{9} = 5.4 \] (1 Mark)
Weight of zinc = \( 5x = 5 \times 5.4 = 27 \) grams. (1 Mark)
Weight of zinc is 27 grams.
Q8. Find the mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18. [3 Marks]
Let the mean proportional be x.
Then, \( 0.08, x, 0.18 \) are in continued proportion.
\( 0.08 : x :: x : 0.18 \)
\[ x^2 = 0.08 \times 0.18 \] (1 Mark) \[ x^2 = \frac{8}{100} \times \frac{18}{100} \] \[ x^2 = \frac{144}{10000} \] (1 Mark) \[ x = \sqrt{\frac{144}{10000}} = \frac{12}{100} = 0.12 \] (1 Mark)
The mean proportional is 0.12.
Q9. If \( A : B = 5 : 8 \) and \( B : C = 16 : 25 \), find \( A : C \). [3 Marks]
We have \( \frac{A}{B} = \frac{5}{8} \) and \( \frac{B}{C} = \frac{16}{25} \).
To find \( A : C \), we multiply the two ratios:
\[ \frac{A}{B} \times \frac{B}{C} = \frac{5}{8} \times \frac{16}{25} \] (1.5 Marks) \[ \frac{A}{C} = \frac{1}{1} \times \frac{2}{5} \] (Simplifying \( \frac{16}{8} \) and \( \frac{5}{25} \)) \[ \frac{A}{C} = \frac{2}{5} \] (1.5 Marks)
\( A : C = 2 : 5 \)
Q10. A car travels 288 km in 4 hours, and a train travels 540 km in 6 hours. Find the ratio of their speeds. [3 Marks]
Speed of the car = \( \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{288}{4} = 72 \text{ km/h} \). (1 Mark)
Speed of the train = \( \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{540}{6} = 90 \text{ km/h} \). (1 Mark)
Ratio of their speeds = \( 72 : 90 \).
Dividing by 18: \( \frac{72}{18} : \frac{90}{18} = 4 : 5 \). (1 Mark)
Ratio of speeds is \( 4 : 5 \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 : 6. Find the measure of each angle of the quadrilateral. [5 Marks]
Let the angles of the quadrilateral be \( 3x \), \( 4x \), \( 5x \), and \( 6x \). (1 Mark)
Property: The sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is \( 360^\circ \).
\[ 3x + 4x + 5x + 6x = 360^\circ \] (1 Mark) \[ 18x = 360^\circ \] \[ x = \frac{360}{18} = 20^\circ \] (1 Mark)
Calculate each angle:
First angle = \( 3x = 3 \times 20^\circ = 60^\circ \)
Second angle = \( 4x = 4 \times 20^\circ = 80^\circ \)
Third angle = \( 5x = 5 \times 20^\circ = 100^\circ \)
Fourth angle = \( 6x = 6 \times 20^\circ = 120^\circ \) (2 Marks for all correct angles)
Angles are \( 60^\circ \), \( 80^\circ \), \( 100^\circ \), and \( 120^\circ \)
Q12. If \( \frac{x}{b+c-a} = \frac{y}{c+a-b} = \frac{z}{a+b-c} \), show that \( (b-c)x + (c-a)y + (a-b)z = 0 \). [5 Marks]
Let \( \frac{x}{b+c-a} = \frac{y}{c+a-b} = \frac{z}{a+b-c} = k \) (where k is a constant). (1 Mark)
Then, we can express x, y, and z in terms of k:
\( x = k(b + c - a) \)
\( y = k(c + a - b) \)
\( z = k(a + b - c) \) (1.5 Marks)
Now, substitute these values into the LHS of the given expression: \( (b-c)x + (c-a)y + (a-b)z \)
\[ = (b-c)[k(b+c-a)] + (c-a)[k(c+a-b)] + (a-b)[k(a+b-c)] \]
Factor out 'k':
\[ = k [ (b-c)(b+c-a) + (c-a)(c+a-b) + (a-b)(a+b-c) ] \] (1 Mark)
Expand the terms inside the bracket:
\[ = k [ (b^2 - c^2 - ab + ac) + (c^2 - a^2 - bc + ab) + (a^2 - b^2 - ac + bc) ] \] (1 Mark)
Cancel out the like terms with opposite signs:
\( b^2 \) cancels with \( -b^2 \), \( -c^2 \) with \( c^2 \), \( -a^2 \) with \( a^2 \), \( -ab \) with \( ab \), \( ac \) with \( -ac \), \( -bc \) with \( bc \).
\[ = k [ 0 ] = 0 = \text{RHS} \] (0.5 Mark)
Hence, proved.
Q13. A sum of ₹ 2,236 is divided among A, B, and C such that A receives 25% more than C, and C receives 25% less than B. What is A's share in the amount? [5 Marks]
Let B's share be \( x \).
C receives 25% less than B, so C's share = \( x - 0.25x = 0.75x = \frac{3}{4}x \). (1 Mark)
A receives 25% more than C, so A's share = \( C + 0.25C = 1.25C = \frac{5}{4} \times \left(\frac{3}{4}x\right) = \frac{15}{16}x \). (1.5 Marks)
Total sum = \( A + B + C = 2236 \)
\[ \frac{15}{16}x + x + \frac{3}{4}x = 2236 \] (0.5 Mark)
To clear the fraction, multiply the entire equation by 16:
\[ 15x + 16x + 12x = 2236 \times 16 \] \[ 43x = 35776 \] \[ x = \frac{35776}{43} = 832 \] (1 Mark)
So, B's share is ₹ 832.
Now, calculate A's share:
A's share = \( \frac{15}{16}x = \frac{15}{16} \times 832 = 15 \times 52 = \text{₹ } 780 \) (1 Mark)
A's share is ₹ 780.
