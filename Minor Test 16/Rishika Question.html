Minor Test 16
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Mathematics (Algebra) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working clearly where required.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Write the algebraic expressions for the following:
(i) Sum of numbers \( a \) and \( b \) subtracted from their product.
(ii) One-fourth of the sum of numbers \( p \) and \( q \).
2.
Identify the numerical coefficients of terms (other than constants) in the following expressions:
(i) \( 100m + 1000n \)
(ii) \( -p^2q^2 + 7pq \)
3.
Identify the like terms in the following group:
\( 4m^2p, \ 3mp^2, \ -7mp^2, \ -4m^2p \)
4.
Classify the following algebraic expressions as monomial, binomial, or trinomial:
(i) \( z^2 - 3z + 8 \)
(ii) \( 100 \)
5.
What is the coefficient of \( x \) in the following expressions?
(i) \( 3x - 15 \)
(ii) \( y^2x + y \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Simplify the expression by combining like terms:
\( p - (p - q) - q - (q - p) \)
7.
Add the following expressions:
\( t - 8tz, \ 3tz - z, \ z - t \)
8.
Subtract \( 24ab - 10b - 18a \) from \( 30ab + 12b + 14a \).
9.
Find the value of the expression \( 2a^2 - ab + b^2 \) when \( a = 1 \) and \( b = -1 \).
10.
What should be the value of '\( k \)' if the value of \( 2x^2 + x - k \) equals to \( 5 \), when \( x = 0 \)?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Solve the following:
(a) Subtract \( 3pq (p - q) \) from \( 2pq (p + q) \).
(b) Add the expressions: \( 5m (3 - m) \) and \( 6m^2 - 13m \).
12.
From the sum of \( 2y^2 + 3yz, \ -y^2 - yz - z^2 \) and \( yz + 2z^2 \), subtract the sum of \( 3y^2 - z^2 \) and \( -y^2 + yz + z^2 \).
13.
Solve the following evaluation problems:
(a) Simplify the expression \( 4(2x - 1) + 3x + 11 \) and find its value when \( x = 2 \).
(b) Find the value of \( n^3 - 5n^2 + 5n - 2 \) when \( n = -2 \).
