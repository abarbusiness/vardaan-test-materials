SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
[Mathematics - Exponents]
Q1.
Evaluate the following:    (i) \( 3^{-2} \)      (ii) \( \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} \)
Q2.
The diameter of a specific microscopic cell is \( 0.000000564 \) meters. Express this measurement in standard form. What is the value of the exponent in this notation?
[Science - Force & Pressure]
Q3.
Define force. State the standard SI unit used to measure force.
Q4.
A blacksmith hammers a hot piece of iron while making a tool. How does the force of hammering affect the piece of iron? Identify the type of force involved.
Q5.
Give any two distinct examples from daily life where a non-contact force is acting on an object.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
[Mathematics - Exponents]
Q6.
Simplify the following expression: \( \frac{25 \times t^{-4}}{5^{-3} \times 10 \times t^{-8}} \) (Assume \( t \neq 0 \))
Q7.
Find the value of \( m \) if: \( \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{-4} \times \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{-5} = \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{3m} \)
[Science - Force & Pressure]
Q8.
List any three distinct effects that a force can produce on an object.
Q9.
Describe a simple activity to show that a magnetic force can act from a distance and is a non-contact force.
Q10.
In a game of tug-of-war, Team X pulls the rope with a force of \( 350 \) N towards the East, and Team Y pulls the rope with a force of \( 400 \) N towards the West. Calculate the net force and determine which team will win the game.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
[Mathematics - Exponents]
Q11.
Simplify the given expression completely by applying the laws of exponents (Show all prime factorization steps): \[ \frac{3^{-5} \times 10^{-5} \times 125}{5^{-7} \times 6^{-5}} \]
Q12.
Solve the following:
(a) The mass of the Earth is \( 5.97 \times 10^{24} \) kg and the mass of the Moon is \( 7.35 \times 10^{22} \) kg. What is their total combined mass? Leave your answer in standard form.
(b) Express the number \( 1042.78 \) in its expanded form using exponents.
[Science - Force & Pressure]
Q13.
Answer the following questions:
(a) Differentiate between Contact and Non-Contact forces giving one example of each. (3 Marks)
(b) A boy pushes a toy car with a force of \( 15 \) N. His friend joins him and pushes the car in the same direction with a force of \( 10 \) N. Calculate the net force applied on the car. What would happen to the net force if his friend pushed from the opposite direction with the same \( 10 \) N force? (2 Marks)
