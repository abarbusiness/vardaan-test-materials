SECTION A (10 Marks)
1.
Write the algebraic expression: (i) 5 added to three times a number \( x \). (ii) The quotient of \( x \) by \( y \) added to their product.
[2 Marks] Solution:
(i) \( 3x + 5 \) [1 mark]
(ii) \( \frac{x}{y} + xy \) [1 mark]
2.
Identify variables and constants in: \( 4x^2 - 7y + 9 \)
[2 Marks] Solution:
Variables: \( x \) and \( y \) [1 mark]
Constants: \( 9 \) (Also accept \( 4, -7, 9 \) as numerical constants/coefficients) [1 mark]
3.
Write the coefficient of \( x \) in: (i) \( -5xy \) (ii) \( 8xyz \)
[2 Marks] Solution:
(i) Coefficient of \( x \) is \( -5y \) [1 mark]
(ii) Coefficient of \( x \) is \( 8yz \) [1 mark]
4.
Classify into monomials, binomials, trinomials: (i) \( 3a + 5b \) (ii) \( 4x^2y \)
[2 Marks] Solution:
(i) \( 3a + 5b \) contains 2 terms, so it is a Binomial. [1 mark]
(ii) \( 4x^2y \) contains 1 term, so it is a Monomial. [1 mark]
5.
State the like terms among: \( 3x^2y, \ -4xy, \ 7yx^2, \ 2x^2 \)
[2 Marks] Solution:
The like terms are \( 3x^2y \) and \( 7yx^2 \).
(Reason: They have the exact same literal factors \( x^2y \), since \( yx^2 = x^2y \)) [2 marks]
SECTION B (15 Marks)
6.
Evaluate \( 3a - 2b + c \) if \( a = 2, b = 3, \) and \( c = 4 \).
[3 Marks] Solution:
Substitute the values into the expression:
= \( 3(2) - 2(3) + 4 \) [1 mark]
= \( 6 - 6 + 4 \) [1 mark]
= \( 4 \) [1 mark]
7.
Add: \( 2x + 3y \) and \( 4x - 5y \)
[3 Marks] Solution:
= \( (2x + 3y) + (4x - 5y) \)
= \( (2x + 4x) + (3y - 5y) \)    (Grouping like terms) [1 mark]
= \( 6x - 2y \) [2 marks]
8.
Subtract \( 3a - 4b \) from \( 5a + 2b \).
[3 Marks] Solution:
= \( (5a + 2b) - (3a - 4b) \) [1 mark]
= \( 5a + 2b - 3a + 4b \)    (Opening brackets, sign changes) [1 mark]
= \( 2a + 6b \) [1 mark]
9.
Solve: \( x - 7 = 15 \)
[3 Marks] Solution:
\( x = 15 + 7 \)    (Transposing -7 to RHS) [1.5 marks]
\( x = 22 \) [1.5 marks]
10.
Solve: \( 3y + 2 = 11 \)
[3 Marks] Solution:
\( 3y = 11 - 2 \)    (Transposing +2 to RHS) [1 mark]
\( 3y = 9 \) [1 mark]
\( y = \frac{9}{3} \)
\( y = 3 \) [1 mark]
SECTION C (15 Marks)
11.
If \( x = 2, y = -1, z = 3 \), find: (i) \( x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \)   (ii) \( 2xy - z \)
[5 Marks] Solution:
(i) \( x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \)
= \( (2)^2 + (-1)^2 + (3)^2 \) [1 mark]
= \( 4 + 1 + 9 \) [1 mark]
= \( 14 \) [1 mark]

(ii) \( 2xy - z \)
= \( 2(2)(-1) - (3) \) [1 mark]
= \( -4 - 3 \)
= \( -7 \) [1 mark]
12.
Read the mathematical statement from the file below. Convert it into an algebraic equation and solve for the unknown number.
[5 Marks] Solution:
From the mathematical statement in the referenced image (A number multiplied by 4 and then increased by 6 gives 30):

Let the unknown number be \( x \). [1 mark]
According to the statement:
\( 4x + 6 = 30 \)    (Equation formulation) [2 marks]
\( 4x = 30 - 6 \)
\( 4x = 24 \) [1 mark]
\( x = \frac{24}{4} \)
\( x = 6 \) [1 mark]
The required number is 6.
13.
(i) Simplify: \( 7x^2 - 3x + 5 - 2x^2 + 8x - 1 \)
(ii) A boy is \( y \) years old. What will be his age 5 years from now? What was his age 3 years ago?
[5 Marks] Solution:
(i) Grouping like terms together:
= \( (7x^2 - 2x^2) + (-3x + 8x) + (5 - 1) \) [1.5 marks]
= \( 5x^2 + 5x + 4 \) [1.5 marks]

(ii) Current age = \( y \) years
Age 5 years from now = \( y + 5 \) years [1 mark]
Age 3 years ago = \( y - 3 \) years [1 mark]
