Minor Test 16 (Percentage)
Class: 6 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working clearly. Direct answers will not be awarded full marks.
  • Ensure units are mentioned in the final answer where applicable.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the following as percentages:
(a) \( \frac{3}{8} \)
(b) \( 0.45 \)
2.
Convert \( 12\frac{1}{2}\% \) into a fraction in its simplest form.
3.
Find the value of \( 15\% \) of \( \text{Rs. } 400 \).
4.
What percentage is \( 45 \) minutes of \( 2 \) hours?
5.
Find the value of \( x \) if \( 30\% \) of \( x \) is \( 120 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Out of \( 800 \) students in a school, \( 45\% \) are girls. Find the total number of boys in the school.
7.
A man saves \( 18\% \) of his monthly income. If his monthly savings amount to \( \text{Rs. } 3600 \), calculate his total monthly income.
8.
In an orchard, \( 20\% \) of the trees are mango trees, \( 35\% \) are apple trees, and the remaining are orange trees. Find the percentage of orange trees. If the total number of trees is \( 400 \), find the exact number of orange trees.
9.
Rahul scored \( 36 \) marks out of \( 60 \) in his Mathematics test and \( 40 \) marks out of \( 50 \) in his Science test. In which subject did he perform better conceptually (in terms of percentage)?
10.
The cost of a bicycle was \( \text{Rs. } 4500 \). Its price is increased by \( 12\% \). What is the new increased price of the bicycle?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Mr. Sharma's monthly salary is \( \text{Rs. } 50,000 \). He spends \( 30\% \) of his salary on food, \( 20\% \) on house rent, \( 15\% \) on the education of his children, and saves the rest. Find:
(a) The percentage of his salary that he saves.
(b) His total monthly savings in Rupees.
(c) The amount spent strictly on food.
12.
In an examination, a candidate must secure \( 40\% \) marks to pass. A student gets \( 140 \) marks and fails by \( 20 \) marks. Find the maximum marks of the examination.
13.
An alloy consists of \( 40\% \) copper, \( 35\% \) zinc, and the rest is nickel. Find the weight of copper, zinc, and nickel separately in \( 2.4 \text{ kg} \) of the alloy. (Give your final answers in grams).
