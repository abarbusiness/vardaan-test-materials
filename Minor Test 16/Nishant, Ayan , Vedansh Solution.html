SECTION A
Q1. If the cost of 8 identical pens is ₹ 120, find the cost of 1 pen.
[2 Marks]
Cost of 8 pens = ₹ 120
Cost of 1 pen = \( \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Number of pens}} \)
\[ \text{Cost of 1 pen} = \frac{120}{8} \]
\[ = 15 \]
Ans: The cost of 1 pen is ₹ 15.
Q2. A train covers a distance of 180 km in 3 hours at a uniform speed. How much distance will it cover in 1 hour?
[2 Marks]
Distance covered in 3 hours = 180 km
Distance covered in 1 hour = \( \frac{180}{3} \)
\[ = 60 \text{ km} \]
Ans: The train covers 60 km in 1 hour.
Q3. The weight of 5 identical mathematics books is 2.5 kg. What is the total weight of 12 such books?
[2 Marks]
Weight of 5 books = 2.5 kg
Weight of 1 book = \( \frac{2.5}{5} = 0.5 \text{ kg} \)
Weight of 12 books = Weight of 1 book × 12
\[ = 0.5 \times 12 \]
\[ = 6.0 \text{ kg} \]
Ans: The weight of 12 books is 6 kg.
Q4. If a dozen bananas cost ₹ 60, find the cost of 7 bananas.
[2 Marks]
1 dozen = 12 items
Cost of 12 bananas = ₹ 60
Cost of 1 banana = \( \frac{60}{12} = \text{₹ } 5 \)
Cost of 7 bananas = \( 5 \times 7 \)
\[ = 35 \]
Ans: The cost of 7 bananas is ₹ 35.
Q5. A daily wage worker earns ₹ 1500 in 5 days. How much money will he earn if he works for 8 days?
[2 Marks]
Earnings in 5 days = ₹ 1500
Earnings in 1 day = \( \frac{1500}{5} = \text{₹ } 300 \)
Earnings in 8 days = \( 300 \times 8 \)
\[ = 2400 \]
Ans: The worker will earn ₹ 2400 in 8 days.
SECTION B
Q6. If 15 bags of wheat weigh 450 kg, how many such bags will be required to get 1200 kg of wheat?
[3 Marks]
Weight of 15 bags = 450 kg
Weight of 1 bag = \( \frac{450}{15} = 30 \text{ kg} \) [1 Mark]
Number of bags required for 1200 kg = \( \frac{\text{Total weight}}{\text{Weight of 1 bag}} \) [1 Mark]
\[ = \frac{1200}{30} \]
\[ = 40 \]
Ans: 40 bags are required. [1 Mark]
Q7. A car travels 228 km on 12 liters of petrol. How many kilometers will it travel on 20 liters of petrol?
[3 Marks]
Distance traveled on 12 liters = 228 km
Distance traveled on 1 liter = \( \frac{228}{12} \) [1 Mark]
\[ = 19 \text{ km} \]
Distance traveled on 20 liters = \( 19 \times 20 \) [1 Mark]
\[ = 380 \text{ km} \]
Ans: The car will travel 380 km. [1 Mark]
Q8. The rent of a house for 4 months is ₹ 18,000. Find the rent of the same house for a complete year.
[3 Marks]
Rent for 4 months = ₹ 18,000
Rent for 1 month = \( \frac{18000}{4} = \text{₹ } 4500 \) [1 Mark]
1 complete year = 12 months [1 Mark]
Rent for 12 months = \( 4500 \times 12 \)
\[ = 54000 \]
Ans: The rent for a year is ₹ 54,000. [1 Mark]
Q9. If 24 meters of cloth costs ₹ 3600, how many meters of the same cloth can be purchased for ₹ 1350?
[3 Marks]
Length of cloth for ₹ 3600 = 24 m
Length of cloth for ₹ 1 = \( \frac{24}{3600} \text{ m} = \frac{1}{150} \text{ m} \) [1.5 Marks]
Length of cloth for ₹ 1350 = \( \frac{1}{150} \times 1350 \) [0.5 Marks]
\[ = \frac{1350}{150} = 9 \]
Ans: 9 meters of cloth can be purchased. [1 Mark]
Q10. A family of 4 members consumes 20 kg of rice in a month. How much rice will be consumed by a family of 7 members in a month?
[3 Marks]
Rice consumed by 4 members = 20 kg
Rice consumed by 1 member = \( \frac{20}{4} = 5 \text{ kg} \) [1.5 Marks]
Rice consumed by 7 members = \( 5 \times 7 \) [0.5 Marks]
\[ = 35 \text{ kg} \]
Ans: The family will consume 35 kg of rice. [1 Mark]
SECTION C
Q11. In an apartment building, the maintenance charge for an apartment with an area of 800 sq. feet is ₹ 3200 per month.
(a) What is the maintenance charge per square foot?
(b) What will be the maintenance charge for an apartment with an area of 1250 sq. feet?
(c) If a resident paid ₹ 5600 as maintenance charge, what is the area of their apartment?
[5 Marks]
Part (a):
Charge for 800 sq. ft = ₹ 3200
Charge for 1 sq. ft = \( \frac{3200}{800} \)
\[ = \text{₹ } 4 \text{ per sq. ft} \] [1.5 Marks]
Part (b):
Charge for 1250 sq. ft = Charge per sq. ft × 1250
\[ = 4 \times 1250 = \text{₹ } 5000 \] [1.5 Marks]
Part (c):
Area for charge of ₹ 4 = 1 sq. ft
Area for charge of ₹ 5600 = \( \frac{5600}{4} \)
\[ = 1400 \text{ sq. ft} \] [2 Marks]
Ans: (a) ₹ 4, (b) ₹ 5000, (c) 1400 sq. feet
Q12. A factory produces 25,200 pens in a standard 30-day month. Assuming production is uniform:
(a) Find the number of pens produced in a single day.
(b) How many pens will be produced in a leap year if the factory operates for 300 days only?
(c) How many days will it take to produce 63,000 pens?
[5 Marks]
Part (a):
Pens produced in 30 days = 25,200
Pens produced in 1 day = \( \frac{25200}{30} \)
\[ = 840 \text{ pens} \] [1.5 Marks]
Part (b):
Note: The mention of a leap year is extra information here, since the working days are explicitly given as 300.
Pens produced in 300 days = Pens per day × 300
\[ = 840 \times 300 \]
\[ = 252,000 \text{ pens} \] [1.5 Marks]
Part (c):
Days required to produce 840 pens = 1 day
Days required to produce 63,000 pens = \( \frac{63000}{840} \)
\[ = 75 \text{ days} \] [2 Marks]
Ans: (a) 840 pens, (b) 252,000 pens, (c) 75 days
Q13. An airplane flies 3150 km in 4.5 hours flying at a constant speed.
(i) Calculate the speed of the airplane in km/hr.
(ii) How much distance will it cover if it flies for 7 hours?
(iii) How much time will it take to cover a distance of 4900 km?
[5 Marks]
Part (i):
Distance in 4.5 hours = 3150 km
Distance in 1 hour (Speed) = \( \frac{3150}{4.5} \)
\[ = \frac{31500}{45} = 700 \text{ km/hr} \] [2 Marks]
Part (ii):
Distance covered in 7 hours = Speed × Time
\[ = 700 \times 7 \]
\[ = 4900 \text{ km} \] [1.5 Marks]
Part (iii):
Time to cover 700 km = 1 hour
Time to cover 4900 km = \( \frac{4900}{700} \)
\[ = 7 \text{ hours} \] [1.5 Marks]
Ans: (i) 700 km/hr, (ii) 4900 km, (iii) 7 hours
