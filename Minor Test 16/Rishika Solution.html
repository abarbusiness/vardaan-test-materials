Detailed Solution Key (CBSE)

Class: 7 CBSE | Subject: Mathematics | Test: Minor Test 16

Topic: Algebraic Expressions | Max Marks: 40

SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Write algebraic expressions: (i) Sum of a and b subtracted from their product. (ii) One-fourth of the sum of p and q.
Solution:
(i) Product is \( ab \). Sum is \( (a + b) \). Expression is \( ab - (a + b) \).
(ii) Sum is \( (p + q) \). One-fourth of this is \( \frac{1}{4}(p + q) \) or \( \frac{p + q}{4} \).
2 Marks (1 mark each)
Q2. Identify numerical coefficients of terms (other than constants): (i) \( 100m + 1000n \) (ii) \( -p^2q^2 + 7pq \)
Solution:
(i) Term \( 100m \) has coefficient 100. Term \( 1000n \) has coefficient 1000.
(ii) Term \( -p^2q^2 \) has coefficient -1. Term \( 7pq \) has coefficient 7.
2 Marks (1 mark each)
Q3. Identify like terms: \( 4m^2p, \ 3mp^2, \ -7mp^2, \ -4m^2p \)
Solution:
Like terms have the exact same algebraic factors.
Pair 1: \( 4m^2p \) and \( -4m^2p \)
Pair 2: \( 3mp^2 \) and \( -7mp^2 \)
2 Marks (1 mark per pair)
Q4. Classify as monomial, binomial, or trinomial: (i) \( z^2 - 3z + 8 \) (ii) \( 100 \)
Solution:
(i) It contains three unlike terms. It is a Trinomial.
(ii) It contains only one term. It is a Monomial.
2 Marks (1 mark each)
Q5. Coefficient of \( x \) in: (i) \( 3x - 15 \) (ii) \( y^2x + y \)
Solution:
(i) In term \( 3x \), coefficient of \( x \) is 3.
(ii) In term \( y^2x \), coefficient of \( x \) is \( y^2 \).
2 Marks (1 mark each)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Simplify: \( p - (p - q) - q - (q - p) \)
Solution:
Opening the brackets (reversing signs inside):
\( = p - p + q - q - q + p \)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (p - p + p) + (q - q - q) \)
\( = (1 - 1 + 1)p + (1 - 1 - 1)q \)
\( = p - q \)
3 Marks (Brackets: 1m, Grouping: 1m, Ans: 1m)
Q7. Add: \( t - 8tz, \ 3tz - z, \ z - t \)
Solution:
\( = (t - 8tz) + (3tz - z) + (z - t) \)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (t - t) + (-8tz + 3tz) + (-z + z) \)
\( = (0) + (-5tz) + (0) \)
\( = -5tz \)
3 Marks (Setup: 1m, Solving: 2m)
Q8. Subtract \( 24ab - 10b - 18a \) from \( 30ab + 12b + 14a \).
Solution:
\( = (30ab + 12b + 14a) - (24ab - 10b - 18a) \)
Opening brackets:
\( = 30ab + 12b + 14a - 24ab + 10b + 18a \)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (30ab - 24ab) + (12b + 10b) + (14a + 18a) \)
\( = 6ab + 22b + 32a \)
3 Marks (Signs: 1m, Solving: 2m)
Q9. Find value of \( 2a^2 - ab + b^2 \) when \( a = 1, b = -1 \).
Solution:
Substitute \( a = 1 \) and \( b = -1 \):
\( = 2(1)^2 - (1)(-1) + (-1)^2 \)
\( = 2(1) - (-1) + (1) \)
\( = 2 + 1 + 1 \)
\( = 4 \)
3 Marks (Subst: 1.5m, Calc: 1.5m)
Q10. Find value of '\( k \)' if \( 2x^2 + x - k = 5 \), when \( x = 0 \).
Solution:
Given expression: \( 2x^2 + x - k = 5 \)
Substitute \( x = 0 \) into the equation:
\( 2(0)^2 + (0) - k = 5 \)
\( 0 + 0 - k = 5 \)
\( -k = 5 \implies \) \( k = -5 \)
3 Marks (Setup: 1.5m, Ans: 1.5m)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. (a) Subtract \( 3pq (p - q) \) from \( 2pq (p + q) \). (b) Add: \( 5m (3 - m) \) and \( 6m^2 - 13m \).
Solution (a):
\( = 2pq(p + q) - 3pq(p - q) \)
\( = (2p^2q + 2pq^2) - (3p^2q - 3pq^2) \)
\( = 2p^2q + 2pq^2 - 3p^2q + 3pq^2 \)
Ans: \( -p^2q + 5pq^2 \)
Solution (b):
\( = 5m(3 - m) + (6m^2 - 13m) \)
\( = 15m - 5m^2 + 6m^2 - 13m \)
\( = (-5m^2 + 6m^2) + (15m - 13m) \)
Ans: \( m^2 + 2m \)
5 Marks ((a): 2.5m, (b): 2.5m)
Q12. From sum of \( 2y^2 + 3yz, \ -y^2 - yz - z^2 \) and \( yz + 2z^2 \), subtract the sum of \( 3y^2 - z^2 \) and \( -y^2 + yz + z^2 \).
Step 1: First Sum
\( = (2y^2 + 3yz) + (-y^2 - yz - z^2) + (yz + 2z^2) \)
\( = (2y^2 - y^2) + (3yz - yz + yz) + (-z^2 + 2z^2) \)
Sum 1 \( = y^2 + 3yz + z^2 \)
Step 2: Second Sum
\( = (3y^2 - z^2) + (-y^2 + yz + z^2) \)
\( = (3y^2 - y^2) + yz + (-z^2 + z^2) \)
Sum 2 \( = 2y^2 + yz \)
Step 3: Subtraction (Sum 1 - Sum 2)
\( = (y^2 + 3yz + z^2) - (2y^2 + yz) \)
\( = y^2 + 3yz + z^2 - 2y^2 - yz \)
Final Ans \( = -y^2 + 2yz + z^2 \)
5 Marks (S1: 2m, S2: 1m, Sub: 2m)
Q13. (a) Simplify \( 4(2x - 1) + 3x + 11 \) and find value when \( x = 2 \). (b) Find value of \( n^3 - 5n^2 + 5n - 2 \) when \( n = -2 \).
Solution (a):
Simplify: \( = 8x - 4 + 3x + 11 \)
Simplified Expression: \( 11x + 7 \)
Substitute \( x = 2 \): \( 11(2) + 7 = 22 + 7 = \) \( 29 \)
Solution (b):
Expression: \( n^3 - 5n^2 + 5n - 2 \)
Substitute \( n = -2 \):
\( = (-2)^3 - 5(-2)^2 + 5(-2) - 2 \)
\( = -8 - 5(4) - 10 - 2 \)
\( = -8 - 20 - 10 - 2 \)
Final Ans \( = -40 \)
5 Marks ((a): 2.5m, (b): 2.5m)
