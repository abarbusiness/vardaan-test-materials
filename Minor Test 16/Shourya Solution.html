SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Define the following terms: (a) Element (b) Compound
[2 Marks]
Solution:

(a) Element (1 Mark): An element is a pure substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances by ordinary chemical methods. It is made up of only one kind of atom.

(b) Compound (1 Mark): A compound is a pure substance formed when two or more elements combine chemically in a fixed ratio by mass.

Q2. Write the algebraic expression for: (a) 5 added to the variable \( p \) (b) 7 subtracted from twice of \( x \).
[2 Marks]
Solution:

(a) The expression for 5 added to \( p \) is:
\( p + 5 \) (1 Mark)

(b) Twice of \( x \) is \( 2x \). 7 subtracted from it is:
\( 2x - 7 \) (1 Mark)

Q4. Identify the like terms in the given algebraic expression: \( 3x^2 + 2x - 5x^2 + 7 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Like terms are terms that have the exact same variable parts and exponents.

In the given expression, the terms are: \( 3x^2 \), \( 2x \), \( -5x^2 \), and \( 7 \).

The like terms are: \( 3x^2 \) and \( -5x^2 \). (2 Marks)

SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Evaluate the value of the expression \( 3a + 2b - c \) if \( a = 2, b = 3, \) and \( c = 4 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Given expression: \( 3a + 2b - c \)

Substituting the values \( a = 2, b = 3, c = 4 \):

\( = 3(2) + 2(3) - (4) \) (1 Mark for correct substitution)
\( = 6 + 6 - 4 \) (1 Mark for correct multiplication)
\( = 12 - 4 \)
\( = \mathbf{8} \) (1 Mark for final correct answer)
Q7. State any three main differences between a mixture and a compound.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Parameter Mixture Compound
1. Composition Components are mixed in any variable proportion. Elements are combined chemically in a fixed ratio by mass.
2. Properties It shows the properties of its constituent components. Its properties are entirely different from its constituent elements.
3. Separation Components can be easily separated by physical methods (e.g., filtration). Components can only be separated by chemical or electrochemical methods.

(1 Mark for each correct valid difference).

Q8. Add the following algebraic expressions: \( (2x + 3y) \) and \( (4x - y) \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:

We need to find the sum: \( (2x + 3y) + (4x - y) \)

Step 1: Open the brackets.
\( = 2x + 3y + 4x - y \) (1 Mark)

Step 2: Group the like terms together.
\( = (2x + 4x) + (3y - y) \) (1 Mark)

Step 3: Add the coefficients of like terms.
\( = \mathbf{6x + 2y} \) (1 Mark for final answer)
Q9. What is meant by a homogeneous mixture? Give two examples.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Definition: A homogeneous mixture is a mixture in which the components are uniformly distributed throughout, and it has a single phase. The different parts cannot be seen separately. (1 Mark)

Examples:
1. Sugar dissolved in water (sugar solution) (1 Mark)
2. Air (a uniform mixture of various gases) (1 Mark)

Q10. Write the numerical coefficient of \( x \) in the following algebraic terms: (a) \( 5x \) (b) \( -7xy \) (c) \( x \)
[3 Marks]
Solution:

The numerical coefficient is the constant (number) part attached to the variables.

(a) \( 5x \) : The numerical coefficient of \( x \) is 5. (1 Mark)

(b) \( -7xy \) : The numerical coefficient attached to the variables is -7. (1 Mark - Deduct 0.5 if negative sign is missed)

(c) \( x \) : This can be written as \( 1 \times x \). Hence, the numerical coefficient is 1. (1 Mark)

SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. (a) Why is water considered a compound and not a mixture? Give two reasons.
(b) Write the chemical formula for: (i) Carbon dioxide (ii) Water (iii) Sodium chloride
[5 Marks]
Solution:

(a) Reasons water is a compound: (2 Marks)

  1. Water (\(H_2O\)) is formed when Hydrogen and Oxygen combine chemically in a fixed ratio by mass (1:8). Mixtures do not have a fixed ratio.
  2. The properties of water are entirely different from its constituent elements (Hydrogen is a combustible gas, Oxygen supports combustion, but water is a liquid used to extinguish fires).

(b) Chemical Formulas: (3 Marks)

  • (i) Carbon dioxide: \( CO_2 \) (1 Mark)
  • (ii) Water: \( H_2O \) (1 Mark)
  • (iii) Sodium chloride: \( NaCl \) (1 Mark)
Q12. Simplify the following algebraic expression: \( 5a - [3a - (2a - 3b)] \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:

According to BODMAS rules, we first solve the innermost bracket i.e., the round brackets ( ).

Given: \( 5a - [3a - (2a - 3b)] \)

Step 1: Remove the round bracket. The negative sign outside changes the signs inside.
\( = 5a - [3a - 2a + 3b] \) (2 Marks)

Step 2: Simplify the terms inside the square bracket.
\( = 5a - [a + 3b] \) (1.5 Marks)

Step 3: Remove the square bracket.
\( = 5a - a - 3b \) (0.5 Marks)

Step 4: Final calculation.
\( = \mathbf{4a - 3b} \) (1 Mark for final correct answer)
Q13. (a) Subtract the expression \( (3p - 4q + 5r) \) from \( (5p + 2q - 3r) \).
(b) Write the algebraic expression for: "The sum of \( x \) and \( y \) is multiplied by \( z \)".
[5 Marks]
Solution:

Part (a): Subtraction (3 Marks)

We need to subtract \( (3p - 4q + 5r) \) from \( (5p + 2q - 3r) \).
Equation: \( (5p + 2q - 3r) - (3p - 4q + 5r) \) (1 Mark for correct setup)

Step 1: Open brackets (sign changes for the second expression).
\( = 5p + 2q - 3r - 3p + 4q - 5r \) (1 Mark)

Step 2: Group like terms and solve.
\( = (5p - 3p) + (2q + 4q) + (-3r - 5r) \)
\( = \mathbf{2p + 6q - 8r} \) (1 Mark for final answer)

Part (b): Algebraic Expression (2 Marks)

The sum of \( x \) and \( y \) is: \( (x + y) \) (1 Mark)
This sum multiplied by \( z \) is written as:
\( z(x + y) \) OR \( (x + y)z \) (1 Mark)
