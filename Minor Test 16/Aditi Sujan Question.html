Minor Test 16
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
  • Show proper working for numerical questions.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the cube root of \( -512 \). Explain your reasoning briefly.
2.
Evaluate the following expression using the laws of exponents/cubes: \( \sqrt[3]{64 \times 729} \)
3.
A shopkeeper buys an article for \( \text{Rs. } 450 \) and sells it for \( \text{Rs. } 540 \). Find his profit percentage.
4.
What is the smallest number by which 81 must be multiplied to make it a perfect cube?
5.
Define Marked Price (M.P.) and State the formula to calculate Discount when M.P. and Selling Price (S.P.) are given.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find the smallest number by which \( 1323 \) must be multiplied so that the product becomes a perfect cube. Show the prime factorization clearly.
7.
The marked price of a ceiling fan is \( \text{Rs. } 1250 \). The shopkeeper allows a discount of \( 12\% \) on it. Find the selling price of the fan.
8.
Rahul bought a pair of shoes for \( \text{Rs. } 896 \), which includes a GST of \( 12\% \). What was the original price of the shoes before GST was added?
9.
By selling an article for \( \text{Rs. } 770 \), a man incurs a loss of \( 12\% \). For how much should he sell it to gain \( 10\% \)?
10.
Calculate the cube of the decimal number \( 2.1 \). Show the step-by-step calculation using fractions.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A dealer marks his goods at \( 30\% \) above the cost price. He then allows a discount of \( 10\% \) for cash payment.
(a)
Find his overall gain or loss percentage. (3 Marks)
(b)
If the selling price of the goods is \( \text{Rs. } 1170 \), find its cost price. (2 Marks)
12.
An electronic item is marked at \( \text{Rs. } 50,000 \). The shopkeeper offers successive discounts of \( 10\% \) and \( 5\% \) on the marked price. Furthermore, a GST of \( 18\% \) is charged on the discounted price.
(a)
Calculate the final selling price after the successive discounts. (2 Marks)
(b)
Calculate the total GST amount the customer has to pay. (1.5 Marks)
(c)
What is the final amount billed to the customer? (1.5 Marks)
13.
Three numbers are in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 4 \). The sum of their cubes is \( 33957 \).
(a)
Formulate an algebraic equation based on the given condition. (2 Marks)
(b)
Solve the equation to find the three numbers. (3 Marks)
