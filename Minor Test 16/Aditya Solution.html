SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Find the complement of \( 35^\circ \) and the supplement of \( 110^\circ \). [2 Marks]
Concept: Complementary angles sum to \( 90^\circ \). Supplementary angles sum to \( 180^\circ \).
Complement of \( 35^\circ = 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ \). (1 Mark)
Supplement of \( 110^\circ = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ \). (1 Mark)
Answers: \( 55^\circ \) and \( 70^\circ \)
Q2. Can a triangle have angles measuring \( 45^\circ \), \( 55^\circ \), and \( 90^\circ \)? Justify your answer. [2 Marks]
Concept: By the Angle Sum Property of a triangle, the sum of all interior angles must be exactly \( 180^\circ \).
Sum of given angles = \( 45^\circ + 55^\circ + 90^\circ = 190^\circ \). (1 Mark)
Since \( 190^\circ \neq 180^\circ \), a triangle with these angles cannot exist. (1 Mark)
Answer: No
Q3. Two supplementary angles are in the ratio \( 3:2 \). Find the measure of both angles. [2 Marks]
Let the two angles be \( 3x \) and \( 2x \).
Since they are supplementary, their sum is \( 180^\circ \).
\[ 3x + 2x = 180^\circ \] \[ 5x = 180^\circ \] \[ x = \frac{180}{5} = 36^\circ \] (1 Mark)
First angle = \( 3x = 3 \times 36^\circ = 108^\circ \)
Second angle = \( 2x = 2 \times 36^\circ = 72^\circ \) (1 Mark)
Angles are \( 108^\circ \) and \( 72^\circ \)
Q4. The exterior angle of a triangle is \( 115^\circ \) and one of its interior opposite angles is \( 45^\circ \). Find the other interior opposite angle. [2 Marks]
Theorem: The measure of an exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the measures of its two interior opposite angles. (0.5 Mark)
Let the unknown interior opposite angle be \( x \).
\[ 115^\circ = 45^\circ + x \] (0.5 Mark) \[ x = 115^\circ - 45^\circ \] \[ x = 70^\circ \] (1 Mark)
The other interior opposite angle is \( 70^\circ \)
Q5. Define vertically opposite angles and state their primary mathematical property. [2 Marks]
Definition: Vertically opposite angles are the angles formed opposite to each other when two lines intersect. (1 Mark)
Property: Vertically opposite angles are always strictly equal in measure. (1 Mark)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Is it possible to construct a triangle with side lengths \( 5 \text{ cm} \), \( 7 \text{ cm} \), and \( 13 \text{ cm} \)? Give reasons for your answer based on geometric properties. [3 Marks]
Theorem Used (Triangle Inequality): The sum of the lengths of any two sides of a triangle must be strictly greater than the length of the third side. (1 Mark)
Let's check the given sides (\( 5, 7, 13 \)):
Sum of the two smaller sides = \( 5 \text{ cm} + 7 \text{ cm} = 12 \text{ cm} \). (1 Mark)
Here, \( 12 \text{ cm} \) is not greater than the third side (\( 13 \text{ cm} \)). (\( 12 < 13 \))
Therefore, construction is not possible. (1 Mark)
Answer: Not possible
Q7. A transversal intersects two parallel lines. If one pair of consecutive interior (co-interior) angles are represented by \( (2x + 10)^\circ \) and \( (3x - 20)^\circ \), find the value of \( x \) and the measure of both angles. [3 Marks]
Property: Consecutive interior (co-interior) angles formed by parallel lines are supplementary (sum = \( 180^\circ \)). (1 Mark)
\[ (2x + 10)^\circ + (3x - 20)^\circ = 180^\circ \] \[ 5x - 10 = 180 \] \[ 5x = 190 \] \[ x = \frac{190}{5} = 38 \] (1 Mark)
Substitute \( x \) back to find the angles:
Angle 1: \( 2(38) + 10 = 76 + 10 = 86^\circ \)
Angle 2: \( 3(38) - 20 = 114 - 20 = 94^\circ \) (1 Mark)
\( x = 38 \); Angles are \( 86^\circ \) and \( 94^\circ \)
Q8. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \( 2:3:4 \). Find the measure of each angle and classify the triangle based on its angles. [3 Marks]
Let the angles be \( 2x \), \( 3x \), and \( 4x \).
By the Angle Sum Property of a triangle:
\[ 2x + 3x + 4x = 180^\circ \] \[ 9x = 180^\circ \] \[ x = 20^\circ \] (1 Mark)
The angles are:
\( 2 \times 20^\circ = 40^\circ \)
\( 3 \times 20^\circ = 60^\circ \)
\( 4 \times 20^\circ = 80^\circ \) (1 Mark)
Since all three angles (\( 40^\circ, 60^\circ, 80^\circ \)) are less than \( 90^\circ \), it is an Acute-Angled Triangle. (1 Mark)
Q9. Find the measure of an angle which is equal to its own supplement. Show your working steps clearly. [3 Marks]
Let the required angle be \( x^\circ \).
The supplement of this angle will be \( (180 - x)^\circ \). (1 Mark)
According to the question, the angle is equal to its supplement:
\[ x = 180 - x \] (1 Mark) \[ x + x = 180 \] \[ 2x = 180 \] \[ x = 90^\circ \] (1 Mark)
The required angle is \( 90^\circ \)
Q10. In an isosceles \( \triangle ABC \), sides \( AB = AC \). If the vertical angle \( \angle A = 50^\circ \), calculate the base angles \( \angle B \) and \( \angle C \). [3 Marks]
Theorem: In a triangle, angles opposite to equal sides are equal. Since \( AB = AC \), we have \( \angle B = \angle C \). (1 Mark)
Let \( \angle B = \angle C = x^\circ \).
By the Angle Sum Property:
\[ \angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ \] \[ 50^\circ + x + x = 180^\circ \] (1 Mark) \[ 2x = 180^\circ - 50^\circ \] \[ 2x = 130^\circ \] \[ x = 65^\circ \] (1 Mark)
\( \angle B = 65^\circ \) and \( \angle C = 65^\circ \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. Lines \( AB \) and \( CD \) intersect at a point \( O \). If \( \angle AOC = (3x + 10)^\circ \) and \( \angle BOD = (4x - 15)^\circ \), solve for: (a) \( x \), (b) \( \angle AOC \), (c) \( \angle AOD \) & \( \angle BOC \). [5 Marks]
(a) Find \( x \):
Since \( AB \) and \( CD \) intersect at \( O \), \( \angle AOC \) and \( \angle BOD \) form a pair of vertically opposite angles. They must be equal.
\[ 3x + 10 = 4x - 15 \] \[ 10 + 15 = 4x - 3x \] \[ 25 = x \implies x = 25 \] (2 Marks)
(b) Find \( \angle AOC \):
Substitute \( x = 25 \) into the expression: \[ \angle AOC = 3(25) + 10 = 75 + 10 = 85^\circ \] (1 Mark)
(c) Find \( \angle AOD \) and \( \angle BOC \):
\( \angle AOC \) and \( \angle AOD \) form a linear pair on straight line \( AB \) (or \( CD \)).
\[ \angle AOC + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \] \[ 85^\circ + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \] \[ \angle AOD = 180^\circ - 85^\circ = 95^\circ \] (1 Mark)
\( \angle BOC \) and \( \angle AOD \) are vertically opposite, so \( \angle BOC = \angle AOD = 95^\circ \). (1 Mark)
Q12. In \( \triangle PQR \), side \( QR \) is produced to a point \( S \) forming an exterior angle. If the exterior angle \( \angle PRS = 105^\circ \) and the interior opposite angles are in the ratio \( \angle P : \angle Q = 3:4 \): Find all three interior angles of \( \triangle PQR \). Clearly state the geometric theorems you have utilized. [5 Marks]
Let \( \angle P = 3x \) and \( \angle Q = 4x \).
Theorem 1: Exterior Angle Theorem. The exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles. (1 Mark)
\[ \angle PRS = \angle P + \angle Q \] \[ 105^\circ = 3x + 4x \] \[ 105^\circ = 7x \] \[ x = \frac{105}{7} = 15^\circ \] (1.5 Marks)
Now, calculate the interior opposite angles:
\( \angle P = 3(15) = 45^\circ \)
\( \angle Q = 4(15) = 60^\circ \) (1 Mark)
Theorem 2: Angle Sum Property OR Linear Pair.
Method A (Linear Pair): \( \angle PRQ + \angle PRS = 180^\circ \implies \angle PRQ = 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ \).
Method B (Angle Sum): \( \angle R = 180^\circ - (45^\circ + 60^\circ) = 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ \). (1.5 Marks)
Interior angles are \( \angle P = 45^\circ \), \( \angle Q = 60^\circ \), and \( \angle PRQ = 75^\circ \)
Q13. Two parallel lines \( l \) and \( m \) are intersected by a transversal \( t \). A pair of alternate interior angles are given by the expressions \( (4y - 10)^\circ \) and \( (2y + 30)^\circ \). Find: (a) \( y \), (b) alternate interior angles, (c) co-interior angles. [5 Marks]
(a) Find \( y \):
Property: If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, the alternate interior angles are equal.
\[ 4y - 10 = 2y + 30 \] \[ 4y - 2y = 30 + 10 \] \[ 2y = 40 \implies y = 20 \] (2 Marks)
(b) Find the measure of these alternate interior angles:
Substitute \( y = 20 \) into either expression:
\[ \text{Angle} = 4(20) - 10 = 80 - 10 = 70^\circ \] (Check: \( 2(20) + 30 = 40 + 30 = 70^\circ \)). Both are \( 70^\circ \). (1 Mark)
(c) Calculate the interior angles on the same side of transversal (co-interior angles):
Co-interior angles are supplementary to the given alternate interior angles (they form a linear pair with the alternate interior angle at the same intersection).
Let the co-interior angle be \( z \). \[ z + 70^\circ = 180^\circ \] \[ z = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ \] (2 Marks)
Co-interior angles measure \( 110^\circ \) each.
