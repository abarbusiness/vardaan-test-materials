SECTION A
1.
Express the following ratio in its simplest form: 15 : 25
[2 Marks]
Solution:
We divide both numbers by their highest common factor (HCF).
The HCF of 15 and 25 is 5.
$$ \text{Ratio} = \frac{15}{25} = \frac{15 \div 5}{25 \div 5} = \frac{3}{5} $$ (1 Mark)
The simplest form is 3 : 5. (1 Mark)
2.
A boy runs a distance of 100 meters in 20 seconds. Find his speed.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Distance = 100 meters, Time = 20 seconds
Formula: $\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$ (1 Mark)
$$ \text{Speed} = \frac{100}{20} = 5 \text{ m/s} $$
The speed of the boy is 5 m/s. (1 Mark)
3.
Find the ratio of 40 cm to 1 meter.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
First, make the units same.
We know, 1 meter = 100 cm. (1 Mark)
Now, find the ratio of 40 cm to 100 cm.
$$ \text{Ratio} = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5} $$
The required ratio is 2 : 5. (1 Mark)
4.
Convert a speed of 18 km/hr into m/s.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
To convert km/hr to m/s, we multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$. (1 Mark)
$$ \text{Speed in m/s} = 18 \times \frac{5}{18} $$ $$ = 1 \times 5 = 5 \text{ m/s} $$
The speed is 5 m/s. (1 Mark)
5.
If $x$ : 3 = 10 : 15, find the value of $x$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
We can write the proportion as fractions:
$$ \frac{x}{3} = \frac{10}{15} $$ (1 Mark)
Cross multiply to find $x$:
$$ 15 \times x = 3 \times 10 $$ $$ 15x = 30 $$ $$ x = \frac{30}{15} = 2 $$
The value of $x$ is 2. (1 Mark)
SECTION B
6.
Divide ₹600 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio 2 : 3.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Total amount = ₹600
Ratio = 2 : 3
Sum of ratio terms = $2 + 3 = 5$. (1 Mark)
Amit's share:
$$ \text{Amit's share} = \frac{2}{5} \times 600 = 2 \times 120 = ₹240 $$ (1 Mark)
Sumit's share:
$$ \text{Sumit's share} = \frac{3}{5} \times 600 = 3 \times 120 = ₹360 $$
Amit gets ₹240 and Sumit gets ₹360. (1 Mark)
7.
A car is moving with a constant speed of 40 km/hr. How much distance will it cover in 3 hours?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Speed = 40 km/hr, Time = 3 hours
Formula: $\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$ (1.5 Marks)
$$ \text{Distance} = 40 \times 3 $$ $$ \text{Distance} = 120 \text{ km} $$
The car will cover a distance of 120 km. (1.5 Marks)
8.
Are the numbers 2, 4, 6, and 12 in proportion? Show your working clearly.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Numbers are in proportion if the product of extremes equals the product of means.
Extremes are the first and last terms: 2 and 12.
Means are the middle terms: 4 and 6.
$$ \text{Product of extremes} = 2 \times 12 = 24 $$ (1 Mark)
$$ \text{Product of means} = 4 \times 6 = 24 $$ (1 Mark)
Since $24 = 24$ (Product of extremes = Product of means),
Yes, the numbers 2, 4, 6, and 12 are in proportion. (1 Mark)
9.
If the cost of 4 pens is ₹48, find the cost of 7 such pens.
[3 Marks]
Solution: (Using Unitary Method)
Cost of 4 pens = ₹48
Cost of 1 pen = $\frac{48}{4} = ₹12$. (1.5 Marks)
Now, calculate the cost of 7 pens.
$$ \text{Cost of 7 pens} = 7 \times 12 = ₹84 $$
The cost of 7 pens is ₹84. (1.5 Marks)
10.
Convert a speed of 15 m/s into km/hr.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
To convert m/s to km/hr, we multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$. (1.5 Marks)
$$ \text{Speed in km/hr} = 15 \times \frac{18}{5} $$ $$ = 3 \times 18 = 54 \text{ km/hr} $$
The speed is 54 km/hr. (1.5 Marks)
SECTION C
11.
In a school, there are 400 boys and 300 girls. Find the ratio of:
(i) The number of boys to the number of girls.
(ii) The number of girls to the total number of students.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Number of boys = 400
Number of girls = 300
Total number of students = $400 + 300 = 700$. (1 Mark)
(i) Ratio of boys to girls:
$$ \text{Ratio} = \frac{400}{300} = \frac{4}{3} $$
The ratio of boys to girls is 4 : 3. (2 Marks)
(ii) Ratio of girls to total students:
$$ \text{Ratio} = \frac{300}{700} = \frac{3}{7} $$
The ratio of girls to total students is 3 : 7. (2 Marks)
12.
A bus travels a distance of 180 km in 4 hours.
(i) Calculate the speed of the bus in km/hr.
(ii) At this speed, how much time will it take to travel 270 km?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(i) Finding the speed:
Distance = 180 km, Time = 4 hours.
$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$ (1 Mark)
$$ \text{Speed} = \frac{180}{4} = 45 \text{ km/hr} $$
The speed of the bus is 45 km/hr. (1 Mark)
(ii) Finding time for new distance:
New Distance = 270 km, Speed = 45 km/hr.
$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$ (1 Mark)
$$ \text{Time} = \frac{270}{45} = 6 \text{ hours} $$
It will take 6 hours to travel 270 km. (2 Marks)
13.
Answer the following questions:
(i) Find the value of $y$ if 5, $y$, 15, and 27 are in proportion.
(ii) A man walks at a speed of 5 m/s. How far will he walk in 2 minutes? (Give your answer in meters).
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(i) Finding $y$ in proportion:
The terms 5, $y$, 15, and 27 are in proportion.
$$ \text{Therefore, } \frac{5}{y} = \frac{15}{27} $$ (1 Mark)
Cross multiply:
$$ 15 \times y = 5 \times 27 $$ $$ 15y = 135 $$ $$ y = \frac{135}{15} = 9 $$
The value of $y$ is 9. (1.5 Marks)
(ii) Finding distance walked:
Speed = 5 m/s. Time = 2 minutes.
First, convert time to seconds to match the speed unit.
$$ \text{Time} = 2 \times 60 = 120 \text{ seconds}. $$ (1 Mark)
$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$
$$ \text{Distance} = 5 \times 120 = 600 \text{ meters}. $$
The man will walk 600 meters. (1.5 Marks)
