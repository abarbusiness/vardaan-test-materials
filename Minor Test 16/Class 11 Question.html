MINOR TEST 16: CHEMICAL BONDING & MOLECULAR STRUCTURE
Class: 11 Subject: Chemistry Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Follow strictly the rationalised NCERT syllabus.
  • Section A: Q1 to Q10 are very short answer/objective type questions carrying 1 mark each.
  • Section B: Q11 to Q15 are short answer questions carrying 2 marks each.
  • Section C: Q16 to Q20 are short answer questions carrying 3 marks each.
  • Section D: Q21 to Q23 are long answer questions carrying 5 marks each.
  • Write precise answers. Use clear diagrams and molecular orbital charts where necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Very Short Answer / Objective Type - 1 Mark Each)
1.
Draw the Lewis dot structure of the carbonate ion, \( \text{CO}_3^{2-} \).
2.
Identify the molecule that has a zero dipole moment: \( \text{NH}_3 \), \( \text{NF}_3 \), \( \text{BF}_3 \), or \( \text{H}_2\text{O} \).
3.
State the essential condition for the formation of a hydrogen bond.
4.
Comparing \( \text{O}_2 \) and \( \text{O}_2^+ \), which species has a shorter bond length?
5.
How many sigma (\( \sigma \)) and pi (\( \pi \)) bonds are present in a molecule of ethyne (\( \text{C}_2\text{H}_2 \))?
6.
Assertion (A): The molecule \( \text{SF}_6 \) is stable despite having an expanded octet.
Reason (R): Sulfur has vacant d-orbitals which allow it to expand its valence shell.
(a) Both A and R are true and R explains A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R does not explain A.
(c) A is true but R is false.
(d) A is false but R is true.
7.
What is the shape of the \( \text{ClF}_3 \) molecule according to VSEPR theory?
8.
Which out of o-nitrophenol and p-nitrophenol has a higher boiling point and why?
9.
Write the state of hybridization of carbon in methane (\( \text{CH}_4 \)) and ethene (\( \text{C}_2\text{H}_4 \)).
10.
Define the term 'Bond Order' in the context of Molecular Orbital Theory.
SECTION B (10 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 2 Marks Each)
11.
Calculate the formal charge on the central oxygen atom in an ozone (\( \text{O}_3 \)) molecule.
12.
Explain why water (\( \text{H}_2\text{O} \)) is a liquid at room temperature whereas hydrogen sulfide (\( \text{H}_2\text{S} \)) is a gas.
13.
Predict the shape and hybridization of the \( \text{XeF}_4 \) molecule.
14.
Although both \( \text{NH}_3 \) and \( \text{H}_2\text{O} \) undergo sp³ hybridization, their bond angles are 107° and 104.5° respectively. Explain this difference.
15.
Give two main points of difference between a Sigma (\( \sigma \)) bond and a Pi (\( \pi \)) bond.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
16.
Define dipole moment. Explain why \( \text{NH}_3 \) has a much higher dipole moment (\( 1.47 \, \text{D} \)) than \( \text{NF}_3 \) (\( 0.23 \, \text{D} \)) despite Fluorine being more electronegative than Nitrogen.
17.
What is meant by hybridization? Describe the hybridization involved in the \( \text{PCl}_5 \) molecule. Why are the axial bonds in \( \text{PCl}_5 \) slightly longer than the equatorial bonds?
18.
Write the molecular orbital electronic configuration for the \( \text{O}_2 \) molecule. Using this configuration, calculate its bond order and explain its paramagnetic behavior.
19.
State the basic postulates of the VSEPR theory. Based on this theory, predict the shape of the \( \text{BrF}_5 \) molecule.
20.
Define resonance. Explain the concept of resonance with reference to the carbonate ion (\( \text{CO}_3^{2-} \)), confirming that all three C-O bonds are of equal length.
SECTION D (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
21.
(a) Differentiate between Intermolecular and Intramolecular hydrogen bonding with one example each.
(b) Discuss the shape of the following molecules using the VSEPR model:
(i) \( \text{SF}_4 \)
(ii) \( \text{PH}_3 \)
(c) Why does \( \text{BeCl}_2 \) possess a zero dipole moment although the Be-Cl bond is polar?
22.
(a) Explain the Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT). Differentiate between bonding and antibonding molecular orbitals (any two points).
(b) Write the molecular orbital configuration for \( \text{N}_2 \), \( \text{N}_2^+ \), and \( \text{N}_2^- \).
(c) Calculate their respective bond orders and arrange them in decreasing order of their stability.
23.
(a) What is Lattice Enthalpy? How does it affect the stability of ionic compounds?
(b) State Fajan's rules. Using these rules, explain why Silver Chloride (\( \text{AgCl} \)) shows covalent character while Sodium Chloride (\( \text{NaCl} \)) is highly ionic.
(c) State the octet rule. Give two examples of molecules that act as exceptions by demonstrating an expanded octet.
