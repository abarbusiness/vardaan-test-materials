Minor Test 16
Class: 10 ICSE Subject: Chemistry (HCl & Ammonia) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write balanced chemical equations wherever necessary.
  • Answers must be exact and based strictly on ICSE curriculum guidelines.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Name the drying agent used in the laboratory preparation of hydrogen chloride gas. Why is concentrated sulphuric acid chosen for this purpose?
2.
Write the balanced chemical equation for the action of warm water on Magnesium nitride (\(Mg_3N_2\)). Name the pungent-smelling gas evolved.
3.
How is "back suction" avoided during the preparation of hydrochloric acid by dissolving hydrogen chloride gas in water? Name the special arrangement used.
4.
State your observation when ammonium hydroxide is added dropwise and then in excess to an aqueous solution of copper (II) sulphate.
5.
What is aqua regia? State its unique chemical property regarding noble metals.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Differentiate between the burning of ammonia in oxygen and the catalytic oxidation of ammonia by stating the conditions and writing the balanced chemical equations for both.
7.
Write balanced chemical equations for the reaction of dilute hydrochloric acid with:
(a) Sodium sulphite
(b) Lead nitrate solution
(c) Sodium hydrogen carbonate (Sodium bicarbonate)
8.
Dry ammonia gas is passed over heated Copper (II) oxide.
(a) State what you observe.
(b) Write the balanced chemical equation for this reaction.
9.
Explain the Fountain Experiment for Hydrogen Chloride gas. State the two specific properties of hydrogen chloride gas demonstrated by this experiment.
10.
Write balanced equations for the reaction of chlorine gas with:
(a) Excess of ammonia.
(b) Ammonia, where chlorine is in excess.
Name the highly explosive product formed in one of these reactions.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
With reference to the laboratory preparation of ammonia from ammonium chloride:
(a) Write the balanced chemical equation for the reaction.
(b) Why is calcium hydroxide preferred over sodium hydroxide for this preparation?
(c) Name the drying agent used for ammonia and explain why concentrated sulphuric acid is NOT used to dry the gas.
(d) How is the ammonia gas collected?
12.
With reference to the manufacture of ammonia by Haber's Process:
(a) State the reactants and their ratio used in the process.
(b) State the optimum temperature, pressure, and the catalyst used.
(c) Write the balanced chemical equation for the process.
(d) State two important uses of ammonia.
13.
Give balanced chemical equations and appropriate observations for the following:
(a) Dilute hydrochloric acid is added to iron (II) sulphide. (Identify the gas evolved).
(b) Silver nitrate solution is added to dilute hydrochloric acid, followed by the addition of excess ammonium hydroxide to the product.
(c) Aqueous ammonia is added drop by drop to iron (III) chloride solution.
