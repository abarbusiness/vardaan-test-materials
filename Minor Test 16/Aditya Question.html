Minor Test 16
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show relevant working steps.
  • Syllabus: Triangles, Lines and Angles.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the complement of \( 35^\circ \) and the supplement of \( 110^\circ \).
2.
Can a triangle have angles measuring \( 45^\circ \), \( 55^\circ \), and \( 90^\circ \)? Justify your answer.
3.
Two supplementary angles are in the ratio \( 3:2 \). Find the measure of both angles.
4.
The exterior angle of a triangle is \( 115^\circ \) and one of its interior opposite angles is \( 45^\circ \). Find the other interior opposite angle.
5.
Define vertically opposite angles and state their primary mathematical property.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Is it possible to construct a triangle with side lengths \( 5 \text{ cm} \), \( 7 \text{ cm} \), and \( 13 \text{ cm} \)? Give reasons for your answer based on geometric properties.
7.
A transversal intersects two parallel lines. If one pair of consecutive interior (co-interior) angles are represented by \( (2x + 10)^\circ \) and \( (3x - 20)^\circ \), find the value of \( x \) and the measure of both angles.
8.
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \( 2:3:4 \). Find the measure of each angle and classify the triangle based on its angles.
9.
Find the measure of an angle which is equal to its own supplement. Show your working steps clearly.
10.
In an isosceles \( \triangle ABC \), sides \( AB = AC \). If the vertical angle \( \angle A = 50^\circ \), calculate the base angles \( \angle B \) and \( \angle C \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Lines \( AB \) and \( CD \) intersect at a point \( O \). If \( \angle AOC = (3x + 10)^\circ \) and \( \angle BOD = (4x - 15)^\circ \), solve for:
(a) The value of \( x \).
(b) The measure of \( \angle AOC \).
(c) The measure of \( \angle AOD \) and \( \angle BOC \).
12.
In \( \triangle PQR \), side \( QR \) is produced to a point \( S \) forming an exterior angle. If the exterior angle \( \angle PRS = 105^\circ \) and the interior opposite angles are in the ratio \( \angle P : \angle Q = 3:4 \):

Find all three interior angles of \( \triangle PQR \). Clearly state the geometric theorems you have utilized.
13.
Two parallel lines \( l \) and \( m \) are intersected by a transversal \( t \). A pair of alternate interior angles are given by the expressions \( (4y - 10)^\circ \) and \( (2y + 30)^\circ \).
(a) Frame an equation and find the value of \( y \).
(b) Find the exact measure of these alternate interior angles.
(c) Calculate the measure of the interior angles on the same side of the transversal (co-interior angles) adjacent to them.
