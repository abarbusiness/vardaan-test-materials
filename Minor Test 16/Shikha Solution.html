SECTION A
Q1. Express the ratio of \( 45 \text{ minutes} \) to \( 1.5 \text{ hours} \) in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Concept: A ratio can only be formed when both quantities are in the same unit.
First, convert hours into minutes to make units the same.
\( 1.5 \text{ hours} = 1.5 \times 60 \text{ minutes} = 90 \text{ minutes} \) [1 Mark]
Now, calculate the ratio:
\[ \text{Ratio} = \frac{45}{90} \]
Dividing numerator and denominator by 45:
\[ = \frac{1}{2} \]
Ans: \( 1 : 2 \) [1 Mark]
Q2. Check if the numbers \( 12, 16, 18, \) and \( 24 \) are in proportion.
[2 Marks]
Concept: Four numbers \( a, b, c, d \) are in proportion if the product of extremes equals the product of means (\( a \times d = b \times c \)).
Extremes (first and last terms) = \( 12 \) and \( 24 \)
Means (middle terms) = \( 16 \) and \( 18 \)
Product of extremes = \( 12 \times 24 = 288 \) [0.5 Mark]
Product of means = \( 16 \times 18 = 288 \) [0.5 Mark]
Since, Product of extremes = Product of means
Ans: Yes, the numbers are in proportion. [1 Mark]
Q3. If \( 8 \) notebooks cost \( \text{Rs. } 120 \), what is the cost of \( 5 \) such notebooks?
[2 Marks]
Concept: Unitary Method - Find the value of one unit first, then multiply to find the value of required units.
Cost of 8 notebooks = \( \text{Rs. } 120 \)
Cost of 1 notebook = \( \frac{120}{8} = \text{Rs. } 15 \) [1 Mark]
Cost of 5 notebooks = \( 15 \times 5 = \text{Rs. } 75 \)
Ans: The cost of 5 notebooks is Rs. 75. [1 Mark]
Q4. Divide \( \text{Rs. } 500 \) between Aman and Bina in the ratio \( 3 : 2 \).
[2 Marks]
Concept: To divide a quantity in a ratio, find the total parts and then distribute according to individual parts.
Sum of the ratio terms = \( 3 + 2 = 5 \text{ parts} \) [0.5 Mark]
Aman's share = \( \frac{3}{5} \times 500 \)
\[ = 3 \times 100 = \text{Rs. } 300 \] [0.5 Mark]
Bina's share = \( \frac{2}{5} \times 500 \)
\[ = 2 \times 100 = \text{Rs. } 200 \] [0.5 Mark]
Ans: Aman gets Rs. 300, Bina gets Rs. 200. [0.5 Mark]
Q5. Find the value of \( x \) if \( x : 4 :: 9 : 12 \).
[2 Marks]
Using the property of proportion: Product of extremes = Product of means.
\[ x \times 12 = 4 \times 9 \] [1 Mark]
\[ 12x = 36 \]
\[ x = \frac{36}{12} \]
\[ x = 3 \]
Ans: The value of \( x \) is 3. [1 Mark]
SECTION B
Q6. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is \( 5 : 3 \). If there are \( 450 \) boys, find the number of girls and the total number of students in the school.
[3 Marks]
Let the number of boys be \( 5x \) and girls be \( 3x \).
Given, number of boys = \( 450 \)
\[ 5x = 450 \implies x = \frac{450}{5} = 90 \] [1 Mark]
Number of girls = \( 3x = 3 \times 90 = 270 \) [1 Mark]
Total number of students = Boys + Girls
\[ = 450 + 270 = 720 \]
Ans: There are 270 girls and a total of 720 students. [1 Mark]
Q7. Which ratio is greater: \( 5 : 8 \) or \( 3 : 4 \)? Show your working.
[3 Marks]
Concept: Convert ratios to fractions and make the denominators equal (LCM method) to compare them.
Write the ratios as fractions: \( \frac{5}{8} \) and \( \frac{3}{4} \)
Find the LCM of the denominators (8 and 4). LCM = 8. [1 Mark]
Convert the fractions:
\( \frac{5}{8} \) remains \( \frac{5}{8} \)
\( \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3 \times 2}{4 \times 2} = \frac{6}{8} \) [1 Mark]
Now compare the numerators: Since \( 6 > 5 \), \( \frac{6}{8} > \frac{5}{8} \).
Ans: The ratio \( 3 : 4 \) is greater. [1 Mark]
Q8. Two numbers are in the ratio \( 4 : 7 \). If their sum is \( 132 \), find the two numbers.
[3 Marks]
Let the common ratio multiplier be \( x \).
So, the two numbers are \( 4x \) and \( 7x \).
According to the question, their sum is 132:
\[ 4x + 7x = 132 \]
\[ 11x = 132 \] [1 Mark]
\[ x = \frac{132}{11} = 12 \] [1 Mark]
First number = \( 4x = 4 \times 12 = 48 \)
Second number = \( 7x = 7 \times 12 = 84 \)
Ans: The two numbers are 48 and 84. [1 Mark]
Q9. A worker earns \( \text{Rs. } 1800 \) in \( 6 \) days. (a) How much will he earn in \( 25 \) days? (b) If he earned \( \text{Rs. } 4200 \), for how many days did he work?
[3 Marks]
First, find the earnings for 1 day (Unitary Method).
Earnings in 1 day = \( \frac{1800}{6} = \text{Rs. } 300 \) [1 Mark]

Part (a):
Earnings in 25 days = \( 300 \times 25 = \text{Rs. } 7500 \) [1 Mark]

Part (b):
To find the number of days worked for Rs. 4200:
Number of days = \( \frac{\text{Total Earnings}}{\text{Earnings per day}} = \frac{4200}{300} \)
\[ = 14 \text{ days} \]
Ans: (a) He will earn Rs. 7500. (b) He worked for 14 days. [1 Mark]
Q10. A car travels \( 165 \text{ km} \) in \( 3 \text{ hours} \) at a uniform speed. How much time will it take to travel \( 275 \text{ km} \)?
[3 Marks]
First, find the distance traveled in 1 hour (Speed).
Distance in 1 hour = \( \frac{165}{3} = 55 \text{ km} \) [1 Mark]
Now, find the time taken to travel 275 km.
Time = \( \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Distance in 1 hour}} \)
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{275}{55} \] [1 Mark]
\[ \text{Time} = 5 \text{ hours} \]
Ans: It will take 5 hours. [1 Mark]
SECTION C
Q11. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 4 \). (a) Find the measure of each angle. (b) If the smallest angle is halved and the largest angle is doubled, what will be the new ratio of the three angles?
[5 Marks]
Concept: Sum of angles in a triangle is always \( 180^\circ \). Link geometry with ratio concepts.
Part (a): Finding initial angles
Let the angles be \( 2x \), \( 3x \), and \( 4x \).
Sum of angles = \( 180^\circ \)
\[ 2x + 3x + 4x = 180^\circ \]
\[ 9x = 180^\circ \implies x = 20^\circ \] [1.5 Marks]
Angles are: \( 2(20^\circ) = 40^\circ \), \( 3(20^\circ) = 60^\circ \), \( 4(20^\circ) = 80^\circ \) [1.5 Marks]

Part (b): Finding new ratio
Smallest angle (\( 40^\circ \)) is halved = \( \frac{40}{2} = 20^\circ \)
Middle angle remains the same = \( 60^\circ \)
Largest angle (\( 80^\circ \)) is doubled = \( 80 \times 2 = 160^\circ \) [1 Mark]
New Ratio = \( 20 : 60 : 160 \)
Dividing all terms by 20:
\[ = 1 : 3 : 8 \] [1 Mark]
Ans: (a) Angles are \( 40^\circ, 60^\circ, 80^\circ \). (b) New ratio is \( 1 : 3 : 8 \).
Q12. A printing machine prints \( 1500 \text{ pages} \) in \( 45 \text{ minutes} \). (a) How many pages will it print in \( 2 \text{ hours} \)? (b) How much time (in hours and minutes) will it take to print \( 6000 \text{ pages} \)?
[5 Marks]
Concept: Convert hours to minutes before applying the Unitary Method.
First, find pages printed in 1 minute.
Pages in 1 minute = \( \frac{1500}{45} = \frac{100}{3} \text{ pages} \) [1 Mark]

Part (a): Pages in 2 hours
Convert 2 hours to minutes: \( 2 \times 60 = 120 \text{ minutes} \) [1 Mark]
Pages printed in 120 minutes = \( \left( \frac{100}{3} \right) \times 120 \)
\[ = 100 \times 40 = 4000 \text{ pages} \] [1 Mark]

Part (b): Time for 6000 pages
Time taken = \( \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Pages per minute}} \)
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{6000}{\frac{100}{3}} = 6000 \times \frac{3}{100} \]
\[ = 60 \times 3 = 180 \text{ minutes} \] [1 Mark]
Convert 180 minutes to hours: \( \frac{180}{60} = 3 \text{ hours} \).
Ans: (a) 4000 pages. (b) 3 hours 0 minutes. [1 Mark]
Q13. The ratio of the monthly income to the savings of a family is \( 11 : 2 \). If their monthly savings are \( \text{Rs. } 2500 \), find: (a) Total monthly income. (b) Monthly expenditure. (c) Ratio of expenditure to income.
[5 Marks]
Concept: Income = Expenditure + Savings. Link the given absolute value (savings) to its corresponding ratio part.
Let the monthly income be \( 11x \) and savings be \( 2x \).
Given that savings = \( \text{Rs. } 2500 \)
\[ 2x = 2500 \implies x = \frac{2500}{2} = 1250 \] [1.5 Marks]

Part (a): Monthly Income
Income = \( 11x = 11 \times 1250 = \text{Rs. } 13,750 \) [1 Mark]

Part (b): Monthly Expenditure
Expenditure = Income - Savings
\[ = 13750 - 2500 = \text{Rs. } 11,250 \] [1.5 Marks]

Part (c): Ratio of Expenditure to Income
Ratio = \( \frac{11250}{13750} \)
Dividing numerator and denominator by 250:
\[ = \frac{45}{55} \]
Dividing by 5:
\[ = \frac{9}{11} \] [1 Mark]
(Alternate quicker method: Expenditure parts = Income parts - Savings parts = 11 - 2 = 9. Ratio = 9:11)
Ans: (a) Rs. 13,750. (b) Rs. 11,250. (c) \( 9 : 11 \).
