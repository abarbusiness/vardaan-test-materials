Minor Test 16
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show relevant working steps.
  • Syllabus: Ratio and Proportion.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the ratio \( 45 \text{ minutes} : 1.5 \text{ hours} \) in its simplest form.
2.
If \( x : y = 3 : 5 \), find the ratio \( (3x + 4y) : (8x - y) \).
3.
Check whether the numbers 15, 20, 30, and 40 are in proportion.
4.
Find the fourth proportional to \( 4, 9, 12 \).
5.
Are the ratios \( 25\text{g} : 30\text{g} \) and \( 40\text{kg} : 48\text{kg} \) in proportion? Justify.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Divide ₹ 3450 among A, B, and C in the ratio 3 : 5 : 7. Find the share of each.
7.
The ratio of copper and zinc in an alloy is 9 : 5. If the weight of copper in the alloy is 48.6 grams, find the weight of zinc in the alloy.
8.
Find the mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18.
9.
If \( A : B = 5 : 8 \) and \( B : C = 16 : 25 \), find \( A : C \).
10.
A car travels 288 km in 4 hours, and a train travels 540 km in 6 hours. Find the ratio of their speeds.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 : 6. Find the measure of each angle of the quadrilateral.
12.
If \( \frac{x}{b+c-a} = \frac{y}{c+a-b} = \frac{z}{a+b-c} \), show that \( (b-c)x + (c-a)y + (a-b)z = 0 \).
13.
A sum of ₹ 2,236 is divided among A, B, and C such that A receives 25% more than C, and C receives 25% less than B. What is A's share in the amount?
