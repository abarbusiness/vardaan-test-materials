MINOR TEST 16: Science & Mathematics
Class: 6 ICSE Subjects: Chemistry & Maths Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Mathematics and Science questions are integrated.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all mathematical steps.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define an element. Give any two examples of solid elements.
2.
Identify the numerical coefficient and the variables in the algebraic term: \( -12x^2yz \).
3.
Write the chemical symbols for the following elements:
(i) Iron
(ii) Sodium
(iii) Carbon
(iv) Potassium
4.
Write the algebraic expression for the following statement:
"Five times a number \( x \) added to 9."
5.
State one main difference between an atom and a molecule.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Classify the following substances into Elements and Compounds:
Water, Oxygen, Carbon dioxide, Gold, Sodium chloride (Common salt), Iron.
7.
Classify the following algebraic expressions as monomial, binomial, or trinomial:
(i) \( 4x + 3y \)
(ii) \( 7x^2yz \)
(iii) \( a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \)
8.
Add the given algebraic expressions:
\( (3a - 2b + c) \) and \( (a + 4b - 3c) \)
9.
What is a compound? Write any two characteristics of a compound.
10.
Evaluate the algebraic expression \( 3x^2 + 4y - 5 \)
when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = 3 \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Write the chemical formulas for the following compounds:
(i) Water   (ii) Carbon dioxide   (iii) Table Salt (Sodium chloride)
(b) Is Air a mixture or a compound? Give one strong reason to support your answer.
12.
Simplify the following expression by grouping and combining the like terms:

\( 6x^2 - 4x + 8 - 3x^2 + 7x - 5 \)
13.
Subtract the expression \( (2p^2 - 5p + 4) \) from \( (6p^2 + 3p - 2) \).
Show your steps clearly.
