SECTION A (10 Marks)
1.
Define an element. Give any two examples of solid elements.
[2 Marks]
Definition: An element is a pure substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances by ordinary chemical or physical methods. It is made up of only one kind of atoms. (1 Mark)
Examples: Iron (Fe), Gold (Au), Carbon (C), Silver (Ag) etc. (Any two correct solid elements). (1 Mark, 0.5 each)
2.
Identify the numerical coefficient and the variables in the algebraic term: \( -12x^2yz \).
[2 Marks]
Numerical Coefficient: \( -12 \) (1 Mark - Deduct 0.5 if negative sign is omitted)
Variables (Literal coefficients): \( x, y, \text{ and } z \) (1 Mark)
3.
Write the chemical symbols for: (i) Iron (ii) Sodium (iii) Carbon (iv) Potassium
[2 Marks]
(i) Iron: Fe (from Latin Ferrum)
(ii) Sodium: Na (from Latin Natrium)
(iii) Carbon: C
(iv) Potassium: K (from Latin Kalium)
(0.5 Marks for each correct symbol. Note: First letter must be capital, second letter small.)
4.
Write the algebraic expression for: "Five times a number \( x \) added to 9."
[2 Marks]
"Five times a number \( x \)" is written as: \( 5x \) (1 Mark)
"added to 9" means we add 9 to it: \( 5x + 9 \) (or \( 9 + 5x \)) (1 Mark)
5.
State one main difference between an atom and a molecule.
[2 Marks]
Atom: It is the smallest particle of an element that may or may not exist independently.
Molecule: It is the smallest particle of an element or compound that is capable of independent existence.
(2 Marks for a clear, contrasting point)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
6.
Classify into Elements and Compounds: Water, Oxygen, Carbon dioxide, Gold, Sodium chloride, Iron.
[3 Marks]
Elements: Oxygen, Gold, Iron. (1.5 Marks - 0.5 each)
Compounds: Water, Carbon dioxide, Sodium chloride. (1.5 Marks - 0.5 each)
7.
Classify as monomial, binomial, or trinomial: (i) \( 4x + 3y \) (ii) \( 7x^2yz \) (iii) \( a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \)
[3 Marks]
(i) \( 4x + 3y \): It has two distinct terms, so it is a Binomial. (1 Mark)
(ii) \( 7x^2yz \): It is a single product term, so it is a Monomial. (1 Mark)
(iii) \( a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \): It has three distinct terms, so it is a Trinomial. (1 Mark)
8.
Add the expressions: \( (3a - 2b + c) \) and \( (a + 4b - 3c) \)
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Write in an addition expression.
\( (3a - 2b + c) + (a + 4b - 3c) \) (1 Mark)
Step 2: Group the like terms together.
\( = (3a + a) + (-2b + 4b) + (c - 3c) \) (1 Mark)
Step 3: Combine coefficients.
\( = 4a + 2b - 2c \) (1 Mark for final correct answer)
9.
What is a compound? Write any two characteristics of a compound.
[3 Marks]
Definition: A compound is a pure substance formed by the chemical combination of two or more elements in a fixed proportion by mass. (1 Mark)
Characteristics:
1. The properties of a compound are entirely different from the properties of its constituent elements.
2. Compounds cannot be separated into their constituents by simple physical methods (requires chemical methods).
3. It has a definite molecular formula and fixed melting/boiling points.
(Any 2 correct characteristics - 1 Mark each)
10.
Evaluate \( 3x^2 + 4y - 5 \) when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = 3 \).
[3 Marks]
Given Expression: \( 3x^2 + 4y - 5 \)
Step 1: Substitute the values \( x = 2, y = 3 \).
\( = 3(2)^2 + 4(3) - 5 \) (1 Mark)
Step 2: Apply BODMAS (calculate squares and multiplication).
\( = 3(4) + 12 - 5 \)
\( = 12 + 12 - 5 \) (1 Mark)
Step 3: Addition and Subtraction.
\( = 24 - 5 = 19 \) (1 Mark)
Final Answer: 19
SECTION C (15 Marks)
11.
(a) Write chemical formulas for (i) Water (ii) Carbon dioxide (iii) Table Salt.
(b) Is Air a mixture or a compound? Give reason.
[5 Marks]
(a) Formulas: (3 Marks - 1 Mark each)
(i) Water: H2O
(ii) Carbon dioxide: CO2
(iii) Table Salt (Sodium chloride): NaCl
(b) Mixture vs Compound:
Air is a mixture. (1 Mark)
Reason: The components of air (Nitrogen, Oxygen, Carbon dioxide, etc.) retain their individual properties and can be separated by physical methods (like fractional distillation). Also, they are not chemically combined in a fixed ratio. (1 Mark for a valid scientific reason)
12.
Simplify by grouping like terms: \( 6x^2 - 4x + 8 - 3x^2 + 7x - 5 \)
[5 Marks]
Expression: \( 6x^2 - 4x + 8 - 3x^2 + 7x - 5 \)
Step 1: Identify and group the like terms together.
\( = (6x^2 - 3x^2) + (-4x + 7x) + (8 - 5) \) (2 Marks)
Step 2: Combine the \( x^2 \) terms.
\( 6x^2 - 3x^2 = 3x^2 \) (1 Mark)
Step 3: Combine the \( x \) terms.
\( -4x + 7x = +3x \) (1 Mark)
Step 4: Combine the constant terms.
\( 8 - 5 = +3 \)
Final Answer:
\( 3x^2 + 3x + 3 \) (1 Mark for final combined expression)
13.
Subtract \( (2p^2 - 5p + 4) \) from \( (6p^2 + 3p - 2) \).
[5 Marks]
Step 1: Set up the subtraction expression correctly.
Remember: "Subtract A from B" means "B - A".
\( (6p^2 + 3p - 2) - (2p^2 - 5p + 4) \) (1.5 Marks for correct setup)
Step 2: Open the brackets. Change the signs of all terms in the second bracket.
\( = 6p^2 + 3p - 2 - 2p^2 + 5p - 4 \) (1.5 Marks for correct sign changes)
Step 3: Group the like terms.
\( = (6p^2 - 2p^2) + (3p + 5p) + (-2 - 4) \) (1 Mark)
Step 4: Combine coefficients to get the final answer.
\( = 4p^2 + 8p - 6 \) (1 Mark for the correct final answer)
*Alternative (Column Method):
\( \ \ \ 6p^2 + 3p - 2 \)
\( \underline{- (2p^2 - 5p + 4)} \)
Change signs: \( (-), (+), (-) \)
Result: \( \mathbf{4p^2 + 8p - 6} \)
