SECTION A (10 Marks)
1.
Define lateral inversion. Give one example of a letter in the English alphabet that does not show lateral inversion.
[2 Marks]
Answer: [1 M]

The phenomenon where the left side of an object appears as the right side of its image in a plane mirror (and vice-versa) is called lateral inversion.

[1 M]

Example: The letter 'A' (or H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y) does not show lateral inversion as it is symmetrical.

2.
Define the terms Atomic Number and Mass Number of an atom.
[2 Marks]
Answer:

[1 M]Atomic Number (Z): The total number of protons present in the nucleus of an atom is called its atomic number.

[1 M]Mass Number (A): The sum of the total number of protons and neutrons present in the nucleus of an atom is called its mass number.

3.
State the two laws of reflection of light.
[2 Marks]
Answer:
  1. [1 M] The angle of incidence is always equal to the angle of reflection. (\( \angle i = \angle r \))
  2. [1 M] The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal at the point of incidence all lie in the same plane.

What is a virtual image? Can it be obtained on a screen?
[2 Marks]
Answer:

[1 M] An image that is formed when reflected light rays appear to meet (but do not actually meet) is called a virtual image.

[1 M] No, a virtual image cannot be obtained on a screen.

7.
An object is placed 15 cm in front of a plane mirror.
(a) What is the distance between the object and its image?
(b) State any two characteristics of the image formed by a plane mirror.
[3 Marks]
Answer:

[1.5 M] (a) In a plane mirror, object distance = image distance.
Image is 15 cm behind the mirror.
Total distance = Object distance + Image distance = 15 cm + 15 cm = 30 cm.

[0.75 M each = 1.5 M] (b) Characteristics:
1. The image is virtual and erect.
2. It is of the same size as the object. (Or: It is laterally inverted).

[3 Marks]

[1 M] (b) The element with atomic number 11 is Sodium (Na).

[1 M] (c) The valency of Sodium is 1.

10.
Write the chemical formula of the following compounds using the criss-cross method (show the steps):
(a) Magnesium chloride (b) Aluminium oxide (c) Calcium hydroxide
[3 Marks]
Answer: [1 M each]

(a) Magnesium chloride: Symbols: Mg, Cl. Valencies: Mg (2), Cl (1).
Criss-cross: Mg\(_1\)Cl\(_2\) → MgCl\(_2\)

(b) Aluminium oxide: Symbols: Al, O. Valencies: Al (3), O (2).
Criss-cross: Al\(_2\)O\(_3\) → Al\(_2\)O\(_3\)

(c) Calcium hydroxide: Symbols: Ca, OH. Valencies: Ca (2), OH (1).
Criss-cross: Ca\(_1\)(OH)\(_2\) → Ca(OH)\(_2\)
