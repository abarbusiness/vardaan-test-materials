Minor Test 16
Class: 7 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Identify and specify direct or inverse variation in your working where applicable.
  • Show all working clearly. Direct answers will not be awarded full marks.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
If the cost of 15 identical articles is ₹ 450, find the cost of 24 such articles.
2.
If 12 men can complete a piece of work in 8 days, in how many days will 6 men complete the same work?
3.
A car takes 4 hours to reach a destination travelling at a speed of 60 km/hr. How long will it take to reach the same destination if it travels at 80 km/hr?
4.
A printing machine prints 1500 pages in 5 minutes. How many pages will it print in 12 minutes?
5.
A military garrison of 120 men has provisions for 30 days. How long will the provisions last if the garrison size increases to 150 men?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A can do a piece of work in 10 days and B can do the same work in 15 days. How long will they take to finish the work if they work together?
7.
If 8 women can weave 120 meters of cloth in a day, how many meters of cloth can 12 women weave in a day working at the same rate?
8.
A scout camp has enough food for 200 students for 20 days. After 5 days, 50 students leave the camp. How long will the remaining food last for the remaining students?
9.
Tap A can fill an empty tank in 4 hours, while Tap B can empty the full tank in 6 hours. If both taps are opened simultaneously, in how many hours will the empty tank be filled?
10.
A wheel makes 360 revolutions in 1 minute. How many revolutions does it make in 45 seconds?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A and B can do a piece of work in 12 days and 18 days respectively. They begin working together, but A leaves after 4 days.
(a) Find the fraction of work completed in the first 4 days.
(b) How many days will B take to finish the remaining work alone?
12.
Working 8 hours a day, 45 men can build a wall in 16 days.
(a) Express the total work required in terms of "man-hours".
(b) How many men are required to build the same wall in 20 days if they work 6 hours a day?
13.
A train travelling at a uniform speed of 72 km/hr takes 15 seconds to cross a stationary telegraph pole.
(i) Calculate the speed of the train in m/s.
(ii) Find the length of the train.
(iii) How much time will the same train take to cross a platform which is 200 meters long?
