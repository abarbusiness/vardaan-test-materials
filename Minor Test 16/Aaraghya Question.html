Minor Test 16
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Science (Physics & Chemistry) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define lateral inversion. Give one example of a letter in the English alphabet that does not show lateral inversion.
2.
Define the terms Atomic Number and Mass Number of an atom.
3.
State the two laws of reflection of light.
4.
Write the chemical symbols for the following elements:
(a) Potassium
(b) Gold
(c) Iron
(d) Lead
5.
What is a virtual image? Can it be obtained on a screen?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Differentiate between an atom and a molecule. (Give any three points of difference).
7.
An object is placed 15 cm in front of a plane mirror.
(a) What is the distance between the object and its image?
(b) State any two characteristics of the image formed by a plane mirror.
8.
An element 'X' has 11 protons and 12 neutrons in its nucleus.
(a) Calculate its atomic number and mass number.
(b) Identify and name the element 'X'.
(c) What is the valency of this element?
9.
(a) Name the three primary colours of light. (1.5 Marks)
(b) What is the speed of light in a vacuum? (1.5 Marks)
10.
Write the chemical formula of the following compounds using the criss-cross method (show the steps):
(a) Magnesium chloride
(b) Aluminium oxide
(c) Calcium hydroxide
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) State any three main postulates of Dalton's Atomic Theory. (3 Marks)
(b) Define the term atomicity. State the atomicity of Oxygen gas. (2 Marks)
12.
(a) Differentiate between a real image and a virtual image (any two points). (2 Marks)
(b) How is a shadow formed? State any two characteristics of a shadow formed by an opaque object. (3 Marks)
13.
(a) Classify the following changes as physical or chemical changes:
(i) Rusting of iron
(ii) Melting of wax
(iii) Burning of paper (3 Marks)
(b) State any two differences between metals and non-metals based on their physical properties. (2 Marks)
