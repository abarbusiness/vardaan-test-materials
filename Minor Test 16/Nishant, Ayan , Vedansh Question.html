Minor Test 16
Class: 6 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working clearly. Direct answers will not be awarded full marks.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
If the cost of 8 identical pens is ₹ 120, find the cost of 1 pen.
2.
A train covers a distance of 180 km in 3 hours at a uniform speed. How much distance will it cover in 1 hour?
3.
The weight of 5 identical mathematics books is 2.5 kg. What is the total weight of 12 such books?
4.
If a dozen bananas cost ₹ 60, find the cost of 7 bananas.
5.
A daily wage worker earns ₹ 1500 in 5 days. How much money will he earn if he works for 8 days?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
If 15 bags of wheat weigh 450 kg, how many such bags will be required to get 1200 kg of wheat?
7.
A car travels 228 km on 12 liters of petrol. How many kilometers will it travel on 20 liters of petrol?
8.
The rent of a house for 4 months is ₹ 18,000. Find the rent of the same house for a complete year.
9.
If 24 meters of cloth costs ₹ 3600, how many meters of the same cloth can be purchased for ₹ 1350?
10.
A family of 4 members consumes 20 kg of rice in a month. How much rice will be consumed by a family of 7 members in a month, assuming the rate of consumption per person is the same?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In an apartment building, the maintenance charge for an apartment with an area of 800 sq. feet is ₹ 3200 per month.
(a) What is the maintenance charge per square foot?
(b) What will be the maintenance charge for an apartment with an area of 1250 sq. feet?
(c) If a resident paid ₹ 5600 as maintenance charge, what is the area of their apartment?
12.
A factory produces 25,200 pens in a standard 30-day month. Assuming the factory produces an equal number of pens every day:
(a) Find the number of pens produced in a single day.
(b) How many pens will be produced in a leap year if the factory operates for 300 days only?
(c) How many days will it take to produce 63,000 pens?
13.
An airplane flies 3150 km in 4.5 hours flying at a constant speed.
(i) Calculate the speed of the airplane in km/hr.
(ii) How much distance will it cover if it flies for 7 hours?
(iii) How much time will it take to cover a distance of 4900 km?
