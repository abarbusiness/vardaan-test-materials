Minor Test 16
Class: 12 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Use standard mathematical notations.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find \( \frac{dy}{dx} \) if \( y = x^{\sin x} \), where \( x > 0 \).
2.
Find the Cartesian equation of the line passing through the point \( (1, 2, -4) \) and parallel to the given line \( \frac{x-3}{2} = \frac{y+1}{7} = \frac{z-2}{-3} \).
3.
If \( x = a(\theta - \sin \theta) \) and \( y = a(1 + \cos \theta) \), find \( \frac{dy}{dx} \) in its simplest form.
4.
Find the direction cosines of the straight line whose vector equation is \( \vec{r} = (2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 3\hat{k}) + \lambda(3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 6\hat{k}) \).
5.
Differentiate \( \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) \) with respect to \( x \), assuming \( -1 < x < 1 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find the shortest distance between the following pair of skew lines: \[ \vec{r} = (\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}) + \lambda(\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) \] \[ \vec{r} = (4\hat{i} + 5\hat{j} + 6\hat{k}) + \mu(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) \]
7.
If \( x^y + y^x = a^b \) (where \( a, b \) are constants), find \( \frac{dy}{dx} \).
8.
Find the coordinates of the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point \( P(2, -1, 5) \) to the line \( \frac{x-11}{10} = \frac{y+2}{-4} = \frac{z+8}{-11} \).
9.
If \( y = (\tan^{-1} x)^2 \), show that \( (x^2+1)^2 \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} + 2x(x^2+1) \frac{dy}{dx} = 2 \).
10.
If \( x = a \cos t + a t \sin t \) and \( y = a \sin t - a t \cos t \), evaluate \( \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \) at \( t = \frac{\pi}{4} \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
If \( y = e^{a \cos^{-1} x} \) where \( -1 \le x \le 1 \), prove that: \[ (1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} - x\frac{dy}{dx} - a^2y = 0 \]
12.
Find the image of the point \( (1, 6, 3) \) in the line \( \frac{x}{1} = \frac{y-1}{2} = \frac{z-2}{3} \). Also, write the equation of the line joining the given point and its image, and find the perpendicular distance from the point to the given line.
13.
Show that the lines \( \frac{x+1}{3} = \frac{y+3}{5} = \frac{z+5}{7} \) and \( \frac{x-2}{1} = \frac{y-4}{3} = \frac{z-6}{5} \) intersect each other. Find their point of intersection.
