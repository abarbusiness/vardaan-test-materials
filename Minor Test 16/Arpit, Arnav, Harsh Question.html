Minor Test 16
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A consists of 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B consists of 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C consists of 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
What is friction? State the direction in which the force of friction acts.
2.
Name two liquids that are good conductors of electricity and two liquids that are poor conductors.
3.
Why are the soles of our shoes grooved? Give a specific reason.
4.
Write the full form of LED. Why is an LED preferred over a normal bulb while testing the electrical conductivity of weak liquids?
5.
Define fluid friction. What is the special name given to the frictional force exerted by fluids?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Explain why sportsmen and athletes use shoes with spikes.
7.
Briefly explain the process of electroplating with the help of a basic example. State one of its main uses.
8.
"Friction is a necessary evil." Justify this statement by giving two examples where friction is useful and one example where it is harmful.
9.
Does pure water (distilled water) conduct electricity? If not, what can we do to make it a good conductor? Explain briefly.
10.
Differentiate between static friction and sliding friction. Which of the two is greater and why?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) What happens when an electric current is passed through water? Write down your observations. (3 Marks)
(b) Why do we generally add a few drops of lemon juice or dilute sulphuric acid to water before conducting electrolysis? (2 Marks)
12.
(a) Explain any three methods used to reduce friction in machines and daily life. (3 Marks)
(b) Why is it always easier to roll a heavy gas cylinder on the ground than to slide it? Name the specific type of friction involved in this case. (2 Marks)
13.
(a) Why does an electrician always use rubber gloves and footwear while repairing an electric switch? (2 Marks)
(b) Describe a simple activity (with a basic circuit consisting of a battery and an LED) to demonstrate that tap water conducts electricity while distilled water does not. (3 Marks)
