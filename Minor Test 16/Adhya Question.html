Minor Test 16
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working where necessary.
  • Topics Covered: Ratio and Proportion, Idea of Speed, Distance and Time.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the following ratio in its simplest form: 15 : 25
2.
A boy runs a distance of 100 meters in 20 seconds. Find his speed.
3.
Find the ratio of 40 cm to 1 meter.
4.
Convert a speed of 18 km/hr into m/s.
5.
If x : 3 = 10 : 15, find the value of x.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Divide ₹600 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio 2 : 3.
7.
A car is moving with a constant speed of 40 km/hr. How much distance will it cover in 3 hours?
8.
Are the numbers 2, 4, 6, and 12 in proportion? Show your working clearly.
9.
If the cost of 4 pens is ₹48, find the cost of 7 such pens.
10.
Convert a speed of 15 m/s into km/hr.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In a school, there are 400 boys and 300 girls. Find the ratio of:
(i) The number of boys to the number of girls. (2 marks)
(ii) The number of girls to the total number of students. (3 marks)
12.
A bus travels a distance of 180 km in 4 hours.
(i) Calculate the speed of the bus in km/hr. (2 marks)
(ii) At this speed, how much time will it take to travel 270 km? (3 marks)
13.
Answer the following questions:
(i) Find the value of y if 5, y, 15, and 27 are in proportion. (2.5 marks)
(ii) A man walks at a speed of 5 m/s. How far will he walk in 2 minutes? (Give your answer in meters). (2.5 marks)
