SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Identify the number of terms and the coefficient of \( x^2 \) in the expression: \( 7x^2y - 4xy^2 + \frac{5}{2}z^2 - x^2 \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Number of terms in the given expression = 4 (1 Mark)
The term containing \( x^2 \) alone is \( -x^2 \).
Therefore, the coefficient of \( x^2 \) is \( -1 \). (1 Mark)
Q2. Find the product of the following monomials: \( (-4p^3) \) and \( (7pq) \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Product = \( (-4p^3) \times (7pq) \)
= \( (-4 \times 7) \times (p^3 \times p) \times (q) \) (1 Mark for grouping)
= \( -28p^4q \) (1 Mark for correct final answer)
Q3. Using a suitable standard identity, evaluate: \( (99)^2 \).
[2 M]
Solution:
We can write \( 99 \) as \( (100 - 1) \).
Using Identity: \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \), where \( a = 100 \), \( b = 1 \). (0.5 Marks)
\( (100 - 1)^2 = (100)^2 - 2(100)(1) + (1)^2 \) (0.5 Marks)
\( = 10000 - 200 + 1 \)
\( = 9801 \) (1 Mark)
Q4. State the four standard algebraic identities.
[2 M]
Solution: (0.5 Marks for each correct identity)
I. \( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \)
II. \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \)
III. \( (a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2 \)
IV. \( (x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab \)
Q5. Simplify the expression: \( 3x(4x - 5) + 3 \) and find its value for \( x = 3 \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Simplifying: \( 3x(4x - 5) + 3 = 12x^2 - 15x + 3 \) (1 Mark)
Putting \( x = 3 \):
Value = \( 12(3)^2 - 15(3) + 3 \)
= \( 12(9) - 45 + 3 = 108 - 45 + 3 \)
= \( 66 \) (1 Mark)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Add the following algebraic expressions:
\( 7xy + 5yz - 3zx \), \( 4yz + 9zx - 4y \), and \( -3xz + 5x - 2xy \).
[3 M]
Solution:
Rearranging and writing like terms together: (1 Mark for grouping correctly)
\( = (7xy - 2xy) + (5yz + 4yz) + (-3zx + 9zx - 3xz) - 4y + 5x \)
Note: \( zx \) and \( xz \) are like terms.
\( = 5xy + 9yz + (-3zx + 9zx - 3zx) - 4y + 5x \) (1 Mark for partial addition)
\( = 5xy + 9yz + 3zx - 4y + 5x \) (1 Mark for final answer)
Q7. Subtract \( 3pq(p - q) \) from \( 2pq(p + q) \). Show all steps clearly.
[3 M]
Solution:
First, expand both expressions:
Expression 1 (to subtract): \( 3pq(p - q) = 3p^2q - 3pq^2 \) (1 Mark)
Expression 2 (from which to subtract): \( 2pq(p + q) = 2p^2q + 2pq^2 \) (0.5 Marks)
Now, subtract:
\( (2p^2q + 2pq^2) - (3p^2q - 3pq^2) \)
\( = 2p^2q + 2pq^2 - 3p^2q + 3pq^2 \) (Changing signs inside the bracket) (0.5 Marks)
Grouping like terms: \( (2p^2q - 3p^2q) + (2pq^2 + 3pq^2) \)
\( = -p^2q + 5pq^2 \) (1 Mark for correct final answer)
Q8. Using the identity \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \), evaluate:
(i) \( (4p - 3q)^2 \) (ii) \( (4.9)^2 \)
[3 M]
Solution:
(i) Here, \( a = 4p, b = 3q \)
\( (4p - 3q)^2 = (4p)^2 - 2(4p)(3q) + (3q)^2 \) (0.5 Marks)
\( = 16p^2 - 24pq + 9q^2 \) (1 Mark)

(ii) \( 4.9 = 5.0 - 0.1 \)
So, \( (5.0 - 0.1)^2 = (5.0)^2 - 2(5.0)(0.1) + (0.1)^2 \) (0.5 Marks)
\( = 25.0 - 1.0 + 0.01 \)
\( = 24.01 \) (1 Mark)
Q9. Find the area of a rectangle whose length is \( (4a^2 - 3ab) \) and breadth is \( (2ab) \). Also, evaluate the area if \( a = 1 \) and \( b = 2 \).
[3 M]
Solution:
Area of Rectangle = \( \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth} \)
Area = \( (4a^2 - 3ab) \times (2ab) \)
= \( (4a^2 \times 2ab) - (3ab \times 2ab) \) (1 Mark for expansion)
= \( 8a^3b - 6a^2b^2 \) (1 Mark for correct expression)

Evaluating at \( a = 1, b = 2 \):
Area = \( 8(1)^3(2) - 6(1)^2(2)^2 \)
= \( 8(1)(2) - 6(1)(4) \)
= \( 16 - 24 = -8 \)
(Note: Mathematically correct calculation. Although area cannot be negative in physical reality, award full marks for correct algebraic evaluation.) (1 Mark)
Q10. Using the identity \( (x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab \), find the product of \( 501 \times 502 \).
[3 M]
Solution:
We can write \( 501 \times 502 \) as \( (500 + 1) \times (500 + 2) \). (1 Mark)
Here, \( x = 500, a = 1, b = 2 \).
Using the identity:
\( = (500)^2 + (1 + 2) \times 500 + (1 \times 2) \) (1 Mark)
\( = 250000 + (3) \times 500 + 2 \)
\( = 250000 + 1500 + 2 \)
\( = 251502 \) (1 Mark)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. (a) Multiply the binomials: \( (2x + 5) \) and \( (4x - 3) \).
(b) Simplify: \( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \)
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) \( (2x + 5)(4x - 3) \)
\( = 2x(4x - 3) + 5(4x - 3) \) (1 Mark)
\( = 8x^2 - 6x + 20x - 15 \)
\( = 8x^2 + 14x - 15 \) (1 Mark)

(b) Expression: \( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \)
Expand the first part:
\( = a(2a - 3b + c) + b(2a - 3b + c) \)
\( = 2a^2 - 3ab + ac + 2ab - 3b^2 + bc \) (1 Mark)
Expand the second part:
\( - (2ac - 3bc) = -2ac + 3bc \) (1 Mark)
Combine all terms:
\( 2a^2 - 3ab + ac + 2ab - 3b^2 + bc - 2ac + 3bc \)
Grouping like terms:
\( = 2a^2 - 3b^2 + (-3ab + 2ab) + (ac - 2ac) + (bc + 3bc) \)
\( = 2a^2 - 3b^2 - ab - ac + 4bc \) (1 Mark for correct final simplified form)
Q12. (a) Evaluate using identity: \( (983)^2 - (17)^2 \).
(b) Show that \( (9p - 5q)^2 + 180pq = (9p + 5q)^2 \).
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Using Identity: \( a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b) \)
Here \( a = 983, b = 17 \).
\( (983)^2 - (17)^2 = (983 - 17)(983 + 17) \) (1 Mark)
\( = (966) \times (1000) \)
\( = 966000 \) (1 Mark)

(b) L.H.S = \( (9p - 5q)^2 + 180pq \)
Expand using \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \):
\( = (9p)^2 - 2(9p)(5q) + (5q)^2 + 180pq \) (1 Mark)
\( = 81p^2 - 90pq + 25q^2 + 180pq \)
\( = 81p^2 + 90pq + 25q^2 \) (Since \( -90pq + 180pq = 90pq \)) (1 Mark)

R.H.S = \( (9p + 5q)^2 \)
Expand using \( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \):
\( = (9p)^2 + 2(9p)(5q) + (5q)^2 \)
\( = 81p^2 + 90pq + 25q^2 \)
Since L.H.S = R.H.S, Hence Proved. (1 Mark)
Q13. (a) Find the volume of cuboidal box with dimensions \( 5a, 3a^2, 7a^4 \).
(b) Simplify: \( (x^2 - 5)(x + 5) + 25 \).
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Volume of Cuboid = Length \( \times \) Breadth \( \times \) Height
Volume = \( 5a \times 3a^2 \times 7a^4 \) (1 Mark)
Multiply the numerical coefficients: \( 5 \times 3 \times 7 = 105 \)
Multiply the variables using law of exponents \( (x^m \times x^n = x^{m+n}) \):
\( a^1 \times a^2 \times a^4 = a^{(1+2+4)} = a^7 \)
Total Volume = \( 105a^7 \) cubic units. (1 Mark)

(b) Expression: \( (x^2 - 5)(x + 5) + 25 \)
Multiply the binomials (Note: this is not \( (a-b)(a+b) \) because terms are \( x^2 \) and \( x \)):
\( = x^2(x + 5) - 5(x + 5) + 25 \) (1 Mark)
\( = (x^3 + 5x^2) - (5x + 25) + 25 \) (1 Mark)
\( = x^3 + 5x^2 - 5x - 25 + 25 \)
\( = x^3 + 5x^2 - 5x \) (1 Mark for correct simplified expression)
