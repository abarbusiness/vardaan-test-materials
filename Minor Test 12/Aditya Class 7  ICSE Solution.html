Minor Test 12: Geometry Basics & Linear Equations
Class: 7 ICSE | Subject: Mathematics | Max Marks: 40
SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Define a 'Line Segment'. How many end points does it have?
[2 M]
Solution:
A line segment is a part or a portion of a line that has fixed boundaries. (1 Mark)
It has exactly two end points. (1 Mark)
Q2. Solve the given simple linear equation: \( y + 8 = 20 \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Given equation: \( y + 8 = 20 \)
Transposing \( +8 \) to the Right Hand Side (RHS): (1 Mark)
\( y = 20 - 8 \)
\( y = 12 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: \( y = 12 \)
Q3. Define a 'Point'. How is it represented geometrically?
[2 M]
Solution:
A point is an exact location or position in space. It has no length, breadth, or thickness. (1 Mark)
It is represented geometrically by a fine dot made by a sharp pencil and named using a capital letter (e.g., A, B, P). (1 Mark)
Q4. Frame a linear equation: "Three times a number \( x \) gives \( 24 \)."
[2 M]
Solution:
"Three times a number \( x \)" is written as \( 3x \). (1 Mark)
"Gives 24" means it is equal to 24.
Ans: \( 3x = 24 \) (1 Mark)
Q5. Define a 'Ray'. Does it have a definite length?
[2 M]
Solution:
A ray is a part of a line which has a fixed starting point (initial point) and extends endlessly in one direction. (1 Mark)
No, a ray does not have a definite length because it extends indefinitely in one direction. (1 Mark)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. What is meant by 'Collinear Points'? Draw a rough figure.
[3 M]
Solution:
Three or more points in a plane are said to be collinear if they all lie on the same straight line. (2 Marks)
Figure Requirement: A straight line drawn with three distinct points (like A, B, and C) marked upon it. (1 Mark)
Q7. Solve the linear equation for \( m \): \( 4m - 7 = 17 \).
[3 M]
Solution:
\( 4m - 7 = 17 \)
Transposing \( -7 \) to the RHS:
\( 4m = 17 + 7 \) (1 Mark)
\( 4m = 24 \) (1 Mark)
\( m = \frac{24}{4} \)
\( m = 6 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: \( m = 6 \)
Q8. Define 'Concurrent Lines'. How many lines can pass through a single point?
[3 M]
Solution:
Three or more straight lines in a plane are called concurrent lines if they all pass through the same common point. (2 Marks)
An infinite (countless) number of lines can pass through a single point. (1 Mark)
Q9. Solve the following linear equation: \( 2(x + 3) = 14 \).
[3 M]
Solution:
Method: Expanding the bracket
\( 2x + 6 = 14 \) (1 Mark)
\( 2x = 14 - 6 \)
\( 2x = 8 \) (1 Mark)
\( x = \frac{8}{2} = 4 \) (1 Mark)

(Alternative: Divide both sides by 2 first: \( x + 3 = 7 \), then \( x = 7 - 3 = 4 \))
Ans: \( x = 4 \)
Q10. Define 'Parallel Lines'. Give two real-life examples.
[3 M]
Solution:
Lines in the same plane that never meet or intersect each other, no matter how far they are extended in either direction, are called parallel lines. The distance between them remains constant. (1.5 Marks)
Examples: Railway tracks, the opposite edges of a book/ruler, steps of a ladder. (Any two valid examples) (1.5 Marks)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. Define the following basic geometrical terms clearly: Plane, Intersecting Lines, Straight Line.
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Plane: A plane is a flat surface that extends infinitely in all directions. It has length and breadth but no thickness. (1 Mark)

(b) Intersecting Lines: Two distinct straight lines that meet or cross each other at exactly one common point are called intersecting lines. The common point is called the point of intersection. (2 Marks)

(c) Straight Line: A straight line is a collection of points forming a straight path that extends infinitely in both directions. It has no end points and no definite length. (2 Marks)
Q12. Solve and verify: \( \frac{2x}{3} + 5 = 11 \).
[5 M]
Solution:
Solving the equation:
\( \frac{2x}{3} + 5 = 11 \)
Transposing \( +5 \) to the RHS:
\( \frac{2x}{3} = 11 - 5 \) (1 Mark)
\( \frac{2x}{3} = 6 \)
Multiply both sides by 3:
\( 2x = 6 \times 3 = 18 \) (1 Mark)
Divide by 2:
\( x = \frac{18}{2} = 9 \) (1 Mark)

Verification:
Substitute \( x = 9 \) in the Left Hand Side (LHS):
LHS = \( \frac{2(9)}{3} + 5 \) (1 Mark)
LHS = \( \frac{18}{3} + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11 \)
LHS = RHS. Hence verified. (1 Mark)
Ans: \( x = 9 \)
Q13. Rahul is 3 years older than his sister. The sum of their present ages is 17 years. Find their present ages.
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Let the sister's present age be \( x \) years.
Then, Rahul's present age = \( (x + 3) \) years. (1 Mark)

(b) The sum of their present ages is 17 years.
Linear equation: \( x + (x + 3) = 17 \) (2 Marks)

(c) Solving the equation:
\( 2x + 3 = 17 \)
Transposing 3 to the RHS:
\( 2x = 17 - 3 \)
\( 2x = 14 \) (1 Mark)
\( x = \frac{14}{2} = 7 \)
Sister's present age = \( 7 \) years.
Rahul's present age = \( 7 + 3 = 10 \) years. (1 Mark)
Ans: Sister is 7 years old, Rahul is 10 years old.
