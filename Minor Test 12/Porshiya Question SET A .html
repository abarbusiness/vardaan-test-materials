Minor Test 12: Profit and Loss
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all essential steps and calculations clearly in Section B and C.
  • Use of calculator is not permitted. Write your answers neatly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the profit or loss if the Cost Price (CP) of an article is \( \text{₹ } 500 \) and the Selling Price (SP) is \( \text{₹ } 620 \).
2.
A toy was bought for \( \text{₹ } 250 \) and sold at a loss of \( \text{₹ } 30 \). Find the selling price of the toy.
3.
Calculate the loss percent if a shopkeeper buys a book for \( \text{₹ } 400 \) and sells it for \( \text{₹ } 360 \).
4.
Ravi bought a pen for \( \text{₹ } 80 \) and sold it for \( \text{₹ } 100 \). Find his profit percent.
5.
The cost price of a table is \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) and the shopkeeper wants to make a profit of \( \text{₹ } 150 \). What should be the selling price?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A fruit seller bought a box of apples for \( \text{₹ } 1200 \). He spent \( \text{₹ } 100 \) on transportation. If he sells the apples for \( \text{₹ } 1560 \), find his total profit percent.
7.
By selling a digital watch for \( \text{₹ } 990 \), a shopkeeper gains \( 10\% \). Find the cost price of the watch.
8.
A boy bought a bicycle for \( \text{₹ } 1500 \) and sold it to his friend at a loss of \( 20\% \). Find the selling price of the bicycle.
9.
If the cost price of \( 10 \) pens is equal to the selling price of \( 8 \) pens, find the gain percent for the shopkeeper.
10.
A dealer bought a chair for \( \text{₹ } 600 \). At what price should he sell it to gain \( 15\% \)?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
An article was sold for \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) at a loss of \( 20\% \).

(a) Find the cost price of the article. (3 Marks)
(b) At what price should it be sold to gain \( 10\% \)? (2 Marks)
12.
A merchant bought \( 240 \) eggs at \( \text{₹ } 15 \) per dozen. Out of these, \( 20 \) eggs were found broken and had to be thrown away. He sold the remaining eggs at \( \text{₹ } 1.50 \) each.

(a) Find his total cost price. (1 Mark)
(b) Find his total selling price. (2 Marks)
(c) Calculate his overall profit or loss percent. (2 Marks)
13.
A shopkeeper buys two table fans for \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) each. He sells one fan at a profit of \( 10\% \) and the other fan at a loss of \( 5\% \).

(a) Find the selling price of the first fan. (1.5 Marks)
(b) Find the selling price of the second fan. (1.5 Marks)
(c) Find the overall profit or loss percent on the whole transaction. (2 Marks)
