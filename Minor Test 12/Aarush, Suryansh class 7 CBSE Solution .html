SECTION A (10 Marks)
Question 1 [2 Marks]
Evaluate the following: \( (-31) \div [(-30) + (-1)] \)
Solution:
According to BODMAS, we solve the bracket first.
Bracket: \( (-30) + (-1) = -30 - 1 = -31 \)
Now evaluate the division: \( (-31) \div (-31) \)
\( = \frac{-31}{-31} = 1 \)
Answer: \( 1 \)
Question 2 [2 Marks]
Identify the coefficient of \( x \) in the term \( -5xy^2 \), and write the constant term in the expression \( 3x^2 - 5x + 8 \).
Solution:
Coefficient of \( x \): In the term \( -5xy^2 \), leaving aside \( x \), the remaining factors are \( -5y^2 \).
So, coefficient of \( x = -5y^2 \)
Constant Term: In \( 3x^2 - 5x + 8 \), the term without any variable is \( +8 \).
So, constant term = \( 8 \)
Answer: Coefficient of \( x = -5y^2 \), Constant term = \( 8 \)
Question 3 [2 Marks]
A submarine is situated at \( 800 \text{ m} \) below sea level. If it ascends \( 250 \text{ m} \), what is its new position? Express the position as an integer.
Solution:
Initial position below sea level is represented as a negative integer: \( -800 \text{ m} \)
Ascending means moving upwards, so we add: \( +250 \text{ m} \)
New position = \( -800 + 250 \)
\( = -550 \text{ m} \)
This means it is \( 550 \text{ m} \) below sea level.
Answer: \( -550 \text{ m} \)
Question 4 [2 Marks]
Subtract \( 2a - 3b + 4c \) from \( 5a - b - 2c \).
Solution:
Write the expression as:
\( (5a - b - 2c) - (2a - 3b + 4c) \)
Open the brackets (signs of the second expression will change):
\( = 5a - b - 2c - 2a + 3b - 4c \)
Group like terms together:
\( = (5a - 2a) + (-b + 3b) + (-2c - 4c) \)
\( = 3a + 2b - 6c \)
Answer: \( 3a + 2b - 6c \)
Question 5 [2 Marks]
Find the product using suitable properties of integers: \( 26 \times (-48) + (-48) \times (-36) \)
Solution:
We use the Distributive Property of multiplication over addition: \( a \times b + a \times c = a \times (b + c) \)
Here, \( a = -48 \).
\( = (-48) \times [26 + (-36)] \)
\( = (-48) \times [26 - 36] \)
\( = (-48) \times (-10) \)
\( = 480 \)
Answer: \( 480 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Question 6 [3 Marks]
Simplify the following algebraic expression: \( 3(x^2 - 5x + 2) - 2(3x^2 - 4x - 5) \)
Solution:
Expand both brackets by multiplying terms:
\( [3(x^2) - 3(5x) + 3(2)] - [2(3x^2) - 2(4x) - 2(5)] \)
\( = (3x^2 - 15x + 6) - (6x^2 - 8x - 10) \)
Remove brackets (change signs for the second part):
\( = 3x^2 - 15x + 6 - 6x^2 + 8x + 10 \)
Combine like terms:
\( = (3x^2 - 6x^2) + (-15x + 8x) + (6 + 10) \)
\( = -3x^2 - 7x + 16 \)
Answer: \( -3x^2 - 7x + 16 \)
Question 7 [3 Marks]
In a class test containing 15 questions, \( 4 \) marks are given for every correct answer and \( (-2) \) marks are given for every incorrect answer. Gurpreet attempts all questions but only \( 9 \) of her answers are correct. What is her total score?
Solution:
Total questions attempted = \( 15 \)
Number of correct answers = \( 9 \)
Number of incorrect answers = \( 15 - 9 = 6 \)
Marks for correct answers:
\( = 9 \times 4 = 36 \text{ marks} \)
Marks for incorrect answers:
\( = 6 \times (-2) = -12 \text{ marks} \)
Total Score = Marks for correct answers + Marks for incorrect answers
Total Score = \( 36 + (-12) = 36 - 12 = 24 \)
Answer: Total Score = \( 24 \)
Question 8 [3 Marks]
What should be added to \( x^2 + xy + y^2 \) to obtain \( 2x^2 + 3xy \)?
Solution:
Let the required expression to be added be '\( P \)'.
\( (x^2 + xy + y^2) + P = 2x^2 + 3xy \)
\( P = (2x^2 + 3xy) - (x^2 + xy + y^2) \)
Open the brackets and change signs:
\( P = 2x^2 + 3xy - x^2 - xy - y^2 \)
Group like terms:
\( P = (2x^2 - x^2) + (3xy - xy) - y^2 \)
\( P = x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \)
Answer: \( x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \) should be added.
Question 9 [3 Marks]
Verify the property \( a - (-b) = a + b \) for the following values of \( a \) and \( b \):
\( a = 21, \quad b = 18 \)
Solution:
Given: \( a = 21, b = 18 \)
LHS Evaluation:
\( \text{LHS} = a - (-b) \)
\( \text{LHS} = 21 - (-18) \)
\( \text{LHS} = 21 + 18 = 39 \)
RHS Evaluation:
\( \text{RHS} = a + b \)
\( \text{RHS} = 21 + 18 = 39 \)
Since \( \text{LHS} = \text{RHS} = 39 \), the property is verified.
Answer: Verified successfully.
Question 10 [3 Marks]
Find the value of the expression \( 2a^2 - 3ab + b^2 \) when \( a = 2 \) and \( b = -1 \).
Solution:
Substitute \( a = 2 \) and \( b = -1 \) into the expression:
\( = 2(2)^2 - 3(2)(-1) + (-1)^2 \)
Calculate squares:
\( = 2(4) - 3(-2) + (1) \)
Multiply terms:
\( = 8 - (-6) + 1 \)
\( = 8 + 6 + 1 \)
\( = 15 \)
Answer: Value = \( 15 \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Question 11 [5 Marks]
(a) An elevator descends into a mine shaft at the rate of \( 6 \text{ m/min} \). If the descent starts from \( 10 \text{ m} \) above the ground level, how long will it take to reach \( -350 \text{ m} \)? [3 Marks]
Solution (a):
Initial height = \( +10 \text{ m} \)
Final depth = \( -350 \text{ m} \)
Total distance to be covered = Final position - Initial position (taking magnitude of descent):
Distance = \( 10 \text{ m} - (-350 \text{ m}) = 10 + 350 = 360 \text{ m} \)
Rate of descent (Speed) = \( 6 \text{ m/min} \)
Time taken = \( \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Rate}} = \frac{360}{6} = 60 \text{ minutes} \)
\( 60 \text{ minutes} = 1 \text{ hour} \)
Answer (a): \( 60 \text{ minutes} \) or \( 1 \text{ hour} \)
(b) Write a pair of negative integers whose difference is \( 8 \). [2 Marks]
Solution (b):
We need to find two negative integers, say \( x \) and \( y \), such that \( x - y = 8 \).
Let's choose \( x = -2 \) and \( y = -10 \).
Check: \( -2 - (-10) = -2 + 10 = 8 \).
(Note: There can be infinitely many such pairs, e.g., -1 and -9, -3 and -11).
Answer (b): \( -2 \) and \( -10 \) (Example pair)
Question 12 [5 Marks]
(a) From the sum of \( 3x - y + 11 \) and \( -y - 11 \), subtract \( 3x - y - 11 \). [3 Marks]
Solution (a):
Step 1: Find the sum.
Sum = \( (3x - y + 11) + (-y - 11) \)
Sum = \( 3x - y - y + 11 - 11 = 3x - 2y \)
Step 2: Subtract the given expression from the sum.
\( (3x - 2y) - (3x - y - 11) \)
\( = 3x - 2y - 3x + y + 11 \)
\( = (3x - 3x) + (-2y + y) + 11 \)
\( = 0 - y + 11 = 11 - y \)
Answer (a): \( 11 - y \)
(b) Classify the following expressions into monomials, binomials and trinomials:
(i) \( 4y - 7z \) \quad (ii) \( y^2 \) \quad (iii) \( x + y - xy \) \quad (iv) \( 100 \) [2 Marks]
Solution (b):
(i) \( 4y - 7z \) : Contains two unlike terms. It is a Binomial.
(ii) \( y^2 \) : Contains only one term. It is a Monomial.
(iii) \( x + y - xy \) : Contains three unlike terms. It is a Trinomial.
(iv) \( 100 \) : Contains only one term (constant). It is a Monomial.
Answer (b): (i) Binomial, (ii) Monomial, (iii) Trinomial, (iv) Monomial
Question 13 [5 Marks]
(a) Simplify: \( [(-16) \div 4] \div (-2) \). State with a reason whether integer division is associative. [2 Marks]
Solution (a):
Simplify bracket first:
\( [(-16) \div 4] = -4 \)
Now, \( (-4) \div (-2) = \frac{-4}{-2} = 2 \)
Checking associativity: \( (a \div b) \div c \neq a \div (b \div c) \)
Let's check RHS: \( -16 \div [4 \div (-2)] = -16 \div (-2) = 8 \)
Since \( 2 \neq 8 \), integer division is not associative.
Answer (a): \( 2 \); Division is not associative.
(b) Simplify the expression \( 3x - 5 - x + 9a \) and find its value if \( x = 3 \) and \( a = -1 \). [3 Marks]
Solution (b):
Step 1: Simplify the expression.
\( 3x - 5 - x + 9a = (3x - x) + 9a - 5 \)
Simplified expression = \( 2x + 9a - 5 \)
Step 2: Substitute values \( x = 3 \) and \( a = -1 \).
\( = 2(3) + 9(-1) - 5 \)
\( = 6 - 9 - 5 \)
\( = 6 - 14 = -8 \)
Answer (b): Simplified: \( 2x + 9a - 5 \), Value: \( -8 \)
