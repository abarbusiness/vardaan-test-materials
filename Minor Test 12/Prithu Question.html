Minor Test 12
Class: 9 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 5 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw graphs wherever required.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the linear equation \( 2x = -5y \) in the standard form \( ax + by + c = 0 \) and indicate the values of \( a, b, \) and \( c \).
2.
Check which of the following are solutions of the equation \( x - 2y = 4 \):
(i) \( (0, 2) \)
(ii) \( (4, 0) \)
3.
Find the value of \( k \), if \( x = 2, y = 1 \) is a solution of the equation \( 2x + 3y = k \).
4.
Write any two distinct solutions for the linear equation \( 2x + y = 7 \).
5.
At what points does the graph of the linear equation \( x + y = 5 \) meet the x-axis and the y-axis?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
The cost of a notebook is twice the cost of a pen. Write a linear equation in two variables to represent this statement. (Take the cost of a notebook to be ₹ \( x \) and that of a pen to be ₹ \( y \)).
7.
Give the geometric representations of \( y = 3 \) as an equation:
(i) in one variable
(ii) in two variables
8.
Solve the equation \( 2x + 1 = x - 3 \), and represent the solution(s) on the Cartesian plane.
9.
If the point \( (3, 4) \) lies on the graph of the equation \( 3y = ax + 7 \), find the value of \( a \).
10.
Yamini and Fatima, two students of Class IX of a school, together contributed ₹ 100 towards the Prime Minister’s Relief Fund to help the earthquake victims. Write a linear equation which satisfies this data.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A taxi fare in a city is as follows: For the first kilometre, the fare is ₹ 8 and for the subsequent distance it is ₹ 5 per km. Taking the distance covered as \( x \) km and total fare as ₹ \( y \).
(i) Write a linear equation for this information.
(ii) Draw its graph.
12.
In countries like USA and Canada, temperature is measured in Fahrenheit, whereas in countries like India, it is measured in Celsius. Here is a linear equation that converts Fahrenheit to Celsius: \( F = \left(\frac{9}{5}\right)C + 32 \)
(i) If the temperature is \( 30^\circ\text{C} \), what is the temperature in Fahrenheit?
(ii) If the temperature is \( 95^\circ\text{F} \), what is the temperature in Celsius?
(iii) Is there a temperature which is numerically the same in both Fahrenheit and Celsius? If yes, find it.
13.
Draw the graph of the linear equation \( x + y = 4 \). From the graph, find the coordinates of the point where the line intersects the x-axis and check if the point \( (2, 2) \) lies on the line.
14.
Give the equations of two lines passing through \( (2, 14) \). How many more such lines are there, and why? Give a logical explanation based on geometric principles.
15.
(i) Write the linear equation such that each point on its graph has an ordinate 3 times its abscissa.
(ii) Find the coordinates where the line \( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{y}{3} = 1 \) intersects the x-axis and y-axis. Verify your answer algebraically.
