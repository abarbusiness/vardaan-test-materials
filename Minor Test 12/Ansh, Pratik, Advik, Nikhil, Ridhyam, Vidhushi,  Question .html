Minor Test 12: Force, Pressure & Moment of Force
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Physics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Formulas and units must be clearly stated in numericals.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define force and state its SI unit.
2.
State two points of difference between thrust and pressure.
3.
Give a scientific reason: Why do school bags have wide straps?
4.
State any two characteristics of liquid pressure.
5.
Define 'moment of force' and write down its mathematical formula.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A force of \( 150 \text{ N} \) acts uniformly over an area of \( 5 \text{ m}^2 \). Calculate the pressure exerted on the surface.
7.
Explain scientifically why the wall of a water dam is made much thicker at the bottom than at the top.
8.
(a) What do you understand by the 'turning effect of a force'?
(b) Give one practical everyday example where the turning effect of force is utilized.
9.
A boy applies a force of \( 50 \text{ N} \) to open a heavy door. If the perpendicular distance of the point of application of force from the hinges is \( 0.4 \text{ m} \), calculate the moment of force produced.
10.
Briefly explain how the pressure exerted by a liquid varies with:
(i) The depth of the liquid.
(ii) The density of the liquid.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Define pressure and write its SI unit. (2)
(b) Explain why cutting tools like knives and axes have sharp edges. (1.5)
(c) A wooden block weighing \( 200 \text{ N} \) rests on a table. If the area of contact between the block and the table is \( 0.5 \text{ m}^2 \), find the pressure exerted by the wooden block. (1.5)
12.
(a) Describe a simple, well-labelled experiment to demonstrate that a liquid exerts pressure equally in all directions at a given depth. (3)
(b) State the SI units of (i) Thrust and (ii) Torque (Moment of force). (2)
13.
(a) A mechanic uses a wrench of length \( 30 \text{ cm} \) to loosen a tight nut. He applies a perpendicular force of \( 40 \text{ N} \) at the end of the wrench. Calculate the torque required to loosen the nut. (3)
(b) Why is it always easier to open a heavy door by applying force at the edge farthest from the hinges rather than near the hinges? (2)
