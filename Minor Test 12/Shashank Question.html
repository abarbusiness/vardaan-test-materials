Minor Test 12
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show necessary calculations step-by-step.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following integer expression: \( (-15) + (+23) - (-12) \)
2.
Convert the decimal \( 3.075 \) into a fraction in its simplest form.
3.
Find the ratio of 45 minutes to 1.5 hours in its simplest form.
4.
Arrange the following integers in ascending order:
\( -8,\ 0,\ -12,\ 5,\ -2 \)
5.
If the numbers \( 3,\ x,\ 15,\ 25 \) are in proportion, find the value of \( x \).
SECTION B (20 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 4 Marks Each)
6.
A rectangular field has a length of \( 42.5 \text{ m} \) and a breadth of \( 18.6 \text{ m} \). Calculate its perimeter and area.
7.
Subtract the sum of \( -345 \) and \( 120 \) from the difference of \( 45 \) and \( -110 \).
8.
Two numbers are in the ratio \( 5 : 7 \). If the sum of the numbers is \( 108 \), find the two numbers.
9.
Simplify the following decimal expression using BODMAS:
\( 18.5 - [6.4 - \{3.2 + (5.6 - 3.1)\}] \)
10.
A car travels \( 165 \text{ km} \) in 3 hours at a uniform speed.
(a) How long will it take to travel \( 440 \text{ km} \)?
(b) How far will it travel in 7 hours?
SECTION C (20 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In a competitive examination, 5 marks are awarded for every correct answer, \( -2 \) marks for every incorrect answer, and 0 marks for questions not attempted.
(a) Rahul attempts all questions. He gets 12 correct and 8 incorrect answers. What is his total score?
(b) Riya scores 20 marks in the same exam. If she got 10 correct answers, how many questions did she guess incorrectly?
12.
The cost of \( 15.5 \text{ kg} \) of apples is Rs. \( 1317.50 \).
(a) Find the cost of \( 1 \text{ kg} \) of apples.
(b) Find the quantity of apples that can be purchased for Rs. \( 595 \).
13.
The ratio of the monthly income to the monthly expenditure of a family is \( 9 : 7 \). If their monthly savings is Rs. \( 7200 \):
(a) What is their total monthly income?
(b) What is their total monthly expenditure?
14.
A piece of electrical wire measuring \( 14.4 \text{ m} \) in length is cut into two pieces in the ratio \( 3 : 5 \).
(a) Find the length of each piece in meters.
(b) Express the length of the smaller piece in centimeters as an integer.
