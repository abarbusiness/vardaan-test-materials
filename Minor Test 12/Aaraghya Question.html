Minor Test 12
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all steps clearly.
  • Use of calculator is strictly prohibited.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Write the degree of the given polynomial: \( 3x^2y - 5xy^3 + 2x^4 - 7 \)
2.
Identify the numerical coefficient and write all the literal factors of the term \( -12x^2yz \).
3.
Classify the following algebraic expressions as monomial, binomial, or trinomial:
(i) \( 4a^2 - 3b + 7c \)
(ii) \( -8x^2yz^3 \)
4.
Subtract \( (3a - 5b) \) from \( (7a + 2b) \).
5.
Find the numerical value of the expression \( x^2 - y^2 + 2xy \) when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Add the following algebraic expressions:
\( 4x^2 - 7xy + 4y^2 - 3 \), \( 5 + 6y^2 - 8xy + x^2 \), and \( 6 - 2xy + 2x^2 - 5y^2 \)
7.
Simplify the following expression:
\( 5x - [3y - \{2x - (y - x)\}] \)
8.
What must be added to \( 2x^2 - 3xy + 5y^2 \) to get \( x^2 + xy - y^2 \)?
9.
If \( A = 3p^2 - 4p + 1 \), \( B = 5p^2 + 7p - 8 \), and \( C = -2p^2 + p + 6 \), find the value of the expression \( A - B + C \).
10.
The length of a rectangular field is \( (3x + 2y) \) units and its breadth is \( (x - 3y) \) units. Find the algebraic expression for the perimeter of the field.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Given \( P = -2x^2 - y^2 + 3xy \), \( Q = x^2 - 2y^2 - xy \) and \( R = x^2 + 3y^2 - 2xy \).
(a) Find the sum of \( P, Q, \) and \( R \).
(b) Evaluate \( P + Q \) if \( x = 1 \) and \( y = -2 \).
12.
Simplify the following expression by removing the brackets strictly in order (vinculum, round, curly, square):
\( 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 3a - (2a - \overline{a - b}) \right\} \right] \)
13.
By how much does the sum of \( (5x^2 - 7x + 4) \) and \( (-3x^2 + 5x + 2) \) exceed the difference when \( (x^2 - x - 1) \) is subtracted from \( (2x^2 + 3x - 5) \)?
14.
(a) Write the algebraic expression for: "Five times a number \( x \) added to the square of a number \( y \), and the whole sum subtracted from the cube of a number \( z \)".
(b) Identify and group the like terms from the following list:
\( 3x^2y, -2xy^2, 5yx^2, 7x^2, -y^2x, -x^2 \)
15.
The sides of a triangle are \( (2x^2 - 3x + 4) \text{ cm} \), \( (x^2 + 5x - 1) \text{ cm} \), and \( (-3x^2 + 4x + 5) \text{ cm} \).
(a) Find the algebraic expression for the perimeter of the triangle.
(b) If \( x = 2 \), what is the numerical value of the perimeter?
