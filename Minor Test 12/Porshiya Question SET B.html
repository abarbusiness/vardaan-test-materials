Minor Test 12
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly, showing all working steps clearly.
  • Use of calculators is not permitted.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the profit or loss percent if Cost Price (CP) = ₹400 and Selling Price (SP) = ₹460.
2.
A shopkeeper buys a toy for ₹250 and sells it at a loss of 12%. Find the selling price of the toy.
3.
What is the cost price of an article which is sold for ₹840 at a profit of 5%?
4.
Define 'Overhead Expenses'. How are they treated while calculating profit or loss?
5.
By selling a pen for ₹45, a shopkeeper loses ₹5. Find his loss percentage.
SECTION B (20 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 4 Marks Each)
6.
A man bought 2 dozen eggs for ₹84. Since 4 of them were broken, he sold the remaining at ₹4 per egg. Find his total profit or loss and the profit/loss percentage.
7.
The selling price of 12 pens is equal to the cost price of 15 pens. Find the gain percent.
8.
A vendor bought lemons at 6 for ₹10 and sold them at 4 for ₹8. Find his gain or loss percent.
9.
Rahul sold two bicycles for ₹3,000 each. On one bicycle, he gained 20%, and on the other, he lost 20%. Find his total gain or loss percent in the whole transaction.
10.
A dealer buys a machine for ₹8,000. He spends ₹500 on its transport and ₹300 on installation. At what price must he sell it to gain 15%?
SECTION C (20 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
By selling an article for ₹960, a man incurs a loss of 4%. At what price should he sell it to make a profit of 16%?
12.
A grocer bought 50 kg of rice at ₹40 per kg and 50 kg of another variety at ₹60 per kg. He mixed the two varieties and sold the mixture at ₹55 per kg. Find his profit or loss percent on the whole transaction.
13.
If an article is sold at a gain of 5% instead of being sold at a loss of 5%, a man gets ₹20 more. Find the cost price of the article.
14.
Rohan bought a second-hand car for ₹1,20,000. He spent ₹15,000 on its repairs and ₹5,000 on new tires. He then sold it to his friend at a profit of 10%. His friend later sold it to another person at a loss of 5%. What did the final person pay for the car?
