SECTION A (10 Marks)
Question 1 [2 Marks]
Write the predecessor of the smallest 5-digit whole number.
Solution:
Smallest 5-digit whole number = \( 10000 \)
Predecessor is found by subtracting \( 1 \) from the number.
Predecessor = \( 10000 - 1 = 9999 \)
Answer: \( 9999 \)
Question 2 [2 Marks]
Find the sum of the additive inverse of \( -34 \) and the absolute value of \( -15 \).
Solution:
Additive inverse of \( -34 \) is \( +34 \) (or just \( 34 \)).
Absolute value of \( -15 \) is written as \( |-15| = 15 \).
Sum = (Additive inverse of \( -34 \)) + (Absolute value of \( -15 \))
Sum = \( 34 + 15 = 49 \)
Answer: \( 49 \)
Question 3 [2 Marks]
Arrange the following integers in ascending order: \( -12, 0, -5, 8, -25, 3 \)
Solution:
Ascending order means arranging from the smallest value to the largest value.
Identify the negative numbers (smaller than 0): \( -25, -12, -5 \)
Order negative numbers from smallest to largest: \( -25 < -12 < -5 \)
Identify the positive numbers (larger than 0): \( 3, 8 \)
Order positive numbers: \( 3 < 8 \)
Combine the ordered sets with \( 0 \) in the middle.
Answer: \( -25, -12, -5, 0, 3, 8 \)
Question 4 [2 Marks]
Using suitable properties of whole numbers, find the value of: \( 297 \times 17 + 297 \times 3 \)
Solution:
We will use the Distributive Property of Multiplication over Addition: \( a \times (b + c) = a \times b + a \times c \)
Here, \( a = 297 \), \( b = 17 \), and \( c = 3 \).
Take \( 297 \) as common:
\( 297 \times 17 + 297 \times 3 = 297 \times (17 + 3) \)
\( = 297 \times (20) \)
\( = 5940 \)
Answer: \( 5940 \)
Question 5 [2 Marks]
State whether true or false: The product of a negative integer and zero is always a negative integer. Give a reason for your answer.
Solution:
The statement is False.
Reason: The property of zero states that the product of any integer (whether positive or negative) and zero is always zero. Zero is neither a positive nor a negative integer.
Example: \( (-5) \times 0 = 0 \), not a negative integer.
Answer: False
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Question 6 [3 Marks]
Simplify the following: \( (-25) - (-14) + (-3) - (8) \)
Solution:
First, resolve the consecutive signs. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding a positive.
\( (-25) + 14 - 3 - 8 \)
Group the positive and negative numbers together:
\( (-25 - 3 - 8) + 14 \)
Sum the negative numbers:
\( -(25 + 3 + 8) = -36 \)
Combine with the positive number:
\( -36 + 14 \)
Since signs are different, find the difference and keep the sign of the larger absolute value:
\( - (36 - 14) = -22 \)
Answer: \( -22 \)
Question 7 [3 Marks]
The temperature of a city was \( 4^{\circ}\text{C} \) on Monday. It dropped by \( 6^{\circ}\text{C} \) on Tuesday and rose by \( 5^{\circ}\text{C} \) on Wednesday. What was the temperature on Wednesday?
Solution:
Temperature on Monday = \( 4^{\circ}\text{C} \)
Temperature dropped on Tuesday by \( 6^{\circ}\text{C} \). "Dropped" implies subtraction.
Temperature on Tuesday = \( 4^{\circ}\text{C} - 6^{\circ}\text{C} = -2^{\circ}\text{C} \)
Temperature rose on Wednesday by \( 5^{\circ}\text{C} \). "Rose" implies addition.
Temperature on Wednesday = Temperature on Tuesday + \( 5^{\circ}\text{C} \)
Temperature on Wednesday = \( -2^{\circ}\text{C} + 5^{\circ}\text{C} \)
\( = 3^{\circ}\text{C} \)
Answer: \( 3^{\circ}\text{C} \)
Question 8 [3 Marks]
Find the product using distributive property: \( 854 \times 102 \)
Solution:
Express \( 102 \) as a sum of two numbers to make calculation easier.
\( 102 = 100 + 2 \)
Substitute this back into the expression:
\( 854 \times (100 + 2) \)
Apply Distributive Property of Multiplication over Addition: \( a \times (b + c) = (a \times b) + (a \times c) \)
\( = (854 \times 100) + (854 \times 2) \)
\( = 85400 + 1708 \)
Add the two results:
\( = 87108 \)
Answer: \( 87108 \)
Question 9 [3 Marks]
Subtract the sum of \( -1032 \) and \( 878 \) from \( -34 \).
Solution:
Part 1: Find the sum of \( -1032 \) and \( 878 \).
Sum = \( (-1032) + 878 \)
Since signs are different, find difference and keep sign of larger absolute value.
Sum = \( -(1032 - 878) = -154 \)
Part 2: Subtract this sum from \( -34 \).
Expression: \( (-34) - (-154) \)
\( = -34 + 154 \)
Find difference: \( 154 - 34 = 120 \)
Answer: \( 120 \)
Question 10 [3 Marks]
Determine the integer whose product with \( -1 \) is: (i) \( -22 \) (ii) \( 37 \) (iii) \( 0 \)
Solution:
Let the unknown integer be \( x \). We need to find \( x \) such that \( x \times (-1) = \text{given result} \).
(i) \( -22 \)
\( x \times (-1) = -22 \)
Since the product is negative and one factor is negative, the other factor must be positive.
\( x = \frac{-22}{-1} = 22 \)
Answer (i): \( 22 \)
(ii) \( 37 \)
\( x \times (-1) = 37 \)
Since the product is positive and one factor is negative, the other factor must also be negative.
\( x = \frac{37}{-1} = -37 \)
Answer (ii): \( -37 \)
(iii) \( 0 \)
\( x \times (-1) = 0 \)
The product of any number and zero is zero.
\( x = \frac{0}{-1} = 0 \)
Answer (iii): \( 0 \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Question 11 [5 Marks]
(a) Simplify: \( 15 \times (-25) \times (-4) \times (-10) \) [2 Marks]
Solution (a):
Group terms to make calculation easier. Multiply the negative numbers first to establish the final sign.
Group as: \( [15 \times (-10)] \times [(-25) \times (-4)] \)
\( 15 \times (-10) = -150 \)
\( (-25) \times (-4) = +100 \) (Product of two negative integers is positive)
Now multiply these intermediate results:
\( (-150) \times 100 = -15000 \)
Answer (a): \( -15000 \)
(b) Verify: \( (-30) \times [13 + (-3)] = [(-30) \times 13] + [(-30) \times (-3)] \) [3 Marks]
Solution (b):
LHS (Left Hand Side): \( (-30) \times [13 + (-3)] \)
Solve bracket first: \( [13 - 3] = 10 \)
\( \text{LHS} = (-30) \times 10 = -300 \)
RHS (Right Hand Side): \( [(-30) \times 13] + [(-30) \times (-3)] \)
Calculate first bracket: \( (-30) \times 13 = -390 \)
Calculate second bracket: \( (-30) \times (-3) = +90 \)
\( \text{RHS} = (-390) + 90 \)
\( \text{RHS} = -(390 - 90) = -300 \)
Since LHS = RHS (\( -300 = -300 \)), the statement is verified. This verifies the Distributive Property.
Answer (b): Verified.
Question 12 [5 Marks]
In a class test containing 15 questions, 4 marks are given for every correct answer and \( -2 \) marks are given for every incorrect answer.
(i) Gurpreet attempts all questions but only 9 of her answers are correct. What is her total score?
(ii) One of her friends gets only 5 answers correct. What will be her score?
Solution:
Marks given for 1 correct answer = \( 4 \)
Marks given for 1 incorrect answer = \( -2 \)
(i) Gurpreet's Score:
Total questions attempted = \( 15 \)
Number of correct answers = \( 9 \)
Marks scored for correct answers = \( 9 \times 4 = 36 \)
Number of incorrect answers = \( \text{Total} - \text{Correct} = 15 - 9 = 6 \)
Marks scored for incorrect answers = \( 6 \times (-2) = -12 \)
Total Score = (Marks for correct) + (Marks for incorrect)
Total Score = \( 36 + (-12) = 36 - 12 = 24 \)
Answer (i): Gurpreet's total score is 24.
(ii) Friend's Score:
Assuming the friend also attempts all 15 questions (as per standard interpretation of such problems unless stated otherwise).
Number of correct answers = \( 5 \)
Marks scored for correct answers = \( 5 \times 4 = 20 \)
Number of incorrect answers = \( 15 - 5 = 10 \)
Marks scored for incorrect answers = \( 10 \times (-2) = -20 \)
Total Score = \( 20 + (-20) = 0 \)
Answer (ii): Friend's total score is 0.
Question 13 [5 Marks]
(a) Find the value of \( x \) if: \( (-12) \times x = 144 \) [2 Marks]
Solution (a):
Given: \( (-12) \times x = 144 \)
To find \( x \), isolate it by dividing both sides by \( -12 \).
\( x = \frac{144}{-12} \)
A positive integer divided by a negative integer results in a negative integer.
\( 144 \div 12 = 12 \), so \( x = -12 \)
Answer (a): \( x = -12 \)
(b) A shopkeeper gains ₹ 1 on each pen and loses 40 paise on each pencil. He sells 45 pens and some pencils losing ₹ 5 in all. How many pencils does he sell? [3 Marks]
Solution (b):
Let profit be denoted by positive integer and loss by negative integer.
Profit on 1 pen = \( \text{₹ } 1 \)
Profit on 45 pens = \( 45 \times 1 = \text{₹ } 45 \)
Total loss = \( \text{₹ } 5 \). Representing loss as an integer: Total outcome = \( -5 \)
Total outcome = (Profit on pens) + (Loss on pencils)
\( -5 = 45 + (\text{Loss on pencils}) \)
Loss on pencils = \( -5 - 45 = -\text{₹ } 50 \)
This means he lost ₹ 50 on selling pencils.
Convert total loss on pencils to paise: \( -50 \times 100 = -5000 \text{ paise} \)
Loss on 1 pencil = \( -40 \text{ paise} \)
Number of pencils sold = (Total loss on pencils) \( \div \) (Loss on 1 pencil)
Number of pencils = \( \frac{-5000}{-40} \)
Number of pencils = \( \frac{500}{4} = 125 \)
Answer (b): He sold 125 pencils.
