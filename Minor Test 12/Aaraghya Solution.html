SECTION A (10 Marks)
[2 Marks] Q1. Write the degree of the given polynomial: \( 3x^2y - 5xy^3 + 2x^4 - 7 \)
Solution:
To find the degree of a polynomial, we find the sum of the powers of variables in each term:
Term 1: \( 3x^2y \) → Degree = \( 2 + 1 = 3 \)
Term 2: \( -5xy^3 \) → Degree = \( 1 + 3 = 4 \)
Term 3: \( 2x^4 \) → Degree = \( 4 \)
Term 4: \( -7 \) → Degree = \( 0 \)
The highest degree among all terms is 4.
Therefore, the degree of the polynomial is 4.

Solution:
In the term \( -12x^2yz \):
The number part is the numerical coefficient.
Numerical Coefficient = \( -12 \) (1 Mark)
The variable parts are the literal factors. Expanding \( x^2yz \):
Literal factors = \( x, x, y, \text{ and } z \) (1 Mark)
[2 Marks] Q3. Classify as monomial, binomial, or trinomial: (i) \( 4a^2 - 3b + 7c \) (ii) \( -8x^2yz^3 \)
Solution:
(i) \( 4a^2 - 3b + 7c \) has three unlike terms.
Classification: Trinomial (1 Mark)
(ii) \( -8x^2yz^3 \) is a single term (no plus or minus signs separating parts).
Classification: Monomial (1 Mark)
[2 Marks] Q4. Subtract \( (3a - 5b) \) from \( (7a + 2b) \).
Solution:
Required expression = \( (7a + 2b) - (3a - 5b) \)
Open the brackets (change signs of the second expression):
\( = 7a + 2b - 3a + 5b \)
Collect like terms together:
\( = (7a - 3a) + (2b + 5b) \)
\( = 4a + 7b \)
[2 Marks] Q5. Find the numerical value of \( x^2 - y^2 + 2xy \) when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \).
Solution:
Substitute \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \) in the expression:
\( = (2)^2 - (-1)^2 + 2(2)(-1) \)
Evaluate the squares and multiplication:
\( = 4 - (1) + (-4) \)
\( = 4 - 1 - 4 \)
\( = -1 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
[3 Marks] Q6. Add: \( 4x^2 - 7xy + 4y^2 - 3 \), \( 5 + 6y^2 - 8xy + x^2 \), and \( 6 - 2xy + 2x^2 - 5y^2 \)
Solution:
Arrange the terms in a row and group like terms (Column method can also be used):
\( (4x^2 - 7xy + 4y^2 - 3) + (x^2 - 8xy + 6y^2 + 5) + (2x^2 - 2xy - 5y^2 + 6) \)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (4x^2 + x^2 + 2x^2) + (-7xy - 8xy - 2xy) + (4y^2 + 6y^2 - 5y^2) + (-3 + 5 + 6) \)
\( = (7x^2) + (-17xy) + (5y^2) + (8) \)
\( = 7x^2 - 17xy + 5y^2 + 8 \)
[3 Marks] Q7. Simplify: \( 5x - [3y - \{2x - (y - x)\}] \)
Solution:
We resolve the brackets from innermost to outermost (BODMAS rule).
Step 1: Open round brackets \( ( ) \)
\( = 5x - [3y - \{2x - y + x\}] \)
Step 2: Simplify terms inside curly brackets \( \{ \} \)
\( = 5x - [3y - \{3x - y\}] \)
Step 3: Open curly brackets \( \{ \} \) by multiplying by negative sign.
\( = 5x - [3y - 3x + y] \)
Step 4: Simplify terms inside square brackets \( [ ] \)
\( = 5x - [4y - 3x] \)
Step 5: Open square brackets.
\( = 5x - 4y + 3x \)
\( = 8x - 4y \)
[3 Marks] Q8. What must be added to \( 2x^2 - 3xy + 5y^2 \) to get \( x^2 + xy - y^2 \)?
Solution:
Let the expression to be added be \( M \).
\( (2x^2 - 3xy + 5y^2) + M = (x^2 + xy - y^2) \)
Therefore, \( M = \text{(Result)} - \text{(Given expression)} \)
\( M = (x^2 + xy - y^2) - (2x^2 - 3xy + 5y^2) \)
Open the brackets:
\( M = x^2 + xy - y^2 - 2x^2 + 3xy - 5y^2 \)
Collect like terms:
\( M = (x^2 - 2x^2) + (xy + 3xy) + (-y^2 - 5y^2) \)
\( M = -x^2 + 4xy - 6y^2 \)
[3 Marks] Q9. If \( A = 3p^2 - 4p + 1 \), \( B = 5p^2 + 7p - 8 \), and \( C = -2p^2 + p + 6 \), find \( A - B + C \).
Solution:
Substitute the expressions for A, B, and C in \( (A) - (B) + (C) \):
\( = (3p^2 - 4p + 1) - (5p^2 + 7p - 8) + (-2p^2 + p + 6) \)
Open the brackets:
\( = 3p^2 - 4p + 1 - 5p^2 - 7p + 8 - 2p^2 + p + 6 \)
Group like terms:
\( = (3p^2 - 5p^2 - 2p^2) + (-4p - 7p + p) + (1 + 8 + 6) \)
\( = (3 - 7)p^2 + (-11 + 1)p + (15) \)
\( = -4p^2 - 10p + 15 \)
[3 Marks] Q10. The length of a rectangular field is \( (3x + 2y) \) and breadth is \( (x - 3y) \). Find the perimeter.
Solution:
Given: Length (\( L \)) \( = 3x + 2y \), Breadth (\( B \)) \( = x - 3y \)
Formula for Perimeter of rectangle \( = 2 \times (L + B) \)
Step 1: Find \( L + B \)
\( L + B = (3x + 2y) + (x - 3y) \)
\( L + B = 3x + x + 2y - 3y = 4x - y \)
Step 2: Multiply by 2
Perimeter \( = 2 \times (4x - y) \)
Distribute the 2 inside the bracket:
Perimeter \( = 8x - 2y \) units
SECTION C (25 Marks)
[5 Marks] Q11. Given \( P = -2x^2 - y^2 + 3xy \), \( Q = x^2 - 2y^2 - xy \) and \( R = x^2 + 3y^2 - 2xy \).
(a) Find the sum of \( P, Q, \) and \( R \).
(b) Evaluate \( P + Q \) if \( x = 1 \) and \( y = -2 \).
Solution:
Part (a): Find \( P + Q + R \) (3 Marks)
\( P + Q + R = (-2x^2 - y^2 + 3xy) + (x^2 - 2y^2 - xy) + (x^2 + 3y^2 - 2xy) \)
Group like terms:
\( = (-2x^2 + x^2 + x^2) + (-y^2 - 2y^2 + 3y^2) + (3xy - xy - 2xy) \)
\( = (0)x^2 + (0)y^2 + (0)xy \)
\( = 0 \)

Part (b): Evaluate \( P + Q \) at \( x = 1, y = -2 \) (2 Marks)
First, find \( P + Q \):
\( P + Q = (-2x^2 - y^2 + 3xy) + (x^2 - 2y^2 - xy) \)
\( P + Q = -x^2 - 3y^2 + 2xy \)
Now, substitute \( x = 1, y = -2 \):
\( = -(1)^2 - 3(-2)^2 + 2(1)(-2) \)
\( = -1 - 3(4) - 4 \)
\( = -1 - 12 - 4 \)
\( = -17 \)
[5 Marks] Q12. Simplify: \( 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 3a - (2a - \overline{a - b}) \right\} \right] \)
Solution:
We apply BODMAS. The bar bracket (vinculum) \(\overline{a - b}\) must be resolved first.
Step 1: Remove vinculum. (A minus sign before it changes the sign of terms under it).
\( = 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 3a - (2a - a + b) \right\} \right] \)
Simplify inside the round bracket: \( (2a - a + b) = (a + b) \)
\( = 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 3a - (a + b) \right\} \right] \)
Step 2: Remove round bracket \( ( ) \)
\( = 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 3a - a - b \right\} \right] \)
Simplify inside curly bracket: \( (3a - a) = 2a \)
\( = 3a - \left[ 2a - \left\{ 2a - b \right\} \right] \)
Step 3: Remove curly bracket \( \{ \} \)
\( = 3a - \left[ 2a - 2a + b \right] \)
Simplify inside square bracket: \( 2a - 2a = 0 \)
\( = 3a - \left[ 0 + b \right] \)
Step 4: Final simplification
\( = 3a - b \)
[5 Marks] Q13. By how much does the sum of \( (5x^2 - 7x + 4) \) and \( (-3x^2 + 5x + 2) \) exceed the difference when \( (x^2 - x - 1) \) is subtracted from \( (2x^2 + 3x - 5) \)?
Solution:
Part 1: Find the Sum
Sum \( = (5x^2 - 7x + 4) + (-3x^2 + 5x + 2) \)
\( = (5x^2 - 3x^2) + (-7x + 5x) + (4 + 2) \)
\( = 2x^2 - 2x + 6 \)     (Let this be S)

Part 2: Find the Difference
Difference \( = (2x^2 + 3x - 5) - (x^2 - x - 1) \)
\( = 2x^2 + 3x - 5 - x^2 + x + 1 \)
\( = (2x^2 - x^2) + (3x + x) + (-5 + 1) \)
\( = x^2 + 4x - 4 \)     (Let this be D)

Part 3: Find how much S exceeds D
Required value \( = S - D \)
\( = (2x^2 - 2x + 6) - (x^2 + 4x - 4) \)
\( = 2x^2 - 2x + 6 - x^2 - 4x + 4 \)
\( = (2x^2 - x^2) + (-2x - 4x) + (6 + 4) \)
\( = x^2 - 6x + 10 \)
[5 Marks] Q14. (a) Write the algebraic expression...
(b) Identify and group the like terms...
Solution:
Part (a): Write Algebraic Expression (2 Marks)
"Five times a number \( x \)" = \( 5x \)
"square of a number \( y \)" = \( y^2 \)
Their sum = \( (5x + y^2) \)
"cube of a number \( z \)" = \( z^3 \)
Subtracting the sum from the cube:
Expression: \( z^3 - (5x + y^2) \)

Part (b): Group Like Terms (3 Marks)
List: \( 3x^2y, -2xy^2, 5yx^2, 7x^2, -y^2x, -x^2 \)
Like terms have exactly the same literal factors with same powers.
Note that \( yx^2 = x^2y \) and \( y^2x = xy^2 \).
Group 1: Terms with \( x^2y \) variables.
\( 3x^2y, \text{ and } 5yx^2 \)
Group 2: Terms with \( xy^2 \) variables.
\( -2xy^2, \text{ and } -y^2x \)
Group 3: Terms with \( x^2 \) variables.
\( 7x^2, \text{ and } -x^2 \)
[5 Marks] Q15. The sides of a triangle are \( (2x^2 - 3x + 4) \text{ cm} \), \( (x^2 + 5x - 1) \text{ cm} \), and \( (-3x^2 + 4x + 5) \text{ cm} \).
(a) Find expression for perimeter. (b) Find value if \( x = 2 \).
Solution:
Part (a): Find the perimeter (3 Marks)
Perimeter of a triangle = Sum of all three sides.
\( \text{Perimeter} = (2x^2 - 3x + 4) + (x^2 + 5x - 1) + (-3x^2 + 4x + 5) \)
Group the like terms:
\( = (2x^2 + x^2 - 3x^2) + (-3x + 5x + 4x) + (4 - 1 + 5) \)
\( = (3x^2 - 3x^2) + (6x) + (8) \)
\( = 0 + 6x + 8 \)
Perimeter = \( (6x + 8) \text{ cm} \)

Part (b): Numerical value at \( x = 2 \) (2 Marks)
Substitute \( x = 2 \) into the perimeter expression:
\( \text{Perimeter} = 6(2) + 8 \)
\( \text{Perimeter} = 12 + 8 \)
Perimeter = \( 20 \text{ cm} \)
