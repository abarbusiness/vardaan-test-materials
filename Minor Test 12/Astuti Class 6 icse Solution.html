SECTION A (10 Marks)
Question 1 [2 Marks]
Convert the mixed fraction \( 3 \frac{4}{25} \) into a decimal fraction.
Solution:
Method 1 (Conversion to improper fraction):
\( 3 \frac{4}{25} = \frac{(3 \times 25) + 4}{25} = \frac{75 + 4}{25} = \frac{79}{25} \)
To convert to decimal, make the denominator a power of 10 (multiply numerator and denominator by 4):
\( \frac{79 \times 4}{25 \times 4} = \frac{316}{100} = 3.16 \)
Answer: \( 3.16 \)
Question 2 [2 Marks]
Simplify using the BODMAS rule: \( 15 - 3 \times 4 + 18 \div 6 \)
Solution:
Applying BODMAS (Division, then Multiplication, then Addition/Subtraction):
Step 1 (Division): \( 18 \div 6 = 3 \)
Expression becomes: \( 15 - 3 \times 4 + 3 \)
Step 2 (Multiplication): \( 3 \times 4 = 12 \)
Expression becomes: \( 15 - 12 + 3 \)
Step 3 (Addition & Subtraction, left to right): \( 3 + 3 = 6 \)
Answer: \( 6 \)
Question 3 [2 Marks]
Find the product of \( 14.35 \) and \( 0.08 \).
Solution:
First, ignore the decimals and multiply the numbers as integers:
\( 1435 \times 8 = 11480 \)
Now, count the total number of decimal places in the factors:
\( 14.35 \) has 2 decimal places, \( 0.08 \) has 2 decimal places. Total = 4 decimal places.
Place the decimal point 4 places from the right in the product:
\( 1.1480 = 1.148 \)
Answer: \( 1.148 \)

SECTION B (15 Marks)
Question 6 [3 Marks]
Simplify: \( 36 - [18 - \{14 - (15 - 4 \div 2 \times 2)\}] \)
Solution:
Follow BODMAS and solve innermost brackets `()` first.
Inside `()`: \( 15 - 4 \div 2 \times 2 \) (Division first: \( 4 \div 2 = 2 \))
\( = 15 - 2 \times 2 \) (Multiplication: \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \))
\( = 15 - 4 = 11 \)
Substitute back: \( 36 - [18 - \{14 - 11\}] \)
Solve curly brackets `{}`: \( \{14 - 11\} = 3 \)
Substitute back: \( 36 - [18 - 3] \)
Solve square brackets `[]`: \( [18 - 3] = 15 \)
Final step: \( 36 - 15 = 21 \)
Answer: \( 21 \)
Question 7 [3 Marks]
A car covers a distance of \( 89.1 \text{ km} \) in \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \). What is the average distance covered by it in \( 1 \text{ hour} \)?
Solution:
Total distance covered = \( 89.1 \text{ km} \)
Total time taken = \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \)
Distance covered in \( 1 \text{ hour} = \text{Total Distance} \div \text{Total Time} \)
\( = 89.1 \div 2.2 \)
Remove decimals by multiplying numerator and denominator by 10:
\( = \frac{89.1 \times 10}{2.2 \times 10} = \frac{891}{22} \)
Dividing 891 by 22:
\( 22 \times 40 = 880 \), Remainder \( = 11 \). \( 110 \div 22 = 5 \). Hence, \( 40.5 \)
Answer: \( 40.5 \text{ km} \)

Solution:

\( (3 \times 2) = 6 \)
Expression: \( \frac{2}{3} \text{ of } 18 + 4 - 6 \)
Next, solve 'of' (Multiplication):
\( \frac{2}{3} \times 18 = 2 \times 6 = 12 \)
Expression: \( 12 + 4 - 6 \)
Next, Addition:
\( 12 + 4 = 16 \)
Finally, Subtraction:

SECTION C (15 Marks)
Question 11 [5 Marks]
Simplify using BODMAS: \( 27 - [38 - \{46 - (15 - \overline{13 - 2})\}] \)
Solution:
According to BODMAS, solve the Vinculum (bar bracket) first:
\( \overline{13 - 2} = 11 \)
Expression becomes: \( 27 - [38 - \{46 - (15 - 11)\}] \)
Next, solve the round brackets `()`:
\( (15 - 11) = 4 \)
Expression becomes: \( 27 - [38 - \{46 - 4\}] \)
Next, solve the curly brackets `{}`:
\( \{46 - 4\} = 42 \)
Expression becomes: \( 27 - [38 - 42] \)
Next, solve the square brackets `[]`:
\( [38 - 42] = -4 \)
Expression becomes: \( 27 - [-4] \)
Finally, subtract the negative number (which means addition):
\( 27 + 4 = 31 \)
Answer: \( 31 \)
Question 12 [5 Marks]
Mrs. Sharma bought \( 3.5 \text{ kg} \) of apples at ₹ \( 85.50 \) per kg, \( 2 \text{ kg} \) of grapes at ₹ \( 115.75 \) per kg, and \( 1.5 \text{ kg} \) of mangoes at ₹ \( 90.00 \) per kg. If she gave a ₹ \( 1000 \) note to the shopkeeper, how much money will she get back?
Solution:
Calculate the cost of each fruit separately:
Cost of Apples = \( 3.5 \times 85.50 = \text{₹ } 299.25 \)
Cost of Grapes = \( 2 \times 115.75 = \text{₹ } 231.50 \)
Cost of Mangoes = \( 1.5 \times 90.00 = \text{₹ } 135.00 \)
Now, calculate total amount spent:
Total Cost \( = 299.25 + 231.50 + 135.00 = \text{₹ } 665.75 \)
Calculate amount returned by shopkeeper:
Money Given = ₹ \( 1000.00 \)
Money Returned = \( 1000.00 - 665.75 = \text{₹ } 334.25 \)
Answer: ₹ \( 334.25 \)
Question 13 [5 Marks total]
(a) Divide \( 0.0768 \) by \( 1.6 \). [2 Marks]
Solution (a):
Write as a fraction: \( \frac{0.0768}{1.6} \)
Multiply numerator and denominator by 10 to remove decimal from denominator:
\( \frac{0.0768 \times 10}{1.6 \times 10} = \frac{0.768}{16} \)
Perform regular division:
\( 0.768 \div 16 = 0.048 \)
Answer (a): \( 0.048 \)
(b) Simplify: \( 4.5 \times 4.5 - 3.2 \times 3.2 + 2.5 \times 4 \) [3 Marks]
Solution (b):
According to BODMAS, perform Multiplication first:
\( 4.5 \times 4.5 = 20.25 \)
\( 3.2 \times 3.2 = 10.24 \)
\( 2.5 \times 4 = 10.0 \)
Substitute these values back into the expression:
\( 20.25 - 10.24 + 10.0 \)
Next, perform Addition (group positive terms):
\( (20.25 + 10.0) - 10.24 \)
\( = 30.25 - 10.24 \)
Finally, perform Subtraction:
\( 30.25 - 10.24 = 20.01 \)
Answer (b): \( 20.01 \)
