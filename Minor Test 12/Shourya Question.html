Minor Test 12
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly showing necessary working.
  • Topic: Ratio and Proportion (Level: Easy).
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the ratio \( 45 : 108 \) in its simplest form.
2.
Find the ratio of \( 40 \) cm to \( 1.2 \) m.
3.
Check whether the numbers \( 4, 6, 8, \) and \( 12 \) are in proportion.
4.
Divide ₹ \( 150 \) between Amit and Sumit in the ratio \( 2 : 3 \).
5.
Define 'Proportion' and write the rule relating the product of extremes and the product of means.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
The length and breadth of a rectangular field are \( 50 \) m and \( 15 \) m respectively. Find the ratio of the length to the breadth, and the breadth to the perimeter.
7.
Find the value of \( x \) if \( 3 : x :: 12 : 20 \).
8.
Out of \( 40 \) students in a class, \( 16 \) are girls and the rest are boys. Find the ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls.
9.
Two numbers are in the ratio \( 3 : 5 \). If their sum is \( 96 \), find the two numbers.
10.
The cost of \( 5 \) identical pens is ₹ \( 120 \). Find the cost of \( 8 \) such pens.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A piece of wire \( 84 \) cm long is cut into three pieces in the ratio \( 3 : 4 : 5 \). Find the length of each piece of the wire.
12.
In a school, there are \( 3200 \) students. If the ratio of boys to girls is \( 5 : 3 \), find:
(i) the total number of boys in the school.
(ii) the total number of girls in the school.
13.
The present ages of A and B are in the ratio \( 7 : 9 \). If the sum of their ages is \( 48 \) years, what are their actual ages? What will be the ratio of their ages after \( 3 \) years?
14.
(a) Are \( 30, 40, 45, \) and \( 60 \) in proportion? Show your working.
(b) The first, second, and fourth terms of a proportion are \( 12, 21, \) and \( 14 \) respectively. Find the third term.
15.
In a mixture of \( 60 \) liters, the ratio of milk to water is \( 2 : 1 \).
(i) How many liters of milk and water are present in the mixture initially?
(ii) If \( 10 \) liters of water is added to the mixture, what will be the new ratio of milk to water?
