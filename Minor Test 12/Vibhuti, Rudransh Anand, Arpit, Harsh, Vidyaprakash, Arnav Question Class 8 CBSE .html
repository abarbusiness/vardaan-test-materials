Minor Test 12: Algebraic Expressions & Identities
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all essential steps for questions in Section B and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the number of terms and the coefficient of \( x^2 \) in the expression: \( 7x^2y - 4xy^2 + \frac{5}{2}z^2 - x^2 \).
2.
Find the product of the following monomials: \( (-4p^3) \) and \( (7pq) \).
3.
Using a suitable standard identity, evaluate: \( (99)^2 \).
4.
State the four standard algebraic identities.
5.
Simplify the expression: \( 3x(4x - 5) + 3 \) and find its value for \( x = 3 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Add the following algebraic expressions:
\( 7xy + 5yz - 3zx \), \( 4yz + 9zx - 4y \), and \( -3xz + 5x - 2xy \).
7.
Subtract \( 3pq(p - q) \) from \( 2pq(p + q) \). Show all steps clearly.
8.
Using the identity \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \), evaluate the following:
(i) \( (4p - 3q)^2 \)
(ii) \( (4.9)^2 \)
9.
Find the area of a rectangle whose length is \( (4a^2 - 3ab) \) and breadth is \( (2ab) \). Also, evaluate the area if \( a = 1 \) and \( b = 2 \).
10.
Using the identity \( (x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab \), find the product of \( (501 \times 502) \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Multiply the binomials: \( (2x + 5) \) and \( (4x - 3) \). (2 Marks)

(b) Simplify the following expression:
\( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \) (3 Marks)
12.
(a) Using Identity III i.e. \( (a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2 \), evaluate: \( (983)^2 - (17)^2 \). (2 Marks)

(b) Show that L.H.S = R.H.S for the following equation:
\( (9p - 5q)^2 + 180pq = (9p + 5q)^2 \) (3 Marks)
13.
(a) The volume of a rectangular cuboidal box is given by length \( \times \) breadth \( \times \) height. Find the volume of the box if its dimensions are \( 5a \), \( 3a^2 \), and \( 7a^4 \). (2 Marks)

(b) Simplify using appropriate identities:
\( (x^2 - 5)(x + 5) + 25 \) (3 Marks)
