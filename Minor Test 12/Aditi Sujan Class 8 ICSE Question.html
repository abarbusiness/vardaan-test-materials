Minor Test 12: Profit, Loss & GST
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all essential steps and calculations clearly in Section B and C.
  • Use of calculator is not permitted. Write your answers neatly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the profit or loss percent if the Cost Price (CP) of an article is \( \text{₹ } 400 \) and the Selling Price (SP) is \( \text{₹ } 460 \).
2.
A shopkeeper bought a chair for \( \text{₹ } 800 \) and spent \( \text{₹ } 50 \) on its transportation. If he sells it for \( \text{₹ } 1000 \), find his total profit.
3.
Define Goods and Services Tax (GST). Name its two main components that are applicable on an intra-state sale (sale within the same state).
4.
Calculate the selling price of a bag if its Marked Price (MP) is \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) and the shopkeeper offers a discount of \( 10\% \).
5.
Raj buys a toy whose price is \( \text{₹ } 500 \). If the rate of GST is \( 12\% \) and the transaction is within the same state, calculate the amount of CGST and SGST he has to pay.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
By selling a watch for \( \text{₹ } 1440 \), a man loses \( 10\% \). At what price should he sell the watch to gain \( 10\% \)?
7.
A dealer buys an article for \( \text{₹ } 6000 \). He marks the price at \( 20\% \) above the cost price and then allows a discount of \( 10\% \). Find the final Selling Price and his Profit percentage.
8.
The selling price of \( 10 \) pens is equal to the cost price of \( 12 \) pens. Find the gain percent for the shopkeeper.
9.
Rahul buys a pair of sports shoes marked at \( \text{₹ } 1500 \) (exclusive of tax). If the GST charged on shoes is \( 18\% \), find the total bill amount Rahul has to pay to the cashier.
10.
A shopkeeper allows a discount of \( 15\% \) on the marked price of a winter jacket. If the customer pays \( \text{₹ } 1700 \) as the selling price, calculate the original marked price of the jacket.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A grocer buys \( 40 \text{ kg} \) of rice at \( \text{₹ } 50 \) per kg. He sells half of the quantity at a profit of \( 10\% \) and the remaining half at a loss of \( 5\% \).

(a) Find the total cost price of the rice. (1 Mark)
(b) Calculate the total selling price of the entire quantity. (3 Marks)
(c) Find his overall profit or loss percentage on the whole transaction. (1 Mark)
12.
The marked price of a television is \( \text{₹ } 24,000 \). The electronics dealer offers two successive discounts of \( 10\% \) and \( 5\% \) during a festival sale.

(a) Calculate the final discounted selling price of the television. (3 Marks)
(b) If an intra-state GST of \( 12\% \) is applied on this final discounted price, what is the total bill amount paid by the customer? (2 Marks)
13.
Mrs. Gupta visits a departmental store in her city and buys the following items:
  • Medicines costing \( \text{₹ } 800 \) (Rate of GST is \( 5\% \))
  • Cosmetics costing \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) (Rate of GST is \( 18\% \))
Assuming both are intra-state transactions, calculate:

(a) The total CGST and total SGST paid by her. (3 Marks)
(b) The final bill amount paid by Mrs. Gupta to the store. (2 Marks)
