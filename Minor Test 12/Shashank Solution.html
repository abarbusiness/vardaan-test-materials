SECTION A (10 Marks)

1.
Evaluate the following expression involving integers:
\( -15 + 8 - (-12) \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
\begin{align*} &= -15 + 8 - (-12) \\ &= -15 + 8 + 12 \quad \text{(Since } - \times - = + \text{)} \\ &= -7 + 12 \\ &= \mathbf{5} \end{align*}
2.
Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 84 using the prime factorization method.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Finding the prime factors of each number:
\begin{align*} 36 &= 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \\ 84 &= 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7 \end{align*}
To find the HCF, take the product of the lowest powers of common prime factors:
\begin{align*} \text{HCF} &= 2^2 \times 3 \\ \text{HCF} &= 4 \times 3 = \mathbf{12} \end{align*}
3.
Express the fraction \( \frac{18}{48} \) in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Find the HCF of the numerator (18) and denominator (48).
HCF of 18 and 48 is 6.
Divide both numerator and denominator by their HCF:
\begin{align*} \frac{18 \div 6}{48 \div 6} = \mathbf{\frac{3}{8}} \end{align*}
4.
Write an algebraic expression for the statement: "Five times a number \( x \) decreased by 7."
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Five times a number \( x \) is \( 5x \).
Decreased by 7 means subtracting 7.
Therefore, the expression is: \( 5x - 7 \)
5.
Find the supplementary angle of \( 45^\circ \).
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Supplementary angles add up to \( 180^\circ \).
\begin{align*} \text{Supplementary angle} &= 180^\circ - \text{given angle} \\ &= 180^\circ - 45^\circ \\ &= \mathbf{135^\circ} \end{align*}

6.
Simplify the following mixed fractions:
\( 3\frac{1}{2} + 2\frac{3}{4} - 1\frac{5}{8} \)
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Convert mixed fractions to improper fractions.
\begin{align*} 3\frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{2}, \quad 2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}, \quad 1\frac{5}{8} = \frac{13}{8} \end{align*}
Step 2: Find the LCM of denominators 2, 4, and 8, which is 8.
Step 3: Make denominators common and solve.
\begin{align*} &= \frac{7 \times 4}{2 \times 4} + \frac{11 \times 2}{4 \times 2} - \frac{13 \times 1}{8 \times 1} \\ &= \frac{28}{8} + \frac{22}{8} - \frac{13}{8} \\ &= \frac{28 + 22 - 13}{8} \\ &= \frac{50 - 13}{8} = \frac{37}{8} \end{align*}
Step 4: Convert back to a mixed fraction. \( = 4\frac{5}{8} \)
7.
A rope of length 15.6 meters is cut into 4 equal pieces. Find the exact length of each piece in meters and centimeters.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Total length of the rope = \( 15.6 \) m
Number of pieces = 4
Length of one piece = \( \frac{15.6}{4} \)
\begin{align*} \frac{15.6}{4} &= 3.9 \text{ meters} \end{align*}
To convert to meters and centimeters:
\( 3.9 \) m = \( 3 \) m + \( 0.9 \) m
Since \( 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} \),
\( 0.9 \text{ m} = 0.9 \times 100 \text{ cm} = 90 \text{ cm} \).
Length of each piece is 3 meters and 90 centimeters.
8.
Solve the following linear equation for \( x \) and verify your answer:
\( 3x + 5 = 20 \)
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Solving for x:
\begin{align*} 3x + 5 &= 20 \\ 3x &= 20 - 5 \\ 3x &= 15 \\ x &= \frac{15}{3} \\ \mathbf{x} &\mathbf{= 5} \end{align*}
Verification:
Substitute \( x = 5 \) into the Left Hand Side (LHS):
\begin{align*} \text{LHS} &= 3(5) + 5 \\ &= 15 + 5 \\ &= 20 \end{align*}
Since LHS = Right Hand Side (RHS) = 20, the solution is verified.
9.
In a class of 40 students, \( \frac{3}{5} \) of the students are boys. Find the total number of girls in the class.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Total number of students = 40
Fraction of boys = \( \frac{3}{5} \)

Method 1:
Number of boys = \( \frac{3}{5} \times 40 = 3 \times 8 = 24 \) boys.
Number of girls = Total students - Number of boys
Number of girls = \( 40 - 24 = \mathbf{16} \)

Method 2:
Fraction of girls = \( 1 - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5} \)
Number of girls = \( \frac{2}{5} \times 40 = 2 \times 8 = \mathbf{16} \)

SECTION C (20 Marks)

11.
Simplify the following mathematical expression strictly using the BODMAS rule:
\( 14 - \left[ 3 + \{8 \div (4 - 2)\} \times 2 \right] \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
According to BODMAS, we solve the inner Brackets first (Round, then Curly, then Square), followed by Of, Division, Multiplication, Addition, and Subtraction.
\begin{align*} &14 - \left[ 3 + \{8 \div \mathbf{(4 - 2)}\} \times 2 \right] \\ &= 14 - \left[ 3 + \mathbf{\{8 \div 2\}} \times 2 \right] \quad \text{(Solved round bracket)} \\ &= 14 - \left[ 3 + \mathbf{4 \times 2} \right] \quad \text{(Solved curly bracket)} \\ &= 14 - \left[ \mathbf{3 + 8} \right] \quad \text{(Multiplication inside square bracket)} \\ &= 14 - \mathbf{11} \quad \text{(Solved square bracket)} \\ &= \mathbf{3} \end{align*}
12.
Find the greatest number which divides 62, 78, and 109 leaving remainders 2, 3, and 4 respectively.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Since remainders of 2, 3, and 4 are left, we must first subtract these remainders from the respective numbers to find the numbers that are exactly divisible.
\begin{align*} 62 - 2 &= 60 \\ 78 - 3 &= 75 \\ 109 - 4 &= 105 \end{align*}
The required greatest number will be the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 60, 75, and 105.

Prime factorizations:
\begin{align*} 60 &= 2 \times 2 \times \mathbf{3} \times \mathbf{5} = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5 \\ 75 &= \mathbf{3} \times \mathbf{5} \times 5 = 3 \times 5^2 \\ 105 &= \mathbf{3} \times \mathbf{5} \times 7 = 3 \times 5 \times 7 \end{align*}
The common prime factors are 3 and 5.
\begin{align*} \text{HCF} &= 3 \times 5 = \mathbf{15} \end{align*}
The greatest number is 15.
13.
Answer the following algebraic parts:
(a) Subtract the sum of \( 2x - 3y + 4z \) and \( 3x + 2y - 5z \) from \( 8x - y + z \).
(b) Evaluate the expression \( 2a^2 - 3ab + b^2 \) when \( a = 2 \) and \( b = -1 \).
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (a) [2.5 Marks]
Step 1: Find the sum of the first two expressions.
\begin{align*} \text{Sum} &= (2x - 3y + 4z) + (3x + 2y - 5z) \\ &= (2x + 3x) + (-3y + 2y) + (4z - 5z) \\ &= 5x - y - z \end{align*}
Step 2: Subtract this sum from \( 8x - y + z \).
\begin{align*} &= (8x - y + z) - (5x - y - z) \\ &= 8x - y + z - 5x + y + z \\ &= (8x - 5x) + (-y + y) + (z + z) \\ &= \mathbf{3x + 2z} \end{align*}

Part (b) [2.5 Marks]
Substitute \( a = 2 \) and \( b = -1 \) into \( 2a^2 - 3ab + b^2 \).
\begin{align*} &= 2(2)^2 - 3(2)(-1) + (-1)^2 \\ &= 2(4) - (-6) + (1) \\ &= 8 + 6 + 1 \\ &= \mathbf{15} \end{align*}
14.
The length of a rectangular playing field is twice its breadth. If the perimeter of the field is 150 m, find the length and breadth of the field. Subsequently, calculate the total area of the field.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let the breadth of the rectangular field be \( b \) meters.
According to the question, the length \( (l) \) is twice the breadth.
Therefore, \( l = 2b \) meters.

Given Perimeter = 150 m.
Formula for perimeter of a rectangle = \( 2(l + b) \)
\begin{align*} 2(2b + b) &= 150 \\ 2(3b) &= 150 \\ 6b &= 150 \\ b &= \frac{150}{6} = \mathbf{25 \text{ m}} \end{align*}
Breadth (\( b \)) = 25 m.
Length (\( l \)) = \( 2 \times 25 \) = 50 m.

Calculation for Area:
Formula for Area = \( l \times b \)
\begin{align*} \text{Area} &= 50 \text{ m} \times 25 \text{ m} \\ &= \mathbf{1250 \text{ sq. m}} \end{align*}
