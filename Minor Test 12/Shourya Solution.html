Detailed Solutions - Minor Test 12 | Class 6 ICSE | Ratio & Proportion

SECTION A (10 Marks)
1. Express the ratio \( 45 : 108 \) in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]

Solution:

Ratio = \( \frac{45}{108} \)
Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 45 and 108.
Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45.
Factors of 108: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 27, 36, 54, 108.
HCF = 9.
Divide both numerator and denominator by 9:
\( \frac{45 \div 9}{108 \div 9} = \frac{5}{12} \)
Simplest form = 5 : 12
2. Find the ratio of \( 40 \) cm to \( 1.2 \) m.
[2 Marks]

Solution:

To find the ratio, both quantities must have the same unit.
Convert meters to centimeters: \( 1 \) m = \( 100 \) cm
\( 1.2 \) m = \( 1.2 \times 100 \) cm = \( 120 \) cm
Ratio = \( \frac{40 \text{ cm}}{120 \text{ cm}} = \frac{40}{120} = \frac{1}{3} \)
Ratio = 1 : 3
3. Check whether the numbers \( 4, 6, 8, \) and \( 12 \) are in proportion.
[2 Marks]

Solution:

Four numbers \(a, b, c, d\) are in proportion if \(a : b = c : d\).
Ratio of first two terms = \( \frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3} \)
Ratio of last two terms = \( \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3} \)
Since \( \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3} \), the ratios are equal.
Alternative method: Product of extremes (\(4 \times 12 = 48\)) = Product of means (\(6 \times 8 = 48\)).
Yes, they are in proportion.
4. Divide ₹ \( 150 \) between Amit and Sumit in the ratio \( 2 : 3 \).
[2 Marks]

Solution:

Total amount = ₹ \( 150 \)
Sum of the ratios = \( 2 + 3 = 5 \)
Amit's share = \( \frac{2}{5} \times 150 = 2 \times 30 = \text{₹ } 60 \)
Sumit's share = \( \frac{3}{5} \times 150 = 3 \times 30 = \text{₹ } 90 \)
Amit gets ₹ 60 and Sumit gets ₹ 90.
5. Define 'Proportion' and write the rule relating the product of extremes and the product of means.
[2 Marks]

Solution:

Proportion: An equation stating that two ratios are equal is called a proportion. (e.g., \(a:b = c:d\))
Rule: In a true proportion, the product of the extreme terms (outer terms) is equal to the product of the mean terms (inner terms).
Product of Extremes = Product of Means
SECTION B (15 Marks)
6. The length and breadth of a rectangular field are \( 50 \) m and \( 15 \) m respectively. Find the ratio of the length to the breadth, and the breadth to the perimeter.
[3 Marks]

Solution:

Length (\(L\)) = \( 50 \) m, Breadth (\(B\)) = \( 15 \) m.
Part 1: Ratio of Length to Breadth = \( \frac{50}{15} = \frac{10}{3} \) (Dividing by 5).
Ratio = \( 10 : 3 \)
Part 2: Perimeter = \( 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (50 + 15) = 2 \times 65 = 130 \) m.
Ratio of Breadth to Perimeter = \( \frac{15}{130} = \frac{3}{26} \) (Dividing by 5).
Length : Breadth = 10 : 3
Breadth : Perimeter = 3 : 26
7. Find the value of \( x \) if \( 3 : x :: 12 : 20 \).
[3 Marks]

Solution:

Given proportion: \( 3 : x = 12 : 20 \)
Using the rule: Product of Extremes = Product of Means
\( 3 \times 20 = x \times 12 \)
\( 60 = 12x \)
\( x = \frac{60}{12} \)
\( x = 5 \)
\( x = 5 \)
8. Out of \( 40 \) students in a class, \( 16 \) are girls and the rest are boys. Find the ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls.
[3 Marks]

Solution:

Total number of students = \( 40 \)
Number of girls = \( 16 \)
Number of boys = Total students - Number of girls = \( 40 - 16 = 24 \)
Ratio of Boys to Girls = \( \frac{\text{Number of Boys}}{\text{Number of Girls}} = \frac{24}{16} \)
Dividing numerator and denominator by 8:
\( \frac{24 \div 8}{16 \div 8} = \frac{3}{2} \)
Ratio = 3 : 2
9. Two numbers are in the ratio \( 3 : 5 \). If their sum is \( 96 \), find the two numbers.
[3 Marks]

Solution:

Let the common multiple be \( x \).
Then, the two numbers are \( 3x \) and \( 5x \).
According to the question, their sum is \( 96 \).
\( 3x + 5x = 96 \)
\( 8x = 96 \)
\( x = \frac{96}{8} \)
\( x = 12 \)
First number = \( 3x = 3 \times 12 = 36 \)
Second number = \( 5x = 5 \times 12 = 60 \)
The numbers are 36 and 60.
10. The cost of \( 5 \) identical pens is ₹ \( 120 \). Find the cost of \( 8 \) such pens.
[3 Marks]

Solution:

Using the Unitary Method:
Cost of \( 5 \) pens = ₹ \( 120 \)
Cost of \( 1 \) pen = \( \frac{120}{5} = \text{₹ } 24 \)
Cost of \( 8 \) pens = \( 8 \times 24 = \text{₹ } 192 \)
The cost of 8 pens is ₹ 192.
SECTION C (25 Marks)
11. A piece of wire \( 84 \) cm long is cut into three pieces in the ratio \( 3 : 4 : 5 \). Find the length of each piece of the wire.
[5 Marks]

Solution:

Total length of the wire = \( 84 \) cm
Ratio of the pieces = \( 3 : 4 : 5 \)
Sum of the ratio parts = \( 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 \) parts
Length of first piece = \( \left(\frac{3}{12}\right) \times 84 = 3 \times 7 = 21 \) cm
Length of second piece = \( \left(\frac{4}{12}\right) \times 84 = 4 \times 7 = 28 \) cm
Length of third piece = \( \left(\frac{5}{12}\right) \times 84 = 5 \times 7 = 35 \) cm
Verification: 21 + 28 + 35 = 84 cm.
The lengths are 21 cm, 28 cm, and 35 cm.
12. In a school, there are \( 3200 \) students. If the ratio of boys to girls is \( 5 : 3 \), find: (i) the total number of boys, (ii) the total number of girls.
[5 Marks]

Solution:

Total number of students = \( 3200 \)
Ratio of boys to girls = \( 5 : 3 \)
Total ratio parts = \( 5 + 3 = 8 \) parts
(i) Number of boys:
\( \text{Boys} = \left(\frac{5}{8}\right) \times 3200 = 5 \times 400 = 2000 \)
(ii) Number of girls:
\( \text{Girls} = \left(\frac{3}{8}\right) \times 3200 = 3 \times 400 = 1200 \)
(i) Boys = 2000    (ii) Girls = 1200
13. The present ages of A and B are in the ratio \( 7 : 9 \). If the sum of their ages is \( 48 \) years, what are their actual ages? What will be the ratio of their ages after \( 3 \) years?
[5 Marks]

Solution:

Let present age of A be \( 7x \) and present age of B be \( 9x \).
Sum of their ages = \( 48 \)
\( 7x + 9x = 48 \)
\( 16x = 48 \)
\( x = \frac{48}{16} = 3 \)
Present age of A = \( 7 \times 3 = 21 \) years
Present age of B = \( 9 \times 3 = 27 \) years

After 3 years:
Age of A will be \( 21 + 3 = 24 \) years
Age of B will be \( 27 + 3 = 30 \) years
New Ratio = \( 24 : 30 = \frac{24}{30} = \frac{4}{5} \) (Dividing by 6)
Present ages: A=21 yrs, B=27 yrs.
Ratio after 3 years = 4 : 5.
14. (a) Are \( 30, 40, 45, \) and \( 60 \) in proportion? (b) The first, second, and fourth terms of a proportion are \( 12, 21, \) and \( 14 \) respectively. Find the third term.
[5 Marks]

Solution:

(a) Checking Proportion:
Product of extremes = \( 30 \times 60 = 1800 \)
Product of means = \( 40 \times 45 = 1800 \)
Since \( 1800 = 1800 \), Yes, they are in proportion. (2 Marks)

(b) Finding third term:
Let the third term be \( x \).
The terms are in proportion: \( 12 : 21 :: x : 14 \)
Product of extremes = Product of means
\( 12 \times 14 = 21 \times x \)
\( 168 = 21x \)
\( x = \frac{168}{21} = 8 \)
(a) Yes    (b) Third term = 8
15. In a mixture of \( 60 \) liters, the ratio of milk to water is \( 2 : 1 \). (i) How many liters of milk and water are present? (ii) If \( 10 \) liters of water is added, what will be the new ratio?
[5 Marks]

Solution:

(i) Initial Mixture:
Total volume = \( 60 \) liters. Ratio = \( 2 : 1 \). Sum of parts = \( 3 \).
Volume of Milk = \( \left(\frac{2}{3}\right) \times 60 = 40 \) liters.
Volume of Water = \( \left(\frac{1}{3}\right) \times 60 = 20 \) liters.

(ii) After adding water:
Water added = \( 10 \) liters.
New volume of water = \( 20 + 10 = 30 \) liters.
Volume of milk remains the same = \( 40 \) liters.
New Ratio of Milk to Water = \( \frac{40}{30} = \frac{4}{3} \)
(i) Milk = 40 L, Water = 20 L.
(ii) New Ratio = 4 : 3
