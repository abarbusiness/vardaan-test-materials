SECTION A
Q1. Evaluate the following integer expression: \( (-15) + (+23) - (-12) \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given expression: \( (-15) + (+23) - (-12) \)
Step 1: Simplify the signs.
\( = -15 + 23 + 12 \)
Step 2: Add the positive integers.
\( = -15 + 35 \)
Step 3: Subtract.
\( = 20 \)
Final Answer: 20
Q2. Convert the decimal \( 3.075 \) into a fraction in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Write the decimal as a fraction with denominator 1000.
\( 3.075 = \frac{3075}{1000} \)
Step 2: Simplify by dividing the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor (HCF).
Divide by 25: \( \frac{3075 \div 25}{1000 \div 25} = \frac{123}{40} \)
Step 3: Convert to mixed fraction (optional but recommended).
\( \frac{123}{40} = 3 \frac{3}{40} \)
Final Answer: \( \frac{123}{40} \) or \( 3 \frac{3}{40} \)
Q3. Find the ratio of 45 minutes to 1.5 hours in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
To find the ratio, both quantities must have the same units.
Convert 1.5 hours to minutes:
\( 1 \text{ hour} = 60 \text{ minutes} \)
\( 1.5 \text{ hours} = 1.5 \times 60 = 90 \text{ minutes} \)
Now, Ratio = \( \frac{45 \text{ minutes}}{90 \text{ minutes}} \)
Ratio = \( \frac{45}{90} = \frac{1}{2} \)
Final Answer: \( 1 : 2 \)
Q4. Arrange the following integers in ascending order: \( -8,\ 0,\ -12,\ 5,\ -2 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Ascending order means from smallest to largest.
Negative numbers with larger absolute values are smaller.
Smallest is \( -12 \), followed by \( -8 \), then \( -2 \).
Then comes \( 0 \), and the largest is \( 5 \).
Final Answer: \( -12,\ -8,\ -2,\ 0,\ 5 \)
Q5. If the numbers \( 3,\ x,\ 15,\ 25 \) are in proportion, find the value of \( x \).
[2 Marks]
Solution:
If numbers are in proportion, then:
Product of extremes = Product of means
\( 3 \times 25 = x \times 15 \)
\( 75 = 15x \)
\( x = \frac{75}{15} \)
\( x = 5 \)
Final Answer: \( x = 5 \)
SECTION B
Q6. A rectangular field has a length of \( 42.5 \text{ m} \) and a breadth of \( 18.6 \text{ m} \). Calculate its perimeter and area.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Length (\( l \)) = \( 42.5 \text{ m} \), Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 18.6 \text{ m} \)

Part 1: Perimeter (2 Marks)
Perimeter = \( 2 \times (l + b) \)
\( = 2 \times (42.5 + 18.6) \)
\( = 2 \times 61.1 \)
\( = 122.2 \text{ m} \)

Part 2: Area (2 Marks)
Area = \( l \times b \)
\( = 42.5 \times 18.6 \)
\( = 790.5 \text{ m}^2 \)
Final Answer: Perimeter = \( 122.2 \text{ m} \), Area = \( 790.5 \text{ m}^2 \)
Q7. Subtract the sum of \( -345 \) and \( 120 \) from the difference of \( 45 \) and \( -110 \).
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Find the sum of \( -345 \) and \( 120 \).
Sum = \( (-345) + 120 = -225 \)

Step 2: Find the difference of \( 45 \) and \( -110 \).
Difference = \( 45 - (-110) = 45 + 110 = 155 \)

Step 3: Subtract the Sum from the Difference.
Result = Difference - Sum
\( = 155 - (-225) \)
\( = 155 + 225 \)
\( = 380 \)
Final Answer: 380
Q8. Two numbers are in the ratio \( 5 : 7 \). If the sum of the numbers is \( 108 \), find the two numbers.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Let the common ratio multiplier be \( x \).
Then, the two numbers are \( 5x \) and \( 7x \).
Given, their sum is 108.
\( 5x + 7x = 108 \)
\( 12x = 108 \)
\( x = \frac{108}{12} \)
\( x = 9 \)

Now, find the numbers:
First number = \( 5x = 5 \times 9 = 45 \)
Second number = \( 7x = 7 \times 9 = 63 \)
Final Answer: The numbers are 45 and 63.
Q9. Simplify the following decimal expression using BODMAS:
\( 18.5 - [6.4 - \{3.2 + (5.6 - 3.1)\}] \)
[4 Marks]
Solution:
According to BODMAS, solve innermost brackets first (round brackets).
\( = 18.5 - [6.4 - \{3.2 + (2.5)\}] \)
Next, solve the curly brackets.
\( = 18.5 - [6.4 - \{5.7\}] \)
Next, solve the square brackets.
\( = 18.5 - [0.7] \)
Finally, perform subtraction.
\( = 18.5 - 0.7 = 17.8 \)
Final Answer: 17.8
Q10. A car travels \( 165 \text{ km} \) in 3 hours at a uniform speed.
(a) How long will it take to travel \( 440 \text{ km} \)?
(b) How far will it travel in 7 hours?
[4 Marks]
Solution:
First, find the uniform speed of the car.
Speed = \( \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{165}{3} = 55 \text{ km/hr} \).

(a) Time to travel 440 km:
Time = \( \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} \)
Time = \( \frac{440}{55} = 8 \text{ hours} \).

(b) Distance traveled in 7 hours:
Distance = Speed \( \times \) Time
Distance = \( 55 \times 7 = 385 \text{ km} \).
Final Answer: (a) 8 hours, (b) 385 km
SECTION C
Q11. In a competitive examination, 5 marks are awarded for every correct answer, \( -2 \) marks for every incorrect answer, and 0 marks for questions not attempted.
(a) Rahul attempts all questions. He gets 12 correct and 8 incorrect answers. What is his total score?
(b) Riya scores 20 marks in the same exam. If she got 10 correct answers, how many questions did she guess incorrectly?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Rahul's Score: (2.5 Marks)
Marks for 12 correct answers = \( 12 \times 5 = 60 \)
Marks for 8 incorrect answers = \( 8 \times (-2) = -16 \)
Total Score = \( 60 + (-16) = 60 - 16 = 44 \)

(b) Riya's Incorrect Answers: (2.5 Marks)
Riya's marks for 10 correct answers = \( 10 \times 5 = 50 \)
Total score obtained by Riya = 20
Marks obtained for incorrect answers = Total Score - Marks for correct answers
\( = 20 - 50 = -30 \)
Since each incorrect answer penalizes 2 marks, number of incorrect answers = \( \frac{-30}{-2} = 15 \)
Final Answer: (a) Rahul's score is 44. (b) Riya guessed 15 questions incorrectly.
Q12. The cost of \( 15.5 \text{ kg} \) of apples is Rs. \( 1317.50 \).
(a) Find the cost of \( 1 \text{ kg} \) of apples.
(b) Find the quantity of apples that can be purchased for Rs. \( 595 \).
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Cost of 1 kg of apples: (2.5 Marks)
Cost of 15.5 kg = Rs. 1317.50
Cost of 1 kg = \( \frac{1317.50}{15.5} \)
Convert decimals to whole numbers by multiplying numerator and denominator by 10:
\( = \frac{13175}{155} \)
Dividing, we get \( = \text{Rs. } 85 \)

(b) Quantity purchased for Rs. 595: (2.5 Marks)
Apples purchased for Rs. 85 = 1 kg
Apples purchased for Rs. 595 = \( \frac{595}{85} \)
Dividing, we get \( = 7 \text{ kg} \)
Final Answer: (a) Cost of 1 kg is Rs. 85. (b) 7 kg can be purchased for Rs. 595.
Q13. The ratio of the monthly income to the monthly expenditure of a family is \( 9 : 7 \). If their monthly savings is Rs. \( 7200 \):
(a) What is their total monthly income?
(b) What is their total monthly expenditure?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let the monthly income be \( 9x \) and the monthly expenditure be \( 7x \).
We know that: Income = Expenditure + Savings
Therefore, Savings = Income - Expenditure
Savings = \( 9x - 7x = 2x \)

Given, Savings = Rs. 7200
\( 2x = 7200 \)
\( x = \frac{7200}{2} = 3600 \)

(a) Total Monthly Income:
Income = \( 9x = 9 \times 3600 = \text{Rs. } 32,400 \)

(b) Total Monthly Expenditure:
Expenditure = \( 7x = 7 \times 3600 = \text{Rs. } 25,200 \)
(Check: 32400 - 25200 = 7200)
Final Answer: (a) Income = Rs. 32,400, (b) Expenditure = Rs. 25,200
Q14. A piece of electrical wire measuring \( 14.4 \text{ m} \) in length is cut into two pieces in the ratio \( 3 : 5 \).
(a) Find the length of each piece in meters.
(b) Express the length of the smaller piece in centimeters as an integer.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Length of each piece in meters: (3 Marks)
Total ratio parts = \( 3 + 5 = 8 \) parts.
Total length = \( 14.4 \text{ m} \)
Value of one part = \( \frac{14.4}{8} = 1.8 \text{ m} \)
Length of first piece = \( 3 \times 1.8 = 5.4 \text{ m} \)
Length of second piece = \( 5 \times 1.8 = 9.0 \text{ m} \)

(b) Smaller piece in centimeters: (2 Marks)
The smaller piece measures \( 5.4 \text{ m} \).
We know \( 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} \).
Length in cm = \( 5.4 \times 100 = 540 \text{ cm} \).
Final Answer: (a) Pieces are 5.4 m and 9.0 m. (b) Smaller piece is 540 cm.
