SECTION A
1. Expand using a suitable identity: \( (x + 3)(x + 3) \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
The expression can be written as \( (x + 3)^2 \).
Using the identity: \( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \)
Here, \( a = x \) and \( b = 3 \).
\begin{align*} (x + 3)^2 &= (x)^2 + 2(x)(3) + (3)^2 \\ &= x^2 + 6x + 9 \end{align*}
Answer: \( x^2 + 6x + 9 \)
2. Factorise: \( y^2 - 49 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
The expression can be written as: \( (y)^2 - (7)^2 \)
Using the identity: \( a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b) \)
Here, \( a = y \) and \( b = 7 \).
\begin{align*} y^2 - 49 = (y + 7)(y - 7) \end{align*}
Answer: \( (y + 7)(y - 7) \)
3. Evaluate the product without multiplying directly: \( 105 \times 106 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
We can rewrite the numbers as: \( 105 = 100 + 5 \) and \( 106 = 100 + 6 \)
Using the identity: \( (x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab \)
Here, \( x = 100 \), \( a = 5 \), and \( b = 6 \).
\begin{align*} (100 + 5)(100 + 6) &= (100)^2 + (5 + 6)(100) + (5 \times 6) \\ &= 10000 + (11)(100) + 30 \\ &= 10000 + 1100 + 30 \\ &= 11130 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 11130 \)
4. Expand: \( (2x - y + z)^2 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Using the identity: \( (a + b + c)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 2ab + 2bc + 2ca \)
Here, \( a = 2x \), \( b = -y \), and \( c = z \).
\begin{align*} (2x - y + z)^2 &= (2x)^2 + (-y)^2 + (z)^2 + 2(2x)(-y) + 2(-y)(z) + 2(z)(2x) \\ &= 4x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 4xy - 2yz + 4zx \end{align*}
Answer: \( 4x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 4xy - 2yz + 4zx \)
5. Write the expanded form: \( (2a + 3b)^3 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Using the identity: \( (x + y)^3 = x^3 + y^3 + 3xy(x + y) \)
Here, \( x = 2a \) and \( y = 3b \).
\begin{align*} (2a + 3b)^3 &= (2a)^3 + (3b)^3 + 3(2a)(3b)(2a + 3b) \\ &= 8a^3 + 27b^3 + 18ab(2a + 3b) \\ &= 8a^3 + 27b^3 + 36a^2b + 54ab^2 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 8a^3 + 27b^3 + 36a^2b + 54ab^2 \)
SECTION B
6. Evaluate \( (998)^2 \) using a suitable identity.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can write \( 998 \) as \( (1000 - 2) \).
Using the identity: \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \)
Here, \( a = 1000 \) and \( b = 2 \).
\begin{align*} (1000 - 2)^2 &= (1000)^2 - 2(1000)(2) + (2)^2 \\ &= 1000000 - 4000 + 4 \\ &= 996000 + 4 \\ &= 996004 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 996004 \)
7. Factorise: \( 4x^2 + 9y^2 + 16z^2 + 12xy - 24yz - 16xz \)
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We use the identity: \( a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 2ab + 2bc + 2ca = (a + b + c)^2 \)
Notice that the terms with \( yz \) and \( xz \) are negative, which means the term involving \( z \) is negative.
Let's rewrite the given expression:
\( 4x^2 = (2x)^2 \)
\( 9y^2 = (3y)^2 \)
\( 16z^2 = (-4z)^2 \) (Taking z term as negative)
Checking the middle terms:
\( 2ab = 2(2x)(3y) = 12xy \)
\( 2bc = 2(3y)(-4z) = -24yz \)
\( 2ca = 2(-4z)(2x) = -16xz \)
Since it perfectly matches the identity, we have:
\begin{align*} (2x)^2 + (3y)^2 + (-4z)^2 + 2(2x)(3y) + 2(3y)(-4z) + 2(-4z)(2x) &= (2x + 3y - 4z)^2 \end{align*}
Answer: \( (2x + 3y - 4z)^2 \) or \( (2x + 3y - 4z)(2x + 3y - 4z) \)
8. If \( x + y = 12 \) and \( xy = 27 \), find the value of \( x^3 + y^3 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We know the identity: \( x^3 + y^3 = (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) \)
Alternatively, we can use: \( (x + y)^3 = x^3 + y^3 + 3xy(x + y) \)
Let's use the second one. Substitute the given values \( x + y = 12 \) and \( xy = 27 \):
\begin{align*} (12)^3 &= x^3 + y^3 + 3(27)(12) \\ 1728 &= x^3 + y^3 + 972 \\ x^3 + y^3 &= 1728 - 972 \\ x^3 + y^3 &= 756 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 756 \)
9. Factorise: \( 27y^3 + 125z^3 \)
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can rewrite the expression as perfect cubes:
\( 27y^3 = (3y)^3 \) and \( 125z^3 = (5z)^3 \)
Using the identity: \( a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) \)
Here, \( a = 3y \) and \( b = 5z \).
\begin{align*} (3y)^3 + (5z)^3 &= (3y + 5z)[(3y)^2 - (3y)(5z) + (5z)^2] \\ &= (3y + 5z)(9y^2 - 15yz + 25z^2) \end{align*}
Answer: \( (3y + 5z)(9y^2 - 15yz + 25z^2) \)
10. Evaluate \( (102)^3 \) using a suitable identity.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can write \( 102 \) as \( (100 + 2) \).
Using the identity: \( (a + b)^3 = a^3 + b^3 + 3ab(a + b) \)
Here, \( a = 100 \) and \( b = 2 \).
\begin{align*} (100 + 2)^3 &= (100)^3 + (2)^3 + 3(100)(2)(100 + 2) \\ &= 1000000 + 8 + 600(102) \\ &= 1000000 + 8 + 61200 \\ &= 1061208 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 1061208 \)
SECTION C
11. Factorise: \( 8x^3 + y^3 + 27z^3 - 18xyz \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let's write the given expression in the standard form.
\( 8x^3 = (2x)^3 \)
\( y^3 = (y)^3 \)
\( 27z^3 = (3z)^3 \)
And, \( 18xyz = 3(2x)(y)(3z) \)
The expression matches the identity:
\( a^3 + b^3 + c^3 - 3abc = (a + b + c)(a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - ab - bc - ca) \)
Here, \( a = 2x \), \( b = y \), and \( c = 3z \).
Applying the identity:
\begin{align*} (2x)^3 + (y)^3 + (3z)^3 - 3(2x)(y)(3z) &= (2x + y + 3z)[(2x)^2 + (y)^2 + (3z)^2 - (2x)(y) - (y)(3z) - (3z)(2x)] \\ &= (2x + y + 3z)(4x^2 + y^2 + 9z^2 - 2xy - 3yz - 6zx) \end{align*}
Answer: \( (2x + y + 3z)(4x^2 + y^2 + 9z^2 - 2xy - 3yz - 6zx) \)
12. (i) Expand: \( (x - \frac{2}{3}y)^3 \)
(ii) Factorise: \( 64a^3 - 27b^3 - 144a^2b + 108ab^2 \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i): [2.5 Marks]
Using identity \( (a - b)^3 = a^3 - b^3 - 3ab(a - b) \) or \( a^3 - b^3 - 3a^2b + 3ab^2 \)
Here, \( a = x \), \( b = \frac{2}{3}y \).
\begin{align*} \left(x - \frac{2}{3}y\right)^3 &= (x)^3 - \left(\frac{2}{3}y\right)^3 - 3(x)^2\left(\frac{2}{3}y\right) + 3(x)\left(\frac{2}{3}y\right)^2 \\ &= x^3 - \frac{8}{27}y^3 - 2x^2y + 3(x)\left(\frac{4}{9}y^2\right) \\ &= x^3 - \frac{8}{27}y^3 - 2x^2y + \frac{4}{3}xy^2 \end{align*}
Part (ii): [2.5 Marks]
We check if the expression is a perfect cube expansion \( (x - y)^3 = x^3 - y^3 - 3x^2y + 3xy^2 \).
\( 64a^3 = (4a)^3 \) and \( 27b^3 = (3b)^3 \). So, let \( x = 4a \) and \( y = 3b \).
Check middle terms:
\( -3x^2y = -3(4a)^2(3b) = -3(16a^2)(3b) = -144a^2b \)
\( +3xy^2 = 3(4a)(3b)^2 = 3(4a)(9b^2) = 108ab^2 \)
Since it matches exactly, it is the expansion of \( (4a - 3b)^3 \).
\begin{align*} 64a^3 - 27b^3 - 144a^2b + 108ab^2 = (4a - 3b)^3 \end{align*}
Answers: (i) \( x^3 - \frac{8}{27}y^3 - 2x^2y + \frac{4}{3}xy^2 \), (ii) \( (4a - 3b)(4a - 3b)(4a - 3b) \)
13. Without actually calculating the cubes, find the value of:
(i) \( (-12)^3 + (7)^3 + (5)^3 \)
(ii) \( (28)^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3 \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
We know the conditional identity:
If \( x + y + z = 0 \), then \( x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = 3xyz \).
Part (i): [2.5 Marks]
Let \( x = -12 \), \( y = 7 \), and \( z = 5 \).
Check sum: \( -12 + 7 + 5 = -12 + 12 = 0 \).
Since the sum is 0, we apply the identity:
\begin{align*} (-12)^3 + (7)^3 + (5)^3 &= 3(-12)(7)(5) \\ &= -36 \times 35 \\ &= -1260 \end{align*}
Part (ii): [2.5 Marks]
Let \( x = 28 \), \( y = -15 \), and \( z = -13 \).
Check sum: \( 28 + (-15) + (-13) = 28 - 28 = 0 \).
Since the sum is 0, we apply the identity:
\begin{align*} (28)^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3 &= 3(28)(-15)(-13) \\ &= 84 \times 195 \\ &= 16380 \end{align*}
Answers: (i) \( -1260 \), (ii) \( 16380 \)
14. Verify that: \( x^3 + y^3 = (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
We need to prove that LHS = RHS. We will start with the Right Hand Side (RHS).
RHS = \( (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) \)
Expand by multiplying each term of the first polynomial by each term of the second polynomial:
\begin{align*} \text{RHS} &= x(x^2 - xy + y^2) + y(x^2 - xy + y^2) \\ &= (x \cdot x^2 - x \cdot xy + x \cdot y^2) + (y \cdot x^2 - y \cdot xy + y \cdot y^2) \\ &= (x^3 - x^2y + xy^2) + (x^2y - xy^2 + y^3) \end{align*}
Now, group the like terms together:
\begin{align*} &= x^3 - x^2y + x^2y + xy^2 - xy^2 + y^3 \end{align*}
Notice that \( -x^2y \) and \( +x^2y \) cancel out. Similarly, \( +xy^2 \) and \( -xy^2 \) cancel out.
\begin{align*} &= x^3 + 0 + 0 + y^3 \\ &= x^3 + y^3 \end{align*}
Which is equal to the Left Hand Side (LHS).
Therefore, LHS = RHS. Hence Verified.
15. Find the following product using appropriate identities: \( (3x + 4)(3x - 5) \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
The expression is in the form of the identity:
\( (x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab \)
Comparing the given expression \( (3x + 4)(3x - 5) \) with the identity:
Let the variable part be \( X = 3x \).
Let the constant terms be \( a = 4 \) and \( b = -5 \).
Substitute these values into the identity:
\begin{align*} (3x + 4)(3x - 5) &= (3x)^2 + (4 + (-5))(3x) + (4)(-5) \end{align*}
Now, simplify each term:
\( (3x)^2 = 9x^2 \)
\( (4 - 5)(3x) = (-1)(3x) = -3x \)
\( (4)(-5) = -20 \)
Putting it all together:
\begin{align*} &= 9x^2 - 3x - 20 \end{align*}
Answer: \( 9x^2 - 3x - 20 \)
