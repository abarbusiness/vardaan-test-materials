Minor Test 12: Algebraic Expressions & Squares
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all essential working steps clearly.
  • Do not do rough work on the margins of the answer sheet.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the terms and their numerical coefficients in the given algebraic expression:
\( 5x^2 - 3xy + 2 \)
2.
Find the square of the number \( 15 \).
3.
Multiply the given monomials: \( 3x \) and \( 4y \).
4.
Which of the following numbers cannot be a perfect square and why?
(i) \( 144 \)
(ii) \( 128 \)
5.
Divide the monomial by another monomial: \( 15x^3 \) by \( 3x \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Multiply the monomial by the binomial: \( 2x(3x - 5) \).
7.
Find the square root of \( 256 \) by the prime factorization method.
8.
Divide the given polynomial by the monomial:
\( (8x^2 + 6x) \div 2x \)
9.
Find the smallest whole number by which \( 18 \) must be multiplied so as to get a perfect square number.
10.
Find the product of the two binomials: \( (x + 2) \) and \( (x + 3) \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Solve the following questions on Squares and Square Roots:

(a) Find the square root of \( 529 \) using the long division method. (3 Marks)
(b) Without doing actual calculation, find the sum of the first five odd natural numbers: \( 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 \). State the rule used. (2 Marks)
12.
Solve the following questions on Algebraic Expressions:

(a) Divide the polynomial by the monomial: \( (12x^3y^2 - 8x^2y) \div 4xy \). (3 Marks)
(b) Write the degree of the algebraic expression: \( 4x^3 - 5x^2y^2 + 7 \). (2 Marks)
13.
Simplify and Evaluate:

(a) Simplify the expression by multiplying: \( 3a(2a - b) + 2b(a + 3b) \). (3 Marks)
(b) Evaluate the square of \( 30 \). (2 Marks)
