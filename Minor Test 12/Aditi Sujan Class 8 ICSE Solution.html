Minor Test 12: Profit, Loss & GST

SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Find the profit or loss percent if CP = \( \text{₹ } 400 \) and SP = \( \text{₹ } 460 \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Given, CP = \( \text{₹ } 400 \) and SP = \( \text{₹ } 460 \).
Since SP > CP, there is a profit.
Profit = SP - CP = \( 460 - 400 = \text{₹ } 60 \) (1 Mark)
\( \text{Profit \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100 \)
\( = \left( \frac{60}{400} \right) \times 100 = 15\% \) (1 Mark)
Ans: 15% Profit
Q2. A shopkeeper bought a chair for \( \text{₹ } 800 \) and spent \( \text{₹ } 50 \) on transportation. If he sells it for \( \text{₹ } 1000 \), find his total profit.
[2 M]
Solution:
Total Cost Price = Price of chair + Overhead expenses (transportation)
Total CP = \( 800 + 50 = \text{₹ } 850 \) (1 Mark)
Selling Price (SP) = \( \text{₹ } 1000 \)
Profit = SP - Total CP
Profit = \( 1000 - 850 = \text{₹ } 150 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: ₹ 150
Q3. Define GST. Name its two main intra-state components.
[2 M]
Solution:
GST: Goods and Services Tax is an indirect comprehensive tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. (1 Mark)
Intra-state Components:
1. CGST (Central Goods and Services Tax)
2. SGST (State Goods and Services Tax) (1 Mark for naming both)
Q4. Calculate the selling price of a bag if MP = \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) and discount is \( 10\% \).
[2 M]
Solution:
Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 1200 \)
Discount = \( 10\% \) of MP = \( \left( \frac{10}{100} \right) \times 1200 = \text{₹ } 120 \) (1 Mark)
Selling Price (SP) = MP - Discount
SP = \( 1200 - 120 = \text{₹ } 1080 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: ₹ 1080
Q5. Rate of GST is \( 12\% \) on an item of \( \text{₹ } 500 \). Calculate CGST and SGST.
[2 M]
Solution:
For an intra-state transaction, GST is divided equally into CGST and SGST.
CGST Rate = SGST Rate = \( \frac{12\%}{2} = 6\% \) (1 Mark)
CGST = \( 6\% \) of \( 500 = \left( \frac{6}{100} \right) \times 500 = \text{₹ } 30 \)
SGST = \( 6\% \) of \( 500 = \left( \frac{6}{100} \right) \times 500 = \text{₹ } 30 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: CGST = ₹ 30, SGST = ₹ 30
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. By selling a watch for \( \text{₹ } 1440 \), a man loses \( 10\% \). At what price should he sell it to gain \( 10\% \)?
[3 M]
Solution:
Case 1: SP = \( \text{₹ } 1440 \), Loss = \( 10\% \)
\( \text{CP} = \left( \frac{100}{100 - \text{Loss}\%} \right) \times \text{SP} \)
\( \text{CP} = \left( \frac{100}{90} \right) \times 1440 = 100 \times 16 = \text{₹ } 1600 \) (1.5 Marks)

Case 2: Required Gain = \( 10\% \), CP = \( \text{₹ } 1600 \)
\( \text{New SP} = \left( \frac{100 + \text{Gain}\%}{100} \right) \times \text{CP} \)
\( \text{New SP} = \left( \frac{110}{100} \right) \times 1600 = 110 \times 16 = \text{₹ } 1760 \) (1.5 Marks)
Ans: ₹ 1760
Q7. A dealer buys an article for \( \text{₹ } 6000 \). He marks it at \( 20\% \) above CP and allows a discount of \( 10\% \). Find SP and Profit %.
[3 M]
Solution:
Cost Price (CP) = \( \text{₹ } 6000 \)
Mark-up = \( 20\% \) of CP = \( \left( \frac{20}{100} \right) \times 6000 = \text{₹ } 1200 \)
Marked Price (MP) = \( 6000 + 1200 = \text{₹ } 7200 \) (1 Mark)

Discount = \( 10\% \) of MP = \( \left( \frac{10}{100} \right) \times 7200 = \text{₹ } 720 \)
Selling Price (SP) = MP - Discount = \( 7200 - 720 = \text{₹ } 6480 \) (1 Mark)

Profit = SP - CP = \( 6480 - 6000 = \text{₹ } 480 \)
\( \text{Profit \%} = \left( \frac{480}{6000} \right) \times 100 = 8\% \) (1 Mark)
Ans: SP = ₹ 6480, Profit = 8%
Q8. The selling price of \( 10 \) pens is equal to the cost price of \( 12 \) pens. Find the gain percent.
[3 M]
Solution:
Let the Cost Price (CP) of 1 pen be \( \text{₹ } 1 \).
Then, CP of 10 pens = \( \text{₹ } 10 \).
CP of 12 pens = \( \text{₹ } 12 \).
According to the question:
Selling Price (SP) of 10 pens = CP of 12 pens = \( \text{₹ } 12 \) (1 Mark for correct assumption/relation)

For 10 pens:
CP = \( \text{₹ } 10 \)
SP = \( \text{₹ } 12 \)
Gain = SP - CP = \( 12 - 10 = \text{₹ } 2 \) (1 Mark)

\( \text{Gain \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Gain}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{2}{10} \right) \times 100 = 20\% \) (1 Mark)
Ans: 20%
Q9. Shoes are marked at \( \text{₹ } 1500 \) (exclusive of tax). GST is \( 18\% \). Find the total bill amount.
[3 M]
Solution:
Price of shoes = \( \text{₹ } 1500 \)
GST Rate = \( 18\% \)
Amount of GST = \( 18\% \) of \( 1500 \)
\( = \left( \frac{18}{100} \right) \times 1500 = \text{₹ } 270 \) (1.5 Marks)

Total Bill Amount = Price + GST
\( = 1500 + 270 = \text{₹ } 1770 \) (1.5 Marks)
Ans: ₹ 1770
Q10. A shopkeeper allows a discount of \( 15\% \). If the selling price is \( \text{₹ } 1700 \), calculate the marked price.
[3 M]
Solution:
Let the Marked Price (MP) be \( x \).
Discount = \( 15\% \)
Selling Price (SP) = \( \text{₹ } 1700 \)
We know, \( \text{SP} = \text{MP} \times \left( \frac{100 - \text{Discount}\%}{100} \right) \) (1 Mark)
\( 1700 = x \times \left( \frac{100 - 15}{100} \right) \)
\( 1700 = x \times \left( \frac{85}{100} \right) \) (1 Mark)
\( x = \frac{1700 \times 100}{85} = 20 \times 100 = 2000 \)
Marked Price = \( \text{₹ } 2000 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: ₹ 2000
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. A grocer buys \( 40 \text{ kg} \) of rice at \( \text{₹ } 50/\text{kg} \). He sells half at \( 10\% \) profit and the rest at \( 5\% \) loss. Find (a) Total CP (b) Total SP (c) Overall Profit/Loss %.
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Total CP = Quantity \( \times \) Rate per kg
Total CP = \( 40 \text{ kg} \times 50 = \text{₹ } 2000 \) (1 Mark)

(b) Total quantity = \( 40 \text{ kg} \). Half quantity = \( 20 \text{ kg} \).
First half: CP of \( 20 \text{ kg} = 20 \times 50 = \text{₹ } 1000 \)
Profit = \( 10\% \)
SP1 = \( 1000 + (10\% \text{ of } 1000) = 1000 + 100 = \text{₹ } 1100 \) (1 Mark)
Second half: CP of \( 20 \text{ kg} = \text{₹ } 1000 \)
Loss = \( 5\% \)
SP2 = \( 1000 - (5\% \text{ of } 1000) = 1000 - 50 = \text{₹ } 950 \) (1 Mark)
Total SP = SP1 + SP2 = \( 1100 + 950 = \text{₹ } 2050 \) (1 Mark)

(c) Total CP = \( \text{₹ } 2000 \), Total SP = \( \text{₹ } 2050 \)
Since SP > CP, there is an overall profit.
Total Profit = \( 2050 - 2000 = \text{₹ } 50 \)
\( \text{Overall Profit \%} = \left( \frac{50}{2000} \right) \times 100 = 2.5\% \) (1 Mark)
Ans: (a) ₹ 2000, (b) ₹ 2050, (c) 2.5% Profit
Q12. MP of TV is \( \text{₹ } 24,000 \). Successive discounts of \( 10\% \) and \( 5\% \). Find (a) Final discounted SP (b) Total bill with \( 12\% \) GST.
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 24000 \)
First Discount = \( 10\% \) of \( 24000 = \text{₹ } 2400 \)
Price after 1st discount = \( 24000 - 2400 = \text{₹ } 21600 \) (1.5 Marks)

Second Discount = \( 5\% \) of \( 21600 = \left( \frac{5}{100} \right) \times 21600 = \text{₹ } 1080 \)
Final Selling Price = \( 21600 - 1080 = \text{₹ } 20520 \) (1.5 Marks)

(b) Final discounted price = \( \text{₹ } 20520 \)
GST = \( 12\% \) on SP
GST amount = \( \left( \frac{12}{100} \right) \times 20520 = \text{₹ } 2462.40 \) (1 Mark)
Total Bill Amount = SP + GST
Total Bill = \( 20520 + 2462.40 = \text{₹ } 22982.40 \) (1 Mark)
Ans: (a) ₹ 20,520 (b) ₹ 22,982.40
Q13. Medicines: \( \text{₹ } 800 \) (\( 5\% \) GST). Cosmetics: \( \text{₹ } 1200 \) (\( 18\% \) GST). Calculate (a) Total CGST and SGST (b) Final bill.
[5 M]
Solution:
(a) Medicines: Value = \( \text{₹ } 800 \), GST = \( 5\% \) (CGST \( 2.5\% \), SGST \( 2.5\% \))
CGST on medicines = \( 2.5\% \) of \( 800 = \text{₹ } 20 \)
SGST on medicines = \( 2.5\% \) of \( 800 = \text{₹ } 20 \) (1 Mark)

Cosmetics: Value = \( \text{₹ } 1200 \), GST = \( 18\% \) (CGST \( 9\% \), SGST \( 9\% \))
CGST on cosmetics = \( 9\% \) of \( 1200 = \text{₹ } 108 \)
SGST on cosmetics = \( 9\% \) of \( 1200 = \text{₹ } 108 \) (1 Mark)

Total Taxes:
Total CGST = \( 20 + 108 = \text{₹ } 128 \)
Total SGST = \( 20 + 108 = \text{₹ } 128 \) (1 Mark)

(b) Final Bill Amount:
Total Base Price = \( 800 + 1200 = \text{₹ } 2000 \) (1 Mark)
Total Amount = Base Price + Total CGST + Total SGST
Total Amount = \( 2000 + 128 + 128 = \text{₹ } 2256 \) (1 Mark)
(Alternatively: Medicines total = \( 840 \), Cosmetics total = \( 1416 \). Total = \( 840 + 1416 = 2256 \))
Ans: (a) CGST = ₹ 128, SGST = ₹ 128 (b) Final Bill = ₹ 2256
