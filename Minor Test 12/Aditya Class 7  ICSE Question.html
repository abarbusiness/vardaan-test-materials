Minor Test 12: Geometry Basics & Linear Equations
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Geometry questions are completely theoretical based on basic definitions.
  • Show essential steps for solving equations clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define a 'Line Segment'. How many end points does it have?
2.
Solve the given simple linear equation: \( y + 8 = 20 \).
3.
Define a 'Point'. How is it represented geometrically?
4.
Frame a linear equation for the following statement: "Three times a number \( x \) gives \( 24 \)."
5.
Define a 'Ray'. Does it have a definite length?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
What is meant by 'Collinear Points'? Draw a rough figure to show three collinear points.
7.
Solve the linear equation for \( m \) and find its value: \( 4m - 7 = 17 \).
8.
Define 'Concurrent Lines'. How many lines can pass through a single point?
9.
Solve the following linear equation: \( 2(x + 3) = 14 \).
10.
Define 'Parallel Lines'. Give two real-life examples of parallel lines.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Define the following basic geometrical terms clearly:

(a) Plane (1 Mark)
(b) Intersecting Lines (2 Marks)
(c) Straight Line (2 Marks)
12.
Solve the given linear equation and verify your answer:

\( \frac{2x}{3} + 5 = 11 \)

(3 Marks for solving, 2 Marks for verification)
13.
Rahul is \( 3 \) years older than his sister. The sum of their present ages is \( 17 \) years.

(a) Taking the sister's present age as \( x \), express Rahul's present age in terms of \( x \). (1 Mark)
(b) Form a linear equation representing the sum of their ages. (2 Marks)
(c) Solve the equation to find their present ages. (2 Marks)
