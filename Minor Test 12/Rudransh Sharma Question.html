SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the base and the exponent for the following:
(i)
\( (-3)^5 \)
(ii)
\( \left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^4 \)
2.
Express the following in its exponential form:
\( p \times p \times p \times q \times q \times r \)
3.
Find the numerical value of:
\( 2^3 \times 3^2 \)
4.
Evaluate the following expression:
\( (-1)^{15} + (-1)^{20} \)
5.
Simplify and state the final answer:
\( 5^0 \times 4^0 \times 3^0 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Express \( 72 \) as a product of its prime factors in exponential form.
7.
Simplify the following using laws of exponents and express the result in exponential form:
\( (3^4 \times 3^5) \div 3^7 \)
8.
Which is greater: \( 2^3 \) or \( 3^2 \)? Show your working clearly.
9.
Simplify using laws of exponents:
\( (2^3)^4 \times 2^5 \)
10.
Express \( 343 \) as a power with base 7.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Simplify and express the result in exponential form using laws of exponents:
\[ \frac{2^5 \times 3^4 \times 5^3}{2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5} \]
12.
If \( 2^n = 128 \), find the value of \( n \). Hence, find the value of \( 2^{n-2} \).
13.
Evaluate the following fractions:
(i)
\( (-2)^3 \times (-3)^2 \)
(ii)
\( \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 \times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 \)
14.
Simplify using laws of exponents:
\[ \frac{a^4 \times b^3 \times c^5}{a^2 \times b \times c^3} \]
(Where \( a, b, c \) are non-zero integers)
15.
Find the value of \( x \) if:
\[ 7^{2x+1} = 7^3 \times 7^2 \]
