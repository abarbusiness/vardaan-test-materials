RIGOROUS PRACTICE MODULE
Class: 10 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 90 Minutes Max. Marks: 50
Topics Covered:
  • Real Numbers
  • Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Coordinate Geometry
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 5 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Show complete working for every question.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Short Answer Type I - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the greatest number of six digits exactly divisible by 18, 24, and 36.
2.
Determine whether the points \( (a, a) \), \( (-a, -a) \) and \( (-a\sqrt{3}, a\sqrt{3}) \) form an equilateral triangle. Justify your answer.
3.
Solve for \( x \) and \( y \):
\[ 99x + 101y = 499 \] \[ 101x + 99y = 501 \]
4.
Find the values of \( k \) for which the quadratic equation \( (k+4)x^2 + (k+1)x + 1 = 0 \) has equal roots.
5.
If two positive integers \( p \) and \( q \) can be expressed as \( p = ab^2 \) and \( q = a^3b \); where \( a, b \) being prime numbers, then prove that \( \text{LCM}(p, q) \times \text{HCF}(p, q) = p \times q \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type II - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find the ratio in which the y-axis divides the line segment joining the points \( (-4, -6) \) and \( (10, 12) \). Also, find the coordinates of the point of division.
7.
Solve the following pair of linear equations graphically:
\[ x - y + 1 = 0 \] \[ 3x + 2y - 12 = 0 \]
Determine the vertices of the triangle formed by these lines and the x-axis.
8.
Prove that \( \sqrt{p} + \sqrt{q} \) is an irrational number, given that \( p \) and \( q \) are prime numbers.
9.
Solve for \( x \):
\[ \frac{1}{a + b + x} = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{x}, \text{ where } a+b \neq 0, x \neq 0, x \neq -(a+b) \]
10.
Find the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from the points \( (7, 6) \) and \( (-3, 4) \).
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Places A and B are 100 km apart on a highway. One car starts from A and another from B at the same time. If the cars travel in the same direction at different speeds, they meet in 5 hours. If they travel towards each other, they meet in 1 hour. What are the speeds of the two cars?
12.
Find the area of the rhombus whose vertices taken in order are the points \( (3, 0) \), \( (4, 5) \), \( (-1, 4) \) and \( (-2, -1) \).
13.
A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 5 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour less for the same journey. Find the speed of the train.
14.
A fraction becomes \( \frac{9}{11} \) if 2 is added to both the numerator and the denominator. If 3 is added to both the numerator and the denominator it becomes \( \frac{5}{6} \). Find the fraction algebraically.
15.
Find the roots of the following quadratic equation using the method of completing the square:
\[ 2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0 \]
