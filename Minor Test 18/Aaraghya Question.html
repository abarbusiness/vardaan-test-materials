EXCELLENCE IN SCIENCE EDUCATION
MINOR TEST 18
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Science (Physics & Chemistry) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Read every question carefully.
  • The question paper consists of three sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • Topic coverage: Physics (Heat Energy) & Chemistry (Air and Atmosphere).
  • Write neat explanations and show step-by-step mathematical calculations where required.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Objective & Very Short Answer Type - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] (a) Define Heat.
(b) State the SI unit and CGS unit of Heat Energy.
Q2.
[2 Marks] (a) Name the three distinct modes of heat transfer.
(b) Which mode of heat transfer does not require any material medium?
Q3.
[2 Marks] State the approximate percentage composition by volume of Nitrogen, Oxygen, and Carbon dioxide in clean, dry air.
Q4.
[2 Marks] (a) What is meant by Air Pollution?
(b) Name any two major gaseous air pollutants.
Q5.
[2 Marks] (a) What instrument is used to measure the degree of hotness or coldness of a body?
(b) Name the chemical reagent that turns milky when carbon dioxide gas is passed through it.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Differentiate between Conduction and Convection on the basis of:
(a) Requirement of a material medium
(b) Actual movement of particles
(c) State of matter in which it primarily occurs
Q7.
[3 Marks] Give scientific reasons for the following daily life observations:
(a) A small gap is always left between two successive iron rails on a railway track. [1.5 Marks]
(b) Cooking utensils are provided with wooden or ebonite handles. [1.5 Marks]
Q8.
[3 Marks] State three important reasons why air is classified as a mixture and not a chemical compound.
Q9.
[3 Marks] (a) What is the Greenhouse Effect? [1 Mark]
(b) Name two major greenhouse gases present in the atmosphere. [1 Mark]
(c) Mention one harmful consequence of Global Warming. [1 Mark]
Q10.
[3 Marks] (a) Explain briefly why Land Breeze blows during the night in coastal areas. [1.5 Marks]
(b) What is Smog? Mention one health hazard caused by smog. [1.5 Marks]
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] (a) Explain how a Thermos Flask (Vacuum Flask) minimizes heat loss or heat gain through conduction, convection, and radiation. [3 Marks]
(b) Convert a normal body temperature of \(37^\circ\text{C}\) into the Fahrenheit scale (\(^\circ\text{F}\)) using the conversion formula: $$\dfrac{C}{5} = \dfrac{F - 32}{9}$$ [2 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] (a) Describe a simple candle experiment to demonstrate that air contains Oxygen which supports combustion. [3 Marks]
(b) What is Acid Rain? State two harmful impacts of acid rain on living organisms or structures. [2 Marks]
Q13.
[5 Marks] (a) Define Thermal Expansion. Arrange solids, liquids, and gases in increasing order of their expansion for the same rise in temperature, explaining why this difference exists. [3 Marks]
(b) What is the Ozone Layer? State its primary importance to life on Earth and name the main pollutant responsible for its depletion. [2 Marks]
