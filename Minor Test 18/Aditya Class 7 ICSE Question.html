Minor Test - General Mathematics
Class: 7 (CBSE/ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show rough work wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following expression using suitable properties of integers:
\( [-15 \times 8] + [-15 \times 2] \)
2.
Find the area of a rectangle whose length is \( 5.7 \text{ cm} \) and breadth is \( 3 \text{ cm} \).
3.
Express the rational number \( \frac{-44}{72} \) in its standard form.
4.
Set up an equation and solve it to find the unknown number:
"If you add \( 5 \) to three times a number, you get \( 14 \)."
5.
Find the complement of an angle measuring \( 35^\circ \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A car covers a distance of \( 89.1 \text{ km} \) in \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \). What is the average distance covered by the car in \( 1 \text{ hour} \)?
7.
Simplify the given algebraic expression and find its value when \( a = 5 \) and \( b = -3 \):
\( 2(a^2 + ab) + 3 - ab \)
8.
The perimeter of a rectangular swimming pool is \( 154 \text{ m} \). Its length is \( 2 \text{ m} \) more than twice its breadth. What are the length and breadth of the pool?
9.
In a triangle \( ABC \), \( \angle A = 50^\circ \) and \( \angle B = 60^\circ \).
(i) Find the measure of \( \angle C \).
(ii) State whether the triangle is acute-angled, obtuse-angled, or right-angled. Justify your answer.
10.
By selling a chair for ₹ \( 1440 \), a shopkeeper loses \( 10\% \). At what price should he sell it to gain \( 10\% \)?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In a class test containing 15 questions, \( 4 \) marks are given for every correct answer and \( -2 \) marks are given for every incorrect answer.
(i) Gurpreet attempts all questions but only \( 9 \) of her answers are correct. What is her total score? (2.5 Marks)
(ii) One of her friends gets only \( 5 \) answers correct out of the 15 questions attempted. What will be her score? (2.5 Marks)
12.
The length and breadth of a rectangular piece of land are in the ratio of \( 5:3 \). If the total cost of fencing it at ₹ \( 24 \) per metre is ₹ \( 9600 \), find:
(i) The length and breadth of the land. (3 Marks)
(ii) The area of the land. (2 Marks)
13.
Answer the following algebraic questions:
(a) Subtract the sum of \( (3l - 4m - 7n^2) \) and \( (2l + 3m - 4n^2) \) from the sum of \( (9l + 2m - 3n^2) \) and \( (-3l + m + 4n^2) \). (3 Marks)
(b) Write a mathematical equation for the following statement and solve it: "Seven times a number plus 7 gets you 77." (2 Marks)
