SECTION A

Q1. Evaluate: \( [-15 \times 8] + [-15 \times 2] \) [2 Marks]
We can solve this using the Distributive Property of Multiplication over Addition: \( a \times (b + c) = (a \times b) + (a \times c) \).
Here, let \( a = -15 \), \( b = 8 \), and \( c = 2 \).

\( [-15 \times 8] + [-15 \times 2] = -15 \times (8 + 2) \)
\( = -15 \times 10 \)
\( = -150 \)
Answer: -150
Q2. Find the area of a rectangle whose length is \( 5.7 \text{ cm} \) and breadth is \( 3 \text{ cm} \). [2 Marks]
Given:
Length (\( l \)) = \( 5.7 \text{ cm} \)
Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 3 \text{ cm} \)
Formula for Area of a rectangle = \( l \times b \)
Area \( = 5.7 \times 3 \)
\( = 17.1 \text{ cm}^2 \)
Answer: 17.1 cm²
Q3. Express the rational number \( \frac{-44}{72} \) in its standard form. [2 Marks]
To express a rational number in standard form, we divide its numerator and denominator by their Highest Common Factor (HCF).
The HCF of 44 and 72 is 4.
Dividing both by 4:
\( \frac{-44 \div 4}{72 \div 4} = \frac{-11}{18} \)
Answer: -11/18
Q4. Set up an equation and solve: "If you add 5 to three times a number, you get 14." [2 Marks]
Let the unknown number be \( x \).
"Three times a number" = \( 3x \)
"Add 5 to it" = \( 3x + 5 \)
According to the question:
\( 3x + 5 = 14 \)
Solving the equation:
\( 3x = 14 - 5 \)
\( 3x = 9 \)
\( x = \frac{9}{3} \)
\( x = 3 \)
Answer: The number is 3.
Q5. Find the complement of an angle measuring \( 35^\circ \). [2 Marks]
Two angles are complementary if their sum is \( 90^\circ \).
Complement of \( 35^\circ = 90^\circ - 35^\circ \)
\( = 55^\circ \)
Answer: 55°

SECTION B

Q6. A car covers a distance of \( 89.1 \text{ km} \) in \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \). What is the average distance covered by the car in \( 1 \text{ hour} \)? [3 Marks]
Total distance covered = \( 89.1 \text{ km} \)
Total time taken = \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \)
Distance covered in 1 hour = \( \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} \)
\( = \frac{89.1}{2.2} \)
To remove decimals, multiply numerator and denominator by 10:
\( = \frac{891}{22} \)
Dividing by 11:
\( = \frac{81}{2} = 40.5 \text{ km} \)
Answer: 40.5 km
Q7. Simplify the expression and find its value when \( a = 5 \) and \( b = -3 \): \( 2(a^2 + ab) + 3 - ab \) [3 Marks]
Step 1: Simplify the expression first.
\( = 2a^2 + 2ab + 3 - ab \)
\( = 2a^2 + (2ab - ab) + 3 \)
\( = 2a^2 + ab + 3 \)
Step 2: Substitute the values \( a = 5 \) and \( b = -3 \).
\( = 2(5)^2 + (5)(-3) + 3 \)
\( = 2(25) - 15 + 3 \)
\( = 50 - 15 + 3 \)
\( = 35 + 3 \)
\( = 38 \)
Answer: 38
Q8. The perimeter of a rectangular swimming pool is \( 154 \text{ m} \). Its length is \( 2 \text{ m} \) more than twice its breadth. Find the length and breadth. [3 Marks]
Let the breadth of the pool be \( b \) meters.
Then, length (\( l \)) = \( 2b + 2 \) meters.
Perimeter of a rectangle = \( 2(l + b) \)
Given Perimeter = \( 154 \text{ m} \)

\( 2((2b + 2) + b) = 154 \)
\( 2(3b + 2) = 154 \)
\( 3b + 2 = \frac{154}{2} \)
\( 3b + 2 = 77 \)
\( 3b = 77 - 2 \)
\( 3b = 75 \)
\( b = 25 \text{ m} \)
Now find the length:
\( l = 2(25) + 2 = 50 + 2 = 52 \text{ m} \)
Answer: Length = 52 m, Breadth = 25 m
Q9. In \( \triangle ABC \), \( \angle A = 50^\circ \) and \( \angle B = 60^\circ \). (i) Find \( \angle C \). (ii) State the type of triangle. [3 Marks]
Part (i): By the Angle Sum Property of a triangle, the sum of all interior angles is \( 180^\circ \).
\( \angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ \)
\( 50^\circ + 60^\circ + \angle C = 180^\circ \)
\( 110^\circ + \angle C = 180^\circ \)
\( \angle C = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ \)
Part (ii): Look at the measures of all three angles: \( 50^\circ, 60^\circ, \) and \( 70^\circ \).
Since all three angles are less than \( 90^\circ \) (they are acute angles), the triangle is an acute-angled triangle.
Answer: (i) 70°, (ii) Acute-angled triangle
Q10. By selling a chair for ₹ 1440, a shopkeeper loses 10%. At what price should he sell it to gain 10%? [3 Marks]
Case 1 (Loss):
Selling Price (SP) = ₹ 1440, Loss% = 10%
Let Cost Price (CP) be \( x \).
\( \text{SP} = \text{CP} - \text{Loss} \)
\( 1440 = x - (10\% \text{ of } x) \)
\( 1440 = x - 0.1x = 0.9x \)
\( x = \frac{1440}{0.9} = \frac{14400}{9} = \text{₹ } 1600 \)
So, CP = ₹ 1600.
Case 2 (Gain):
Desired Gain% = 10%
\( \text{New SP} = \text{CP} + \text{Gain} \)
\( = 1600 + (10\% \text{ of } 1600) \)
\( = 1600 + 160 \)
\( = \text{₹ } 1760 \)
Answer: He should sell it for ₹ 1760.

SECTION C

Q11. Test containing 15 questions, +4 marks for correct, -2 for incorrect. (i) Gurpreet: 9 correct out of 15. (ii) Friend: 5 correct out of 15. [5 Marks]
(i) Gurpreet's Score:
Total questions attempted = 15. Number of correct answers = 9.
Number of incorrect answers = \( 15 - 9 = 6 \).
Marks for correct answers = \( 9 \times 4 = 36 \)
Marks for incorrect answers = \( 6 \times (-2) = -12 \)
Total Score = \( 36 + (-12) = 24 \)
(ii) Friend's Score:
Total questions attempted = 15. Number of correct answers = 5.
Number of incorrect answers = \( 15 - 5 = 10 \).
Marks for correct answers = \( 5 \times 4 = 20 \)
Marks for incorrect answers = \( 10 \times (-2) = -20 \)
Total Score = \( 20 + (-20) = 0 \)
Answer: (i) Gurpreet's score is 24. (ii) Friend's score is 0.
Q12. Length and breadth ratio is 5:3. Cost of fencing at ₹ 24/m is ₹ 9600. Find dimensions and area. [5 Marks]
Part (i): Finding dimensions
Let length = \( 5x \) and breadth = \( 3x \).
Fencing implies Perimeter. Total cost = ₹ 9600, Rate = ₹ 24/m.
\( \text{Perimeter} = \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Rate}} = \frac{9600}{24} = 400 \text{ m} \)
Formula for perimeter = \( 2(\text{length} + \text{breadth}) \)
\( 2(5x + 3x) = 400 \)
\( 2(8x) = 400 \)
\( 16x = 400 \)
\( x = \frac{400}{16} = 25 \)
Length = \( 5 \times 25 = 125 \text{ m} \)
Breadth = \( 3 \times 25 = 75 \text{ m} \)
Part (ii): Finding Area
\( \text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{breadth} \)
\( = 125 \times 75 \)
\( = 9375 \text{ m}^2 \)
Answer: Length = 125m, Breadth = 75m. Area = 9375 m².
Q13. (a) Subtract sum of \( (3l - 4m - 7n^2) \) and \( (2l + 3m - 4n^2) \) from sum of \( (9l + 2m - 3n^2) \) and \( (-3l + m + 4n^2) \).
(b) Write equation and solve: "Seven times a number plus 7 gets you 77." [5 Marks]
Part (a)
Let Sum 1 = \( (3l - 4m - 7n^2) + (2l + 3m - 4n^2) \)
\( \text{Sum 1} = (3l + 2l) + (-4m + 3m) + (-7n^2 - 4n^2) = 5l - m - 11n^2 \)
Let Sum 2 = \( (9l + 2m - 3n^2) + (-3l + m + 4n^2) \)
\( \text{Sum 2} = (9l - 3l) + (2m + m) + (-3n^2 + 4n^2) = 6l + 3m + n^2 \)
Now, we need to subtract Sum 1 from Sum 2: (Sum 2 - Sum 1)
\( = (6l + 3m + n^2) - (5l - m - 11n^2) \)
\( = 6l + 3m + n^2 - 5l + m + 11n^2 \)
\( = (6l - 5l) + (3m + m) + (n^2 + 11n^2) \)
\( = l + 4m + 12n^2 \)
Part (b)
Let the number be \( x \).
"Seven times a number" = \( 7x \). "Plus 7" = \( 7x + 7 \). "Gets you 77" means equals 77.
Equation: \( 7x + 7 = 77 \)
\( 7x = 77 - 7 \)
\( 7x = 70 \)
\( x = 10 \)
Answer: (a) \( l + 4m + 12n^2 \) (b) Equation is \( 7x + 7 = 77 \), Number is 10.
