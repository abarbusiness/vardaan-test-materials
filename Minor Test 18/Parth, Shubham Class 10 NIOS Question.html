FOUNDATIONAL ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE
MINOR TEST 20
Class: 10 CBSE / ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Foundational & Basic) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Read each calculation carefully.
  • The question paper consists of three sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • Topic coverage: Integers, Fractions, HCF & LCM, Basic Algebra, Simple Linear Equations, and Basic Geometry/Probability.
  • Show proper step-by-step working for full marks.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Very Short Answer Type - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] Perform the following basic integer operations:
(a) Add: \((-15) + (+25)\)
(b) Subtract: \(18 - (-12)\)
Q2.
[2 Marks] Find the **LCM** (Lowest Common Multiple) of \(12\) and \(18\) by using prime factorization or division method.
Q3.
[2 Marks] Solve the following fraction additions:
(a) \(\dfrac{3}{7} + \dfrac{2}{7}\)
(b) \(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{2}{3}\)
Q4.
[2 Marks] Find the **HCF** (Highest Common Factor) of \(24\) and \(36\).
Q5.
[2 Marks] Solve the simple direct linear equation to find the value of \(x\): \[ 2x + 5 = 15 \]
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Perform the following operations on fractions:
(a) Subtract: \(\dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{1}{3}\) [1.5 Marks]
(b) Multiply: \(\dfrac{4}{5} \times \dfrac{10}{12}\) [1.5 Marks]
Q7.
[3 Marks] Evaluate the following expressions involving directed numbers:
(a) \((-8) \times (-5)\) [1 Mark]
(b) \((-36) \div (+9)\) [1 Mark]
(c) \(15 + (-7) - (-4)\) [1 Mark]
Q8.
[3 Marks] Find the **HCF** and **LCM** of \(15\) and \(25\). Also verify that: \[ \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = \text{Product of the two numbers} \]
Q9.
[3 Marks] Solve the following pair of simple equations to find the values of \(x\) and \(y\): \[ x + y = 10 \] \[ x - y = 4 \]
Q10.
[3 Marks] Given the algebraic polynomial \(P(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 5\), find the value of \(P(x)\) at:
(a) \(x = 1\) [1.5 Marks]
(b) \(x = 0\) [1.5 Marks]
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] (a) Express \(120\) as a product of its prime factors using tree or division method. [2 Marks]
(b) Find the **LCM** and **HCF** of three numbers: \(24\), \(36\), and \(48\). [3 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] (a) Simplify the following fraction problem step-by-step: [2.5 Marks] \[ \left(\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{2}\right) - \frac{3}{10} \] (b) A bag contains \(5\) red balls and \(3\) blue balls. One ball is taken out at random. What is the probability of getting: [2.5 Marks]
(i) A red ball?
(ii) A blue ball?
Q13.
[5 Marks] (a) The perimeter of a rectangle is \(30\text{ cm}\). If its length is \(9\text{ cm}\), find its breadth (width) and its area. [2.5 Marks]
(b) Find the distance between the two points \(A(0, 0)\) and \(B(6, 8)\) using the simple distance formula: [2.5 Marks] \[ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \]
