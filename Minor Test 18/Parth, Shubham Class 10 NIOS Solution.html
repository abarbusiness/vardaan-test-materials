SECTION A - DETAILED SOLUTIONS (10 Marks)

Q1. (a) \((-15) + (+25)\)   (b) \(18 - (-12)\)
[2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Addition of integers:

\[ (-15) + 25 = 25 - 15 = 10 \]

(b) Subtraction of integers:

Subtracting a negative number is equivalent to adding its positive value (\(- \times - = +\)):

\[ 18 - (-12) = 18 + 12 = 30 \]
Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for correct answer \(10\); 1 Mark for correct answer \(30\).
Q2. LCM of 12 and 18
[2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

First, write the prime factorizations of both numbers:

\[ 12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^1 \]
\[ 18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^1 \times 3^2 \]

To find the LCM, take the highest power of each prime factor:

\[ \text{LCM}(12, 18) = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36 \]

Answer: The LCM of 12 and 18 is 36.

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for writing prime factors; 1 Mark for calculating LCM = 36.
Q3. (a) \(\dfrac{3}{7} + \dfrac{2}{7}\)   (b) \(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{2}{3}\)
[2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Same denominator:

\[ \frac{3}{7} + \frac{2}{7} = \frac{3 + 2}{7} = \frac{5}{7} \]

(b) Different denominators:

LCM of denominators \(4\) and \(3\) is \(12\):

\[ \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1 \times 3}{4 \times 3} = \frac{3}{12} \]
\[ \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \frac{8}{12} \]
\[ \frac{3}{12} + \frac{8}{12} = \frac{3 + 8}{12} = \frac{11}{12} \]
Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for \(\dfrac{5}{7}\); 1 Mark for \(\dfrac{11}{12}\).
Q4. HCF of 24 and 36
[2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

Prime factors of \(24\) and \(36\):

\[ 24 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^3 \times 3^1 \]
\[ 36 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \]

To find the HCF, take the smallest power of common prime factors:

\[ \text{HCF}(24, 36) = 2^2 \times 3^1 = 4 \times 3 = 12 \]

Answer: The HCF of 24 and 36 is 12.

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for prime factors; 1 Mark for correct HCF = 12.
Q5. Solve for \(x\): \(2x + 5 = 15\)
[2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

Given linear equation:

\[ 2x + 5 = 15 \]

Subtract \(5\) from both sides:

\[ 2x = 15 - 5 \]
\[ 2x = 10 \]

Divide both sides by \(2\):

\[ x = \frac{10}{2} = 5 \]

Answer: \(x = 5\).

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for transposing 5 to get \(2x = 10\); 1 Mark for getting \(x = 5\).

Q6. (a) \(\dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{1}{3}\)   (b) \(\dfrac{4}{5} \times \dfrac{10}{12}\)
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Subtraction of fractions:

LCM of denominators \(6\) and \(3\) is \(6\):

\[ \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{5}{6} - \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5 - 2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \]

(b) Multiplication of fractions:

\[ \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{10}{12} = \frac{4 \times 10}{5 \times 12} = \frac{40}{60} = \frac{2}{3} \]
Marking Scheme: 1.5 Marks for simplifying part (a) to \(\dfrac{1}{2}\); 1.5 Marks for simplifying part (b) to \(\dfrac{2}{3}\).

Q8. HCF, LCM, and verification for 15 and 25
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

Prime factorizations:

\[ 15 = 3 \times 5 \]
\[ 25 = 5 \times 5 = 5^2 \]

Calculating HCF & LCM:

  • \(\text{HCF} = 5\)
  • \(\text{LCM} = 3 \times 5^2 = 3 \times 25 = 75\)

Verification:

\[ \text{LHS} = \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = 5 \times 75 = 375 \]
\[ \text{RHS} = \text{Product of numbers} = 15 \times 25 = 375 \]

Since \(\text{LHS} = \text{RHS} = 375\), the property is verified.

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for HCF = 5 and LCM = 75; 2 Marks for step-by-step verification showing LHS = RHS = 375.
Q9. Solve system: \(x + y = 10\) and \(x - y = 4\)
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

Given equations:

\[ (1) \quad x + y = 10 \]
\[ (2) \quad x - y = 4 \]

Add equation (1) and equation (2):

\[ (x + y) + (x - y) = 10 + 4 \]
\[ 2x = 14 \implies x = \frac{14}{2} = 7 \]

Substitute \(x = 7\) into equation (1):

\[ 7 + y = 10 \implies y = 10 - 7 = 3 \]

Answer: \(x = 7\) and \(y = 3\).

Marking Scheme: 1.5 Marks for finding \(x = 7\); 1.5 Marks for finding \(y = 3\).
Q10. Evaluate polynomial \(P(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 5\) at \(x=1\) and \(x=0\)
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

Given: \(P(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 5\)

(a) For \(x = 1\):

\[ P(1) = 2(1)^2 + 3(1) - 5 \]
\[ P(1) = 2(1) + 3 - 5 = 2 + 3 - 5 = 5 - 5 = 0 \]

(b) For \(x = 0\):

\[ P(0) = 2(0)^2 + 3(0) - 5 \]
\[ P(0) = 0 + 0 - 5 = -5 \]
Marking Scheme: 1.5 Marks for \(P(1) = 0\); 1.5 Marks for \(P(0) = -5\).

Q11. Prime factorization of 120 and HCF/LCM of (24, 36, 48)
[5 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Prime factorization of 120:

\[ 120 = 2 \times 60 = 2 \times 2 \times 30 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 15 = 2^3 \times 3^1 \times 5^1 \]

Product form: \(2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5\).

(b) LCM and HCF of 24, 36, and 48:

\[ 24 = 2^3 \times 3^1 \]
\[ 36 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \]
\[ 48 = 2^4 \times 3^1 \]

Calculating HCF: Smallest powers of common prime factors:

\[ \text{HCF} = 2^2 \times 3^1 = 4 \times 3 = 12 \]

Calculating LCM: Highest powers of all prime factors:

\[ \text{LCM} = 2^4 \times 3^2 = 16 \times 9 = 144 \]
Marking Scheme: (a) 2 Marks for prime factors of 120. (b) 1.5 Marks for HCF = 12 and 1.5 Marks for LCM = 144.
Q12. Fraction simplification & basic probability calculation
[5 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Simplifying fractions:

\[ \left(\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{2}\right) - \frac{3}{10} \]

First calculate inside brackets (LCM of 5 and 2 is 10):

\[ \frac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2} + \frac{1 \times 5}{2 \times 5} = \frac{4}{10} + \frac{5}{10} = \frac{9}{10} \]

Now subtract \(\dfrac{3}{10}\):

\[ \frac{9}{10} - \frac{3}{10} = \frac{9 - 3}{10} = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5} \]

(b) Probability calculations:

Total number of balls \(= 5 \text{ (red)} + 3 \text{ (blue)} = 8 \text{ balls}\).

  • (i) Probability of Red ball: \[ P(\text{Red}) = \frac{\text{Number of Red balls}}{\text{Total balls}} = \frac{5}{8} \]
  • (ii) Probability of Blue ball: \[ P(\text{Blue}) = \frac{\text{Number of Blue balls}}{\text{Total balls}} = \frac{3}{8} \]
Marking Scheme: (a) 2.5 Marks for step-by-step simplification to \(\dfrac{3}{5}\). (b) 1.25 Marks each for \(P(\text{Red}) = \dfrac{5}{8}\) and \(P(\text{Blue}) = \dfrac{3}{8}\).
Q13. Perimeter/Area of rectangle & Distance formula calculation
[5 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Mensuration problem:

Given: Perimeter \(P = 30\text{ cm}\), Length \(l = 9\text{ cm}\).

Formula for perimeter of a rectangle: \(P = 2(l + b)\)

\[ 30 = 2(9 + b) \]
\[ 9 + b = \frac{30}{2} = 15 \implies b = 15 - 9 = 6\text{ cm} \]

Now find the Area (\(A = l \times b\)):

\[ \text{Area} = 9 \times 6 = 54\text{ cm}^2 \]

(b) Distance Formula:

Given points: \(A(0, 0)\) and \(B(6, 8)\). Here \(x_1 = 0, y_1 = 0, x_2 = 6, y_2 = 8\).

\[ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \]
\[ d = \sqrt{(6 - 0)^2 + (8 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} \]
\[ d = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ units} \]
Marking Scheme: (a) 1.5 Marks for breadth = \(6\text{ cm}\), 1 Mark for area = \(54\text{ cm}^2\). (b) 2.5 Marks for step-by-step evaluation showing distance = \(10\text{ units}\).
