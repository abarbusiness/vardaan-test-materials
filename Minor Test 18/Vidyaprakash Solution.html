Q1. Reanna has a special chocolate that triples every day if not eaten. If she has 1 chocolate on day 1, express the number of chocolates she will have on day \( n \) in exponential form.
[2 Marks]
Day 1: \( 1 \) chocolate
Day 2: \( 1 \times 3 = 3^1 \) chocolates
Day 3: \( 3 \times 3 = 3^2 \) chocolates
Following the pattern, on Day \( n \), the exponent is one less than the day number.
Final Answer: The number of chocolates on day \( n \) is \( 3^{n-1} \). (2 Marks)

Q13. State the congruence rule used to prove that the opposite sides of a rectangle are equal. Show the steps of proof by drawing a diagonal in rectangle ABCD.
[3 Marks]
Draw diagonal \( AC \) in rectangle \( ABCD \). A rectangle is a parallelogram where all angles are \( 90^\circ \). Therefore \( AB \parallel DC \) and \( AD \parallel BC \).
In \( \Delta ABC \) and \( \Delta CDA \):
1. \( \angle BAC = \angle DCA \) (Alternate interior angles, since \( AB \parallel DC \))
2. \( AC = CA \) (Common diagonal)
3. \( \angle BCA = \angle DAC \) (Alternate interior angles, since \( AD \parallel BC \))
(1.5 Marks for listing criteria)
Therefore, \( \Delta ABC \cong \Delta CDA \) by ASA (Angle-Side-Angle) congruence rule. (0.5 Marks for Rule)
By CPCT (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles), \( AB = CD \) and \( AD = BC \).
Final Answer: Proved using ASA congruence. (1 Mark for conclusion)
Q14. Test the divisibility of the number 661314 by 11. Clearly state the rule and show your calculation.
[3 Marks]
Rule: A number is divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of digits at odd places and the sum of digits at even places is either 0 or a multiple of 11. (1 Mark)
For the number 661314:
Sum of digits at odd places (1st, 3rd, 5th from right) = \( 4 + 3 + 6 = 13 \).
Sum of digits at even places (2nd, 4th, 6th from right) = \( 1 + 1 + 6 = 8 \).
(1 Mark for correct sums)
Difference = \( 13 - 8 = 5 \).
Since 5 is neither 0 nor a multiple of 11, the number is not divisible by 11.
Final Answer: Not divisible by 11. (1 Mark)

Q21. (a) Define a rhombus. (b) Prove geometrically that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.
[5 Marks]
(a) Definition: A rhombus is a special type of parallelogram in which all four sides are of equal length. (1 Mark)
(b) Proof: Let \( ABCD \) be a rhombus with diagonals \( AC \) and \( BD \) intersecting at point \( O \).
Since a rhombus is a parallelogram, we know its diagonals bisect each other. Thus, \( OA = OC \) and \( OB = OD \). (1 Mark)
Consider \( \Delta AOB \) and \( \Delta AOD \):
1. \( AB = AD \) (Sides of a rhombus are equal)
2. \( OA = OA \) (Common side)
3. \( OB = OD \) (Diagonals bisect each other)
(1.5 Marks for criteria)
Therefore, \( \Delta AOB \cong \Delta AOD \) by SSS (Side-Side-Side) congruence rule.
By CPCT, \( \angle AOB = \angle AOD \).
Since \( BD \) is a straight line, \( \angle AOB + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \) (Linear Pair).
$$ 2 \angle AOB = 180^\circ \implies \angle AOB = 90^\circ $$
Final Answer: Hence, the diagonals bisect each other at right angles. (1.5 Marks for logical conclusion)

Q23. (a) Write the generalised expanded form of a 3-digit number. (b) Prove the test of divisibility by 9 using this form.
[5 Marks]
(a) Generalised Form: A 3-digit number with digits \( x \), \( y \), and \( z \) (from left to right) is written as:
$$ 100x + 10y + z $$
(1 Mark)
(b) Proof of Divisibility by 9:
We can rewrite the generalized form by grouping multiples of 9:
$$ 100x + 10y + z = (99x + x) + (9y + y) + z $$ $$ = (99x + 9y) + (x + y + z) $$ $$ = 9(11x + y) + (x + y + z) $$
(2 Marks for algebraic manipulation)
The first part, \( 9(11x + y) \), is clearly a multiple of 9 and therefore always divisible by 9.
For the entire number to be divisible by 9, the remaining part, which is \( (x + y + z) \), must also be divisible by 9.
(1 Mark for reasoning)
Notice that \( (x + y + z) \) is exactly the sum of the digits of the original number.
Final Answer: Thus, a number is divisible by 9 if and only if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9. (1 Mark)

Q25. (a) Differentiate between convex and concave quadrilateral. (b) State three properties of a Kite.
[5 Marks]
(a) Convex vs Concave Quadrilateral:
- Convex: A quadrilateral in which all interior angles are less than \( 180^\circ \), and both diagonals lie entirely inside the figure.
- Concave: A quadrilateral in which at least one interior angle is greater than \( 180^\circ \) (reflex angle), and at least one diagonal lies partially or completely outside the figure.
(2 Marks for correct differentiation)
(b) Properties of a Kite:
1. It has two non-overlapping pairs of adjacent sides that are equal in length.
2. The diagonals intersect at right angles (\( 90^\circ \)).
3. The longer diagonal bisects the shorter diagonal. (Additionally, the longer diagonal bisects the angles at the vertices it connects).
(3 Marks - 1 mark for each correct property)
Q26. (a) Digital Root of 5910821. (b) Correlation with remainder by 9. (c) 10,000 in exponential form.
[5 Marks]
(a) Digital Root: The digital root is a single-digit value obtained by repeatedly summing the digits of a number.
$$ 5910821 \rightarrow 5 + 9 + 1 + 0 + 8 + 2 + 1 = 26 $$ $$ 26 \rightarrow 2 + 6 = 8 $$
Answer (a): The digital root is 8. (2 Marks)
(b) Correlation: The digital root of a number is exactly equal to the remainder obtained when that number is divided by 9. The only exception is when the remainder is 0, in which case the digital root is 9.

(c) Exponential Form: 10,000 has four zeros.

Answer (c): \( 10^4 \) (1 Mark)
