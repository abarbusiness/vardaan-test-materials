General Mathematics Minor Test
Class: 8 (CBSE/ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Proper steps must be shown.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find two rational numbers between \(\frac{1}{4}\) and \(\frac{1}{2}\).
2.
Solve the linear equation for \(x\): \(3x - 5 = 2x + 7\)
3.
The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4 : 6. Find the measure of the largest angle.
4.
Evaluate the following using laws of exponents: \((2^{-1} \times 4^{-1}) \div 2^{-2}\)
5.
Find the product of the algebraic expressions: \((3x + 2y)\) and \((3x - 2y)\).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A rectangular piece of paper \(11\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm}\) is folded without overlapping to make a cylinder of height \(4\text{ cm}\). Find the volume of the cylinder. \(\left(\text{Take } \pi = \frac{22}{7}\right)\)
7.
The sum of three consecutive multiples of \(8\) is \(888\). Find the multiples.
8.
Factorise the given expression completely: \(a^4 - b^4\)
9.
Find the smallest whole number by which \(252\) must be multiplied to get a perfect square. Also, find the square root of the perfect square so obtained.
10.
Simplify the following using laws of exponents: \[ \frac{3^{-5} \times 10^{-5} \times 125}{5^{-7} \times 6^{-5}} \]
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The parallel sides of a trapezium are \(25\text{ cm}\) and \(13\text{ cm}\). Its non-parallel sides are equal, each being \(10\text{ cm}\). Find the area of the trapezium.
12.
(i) Using suitable algebraic identities, prove that \((a+b)^2 - (a-b)^2 = 4ab\).
(ii) Hence, evaluate \(102^2 - 98^2\).
(iii) Simplify: \((2x+5)^2 - (2x-5)^2\).
13.
A shopkeeper bought a VCR and a TV for ₹8,000 each. He made a loss of 4% on the VCR and a profit of 8% on the TV. Find the total gain or loss percentage on the whole transaction.
