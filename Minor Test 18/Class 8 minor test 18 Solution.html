Q1. Additive inverse of the product of $-\frac{3}{7}$ and $\frac{14}{9}$.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (b) $\frac{2}{3}$
Step 1: Find the product:
$$\text{Product} = \left(-\frac{3}{7}\right) \times \left(\frac{14}{9}\right) = -\frac{3 \times 14}{7 \times 9} = -\frac{42}{63} = -\frac{2}{3}$$
Step 2: Find the additive inverse (change sign):
$$\text{Additive Inverse of } \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) = +\frac{2}{3}$$
Q2. Value of $x^2 - 10$ if $3x - 5 = 2x + 7$.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (a) $134$
Step 1: Solve for $x$:
$$3x - 2x = 7 + 5 \implies x = 12$$
Step 2: Evaluate $x^2 - 10$:
$$x^2 - 10 = (12)^2 - 10 = 144 - 10 = 134$$
Q3. Sum of interior angles of a convex polygon with 6 sides.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (c) $720^\circ$
Formula for the sum of interior angles of an $n$-sided polygon:
$$\text{Sum} = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$$
For $n = 6$:
$$\text{Sum} = (6 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 4 \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ$$
Q4. Smallest multiplier to make $180$ a perfect square.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (c) $5$
Prime Factorization of 180:
$$180 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^1$$
Here, $5$ is unpaired. Multiplying by $5$ gives $180 \times 5 = 900 = 30^2$.
Q5. Profit percentage for C.P. = ₹800 and S.P. = ₹960.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (b) $20\%$
$$\text{Profit} = \text{S.P.} - \text{C.P.} = 960 - 800 = \text{₹}160$$
$$\text{Profit } \% = \left(\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{C.P.}}\right) \times 100 = \left(\frac{160}{800}\right) \times 100 = 20\%$$

Q7. Solve $\frac{x + 3}{2} - \frac{2x - 1}{3} = \frac{5}{6}$.
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: $x = 6$
Take LCM of denominators $2$ and $3$ on LHS, which is $6$:
$$\frac{3(x + 3) - 2(2x - 1)}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$$
Multiply both sides by $6$:
$$3(x + 3) - 2(2x - 1) = 5$$
$$3x + 9 - 4x + 2 = 5$$
$$-x + 11 = 5 \implies -x = 5 - 11 \implies -x = -6 \implies x = 6$$
Q8. Parallelogram $ABCD$ with adjacent angles $\angle A = (2x + 10)^\circ$ and $\angle B = (3x - 20)^\circ$.
[3 Marks]
Angles: $\angle A = 86^\circ, \angle B = 94^\circ, \angle C = 86^\circ, \angle D = 94^\circ$
Parallelogram Angle Geometry for Q8
Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary ($\text{Sum} = 180^\circ$):
$$\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ \implies (2x + 10) + (3x - 20) = 180$$
$$5x - 10 = 180 \implies 5x = 190 \implies x = 38$$
Calculations for angles:
$$\angle A = 2(38) + 10 = 76 + 10 = 86^\circ$$
$$\angle B = 3(38) - 20 = 114 - 20 = 94^\circ$$
Since opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal:
$$\angle C = \angle A = 86^\circ, \quad \angle D = \angle B = 94^\circ$$
Q9. Square root of $1764$ by prime factorization.
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: $\sqrt{1764} = 42$
Prime Factorization Steps:
$$1764 \div 2 = 882$$
$$882 \div 2 = 441$$
$$441 \div 3 = 147$$
$$147 \div 3 = 49$$
$$49 \div 7 = 7$$
$$7 \div 7 = 1$$
Pairing Factors:
$$1764 = (2 \times 2) \times (3 \times 3) \times (7 \times 7)$$
$$\sqrt{1764} = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$$
Q10. Simplify $(2a + 3b)^2 - (2a - 3b)^2$.
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: $24ab$
Using identity $x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y)$ where $x = (2a + 3b)$ and $y = (2a - 3b)$:
$$\text{Expression} = [(2a + 3b) + (2a - 3b)] \times [(2a + 3b) - (2a - 3b)]$$
$$= [4a] \times [2a + 3b - 2a + 3b] = [4a] \times [6b] = 24ab$$

Q11. Rectangular park: Perimeter $= 180\text{ m}$, length is $10\text{ m}$ more than twice breadth.
[5 Marks]
Breadth $= 26.67\text{ m}$, Length $= 63.33\text{ m}$, Area $= 1688.89\text{ m}^2$
(a) Formulating Equation [2 Marks]:
Let breadth of the rectangle $= x\text{ m}$.
Then length $= (2x + 10)\text{ m}$.
$$\text{Perimeter} = 2(\text{Length} + \text{Breadth}) \implies 2[(2x + 10) + x] = 180$$
$$2(3x + 10) = 180 \implies 6x + 20 = 180$$
(b) Finding Dimensions [2 Marks]:
$$6x = 180 - 20 = 160 \implies x = \frac{160}{6} = \frac{80}{3} = 26.67\text{ m}$$
$$\text{Breadth } = \frac{80}{3}\text{ m} \approx 26.67\text{ m}$$
$$\text{Length } = 2\left(\frac{80}{3}\right) + 10 = \frac{160}{3} + \frac{30}{3} = \frac{190}{3}\text{ m} \approx 63.33\text{ m}$$
(c) Calculating Area [1 Mark]:
$$\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth} = \left(\frac{190}{3}\right) \times \left(\frac{80}{3}\right) = \frac{15200}{9} \approx 1688.89\text{ m}^2$$
Q12. Investment ₹$12,500$ for $2$ years at $10\%$ per annum (S.I. vs C.I.).
[5 Marks]
Gain from C.I. over S.I. = ₹$125$
(a) Simple Interest Calculation [2 Marks]:
$$\text{S.I.} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100} = \frac{12500 \times 10 \times 2}{100} = \text{₹}2,500$$
$$\text{Total Amount (S.I.)} = 12500 + 2500 = \text{₹}15,000$$
(b) Compound Interest Calculation [2 Marks]:
$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T = 12500\left(1 + \frac{10}{100}\right)^2 = 12500\left(\frac{11}{10}\right)^2$$
$$A = 12500 \times \frac{121}{100} = 125 \times 121 = \text{₹}15,125$$
$$\text{C.I.} = A - P = 15125 - 12500 = \text{₹}2,625$$
(c) Extra Gain [1 Mark]:
$$\text{Extra Gain} = \text{C.I.} - \text{S.I.} = 2625 - 2500 = \text{₹}125$$
Q13. Quadrilateral $PQRS$ with diagonal $PR = 16\text{ cm}$ and offsets $6\text{ cm}$ and $8\text{ cm}$.

Quadrilateral PQRS Sketch with Diagonal and Offsets
