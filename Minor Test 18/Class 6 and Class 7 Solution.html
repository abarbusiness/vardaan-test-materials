DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Minor Test 18 (General Maths)
Section A (Objective Type)
Q1. What is the value of \( (-15) + (+8) \)?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) \( -7 \)
Explanation:
When adding integers with different signs, we subtract their absolute values and assign the sign of the larger absolute value.
\( |-15| = 15 \) and \( |+8| = 8 \).
Difference = \( 15 - 8 = 7 \).
Since 15 is greater and carries a negative sign, the result is \( -7 \).
Q2. Which of the following fractions is the largest? \( \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{2}{5}, \frac{5}{8} \)
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) \( \frac{3}{4} \)
Explanation:
Convert each fraction to a decimal or find a common denominator (LCM of 2, 4, 5, 8 is 40):
  • \( \frac{1}{2} = 0.50 \) (or \( \frac{20}{40} \))
  • \( \frac{3}{4} = 0.75 \) (or \( \frac{30}{40} \))
  • \( \frac{2}{5} = 0.40 \) (or \( \frac{16}{40} \))
  • \( \frac{5}{8} = 0.625 \) (or \( \frac{25}{40} \))
Comparing the values, \( 0.75 \) is the largest, hence \( \frac{3}{4} \) is the largest fraction.
Q3. If \( x = 3 \), find the value of \( 2x + 5 \).
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 11
Explanation:
Substitute \( x = 3 \) into the expression:
\( 2(3) + 5 \)
\( = 6 + 5 \)
\( = 11 \)
Q4. The ratio of 500g to 2kg is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (a) 1:4
Explanation:
To find the ratio, both quantities must be in the same unit. Convert 2kg to grams:
\( 2\text{ kg} = 2 \times 1000\text{ g} = 2000\text{ g} \).
Now, find the ratio:
\( \frac{500}{2000} = \frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4} \).
Therefore, the ratio is 1:4.
Q5. A piece of string is 20 cm long. What will be the length of each side if the string is used to form a square?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) 5 cm
Explanation:
The total length of the string forms the perimeter of the square.
Perimeter of a square = \( 4 \times \text{side} \)
\( 20 = 4 \times \text{side} \)
\( \text{Side} = \frac{20}{4} = 5\text{ cm} \).
Q6. The product of \( 2.5 \times 0.4 \) is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (d) 1.0
Explanation:
First, multiply the numbers ignoring the decimals: \( 25 \times 4 = 100 \).
Count the total decimal places in the original numbers (1 in 2.5 + 1 in 0.4 = 2 decimal places).
Place the decimal point 2 places from the right in the product: \( 1.00 = 1.0 \).
Q7. What is the average (mean) of the first 5 natural numbers?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (a) 3
Explanation:
The first 5 natural numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
Sum = \( 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15 \).
Average = \( \frac{\text{Sum of observations}}{\text{Number of observations}} = \frac{15}{5} = 3 \).
Q8. The fraction \( \frac{3}{4} \) expressed as a percentage is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 75%
Explanation:
To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply by 100%.
\( \frac{3}{4} \times 100\% = 3 \times 25\% = 75\% \).
Q9. Which property is shown by the equation \( a + b = b + a \)?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) Commutative
Explanation:
The commutative property of addition states that changing the order of the operands does not change the sum. Hence, \( a + b = b + a \).
Q10. The temperature in Shimla was 5°C. At night, it dropped by 7°C. What was the night temperature?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) -2°C
Explanation:
Initial temperature = 5°C.
Drop in temperature implies subtraction.
Night temperature = \( 5^\circ\text{C} - 7^\circ\text{C} = -2^\circ\text{C} \).
Section B (Short Answer Type)
Q11. Simplify the given expression: \( \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} \)
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: \( \frac{3}{4} \)
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the denominators 3, 4, and 6. LCM(3, 4, 6) = 12. (1 Mark)
2. Make the denominators equal by finding equivalent fractions:
\[ \frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} + \frac{1 \times 3}{4 \times 3} - \frac{1 \times 2}{6 \times 2} \] \[ = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} - \frac{2}{12} \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
3. Combine the numerators over the common denominator:
\[ = \frac{8 + 3 - 2}{12} = \frac{11 - 2}{12} = \frac{9}{12} \]
4. Simplify the fraction to its lowest terms by dividing numerator and denominator by 3:
\[ = \frac{9 \div 3}{12 \div 3} = \frac{3}{4} \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
Q12. Form an algebraic equation and solve it:
"I think of a number, multiply it by 4 and add 7 to the product. The final result is 35. Find the number."
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: The number is 7
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Let the unknown number be \( x \). (0.5 Mark)
2. Multiply the number by 4: \( 4x \)
3. Add 7 to the product: \( 4x + 7 \)
4. Set the expression equal to the final result to form the equation:
\[ 4x + 7 = 35 \quad \text{(1 Mark for equation)} \]
5. Solve the equation: Subtract 7 from both sides:
\[ 4x = 35 - 7 \] \[ 4x = 28 \quad \text{(0.5 Mark)} \]
6. Divide by 4:
\[ x = \frac{28}{4} = 7 \quad \text{(1 Mark for final answer)} \]
Q13. Divide an amount of ₹ 1500 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio 2:3. How much will each of them get?
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: Amit gets ₹ 600, Sumit gets ₹ 900
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The given ratio is 2 : 3.
2. Find the total number of ratio parts:
\[ \text{Total parts} = 2 + 3 = 5 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
3. Calculate Amit's share:
\[ \text{Amit's share} = \frac{2}{5} \times 1500 = 2 \times 300 = ₹ 600 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
4. Calculate Sumit's share:
\[ \text{Sumit's share} = \frac{3}{5} \times 1500 = 3 \times 300 = ₹ 900 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
(Alternatively, Sumit's share = Total - Amit's share = 1500 - 600 = ₹ 900).
Q14. Find the total cost of fencing a rectangular park of length 50m and breadth 30m, if the cost of fencing is ₹ 15 per metre.
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: ₹ 2400
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Identify the formula for the perimeter of a rectangle since fencing goes around the boundary:
\[ \text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{Length} + \text{Breadth}) \quad \text{(0.5 Mark)} \]
2. Substitute the given values (L = 50m, B = 30m):
\[ \text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (50 + 30) = 2 \times 80 = 160\text{ m} \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
3. Calculate the total cost of fencing:
\[ \text{Total Cost} = \text{Perimeter} \times \text{Rate per metre} \] \[ \text{Total Cost} = 160 \times 15 = ₹ 2400 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
Q15. In a class test containing 20 questions, 3 marks are given for every correct answer and (-1) mark is given for every incorrect answer. Radhika attempted 15 questions in total. If she got 10 correct answers and 5 incorrect answers, what is her total score?
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: 25 Marks
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Calculate the marks awarded for correct answers:
\[ \text{Marks for 10 correct answers} = 10 \times (+3) = +30 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
2. Calculate the marks deducted for incorrect answers:
\[ \text{Marks for 5 incorrect answers} = 5 \times (-1) = -5 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
3. Calculate the total score:
\[ \text{Total Score} = 30 + (-5) = 30 - 5 = 25 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
Section C (Long Answer / Competency Based)
Q16. A rectangular classroom floor is 10m long and 8m wide. A square carpet of side 6m is placed perfectly in the center of the floor.
(i) Find the total area of the classroom floor.
(ii) Find the area of the carpet.
(iii) Calculate the area of the floor that is NOT covered by the carpet.
(iv) If the cost of the carpet is ₹ 120 per sq.m, find the total cost of the carpet.
[5 Marks]
Final Answers: (i) 80 m², (ii) 36 m², (iii) 44 m², (iv) ₹ 4320
Step-by-Step Solution:
(i) Total area of the classroom floor:
The floor is a rectangle.
\[ \text{Area of rectangle} = \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth} = 10\text{m} \times 8\text{m} = 80\text{ m}^2 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
(ii) Area of the carpet:
The carpet is a square.
\[ \text{Area of square} = \text{side} \times \text{side} = 6\text{m} \times 6\text{m} = 36\text{ m}^2 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
(iii) Area NOT covered by the carpet:
\[ \text{Uncovered Area} = \text{Total Area of Floor} - \text{Area of Carpet} \] \[ \text{Uncovered Area} = 80\text{ m}^2 - 36\text{ m}^2 = 44\text{ m}^2 \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
(iv) Total cost of the carpet:
\[ \text{Total Cost} = \text{Area of Carpet} \times \text{Rate} \] \[ \text{Total Cost} = 36 \times 120 = ₹ 4320 \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
Q17. Rahul's monthly salary is ₹ 40,000. He plans his budget strictly. He spends \( \frac{1}{4} \) of his salary on house rent, \( \frac{3}{8} \) on food and groceries, and saves the remaining amount in his bank account.
(i) Calculate the exact amount he spends on house rent.
(ii) Calculate the exact amount he spends on food and groceries.
(iii) Find his total monthly savings.
(iv) What fraction of his total income does he manage to save?
[5 Marks]
Final Answers: (i) ₹ 10,000, (ii) ₹ 15,000, (iii) ₹ 15,000, (iv) \( \frac{3}{8} \)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Total Salary = ₹ 40,000
(i) Amount spent on house rent:
\[ \text{Rent} = \frac{1}{4} \text{ of } 40,000 = \frac{1}{4} \times 40,000 = ₹ 10,000 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
(ii) Amount spent on food and groceries:
\[ \text{Food} = \frac{3}{8} \text{ of } 40,000 = \frac{3}{8} \times 40,000 = 3 \times 5,000 = ₹ 15,000 \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
(iii) Total monthly savings:
First, find total expenditure.
\[ \text{Total Expenditure} = 10,000 + 15,000 = ₹ 25,000 \] \[ \text{Savings} = \text{Total Salary} - \text{Total Expenditure} \] \[ \text{Savings} = 40,000 - 25,000 = ₹ 15,000 \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
(iv) Fraction of income saved:
\[ \text{Fraction saved} = \frac{\text{Savings}}{\text{Total Income}} = \frac{15,000}{40,000} = \frac{15}{40} = \frac{3}{8} \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
Q18. The length of a rectangular garden is exactly twice its breadth. The total perimeter of this garden is 72 cm.
(i) Let the breadth be '\(x\)'. Write down the expression for the length.
(ii) Form an equation using the perimeter formula.
(iii) Solve the equation to find the actual length and breadth in cm.
(iv) Calculate the total area of the garden.
[5 Marks]
Final Answers: (i) \(2x\), (ii) \( 2(2x + x) = 72 \), (iii) B = 12cm, L = 24cm, (iv) 288 cm²
Step-by-Step Solution:
(i) Expression for the length:
Let Breadth (\(B\)) = \(x\). Since length is twice the breadth,
Length (\(L\)) = \( 2x \) \quad (1 Mark)
(ii) Form the equation:
Formula for perimeter is \( P = 2(L + B) \). Given \( P = 72 \).
\[ 2(2x + x) = 72 \quad \text{(1 Mark)} \]
(iii) Solve the equation:
\begin{align*} 2(3x) &= 72 \\ 6x &= 72 \\ x &= \frac{72}{6} \\ x &= 12 \end{align*}
Breadth (\(B\)) = \( x = 12\text{ cm} \).
Length (\(L\)) = \( 2x = 2(12) = 24\text{ cm} \). \quad (1.5 Marks)
(iv) Calculate the total area:
\[ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth} \] \[ \text{Area} = 24\text{ cm} \times 12\text{ cm} = 288\text{ cm}^2 \quad \text{(1.5 Marks)} \]
