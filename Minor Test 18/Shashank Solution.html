SECTION A - DETAILED SOLUTIONS (10 Marks)

Q1. (a) Place value of digit 7 in \(45.3\underline{7}8\). (b) Convert \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) to decimal.
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Place Value:

In the decimal number \(45.3\underline{7}8\):

  • The digit \(3\) is at the tenths place (\(\dfrac{3}{10}\)).
  • The digit \(7\) is at the hundredths place (\(\dfrac{7}{100}\) or \(0.07\)).

Answer: Hundredths (\(\dfrac{7}{100}\) or \(0.07\)).

(b) Fraction to Decimal:

\[ \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{3 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \dfrac{6}{10} = 0.6 \]

Answer: \(0.6\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for identifying place value correctly; 1 Mark for correct decimal conversion (\(0.6\)).
Q2. (a) Convert \(0.65\) to fraction. (b) Express \(5\text{ m } 25\text{ cm}\) in metres.
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Decimal to Simplest Fraction:

\[ 0.65 = \dfrac{65}{100} \]

Dividing numerator and denominator by their HCF (\(5\)):

\[ \dfrac{65 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \dfrac{13}{20} \]

Answer: \(\dfrac{13}{20}\)

(b) Expressing in Metres:

We know that \(100\text{ cm} = 1\text{ m} \implies 1\text{ cm} = \dfrac{1}{100}\text{ m}\).

\[ 25\text{ cm} = \dfrac{25}{100}\text{ m} = 0.25\text{ m} \]
\[ 5\text{ m } 25\text{ cm} = 5\text{ m} + 0.25\text{ m} = 5.25\text{ m} \]

Answer: \(5.25\text{ metres}\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for fraction in simplest form (\(13/20\)); 1 Mark for expressing as \(5.25\text{ m}\).
Q3. (a) Algebraic expression for statement. (b) Identify variable, coefficient, and constant in \(4y - 9\).
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Algebraic Expression:

Twice a number \(x = 2x\)

7 added to twice a number \(x = 2x + 7\)

Answer: \(2x + 7\)

(b) In the expression \(4y - 9\):

  • Variable: \(y\)
  • Coefficient of \(y\): \(4\)
  • Constant term: \(-9\) (or \(9\))
Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for correct expression (\(2x + 7\)); 1 Mark for identifying variable, coefficient, and constant.
Q4. Solve simple equations: (a) \(x + 8 = 15\) (b) \(3y = 21\)
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) \(x + 8 = 15\):

Transposing \(8\) to RHS:

\[ x = 15 - 8 \implies x = 7 \]

(b) \(3y = 21\):

Dividing both sides by \(3\):

\[ y = \dfrac{21}{3} \implies y = 7 \]
Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for solving \(x = 7\); 1 Mark for solving \(y = 7\).
Q5. Find value of \(5a - 3\) when \(a = 4\).
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given expression: \(5a - 3\)

Substitute \(a = 4\):

\[ 5a - 3 = 5 \times 4 - 3 \]
\[ = 20 - 3 = 17 \]

Answer: \(17\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for substitution; 1 Mark for getting final answer \(17\).

\(24.56\), \(8.90\), \(102.34\)

Sum = \(135.80\) or \(135.8\)

(b) Subtraction: Convert to like decimals:

\(45.80 - 18.25\)

Difference = \(27.55\)

Q7. (a) Multiply \(3.15 \times 1.2\). (b) Divide \(16.8 \div 4\).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Multiplication:

First multiply numbers ignoring decimal points: \(315 \times 12 = 3780\).

Total decimal places = \(2\) (in \(3.15\)) \(+\) \(1\) (in \(1.2\)) \(= 3\) decimal places.

\[ 3.15 \times 1.2 = 3.780 = 3.78 \]

(b) Division:

\[ 16.8 \div 4 = \dfrac{16.8}{4} = 4.2 \]

Explanation: \(16 \div 4 = 4\); place decimal point; then \(8 \div 4 = 2\). So, result is \(4.2\).

Marking Scheme: 1.5 Marks for multiplication (\(3.78\)); 1.5 Marks for division (\(4.2\)).
Q8. Add algebraic expressions: \((3x + 4y - 5)\) and \((2x - 2y + 8)\).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Arrange and combine like terms together:

\[ \text{Sum} = (3x + 4y - 5) + (2x - 2y + 8) \]

Grouping like terms:

\[ = (3x + 2x) + (4y - 2y) + (-5 + 8) \]
\[ = 5x + 2y + 3 \]

Answer: \(5x + 2y + 3\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for setting up addition; 1 Mark for grouping like terms; 1 Mark for final answer (\(5x + 2y + 3\)).
Q9. Subtract \((2a - 5b + 3)\) from \((6a + 2b - 1)\).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Write expression to subtract from first, and change signs of terms being subtracted:

\[ (6a + 2b - 1) - (2a - 5b + 3) \]

Opening brackets (changing signs of subtracted expression):

\[ = 6a + 2b - 1 - 2a + 5b - 3 \]

Grouping like terms:

\[ = (6a - 2a) + (2b + 5b) + (-1 - 3) \]
\[ = 4a + 7b - 4 \]

Answer: \(4a + 7b - 4\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for opening bracket with sign changes; 1 Mark for grouping like terms; 1 Mark for simplified answer.

SECTION C - DETAILED SOLUTIONS (15 Marks)

Q11. (a) Solve & verify \(2x + 5 = 19\). (b) Evaluate \(3p^2 - 4q + 5\) for \(p=3, q=2\).
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Solving \(2x + 5 = 19\):

Transposing \(5\) to RHS:

\[ 2x = 19 - 5 \implies 2x = 14 \]

Dividing by \(2\):

\[ x = \dfrac{14}{2} \implies x = 7 \]

Verification:

Substitute \(x = 7\) in LHS of original equation:

\[ \text{LHS} = 2(7) + 5 = 14 + 5 = 19 = \text{RHS} \]

Hence verified.

(b) Evaluating Expression:

Given: \(p = 3\) and \(q = 2\).

\[ 3p^2 - 4q + 5 = 3(3)^2 - 4(2) + 5 \]
\[ = 3(9) - 8 + 5 = 27 - 8 + 5 = 24 \]

Answer: \(24\)

Marking Scheme: (a) 2 Marks for finding \(x = 7\), 1 Mark for verification. (b) 1 Mark for substitution, 1 Mark for final value (\(24\)).
Q12. Word Problems: (a) Water remaining in drum. (b) Distance covered by car.
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Remaining Water in Drum:

  • Total capacity of water = \(45.50\text{ litres}\)
  • Water drawn out = \(18.75\text{ litres}\)
\[ \text{Remaining water} = 45.50 - 18.75 = 26.75\text{ litres} \]

Answer: \(26.75\text{ litres}\)

(b) Distance Covered by Car:

  • Distance covered in \(1\text{ litre}\) of petrol = \(14.25\text{ km}\)
  • Petrol available = \(6\text{ litres}\)

Total distance = \(14.25 \times 6\)

\[ \begin{array}{r@{\quad}l} 14.25 \\ \times \quad 6 \\ \hline 85.50 \end{array} \]

Answer: The car will cover \(85.5\text{ km}\) (or \(85.50\text{ km}\)).

Marking Scheme: (a) 2 Marks for calculating \(26.75\text{ litres}\). (b) 1 Mark for identifying multiplication operation, 2 Marks for correct product (\(85.5\text{ km}\)).
Q13. (a) Form equation & solve word problem. (b) Simplify algebraic expression.
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Forming & Solving Equation:

Let the required number be \(y\).

4 times the number = \(4y\)

Subtracting 6 from 4 times the number = \(4y - 6\)

According to the given condition:

\[ 4y - 6 = 18 \]

Transposing \(-6\) to RHS:

\[ 4y = 18 + 6 \implies 4y = 24 \]
\[ y = \dfrac{24}{4} \implies y = 6 \]

Answer: The required number is \(6\).

(b) Simplifying Algebraic Expression:

Given expression: \((5x + 3y) - (2x - 4y) + (x - 2y)\)

Remove brackets carefully:

\[ = 5x + 3y - 2x + 4y + x - 2y \]

Group like terms of \(x\) and \(y\):

\[ = (5x - 2x + x) + (3y + 4y - 2y) \]
\[ = 4x + 5y \]

Answer: \(4x + 5y\)

Marking Scheme: (a) 1 Mark for equation formation (\(4y - 6 = 18\)), 2 Marks for finding \(y = 6\). (b) 1 Mark for opening brackets properly, 1 Mark for final answer (\(4x + 5y\)).
