EXCELLENCE IN ACADEMIC ASSESSMENT
MINOR TEST 18
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Chemistry (Science) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Read each question carefully before answering.
  • The question paper consists of three sections: A (10 Marks), B (15 Marks), and C (15 Marks).
  • Write neat, clear, and well-structured answers using proper terms.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] Define the following terms in one sentence each:
(a) Potable water
(b) Transpiration
Q2.
[2 Marks] Name the two gases released from vehicular exhausts and factories that mix with rainwater to form acid rain. State one harmful effect of acid rain on historical monuments.
Q3.
[2 Marks] Define a solute and a solvent. In a sugar-water solution, identify which substance acts as the solute and which acts as the solvent.
Q4.
[2 Marks] What is an aquifer? Name any two man-made structures or devices used to extract underground water for human use.
Q5.
[2 Marks] Differentiate clearly between Evaporation and Condensation in the context of the natural water cycle.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] (a) Why is water termed as a universal solvent?
(b) What do you understand by a saturated solution? What happens when a saturated solution is heated?
Q7.
[3 Marks] Water exists on Earth in all three physical states (solid, liquid, and gas). Name these three states and provide one specific natural example where each state is found.
Q8.
[3 Marks] Explain the term 'Algal Bloom'. Describe briefly how the excessive washing of agricultural fertilisers into water bodies causes aquatic animals (like fish) to die of oxygen starvation.
Q9.
[3 Marks] Compare a Drought and a Flood by stating:
(a) The main cause of each condition.
(b) One major adverse effect of each condition on human life or the environment.
Q10.
[3 Marks] (a) What is meant by Rainwater Harvesting?
(b) List two simple, effective practices that can be followed at home to avoid wasting clean drinking water.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] The municipal supply of water to urban homes involves systematic purification stages.
(a) State the specific purpose and process occurring in each of the following tanks during municipal water treatment:
i. Loading Tank (addition of alum)
ii. Filtration Tank
iii. Chlorination Tank
(b) Mention two common chemical/household methods used to make water safe for drinking at home.
Q12.
[5 Marks] (a) Describe the continuous Water Cycle in nature step-by-step, explaining how water moves between the Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
(b) State two important reasons why the water cycle is essential for sustaining life on Earth.
Q13.
[5 Marks] (a) Define water pollution and water pollutants.
(b) Discuss three major human activities or sources that contaminate natural water bodies.
(c) State one preventive measure factories should take before discharging liquid waste into rivers.
