SECTION A (10 Marks) (Multiple Choice & Concept Check — 5 Questions $\times$ 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] What is the additive inverse of the product of $-\frac{3}{7}$ and $\frac{14}{9}$?
(a) $-\frac{2}{3}$
(b) $\frac{2}{3}$
(c) $\frac{3}{2}$
(d) $-\frac{3}{2}$
Q2.
[2 Marks] If $3x - 5 = 2x + 7$, then the value of $x^2 - 10$ is equal to:
(a) $134$
(b) $12$
(c) $144$
(d) $154$
Q3.
[2 Marks] The sum of interior angles of a convex polygon with $6$ sides (Hexagon) is:
(a) $360^\circ$
(b) $540^\circ$
(c) $720^\circ$
(d) $900^\circ$
Q4.
[2 Marks] By what smallest number should $180$ be multiplied to make it a perfect square?
(a) $2$
(b) $3$
(c) $5$
(d) $10$
Q5.
[2 Marks] A shopkeeper bought a shirt for ₹$800$ and sold it for ₹$960$. What is his profit percentage?
(a) $15\%$
(b) $20\%$
(c) $25\%$
(d) $16.6\%$
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type — 5 Questions $\times$ 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Represent $\frac{-3}{5}$ and $\frac{7}{5}$ on the same number line.
Q7.
[3 Marks] Solve the linear equation for $x$: $$\frac{x + 3}{2} - \frac{2x - 1}{3} = \frac{5}{6}$$
Q8.
[3 Marks] In a parallelogram $ABCD$, angle $\angle A = (2x + 10)^\circ$ and adjacent angle $\angle B = (3x - 20)^\circ$. Find the value of $x$ and measure of all four angles of the parallelogram.
Q9.
[3 Marks] Find the square root of $1764$ using the prime factorization method.
Q10.
[3 Marks] Simplify using algebraic identities: $(2a + 3b)^2 - (2a - 3b)^2$.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer & Application Type — 3 Questions $\times$ 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] The perimeter of a rectangular park is $180\text{ m}$. If its length is $10\text{ m}$ more than twice its breadth:
(a) Formulate a linear equation representing this situation. [2 Marks]
(b) Find the dimensions (length and breadth) of the rectangular park. [2 Marks]
(c) Calculate the area of the park. [1 Mark]
Q12.
[5 Marks] Rohan invested ₹$12,500$ in a bank for $2$ years at an interest rate of $10\%$ per annum.
(a) Calculate the Simple Interest (S.I.) and total amount if interest is calculated simply. [2 Marks]
(b) Calculate the Compound Interest (C.I.) compounded annually. [2 Marks]
(c) Find the extra gain Rohan receives by choosing compound interest over simple interest. [1 Mark]
Q13.
[5 Marks] In the quadrilateral $PQRS$, diagonal $PR = 16\text{ cm}$. The perpendicular distances (offsets) to $PR$ from opposite vertices $Q$ and $S$ are $h_1 = 6\text{ cm}$ and $h_2 = 8\text{ cm}$ respectively.
(a) Draw a clear rough sketch of quadrilateral $PQRS$ with its given diagonal and offsets. [2 Marks]
(b) Derive the formula for the area of a general quadrilateral given a diagonal and its offsets. [1 Mark]
(c) Calculate the total area of quadrilateral $PQRS$. [2 Marks]
