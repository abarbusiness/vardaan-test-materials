Minor Test 18 - General Mathematics
Class: 8 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Show all essential steps in mathematical calculations.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate: \( \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{-2} + \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{-2} + \left( \frac{1}{4} \right)^{-2} \)
2.
Find the smallest natural number by which \(2560\) must be multiplied so that the product becomes a perfect cube.
3.
Solve the following linear equation for \(x\):
\( 3(x - 3) = 5(2x + 1) \)
4.
Find the measure of each exterior angle of a regular polygon having 15 sides.
5.
Factorise the algebraic expression completely: \( p^2 - 10p + 25 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A shopkeeper marks his goods at 20% above the cost price and allows a discount of 10% to the customers. Find his overall gain percentage.
7.
A garrison of 1200 men has provisions for 60 days. After 15 days, 300 men leave the garrison. How long will the remaining food last for the rest of the men?
8.
Subtract \( 3l(l - 4m + 5n) \) from \( 4l(10n - 3m + 2l) \). Simplify your answer.
9.
The area of a rhombus is \( 240 \text{ cm}^2 \) and one of its diagonals is \( 16 \text{ cm} \). Find the length of the other diagonal and calculate the perimeter of the rhombus.
10.
Find any three rational numbers between \( \frac{-1}{2} \) and \( \frac{1}{4} \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A closed cylindrical tank of radius \( 7 \text{ m} \) and height \( 3 \text{ m} \) is to be made from a sheet of metal.
(a) How much sheet of metal is required to make the tank? (Assume \( \pi = \frac{22}{7} \))
(b) If the cost of the metal sheet is ₹ 50 per \( \text{m}^2 \), find the total cost of the metal used.
12.
The digits of a two-digit positive number differ by \( 3 \). If the digits are interchanged, and the resulting newly formed number is added to the original number, the sum is \( 143 \). Find the possible values of the original number.
13.
Arun borrows ₹ 16,000 for \( 1 \frac{1}{2} \) years at \( 10\% \) per annum.
(a) Calculate the total amount he has to pay if the interest is compounded half-yearly.
(b) Calculate the total amount if the interest was compounded annually. Which option results in him paying more interest, and by how much?
