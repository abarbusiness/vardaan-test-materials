Minor Test 18 : General Mathematics
Class: 6 & 7 (Combined) Subject: General Maths Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • The question paper consists of 18 questions divided into 3 sections.
  • Section A: 10 Multiple Choice Questions of 1 mark each.
  • Section B: 5 Short Answer Questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C: 3 Long Answer/Competency Questions of 5 marks each.
  • Use of calculators is strictly prohibited. Show clear steps for Section B and C.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Objective Type - Attempt all questions - 1 Mark Each)
Q1. What is the value of \( (-15) + (+8) \)?
(a) \( -23 \)
(b) \( -7 \)
(c) \( +7 \)
(d) \( +23 \)
Q2. Which of the following fractions is the largest?
(a) \( \frac{1}{2} \)
(b) \( \frac{3}{4} \)
(c) \( \frac{2}{5} \)
(d) \( \frac{5}{8} \)
Q3. If \( x = 3 \), find the value of \( 2x + 5 \).
(a) 10
(b) 11
(c) 16
(d) 8
Q4. The ratio of 500g to 2kg is:
(a) 1:4
(b) 4:1
(c) 1:2
(d) 500:2
Q5. A piece of string is 20 cm long. What will be the length of each side if the string is used to form a square?
(a) 10 cm
(b) 4 cm
(c) 5 cm
(d) 20 cm
Q6. The product of \( 2.5 \times 0.4 \) is:
(a) 10.0
(b) 0.1
(c) 100
(d) 1.0
Q7. What is the average (mean) of the first 5 natural numbers?
(a) 3
(b) 4
(c) 5
(d) 15
Q8. The fraction \( \frac{3}{4} \) expressed as a percentage is:
(a) 34%
(b) 75%
(c) 60%
(d) 80%
Q9. Which property is shown by the equation \( a + b = b + a \)?
(a) Associative
(b) Distributive
(c) Commutative
(d) Closure
Q10. The temperature in Shimla was 5°C. At night, it dropped by 7°C. What was the night temperature?
(a) 12°C
(b) -2°C
(c) 2°C
(d) -12°C
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q11.
Simplify the given expression: \( \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} \)
Q12.
Form an algebraic equation and solve it:
"I think of a number, multiply it by 4 and add 7 to the product. The final result is 35. Find the number."
Q13.
Divide an amount of ₹ 1500 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio 2:3. How much will each of them get?
Q14.
Find the total cost of fencing a rectangular park of length 50m and breadth 30m, if the cost of fencing is ₹ 15 per metre.
Q15.
In a class test containing 20 questions, 3 marks are given for every correct answer and (-1) mark is given for every incorrect answer. Radhika attempted 15 questions in total. If she got 10 correct answers and 5 incorrect answers, what is her total score?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer / Competency Based - 5 Marks Each)
Q16.
(Competency - Area & Perimeter)
A rectangular classroom floor is 10m long and 8m wide. A square carpet of side 6m is placed perfectly in the center of the floor.
(i) Find the total area of the classroom floor.
(ii) Find the area of the carpet.
(iii) Calculate the area of the floor that is NOT covered by the carpet.
(iv) If the cost of the carpet is ₹ 120 per sq.m, find the total cost of the carpet.
Q17.
(Real-life Application - Fractions)
Rahul's monthly salary is ₹ 40,000. He plans his budget strictly. He spends \( \frac{1}{4} \) of his salary on house rent, \( \frac{3}{8} \) on food and groceries, and saves the remaining amount in his bank account.
(i) Calculate the exact amount he spends on house rent.
(ii) Calculate the exact amount he spends on food and groceries.
(iii) Find his total monthly savings.
(iv) What fraction of his total income does he manage to save?
Q18.
(Algebra & Geometry Integration)
The length of a rectangular garden is exactly twice its breadth. The total perimeter of this garden is 72 cm.
(i) Let the breadth be '\(x\)'. Write down the expression for the length.
(ii) Form an equation using the perimeter formula.
(iii) Solve the equation to find the actual length and breadth in cm.
(iv) Calculate the total area of the garden.
