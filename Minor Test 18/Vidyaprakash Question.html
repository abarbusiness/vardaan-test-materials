Minor Test 18 - Mathematics
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 2 Hours Max. Marks: 80
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 10 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 10 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 6 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Show all essential steps of working. Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (20 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Reanna has a special chocolate that triples every day if not eaten. If she has 1 chocolate on day 1, express the number of chocolates she will have on day \( n \) in exponential form.
2.
How many \( 1 \times 1 \) unit squares and \( 8 \times 8 \) squares can be made on a standard \( 8 \times 8 \) chess board?
3.
Use the special case of the distributive property to perform the quick multiplication: \( 14 \times 17 \).
4.
Without actually computing, determine whether the arithmetic expression \( 53 \times 47 \) has an even or odd parity. Give a reason for your answer.
5.
Using the rules of divisibility, check if the number 417 is divisible by 3. Show your working.
6.
A car travels 275 km in 5 hours. How many kilometers will it cover in 3.5 hours?
7.
Find the digital root of the number 352.
8.
Name the quadrilateral in which two non-overlapping pairs of adjacent sides are equal, and whose longer diagonal bisects the shorter diagonal at \( 90^\circ \).
9.
Express the number 25 as the sum of two consecutive natural numbers.
10.
Write the algebraic expression for a 2-digit number in its generalised form, assuming the tens digit is \( x \) and the ones digit is \( y \).
SECTION B (30 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
11.
Prove algebraically that the sum of any four consecutive integers always has an even parity.
12.
The income of Shobha is ₹1,44,000 in 15 months. Apply proportional reasoning to find how much she will earn in 7 months.
13.
State the congruence rule used to prove that the opposite sides of a rectangle are equal. Show the steps of proof by drawing a diagonal in rectangle ABCD.
14.
Test the divisibility of the number 661314 by 11. Clearly state the rule and show your calculation.
15.
In a quadrilateral, the four angles are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5 : 8. Find the measure of each angle.
16.
Perform the multiplication \( 24 \times 102 \) systematically by expanding the algebraic expression using the distributive property.
17.
The sum of six consecutive numbers is 87. Formulate an equation and find all six numbers.
18.
A map has a scale of 1 cm = 2 km. If the distance between two colonies on the map is 3.5 cm, use the method of cross-multiplication (Rule of Three) to find the actual distance.
19.
Show that when we add an even number that is a multiple of 4 to an even number that is NOT a multiple of 4, the sum leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 4.
20.
Gautam has two pocket money options for 10 days:
Option 1: Take ₹100 every day.
Option 2: Take ₹2 on the first day, and double the amount of the previous day every subsequent day.
Calculate the total amount in both cases and state which option he should choose.
SECTION C (30 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
21.
(a) Define a rhombus. (1 Mark)
(b) Using congruent triangles, prove geometrically that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles. (4 Marks)
22.
If 15 envelopes cost ₹750, solve the following using proportional reasoning:
(a) What will be the cost of 36 such envelopes? (2.5 Marks)
(b) How many envelopes can be bought for ₹450? (2.5 Marks)
23.
(a) Write the generalised expanded form of a 3-digit number having \( x \), \( y \), and \( z \) at the hundreds, tens, and ones places respectively. (1 Mark)
(b) Using this generalised form, derive and prove the test of divisibility by 9. (4 Marks)
24.
(a) The sum of four consecutive numbers is 58. Find the numbers algebraically. (3 Marks)
(b) If \( p \) is the greatest of five consecutive numbers, write the algebraic expressions for the other four numbers in terms of \( p \). (2 Marks)
25.
(a) Differentiate between a convex and a concave quadrilateral. (2 Marks)
(b) State any three specific properties of a Kite. (3 Marks)
26.
(a) What is the "Digital Root" of a number? Calculate the digital root of 5910821. (2 Marks)
(b) State the correlation between the digital root of a number and the remainder when that number is divided by 9. (2 Marks)
(c) Express the number 10,000 in exponential form with base 10. (1 Mark)
