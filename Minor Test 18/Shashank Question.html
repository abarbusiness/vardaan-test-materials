EXCELLENCE IN MATHEMATICAL LEARNING
MINOR TEST 18
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Decimals & Algebra) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Read each question carefully.
  • The question paper consists of three sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • Show proper calculation steps wherever required. Neat work is appreciated.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] (a) Write the place value of the underlined digit in the decimal number \(45.3\underline{7}8\).
(b) Convert the fraction \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) into a decimal number.
Q2.
[2 Marks] (a) Convert the decimal \(0.65\) into a fraction in its simplest form.
(b) Express \(5\text{ m } 25\text{ cm}\) in metres using decimal notation.
Q3.
[2 Marks] (a) Write an algebraic expression for: "7 added to twice a number \(x\)".
(b) In the algebraic expression \(4y - 9\), identify the variable, coefficient of \(y\), and the constant term.
Q4.
[2 Marks] Solve the following simple linear equations:
(a) \(x + 8 = 15\)
(b) \(3y = 21\)
Q5.
[2 Marks] Find the numerical value of the algebraic expression \(5a - 3\) when \(a = 4\).
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Perform the following operations with decimals:
(a) Add: \(24.56 + 8.9 + 102.34\)
(b) Subtract: \(45.8 - 18.25\)
Q7.
[3 Marks] Calculate the following:
(a) Multiply: \(3.15 \times 1.2\)
(b) Divide: \(16.8 \div 4\)
Q8.
[3 Marks] Add the algebraic expressions: \((3x + 4y - 5)\) and \((2x - 2y + 8)\).
Q9.
[3 Marks] Subtract \((2a - 5b + 3)\) from \((6a + 2b - 1)\).
Q10.
[3 Marks] Rohan had \(\text{₹ } 50.00\). He bought a notebook for \(\text{₹ } 22.50\) and a pen for \(\text{₹ } 12.75\). How much money is left with Rohan?
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] (a) Solve the linear equation: \(2x + 5 = 19\). Show step-by-step working and verify your answer by substituting the value back into the equation. [3 Marks]
(b) If \(p = 3\) and \(q = 2\), find the value of the algebraic expression: \(3p^2 - 4q + 5\). [2 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] (a) A drum contains \(45.5\text{ litres}\) of water. If \(18.75\text{ litres}\) of water is drawn out, how much water is remaining in the drum? [2 Marks]
(b) A car covers a distance of \(14.25\text{ km}\) in \(1\text{ litre}\) of petrol. How much total distance will it cover in \(6\text{ litres}\) of petrol? [3 Marks]
Q13.
[5 Marks] (a) Form a linear equation and solve it: "If 6 is subtracted from 4 times a number \(y\), the result is 18. Find the number \(y\)." [3 Marks]
(b) Simplify the expression by combining like terms: \((5x + 3y) - (2x - 4y) + (x - 2y)\). [2 Marks]
