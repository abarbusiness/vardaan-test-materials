Minor Test 18 - Mathematics
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Factorization) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Show all essential steps of working.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Factorise by taking out the highest common factor:
\( 12x^2y^3 - 21x^3y^2 \)
2.
Factorise the given expression completely:
\( a(x + y) + b(x + y) \)
3.
Factorise using the identity for the difference of two squares:
\( x^2 - 81 \)
4.
Factorise the quadratic trinomial:
\( p^2 - p - 42 \)
5.
Evaluate using factorization:
\( (9.8)^2 - (0.2)^2 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Factorise by grouping the terms appropriately:
\( ax + bx - ay - by \)
7.
Factorise completely:
\( 16a^4 - 81b^4 \)
8.
Factorise by splitting the middle term:
\( 2x^2 + 7x + 6 \)
9.
Factorise the expression:
\( (x - y)^2 - 4(x - y) - 21 \)
(Hint: Substitute \( x - y \) with another variable first)
10.
Factorise using standard identities:
\( 25(a - b)^2 - 16(a + b)^2 \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Factorise completely: \( x^4 - y^4 \) (2.5 Marks)
(b) Factorise: \( x^2 - 1 - 2a - a^2 \) (2.5 Marks)
12.
(a) Factorise the quadratic expression: \( 3x^2 - 14x + 8 \) (2.5 Marks)
(b) The area of a rectangle is given by the polynomial \( 2x^2 + 9x - 5 \). Find the possible algebraic expressions for its length and breadth. (2.5 Marks)
13.
(a) Factorise by rearranging and grouping the terms: \( xy(z^2 + 1) + z(x^2 + y^2) \) (2.5 Marks)
(b) Evaluate using the algebraic formula for the difference of two squares: \( 105 \times 95 \) (2.5 Marks)
