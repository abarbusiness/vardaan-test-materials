Minor Test 14 - Set B: Current Electricity (Part 2)
Class: 12 CBSE Subject: Physics Time: 2 Hours Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Focus on numerical accuracy and logical steps.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A consists of 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B consists of 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C consists of 5 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Use of log tables is permitted. Calculators are strictly prohibited.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
How does the drift velocity of free electrons in a metallic conductor vary with (i) an increase in temperature (keeping potential difference constant), and (ii) an increase in the applied potential difference? Give reasons.
2.
A uniform metallic wire of resistance \(10 \, \Omega\) is stretched so that its length increases by 10%. Calculate the new resistance of the wire.
3.
A heating coil is rated at \(100 \text{ W}, 220 \text{ V}\). It is cut into two identical halves and both halves are connected in parallel to the same source of \(220 \text{ V}\). Find the total power consumed by the new combination.
4.
In a potentiometer arrangement, a cell of emf \(1.25 \text{ V}\) gives a balance point at \(35.0 \text{ cm}\) length of the wire. If the cell is replaced by another cell and the balance point shifts to \(63.0 \text{ cm}\), what is the emf of the second cell?
5.
The resistance of a platinum wire in a resistance thermometer at the ice point is \(5.00 \, \Omega\) and at the steam point is \(5.23 \, \Omega\). When the thermometer is inserted in a hot bath, the resistance of the platinum wire becomes \(5.795 \, \Omega\). Calculate the temperature of the bath.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer / Numerical Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A cell of emf '\(E\)' and internal resistance '\(r\)' supplies a current of \(0.5 \text{ A}\) when connected to a \(12 \, \Omega\) resistor, and a current of \(0.25 \text{ A}\) when connected to a \(25 \, \Omega\) resistor. Calculate the values of \(E\) and \(r\).
7.
In a meter bridge, the balance point is found to be at \(39.5 \text{ cm}\) from the left end A when the known resistor \(Y\) in the right gap is of \(12.5 \, \Omega\).
(i) Determine the resistance of \(X\) in the left gap.
(ii) What happens if the galvanometer and cell are interchanged at the balance point of the bridge? Would the galvanometer show any current?
8.
State the working principle of a potentiometer. Explain with the help of a relevant formula how the sensitivity of a potentiometer can be increased.
9.
24 identical cells, each of emf \(1.5 \text{ V}\) and internal resistance \(0.5 \, \Omega\), are connected in a mixed grouping of \(m\) rows and \(n\) columns. They are configured to send maximum current through an external resistance of \(3 \, \Omega\). Find the values of \(m\) and \(n\), and calculate this maximum current.
10.
The V-I graph for a non-ideal cell is a straight line intersecting the Voltage axis at \(1.5 \text{ V}\) and the Current axis at \(3.0 \text{ A}\). Deduce:
(i) the EMF of the cell.
(ii) the internal resistance of the cell.
(iii) the maximum power that this cell can deliver to an external circuit.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer / Complex Numerical Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Find the equivalent resistance of the infinite ladder network shown below between points A and B. The network consists of series resistors of \(1 \, \Omega\) and parallel (shunt) resistors of \(2 \, \Omega\).
12.
Apply Kirchhoff's laws to the given electrical circuit to calculate the magnitude and direction of the current flowing through the \(5 \, \Omega\) resistor.
13.
(a) State the necessary condition for a Wheatstone bridge to be balanced. [1]
(b) In the network shown below, calculate the value of the unknown resistance \(R\) for which the galvanometer shows zero deflection. [4]
14.
Two heating coils of resistances \(R_1\) and \(R_2\) take times \(t_1 = 15 \text{ min}\) and \(t_2 = 30 \text{ min}\) respectively to boil a certain mass of water when connected individually across a source of constant voltage \(V\). Calculate the time taken to boil the exact same mass of water if these coils are connected:
(a) in series across the same source. [2.5]
(b) in parallel across the same source. [2.5]
15.
(a) Derive an expression for the equivalent emf and internal resistance of two cells of emfs \(E_1\) and \(E_2\) and internal resistances \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) connected in series. [3]
(b) Find the potential difference between points X and Y (\(V_X - V_Y\)) in the given branch of a circuit. A steady current of \(2 \text{ A}\) flows from X to Y. [2]
