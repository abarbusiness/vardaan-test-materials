Mega Test - Trigonometry & Heights and Distances
Class: 10 Subject: Mathematics Time: 2 Hours Max. Marks: 150
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Pace yourself strictly due to the 2-hour limit.
  • The paper is divided into four sections: A, B, C, and D.
  • Section A comprises 15 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 15 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 5 questions of 4 marks each.
  • Section D comprises 11 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write answers neatly and draw clear, precise diagrams for height and distance problems.
SECTION A (30 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following expression:
$$ \frac{2 \tan 30^\circ}{1 + \tan^2 30^\circ} $$
2.
If $\sin(A - B) = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\cos(A + B) = \frac{1}{2}$, where $0^\circ \lt A + B \le 90^\circ$ and $A \gt B$, find the values of $A$ and $B$.
3.
Prove the following trigonometric identity:
$$ (\sec A + \tan A)(1 - \sin A) = \cos A $$
4.
Find the numerical value of the expression: $9 \sec^2 A - 9 \tan^2 A$. Show the identity used.
5.
The shadow of a vertical tower on level ground is observed to be $\sqrt{3}$ times its height. Find the angle of elevation of the sun at that moment.
6.
If $\tan A = \frac{4}{3}$, find the value of $\sin A + \cos A$.
7.
Evaluate: $\frac{\sin^2 63^\circ + \sin^2 27^\circ}{\cos^2 17^\circ + \cos^2 73^\circ}$
8.
A ladder $15$ m long just reaches the top of a vertical wall. If the ladder makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the wall, find the height of the wall.
9.
Prove that $(\sin \theta + \cos \theta)^2 = 1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta$.
10.
Find the value of $(\sec^2 \theta - 1)(1 - \text{cosec}^2 \theta)$.
11.
If $\sin \theta = \cos \theta$, find the value of $2 \tan^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta - 1$.
12.
In $\Delta PQR$, right-angled at $Q$, $PR + QR = 25$ cm and $PQ = 5$ cm. Determine the value of $\sin P$.
13.
If $\sin(A+2B) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\cos(A+4B) = 0$ (where angles are acute), find the values of $A$ and $B$.
14.
Prove that $\frac{\sin^3 \theta + \cos^3 \theta}{\sin \theta + \cos \theta} + \sin \theta \cos \theta = 1$.
15.
A vertical pole casts a shadow of length $2\sqrt{3}$ m on the ground when the sun's elevation is $60^\circ$. Find the height of the pole.
SECTION B (45 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
16.
If $3 \cot A = 4$, check whether the following equation is true or false. Show complete working.
$$ \frac{1 - \tan^2 A}{1 + \tan^2 A} = \cos^2 A - \sin^2 A $$
17.
Prove that:
$$ \frac{\cos A}{1 + \sin A} + \frac{1 + \sin A}{\cos A} = 2 \sec A $$
18.
Prove the following identity by rationalizing the denominator inside the root:
$$ \sqrt{\frac{1 + \sin A}{1 - \sin A}} = \sec A + \tan A $$
19.
A kite is flying at a height of 60 m above the ground. The string attached to the kite is temporarily tied to a point on the ground. The inclination of the string with the ground is $60^\circ$. Find the length of the string, assuming that there is no slack in the string.
20.
Prove the following important trigonometric identity:
$$ (\sin A + \text{cosec} A)^2 + (\cos A + \sec A)^2 = 7 + \tan^2 A + \cot^2 A $$
21.
If $\sec \theta = x + \frac{1}{4x}$, prove that $\sec \theta + \tan \theta = 2x$ or $\frac{1}{2x}$.
22.
Prove that $$ \frac{\sin \theta - \cos \theta + 1}{\sin \theta + \cos \theta - 1} = \frac{1}{\sec \theta - \tan \theta} $$ using the identity $\sec^2 \theta = 1 + \tan^2 \theta$.
23.
The angle of elevation of the top of a building from the foot of the tower is $30^\circ$ and the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from the foot of the building is $60^\circ$. If the tower is 50 m high, find the height of the building.
24.
If $\frac{\cos \alpha}{\cos \beta} = m$ and $\frac{\cos \alpha}{\sin \beta} = n$, show that $(m^2 + n^2) \cos^2 \beta = n^2$.
25.
An observer $1.5$ m tall is $28.5$ m away from a chimney. The angle of elevation of the top of the chimney from her eyes is $45^\circ$. What is the height of the chimney?
26.
If $x \sin^3 \theta + y \cos^3 \theta = \sin \theta \cos \theta$ and $x \sin \theta = y \cos \theta$, prove that $x^2 + y^2 = 1$.
27.
Prove that: $(1 + \cot A - \text{cosec} A)(1 + \tan A + \sec A) = 2$.
28.
From the top of a 7 m high building, the angle of elevation of the top of a cable tower is $60^\circ$ and the angle of depression of its foot is $45^\circ$. Determine the height of the tower.
29.
If $\cos \theta + \sin \theta = \sqrt{2} \cos \theta$, show that $\cos \theta - \sin \theta = \sqrt{2} \sin \theta$.
30.
The angle of elevation of the top of a vertical tower from a point on the ground is $60^\circ$. From another point 10 m vertically above the first, its angle of elevation is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
SECTION C (20 Marks)(Long Answer Type I - 4 Marks Each)
31.
Two poles of equal heights are standing opposite each other on either side of the road, which is 80 m wide. From a point between them on the road, the angles of elevation of the top of the poles are $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$, respectively. Find the height of the poles and the distances of the point from the poles.
32.
A TV tower stands vertically on a bank of a canal. From a point on the other bank directly opposite the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $60^\circ$. From another point 20 m away from this point on the line joining this point to the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $30^\circ$. Find the height of the tower and the width of the canal.
33.
Prove that:
$$ \frac{\tan^3 \theta}{1 + \tan^2 \theta} + \frac{\cot^3 \theta}{1 + \cot^2 \theta} = \sec \theta \text{cosec} \theta - 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta $$
34.
If $m = \cos A - \sin A$ and $n = \cos A + \sin A$, show that $$ \frac{m^2 + n^2}{m^2 - n^2} = -\frac{1}{2} \sec A \text{cosec} A $$
35.
A straight highway leads to the foot of a tower. A man standing at the top of the tower observes a car at an angle of depression of $30^\circ$, which is approaching the foot of the tower with a uniform speed. Six seconds later, the angle of depression of the car is found to be $60^\circ$. Find the time taken by the car to reach the foot of the tower from this point.
SECTION D (55 Marks)(Long Answer Type II - 5 Marks Each)
36.
Expressing the ratios in terms of sine and cosine, prove that:
$$ \frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} = 1 + \sec \theta \text{cosec} \theta $$
37.
If $\sin \theta + \cos \theta = \sqrt{3}$, then prove that $\tan \theta + \cot \theta = 1$.
38.
A $1.2$ m tall girl spots a balloon moving with the wind in a horizontal line at a height of $88.2$ m from the ground. The angle of elevation of the balloon from the eyes of the girl at any instant is $60^\circ$. After some time, the angle of elevation reduces to $30^\circ$. Find the distance travelled by the balloon during the interval.

39.
The angle of elevation of an aeroplane from a point on the ground is $60^\circ$. After a flight of $15$ seconds, the angle of elevation changes to $30^\circ$. If the aeroplane is flying at a constant height of $1500\sqrt{3}$ m, find the speed of the plane in km/hr.
40.
The angle of elevation of a cloud from a point $60$ m above a lake is $30^\circ$ and the angle of depression of the reflection of the cloud in the lake is $60^\circ$. Find the height of the cloud from the surface of the lake.
41.
If $\sec \theta + \tan \theta = p$, prove that $$ \sin \theta = \frac{p^2 - 1}{p^2 + 1} $$
42.
Prove that $\frac{\cos A - \sin A + 1}{\cos A + \sin A - 1} = \text{cosec} A + \cot A$, using the identity $\text{cosec}^2 A = 1 + \cot^2 A$.
43.
An aeroplane is flying at a height of 300 m above the ground. Flying at this height, the angles of depression from the aeroplane of two points on both banks of a river in opposite directions are $45^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ respectively. Find the width of the river. (Take $\sqrt{3} = 1.732$)
44.
Prove that: $$ \frac{\tan \theta + \sec \theta - 1}{\tan \theta - \sec \theta + 1} = \frac{1 + \sin \theta}{\cos \theta} $$
45.
A boy standing on a horizontal plane finds a bird flying at a distance of 100 m from him at an elevation of $30^\circ$. A girl standing on the roof of a 20 m high building, finds the angle of elevation of the same bird to be $45^\circ$. Both the boy and the girl are on opposite sides of the bird. Find the distance of the bird from the girl.
46.
If $\tan \theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}$, find the exact numerical value of:
$$ \frac{\text{cosec}^2 \theta - \sec^2 \theta}{\text{cosec}^2 \theta + \sec^2 \theta} $$
