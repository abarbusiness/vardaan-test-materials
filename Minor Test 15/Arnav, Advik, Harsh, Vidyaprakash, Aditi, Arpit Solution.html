SECTION A
Q1. Find the selling price of an article if its cost price is ₹ 500 and the profit made on it is 10%.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Cost Price (CP) = \( \text{₹ } 500 \), Profit % = \( 10\% \).
\[ \text{Selling Price (SP)} = \text{CP} \times \left( 1 + \frac{\text{Profit \%}}{100} \right) \] \[ \text{SP} = 500 \times \left( 1 + \frac{10}{100} \right) = 500 \times \frac{110}{100} \] \[ \text{SP} = 5 \times 110 = 550 \]
The selling price of the article is ₹ 550.
Q2. A shirt marked at ₹ 800 is sold for ₹ 680 during a festive sale. Find the discount given and the discount percentage.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 800 \), Selling Price (SP) = \( \text{₹ } 680 \).
\[ \text{Discount} = \text{MP} - \text{SP} = 800 - 680 = \text{₹ } 120 \] \[ \text{Discount \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Discount}}{\text{MP}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Discount \%} = \left( \frac{120}{800} \right) \times 100\% = \frac{120}{8}\% = 15\% \]
Discount given is ₹ 120 and the Discount Percentage is 15%.
Q3. Calculate the VAT (Value Added Tax) charged at 5% on a washing machine bought for ₹ 12,000.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Price of washing machine = \( \text{₹ } 12,000 \), VAT Rate = \( 5\% \).
\[ \text{VAT Amount} = \text{Rate of VAT} \times \text{Price} \] \[ \text{VAT Amount} = \frac{5}{100} \times 12,000 = 5 \times 120 = \text{₹ } 600 \]
The VAT charged is ₹ 600.
Q4. By selling a textbook for ₹ 250, a bookseller makes a profit of ₹ 50. Find his profit percentage.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Selling Price (SP) = \( \text{₹ } 250 \), Profit = \( \text{₹ } 50 \).
First, find the Cost Price (CP).
\[ \text{CP} = \text{SP} - \text{Profit} = 250 - 50 = \text{₹ } 200 \] \[ \text{Profit \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Profit \%} = \left( \frac{50}{200} \right) \times 100\% = \frac{1}{4} \times 100\% = 25\% \]
The bookseller's profit percentage is 25%.
Q5. The marked price of an electronic toy is ₹ 1,200. A discount of 15% is allowed by the shopkeeper. Find the final selling price of the toy.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 1,200 \), Discount % = \( 15\% \).
\[ \text{Discount Amount} = \frac{15}{100} \times 1,200 = 15 \times 12 = \text{₹ } 180 \] \[ \text{Selling Price (SP)} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount Amount} \] \[ \text{SP} = 1,200 - 180 = \text{₹ } 1,020 \]
The final selling price of the toy is ₹ 1,020.
SECTION B
Q6. A shopkeeper buys 80 articles for ₹ 2,400 and sells them for a profit of 16%. Find the selling price of one article.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Cost Price (CP) of 80 articles = \( \text{₹ } 2,400 \).
\[ \text{CP of 1 article} = \frac{2400}{80} = \text{₹ } 30 \]
Profit % = \( 16\% \).
\[ \text{SP of 1 article} = \text{CP} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Profit \%}}{100}\right) \] \[ \text{SP of 1 article} = 30 \times \left(1 + \frac{16}{100}\right) = 30 \times \frac{116}{100} \] \[ \text{SP of 1 article} = 3 \times 11.6 = \text{₹ } 34.80 \]
The selling price of one article is ₹ 34.80.
Q7. A dining table is marked at ₹ 15,000 and is available at a discount of 20%. Find its selling price. If 5% GST is charged on the selling price, find the total amount the customer has to pay.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 15,000 \), Discount % = \( 20\% \).
\[ \text{Discount} = \frac{20}{100} \times 15,000 = \text{₹ } 3,000 \] \[ \text{Selling Price (SP)} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount} = 15,000 - 3,000 = \text{₹ } 12,000 \]
GST is charged at \( 5\% \) on the SP.
\[ \text{GST Amount} = \frac{5}{100} \times 12,000 = 5 \times 120 = \text{₹ } 600 \] \[ \text{Total Amount to pay} = \text{SP} + \text{GST Amount} = 12,000 + 600 = \text{₹ } 12,600 \]
The selling price is ₹ 12,000 and the final amount paid is ₹ 12,600.
Q8. Rohan bought a second-hand car for ₹ 45,000 and spent ₹ 5,000 on its repairs and painting. He then sold it for ₹ 55,000. Find his total gain or loss percent.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Purchase Price = \( \text{₹ } 45,000 \)
Overhead expenses (Repairs) = \( \text{₹ } 5,000 \)
\[ \text{Total Cost Price (CP)} = \text{Purchase Price} + \text{Overhead Expenses} \] \[ \text{Total CP} = 45,000 + 5,000 = \text{₹ } 50,000 \]
Selling Price (SP) = \( \text{₹ } 55,000 \)
Since \( \text{SP} > \text{CP} \), Rohan makes a Gain.
\[ \text{Gain} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 55,000 - 50,000 = \text{₹ } 5,000 \] \[ \text{Gain \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Gain}}{\text{Total CP}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Gain \%} = \left( \frac{5,000}{50,000} \right) \times 100\% = \frac{1}{10} \times 100\% = 10\% \]
Rohan's total gain is 10%.
Q9. Find the final buying price of a television set, when an 8% sales tax is added on the purchase price of ₹ 15,500.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Purchase Price of TV = \( \text{₹ } 15,500 \)
Sales Tax Rate = \( 8\% \)
\[ \text{Sales Tax} = \frac{8}{100} \times 15,500 = 8 \times 155 = \text{₹ } 1,240 \] \[ \text{Final Buying Price} = \text{Purchase Price} + \text{Sales Tax} \] \[ \text{Final Buying Price} = 15,500 + 1,240 = \text{₹ } 16,740 \]
The final buying price of the television set is ₹ 16,740.
Q10. A shopkeeper marks his goods at 25% above the cost price and then allows a discount of 10% on the marked price. Find his overall gain percent.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Let the Cost Price (CP) of the goods be \( \text{₹ } 100 \).
\[ \text{Marked Price (MP)} = \text{CP} + 25\% \text{ of CP} \] \[ \text{MP} = 100 + 25 = \text{₹ } 125 \]
Discount allowed = \( 10\% \) on MP.
\[ \text{Discount} = \frac{10}{100} \times 125 = \text{₹ } 12.50 \] \[ \text{Selling Price (SP)} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount} = 125 - 12.50 = \text{₹ } 112.50 \]
Now calculate overall gain on original CP.
\[ \text{Gain} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 112.50 - 100 = \text{₹ } 12.50 \] \[ \text{Gain \%} = \left( \frac{12.50}{100} \right) \times 100\% = 12.5\% \]
The shopkeeper's overall gain is 12.5%.
SECTION C
Q11. A man sold two ceiling fans for ₹ 990 each. On one he gains 10% and on the other he loses 10%. Find his total cost price and his total gain or loss percent in the whole transaction.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Selling Price (SP) of each fan = \( \text{₹ } 990 \). Total SP = \( 990 + 990 = \text{₹ } 1980 \).

For the first fan (Gain = 10%):
\[ \text{CP}_1 = \frac{\text{SP} \times 100}{100 + \text{Gain \%}} = \frac{990 \times 100}{100 + 10} = \frac{99000}{110} = \text{₹ } 900 \]
For the second fan (Loss = 10%):
\[ \text{CP}_2 = \frac{\text{SP} \times 100}{100 - \text{Loss \%}} = \frac{990 \times 100}{100 - 10} = \frac{99000}{90} = \text{₹ } 1100 \]

Total Transaction:
\[ \text{Total Cost Price (CP)} = 900 + 1100 = \text{₹ } 2000 \] \[ \text{Since Total CP } (2000) > \text{Total SP } (1980), \text{ there is a Loss.} \] \[ \text{Total Loss} = 2000 - 1980 = \text{₹ } 20 \] \[ \text{Loss \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Loss}}{\text{Total CP}} \right) \times 100\% = \left( \frac{20}{2000} \right) \times 100\% = 1\% \]
His total cost price is ₹ 2,000 and his total loss in the transaction is 1%.
Q12. Megha bought a pair of roller skates at a clearance sale where a discount of 20% was given. If the amount she pays after the discount is ₹ 1,600, find the original marked price. Also, find the final billing amount if a 12% GST is applied to the discounted price.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let the Marked Price (MP) be \( x \).
Discount = \( 20\% \) of \( x = 0.20x \).
Selling Price (SP) after discount = \( \text{₹ } 1,600 \).
\[ \text{SP} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount} \] \[ 1600 = x - 0.20x \] \[ 1600 = 0.80x \implies x = \frac{1600}{0.80} = \frac{16000}{8} = \text{₹ } 2000 \]
So, Marked Price = \( \text{₹ } 2,000 \).

Now, a GST of 12% is applied to the discounted price (₹ 1,600).
\[ \text{GST Amount} = \frac{12}{100} \times 1600 = 12 \times 16 = \text{₹ } 192 \] \[ \text{Final Billing Amount} = \text{Discounted Price} + \text{GST} = 1600 + 192 = \text{₹ } 1792 \]
The original marked price is ₹ 2,000. The final billing amount is ₹ 1,792.
Q13. A dealer bought two identical TV sets at ₹ 10,000 each. He sold one at a profit of 10% and the other at a loss of 10%. Calculate the selling price of each TV set. Did he make an overall profit or loss, and by what percentage?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Cost Price (CP) of each TV = \( \text{₹ } 10,000 \).
Total CP for both TVs = \( 10,000 + 10,000 = \text{₹ } 20,000 \).

For the first TV (10% Profit):
\[ \text{Profit} = \frac{10}{100} \times 10,000 = \text{₹ } 1,000 \] \[ \text{SP}_1 = 10,000 + 1,000 = \text{₹ } 11,000 \]
For the second TV (10% Loss):
\[ \text{Loss} = \frac{10}{100} \times 10,000 = \text{₹ } 1,000 \] \[ \text{SP}_2 = 10,000 - 1,000 = \text{₹ } 9,000 \]

Overall Analysis:
\[ \text{Total SP} = \text{SP}_1 + \text{SP}_2 = 11,000 + 9,000 = \text{₹ } 20,000 \] \[ \text{Since Total CP } (20,000) = \text{Total SP } (20,000) \]
There is no gain and no loss.
Selling Prices are ₹ 11,000 and ₹ 9,000. He made no profit and no loss (0%).
Q14. The price of a refrigerator is ₹ 24,000. The shopkeeper gives successive discounts of 10% and 5% to a customer. Find the final selling price of the refrigerator. Also, calculate the total equivalent discount given in percentage.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Marked Price (MP) = \( \text{₹ } 24,000 \).

First Discount (10%):
\[ \text{D}_1 = \frac{10}{100} \times 24000 = \text{₹ } 2400 \] \[ \text{Price after 1st discount} = 24000 - 2400 = \text{₹ } 21600 \]
Second Discount (5% on the new price):
\[ \text{D}_2 = \frac{5}{100} \times 21600 = 5 \times 216 = \text{₹ } 1080 \] \[ \text{Final Selling Price} = 21600 - 1080 = \text{₹ } 20520 \]
Total Equivalent Discount Percentage:
\[ \text{Total Discount Amount} = 2400 + 1080 = \text{₹ } 3480 \] \[ \text{Equivalent Discount \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Discount}}{\text{Initial MP}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Equivalent Discount \%} = \left( \frac{3480}{24000} \right) \times 100\% = \frac{348}{24} = 14.5\% \]
The final selling price is ₹ 20,520 and the equivalent discount is 14.5%.
Q15. A retailer buys a geyser for ₹ 4,800. He marks the price 20% above his cost price. If he gives a 10% discount on the marked price and charges 18% GST on the discounted price, calculate: (a) MP (b) Discount (c) SP (d) Final amount.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Cost Price (CP) = \( \text{₹ } 4,800 \).

(a) Marked Price (MP): He marks up by 20%.
\[ \text{Markup} = \frac{20}{100} \times 4800 = \text{₹ } 960 \] \[ \text{MP} = \text{CP} + \text{Markup} = 4800 + 960 = \text{₹ } 5760 \]
(b) Discount amount in rupees: Discount is 10% on MP.
\[ \text{Discount} = \frac{10}{100} \times 5760 = \text{₹ } 576 \]
(c) Selling Price (SP) before tax:
\[ \text{SP} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount} = 5760 - 576 = \text{₹ } 5184 \]
(d) Final total amount paid by customer (including 18% GST): GST is charged on the Selling Price.
\[ \text{GST} = \frac{18}{100} \times 5184 = 0.18 \times 5184 = \text{₹ } 933.12 \] \[ \text{Final Amount} = \text{SP} + \text{GST} = 5184 + 933.12 = \text{₹ } 6117.12 \]
(a) MP = ₹ 5,760
(b) Discount = ₹ 576
(c) Selling Price = ₹ 5,184
(d) Final Amount = ₹ 6,117.12
