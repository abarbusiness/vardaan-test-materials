SECTION A
Q1. State whether the following are physical or chemical changes: (a) Melting of wax (b) Rusting of iron
[2 Marks]
(a) Melting of wax: Physical change. (1 mark)
(b) Rusting of iron: Chemical change. (1 mark)
Explanation (optional for student but good for clarity): Melting only changes state (solid to liquid), while rusting forms a new substance (iron oxide).
Q2. Explain briefly why tearing a sheet of paper is considered a physical change while burning the same paper is a chemical change.
[2 Marks]
Tearing paper is a physical change because no new substance is formed; only the shape and size change. (1 mark)
Burning paper is a chemical change because a chemical reaction occurs with oxygen, forming entirely new substances (ash, carbon dioxide, water vapour). (1 mark)

Gas produced: Carbon dioxide (\( CO_2 \)) (1 mark)
Word equation: Vinegar (Acetic acid) + Baking soda (Sodium hydrogencarbonate) \( \rightarrow \) Carbon dioxide + other substances (1 mark)
Type of change: Chemical change (because new substances are formed). (1 mark)
Q7. List three differences between a physical change and a chemical change.
[3 Marks]
Physical Change Chemical Change
No new substance is formed. One or more new substances are formed.
Usually reversible. Usually irreversible (permanent).
Only physical properties (shape, size, state) change. Chemical properties and composition change.

Q8. Rahul travelled 14.5 km by bus and 2.75 km by auto rickshaw. (a) What is the total distance he travelled? (b) Express the total distance in meters.
[3 Marks]
(a) Total distance:
Distance by bus = \( 14.50 \text{ km} \) (converting to like decimal)
Distance by auto = \( 2.75 \text{ km} \)
Total = \( 14.50 + 2.75 = \)\( 17.25 \text{ km} \) (2 marks)

(b) Convert to meters:
We know \( 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \)
Distance in meters = \( 17.25 \times 1000 \)
\( = 17250 \text{ m} \) (1 mark)
Q9. A car covers a distance of 89.1 km in 2.2 hours. What is the average distance covered by it in 1 hour?
[3 Marks]
Total distance covered = \( 89.1 \text{ km} \)
Total time taken = \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \) (0.5 marks)
Distance covered in 1 hour (Average speed) = \( \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} \)
\( = \frac{89.1}{2.2} \) (1 mark)
Multiply numerator and denominator by 10 to remove decimals:
\( = \frac{891}{22} \)
Dividing 891 by 22: \( 891 \div 22 = 40.5 \)
The average distance covered in 1 hour is \( 40.5 \text{ km} \). (1.5 marks)
Q10. Solve the following expression using the rules of operations on decimals: \( 12.5 + (3.4 \times 2) - 1.5 \)
[3 Marks]
According to BODMAS rules, we solve the brackets first.
Step 1: Bracket: \( 3.4 \times 2 = 6.8 \) (1 mark)
Expression becomes: \( 12.5 + 6.8 - 1.5 \)
Step 2: Addition: \( 12.5 + 6.8 = 19.3 \) (1 mark)
Expression becomes: \( 19.3 - 1.5 \)

SECTION C
Q11. (a) Explain the process of rusting of iron. Write the conditions necessary for rusting to occur. (b) Suggest two methods to prevent the rusting of iron.
[5 Marks]
(a) Process and Conditions (3 marks):
Rusting is a chemical change where iron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form a new reddish-brown substance called iron oxide (rust). (1 mark)
\( \text{Iron (Fe)} + \text{Oxygen } (O_2, \text{ from air}) + \text{Water } (H_2O) \rightarrow \text{Rust (Iron oxide, } Fe_2O_3 \cdot xH_2O) \) (1 mark for equation)
Conditions necessary: The presence of both Oxygen (air) and Water (moisture) is essential for rusting to take place. (1 mark)

(b) Prevention methods (2 marks, 1 mark each):
1. Painting/Greasing: Applying a coat of paint, oil, or grease cuts off the contact of iron with air and moisture.
2. Galvanisation: Depositing a layer of a more reactive metal like zinc or chromium on iron to protect it from rusting.
Q12. When a candle burns, both physical and chemical changes take place. Identify and explain both changes. Give one more example where both occur.
[5 Marks]
(a) Physical Change (2 marks):
Melting of wax: The heat of the flame melts the solid wax into liquid wax, and then some of it evaporates into wax vapour. This is a physical change because the substance remains wax, just its state changes from solid to liquid to gas, which is reversible upon cooling.

(b) Chemical Change (2 marks):
Burning of wax vapour: The wax vapour reacts with oxygen in the air and burns (combustion) to produce heat, light, carbon dioxide, and water vapour. This is a chemical change because entirely new substances are formed, and it is irreversible.

(c) Another Example (1 mark):
Eating/Digestion of food.
Physical change: Chewing and breaking food into smaller pieces.
Chemical change: Enzymes breaking down the food chemically in the stomach to form new substances for energy.
Q13. A rectangular garden has a length of 15.75 m and a width of 8.4 m. Find its perimeter, area, and total cost of fencing at ₹ 20.50 per meter.
[5 Marks]
Given:
Length (\( l \)) = \( 15.75 \text{ m} \)
Width (\( w \)) = \( 8.4 \text{ m} \)

(a) Perimeter (2 marks):
Perimeter of a rectangle = \( 2 \times (l + w) \)
\( = 2 \times (15.75 + 8.40) \)
\( = 2 \times 24.15 \)
\( = 48.3 \text{ m} \)

(b) Area (2 marks):
Area of a rectangle = \( l \times w \)
\( = 15.75 \times 8.4 \)
Multiply \( 1575 \times 84 = 132300 \)
Total decimal places = 2 + 1 = 3.
Area \( = 132.300 \text{ m}^2 \) or \( 132.3 \text{ m}^2 \)

(c) Cost of Fencing (1 mark):
Fencing is done around the boundary, so we use the perimeter.
Cost per meter = ₹ \( 20.50 \)
Total Cost = Perimeter \( \times \) Rate
\( = 48.3 \times 20.50 \)
Multiply \( 483 \times 205 = 99015 \)
Adjusting decimals (1 + 1 = 2 places): ₹ \( 990.15 \)
