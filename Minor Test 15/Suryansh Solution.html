PART I : SCIENCE SOLUTIONS
Q1. Define the terms malleability and ductility. Give one example of a metal that exhibits both properties highly.
[2 Marks]
Ans:
  • Malleability: The property of metals by which they can be beaten into thin sheets without breaking. (1/2 Mark)
  • Ductility: The property of metals by which they can be drawn into thin wires. (1/2 Mark)
Example: Gold (or Silver) is highly malleable and ductile. (1 Mark)
Q2. Why is sodium metal always kept immersed in kerosene oil? State the reason scientifically.
[2 Marks]
Ans:
Sodium is a highly reactive metal. It reacts vigorously with oxygen and moisture present in the air at room temperature, catching fire. (1 Mark)
To prevent this accidental fire and protect the metal, it is kept immersed in kerosene oil, as it does not react with kerosene. (1 Mark)
Q3. Differentiate between metals and non-metals based on their electrical conductivity. Name one non-metal that is an exception to this rule.
[2 Marks]
Ans:
Metals: They are generally good conductors of electricity (allow current to pass through them).
Non-metals: They are generally poor conductors (insulators) of electricity. (1 Mark for diff)
Exception: Graphite (an allotrope of carbon, which is a non-metal) is a good conductor of electricity. (1 Mark)
Q4. What happens when typical metals react with dilute acids? Explain by writing the word equation for the reaction between Zinc and dilute Hydrochloric acid. Mention how you would test the gas evolved.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
When typical reactive metals react with dilute acids, they form a metal salt and release hydrogen gas. (1 Mark)
Word Equation: Zinc + Hydrochloric Acid → Zinc Chloride + Hydrogen (1 Mark)
Test for Gas: Bring a burning matchstick near the mouth of the test tube. The hydrogen gas burns with a characteristic "pop" sound. (1 Mark)
Q5. A student placed a copper coin in a beaker containing zinc sulphate solution. Will any reaction occur? Give a reason for your answer and state the underlying principle.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
No reaction will occur. (1 Mark)
Reason: Copper is less reactive than zinc. Therefore, it cannot displace zinc from its salt solution (zinc sulphate). (1 Mark)
Principle: Reactivity Series of metals (A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its salt solution, but the reverse is not possible). (1 Mark)
Q6. Non-metals are essential for our existence. Justify this statement by listing three vital uses of non-metals in our daily lives or industry.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
(Any three of the following for 1 mark each):
  • Oxygen: Essential for breathing (respiration) of all living beings and for combustion.
  • Nitrogen: Used in fertilizers to enhance plant growth and used to preserve food packaging.
  • Chlorine: Used in the water purification process to kill bacteria.
  • Iodine: Used as an antiseptic solution (tincture of iodine) applied on wounds.
Q7. (a) What do you understand by a 'displacement reaction'?
(b) What happens when an iron nail is placed in a solution of copper sulphate? Write the word equation and describe the color changes observed.
[5 Marks]
Ans:
(a) Displacement Reaction: A chemical reaction in which a more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its salt solution is called a displacement reaction. (2 Marks)

(b) Iron in Copper Sulphate:
When an iron nail is placed in a copper sulphate solution, iron displaces copper from the solution because iron is more reactive than copper. (1 Mark)
Word Equation: Iron + Copper Sulphate → Iron Sulphate + Copper (1 Mark)
Color Changes: The blue color of the copper sulphate solution slowly fades and turns pale green (due to the formation of iron sulphate). A reddish-brown deposit of copper forms on the surface of the iron nail. (1 Mark)
PART II : MATHEMATICS SOLUTIONS
Q8. Identify the terms and their respective numerical coefficients in the expression: \( 5x^2y - 3xy^2 + 7 \)
[2 Marks]
Ans:
The expression consists of three terms. (1 Mark for identifying terms)
  • Term 1: \( 5x^2y \)   →   Numerical Coefficient: \( 5 \)
  • Term 2: \( -3xy^2 \)  →   Numerical Coefficient: \( -3 \)
  • Term 3: \( 7 \)          →   Numerical Coefficient: \( 7 \) (Constant term)
(1 Mark for correct coefficients, half mark deducted if negative sign is missed)
Q9. Subtract \( 3a - 4b + 5c \) from \( 5a + 2b - 3c \).
[2 Marks]
Ans:
Writing the subtraction mathematically:
\( (5a + 2b - 3c) - (3a - 4b + 5c) \) (1/2 Mark)

Opening the brackets (changing signs of the second expression):
\( = 5a + 2b - 3c - 3a + 4b - 5c \) (1/2 Mark)

Grouping like terms:
\( = (5a - 3a) + (2b + 4b) + (-3c - 5c) \) (1/2 Mark)

\( = 2a + 6b - 8c \) (1/2 Mark for correct final answer)
Q10. Find the value of the expression \( 2x^2 - 3x + 5 \) when \( x = -2 \).
[2 Marks]
Ans:
Substitute \( x = -2 \) into the expression:
\( = 2(-2)^2 - 3(-2) + 5 \) (1 Mark for correct substitution)

\( = 2(4) + 6 + 5 \) (1/2 Mark for correct square and sign multiplication)

\( = 8 + 6 + 5 \)
\( = 19 \) (1/2 Mark for correct final answer)
Q11. Simplify the expression and find its value if \( a = 5 \) and \( b = -3 \): \( 2(a^2 + ab) + 3 - ab \)
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Step 1: Simplify the expression
\( 2(a^2 + ab) + 3 - ab \)
Expand the bracket:
\( = 2a^2 + 2ab + 3 - ab \) (1 Mark)
Combine like terms (\( 2ab \) and \( -ab \)):
\( = 2a^2 + ab + 3 \) (1 Mark)

Step 2: Substitute values
Substitute \( a = 5, b = -3 \):
\( = 2(5)^2 + (5)(-3) + 3 \)
\( = 2(25) - 15 + 3 \) (1/2 Mark)
\( = 50 - 15 + 3 \)
\( = 35 + 3 = 38 \) (1/2 Mark)
Q12. Add the following algebraic terms: \( 4x^2y, -3xy^2, -5xy^2, \) and \( 5x^2y \). Group the like terms together clearly.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Expression to add:
\( 4x^2y + (-3xy^2) + (-5xy^2) + 5x^2y \) (1 Mark)

Grouping the like terms (Terms with exact same variables and powers):
The terms with \( x^2y \) are \( 4x^2y \) and \( 5x^2y \).
The terms with \( xy^2 \) are \( -3xy^2 \) and \( -5xy^2 \).
\( = (4x^2y + 5x^2y) + (-3xy^2 - 5xy^2) \) (1 Mark)

Adding the coefficients:
\( = (4+5)x^2y + (-3-5)xy^2 \)
\( = 9x^2y - 8xy^2 \) (1 Mark)
Q13. What should be added to \( x^2 + xy + y^2 \) to obtain \( 2x^2 + 3xy \)?
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Let the required expression to be added be '\( P \)'.
According to the question:
\( (x^2 + xy + y^2) + P = 2x^2 + 3xy \) (1 Mark)

Transposing \( (x^2 + xy + y^2) \) to the right side:
\( P = (2x^2 + 3xy) - (x^2 + xy + y^2) \) (1 Mark)

Opening brackets and grouping like terms:
\( P = 2x^2 + 3xy - x^2 - xy - y^2 \)
\( P = (2x^2 - x^2) + (3xy - xy) - y^2 \)
\( P = x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \) (1 Mark)
Thus, \( x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \) should be added.
Q14. (a) From the sum of \( 2y^2 + 3yz \) and \( -y^2 - yz - z^2 \), subtract the sum of \( 3y^2 - z^2 \) and \( -y^2 + yz + z^2 \).
(b) Classify as monomials, binomials, or trinomials: (i) \( 4y - 7z \) (ii) \( 100 \) (iii) \( x + y - xy \) (iv) \( 5x^2 \)
[5 Marks]
Ans:
Part (a): [3 Marks]
Step 1: Calculate the First Sum
Sum 1 = \( (2y^2 + 3yz) + (-y^2 - yz - z^2) \)
\( = 2y^2 - y^2 + 3yz - yz - z^2 \)
\( = y^2 + 2yz - z^2 \) (1 Mark)

Step 2: Calculate the Second Sum
Sum 2 = \( (3y^2 - z^2) + (-y^2 + yz + z^2) \)
\( = 3y^2 - y^2 + yz - z^2 + z^2 \)
\( = 2y^2 + yz \) (1 Mark)

Step 3: Perform the Subtraction (Sum 1 - Sum 2)
\( = (y^2 + 2yz - z^2) - (2y^2 + yz) \)
\( = y^2 + 2yz - z^2 - 2y^2 - yz \)
\( = (y^2 - 2y^2) + (2yz - yz) - z^2 \)
\( = -y^2 + yz - z^2 \) (1 Mark)

Part (b): Classification [2 Marks]
(i) \( 4y - 7z \) : Contains two unlike terms → Binomial (0.5 Mark)
(ii) \( 100 \) : Contains one term → Monomial (0.5 Mark)
(iii) \( x + y - xy \) : Contains three unlike terms → Trinomial (0.5 Mark)
(iv) \( 5x^2 \) : Contains one term → Monomial (0.5 Mark)
