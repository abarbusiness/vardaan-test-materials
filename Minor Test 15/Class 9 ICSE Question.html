Minor Test - Linear & Simultaneous Equations
Class: 9 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working steps clearly. Missing steps may result in deduction of marks.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Solve the following linear equation for \( x \):
\( \frac{2x - 3}{5} = \frac{x + 1}{3} \)
2.
Check whether \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \) is a solution to the following pair of simultaneous equations:
\( 3x + 2y = 4 \)
\( 2x - y = 5 \)
3.
Solve the simultaneous equations using the substitution method:
\( x + y = 7 \)
\( x - y = 3 \)
4.
Find the value of '\( a \)' if the point \( (2, -3) \) lies on the straight line given by the equation \( 3x - ay = 12 \).
5.
Frame a linear equation in two variables for the given statement:
"The sum of a two-digit number and the number obtained by reversing its digits is 121."
(Assume the tens digit to be \( x \) and the units digit to be \( y \). Do not solve.)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Solve the following system of equations by the method of elimination:
\( 3x + 4y = 10 \)
\( 2x - 2y = 2 \)
7.
Solve for \( x \) and \( y \):
\( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{2y}{3} = -1 \)
\( x - \frac{y}{3} = 3 \)
8.
Five years ago, a man was thrice as old as his son. Ten years later, the man will be twice as old as his son. Find their present ages.
9.
Solve the following simultaneous equations:
\( 2(x - 3) + 3(y - 4) = 1 \)
\( 5(x + 1) - 4(y - 1) = 22 \)
10.
A fraction becomes \( \frac{9}{11} \) if 2 is added to both the numerator and the denominator. If 3 is added to both the numerator and the denominator it becomes \( \frac{5}{6} \). Find the fraction.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Solve for \( x \) and \( y \):
\( \frac{5}{x+y} - \frac{2}{x-y} = -1 \)
\( \frac{15}{x+y} + \frac{7}{x-y} = 10 \)
(Where \( x+y \neq 0 \) and \( x-y \neq 0 \))
12.
The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the two-digit number.
13.
3 school bags and 4 pens together cost ₹ 257 whereas 4 school bags and 3 pens together cost ₹ 324. Find the total cost of 1 school bag and 10 pens.
