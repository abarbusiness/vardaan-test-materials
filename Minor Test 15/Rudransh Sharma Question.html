Minor Test 15
Class: 7 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
  • Draw neat and labeled diagrams wherever necessary.
  • Topics Covered: Triangles & Fundamental Concepts of Algebra.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define an exterior angle of a triangle. State the theorem related to the exterior angle of a triangle.
2.
Identify the constants and variables in the following algebraic expression:
\( 3x^2y - 7xy + \frac{5}{2} - z \)
3.
In a triangle \(ABC\), if \(\angle A = 45^\circ\) and \(\angle B = 65^\circ\), find the measure of \(\angle C\). What type of triangle is \(ABC\) based on its angles?
4.
Write down the algebraic expression for the following statement:
"Five times a number \(x\) is subtracted from the sum of \(y\) and \(z\)."
5.
Is it possible to construct a triangle with side lengths \(3\text{ cm}\), \(4\text{ cm}\), and \(8\text{ cm}\)? Give a valid mathematical reason for your answer.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Evaluate the algebraic expression \( 2a^2 - 3ab + b^2 \) when \( a = -2 \) and \( b = 3 \).
7.
In an isosceles triangle \(PQR\), \(PQ = PR\). If the vertical angle \(\angle P\) is \(50^\circ\), calculate the base angles \(\angle Q\) and \(\angle R\). State the property used.
8.
Classify the following algebraic expressions as monomials, binomials, or trinomials. Also, state the degree of expression (b):
(a) \( 7pqr \)
(b) \( 4x^3 - 2xy^2 + 5 \)
(c) \( m^2 - n^2 \)
9.
The exterior angle of a triangle is \(110^\circ\) and one of its interior opposite angles is \(40^\circ\). Find the other two angles of the triangle.
10.
Add the following algebraic expressions:
\( (3x^2 - 5x + 2) \) and \( (-x^2 + 4x - 6) \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In a triangle \(ABC\), the angles are in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 4 \).
(a) Find the measure of each angle of the triangle. (3 Marks)
(b) Classify the triangle based on its angles. (1 Mark)
(c) State the property of triangles used to solve this problem. (1 Mark)
12.
Simplify the following algebraic expression by opening the brackets and combining like terms:
\( 5x - [3y - \{2x - (y - x)\}] \)
Show each step of your working clearly.
13.
Consider the algebraic expression: \( 4x^3y - 5x^2y^2 + 7xy^3 - 2 \)
(a) How many terms are there in this expression? (1 Mark)
(b) Write the coefficient of \(x^2\) in the term \( -5x^2y^2 \). (1 Mark)
(c) What is the numerical coefficient of the term \( 4x^3y \)? (1 Mark)
(d) Find the degree of the entire algebraic expression. Explain how you arrived at it. (2 Marks)
