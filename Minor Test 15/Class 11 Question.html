MINOR TEST 15: CLASSIFICATION OF ELEMENTS & PERIODICITY
Class: 11 Subject: Chemistry Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Follow strictly the rationalised NCERT syllabus.
  • Section A: Q1 to Q10 are very short answer/objective type questions carrying 1 mark each.
  • Section B: Q11 to Q15 are short answer questions carrying 2 marks each.
  • Section C: Q16 to Q20 are short answer questions carrying 3 marks each.
  • Section D: Q21 to Q23 are long answer questions carrying 5 marks each.
  • Write precise answers. Show all logical reasoning where anomalous trends are asked.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Very Short Answer / Objective Type - 1 Mark Each)
1.
Write the IUPAC name and symbol for the element with atomic number 114.
2.
What is the general outer electronic configuration of d-block elements?
3.
Name the most electronegative element in the periodic table.
4.
Arrange the following in decreasing order of their ionic radii: \( \text{O}^{2-} \), \( \text{F}^- \), \( \text{Na}^+ \), \( \text{Mg}^{2+} \).
5.
Define Ionization Enthalpy.
6.
Write the formula of the compound formed by the element Eka-aluminium (predicted by Mendeleev) with oxygen.
7.
Assertion (A): The first ionization enthalpy of Nitrogen is greater than that of Oxygen.
Reason (R): Nitrogen has exactly half-filled p-orbitals which provides extra stability.
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true but R is false.
(d) A is false but R is true.
8.
Give one example to illustrate the diagonal relationship in the periodic table.
9.
The elements of Group 16 are collectively known as what?
10.
What is the characteristic valency of alkaline earth metals?
SECTION B (10 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 2 Marks Each)
11.
Why is the electron gain enthalpy of Fluorine less negative than that of Chlorine?
12.
Explain the shielding effect (screening effect) and mention how it impacts the ionization enthalpy down a group.
13.
What are transition elements? Why are Zinc, Cadmium, and Mercury not considered as typical transition elements?
14.
Consider the elements N, P, O, and S. Arrange them in order of:
(i) Increasing first ionization enthalpy
(ii) Increasing non-metallic character
15.
How does the metallic character of elements vary along a period and down a group in the periodic table? Give reasons.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
16.
The first ionization enthalpy of Aluminium (\( \text{Al} \)) is lower than that of Magnesium (\( \text{Mg} \)). Explain this anomaly clearly with respect to their electronic configurations and orbital penetration.
17.
(a) What do you understand by isoelectronic species?
(b) Name a cation and an anion that will be isoelectronic with the Neon (\( \text{Ne} \)) atom.
(c) Name a cation and an anion that will be isoelectronic with the Argon (\( \text{Ar} \)) atom.
18.
Discuss the anomalous behavior of the first element of each group in the s- and p-blocks (e.g., Lithium and Beryllium). State any two reasons for this anomalous behavior.
19.
(a) Explain the factors on which the electronegativity of an element depends.
(b) How does electronegativity differ from electron gain enthalpy? (Mention one key difference).
20.
Predict the formulas of the stable compounds formed by the following pairs of elements using their respective group numbers and typical oxidation states:
(a) Silicon and Oxygen
(b) Aluminium and Bromine
(c) Calcium and Iodine
SECTION D (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
21.
(a) Define Electron Gain Enthalpy (\( \Delta_{eg}H \)).
(b) Why do noble gases have large positive values of electron gain enthalpy?
(c) How does electron gain enthalpy vary in general when we move from left to right across a period and from top to bottom in a group? Explain the reasons for these trends.
22.
(a) State the Modern Periodic Law.
(b) Based on electronic configurations, elements are classified into s, p, d, and f blocks. Define s-block and p-block elements and write their general outer electronic configurations.
(c) State the group number and period number of the element with atomic number 24. Also, identify its block.
23.
(a) Write the atomic number of the element present in the third period and seventeenth group of the periodic table.
(b) The first ionization enthalpy values (in kJ/mol) of the third period elements Na, Mg, and Si are respectively 496, 737, and 786. Predict whether the first IE value for Al will be closer to 575 or 760 kJ/mol? Justify your prediction thoroughly using electronic configuration.
(c) Between \( \text{Na}^+ \) and \( \text{Mg}^{2+} \), which ion is smaller in size and why?
