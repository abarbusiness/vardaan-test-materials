SECTION A
Q1. Solve for \( x \): \( \frac{2x - 3}{5} = \frac{x + 1}{3} \)
Solution:
Cross multiplying, we get:
\( 3(2x - 3) = 5(x + 1) \)
\( 6x - 9 = 5x + 5 \)
Transposing variables to one side:
\( 6x - 5x = 5 + 9 \)
\( x = 14 \)

Q2. Check whether \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \) is a solution to the equations: \( 3x + 2y = 4 \) and \( 2x - y = 5 \).
Solution:
Substitute \( x = 2, y = -1 \) in Equation 1:
L.H.S = \( 3(2) + 2(-1) = 6 - 2 = 4 = \) R.H.S (Satisfied)

Substitute \( x = 2, y = -1 \) in Equation 2:
L.H.S = \( 2(2) - (-1) = 4 + 1 = 5 = \) R.H.S (Satisfied)
Yes, \( (2, -1) \) is the solution to the given system.
[2 Marks]
Q3. Solve using substitution method: \( x + y = 7 \) and \( x - y = 3 \).
Solution:
Let \( x + y = 7 \) ---(i)
Let \( x - y = 3 \) ---(ii)
From (ii), we can express \( x \) in terms of \( y \):
\( x = y + 3 \)
Substitute this in equation (i):
\( (y + 3) + y = 7 \)
\( 2y + 3 = 7 \implies 2y = 4 \implies y = 2 \)
Now, substitute \( y = 2 \) back into \( x = y + 3 \):
\( x = 2 + 3 \implies x = 5 \)
\( x = 5, y = 2 \)

Q4. Find '\( a \)' if point \( (2, -3) \) lies on the line \( 3x - ay = 12 \).
Solution:
Since the point lies on the line, it must satisfy the equation.
Substitute \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -3 \) into \( 3x - ay = 12 \):
\( 3(2) - a(-3) = 12 \)
\( 6 + 3a = 12 \)
\( 3a = 12 - 6 \)
\( 3a = 6 \)
\( a = 2 \)

Q5. Frame a linear equation: "The sum of a two-digit number and the number obtained by reversing its digits is 121." (Let tens digit = \( x \), units digit = \( y \)).
Solution:
Tens digit = \( x \), Units digit = \( y \).
Original Number = \( 10x + y \)
Reversed Number = \( 10y + x \)
According to the condition:
\( (10x + y) + (10y + x) = 121 \)
\( 11x + 11y = 121 \)
Dividing the whole equation by 11:
\( x + y = 11 \)

SECTION B
Q6. Solve by method of elimination: \( 3x + 4y = 10 \) and \( 2x - 2y = 2 \).
Solution:
\( 3x + 4y = 10 \) ---(i)
\( 2x - 2y = 2 \) ---(ii)
Multiply eq (ii) by 2 to make the coefficients of y equal:
\( 4x - 4y = 4 \) ---(iii)
Adding eq (i) and eq (iii):
\( (3x + 4y) + (4x - 4y) = 10 + 4 \)
\( 7x = 14 \implies x = 2 \)
Substitute \( x = 2 \) in eq (ii):
\( 2(2) - 2y = 2 \implies 4 - 2y = 2 \implies 2y = 2 \implies y = 1 \)
\( x = 2, y = 1 \)

Q7. Solve for \( x \) and \( y \): \( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{2y}{3} = -1 \) and \( x - \frac{y}{3} = 3 \)
Solution:
Simplify Equation 1: \( \frac{3x + 4y}{6} = -1 \implies 3x + 4y = -6 \) ---(i)
Simplify Equation 2: \( \frac{3x - y}{3} = 3 \implies 3x - y = 9 \) ---(ii)
Subtract eq (ii) from eq (i):
\( (3x + 4y) - (3x - y) = -6 - 9 \)
\( 5y = -15 \implies y = -3 \)
Substitute \( y = -3 \) in eq (ii):
\( 3x - (-3) = 9 \)
\( 3x + 3 = 9 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2 \)
\( x = 2, y = -3 \)

Q8. Five years ago, a man was thrice as old as his son. Ten years later, the man will be twice as old as his son. Find their present ages.
Solution:
Let present age of man = \( x \) years, and son = \( y \) years.
Case 1 (5 years ago):
Man was \( x - 5 \), Son was \( y - 5 \).
\( x - 5 = 3(y - 5) \implies x - 5 = 3y - 15 \implies x - 3y = -10 \) ---(i)
Case 2 (10 years later):
Man will be \( x + 10 \), Son will be \( y + 10 \).
\( x + 10 = 2(y + 10) \implies x + 10 = 2y + 20 \implies x - 2y = 10 \) ---(ii)
Subtract eq (i) from eq (ii):
\( (x - 2y) - (x - 3y) = 10 - (-10) \)
\( y = 20 \)
Substitute \( y = 20 \) in eq (ii):
\( x - 2(20) = 10 \implies x - 40 = 10 \implies x = 50 \)
Man's age = 50 years, Son's age = 20 years.

Q9. Solve: \( 2(x - 3) + 3(y - 4) = 1 \) and \( 5(x + 1) - 4(y - 1) = 22 \)
Solution:
Expand Equation 1:
\( 2x - 6 + 3y - 12 = 1 \implies 2x + 3y - 18 = 1 \implies 2x + 3y = 19 \) ---(i)
Expand Equation 2:
\( 5x + 5 - 4y + 4 = 22 \implies 5x - 4y + 9 = 22 \implies 5x - 4y = 13 \) ---(ii)
Multiply (i) by 4 and (ii) by 3 to eliminate y:
\( 8x + 12y = 76 \)
\( 15x - 12y = 39 \)
Adding both:
\( 23x = 115 \implies x = \frac{115}{23} = 5 \)
Substitute \( x = 5 \) in eq (i):
\( 2(5) + 3y = 19 \implies 10 + 3y = 19 \implies 3y = 9 \implies y = 3 \)
\( x = 5, y = 3 \)

Q10. A fraction becomes \( \frac{9}{11} \) if 2 is added to numerator and denominator. If 3 is added to both, it becomes \( \frac{5}{6} \). Find fraction.
Solution:
Let fraction be \( \frac{x}{y} \).
Case 1: \( \frac{x+2}{y+2} = \frac{9}{11} \implies 11(x+2) = 9(y+2) \implies 11x + 22 = 9y + 18 \implies 11x - 9y = -4 \) ---(i)
Case 2: \( \frac{x+3}{y+3} = \frac{5}{6} \implies 6(x+3) = 5(y+3) \implies 6x + 18 = 5y + 15 \implies 6x - 5y = -3 \) ---(ii)
Multiply (i) by 5 and (ii) by 9 to eliminate y:
\( 55x - 45y = -20 \)
\( 54x - 45y = -27 \)
Subtracting the two equations:
\( x = -20 - (-27) \implies x = 7 \)
Substitute \( x = 7 \) in (ii):
\( 6(7) - 5y = -3 \implies 42 - 5y = -3 \implies 45 = 5y \implies y = 9 \)
Required Fraction = \( \frac{7}{9} \)

SECTION C
Q11. Solve: \( \frac{5}{x+y} - \frac{2}{x-y} = -1 \) and \( \frac{15}{x+y} + \frac{7}{x-y} = 10 \)
Solution:
Let \( \frac{1}{x+y} = u \) and \( \frac{1}{x-y} = v \).
The equations become:
\( 5u - 2v = -1 \) ---(i)
\( 15u + 7v = 10 \) ---(ii)
Multiply eq (i) by 3:
\( 15u - 6v = -3 \) ---(iii)
Subtract (iii) from (ii):
\( (15u + 7v) - (15u - 6v) = 10 - (-3) \)
\( 13v = 13 \implies v = 1 \)
Substitute \( v = 1 \) in eq (i):
\( 5u - 2(1) = -1 \implies 5u = 1 \implies u = \frac{1}{5} \)

Now back substitute:
\( \frac{1}{x+y} = \frac{1}{5} \implies x + y = 5 \) ---(A)
\( \frac{1}{x-y} = 1 \implies x - y = 1 \) ---(B)
Adding (A) and (B): \( 2x = 6 \implies x = 3 \).
Substitute \( x = 3 \) in (A): \( 3 + y = 5 \implies y = 2 \).
\( x = 3, y = 2 \)

Q12. The sum of digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, 9 times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing digits. Find the number.
Solution:
Let tens digit = \( x \) and units digit = \( y \).
Sum of digits: \( x + y = 9 \) ---(i)
Original Number = \( 10x + y \)
Reversed Number = \( 10y + x \)
According to the condition:
\( 9(10x + y) = 2(10y + x) \)
\( 90x + 9y = 20y + 2x \)
\( 90x - 2x = 20y - 9y \)
\( 88x = 11y \)
Dividing by 11: \( 8x = y \implies y = 8x \) ---(ii)
Substitute (ii) in (i):
\( x + 8x = 9 \)
\( 9x = 9 \implies x = 1 \)
From (ii), \( y = 8(1) = 8 \).
The required number is 18.

Q13. 3 school bags and 4 pens together cost ₹ 257 whereas 4 school bags and 3 pens together cost ₹ 324. Find the total cost of 1 school bag and 10 pens.
Solution:
Let cost of 1 school bag = ₹ \( x \) and cost of 1 pen = ₹ \( y \).
\( 3x + 4y = 257 \) ---(i)
\( 4x + 3y = 324 \) ---(ii)
When coefficients are interchanged, we use Add-Subtract rule.
Adding (i) & (ii):
\( 7x + 7y = 581 \implies x + y = 83 \) ---(iii)
Subtracting (i) from (ii):
\( (4x + 3y) - (3x + 4y) = 324 - 257 \)
\( x - y = 67 \) ---(iv)
Solve (iii) and (iv):
Adding both equations:
\( 2x = 150 \implies x = 75 \) (Cost of 1 bag = ₹ 75)
Substitute \( x = 75 \) in (iii):
\( 75 + y = 83 \implies y = 8 \) (Cost of 1 pen = ₹ 8)

We need to find the total cost of 1 school bag and 10 pens:
Cost = \( x + 10y \)
Cost = \( 75 + 10(8) = 75 + 80 = 155 \)
Total cost is ₹ 155.
