MINOR TEST 15
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Proper units and mathematical steps carry marks.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Reduce the fraction \(\frac{144}{180}\) to its simplest form.
2.
Evaluate the following integer expression:
\((-15) + (+23) - (-12) - (+5)\)
3.
Divide an amount of ₹ 1,200 between A and B in the ratio 3 : 5.
4.
Write down all the prime numbers between 20 and 40.
5.
Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 84 using the prime factorization method.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Are the numbers 12, 16, 18, and 24 in proportion? Provide mathematical reasoning to justify your answer.
7.
At 6:00 AM, the temperature in a town was \(-4^\circ\text{C}\). By noon, the temperature rose by \(7^\circ\text{C}\), and by midnight, it dropped by \(5^\circ\text{C}\). What was the exact temperature at midnight?
8.
Arrange the following fractions in descending order:
\[\frac{2}{3}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{6}, \frac{7}{12}\]
9.
Three bells toll at intervals of 12, 15, and 20 minutes respectively. If they all toll together at 8:00 AM, at what time will they toll together again?
10.
In a class of 60 students, \(\frac{2}{5}\) of the total students are girls and the rest are boys. Find the ratio of the number of boys to the total number of students in the class.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Simplify the following expression using the correct order of operations (BODMAS):
\[ 3 \frac{1}{2} - \left[ \frac{2}{3} \times \left( \frac{5}{6} + \frac{1}{2} \right) \right] \]
12.
The ratio of the length to the breadth of a rectangular field is 5 : 3. If the perimeter of the field is 128 meters, determine the length and breadth of the field. Also, calculate its total area.
13.
Find the greatest number of 4 digits which is exactly divisible by 12, 16, and 24. Show all your working steps clearly.
