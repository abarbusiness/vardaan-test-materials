SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1.
Add the following algebraic expressions: $3x^2 - 5x + 7$ and $-2x^2 + 3x - 4$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Arranging the like terms together:
\begin{aligned} & (3x^2 - 5x + 7) + (-2x^2 + 3x - 4) \\ &= 3x^2 - 2x^2 - 5x + 3x + 7 - 4 \\ &= (3 - 2)x^2 + (-5 + 3)x + (7 - 4) \\ &= x^2 - 2x + 3 \end{aligned}
Ans: $x^2 - 2x + 3$
Q2.
Subtract $4a - 7ab + 3b + 12$ from $12a - 9ab + 5b - 3$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
The expression to be subtracted is written inside brackets with a minus sign in front:
\begin{aligned} & (12a - 9ab + 5b - 3) - (4a - 7ab + 3b + 12) \\ &= 12a - 9ab + 5b - 3 - 4a + 7ab - 3b - 12 \quad \text{(Changing signs)} \\ &= (12a - 4a) + (-9ab + 7ab) + (5b - 3b) + (-3 - 12) \\ &= 8a - 2ab + 2b - 15 \end{aligned}
Ans: $8a - 2ab + 2b - 15$
Q3.
Find the product of the binomials: $(3x - 2y)(4x + 5y)$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Multiply each term of the first binomial by each term of the second:
\begin{aligned} & (3x - 2y)(4x + 5y) \\ &= 3x(4x + 5y) - 2y(4x + 5y) \\ &= 12x^2 + 15xy - 8xy - 10y^2 \\ &= 12x^2 + 7xy - 10y^2 \end{aligned}
Ans: $12x^2 + 7xy - 10y^2$
Q4.
Expand the following expression using a suitable identity: $(2p + 3q)^2$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Using the standard identity: $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
Here, $a = 2p$ and $b = 3q$.
\begin{aligned} (2p + 3q)^2 &= (2p)^2 + 2(2p)(3q) + (3q)^2 \\ &= 4p^2 + 12pq + 9q^2 \end{aligned}
Ans: $4p^2 + 12pq + 9q^2$
Q5.
Simplify the given expression: $5x(x - 2) - 3x^2 + 10x$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
First, expand the bracket:
\begin{aligned} & 5x(x - 2) - 3x^2 + 10x \\ &= 5x^2 - 10x - 3x^2 + 10x \\ &= (5x^2 - 3x^2) + (-10x + 10x) \\ &= 2x^2 + 0 \end{aligned}
Ans: $2x^2$
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6.
Divide the polynomial $15m^3n^2 - 10m^2n^3 + 5mn$ by the monomial $5mn$.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Divide each term of the polynomial by the monomial separately:
\begin{aligned} & \frac{15m^3n^2 - 10m^2n^3 + 5mn}{5mn} \\ &= \frac{15m^3n^2}{5mn} - \frac{10m^2n^3}{5mn} + \frac{5mn}{5mn} \\ &= 3m^{3-1}n^{2-1} - 2m^{2-1}n^{3-1} + 1 \\ &= 3m^2n - 2mn^2 + 1 \end{aligned}
Ans: $3m^2n - 2mn^2 + 1$
Q7.
Evaluate the square of $99$ by using a standard algebraic identity.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can write $99$ as $(100 - 1)$.
Using the identity: $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
Here, $a = 100$ and $b = 1$.
\begin{aligned} 99^2 &= (100 - 1)^2 \\ &= (100)^2 - 2(100)(1) + (1)^2 \\ &= 10000 - 200 + 1 \\ &= 9800 + 1 \\ &= 9801 \end{aligned}
Ans: $9801$
Q8.
Multiply and simplify: $(x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$. What do you observe?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Distribute the terms of the binomial into the trinomial:
\begin{aligned} & (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) \\ &= x(x^2 - xy + y^2) + y(x^2 - xy + y^2) \\ &= (x^3 - x^2y + xy^2) + (x^2y - xy^2 + y^3) \\ &= x^3 - x^2y + x^2y + xy^2 - xy^2 + y^3 \\ &= x^3 + 0 + 0 + y^3 \\ &= x^3 + y^3 \end{aligned}
Observation: This is a standard identity for the sum of cubes $(x^3 + y^3)$.
Ans: $x^3 + y^3$
Q9.
If $x + \frac{1}{x} = 4$, find the value of $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Given: $x + \frac{1}{x} = 4$
Squaring both sides:
\begin{aligned} \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 &= 4^2 \\ x^2 + 2(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 &= 16 \quad \text{[Using } (a+b)^2 = a^2+2ab+b^2\text{]}\\ x^2 + 2 + \frac{1}{x^2} &= 16 \\ x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} &= 16 - 2 \\ x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} &= 14 \end{aligned}
Ans: $14$
Q10.
Subtract the sum of $3l - 4m - 7n^2$ and $2l + 3m - 4n^2$ from the sum of $9l + 2m - 3n^2$ and $-3l + m + 4n^2$.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Find the first sum.
\begin{aligned} S_1 &= (3l - 4m - 7n^2) + (2l + 3m - 4n^2) \\ &= 5l - m - 11n^2 \end{aligned}
Step 2: Find the second sum.
\begin{aligned} S_2 &= (9l + 2m - 3n^2) + (-3l + m + 4n^2) \\ &= 6l + 3m + n^2 \end{aligned}
Step 3: Subtract $S_1$ from $S_2$.
\begin{aligned} & S_2 - S_1 \\ &= (6l + 3m + n^2) - (5l - m - 11n^2) \\ &= 6l + 3m + n^2 - 5l + m + 11n^2 \\ &= l + 4m + 12n^2 \end{aligned}
Ans: $l + 4m + 12n^2$
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11.
Simplify the following expression by correctly removing the brackets:
$2x[3x - (4x - 5)] - 3[x^2 - (2x - 3)]$
[5 Marks]
Solution:
We follow BODMAS rules and open the innermost brackets first.
\begin{aligned} & 2x[3x - (4x - 5)] - 3[x^2 - (2x - 3)] \\ &= 2x[3x - 4x + 5] - 3[x^2 - 2x + 3] \quad \text{(Removing innermost parentheses)} \\ &= 2x[-x + 5] - 3[x^2 - 2x + 3] \quad \text{(Simplifying inside square brackets)} \\ &= (2x)(-x) + (2x)(5) - 3(x^2) - 3(-2x) - 3(3) \quad \text{(Multiplying out)} \\ &= -2x^2 + 10x - 3x^2 + 6x - 9 \\ &= (-2x^2 - 3x^2) + (10x + 6x) - 9 \quad \text{(Grouping like terms)} \\ &= -5x^2 + 16x - 9 \end{aligned}
Ans: $-5x^2 + 16x - 9$
Q12.
Use suitable standard identities to solve the following:
(a) Evaluate without direct multiplication: $103 \times 97$.
(b) Prove that: $(3a + 5b)^2 - 60ab = (3a - 5b)^2$.
[2 + 3 Marks]
Solution (a): [2 Marks]
We can rewrite the numbers as:
\begin{aligned} 103 \times 97 &= (100 + 3)(100 - 3) \end{aligned}
Using identity: $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$
\begin{aligned} &= (100)^2 - (3)^2 \\ &= 10000 - 9 \\ &= 9991 \end{aligned}
Solution (b): [3 Marks]
To prove LHS = RHS. Let's solve the LHS (Left Hand Side):
\begin{aligned} LHS &= (3a + 5b)^2 - 60ab \\ &= [(3a)^2 + 2(3a)(5b) + (5b)^2] - 60ab \quad \text{[Using } (x+y)^2 = x^2+2xy+y^2\text{]}\\ &= 9a^2 + 30ab + 25b^2 - 60ab \\ &= 9a^2 + (30ab - 60ab) + 25b^2 \\ &= 9a^2 - 30ab + 25b^2 \end{aligned}
Now, expanding RHS (Right Hand Side):
\begin{aligned} RHS &= (3a - 5b)^2 \\ &= (3a)^2 - 2(3a)(5b) + (5b)^2 \quad \text{[Using } (x-y)^2 = x^2-2xy+y^2\text{]}\\ &= 9a^2 - 30ab + 25b^2 \end{aligned}
Since LHS = RHS, the statement is proved.
Hence Proved.
Q13.
The length and breadth of a rectangular field are represented by the expressions $(5x - 2y)$ units and $(3x + 4y)$ units, respectively.
(a) Find the perimeter of the rectangle.
(b) Find the area of the rectangle.
[2 + 3 Marks]
Given:
Length $(L) = (5x - 2y)$ units
Breadth $(B) = (3x + 4y)$ units
Solution (a): Perimeter [2 Marks]
Formula: $\text{Perimeter} = 2(L + B)$
\begin{aligned} \text{Perimeter} &= 2 [ (5x - 2y) + (3x + 4y) ] \\ &= 2 [ 5x + 3x - 2y + 4y ] \\ &= 2 [ 8x + 2y ] \\ &= 16x + 4y \text{ units} \end{aligned}
Perimeter: $16x + 4y$ units
Solution (b): Area [3 Marks]
Formula: $\text{Area} = L \times B$
\begin{aligned} \text{Area} &= (5x - 2y)(3x + 4y) \\ &= 5x(3x + 4y) - 2y(3x + 4y) \\ &= 15x^2 + 20xy - 6xy - 8y^2 \\ &= 15x^2 + 14xy - 8y^2 \text{ sq. units} \end{aligned}
Area: $15x^2 + 14xy - 8y^2$ sq. units
