Minor Test - Science & Mathematics
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Science & Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • The paper consists of two parts: Part I (Science) and Part II (Mathematics).
  • Both parts carry 20 marks each. All questions are compulsory.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all working steps for Mathematics.
PART I : SCIENCE (Force) - 20 Marks
SECTION A (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define force. State its SI unit. [2]
2.
Differentiate between contact and non-contact forces. Give one example of each. [2]
SECTION B (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
3.
State any three effects that a force can have on an object. [3]
4.
What is frictional force? State one advantage and one disadvantage of friction in our daily lives. [3]
SECTION C (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
5.
(a) A boy is pushing a heavy rock with all his strength, but the rock does not move. Is he applying a force? Explain your reason.
(b) Explain gravitational force and electrostatic force with the help of one example for each. [2+3=5]
6.
Identify the type of force acting in each of the following scenarios:
(i) A plastic comb rubbed with dry hair attracts tiny pieces of paper.
(ii) A ball thrown upwards comes down to the ground.
(iii) A bullock pulling a cart on a rough road.
(iv) Wearing out of car tyres after long usage.
(v) A magnet attracting iron nails scattered on a table.
[5×1=5]
PART II : MATHEMATICS (Ratio) - 20 Marks
SECTION A (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
7.
Express the ratio $45 : 108$ in its simplest form. [2]
8.
Find the ratio of $40 \text{ minutes}$ to $1.5 \text{ hours}$. [2]
SECTION B (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
9.
Check whether the ratios $15 : 18$ and $25 : 30$ are equivalent. Show your working clearly. [3]
10.
Two numbers are in the ratio $3 : 5$. If the sum of the numbers is $144$, find the two numbers. [3]
SECTION C (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Divide ₹ $4500$ among A, B, and C such that their shares are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. [5]
12.
(a) The ratio of the length to the breadth of a rectangular school field is $5 : 3$. If the length of the field is $120 \text{ m}$, find its breadth.

(b) Compare the ratios $3 : 4$ and $5 : 7$. Which ratio is greater? [2.5+2.5=5]
