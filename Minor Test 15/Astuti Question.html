Minor Test - Science & Mathematics

General Instructions:
  • The paper is divided into two parts: PART I (Science) and PART II (Mathematics).
  • All questions are compulsory. Marks are indicated at the beginning of each section.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all mathematical calculations clearly.
PART I: SCIENCE - MATTER (20 Marks)
SECTION A (4 Marks)(Objective / Very Short Answer - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
Define the following terms:
(a) Matter
(b) Intermolecular space
Q2.
State any two fundamental characteristics of the gaseous state of matter.
SECTION B (6 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q3.
Differentiate between solids, liquids, and gases on the basis of their shape and volume.
Q4.
What is sublimation? Give one practical example of a substance that undergoes sublimation.
SECTION C (10 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q5.
Explain the molecular arrangement in solids, liquids, and gases. How does this arrangement affect their compressibility?
Q6.
Describe what happens to a block of ice at \(0^\circ\text{C}\) when it is continuously heated until it turns into steam at \(100^\circ\text{C}\). Name the changes of state occurring during this process.
PART II: MATHEMATICS - DECIMALS (20 Marks)
SECTION A (4 Marks)(Objective / Short Calculation - 2 Marks Each)
Q7.
Express the mixed fraction \(45 \frac{7}{100}\) as a decimal. What is the place value of the digit 7 in your answer?
Q8.
Add the following decimals: \(23.45 + 7.009 + 112.5\)
SECTION B (6 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q9.
Simplify the given expression: \(85.02 + 12.4 - 43.156\)
Q10.
A roll of ribbon is \(25.5 \text{ m}\) long. If it is cut exactly into 5 equal pieces, what will be the length of each piece?
SECTION C (10 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
Rahul bought \(5.5 \text{ kg}\) of apples at ₹ \(120.00\) per kg and \(3.5 \text{ kg}\) of oranges at ₹ \(80.50\) per kg.
(a) What is the total amount he spent?
(b) If he gave a ₹ 1000 note to the shopkeeper, how much change did he receive?
Q12.
Simplify the following expression using the correct order of operations (BODMAS):
\( 4.5 \times (3.2 + 1.8) - 12.6 \div 3 + 2.15 \)
