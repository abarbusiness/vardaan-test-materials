SECTION A (10 Marks)
1. Express the ratio 75 cm to 1.5 m in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
To compare two quantities, their units must be the same. Let us convert meters to centimeters.
$$ \begin{aligned} 1 \text{ m} &= 100 \text{ cm} \\ \therefore 1.5 \text{ m} &= 1.5 \times 100 = 150 \text{ cm} \end{aligned} $$
Now, express the ratio as a fraction and simplify:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Ratio} &= \frac{75 \text{ cm}}{150 \text{ cm}} \\ &= \frac{75 \div 75}{150 \div 75} \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The ratio in simplest form is 1 : 2.
2. Convert a speed of 72 km/hr into metres per second (m/s).
[2 Marks]
Solution:
To convert speed from km/hr to m/s, we multiply the given speed by $\frac{5}{18}$.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Speed} &= 72 \times \frac{5}{18} \text{ m/s} \\ &= 4 \times 5 \text{ m/s} \\ &= 20 \text{ m/s} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The speed is 20 m/s.
3. If the cost of 8 dozen bananas is ₹480, find the cost of 1 dozen bananas.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Applying the direct unitary method:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Cost of 8 dozen bananas} &= \text{₹} 480 \\ \text{Cost of 1 dozen bananas} &= \frac{480}{8} \\ &= \text{₹} 60 \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The cost of 1 dozen bananas is ₹60.
4. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If the sum of the numbers is 120, find the two numbers.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Let the common ratio multiplier be $x$. Therefore, the two numbers are $3x$ and $5x$.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Sum of numbers} &= 120 \\ 3x + 5x &= 120 \\ 8x &= 120 \\ x &= \frac{120}{8} = 15 \end{aligned} $$
Now, calculate the numbers:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{First number} &= 3x = 3 \times 15 = 45 \\ \text{Second number} &= 5x = 5 \times 15 = 75 \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The two numbers are 45 and 75.
5. A cyclist covers a distance of 15 km in 2 hours. Calculate his average speed in km/hr.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Distance ($D$) = 15 km, Time ($T$) = 2 hours.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Speed} &= \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} \\ &= \frac{15}{2} \text{ km/hr} \\ &= 7.5 \text{ km/hr} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The cyclist's speed is 7.5 km/hr.
SECTION B (15 Marks)
6. Divide an amount of ₹3,600 among A, B, and C in the ratio of 2 : 3 : 4. Find the share of each person.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Total amount to be divided = ₹3,600.
Given ratio = 2 : 3 : 4.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Total parts} &= 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 \text{ parts} \end{aligned} $$
Value of 1 part = $\frac{3600}{9} = \text{₹}400$.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{A's share} &= \frac{2}{9} \times 3600 = 2 \times 400 = \text{₹} 800 \\ \text{B's share} &= \frac{3}{9} \times 3600 = 3 \times 400 = \text{₹} 1200 \\ \text{C's share} &= \frac{4}{9} \times 3600 = 4 \times 400 = \text{₹} 1600 \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: A gets ₹800, B gets ₹1200, and C gets ₹1600.
7. A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How much time (in seconds) will it take to cross a stationary pole?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
To cross a stationary pole, the train must cover a distance equal to its own length.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Distance } (D) &= 150 \text{ m} \end{aligned} $$
Convert speed from km/hr to m/s:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Speed } (S) &= 54 \text{ km/hr} \\ &= 54 \times \frac{5}{18} \text{ m/s} \\ &= 3 \times 5 = 15 \text{ m/s} \end{aligned} $$
Now, calculate the time taken:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Time } (T) &= \frac{D}{S} \\ &= \frac{150}{15} = 10 \text{ seconds} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The train takes 10 seconds to cross the pole.
8. A printing machine can print 1,500 pages in 30 minutes. Using the unitary method, calculate how many pages it will print in 2 hours.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
First, convert the target time (2 hours) into minutes to match units.
$$ \begin{aligned} 2 \text{ hours} &= 2 \times 60 = 120 \text{ minutes} \end{aligned} $$
Apply unitary method:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Pages printed in 30 min} &= 1500 \\ \text{Pages printed in 1 min} &= \frac{1500}{30} = 50 \text{ pages} \\ \text{Pages printed in 120 min} &= 50 \times 120 = 6000 \text{ pages} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The machine will print 6,000 pages in 2 hours.
9. Check if the given ratios form a proportion: 25 g : 30 g and 40 kg : 48 kg. Show your mathematical reasoning.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
For two ratios to be in proportion, their simplest forms must be equal ($a:b = c:d$). Note that ratio units cancel out.
Simplifying the first ratio:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Ratio } 1 &= \frac{25 \text{ g}}{30 \text{ g}} = \frac{25 \div 5}{30 \div 5} = \frac{5}{6} \text{ or } 5 : 6 \end{aligned} $$
Simplifying the second ratio:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Ratio } 2 &= \frac{40 \text{ kg}}{48 \text{ kg}} = \frac{40 \div 8}{48 \div 8} = \frac{5}{6} \text{ or } 5 : 6 \end{aligned} $$
Since $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$, the ratios are equal.
Final Answer: Yes, they form a proportion because their simplest fractions are equal.
10. Rahul travels from his home to school at a speed of 12 km/hr and takes 20 minutes to reach. Find the total distance between his home and the school.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Given: Speed ($S$) = 12 km/hr. Time ($T$) is in minutes, so it must be converted to hours to match the speed unit.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Time } (T) &= 20 \text{ minutes} \\ &= \frac{20}{60} \text{ hours} = \frac{1}{3} \text{ hour} \end{aligned} $$
Calculate distance using the formula $D = S \times T$:
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Distance } (D) &= 12 \times \frac{1}{3} \\ &= 4 \text{ km} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: The distance to school is 4 km.
SECTION C (15 Marks)
11. The cost of 15 meters of cloth is ₹1,425.

(i) Find the cost of 8 meters of the same cloth.
(ii) How many meters of cloth can be purchased for ₹3,325?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i): [2 Marks] Using Unitary Method to find cost.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Cost of 15 m cloth} &= \text{₹} 1425 \\ \text{Cost of 1 m cloth} &= \frac{1425}{15} = \text{₹} 95 \\ \text{Cost of 8 m cloth} &= 95 \times 8 = \text{₹} 760 \end{aligned} $$
Part (ii): [3 Marks] Reversing the unitary relation.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Cloth purchased for ₹} 95 &= 1 \text{ m} \\ \text{Cloth purchased for ₹} 1 &= \frac{1}{95} \text{ m} \\ \text{Cloth purchased for ₹} 3325 &= \frac{1}{95} \times 3325 \end{aligned} $$
Dividing 3325 by 95:
$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{3325}{95} &= 35 \text{ m} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: (i) The cost is ₹760. (ii) 35 meters can be purchased.
12. A car travels from City P to City Q at a speed of 40 km/hr and returns from Q to P at 60 km/hr. Distance = 120 km. Calculate:

(i) Time taken for onward journey.
(ii) Time taken for return journey.
(iii) Average speed for the entire journey.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i): [1.5 Marks] Onward Journey (P to Q)
Distance ($D_1$) = 120 km. Speed ($S_1$) = 40 km/hr.
$$ \begin{aligned} T_1 &= \frac{D_1}{S_1} = \frac{120}{40} = 3 \text{ hours} \end{aligned} $$
Part (ii): [1.5 Marks] Return Journey (Q to P)
Distance ($D_2$) = 120 km. Speed ($S_2$) = 60 km/hr.
$$ \begin{aligned} T_2 &= \frac{D_2}{S_2} = \frac{120}{60} = 2 \text{ hours} \end{aligned} $$
Part (iii): [2 Marks] Average Speed
Average speed is Total Distance divided by Total Time.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Total Distance} &= 120 + 120 = 240 \text{ km} \\ \text{Total Time} &= 3 + 2 = 5 \text{ hours} \\ \text{Average Speed} &= \frac{240}{5} = 48 \text{ km/hr} \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: (i) 3 hours. (ii) 2 hours. (iii) 48 km/hr.
13. Ratio of boys to girls in a school of 1,200 students is 7 : 5.

(i) Find initial number of boys and girls.
(ii) If 40 more girls are admitted, what is the new ratio of boys to girls in simplest form?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i): [2 Marks] Initial Distribution
Total students = 1200. Ratio = 7 : 5. Total parts = 7 + 5 = 12.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Number of Boys} &= \frac{7}{12} \times 1200 = 7 \times 100 = 700 \\ \text{Number of Girls} &= \frac{5}{12} \times 1200 = 5 \times 100 = 500 \end{aligned} $$
Part (ii): [3 Marks] New Ratio Calculation
40 new girls are admitted. The number of boys remains the same.
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{New number of Girls} &= 500 + 40 = 540 \\ \text{Number of Boys} &= 700 \end{aligned} $$
Calculate the new ratio (Boys : Girls):
$$ \begin{aligned} \text{New Ratio} &= 700 : 540 \\ &= \frac{700}{540} = \frac{70}{54} \end{aligned} $$
Dividing numerator and denominator by their HCF (2):
$$ \begin{aligned} &= \frac{35}{27} \text{ or } 35 : 27 \end{aligned} $$
Final Answer: (i) Boys = 700, Girls = 500. (ii) New Ratio is 35 : 27.
