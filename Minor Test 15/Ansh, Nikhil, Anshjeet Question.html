MIT 15
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Algebraic Expressions) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all working clearly.
  • Use of standard algebraic identities is expected where applicable.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Add the following algebraic expressions: $3x^2 - 5x + 7$ and $-2x^2 + 3x - 4$.
2.
Subtract $4a - 7ab + 3b + 12$ from $12a - 9ab + 5b - 3$.
3.
Find the product of the binomials: $(3x - 2y)(4x + 5y)$.
4.
Expand the following expression using a suitable identity: $(2p + 3q)^2$.
5.
Simplify the given expression: $5x(x - 2) - 3x^2 + 10x$.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Divide the polynomial $15m^3n^2 - 10m^2n^3 + 5mn$ by the monomial $5mn$.
7.
Evaluate the square of $99$ by using a standard algebraic identity.
8.
Multiply and simplify: $(x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$. What do you observe?
9.
If $x + \frac{1}{x} = 4$, find the value of $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$.
10.
Subtract the sum of $3l - 4m - 7n^2$ and $2l + 3m - 4n^2$ from the sum of $9l + 2m - 3n^2$ and $-3l + m + 4n^2$.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Simplify the following expression by correctly removing the brackets:

$2x[3x - (4x - 5)] - 3[x^2 - (2x - 3)]$
12.
Use suitable standard identities to solve the following:
(a) Evaluate without direct multiplication: $103 \times 97$. [2 Marks]
(b) Prove that: $(3a + 5b)^2 - 60ab = (3a - 5b)^2$. [3 Marks]
13.
The length and breadth of a rectangular field are represented by the expressions $(5x - 2y)$ units and $(3x + 4y)$ units, respectively.
(a) Find the perimeter of the rectangle. [2 Marks]
(b) Find the area of the rectangle. [3 Marks]
