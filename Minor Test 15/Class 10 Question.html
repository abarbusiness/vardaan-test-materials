Minor Test 15 - Trigonometry & Heights and Distances
Class: 10 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working steps clearly. Draw neat and labelled diagrams wherever necessary.
  • Use of calculator is not permitted. Strictly based on Rationalized NCERT Syllabus.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
If \( \tan A = \frac{3}{4} \), find the value of \( \sin A \cos A \).
2.
Evaluate: \( 2 \tan^2 45^\circ + \cos^2 30^\circ - \sin^2 60^\circ \).
3.
Prove the identity: \( (1 - \sin^2 \theta) \sec^2 \theta = 1 \).
4.
A tower is \( 10\sqrt{3} \) m high. Find the angle of elevation of its top from a point 30 m away from its foot on the ground.
5.
If \( \sin(A - B) = \frac{1}{2} \) and \( \cos(A + B) = \frac{1}{2} \), where \( 0^\circ < A + B \le 90^\circ \) and \( A > B \), find \( A \) and \( B \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Prove that: \( \frac{\cos A}{1 + \sin A} + \frac{1 + \sin A}{\cos A} = 2 \sec A \).
7.
A circus artist is climbing a 20 m long rope, which is tightly stretched and tied from the top of a vertical pole to the ground. Find the height of the pole, if the angle made by the rope with the ground level is \( 30^\circ \).
8.
If \( x = a \sin \theta \) and \( y = b \tan \theta \), prove that \( \frac{a^2}{x^2} - \frac{b^2}{y^2} = 1 \).
9.
The shadow of a tower standing on a level ground is found to be 40 m longer when the Sun's altitude is \( 30^\circ \) than when it is \( 60^\circ \). Find the height of the tower.
10.
Prove that: \( \sqrt{\frac{1+\sin A}{1-\sin A}} = \sec A + \tan A \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
From the top of a 7 m high building, the angle of elevation of the top of a cable tower is \( 60^\circ \) and the angle of depression of its foot is \( 45^\circ \). Determine the height of the tower.
12.
Prove that: \( \frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} = 1 + \sec \theta \csc \theta \).
13.
A straight highway leads to the foot of a tower. A man standing at the top of the tower observes a car at an angle of depression of \( 30^\circ \), which is approaching the foot of the tower with a uniform speed. Six seconds later, the angle of depression of the car is found to be \( 60^\circ \). Find the time taken by the car to reach the foot of the tower from this point.
