Minor Test: Science & Mathematics
Class: 6 ICSE Subjects: Science & Maths Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into Two Parts: PART I (Science - 20 Marks) and PART II (Maths - 20 Marks).
  • Each part is further divided into Sections A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working for mathematical calculations.
PART I : SCIENCE (20 Marks)
SECTION A (4 Marks)(Objective / Short Answer - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
State any two fundamental differences between an Element and a Compound.
Q2.
Define contact and non-contact forces. Give one example of each.
SECTION B (6 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q3.
Identify the most appropriate separation technique used for separating the following mixtures:
(a) Iron filings from sand
(b) Pure water from a salt solution
(c) Mud from muddy water
Q4.
A heavy wooden box is resting on the floor. State any three effects that a force can produce on a body, illustrating them with examples from everyday life.
SECTION C (10 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q5.
(a) Define a mixture. (2 Marks)
(b) State three distinct properties that differentiate a mixture from a chemical compound. (3 Marks)
Q6.
(a) What is friction? Which type of force is it (contact or non-contact)? (2 Marks)
(b) "Friction is a necessary evil." Justify this statement by stating two advantages and two disadvantages of friction in our daily life. (3 Marks)
PART II : MATHEMATICS (20 Marks)
SECTION A (4 Marks)(Objective / Short Answer - 2 Marks Each)
Q7.
Convert the following fractions into decimals:
(a) \( \frac{45}{100} \)
(b) \( 7\frac{3}{10} \)
Q8.
Arrange the following decimal numbers in ascending order:
\( 3.14, \quad 3.41, \quad 3.04, \quad 3.104, \quad 3.401 \)
SECTION B (6 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q9.
Simplify the following decimal expression:
\( 14.56 + 23.05 - 8.791 \)
Q10.
A tailor requires \( 2.45 \) meters of cloth to stitch one uniform shirt. Calculate the total length of cloth required to stitch 15 such shirts.
SECTION C (10 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
Evaluate the following:
(a) \( 4.56 \times 1.2 \)    (2 Marks)
(b) \( 15.12 \div 3.6 \)    (3 Marks)
Q12.
Rahul went to the market and bought \( 5.5 \text{ kg} \) of apples at the rate of Rs. 120.50 per kg and \( 2.75 \text{ kg} \) of oranges at the rate of Rs. 80.25 per kg. Calculate the total amount paid by Rahul to the shopkeeper. Show all your calculations clearly.
