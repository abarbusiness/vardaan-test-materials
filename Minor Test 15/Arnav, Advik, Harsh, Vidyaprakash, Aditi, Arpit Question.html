Minor Test 15
Class: 8 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1.5 Hours Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 5 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and show necessary calculations.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the selling price of an article if its cost price is ₹ 500 and the profit made on it is 10%.
2.
A shirt marked at ₹ 800 is sold for ₹ 680 during a festive sale. Find the discount given and the discount percentage.
3.
Calculate the VAT (Value Added Tax) charged at 5% on a washing machine bought for ₹ 12,000.
4.
By selling a textbook for ₹ 250, a bookseller makes a profit of ₹ 50. Find his profit percentage.
5.
The marked price of an electronic toy is ₹ 1,200. A discount of 15% is allowed by the shopkeeper. Find the final selling price of the toy.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A shopkeeper buys 80 articles for ₹ 2,400 and sells them for a profit of 16%. Find the selling price of one article.
7.
A dining table is marked at ₹ 15,000 and is available at a discount of 20%. Find its selling price. If 5% GST is charged on the selling price, find the total amount the customer has to pay.
8.
Rohan bought a second-hand car for ₹ 45,000 and spent ₹ 5,000 on its repairs and painting. He then sold it for ₹ 55,000. Find his total gain or loss percent.
9.
Find the final buying price of a television set, when an 8% sales tax is added on the purchase price of ₹ 15,500.
10.
A shopkeeper marks his goods at 25% above the cost price and then allows a discount of 10% on the marked price. Find his overall gain percent.
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A man sold two ceiling fans for ₹ 990 each. On one he gains 10% and on the other he loses 10%. Find his total cost price and his total gain or loss percent in the whole transaction.
12.
Megha bought a pair of roller skates at a clearance sale where a discount of 20% was given. If the amount she pays after the discount is ₹ 1,600, find the original marked price. Also, find the final billing amount if a 12% GST is applied to the discounted price.
13.
A dealer bought two identical TV sets at ₹ 10,000 each. He sold one at a profit of 10% and the other at a loss of 10%. Calculate the selling price of each TV set. Did he make an overall profit or loss, and by what percentage?
14.
The price of a refrigerator is ₹ 24,000. The shopkeeper gives successive discounts of 10% and 5% to a customer. Find the final selling price of the refrigerator. Also, calculate the total equivalent discount given in percentage.
15.
A retailer buys a geyser for ₹ 4,800. He marks the price 20% above his cost price. If he gives a 10% discount on the marked price and charges 18% GST on the discounted price, calculate:
(a) The Marked Price
(b) The Discount amount in rupees
(c) The Selling Price before tax
(d) The final total amount paid by the customer.
