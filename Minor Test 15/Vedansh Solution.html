Q11. Divide ₹ $4500$ among A, B, and C such that their shares are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. [5]
Ans:
Total amount to be divided = ₹ $4500$
Given ratio of shares = $2 : 3 : 4$
Sum of the ratio parts = $2 + 3 + 4 = 9$ [1 Mark]

Value of one part: $$ \frac{4500}{9} = \text{₹ } 500 $$ [1 Mark]

Now, calculating individual shares:
A's share = $2 \text{ parts} = 2 \times 500 = \text{₹ } 1000$ [1 Mark]
B's share = $3 \text{ parts} = 3 \times 500 = \text{₹ } 1500$ [1 Mark]
C's share = $4 \text{ parts} = 4 \times 500 = \text{₹ } 2000$ [1 Mark]
Q12. (a) The ratio of the length to the breadth of a rectangular field is $5 : 3$. If the length is $120 \text{ m}$, find its breadth.
(b) Compare the ratios $3 : 4$ and $5 : 7$. Which ratio is greater? [5]
Ans:
(a) Ratio of length to breadth = $5 : 3$.
Let the length be $5x$ and the breadth be $3x$. [0.5 Mark]
Given length = $120 \text{ m}$.
\begin{align*} 5x &= 120 \\ x &= \frac{120}{5} = 24 \end{align*}
[1 Mark]
Breadth = $3x = 3 \times 24 = 72 \text{ m}$.
The breadth of the field is $72 \text{ m}$. [1 Mark]

(b) We have the fractions $\frac{3}{4}$ and $\frac{5}{7}$.
To compare them, make the denominators equal by finding the LCM of $4$ and $7$.
LCM(4, 7) = $28$. [0.5 Mark]
\begin{align*} \frac{3 \times 7}{4 \times 7} &= \frac{21}{28} \\ \frac{5 \times 4}{7 \times 4} &= \frac{20}{28} \end{align*}
[1 Mark]
Comparing numerators: $21 > 20$, therefore $\frac{21}{28} > \frac{20}{28}$.
Hence, the ratio $3 : 4$ is greater. [1 Mark]
