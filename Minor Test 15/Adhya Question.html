MINOR TEST 15
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working clearly where required.
  • Syllabus: Ratio, Speed Time & Distance, The Unitary Method.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Express the ratio 75 cm to 1.5 m in its simplest form.
2.
Convert a speed of 72 km/hr into metres per second (m/s).
3.
If the cost of 8 dozen bananas is ₹480, find the cost of 1 dozen bananas.
4.
Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If the sum of the numbers is 120, find the two numbers.
5.
A cyclist covers a distance of 15 km in 2 hours. Calculate his average speed in km/hr.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Divide an amount of ₹3,600 among A, B, and C in the ratio of 2 : 3 : 4. Find the share of each person.
7.
A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How much time (in seconds) will it take to cross a stationary pole?
8.
A printing machine can print 1,500 pages in 30 minutes. Using the unitary method, calculate how many pages it will print in 2 hours.
9.
Check if the given ratios form a proportion: 25 g : 30 g and 40 kg : 48 kg. Show your mathematical reasoning.
10.
Rahul travels from his home to school at a speed of 12 km/hr and takes 20 minutes to reach. Find the total distance between his home and the school.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The cost of 15 meters of cloth is ₹1,425.
(i)
Find the cost of 8 meters of the same cloth. (2 Marks)
(ii)
How many meters of cloth can be purchased for ₹3,325? (3 Marks)
12.
A car travels from City P to City Q at a speed of 40 km/hr and returns from City Q to City P at a speed of 60 km/hr. The distance between the two cities is 120 km. Calculate:
(i)
The time taken for the onward journey (P to Q). (1.5 Marks)
(ii)
The time taken for the return journey (Q to P). (1.5 Marks)
(iii)
The average speed for the entire journey. (2 Marks)
13.
The ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls in a school of 1,200 students is 7 : 5.
(i)
Find the initial number of boys and girls in the school. (2 Marks)
(ii)
If 40 more girls are admitted to the school, what will be the new ratio of boys to girls in its simplest form? (3 Marks)
