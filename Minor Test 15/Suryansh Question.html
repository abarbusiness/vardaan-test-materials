Minor Test - 01
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Science & Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into two parts: Part I (Science) and Part II (Mathematics), each carrying 20 marks.
  • Write the question number clearly before answering.
  • Draw neat, labeled diagrams wherever necessary. Show all mathematical steps clearly.
PART I : SCIENCE (Metals & Non-Metals) - 20 Marks
Very Short Answer Type Questions (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define the terms malleability and ductility. Give one example of a metal that exhibits both properties highly.
2.
Why is sodium metal always kept immersed in kerosene oil? State the reason scientifically.
3.
Differentiate between metals and non-metals based on their electrical conductivity. Name one non-metal that is an exception to this rule.
Short Answer Type Questions (Attempt all questions - 3 Marks Each)
4.
What happens when typical metals react with dilute acids? Explain by writing the word equation for the reaction between Zinc and dilute Hydrochloric acid. Mention how you would test the gas evolved.
5.
A student placed a copper coin in a beaker containing zinc sulphate solution. Will any reaction occur? Give a reason for your answer and state the underlying principle.
6.
Non-metals are essential for our existence. Justify this statement by listing three vital uses of non-metals in our daily lives or industry.
Long Answer Type Question (Attempt all questions - 5 Marks Each)
7.
(a) What do you understand by a 'displacement reaction'? (2 marks)
(b) What happens when an iron nail is placed in a solution of copper sulphate? Write the word equation and describe the color changes observed in the solution and on the nail. (3 marks)
PART II : MATHEMATICS (Algebraic Expressions) - 20 Marks
Very Short Answer Type Questions (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
8.
Identify the terms and their respective numerical coefficients in the expression: \( 5x^2y - 3xy^2 + 7 \)
9.
Subtract \( 3a - 4b + 5c \) from \( 5a + 2b - 3c \).
10.
Find the value of the expression \( 2x^2 - 3x + 5 \) when \( x = -2 \).
Short Answer Type Questions (Attempt all questions - 3 Marks Each)
11.
Simplify the expression and find its value if \( a = 5 \) and \( b = -3 \):
\( 2(a^2 + ab) + 3 - ab \)
12.
Add the following algebraic terms: \( 4x^2y, -3xy^2, -5xy^2, \) and \( 5x^2y \). Group the like terms together clearly.
13.
What should be added to \( x^2 + xy + y^2 \) to obtain \( 2x^2 + 3xy \)?
Long Answer Type Question (Attempt all questions - 5 Marks Each)
14.
(a) From the sum of \( 2y^2 + 3yz \) and \( -y^2 - yz - z^2 \), subtract the sum of \( 3y^2 - z^2 \) and \( -y^2 + yz + z^2 \). (3 marks)
(b) Classify the following algebraic expressions as monomials, binomials, or trinomials: (2 marks)
(i) \( 4y - 7z \)     (ii) \( 100 \)     (iii) \( x + y - xy \)     (iv) \( 5x^2 \)
