Minor Test 15: Algebra & Linear Equations
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show step-by-step working.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the terms and their numerical coefficients in the following algebraic expression:
\( 3x^2y - 5xy^2 + 7 \)
2.
Translate the following statements into mathematical equations:
(i) 5 times a number \( x \) decreased by 7 results in 23.
(ii) One-fourth of a number \( m \) is 3 more than 7.
3.
Group the like terms together from the following given list of terms:
\( 4x^2y, \ -3xy^2, \ 7xy, \ -5x^2y, \ 2xy^2 \)
4.
Solve the linear equation \( 3p - 10 = 5 \) and verify your result by substituting the root back into the equation.
5.
Find the numerical value of the expression \( a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \) when \( a = 3 \) and \( b = -2 \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Subtract the expression \( (3a - 4b - c) \) from \( (4a + 5b - 3c) \). Then, evaluate the resulting expression if \( a=1 \), \( b=-1 \), and \( c=2 \).
7.
The sum of three consecutive odd integers is 69. Formulate an equation and find the three integers.
8.
Simplify the following algebraic expression by combining the like terms:
\( 7x^2 - 3x + 5 - (2x^2 - 5x - 7) + 4x \)
9.
Solve the following linear equation for \( y \):
\[ \frac{y}{2} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{y}{3} \]
10.
Rahul's father is 26 years younger than Rahul's grandfather and 29 years older than Rahul. The sum of the ages of all three is 135 years. Set up a linear equation to find the present age of each person.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
From the sum of \( 2x^2 + 3xy - 5 \) and \( -4x^2 + 2xy + 7 \), subtract the sum of \( 3x^2 - xy + 2 \) and \( -x^2 - 4xy - 3 \). Show all intermediate addition and subtraction steps clearly.
12.
A rational number is such that when you multiply it by \( \frac{5}{2} \) and add \( \frac{2}{3} \) to the product, you get \( -\frac{7}{12} \). Formulate a linear equation in one variable and calculate the original number. Justify your answer.
13.
The perimeter of a rectangular swimming pool is 154 meters. Its length is 2 meters more than twice its breadth.
(a) Write the expressions for the length and breadth in terms of a single variable.
(b) Set up the linear equation representing the perimeter.
(c) Solve the equation to find the exact length and breadth of the pool.
