SECTION A (10 Marks)
1. Reduce the fraction \(\frac{144}{180}\) to its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
To reduce the fraction to its simplest form, we find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of the numerator and the denominator.
Numerator = 144, Denominator = 180.
Prime factorization of 144 = \(2^4 \times 3^2\)
Prime factorization of 180 = \(2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5\)
HCF = \(2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36\)
Dividing both numerator and denominator by 36:
\[ \frac{144 \div 36}{180 \div 36} = \frac{4}{5} \]
Final Answer: \(\frac{4}{5}\)

3. Divide an amount of ₹ 1,200 between A and B in the ratio 3 : 5.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Given ratio = \(3 : 5\)
Total parts = \(3 + 5 = 8\) parts.
Total amount = ₹ 1,200
A's share = \(\frac{3}{8}\) of ₹ 1,200
\[ \text{A's share} = \frac{3}{8} \times 1200 = 3 \times 150 = \text{₹ } 450 \]
B's share = \(\frac{5}{8}\) of ₹ 1,200
\[ \text{B's share} = \frac{5}{8} \times 1200 = 5 \times 150 = \text{₹ } 750 \]
Final Answer: A gets ₹ 450, B gets ₹ 750.

5. Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 84 using the prime factorization method.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Find the prime factorization of each number.
\[ 36 = 2 \times 18 = 2 \times 2 \times 9 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \] \[ 84 = 2 \times 42 = 2 \times 2 \times 21 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7 \]
Step 2: Take the lowest power of common prime factors.
Common prime factors are 2 and 3.
Lowest power of 2 is \(2^2\). Lowest power of 3 is \(3^1\).
\[ \text{HCF} = 2^2 \times 3 = 4 \times 3 = 12 \]
Final Answer: 12
SECTION B (15 Marks)
6. Are the numbers 12, 16, 18, and 24 in proportion? Provide mathematical reasoning to justify your answer.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Four numbers \(a, b, c, d\) are in proportion if \(a : b = c : d\), which means the Product of Extremes equals the Product of Means (\(a \times d = b \times c\)).
Here, the numbers are 12, 16, 18, and 24.
Extremes (first and last terms) = 12 and 24.
\[ \text{Product of Extremes} = 12 \times 24 = 288 \]
Means (middle terms) = 16 and 18.
\[ \text{Product of Means} = 16 \times 18 = 288 \]
Since the Product of Extremes = Product of Means (288 = 288), the numbers are in proportion.
Final Answer: Yes, they are in proportion.
7. At 6:00 AM, the temperature in a town was \(-4^\circ\text{C}\). By noon, the temperature rose by \(7^\circ\text{C}\), and by midnight, it dropped by \(5^\circ\text{C}\). What was the exact temperature at midnight?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Initial temperature at 6:00 AM = \(-4^\circ\text{C}\)
Rise in temperature by noon = \(+7^\circ\text{C}\) (Rise indicates addition)
\[ \text{Temperature at noon} = -4 + 7 = 3^\circ\text{C} \]
Drop in temperature by midnight = \(5^\circ\text{C}\) (Drop indicates subtraction)
\[ \text{Temperature at midnight} = 3 - 5 = -2^\circ\text{C} \]
Final Answer: \(-2^\circ\text{C}\)
8. Arrange the following fractions in descending order: \(\frac{2}{3}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{6}, \frac{7}{12}\)
[3 Marks]
Solution:
To compare fractions, we make their denominators equal by finding the LCM of 3, 4, 6, and 12.
LCM of 3, 4, 6, 12 is 12.
Convert each fraction into an equivalent fraction with denominator 12:
\begin{align*} \frac{2}{3} &= \frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \frac{8}{12} \\ \frac{3}{4} &= \frac{3 \times 3}{4 \times 3} = \frac{9}{12} \\ \frac{5}{6} &= \frac{5 \times 2}{6 \times 2} = \frac{10}{12} \\ \frac{7}{12} &= \frac{7 \times 1}{12 \times 1} = \frac{7}{12} \end{align*}
Now compare the numerators: 10 > 9 > 8 > 7.
So, \(\frac{10}{12} > \frac{9}{12} > \frac{8}{12} > \frac{7}{12}\)
Final Answer: \(\frac{5}{6} > \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} > \frac{7}{12}\)
9. Three bells toll at intervals of 12, 15, and 20 minutes respectively. If they all toll together at 8:00 AM, at what time will they toll together again?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
The time after which they will toll together again is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of their intervals (12, 15, 20).
Prime factorization:
\[ 12 = 2^2 \times 3 \] \[ 15 = 3 \times 5 \] \[ 20 = 2^2 \times 5 \]
LCM = Highest power of all prime factors present = \(2^2 \times 3 \times 5\)
\[ \text{LCM} = 4 \times 3 \times 5 = 60 \text{ minutes} \]
60 minutes = 1 Hour.
If they tolled together at 8:00 AM, they will toll together after 1 hour.
\[ \text{New Time} = 8:00 \text{ AM} + 1 \text{ Hour} = 9:00 \text{ AM} \]
Final Answer: 9:00 AM
10. In a class of 60 students, \(\frac{2}{5}\) of the total students are girls and the rest are boys. Find the ratio of the number of boys to the total number of students in the class.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Total number of students = 60
Number of girls = \(\frac{2}{5}\) of 60
\[ \text{Girls} = \frac{2}{5} \times 60 = 2 \times 12 = 24 \]
Number of boys = Total students - Number of girls
\[ \text{Boys} = 60 - 24 = 36 \]
Ratio of number of boys to total number of students = Boys : Total
\[ \text{Ratio} = 36 : 60 \]
Divide both by 12 (their HCF) to simplify:
\[ \frac{36}{12} : \frac{60}{12} = 3 : 5 \]
Final Answer: 3 : 5
SECTION C (15 Marks)
11. Simplify the following expression using the correct order of operations (BODMAS):
\[ 3 \frac{1}{2} - \left[ \frac{2}{3} \times \left( \frac{5}{6} + \frac{1}{2} \right) \right] \]
[5 Marks]
Solution:
According to BODMAS, we solve the inner round brackets ( ) first.
\[ \left( \frac{5}{6} + \frac{1}{2} \right) \]
LCM of 6 and 2 is 6.
\[ = \left( \frac{5}{6} + \frac{3}{6} \right) = \frac{8}{6} \]
Simplifying \(\frac{8}{6}\) by dividing by 2: \(\frac{4}{3}\)
Now substitute this back into the square bracket [ ]:
\[ \left[ \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{3} \right] \]
Multiply numerator with numerator and denominator with denominator:
\[ = \frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 3} = \frac{8}{9} \]
Now, subtract this result from the first fraction. First, convert the mixed fraction to an improper fraction:
\[ 3 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{(3 \times 2) + 1}{2} = \frac{7}{2} \]
Now perform the subtraction:
\[ \frac{7}{2} - \frac{8}{9} \]
LCM of 2 and 9 is 18. Convert to equivalent fractions:
\[ \frac{7 \times 9}{2 \times 9} - \frac{8 \times 2}{9 \times 2} = \frac{63}{18} - \frac{16}{18} \] \[ = \frac{47}{18} \]
Convert back to a mixed fraction:
\[ 47 \div 18 = 2 \text{ with a remainder of } 11 \rightarrow 2 \frac{11}{18} \]
Final Answer: \( 2 \frac{11}{18} \)
12. The ratio of the length to the breadth of a rectangular field is 5 : 3. If the perimeter of the field is 128 meters, determine the length and breadth of the field. Also, calculate its total area.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let the common ratio multiplier be \(x\).
Length (\(L\)) = \(5x\)
Breadth (\(B\)) = \(3x\)
The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is \(2 \times (L + B)\).
Given perimeter = 128 meters.
\[ 2 \times (5x + 3x) = 128 \] \[ 2 \times (8x) = 128 \] \[ 16x = 128 \]
Solving for \(x\):
\[ x = \frac{128}{16} = 8 \]
Now, calculate exact dimensions:
\[ \text{Length } (L) = 5x = 5 \times 8 = 40 \text{ meters} \] \[ \text{Breadth } (B) = 3x = 3 \times 8 = 24 \text{ meters} \]
Calculate the Area of the rectangular field:
\[ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth} \] \[ \text{Area} = 40 \times 24 = 960 \text{ sq. meters} (m^2) \]
Final Answer: Length = 40m, Breadth = 24m, Area = 960 m\(^2\)
13. Find the greatest number of 4 digits which is exactly divisible by 12, 16, and 24. Show all your working steps clearly.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 12, 16, and 24. This will give us the smallest number divisible by all three.
Prime Factorization:
\begin{align*} 12 &= 2^2 \times 3 \\ 16 &= 2^4 \\ 24 &= 2^3 \times 3 \end{align*}
LCM = Highest powers of prime factors = \(2^4 \times 3 = 16 \times 3 = 48\).
Step 2: Identify the greatest 4-digit number.
The greatest 4-digit number is 9999.
Step 3: Divide the greatest 4-digit number by the LCM to find the remainder.
\[ 9999 \div 48 \]
- 48 goes into 99 twice (48 × 2 = 96). Remainder = 3.
- Bring down 9 to make 39. 48 goes into 39 zero times. Remainder = 39.
- Bring down 9 to make 399. 48 goes into 399 eight times (48 × 8 = 384).
- Remainder = 399 - 384 = 15.
\[ 9999 = (48 \times 208) + 15 \]
Step 4: Subtract the remainder from 9999.
If we subtract this remainder (15) from 9999, the result will be perfectly divisible by 48 (and therefore divisible by 12, 16, and 24).
\[ \text{Required Number} = 9999 - 15 = 9984 \]
Final Answer: 9984
