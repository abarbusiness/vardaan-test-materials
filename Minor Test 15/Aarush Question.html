Minor Test: Maths & Science (CBSE)
Class: 7 Subject: Science & Maths Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
  • Science questions are presented first, followed by Maths questions in each section.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
State whether the following are physical or chemical changes:
(a) Melting of wax
(b) Rusting of iron
Q2.
Explain briefly why tearing a sheet of paper is considered a physical change while burning the same paper is a chemical change.
Q3.
Evaluate the following decimal addition and subtraction:
(a) \( 15.65 + 3.2 \)
(b) \( 24.5 - 11.23 \)
Q4.
Multiply the following decimals:
(a) \( 3.5 \times 10 \)
(b) \( 1.2 \times 0.4 \)
Q5.
Divide the following decimals:
(a) \( 14.5 \div 10 \)
(b) \( 3.6 \div 0.6 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
Identify the gas produced when vinegar (acetic acid) is added to baking soda (sodium hydrogencarbonate). Write the word equation for this reaction. What type of change is this?
Q7.
List three differences between a physical change and a chemical change.
Q8.
Rahul travelled \( 14.5 \text{ km} \) by bus and \( 2.75 \text{ km} \) by auto rickshaw to reach his school.
(a) What is the total distance he travelled?
(b) Express the total distance in meters.
Q9.
A car covers a distance of \( 89.1 \text{ km} \) in \( 2.2 \text{ hours} \). What is the average distance covered by it in 1 hour?
Q10.
Solve the following expression using the rules of operations on decimals:
\( 12.5 + (3.4 \times 2) - 1.5 \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
(a) Explain the process of rusting of iron. Write the conditions necessary for rusting to occur. (3 marks)
(b) Suggest two methods to prevent the rusting of iron. (2 marks)
Q12.
When a candle burns, both physical and chemical changes take place.
(a) Identify and explain the physical change. (2 marks)
(b) Identify and explain the chemical change. (2 marks)
(c) Give one more example of a daily life process where both changes occur simultaneously. (1 mark)
Q13.
A rectangular garden has a length of \( 15.75 \text{ m} \) and a width of \( 8.4 \text{ m} \).
(a) Find the perimeter of the garden. (2 marks)
(b) Find the area of the garden. (2 marks)
(c) If fencing costs ₹ \( 20.50 \) per meter, calculate the total cost of fencing the garden. (1 mark)
