Q1. Identify the terms and their numerical coefficients in the following algebraic expression: \( 3x^2y - 5xy^2 + 7 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
An algebraic expression is separated into terms by \(+\) or \(-\) signs.
  • Term 1: \( 3x^2y \)  →  Numerical Coefficient = 3 [1 mark]
  • Term 2: \( -5xy^2 \)  →  Numerical Coefficient = -5 (Sign is important) [0.5 mark]
  • Term 3: \( 7 \)  →  Numerical Coefficient = 7 (Constant term) [0.5 mark]
Q2. Translate the statements into equations:
(i) 5 times a number \( x \) decreased by 7 results in 23.
(ii) One-fourth of a number \( m \) is 3 more than 7.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
(i) "5 times a number \( x \)" is \( 5x \). Decreased by 7 means \( -7 \). Results in 23 means \( = 23 \).
\[ 5x - 7 = 23 \]
[1 mark]
(ii) "One-fourth of \( m \)" is \( \frac{m}{4} \). "3 more than 7" means \( 7 + 3 \) or \( 10 \).
\[ \frac{m}{4} = 7 + 3 \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{m}{4} = 10 \]
[1 mark]
Q3. Group the like terms together: \( 4x^2y, \ -3xy^2, \ 7xy, \ -5x^2y, \ 2xy^2 \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Like terms have identically the same algebraic factors (same variables raised to the same powers).
  • Group 1: Terms with \( x^2y \) are \( 4x^2y \) and \( -5x^2y \)
  • Group 2: Terms with \( xy^2 \) are \( -3xy^2 \) and \( 2xy^2 \)
  • Group 3: Term with \( xy \) is \( 7xy \) (Unpaired)
Q4. Solve \( 3p - 10 = 5 \) and verify the result.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Solving: Transposing \(-10\) to the Right Hand Side (RHS):
\[ 3p = 5 + 10 \]
\[ 3p = 15 \]
\[ p = \frac{15}{3} \implies \mathbf{p = 5} \]
[1 mark]
Verification: Substitute \( p = 5 \) in LHS.
LHS = \( 3(5) - 10 = 15 - 10 = 5 \).
RHS = 5. Since LHS = RHS, Verified. [1 mark]

Q6. Subtract \( (3a - 4b - c) \) from \( (4a + 5b - 3c) \). Then evaluate if \( a=1, b=-1, c=2 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Subtraction setup
Always put the expression "from" which we are subtracting first.
\[ (4a + 5b - 3c) - (3a - 4b - c) \]
Step 2: Remove brackets and reverse signs of the second expression:
\[ = 4a + 5b - 3c - 3a + 4b + c \]
Step 3: Combine like terms:
\[ = (4a - 3a) + (5b + 4b) + (-3c + c) \] \[ = \mathbf{a + 9b - 2c} \] [1.5 marks]
Step 4: Substitution: Put \( a=1, b=-1, c=2 \)
\[ = (1) + 9(-1) - 2(2) \] \[ = 1 - 9 - 4 \] \[ = -8 - 4 = \mathbf{-12} \]
Answer = -12
[1.5 marks]
Q7. The sum of three consecutive odd integers is 69. Find the integers.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Let the first odd integer be \( x \).
Since odd integers have a gap of 2, the next consecutive odd integers will be \( (x + 2) \) and \( (x + 4) \). [1 mark]
According to the problem, their sum is 69:
\[ x + (x + 2) + (x + 4) = 69 \]
\[ 3x + 6 = 69 \]
\[ 3x = 69 - 6 \]
\[ 3x = 63 \implies x = \frac{63}{3} \implies x = 21 \] [1 mark]
Therefore, the three integers are:
First = 21
Second = \( 21 + 2 = 23 \)
Third = \( 21 + 4 = 25 \)
The consecutive odd integers are 21, 23, and 25.
[1 mark]
Q8. Simplify: \( 7x^2 - 3x + 5 - (2x^2 - 5x - 7) + 4x \)
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Open the parenthesis, remember to multiply each term inside by the negative sign outside.
\[ = 7x^2 - 3x + 5 - 2x^2 + 5x + 7 + 4x \] [1 mark]
Step 2: Group the like terms:
\[ = (7x^2 - 2x^2) + (-3x + 5x + 4x) + (5 + 7) \] [1 mark]
Step 3: Add/Subtract coefficients:
\[ = (5)x^2 + (6)x + (12) \]
Answer = \( 5x^2 + 6x + 12 \)
[1 mark]
Q9. Solve: \( \frac{y}{2} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{y}{3} \)
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Group variable terms on one side and constant terms on the other.
Transpose \( \frac{y}{3} \) to LHS and \( -\frac{3}{4} \) to RHS:
\[ \frac{y}{2} - \frac{y}{3} = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{3}{4} \] [1 mark]
Step 2: Take LCM on both sides.
LCM of 2 and 3 (LHS) is 6. Denominators on RHS are already identical (4).
\[ \frac{3y - 2y}{6} = \frac{5 + 3}{4} \]
\[ \frac{y}{6} = \frac{8}{4} \] [1 mark]
Step 3: Simplify and solve.
\[ \frac{y}{6} = 2 \] \[ y = 2 \times 6 \]
\( y = 12 \)
[1 mark]
Q10. Age word problem: Sum of ages is 135 years. Find ages of Rahul, Father, and Grandfather.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Let Rahul's present age be \( x \) years.
Rahul's father is 29 years older than Rahul.
Father's age = \( x + 29 \).
Father is 26 years younger than grandfather, which means grandfather is 26 years older than the father.
Grandfather's age = \( (x + 29) + 26 = x + 55 \). [1 mark]
Equation: Sum of ages = 135.
\[ x + (x + 29) + (x + 55) = 135 \]
\[ 3x + 84 = 135 \]
\[ 3x = 135 - 84 \]
\[ 3x = 51 \implies x = \frac{51}{3} = 17 \] [1 mark]
Calculating ages:
  • Rahul = 17 years
  • Father = 17 + 29 = 46 years
  • Grandfather = 17 + 55 = 72 years
[1 mark]

Q11. From the sum of \( 2x^2 + 3xy - 5 \) and \( -4x^2 + 2xy + 7 \), subtract the sum of \( 3x^2 - xy + 2 \) and \( -x^2 - 4xy - 3 \).
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part 1: Find the first sum. Let this be \( S_1 \).
\[ S_1 = (2x^2 + 3xy - 5) + (-4x^2 + 2xy + 7) \] \[ S_1 = (2 - 4)x^2 + (3 + 2)xy + (-5 + 7) \] \[ S_1 = \mathbf{-2x^2 + 5xy + 2} \] [1.5 marks]
Part 2: Find the second sum. Let this be \( S_2 \).
\[ S_2 = (3x^2 - xy + 2) + (-x^2 - 4xy - 3) \] \[ S_2 = (3 - 1)x^2 + (-1 - 4)xy + (2 - 3) \] \[ S_2 = \mathbf{2x^2 - 5xy - 1} \] [1.5 marks]
Part 3: Subtract \( S_2 \) from \( S_1 \).
\[ \text{Result} = S_1 - S_2 \] \[ = (-2x^2 + 5xy + 2) - (2x^2 - 5xy - 1) \] [1 mark]
Open brackets and change signs of \( S_2 \):
\[ = -2x^2 + 5xy + 2 - 2x^2 + 5xy + 1 \] \[ = (-2 - 2)x^2 + (5 + 5)xy + (2 + 1) \]
Final Answer = \( -4x^2 + 10xy + 3 \)
[1 mark]
Q12. A rational number is such that when you multiply it by \( \frac{5}{2} \) and add \( \frac{2}{3} \), you get \( -\frac{7}{12} \). Calculate the number.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Let the unknown rational number be \( x \).
According to the problem statement:
  • Multiply by \( \frac{5}{2} \rightarrow x \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{5x}{2} \)
  • Add \( \frac{2}{3} \rightarrow \frac{5x}{2} + \frac{2}{3} \)
Equation formulated:
\[ \frac{5x}{2} + \frac{2}{3} = -\frac{7}{12} \] [2 marks]
Solving: Transpose \( \frac{2}{3} \) to RHS.
\[ \frac{5x}{2} = -\frac{7}{12} - \frac{2}{3} \]
Take LCM of denominators on RHS (LCM of 12 and 3 is 12).
\[ \frac{5x}{2} = \frac{-7 - (2 \times 4)}{12} \] \[ \frac{5x}{2} = \frac{-7 - 8}{12} \] \[ \frac{5x}{2} = \frac{-15}{12} \] [1.5 marks]
Simplify RHS: \( \frac{-15}{12} \) reduces to \( -\frac{5}{4} \).
\[ \frac{5x}{2} = -\frac{5}{4} \]
Isolate \( x \) by cross multiplying:
\[ x = -\frac{5}{4} \times \frac{2}{5} \] \[ x = -\frac{10}{20} = -\frac{1}{2} \]
The rational number is \( -\frac{1}{2} \)
[1.5 marks]
Q13. Perimeter of a rectangular pool is 154m. Length is 2m more than twice its breadth. Find exact dimensions.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Expressions: Let the breadth of the pool be \( b \) meters.
"Length is 2m more than twice its breadth" means:
Length (\( l \)) = \( 2b + 2 \) [1 mark]
(b) Setting up the Equation:
Perimeter of a rectangle = \( 2 \times (\text{Length} + \text{Breadth}) \)
\[ 2(l + b) = 154 \]
Substitute \( l = 2b + 2 \):
\[ 2((2b + 2) + b) = 154 \] [1 mark]
\[ 2(3b + 2) = 154 \]
(c) Solving for Length and Breadth:
Divide both sides by 2:
\[ 3b + 2 = \frac{154}{2} \] \[ 3b + 2 = 77 \]
Subtract 2 from both sides:
\[ 3b = 77 - 2 \] \[ 3b = 75 \] \[ b = \frac{75}{3} \implies \mathbf{b = 25} \] [1.5 marks]
Now find the Length:
\[ l = 2b + 2 \] \[ l = 2(25) + 2 \] \[ l = 50 + 2 = \mathbf{52} \]
Breadth = 25m, Length = 52m
[1.5 marks]
