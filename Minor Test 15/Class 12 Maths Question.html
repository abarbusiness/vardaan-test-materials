MINOR TEST 15 - VECTOR ALGEBRA
Class: 12 (CBSE) Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Use standard vector notations explicitly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the unit vector in the direction of the vector $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}$.
2.
Find the projection of the vector $\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 7\hat{k}$ on the vector $7\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 8\hat{k}$.
3.
Find the angle between the vectors $\vec{a} = \hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}$.
4.
Write two different non-zero vectors having the same direction.
5.
For what value of $\lambda$ are the vectors $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} + \lambda\hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$ perpendicular to each other?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find a vector of magnitude $5$ units perpendicular to each of the vectors $(\vec{a} + \vec{b})$ and $(\vec{a} - \vec{b})$, where $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$.
7.
If $\vec{a}, \vec{b}, \vec{c}$ are unit vectors such that $\vec{a} + \vec{b} + \vec{c} = \vec{0}$, find the value of the expression: $$ \vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} + \vec{b}\cdot\vec{c} + \vec{c}\cdot\vec{a} $$
8.
Find the area of a parallelogram whose adjacent sides are determined by the vectors $\vec{a} = \hat{i} - \hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = 2\hat{i} - 7\hat{j} + \hat{k}$.
9.
Let $\vec{a}, \vec{b}$ and $\vec{c}$ be three vectors such that $|\vec{a}| = 3$, $|\vec{b}| = 4$, and $|\vec{c}| = 5$. If each vector is perpendicular to the sum of the other two, find the magnitude of the vector $|\vec{a} + \vec{b} + \vec{c}|$.
10.
Find the direction cosines of the vector joining the points $A(1, 2, -3)$ and $B(-1, -2, 1)$, directed from $A$ to $B$.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Let $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$, $\vec{b} = 3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 7\hat{k}$ and $\vec{c} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$. Find a vector $\vec{d}$ which is perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$, and satisfies the condition $\vec{c}\cdot\vec{d} = 15$.
12.
Prove by vector method that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.
13.
Use vectors to find the area of the triangle with vertices $A(1, 1, 2)$, $B(2, 3, 5)$ and $C(1, 5, 5)$.
