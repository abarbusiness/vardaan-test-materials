Minor Test 15 - Coordinate Geometry & Circles
Class: 9 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working steps clearly. Missing steps may result in deduction of marks.
  • Use of calculator is not permitted. Keep diagrams neat and labeled.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
In which quadrant or on which axis do the points \( A(-3, 5) \) and \( B(0, -4) \) lie?
2.
Write the coordinates of a point which lies on the x-axis and is at a distance of 7 units in the negative direction of the x-axis. Also, state its perpendicular distance from the y-axis.
3.
If the length of a chord of a circle is 16 cm and it is at a perpendicular distance of 6 cm from the centre of the circle, then find the radius of the circle.
4.
In a cyclic quadrilateral \( ABCD \), if \( \angle A = 70^\circ \) and \( \angle B = 100^\circ \), find the measure of \( \angle C \) and \( \angle D \).
5.
Find the perpendicular distance of the point \( P(4, -5) \) from the x-axis and the y-axis respectively.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Plot the points \( P(-2, 3) \), \( Q(4, 3) \), and \( R(4, -2) \) on a Cartesian plane. Find the coordinates of point \( S \) such that \( PQRS \) forms a rectangle.
7.
A chord of a circle is equal to the radius of the circle. Find the angle subtended by the chord at a point on the minor arc and also at a point on the major arc.
8.
In the given figure, A, B and C are three points on a circle with centre O such that \( \angle BOC = 30^\circ \) and \( \angle AOB = 60^\circ \). If D is a point on the circle other than the arc ABC, find \( \angle ADC \).
9.
Prove that if chords of congruent circles subtend equal angles at their centres, then the chords are equal in length.
10.
\( ABCD \) is a cyclic quadrilateral whose diagonals intersect at a point E. If \( \angle DBC = 70^\circ \), \( \angle BAC = 30^\circ \), find \( \angle BCD \). Further, if \( AB = BC \), find \( \angle ECD \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Plot the points \( A(2, 0) \), \( B(2, 2) \), \( C(0, 2) \) and the origin \( O(0,0) \). Draw the line segments OA, AB, BC and CO.
(i) What geometrical figure do you obtain?
(ii) Find the area of the figure obtained.
(iii) Write the coordinates of the point of intersection of its diagonals.
12.
(a) Prove that the angle subtended by an arc at the centre is double the angle subtended by it at any remaining part of the circle.
(b) Using the above theorem, find the value of \( x \) if the angle at the centre is \( 130^\circ \).
13.
Two parallel chords of a circle, whose diameter is 13 cm, are respectively 5 cm and 12 cm in length. Find the distance between them if they lie on opposite sides of the centre of the circle.
