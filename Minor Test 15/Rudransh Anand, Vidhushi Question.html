MIT 15
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working clearly where necessary.
  • Use of calculators is not permitted.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the ratio of \( 5 \text{ km} \) to \( 500 \text{ m} \) in its simplest form.
2.
Are the ratios \( 1:2 \) and \( 2:3 \) equivalent? Justify your answer with a mathematical comparison.
3.
If the cost of \( 7 \) meters of a certain cloth is \( \text{₹ } 294 \), find the cost of \( 5 \) meters of the same cloth.
4.
\( 6 \) pipes are required to fill a tank in \( 1 \text{ hour and } 20 \text{ minutes} \). How long will it take if only \( 5 \) pipes of the same type are used?
5.
The scale of a map is given as \( 1 : 30,000,000 \). Two cities are \( 4 \text{ cm} \) apart on the map. Find the actual distance between them in kilometers.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A loaded truck travels \( 14 \text{ km} \) in \( 25 \text{ minutes} \). If the speed remains the same, how far can it travel in \( 5 \text{ hours} \)?
7.
If \( 15 \) workers can build a wall in \( 48 \text{ hours} \), how many workers will be required to do the same work in \( 30 \text{ hours} \)? Assume each worker works at the same rate.
8.
In a school, there are \( 8 \) periods a day, each of \( 45 \text{ minutes} \) duration. How long would each period be, if the school decides to have \( 9 \) periods a day, assuming the total school hours remain the same?
9.
A photograph of a bacteria enlarged \( 50,000 \) times attains a length of \( 5 \text{ cm} \).
(i) What is the actual length of the bacteria?
(ii) If the photograph is enlarged \( 20,000 \) times only, what would be its enlarged length?
10.
Suppose \( 2 \text{ kg} \) of sugar contains \( 9 \times 10^6 \) crystals. How many sugar crystals are there in:
(a) \( 5 \text{ kg} \) of sugar?
(b) \( 1.2 \text{ kg} \) of sugar?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A military garrison consisting of \( 1200 \) men is provisioned with food meant to last for \( 60 \text{ days} \). After \( 15 \text{ days} \), a reinforcement of men was expected, but instead, \( 300 \) men leave the garrison.

Find exactly how many more days the remaining food will last for the remaining men. State the type of proportion used.
12.
A train is moving at a uniform speed of \( 75 \text{ km/hr} \). Using the concepts of direct and inverse proportion, calculate the following:
(a) How far will it travel in \( 20 \text{ minutes} \)?
(b) Find the time required to cover a distance of \( 250 \text{ km} \). Express your answer in hours and minutes.
13.
Two persons could fit new windows in a house in \( 3 \text{ days} \).
(a) One of the persons fell ill before the work started. How long would the job take now?
(b) How many persons would be needed to fit all the windows in exactly one day?
(c) Justify why the number of persons and the number of days taken to complete the job are in inverse proportion.
