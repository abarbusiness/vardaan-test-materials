General Instructions for Practice:
  • This practice sheet comprises 10 high-yield questions covering three core NCERT chapters: Life Processes, How do Organisms Reproduce?, and Control & Coordination.
  • All questions are designed to evaluate deep conceptual understanding and key board exam terminology.
  • Complete all 10 questions within the recommended 45-minute duration to build exam speed and precision.
Q1.
[Chapter: Life Processes]
Write the balanced chemical equation representing the process of Photosynthesis. State two main events that occur in the chloroplast during this autotrophic nutrition process, and explain how stomata regulate gaseous exchange in leaves.
Q2.
[Chapter: Life Processes]
Why is the human circulatory system referred to as a Double Circulation System? Trace the pathway of blood from the lungs to the body tissues and back, and explain why separation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood is essential in mammals.
Q3.
[Chapter: Control & Coordination]
What is a Reflex Arc? Trace the sequential path of a nerve impulse when your hand accidentally touches a hot object. Why are reflex actions governed by the spinal cord rather than the brain?
Q4.
[Chapter: Control & Coordination]
Explain the mechanism of Phototropism in plants with special reference to the hormone Auxin. How does auxin concentration on the shaded side cause a plant shoot to bend toward sunlight?
Q5.
[Chapter: How do Organisms Reproduce?]
Differentiate between the binary fission observed in Amoeba and that in Leishmania. Why is variation beneficial to a species over time even if it is not advantageous to an individual organism?
Q6.
[Chapter: How do Organisms Reproduce?]
State the anatomical reason why human testes are located outside the abdominal cavity within the scrotum. Mention two key functions performed by the hormone Testosterone in human males.
Q7.
[Chapter: Life Processes]
Schematically illustrate the three different pathways of Glucose Breakdown in living organisms (in the presence of oxygen, lack of oxygen, and absence of oxygen). Mention the end-products and site of reaction in each case.
Q8.
[Chapter: Control & Coordination]
Describe the structure of a Neuron. How does an electrical impulse travel along a neuron, and how is this signal transmitted chemically across a Synapse to the next neuron?
Q9.
[Chapter: How do Organisms Reproduce?]
Describe the process of Double Fertilization in flowering plants (angiosperms). What post-fertilization changes occur in (a) the Ovule, and (b) the Ovary?
Q10.
[Chapter: Life Processes]
Name the structural and functional unit of the human kidney. Explain the mechanism of Urine Formation involving Filtration, Selective Reabsorption, and Secretion in a Nephron.
