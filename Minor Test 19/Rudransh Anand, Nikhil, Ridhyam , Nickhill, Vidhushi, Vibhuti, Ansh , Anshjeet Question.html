SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer / Competency Based - 5 Marks Each)
Q16.
(Competency - Mensuration in Real Life)
A cylindrical water tank has a radius of 1.4 m and a height of 5 m.
(i) Find the Curved Surface Area (CSA) of the tank.
(ii) Find the Total Surface Area (TSA) of the tank (Assume it is closed at the top and bottom).
(iii) If the cost of painting the outside curved surface is ₹ 20 per sq.m, find the total cost of painting.
(iv) Calculate the volume of the tank in liters. (Given: 1 \(m^3\) = 1000 Liters, use \(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\)).
Q17.
(Real-life Application - Commercial Mathematics)
Ramesh borrows ₹ 25,000 from a bank to start a small business. The bank charges interest at the rate of 8% per annum compounded annually for a period of 2 years.
(i) Write down the formula for calculating the amount under compound interest.
(ii) Calculate the total amount Ramesh has to pay back at the end of 2 years.
(iii) Find the total compound interest paid by Ramesh over these 2 years.
Q18.
(Integration - Geometry and Algebra)

(i) State the geometrical property regarding the adjacent angles of a parallelogram.
(ii) Form an equation and solve it to find the value of \(x\).
(iii) Find the exact measures of \( \angle A \) and \( \angle B \).
(iv) Hence, write down the measures of \( \angle C \) and \( \angle D \).
