Minor Test 19 - General Mathematics
Class: 6 & 7 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A comprises 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B comprises 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C comprises 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Show all essential steps of working. Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following expression involving integers: \( -15 + 8 - (-12) \)
2.
Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of the numbers 12 and 18.
3.
Express the fraction \( \frac{3}{4} \) as a percentage and as a decimal.
4.
Simplify the algebraic expression: \( 3x + 5y - 2x + y \) and find its value when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = 3 \).
5.
Find the perimeter of a regular hexagon with a side length of \( 5 \text{ cm} \).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A car travels a distance of \( 220 \text{ km} \) in \( 4 \text{ hours} \). Using the unitary method, find how far it will travel in \( 7 \text{ hours} \) at the same uniform speed.
7.
Solve the following linear equation for \( m \):
\( 3(m - 2) + 5 = 14 \)
8.
A rectangular garden is \( 25 \text{ m} \) long and \( 16 \text{ m} \) wide. Find the area of the garden. Also, find the cost of fencing it at the rate of ₹ 15 per metre.
9.
Two angles of a triangle are \( 45^\circ \) and \( 60^\circ \). Find the measure of the third angle. Based on the measure of its angles, what type of triangle is this?
10.
Divide ₹ 1500 between two brothers, A and B, in the ratio 3 : 2. How much does each brother receive?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Simplify the following using BODMAS/fraction rules: \( \left( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} \right) \div \frac{3}{2} \) (2 Marks)
(b) A student read \( \frac{1}{4} \) of a book on Monday and \( \frac{3}{8} \) of it on Tuesday. What fraction of the book is still left to be read? (3 Marks)
12.
(a) Construct an algebraic equation for the following statement and solve it: "Five times a number decreased by 7 gives 23." (3 Marks)
(b) Write the number 108 as a product of its prime factors in exponential form. (2 Marks)
13.
A rectangular floor of length \( 8 \text{ m} \) and breadth \( 6 \text{ m} \) is to be completely covered with square tiles of side \( 0.5 \text{ m} \).
(a) Find the total area of the floor in square metres. (1 Mark)
(b) Find the area of one square tile. (2 Marks)
(c) Calculate the total number of tiles required to cover the entire floor. (2 Marks)
