DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Minor Test 19 (Class 8 General Maths)
SECTION A - Objective Type
Q1. What is the multiplicative inverse of \( \frac{-3}{4} \)?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) \( \frac{-4}{3} \)
Explanation:
The multiplicative inverse (or reciprocal) of a rational number \( \frac{a}{b} \) is \( \frac{b}{a} \). Therefore, the multiplicative inverse of \( \frac{-3}{4} \) is \( \frac{4}{-3} \), which is written in standard form as \( \frac{-4}{3} \).
Q2. The value of \( (2^{-1} \times 4^{-1}) \div 2^{-2} \) is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) \( \frac{1}{2} \)
Explanation:
Using the law of exponents \( a^{-m} = \frac{1}{a^m} \):
\( (2^{-1} \times 4^{-1}) \div 2^{-2} = \left(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{4}\right) \div \frac{1}{2^2} \)
\( = \frac{1}{8} \div \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{8} \times 4 = \frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2} \).
Q3. What is the square root of 12.25?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (a) 3.5
Explanation:
Ignore the decimal point temporarily to find the square root of 1225. We know \( 35 \times 35 = 1225 \). Since there are two decimal places in 12.25, the square root will have one decimal place. Thus, \( \sqrt{12.25} = 3.5 \).
Q4. If \( 2x - 3 = 7 \), then what is the value of \( x \)?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 5
Explanation:
Solve the linear equation:
\( 2x - 3 = 7 \)
\( 2x = 7 + 3 \)
\( 2x = 10 \)
\( x = \frac{10}{2} = 5 \).
Q5. The sum of the exterior angles of any regular polygon is always:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 360°
Explanation:
By the property of polygons, the sum of all the exterior angles (taking one at each vertex) of any convex polygon is exactly 360°.
Q6. Which of the following is a perfect cube?
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 343
Explanation:
Let's verify: \( 7 \times 7 \times 7 = 343 \). Therefore, 343 is a perfect cube. (Note: 243 is \( 3^5 \), 441 is \( 21^2 \), 100 is \( 10^2 \)).
Q7. The standard form of 0.000064 is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) \( 6.4 \times 10^{-5} \)
Explanation:
To write in standard form (scientific notation), move the decimal point 5 places to the right to get a number between 1 and 10.
This gives 6.4. Since we moved the decimal right, the exponent is negative 5. Result: \( 6.4 \times 10^{-5} \).
Q8. 25% of 120 is equal to:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) 30
Explanation:
\( 25\% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4} \).
\( \frac{1}{4} \text{ of } 120 = \frac{120}{4} = 30 \).
Q9. The volume of a cube with side length 4 cm is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) 64 cm³
Explanation:
Volume of a cube = \( \text{side}^3 \).
\( V = 4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64 \text{ cm}^3 \).
Q10. The degree of the polynomial \( 4x^3 - 5x^2 + 2 \) is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) 3
Explanation:
The degree of a polynomial in one variable is the highest power of the variable present in the polynomial. In \( 4x^3 - 5x^2 + 2 \), the highest power of \(x\) is 3.
SECTION B - Short Answer Type
Q11. Find any three rational numbers between \( \frac{1}{4} \) and \( \frac{1}{2} \).
[3 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
First, find a common denominator for the two fractions. Let's use 8.
\( \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{8} \) and \( \frac{1}{2} = \frac{4}{8} \). [1 Mark for common denominator setup]

Since we need three rational numbers, there is only one integer between numerators 2 and 4. We need to expand the gap by multiplying numerator and denominator by a larger number, say 4.
\( \frac{2 \times 4}{8 \times 4} = \frac{8}{32} \)
\( \frac{4 \times 4}{8 \times 4} = \frac{16}{32} \) [1 Mark for expanding]

Now we can easily pick three rational numbers between \( \frac{8}{32} \) and \( \frac{16}{32} \):
\( \frac{9}{32}, \frac{10}{32}, \frac{11}{32} \) [1 Mark for correct final values. Note: Other valid fractions are acceptable]
Q12. Simplify: \( (2x + 5)(4x - 3) \)
[3 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
We use the distributive property (FOIL method) to multiply the binomials.
\( (2x + 5)(4x - 3) \)
\( = 2x(4x - 3) + 5(4x - 3) \) [1 Mark for distributive setup]
\( = (2x \cdot 4x) - (2x \cdot 3) + (5 \cdot 4x) - (5 \cdot 3) \)
\( = 8x^2 - 6x + 20x - 15 \) [1 Mark for correct multiplication]

Combine like terms (\(-6x\) and \(+20x\)):
\( = 8x^2 + 14x - 15 \) [1 Mark for final simplified expression]
Q13. The sum of three consecutive multiples of 8 is 888. Find these multiples.
[3 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
Let the three consecutive multiples of 8 be \( 8x \), \( 8(x+1) \), and \( 8(x+2) \).
Alternatively, let the multiples be \( x, x+8, x+16 \). Let's use the latter for simplicity.
Equation: \( x + (x + 8) + (x + 16) = 888 \) [1 Mark for forming equation]

Solving for \( x \):
\( 3x + 24 = 888 \)
\( 3x = 888 - 24 \)
\( 3x = 864 \) [1 Mark for simplifying to 3x = 864]
\( x = \frac{864}{3} = 288 \).

The multiples are:
1st multiple = 288
2nd multiple = 288 + 8 = 296
3rd multiple = 296 + 8 = 304
The multiples are 288, 296, and 304. [1 Mark for calculating all three multiples]
Q14. Gadget sold for ₹ 540, profit of 20%. Calculate Cost Price (CP).
[3 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
Selling Price (SP) = ₹ 540
Profit % = 20%
Let the Cost Price be \( x \).
We know that, \( \text{SP} = \text{CP} + \text{Profit} \).
Profit = 20% of CP = \( 0.20x \) [1 Mark for conceptual setup/formula]

So, \( x + 0.20x = 540 \)
\( 1.20x = 540 \) [1 Mark for equation formation]
\( x = \frac{540}{1.20} \)
\( x = \frac{54000}{120} = 450 \)
The Cost Price of the gadget is ₹ 450. [1 Mark for final correct value]
Q15. Simplify: \( \displaystyle \frac{3^{-5} \times 10^{-5} \times 125}{5^{-7} \times 6^{-5}} \)
[3 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
First, prime factorize the composite numbers:
\( 10 = 2 \times 5 \), \( 125 = 5^3 \), \( 6 = 2 \times 3 \).
Substitute these into the expression: [1 Mark for prime factorization]
\( = \frac{3^{-5} \times (2 \times 5)^{-5} \times 5^3}{5^{-7} \times (2 \times 3)^{-5}} \)

Apply the law \( (ab)^m = a^m b^m \):
\( = \frac{3^{-5} \times 2^{-5} \times 5^{-5} \times 5^3}{5^{-7} \times 2^{-5} \times 3^{-5}} \) [1 Mark for expanding]

Group the terms with the same base and apply the laws of exponents (\( \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n} \) and \( a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n} \)):
\( = 2^{-5 - (-5)} \times 3^{-5 - (-5)} \times 5^{-5 + 3 - (-7)} \)
\( = 2^0 \times 3^0 \times 5^{-2 + 7} \)
\( = 1 \times 1 \times 5^5 \)

\( = 5^5 \text{ (or } 3125) \) [1 Mark for correct final answer]
SECTION C - Long Answer / Competency Based
Q16. Cylindrical tank: r = 1.4 m, h = 5 m. Find CSA, TSA, Cost of painting outside curved surface @ ₹ 20/m², Volume in Liters.
[5 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
Given: Radius (\(r\)) = 1.4 m, Height (\(h\)) = 5 m, \(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\).

(i) Curved Surface Area (CSA):
\( \text{CSA} = 2\pi rh \)
\( = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 5 \)
\( = 2 \times 22 \times 0.2 \times 5 = 44 \text{ m}^2 \) [1.5 Marks]

(ii) Total Surface Area (TSA):
\( \text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r + h) \)
\( = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times (1.4 + 5) \)
\( = 44 \times 0.2 \times 6.4 \)
\( = 8.8 \times 6.4 = 56.32 \text{ m}^2 \) [1.5 Marks]

(iii) Cost of painting curved surface:
Cost = CSA \( \times \) Rate
\( \text{Cost} = 44 \times 20 = \text{₹ } 880 \) [1 Mark]

(iv) Volume in liters:
\( \text{Volume (m}^3) = \pi r^2 h = \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 1.4 \times 5 \)
\( = 22 \times 0.2 \times 1.4 \times 5 = 30.8 \text{ m}^3 \).
Since 1 m³ = 1000 Liters, Volume = \( 30.8 \times 1000 = 30,800 \) Liters. [1 Mark]
Q17. Ramesh borrows ₹ 25,000 at 8% p.a. compounded annually for 2 years. Formula, Amount after 2 yrs, Total Compound Interest.
[5 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
Principal (\(P\)) = ₹ 25,000
Rate (\(R\)) = 8% per annum
Time (\(n\)) = 2 years

(i) Formula for Amount (Compound Interest):
\( A = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n \) [1 Mark]

(ii) Total Amount after 2 years:
\( A = 25000 \left(1 + \frac{8}{100}\right)^2 \)
\( A = 25000 \left(\frac{108}{100}\right)^2 \)
\( A = 25000 \times \frac{108}{100} \times \frac{108}{100} \) [1.5 Marks for substitution]

Simplifying zeroes:
\( A = 2.5 \times 108 \times 108 \)
\( A = 2.5 \times 11664 = \text{₹ } 29,160 \) [1.5 Marks for correct calculation of Amount]

(iii) Total Compound Interest (CI):
\( \text{CI} = \text{Amount} (A) - \text{Principal} (P) \)
\( \text{CI} = 29160 - 25000 = \text{₹ } 4,160 \) [1 Mark]
Q18. Parallelogram ABCD: \(\angle A = (3x + 15)^\circ\), \(\angle B = (5x - 35)^\circ\). Find property, x, \(\angle A, \angle B, \angle C, \angle D\).
[5 Marks]
Step-by-step Solution:
(i) Geometrical property:
In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are supplementary (their sum is 180°). [1 Mark]

(ii) Form an equation and solve for x:
\( \angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ \)
\( (3x + 15) + (5x - 35) = 180 \) [1 Mark]
\( 8x - 20 = 180 \)
\( 8x = 200 \)
\( x = \frac{200}{8} = 25 \) [1 Mark]

(iii) Exact measures of \(\angle A\) and \(\angle B\):
\( \angle A = 3(25) + 15 = 75 + 15 = 90^\circ \) [0.5 Mark]
\( \angle B = 5(25) - 35 = 125 - 35 = 90^\circ \) [0.5 Mark]
(Note: Since adjacent angles are 90°, this parallelogram is actually a rectangle.)

(iv) Measures of \(\angle C\) and \(\angle D\):
In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal.
Therefore, \( \angle C = \angle A = 90^\circ \) [0.5 Mark]
And \( \angle D = \angle B = 90^\circ \) [0.5 Mark]
