SECTION A

Q1. Evaluate the following expression involving integers: \( -15 + 8 - (-12) \)
[2 Marks]
Given expression: \( -15 + 8 - (-12) \)
First, resolve the double negative sign. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding a positive.
$$ = -15 + 8 + 12 $$
(1 Mark for sign correction)
Next, add the positive numbers together: \( 8 + 12 = 20 \)
$$ = -15 + 20 $$ $$ = 5 $$
Final Answer: 5 (1 Mark)
Q2. Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of the numbers 12 and 18.
[2 Marks]
Using prime factorization method:
Factors of 12 = \( 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^1 \) (0.5 Marks)
Factors of 18 = \( 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^1 \times 3^2 \) (0.5 Marks)
To find LCM, take the highest power of each prime factor present:
$$ \text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36 $$
Final Answer: The LCM of 12 and 18 is 36. (1 Mark)
Q3. Express the fraction \( \frac{3}{4} \) as a percentage and as a decimal.
[2 Marks]
To Percentage: Multiply the fraction by 100%.
$$ \frac{3}{4} \times 100\% = 3 \times 25\% = 75\% $$
(1 Mark for Percentage)
To Decimal: Convert the percentage to decimal or divide 3 by 4.
$$ \frac{75}{100} = 0.75 $$
Final Answer: 75% and 0.75 (1 Mark for Decimal)
Q4. Simplify the algebraic expression: \( 3x + 5y - 2x + y \) and find its value when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = 3 \).
[2 Marks]
Combine like terms to simplify the expression:
$$ (3x - 2x) + (5y + y) = x + 6y $$
(1 Mark for Simplification)
Substitute \( x = 2 \) and \( y = 3 \) into the simplified expression:
$$ (2) + 6(3) $$ $$ = 2 + 18 = 20 $$
Final Answer: Simplified: \( x + 6y \), Value: 20 (1 Mark for Evaluation)
Q5. Find the perimeter of a regular hexagon with a side length of \( 5 \text{ cm} \).
[2 Marks]
A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides. (1 Mark for recognizing properties)
Perimeter = \( 6 \times \text{side length} \)
$$ \text{Perimeter} = 6 \times 5 \text{ cm} = 30 \text{ cm} $$
Final Answer: 30 cm (1 Mark)

SECTION B

Q6. A car travels a distance of \( 220 \text{ km} \) in \( 4 \text{ hours} \). Using the unitary method, find how far it will travel in \( 7 \text{ hours} \) at the same uniform speed.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Find the distance traveled in 1 hour (Unit value).
$$ \text{Distance in 1 hr} = \frac{220}{4} = 55 \text{ km/hr} $$
(1.5 Marks for finding unit rate)
Step 2: Multiply unit rate by the desired time (7 hours).
$$ \text{Distance in 7 hrs} = 55 \times 7 $$ $$ = 385 \text{ km} $$
Final Answer: The car will travel 385 km. (1.5 Marks)
Q7. Solve the following linear equation for \( m \): \( 3(m - 2) + 5 = 14 \)
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Use the distributive property to expand the brackets.
$$ 3m - 6 + 5 = 14 $$
(1 Mark for expansion)
Step 2: Combine the constant terms on the left side.
$$ 3m - 1 = 14 $$
Step 3: Add 1 to both sides to isolate the variable term.
$$ 3m = 14 + 1 $$ $$ 3m = 15 $$
(1 Mark for simplifying equation)
Step 4: Divide by 3.
$$ m = \frac{15}{3} = 5 $$
Final Answer: \( m = 5 \) (1 Mark)
Q8. A rectangular garden is \( 25 \text{ m} \) long and \( 16 \text{ m} \) wide. Find the area of the garden. Also, find the cost of fencing it at the rate of ₹ 15 per metre.
[3 Marks]
Area of Rectangle: Length × Breadth
$$ \text{Area} = 25 \text{ m} \times 16 \text{ m} = 400 \text{ m}^2 $$
(1 Mark for Area)
Perimeter for fencing: \( 2 \times (\text{Length} + \text{Breadth}) \)
$$ \text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (25 + 16) = 2 \times 41 = 82 \text{ m} $$
(1 Mark for Perimeter)
Cost of fencing: Perimeter × Rate per metre
$$ \text{Cost} = 82 \times 15 = 1230 $$
Final Answer: Area = \( 400 \text{ m}^2 \), Cost = ₹ 1230 (1 Mark for Final Cost)
Q9. Two angles of a triangle are \( 45^\circ \) and \( 60^\circ \). Find the measure of the third angle. Based on the measure of its angles, what type of triangle is this?
[3 Marks]
By the angle sum property of a triangle, the sum of all three interior angles is \( 180^\circ \).
Let the third angle be \( x \).
$$ 45^\circ + 60^\circ + x = 180^\circ $$ $$ 105^\circ + x = 180^\circ $$ $$ x = 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ $$
(2 Marks for finding third angle)
Since all three angles (\( 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 75^\circ \)) are less than \( 90^\circ \), it is an acute-angled triangle.
Final Answer: Third angle = \( 75^\circ \). Type: Acute-angled triangle. (1 Mark for identification)
Q10. Divide ₹ 1500 between two brothers, A and B, in the ratio 3 : 2. How much does each brother receive?
[3 Marks]
Let the common ratio multiplier be \( x \). The shares are \( 3x \) and \( 2x \).
Total sum = \( 3x + 2x = 1500 \)
$$ 5x = 1500 $$ $$ x = \frac{1500}{5} = 300 $$
(1.5 Marks for finding x)
Share of A = \( 3x = 3 \times 300 = 900 \)
Share of B = \( 2x = 2 \times 300 = 600 \)
Final Answer: A gets ₹ 900 and B gets ₹ 600. (1.5 Marks for final shares)

SECTION C

Q11. (a) Simplify: \( \left( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} \right) \div \frac{3}{2} \) (b) Fraction of book left to read.
[5 Marks]
Part (a): First, solve the brackets (multiplication).
$$ \left( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} \right) = \frac{2 \times 9}{3 \times 4} = \frac{18}{12} $$
Simplify the fraction: \( \frac{18}{12} = \frac{3}{2} \)
Now perform the division by multiplying by the reciprocal:
$$ \frac{3}{2} \div \frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{6} = 1 $$
Answer (a): 1 (2 Marks)

Part (b): Fraction read on Monday = \( \frac{1}{4} \)
Fraction read on Tuesday = \( \frac{3}{8} \)
Total fraction read = \( \frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{8} \)
LCM of 4 and 8 is 8. Convert to common denominators:
$$ = \frac{2}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8} $$
(2 Marks for finding total read)
Fraction left to read = Total Book (1) - Fraction Read
$$ = 1 - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8} $$
Answer (b): \( \frac{3}{8} \) of the book is left. (1 Mark)
Q12. (a) Construct and solve algebraic equation. (b) Prime factors of 108 in exponential form.
[5 Marks]
Part (a): Let the unknown number be \( x \).
"Five times a number" = \( 5x \).
"Decreased by 7 gives 23" becomes:
$$ 5x - 7 = 23 $$
(1 Mark for Equation Formulation)
Solving for \( x \):
$$ 5x = 23 + 7 $$ $$ 5x = 30 $$ $$ x = \frac{30}{5} = 6 $$
Answer (a): The equation is \( 5x - 7 = 23 \) and the number is 6. (2 Marks for solving)

Part (b): Find prime factorization of 108 by dividing by smallest primes.
108 ÷ 2 = 54
54 ÷ 2 = 27
27 ÷ 3 = 9
9 ÷ 3 = 3
3 ÷ 3 = 1
The factors are \( 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \).
Answer (b): In exponential form: \( 2^2 \times 3^3 \). (2 Marks)
Q13. Tiling a rectangular floor of \( 8 \text{ m} \times 6 \text{ m} \) with square tiles of side \( 0.5 \text{ m} \).
[5 Marks]
(a) Area of the floor:
Area of rectangle = Length × Breadth
$$ \text{Area of Floor} = 8 \text{ m} \times 6 \text{ m} = 48 \text{ m}^2 $$
Answer (a): \( 48 \text{ m}^2 \) (1 Mark)

(b) Area of one square tile:
Area of square = Side × Side
$$ \text{Area of Tile} = 0.5 \text{ m} \times 0.5 \text{ m} = 0.25 \text{ m}^2 $$
Answer (b): \( 0.25 \text{ m}^2 \) (2 Marks)

(c) Total number of tiles required:
Number of tiles = (Total Area of Floor) / (Area of One Tile)
$$ \text{Number of tiles} = \frac{48}{0.25} $$
To remove the decimal, multiply numerator and denominator by 100:
$$ = \frac{4800}{25} = 192 $$
Answer (c): 192 tiles are required. (2 Marks)
