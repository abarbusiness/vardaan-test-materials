DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
1. The SI unit of amount of substance is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Mole
Explanation:
According to the International System of Units (SI), there are seven base physical quantities. The mole (symbol: mol) is the base unit for the amount of substance.
2. How many significant figures are there in the number \( 0.0025 \)?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) 2
Explanation:
Leading zeros (zeros to the left of the first non-zero digit) are not significant. They only indicate the position of the decimal point. Thus, only '2' and '5' are significant.
3. Which of the following has the highest number of significant figures?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 5.300
Explanation:
(A) 0.0053 has 2 sig figs. (B) 5.300 has 4 sig figs (trailing zeros after decimal are significant). (C) 530 has 2 (or 3, ambiguous) sig figs. (D) 5.3 has 2 sig figs.
4. The prefix "nano" corresponds to the multiplication factor:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) \( 10^{-9} \)
Explanation:
In the metric system, the prefix "nano" is used to denote a factor of \( 10^{-9} \). For example, 1 nanometer (nm) = \( 10^{-9} \) meters.
5. The Law of Conservation of Mass was proposed by:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) Antoine Lavoisier
Explanation:
Antoine Lavoisier (1789) established the Law of Conservation of Mass, which states that matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.
6. Which law states that a given chemical compound always contains its component elements in fixed ratio by mass?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Law of Definite Proportions
Explanation:
Joseph Proust proposed the Law of Definite Proportions, stating that a pure chemical compound always contains exactly the same proportion of elements by mass, regardless of its source.
7. The pair of compounds \(CO\) and \(CO_2\) illustrates the:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Law of Multiple Proportions
Explanation:
Carbon combines with Oxygen to form two compounds (CO and CO₂). The masses of oxygen (16g and 32g) that combine with a fixed mass of carbon (12g) bear a simple ratio of 1:2.
8. 1 amu (atomic mass unit) is exactly equal to:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 1/12th the mass of one C-12 atom
Explanation:
The atomic mass unit (amu or 'u') is officially defined as a mass equal to one-twelfth (\( \frac{1}{12} \)) of the mass of a single carbon-12 atom.
9. What is the molar mass of Water (\(H_2O\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 18 g/mol
Explanation:
Molar mass of \(H_2O\) = (2 × Atomic mass of H) + (1 × Atomic mass of O)
= \( (2 \times 1) + 16 = 18 \text{ g/mol} \).
10. Avogadro's Number (\(N_A\)) is equal to:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) \( 6.022 \times 10^{23} \)
Explanation:
Avogadro's number represents the number of fundamental particles (atoms, molecules, ions, etc.) in exactly 1 mole of a substance. Its value is \( 6.022 \times 10^{23} \).
11. The volume occupied by 1 mole of an ideal gas at STP is approximately:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) 22.4 L
Explanation:
At Standard Temperature and Pressure (STP, which is 0°C and 1 atm), 1 mole of any ideal gas occupies a volume of 22.4 Liters (or 22710 mL at 1 bar).
12. How many moles are present in 44 g of Carbon Dioxide (\(CO_2\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 1.0 mole
Explanation:
Molar mass of \(CO_2\) = \( 12 + (2 \times 16) = 44 \text{ g/mol} \).
Number of Moles = \( \frac{\text{Given Mass}}{\text{Molar Mass}} = \frac{44}{44} = 1.0 \text{ mole} \).
13. Which formula represents the simplest whole number ratio of atoms in a compound?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) Empirical Formula
Explanation:
The empirical formula gives the simplest, most reduced whole-number ratio of the atoms of elements present in a compound, whereas molecular formula gives the exact number.
14. What is the empirical formula of Glucose (\(C_6H_{12}O_6\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) \( CH_2O \)
Explanation:
The molecular formula is \( C_6H_{12}O_6 \). Dividing the subscripts by their greatest common divisor (6) gives the simplest ratio: 1:2:1. Thus, the empirical formula is \( CH_2O \).
15. Molarity is defined as the number of moles of solute dissolved per:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 1 Liter of solution
Explanation:
Molarity (M) is the most widely used unit of concentration. It is defined as the number of moles of solute dissolved in 1 Liter (1000 mL) of the entire solution.
16. Which of the following concentration terms is independent of temperature?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) Molality
Explanation:
Molality is moles of solute per kg of solvent. Since both moles and mass do not change with temperature changes, molality is temperature independent. Molarity depends on volume, which expands/contracts with temperature.
17. What is the mass of 0.5 moles of Nitrogen gas (\(N_2\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 14 g
Explanation:
Molar mass of \(N_2\) gas = \( 2 \times 14 = 28 \text{ g/mol} \).
Mass = Moles × Molar Mass = \( 0.5 \times 28 = 14 \text{ g} \).
18. The sum of the mole fractions of all components in a solution is always equal to:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 1
Explanation:
By definition, mole fraction (\(X\)) is a ratio of parts to the whole. For a mixture with components A, B, C..., \( X_A + X_B + X_C... = 1 \).
19. The reactant which is entirely consumed in a chemical reaction is called the:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Limiting Reagent
Explanation:
The limiting reagent determines the amount of product formed because it is completely consumed first, stopping the reaction.
20. Number of atoms present in 1 mole of an element is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) \( 6.022 \times 10^{23} \)
Explanation:
One mole of any elementary entity (atoms, molecules, ions, electrons) always contains Avogadro's number (\( 6.022 \times 10^{23} \)) of those entities.
21. What is the mass percentage of Hydrogen in Water (\(H_2O\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 11.11%
Explanation:
Molar mass of \(H_2O = 18 \text{ g/mol}\). Mass of H = 2 g.
Mass % of H = \( (\frac{2}{18}) \times 100 = 11.11\% \).
22. The unit of density is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) kg / m³
Explanation:
Density is Mass / Volume. The SI unit for mass is kg, and for volume is \(m^3\). Hence, density is \( \text{kg m}^{-3} \).
23. Scientific notation of 0.000045 is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) \( 4.5 \times 10^{-5} \)
Explanation:
Shift the decimal 5 places to the right to get a number between 1 and 10 (which is 4.5). Therefore, the exponent is -5.
24. Which of the following is a homogeneous mixture?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Sugar dissolved in water
Explanation:
A homogeneous mixture has a uniform composition throughout. Sugar completely dissolves in water to form a uniform true solution. Sand, oil, and smoke form non-uniform (heterogeneous) mixtures.
25. What is the molar mass of Sodium Hydroxide (\(NaOH\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) 40 g/mol
Explanation:
Molar mass = Atomic mass of Na + Atomic mass of O + Atomic mass of H
= \( 23 + 16 + 1 = 40 \text{ g/mol} \).
26. How many moles are in 3.6 g of \(H_2O\)?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 0.2
Explanation:
Moles = Mass / Molar Mass = \( 3.6 \text{ g} / 18 \text{ g/mol} = 0.2 \text{ moles} \).
27. The prefix "pico" corresponds to:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) \( 10^{-12} \)
Explanation:
In the metric system, "pico" (symbol: p) denotes a factor of one trillionth, which is \( 10^{-12} \).
28. Which element has the lowest atomic mass?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) Hydrogen
Explanation:
Hydrogen is the lightest element with an atomic mass of approximately 1.008 amu.
29. If the empirical formula is \(CH\) and the molecular mass is 26, the molecular formula is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) \( C_2H_2 \)
Explanation:
Empirical formula mass (CH) = 12 + 1 = 13.
\( n = \frac{\text{Molecular Mass}}{\text{Empirical Mass}} = \frac{26}{13} = 2 \).
Molecular formula = (CH)₂ = \( C_2H_2 \).
30. How many significant figures are in 100.0?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (D) 4
Explanation:
Trailing zeros in a number containing a decimal point are considered significant. Thus, 100.0 has four significant figures (1, 0, 0, 0).
31. What is the molarity of a solution containing 40 g of \(NaOH\) in 1 L of solution?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 1.0 M
Explanation:
Molar mass of NaOH = 40 g/mol.
Moles of NaOH = 40 / 40 = 1 mole.
Molarity = Moles / Volume (in L) = 1 / 1 = 1.0 M.
32. The volume of 0.5 moles of \(O_2\) gas at STP is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 11.2 L
Explanation:
1 mole of gas at STP occupies 22.4 L.
Therefore, 0.5 moles will occupy = \( 0.5 \times 22.4 = 11.2 \text{ L} \).
33. One mole of any substance contains:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) Avogadro's number of particles
Explanation:
A mole is defined as containing exactly \( 6.02214076 \times 10^{23} \) elementary entities (Avogadro's number).
34. Rounding off 3.14159 to three significant figures gives:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) 3.14
Explanation:
To round to 3 sig figs, look at the 4th digit (which is 1). Since 1 is less than 5, the preceding digit (4) remains unchanged. Result: 3.14.
35. The symbol for the SI unit of luminous intensity is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) cd
Explanation:
The base SI unit for luminous intensity is the candela, and its symbol is 'cd'.
36. Which law deals specifically with the volumes of reacting gases?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Gay Lussac's Law of Gaseous Volumes
Explanation:
Gay Lussac's Law states that when gases combine or are produced in a chemical reaction they do so in a simple ratio by volume, provided all gases are at the same temperature and pressure.
37. Mass percentage of Carbon in Methane (\(CH_4\)) is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) 75%
Explanation:
Molar mass of \(CH_4 = 12 + 4 = 16 \text{ g/mol}\).
Mass of Carbon = 12 g.
Mass % of C = \( (12 / 16) \times 100 = 75\% \).
38. Value of 1 amu in grams is approximately:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) \( 1.66 \times 10^{-24} \text{ g} \)
Explanation:
\( 1 \text{ amu} = \frac{1}{N_A} \text{ g} = \frac{1}{6.022 \times 10^{23}} = 1.66056 \times 10^{-24} \text{ g} \).
39. What is the molar mass of Sulphuric Acid (\(H_2SO_4\))?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 98 g/mol
Explanation:
Molar mass = \( (2 \times 1) + 32 + (4 \times 16) = 2 + 32 + 64 = 98 \text{ g/mol} \).
40. How many atoms of Hydrogen are in 1 mole of \(H_2O\)?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) \( 1.204 \times 10^{24} \)
Explanation:
1 molecule of \(H_2O\) has 2 H atoms.
1 mole of \(H_2O\) has \( 2 \times N_A \) atoms of H.
\( 2 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 1.2044 \times 10^{24} \) atoms.
41. If 1L of a 1.0 M solution is diluted to 2L, the new molarity is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 0.5 M
Explanation:
Using the dilution formula: \( M_1 V_1 = M_2 V_2 \)
\( 1.0 \times 1 = M_2 \times 2 \implies M_2 = 0.5 \text{ M} \).
42. The number of significant figures in \( 6.022 \times 10^{23} \) is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) 4
Explanation:
In scientific notation \( A \times 10^n \), only the digits in 'A' are significant. The number 6.022 has four significant figures.
43. Closeness of a measured value to the true value is known as:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Accuracy
Explanation:
Accuracy refers to the agreement of a particular value to the true (or accepted) value. Precision refers to the closeness of various measurements to each other.
44. How many base SI units are there?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) 7
Explanation:
The International System of Units (SI) is built upon seven fundamental base units: meter, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, and candela.
45. A pure substance made of two or more elements combined in a fixed ratio by mass is a:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Compound
Explanation:
By definition, a compound is a pure substance formed by chemically combining two or more different elements in a fixed, definite proportion by mass.
46. Which of the following has zero mass (approximately) compared to protons and neutrons?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (B) Electron
Explanation:
An electron's mass is about \( \frac{1}{1836} \) of a proton's mass (approx. \( 9.1 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg} \)), which is considered negligible (zero) when calculating mass numbers.
47. Amount of \(CO_2\) produced when 12g of Carbon burns completely in excess oxygen is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (D) 44 g
Explanation:
Reaction: \( C + O_2 \rightarrow CO_2 \).
1 mole of C (12g) yields 1 mole of \(CO_2\). Molar mass of \(CO_2\) is 44g. Therefore, 44g is produced.
48. The formula of calculating number of moles is:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (A) Mass / Molar Mass
Explanation:
The standard formula to find the number of moles from a given mass is \( n = \frac{m}{M} \), where 'm' is the given mass and 'M' is the molar mass.
49. What is the SI unit of Temperature?
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (C) Kelvin (K)
Explanation:
While Celsius is commonly used, the official SI base unit for thermodynamic temperature is the Kelvin (K).
50. According to Dalton's atomic theory, atoms of the same element are identical in:
[4 Marks]
Correct Answer: (D) Mass, size, and chemical properties
Explanation:
John Dalton postulated that all atoms of a given element are identical in all respects, including mass, size, and chemical properties (though later discoveries of isotopes showed variations in mass).
