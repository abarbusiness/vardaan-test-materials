Q36.
[4 Marks]
CASE STUDY 1: Classroom Seating Plan (Coordinate Geometry)
In a modern digital smart classroom, four friends $A, B, C,$ and $D$ are seated at specific grid positions for a group activity. The teacher tracks their coordinates using an interactive grid board calibrated in meters: $A(3, 4)$, $B(6, 7)$, $C(9, 4)$, and $D(6, 1)$.
(i) Find the distance between student $A$ and student $C$. [1 Mark]
(ii) Find the coordinates of the midpoint of the line segment joining student $B$ and student $D$. [1 Mark]
(iii) Check whether the quadrilateral $ABCD$ formed by these four seating positions is a square. Justify your answer using the distance formula. [2 Marks]
OR
Find the length of the diagonal $BD$ and determine the area enclosed by the quadrilateral $ABCD$. [2 Marks]
Q37.
[4 Marks]
CASE STUDY 2: Highway Bridge Arch Design (Polynomials)
An infrastructure company designs a parabolic arch bridge over a river. Mathematically, the profile of the arch is represented by the quadratic polynomial: $$p(x) = -x^2 + 2x + 8$$ where $x$ represents the horizontal distance in meters from the central pillar, and $p(x)$ represents the height of the arch above the water level.
(i) Find the zeroes of the polynomial $p(x)$ to determine where the arch touches the bridge platform level ($p(x) = 0$). [1 Mark]
(ii) Calculate the maximum height of the parabolic arch above the bridge deck level (which occurs at $x = 1\text{ m}$). [1 Mark]
(iii) Find a new quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are twice the zeroes of the original polynomial $p(x)$. [2 Marks]
Q38.
[4 Marks]
CASE STUDY 3: Drone Surveying & Coastline Monitoring (Applications of Trigonometry)
A high-precision surveillance drone is hovering stationary at a vertical height of $100\text{ m}$ above sea level. The drone camera tracks two cargo ships, $Ship\ A$ and $Ship\ B$, which are moving directly towards the observation tower along a straight line. The angles of depression of $Ship\ A$ and $Ship\ B$ as observed from the drone are $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$ respectively.
(i) Find the horizontal distance of $Ship\ B$ (closer ship) from the vertical line directly beneath the drone. [1 Mark]
(ii) Find the horizontal distance of $Ship\ A$ (farther ship) from the point directly beneath the drone. [1 Mark]
(iii) Calculate the exact distance between $Ship\ A$ and $Ship\ B$. Express your answer in simplified radical form and decimal form (Take $\sqrt{3} = 1.732$). [2 Marks]
