[3 Marks]
Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 48, or divide by common factors step-by-step.
Dividing numerator and denominator by 12 (since HCF is 12):
\[ \frac{36 \div 12}{48 \div 12} = \frac{3}{4} \]
Lowest term = 3/4
7. Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of 12 and 18.
[3 Marks]
Write the prime factorization of each number:
\( 12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3 \)
\( 18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2 \times 3^2 \)
The LCM is found by multiplying the highest powers of all prime factors present:
LCM = \( 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36 \)
LCM = 36
8. A boy bought a book for ₹120 and sold it for ₹150. Find his profit percentage.
[3 Marks]
Cost Price (CP) = ₹120
Selling Price (SP) = ₹150
Profit = SP - CP = \( 150 - 120 = 30 \)
Profit Percentage = \( (\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}}) \times 100 \)
\[ \text{Profit \%} = \left(\frac{30}{120}\right) \times 100 = \frac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25\% \]
Profit Percentage = 25%
9. Calculate the perimeter and area of a rectangle whose length is 8 cm and breadth is 5 cm.
[3 Marks]
Given: Length (\( l \)) = 8 cm, Breadth (\( b \)) = 5 cm
Perimeter = \( 2 \times (l + b) \)
Perimeter = \( 2 \times (8 + 5) = 2 \times 13 = 26 \text{ cm} \)
Area = \( l \times b \)
Area = \( 8 \times 5 = 40 \text{ cm}^2 \)
Perimeter = 26 cm, Area = 40 cm²

11. (a) Arrange in ascending order: \( \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{2}{5} \). (b) Convert \( \frac{3}{4} \) into a percentage.
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Find LCM of denominators 2, 4, 5. LCM = 20.
Convert each fraction to have denominator 20:
\[ \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1 \times 10}{2 \times 10} = \frac{10}{20} \] \[ \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3 \times 5}{4 \times 5} = \frac{15}{20} \] \[ \frac{2}{5} = \frac{2 \times 4}{5 \times 4} = \frac{8}{20} \]
Comparing numerators: \( 8 < 10 < 15 \).
Therefore, ascending order is: \( \frac{2}{5} < \frac{1}{2} < \frac{3}{4} \)

Part (b) [2 Marks]:
To convert a fraction to percentage, multiply by 100%:
\( \frac{3}{4} \times 100\% = 3 \times 25\% = 75\% \)
(a) 2/5, 1/2, 3/4    (b) 75%
12. Marks of 5 students: 45, 67, 89, 55, 74. (a) Find the mean (average) marks. (b) What is the range of the given marks?
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Mean = (Sum of all observations) / (Total number of observations)
Sum = \( 45 + 67 + 89 + 55 + 74 = 330 \)
Mean = \( \frac{330}{5} = 66 \)

Part (b) [2 Marks]:
Range = Highest mark - Lowest mark
Highest mark = 89, Lowest mark = 45
Range = \( 89 - 45 = 44 \)
(a) Mean = 66    (b) Range = 44
13. (a) If \( a = 3 \) and \( b = 4 \), find value of \( 2a^2 + 3b - 5 \). (b) Write an expression for: "Five times a number \( y \) decreased by 7".
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Given expression: \( 2a^2 + 3b - 5 \)
Substitute \( a = 3 \) and \( b = 4 \):
\( = 2(3)^2 + 3(4) - 5 \)
\( = 2(9) + 12 - 5 \)
\( = 18 + 12 - 5 \)
\( = 30 - 5 = 25 \)

Part (b) [2 Marks]:
"Five times a number \( y \)" is \( 5y \).
"decreased by 7" means we subtract 7.
Expression: \( 5y - 7 \)
(a) Value = 25    (b) Expression: 5y - 7
