VARDAAN LEARNING INSTITUTE

Minor Test 21: Basic Mathematics
Class: 6 & 7 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show rough work clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Calculate the sum of the smallest 4-digit number and the largest 3-digit number.
2.
Solve the following operations involving integers:
(i) \( -15 + 8 \)
(ii) \( -4 \times (-6) \)
3.
Convert the following fractions into decimals:
(i) \( \frac{3}{4} \)
(ii) \( \frac{7}{10} \)
4.
Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length is \( 12 \) cm and breadth is \( 5 \) cm.
5.
Write the algebraic expression for: "Five times a number \( x \) decreased by \( 3 \)".
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Evaluate the following expression using the order of operations (BODMAS):
$$ 25 - (15 \div 3) + 4 \times 2 $$
7.
A piece of wire is \( \frac{7}{8} \) meters long. It is cut into two pieces. If one piece is \( \frac{1}{4} \) meters long, how long is the other piece?
8.
Solve the simple equation to find the value of \( y \): $$ 3y + 5 = 20 $$
9.
Find the HCF (Highest Common Factor) and LCM (Lowest Common Multiple) of \( 12 \) and \( 18 \).
10.
The marks obtained by \( 7 \) students in a math test are: \( 15, 18, 12, 14, 20, 16, 12 \).
(i) Find the range of the marks.
(ii) Find the mean (average) marks.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
An orchard has \( 450 \) trees. Out of these, \( 30\% \) are apple trees, \( \frac{2}{5} \) are mango trees, and the rest are orange trees.
(a) How many apple trees are there? (2 Marks)
(b) How many mango trees are there? (2 Marks)
(c) How many orange trees are there? (1 Mark)
12.
A rectangular garden is \( 40 \) m long and \( 25 \) m wide.
(a) Find the area of the garden. (2 Marks)
(b) A path of width \( 2 \) m is built outside around the garden. Find the total area of the garden including the path. (3 Marks)
13.
Simplify the following algebraic expressions:
(a) Add: \( (3x^2 - 4x + 5) \) and \( (2x^2 + 7x - 2) \) (2.5 Marks)
(b) Subtract: \( (5a - 3b + 2c) \) from \( (8a + 2b - 4c) \) (2.5 Marks)
