SECTION A

Q1. Solve for $x$: $\frac{3x - 2}{2} = 5$
[2 Marks]
Final Answer: $x = 4$
Step 1: Multiply both sides by 2 to eliminate the denominator.
$$ 3x - 2 = 5 \times 2 $$
$$ 3x - 2 = 10 $$
Step 2: Add 2 to both sides.
$$ 3x = 10 + 2 $$
$$ 3x = 12 $$
Step 3: Divide by 3.
$$ x = \frac{12}{3} = 4 $$
Q2. Two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal. If one interior angle is $65^\circ$, find the measure of the co-interior angle on the same side of the transversal.
[2 Marks]
Final Answer: $115^\circ$
Property: The sum of co-interior angles (consecutive interior angles) on the same side of a transversal intersecting two parallel lines is always $180^\circ$ (supplementary).
Let the unknown angle be $x$.
$$ x + 65^\circ = 180^\circ $$
$$ x = 180^\circ - 65^\circ $$
$$ x = 115^\circ $$
Q3. The sum of two consecutive multiples of $3$ is $39$. Form the linear equation representing this situation and find the two numbers.
[2 Marks]
Equation: $x + (x+3) = 39$. Numbers are $18$ and $21$.
Let the first multiple of $3$ be $x$.
Then the next consecutive multiple of $3$ will be $(x + 3)$.
According to the question:
$$ x + (x + 3) = 39 $$
$$ 2x + 3 = 39 $$
$$ 2x = 36 \implies x = 18 $$
First number $= 18$. Second number $= 18 + 3 = 21$.

Q5. Solve: $2(x - 3) = 3(x - 4)$
[2 Marks]
Final Answer: $x = 6$
Expand the brackets on both sides:
$$ 2x - 6 = 3x - 12 $$
Transposing $2x$ to RHS and $-12$ to LHS:
$$ -6 + 12 = 3x - 2x $$
$$ 6 = x $$

SECTION B

Q6. Ray $AB \parallel$ ray $CD$. $O$ is between them. $\angle OAB = 45^\circ$, $\angle OCD = 35^\circ$. Find reflex $\angle AOC$.
[3 Marks]
Reflex $\angle AOC = 280^\circ$
Construction: Draw a line $PQ$ passing through $O$ parallel to $AB$ (and hence parallel to $CD$).
Since $AB \parallel PQ$ and $AO$ is the transversal:
$\angle AOP = \angle OAB = 45^\circ$ (Alternate Interior Angles)
Since $CD \parallel PQ$ and $CO$ is the transversal:
$\angle COP = \angle OCD = 35^\circ$ (Alternate Interior Angles)
Interior $\angle AOC = \angle AOP + \angle COP = 45^\circ + 35^\circ = 80^\circ$.
Reflex $\angle AOC = 360^\circ - \text{Interior } \angle AOC = 360^\circ - 80^\circ = 280^\circ$.
Q7. Ten years ago, father was $3$ times son's age. After $10$ years, father will be twice as old. Find present ages.
[3 Marks]
Son = $30$ years, Father = $70$ years
Let the age of the son 10 years ago be $x$.
Then, father's age 10 years ago was $3x$.
Present Ages: Son $= x + 10$, Father $= 3x + 10$.
Ages after 10 years: Son $= (x + 10) + 10 = x + 20$. Father $= (3x + 10) + 10 = 3x + 20$.
Given that after 10 years, father is twice as old as son:
$$ 3x + 20 = 2(x + 20) $$
$$ 3x + 20 = 2x + 40 $$
$$ 3x - 2x = 40 - 20 \implies x = 20 $$
Present age of son $= 20 + 10 = 30$ years.
Present age of father $= 3(20) + 10 = 60 + 10 = 70$ years.
Q8. Digits of a two-digit number differ by $3$. Original + Reversed = $143$. Find original number.
[3 Marks]
Number is $85$ or $58$
Let the unit's digit be $x$. Since digits differ by 3, let ten's digit be $(x + 3)$.
Original Number $= 10(x + 3) + x = 10x + 30 + x = 11x + 30$.
Number obtained on reversing digits $= 10(x) + (x + 3) = 11x + 3$.
Sum of the numbers $= 143$:
$$ (11x + 30) + (11x + 3) = 143 $$
$$ 22x + 33 = 143 $$
$$ 22x = 110 \implies x = 5 $$
Unit digit $= 5$. Ten's digit $= 5 + 3 = 8$. Number is $85$.
(Note: If we took ten's digit as $x$ and unit's as $x+3$, the number would be $58$. Both are correct).
Q9. Line $l \parallel m$ and $p \parallel q$. One angle is $70^\circ$. Find $x, y, z$.
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: $x = 110^\circ, z = 70^\circ, y = 110^\circ$
For $z$: Lines $l \parallel m$ are intersected by transversal $p$. $\angle z$ (top-left) and the $70^\circ$ angle (bottom-right) form a pair of alternate interior angles.
$$ \therefore z = 70^\circ $$
For $x$: Lines $p \parallel q$ are intersected by transversal $l$. The angle corresponding to $70^\circ$ at the right intersection ($l, q$) is also $70^\circ$ (bottom-right). $\angle x$ (top-right) and this $70^\circ$ angle form a linear pair on line $l$.
$$ \therefore x = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ $$
For $y$: Lines $p \parallel q$ are intersected by transversal $m$. $\angle y$ (bottom-left) corresponds to the bottom-left angle at intersection ($l, q$). This angle is vertically opposite to $\angle x$. Therefore, $y$ is equal to $x$.
$$ \therefore y = x = 110^\circ $$
Q10. Solve: $\frac{x+2}{6} - \left(\frac{11-x}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\right) = \frac{3x-4}{12}$
[3 Marks]
Final Answer: $x = 11$
First, open the bracket:
$$ \frac{x+2}{6} - \frac{11-x}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3x-4}{12} $$
Multiply entire equation by the LCM of denominators $(6, 3, 4, 12)$, which is $12$:
$$ 12 \left( \frac{x+2}{6} \right) - 12 \left( \frac{11-x}{3} \right) + 12 \left( \frac{1}{4} \right) = 12 \left( \frac{3x-4}{12} \right) $$
$$ 2(x + 2) - 4(11 - x) + 3 = 3x - 4 $$
$$ 2x + 4 - 44 + 4x + 3 = 3x - 4 $$
$$ 6x - 37 = 3x - 4 $$
$$ 6x - 3x = -4 + 37 $$
$$ 3x = 33 \implies x = 11 $$

Q11. $AB \parallel CD$, transversal $XY$. Interior angles on same side are in ratio $2:3$. Find all 8 angles.
[5 Marks]
Angles are $72^\circ, 108^\circ, 72^\circ, 108^\circ, 72^\circ, 108^\circ, 72^\circ, 108^\circ$
Let the two interior angles on the same side of transversal be $2x$ and $3x$.
We know that consecutive interior angles (co-interior) sum to $180^\circ$.
$$ 2x + 3x = 180^\circ $$
$$ 5x = 180^\circ \implies x = 36^\circ $$
The two angles are $2(36^\circ) = 72^\circ$ and $3(36^\circ) = 108^\circ$.
Let these be $\angle 3 = 108^\circ$ and $\angle 6 = 72^\circ$ (standard numbering).
Finding remaining angles:
1. Vertically opposite to $\angle 3$ is $\angle 1 = 108^\circ$.
2. Linear pair with $\angle 3$ is $\angle 4 = 180^\circ - 108^\circ = 72^\circ$.
3. Vertically opposite to $\angle 4$ is $\angle 2 = 72^\circ$.
4. Alternate interior to $\angle 3$ is $\angle 5 = 108^\circ$.
5. Alternate interior to $\angle 4$ is $\angle 6 = 72^\circ$.
6. Vertically opposite to $\angle 5$ is $\angle 7 = 108^\circ$.
7. Vertically opposite to $\angle 6$ is $\angle 8 = 72^\circ$.
All four acute angles are $72^\circ$, and all four obtuse angles are $108^\circ$.
Q12(a). Rational number: Numerator is $5$ less than denominator. If $3$ is added to both, it becomes $3/4$. Find original. (3m)
Q12(b). Solve: $\frac{2x+5}{3} - \frac{x-1}{4} = 3$. (2m)
[5 Marks]
(a) Original fraction = $\frac{12}{17}$. (b) $x = \frac{13}{5}$
Part (a):
Let the denominator be $x$. Then, numerator is $x - 5$.
Original fraction $= \frac{x - 5}{x}$.
Adding $3$ to both: $$ \frac{x - 5 + 3}{x + 3} = \frac{3}{4} $$
$$ \frac{x - 2}{x + 3} = \frac{3}{4} $$
Cross multiply: $4(x - 2) = 3(x + 3) \implies 4x - 8 = 3x + 9 \implies x = 17$.
Numerator $= 17 - 5 = 12$. Rational number $= \frac{12}{17}$.
Part (b):
$$ \frac{2x+5}{3} - \frac{x-1}{4} = 3 $$
Take LCM of $3$ and $4$ which is $12$:
$$ \frac{4(2x+5) - 3(x-1)}{12} = 3 $$
$$ \frac{8x + 20 - 3x + 3}{12} = 3 $$
$$ 5x + 23 = 36 $$
$$ 5x = 13 \implies x = \frac{13}{5} = 2.6 $$
Q13. Motorboat goes downstream in $4$ hrs, upstream in $5$ hrs. Speed of stream $= 3 \text{ km/hr}$. Find speed of boat in still water and total distance one-way.
[5 Marks]
Boat speed = $27 \text{ km/hr}$, Distance = $120 \text{ km}$
Let the speed of the motorboat in still water be $v \text{ km/hr}$.
Speed of stream $= 3 \text{ km/hr}$.
Downstream speed $= (v + 3) \text{ km/hr}$.
Upstream speed $= (v - 3) \text{ km/hr}$.
Distance $= \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$. Since distance covered upstream and downstream is the same:
$$ \text{Distance Downstream} = \text{Distance Upstream} $$
$$ 4(v + 3) = 5(v - 3) $$
$$ 4v + 12 = 5v - 15 $$
$$ 12 + 15 = 5v - 4v \implies v = 27 $$
Speed of boat in still water $= 27 \text{ km/hr}$.
Total distance in one direction $= 4(v + 3) = 4(27 + 3) = 4(30) = 120 \text{ km}$.
