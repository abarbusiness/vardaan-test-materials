Minor Test - Simple Machines
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Physics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
  • Draw neat and labeled diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define a simple machine. Give one example.
2.
Name the three important parts of a lever.
3.
What is an inclined plane?
4.
State the difference between a wedge and a screw.
5.
Which class of lever always has the fulcrum in the middle? Give an example.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Differentiate between Class II and Class III levers with one example each.
7.
How does a single fixed pulley help us in drawing water from a well?
8.
Define 'Load' and 'Effort' in the context of a simple machine.
9.
A wheel and axle is a simple machine. Explain briefly with a common example from daily life.
10.
Why is a pair of scissors considered a Class I lever? Explain.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) State the principle of a lever.
(b) Identify the class of lever for the following:
(i) Nutcracker
(ii) Sugar tongs
(iii) See-saw
12.
List the six different types of simple machines. Provide one daily life example for each type.
13.
(a) Describe a Class II lever and state exactly where the load is positioned relative to the other parts.
(b) Give two examples where an inclined plane is used in everyday life to make work easier.
