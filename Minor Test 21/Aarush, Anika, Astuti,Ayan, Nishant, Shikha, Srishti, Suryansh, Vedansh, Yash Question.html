SECTION A

1. Calculate the sum of the smallest 4-digit number and the largest 3-digit number.
[2 Marks]
Smallest 4-digit number = \( 1000 \)
Largest 3-digit number = \( 999 \)
Sum = \( 1000 + 999 \)
Answer: \( 1999 \)
2. Solve the following operations involving integers:
(i) \( -15 + 8 \)
(ii) \( -4 \times (-6) \)
[2 Marks]
(i) When adding integers with different signs, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger absolute value and keep the sign of the larger number.
\( |-15| - |8| = 15 - 8 = 7 \). Since \( 15 \) is larger and negative, the result is \( -7 \).
(i) Answer: \( -7 \)
(ii) The product of two negative integers is always a positive integer.
\( 4 \times 6 = 24 \)
(ii) Answer: \( 24 \)
3. Convert the following fractions into decimals:
(i) \( \frac{3}{4} \)
(ii) \( \frac{7}{10} \)
[2 Marks]
(i) To convert \( \frac{3}{4} \) to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator: \( 3 \div 4 \). Alternatively, multiply the numerator and denominator by 25 to make the denominator 100: \( \frac{3 \times 25}{4 \times 25} = \frac{75}{100} = 0.75 \).
(i) Answer: \( 0.75 \)
(ii) Fractions with 10 as the denominator can be written directly by placing the decimal point one place to the left.
(ii) Answer: \( 0.7 \)
4. Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length is \( 12 \) cm and breadth is \( 5 \) cm.
[2 Marks]
Given: Length (\( l \)) = \( 12 \) cm, Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 5 \) cm.
Formula for the perimeter of a rectangle = \( 2 \times (l + b) \)
$$ Perimeter = 2 \times (12 + 5) $$
$$ Perimeter = 2 \times (17) $$
$$ Perimeter = 34 \text{ cm} $$
Answer: \( 34 \text{ cm} \)
5. Write the algebraic expression for: "Five times a number \( x \) decreased by \( 3 \)".
[2 Marks]
"Five times a number \( x \)" translates to \( 5x \).
"decreased by 3" means we subtract 3 from that product.
Therefore, the expression is \( 5x - 3 \).
Answer: \( 5x - 3 \)

SECTION B

6. Evaluate the following expression using the order of operations (BODMAS): $$ 25 - (15 \div 3) + 4 \times 2 $$
[3 Marks]
According to BODMAS (Brackets, Orders, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction):
Step 1: Brackets Evaluate \( (15 \div 3) = 5 \)
Expression becomes: \( 25 - 5 + 4 \times 2 \)
Step 2: Multiplication Evaluate \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \)
Expression becomes: \( 25 - 5 + 8 \)
Step 3: Addition & Subtraction (Left to right)
\( 25 - 5 = 20 \)
\( 20 + 8 = 28 \)
Answer: \( 28 \)
7. A piece of wire is \( \frac{7}{8} \) meters long. It is cut into two pieces. If one piece is \( \frac{1}{4} \) meters long, how long is the other piece?
[3 Marks]
Total length of wire = \( \frac{7}{8} \) m
Length of one piece = \( \frac{1}{4} \) m
Length of the other piece = Total length \( - \) Length of one piece
$$ \text{Length} = \frac{7}{8} - \frac{1}{4} $$
To subtract, find the LCM of denominators \( 8 \) and \( 4 \), which is \( 8 \).
Convert \( \frac{1}{4} \) to an equivalent fraction with denominator \( 8 \): \( \frac{1 \times 2}{4 \times 2} = \frac{2}{8} \)
$$ \text{Length} = \frac{7}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{7 - 2}{8} = \frac{5}{8} \text{ m} $$
Answer: The other piece is \( \frac{5}{8} \) meters long.
8. Solve the simple equation to find the value of \( y \): $$ 3y + 5 = 20 $$
[3 Marks]
Given equation: \( 3y + 5 = 20 \)
Transpose \( +5 \) to the Right Hand Side (RHS), it becomes \( -5 \).
$$ 3y = 20 - 5 $$
$$ 3y = 15 $$
Divide both sides by \( 3 \) to isolate \( y \).
$$ y = \frac{15}{3} $$
$$ y = 5 $$
Answer: \( y = 5 \)
9. Find the HCF and LCM of \( 12 \) and \( 18 \).
[3 Marks]
Prime Factorization:
\( 12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^1 \)
\( 18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^1 \times 3^2 \)
Finding HCF (Highest Common Factor):
Product of the smallest power of each common prime factor.
Common factors are \( 2 \) and \( 3 \). Smallest powers: \( 2^1 \) and \( 3^1 \).
\( HCF = 2^1 \times 3^1 = 2 \times 3 = 6 \)
Finding LCM (Lowest Common Multiple):
Product of the greatest power of each prime factor involved.
Greatest powers: \( 2^2 \) and \( 3^2 \).
\( LCM = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36 \)
Answer: HCF = \( 6 \), LCM = \( 36 \)
10. The marks obtained by \( 7 \) students in a math test are: \( 15, 18, 12, 14, 20, 16, 12 \).
(i) Find the range of the marks.
(ii) Find the mean (average) marks.
[3 Marks]
First, let's arrange the data in ascending order: \( 12, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20 \)
(i) Range:
Range = Highest Value \( - \) Lowest Value
Range = \( 20 - 12 = 8 \)
(i) Range = \( 8 \)
(ii) Mean:
Mean = \( \frac{\text{Sum of all observations}}{\text{Total number of observations}} \)
$$ \text{Mean} = \frac{15 + 18 + 12 + 14 + 20 + 16 + 12}{7} $$
$$ \text{Mean} = \frac{107}{7} $$
$$ \text{Mean} = 15.28 \text{ (approx)} $$
(ii) Mean = \( 15.28 \)

SECTION C

11. An orchard has \( 450 \) trees. Out of these, \( 30\% \) are apple trees, \( \frac{2}{5} \) are mango trees, and the rest are orange trees.
(a) How many apple trees are there?
(b) How many mango trees are there?
(c) How many orange trees are there?
[5 Marks]
Total number of trees = \( 450 \)
(a) Apple Trees (2 Marks):
Apple trees = \( 30\% \) of Total trees
$$ = \frac{30}{100} \times 450 $$
$$ = 3 \times 45 = 135 \text{ trees} $$
(a) Apple Trees = \( 135 \)
(b) Mango Trees (2 Marks):
Mango trees = \( \frac{2}{5} \) of Total trees
$$ = \frac{2}{5} \times 450 $$
$$ = 2 \times 90 = 180 \text{ trees} $$
(b) Mango Trees = \( 180 \)
(c) Orange Trees (1 Mark):
Orange trees = Total trees \( - \) (Apple trees \( + \) Mango trees)
$$ = 450 - (135 + 180) $$
$$ = 450 - 315 $$
$$ = 135 \text{ trees} $$
(c) Orange Trees = \( 135 \)
12. A rectangular garden is \( 40 \) m long and \( 25 \) m wide.
(a) Find the area of the garden.
(b) A path of width \( 2 \) m is built outside around the garden. Find the total area of the garden including the path.
[5 Marks]
(a) Area of the garden (2 Marks):
Length (\( l \)) = \( 40 \) m, Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 25 \) m
Area = Length \( \times \) Breadth
$$ \text{Area} = 40 \times 25 = 1000 \text{ m}^2 $$
(a) Area of Garden = \( 1000 \text{ m}^2 \)
(b) Total area including path (3 Marks):
The path is built outside the garden. We need to add the path width twice (for both sides) to the original length and breadth.
New Length (including path) = \( 40 + 2 + 2 = 44 \) m
New Breadth (including path) = \( 25 + 2 + 2 = 29 \) m
Total Area = New Length \( \times \) New Breadth
$$ \text{Total Area} = 44 \times 29 $$
$$ \text{Total Area} = 1276 \text{ m}^2 $$
(b) Total Area = \( 1276 \text{ m}^2 \)
13. Simplify the following algebraic expressions:
(a) Add: \( (3x^2 - 4x + 5) \) and \( (2x^2 + 7x - 2) \)
(b) Subtract: \( (5a - 3b + 2c) \) from \( (8a + 2b - 4c) \)
[5 Marks]
(a) Addition (2.5 Marks):
Place like terms together:
$$ (3x^2 + 2x^2) + (-4x + 7x) + (5 - 2) $$
Combine the coefficients:
$$ = 5x^2 + 3x + 3 $$
(a) Answer: \( 5x^2 + 3x + 3 \)
(b) Subtraction (2.5 Marks):
Subtracting the first expression from the second means:
$$ (8a + 2b - 4c) - (5a - 3b + 2c) $$
Distribute the negative sign to all terms inside the second bracket:
$$ = 8a + 2b - 4c - 5a + 3b - 2c $$
Group like terms together:
$$ = (8a - 5a) + (2b + 3b) + (-4c - 2c) $$
Combine the coefficients:
$$ = 3a + 5b - 6c $$
(b) Answer: \( 3a + 5b - 6c \)
