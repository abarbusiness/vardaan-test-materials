Minor Test 21: Basic Mathematics
Class: 8 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show rough work clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate using laws of exponents: \( 3^{-2} \times 3^5 \).
2.
Find the square root of \( 729 \) using the prime factorization method.
3.
Find two rational numbers between \( \frac{1}{4} \) and \( \frac{1}{2} \).
4.
Add the following algebraic expressions: \( 4p^2q - 3pq + 5pq^2 \) and \( -10p^2q + 7pq - 4pq^2 \).
5.
Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram have equal measure. Find the measure of each of the angles of the parallelogram.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Solve the linear equation for \( x \):
$$ \frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{x}{3} + \frac{1}{4} $$
7.
The area of a rhombus is \( 240 \text{ cm}^2 \) and one of the diagonals is \( 16 \text{ cm} \). Find the length of the other diagonal.
8.
Simplify the following exponential expression: $$ \frac{8^{-1} \times 5^3}{2^{-4}} $$
9.
Use a suitable algebraic identity to evaluate \( (102)^2 \).
10.
Find the smallest number by which \( 256 \) must be multiplied to obtain a perfect cube.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The ages of Rahul and Haroon are in the ratio \( 5:7 \). Four years later, the sum of their ages will be \( 56 \) years. What are their present ages?
12.
A suitcase with dimensions \( 80 \text{ cm} \times 48 \text{ cm} \times 24 \text{ cm} \) is to be covered with a tarpaulin cloth. How many meters of tarpaulin of width \( 96 \text{ cm} \) is required to cover \( 100 \) such suitcases?
13.
Solve the following algebraic problems:
(a) Simplify using identities: \( (3x - 5y)^2 - (3x + 5y)^2 \) (2.5 Marks)
(b) Solve the equation: \( 5x + \frac{7}{2} = \frac{3}{2}x - 14 \) (2.5 Marks)
