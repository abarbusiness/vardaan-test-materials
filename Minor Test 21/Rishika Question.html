Minor Test 21
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: General Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly, showing all necessary steps.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following:
(a) \((-30) \div 10\)
(b) \((-15) \times 0 \times (-18)\)
2.
Find the product: \(2.5 \times 4\).
3.
Write an algebraic equation for the following statement:
"The sum of numbers \(x\) and \(4\) is \(9\)."
4.
Find the mean of the first five whole numbers.
5.
Find the complement of an angle of \(35^\circ\).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A car covers a distance of \(89.1\) km in \(2.2\) hours. What is the average distance covered by it in 1 hour?
7.
Solve the following equation for \(n\):
\[3n - 2 = 46\]
8.
The scores in a mathematics test (out of 25) of 15 students is as follows:
19, 25, 23, 20, 9, 20, 15, 10, 5, 16, 25, 20, 24, 12, 20.
Find the mode and median of this data. Are they the same?
9.
In a quiz, positive marks are given for correct answers and negative marks are given for incorrect answers. If Jack's scores in five successive rounds were \(25\), \(-5\), \(-10\), \(15\) and \(10\), what was his total score at the end?
10.
An angle is equal to its supplement. What is the measure of the angle? Explain your reasoning.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Solve the equation: \(2(x + 4) = 12\) (2 Marks)
(b) Set up an equation and solve it: "Add \(4\) to eight times a number; you get \(60\)." (3 Marks)
12.
A cement company earns a profit of ₹ 8 per bag of white cement sold and a loss of ₹ 5 per bag of grey cement sold.
(a) The company sells 3,000 bags of white cement and 5,000 bags of grey cement in a month. What is its total profit or loss? (2.5 Marks)
(b) What is the number of white cement bags it must sell to have neither profit nor loss, if the number of grey bags sold is 6,400 bags? (2.5 Marks)
13.
(a) Sushant reads \(\frac{1}{3}\) part of a book in 1 hour. How much part of the book will he read in \(2\frac{1}{5}\) hours? (3 Marks)
(b) Express \(5\) cm in metres and kilometres. (2 Marks)
