Q1. Evaluate: (a) \((-30) \div 10\)   (b) \((-15) \times 0 \times (-18)\)
[2 Marks]
(a) Division of a negative integer by a positive integer yields a negative integer.
\(-30 \div 10 = -3\)
(b) Any number multiplied by zero is zero.
\(-15 \times 0 \times (-18) = 0\)
Answers: (a) -3, (b) 0

\(25 \times 4 = 100\)
Since there is one digit after the decimal point in \(2.5\), place the decimal point one place from the right in the result:
\(10.0\)

Q3. Write an algebraic equation for: "The sum of numbers \(x\) and \(4\) is \(9\)."
[2 Marks]
"The sum of numbers \(x\) and \(4\)" translates to \(x + 4\).
"Is 9" translates to \(= 9\).
Answer: \(x + 4 = 9\)
Q4. Find the mean of the first five whole numbers.
[2 Marks]
The first five whole numbers are: \(0, 1, 2, 3, 4\).
Sum of the numbers \(= 0 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10\).
Number of observations \(= 5\).
\[\text{Mean} = \frac{\text{Sum of observations}}{\text{Number of observations}} = \frac{10}{5} = 2\]
Answer: 2
Q5. Find the complement of an angle of \(35^\circ\).
[2 Marks]
Two angles are complementary if their sum is \(90^\circ\).
Let the complementary angle be \(x\).
\(x + 35^\circ = 90^\circ\)
\(x = 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ\)
Answer: \(55^\circ\)
SECTION B
Q6. A car covers a distance of \(89.1\) km in \(2.2\) hours. What is the average distance covered by it in 1 hour?
[3 Marks]
Total distance covered \(= 89.1\) km.
Total time taken \(= 2.2\) hours.
Average distance covered in 1 hour \(= \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}\)
\[= \frac{89.1}{2.2} = \frac{\frac{891}{10}}{\frac{22}{10}} = \frac{891}{22}\]
Dividing 891 by 22:
\(891 \div 22 = 40.5\)
Answer: 40.5 km
Q7. Solve the following equation for \(n\): \(3n - 2 = 46\)
[3 Marks]
Given equation: \(3n - 2 = 46\)
Step 1: Transpose \(-2\) to the right hand side (RHS).
\(3n = 46 + 2\)
\(3n = 48\)
Step 2: Divide both sides by 3.
\[n = \frac{48}{3}\] \[n = 16\]
Answer: \(n = 16\)
Q8. The scores of 15 students are: 19, 25, 23, 20, 9, 20, 15, 10, 5, 16, 25, 20, 24, 12, 20. Find the mode and median. Are they the same?
[3 Marks]
First, arrange the data in ascending order:
\(5, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 19, 20, 20, 20, 20, 23, 24, 25, 25\)

Mode: The observation that occurs most frequently.
Here, 20 occurs 4 times, which is the maximum.
Mode \(= 20\)

Median: The middle value of the sorted data.
Since \(n = 15\) (odd), Median \(= \left(\frac{15+1}{2}\right)\)th observation \(= 8\)th observation.
Looking at the sorted list, the 8th observation is 20.
Median \(= 20\)

Yes, both the mode and median are the same.
Answer: Mode = 20, Median = 20. Yes, they are the same.
Q9. Jack's scores in five rounds were \(25\), \(-5\), \(-10\), \(15\) and \(10\). What was his total score?
[3 Marks]
To find the total score, we add all the scores together:
Total Score \(= 25 + (-5) + (-10) + 15 + 10\)
\[= 25 - 5 - 10 + 15 + 10\]
Group positive and negative integers:
Positive sum \(= 25 + 15 + 10 = 50\)
Negative sum \(= -5 - 10 = -15\)
Total \(= 50 + (-15) = 50 - 15 = 35\)
Answer: Total Score = 35
Q10. An angle is equal to its supplement. What is the measure of the angle?
[3 Marks]
Two angles are supplementary if their sum is \(180^\circ\).
Let the angle be \(x\).
Since its supplement is equal to the angle itself, the supplement is also \(x\).
Therefore, \(x + x = 180^\circ\)
\(2x = 180^\circ\)
\[x = \frac{180^\circ}{2} = 90^\circ\]
The angle is a right angle (\(90^\circ\)).
Answer: \(90^\circ\)
SECTION C
Q11. (a) Solve: \(2(x + 4) = 12\)
(b) Set up an equation and solve: "Add \(4\) to eight times a number; you get \(60\)."
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [2 Marks]:
Given: \(2(x + 4) = 12\)
Divide both sides by 2:
\(x + 4 = 6\)
Transpose 4 to the RHS:
\(x = 6 - 4\)
\(x = 2\)

Part (b) [3 Marks]:
Let the required number be \(y\).
Eight times the number \(= 8y\).
Adding 4 to it \(= 8y + 4\).
Result is 60, so the equation is:
\(8y + 4 = 60\)
Solving the equation:
Transpose 4 to RHS:
\(8y = 60 - 4\)
\(8y = 56\)
Divide by 8:
\[y = \frac{56}{8} = 7\]
Answer: (a) \(x = 2\), (b) Eq: \(8y + 4 = 60\), Number = 7
Q12. A company earns profit of ₹ 8/bag of white cement and loss of ₹ 5/bag of grey cement.
(a) Sells 3,000 white bags and 5,000 grey bags. Profit or loss?
(b) White bags to sell to have neither profit nor loss, if grey bags sold are 6,400.
[5 Marks]
Let profit be denoted by a positive integer and loss by a negative integer.
Profit per white cement bag = ₹ \(+8\)
Loss per grey cement bag = ₹ \(-5\)

Part (a) [2.5 Marks]:
Profit earned on selling 3,000 white bags \(= 3000 \times 8 =\) ₹ \(24,000\)
Loss incurred on selling 5,000 grey bags \(= 5000 \times (-5) =\) ₹ \(-25,000\)
Total profit/loss \(= 24000 + (-25000) =\) ₹ \(-1,000\)
Since the value is negative, it is a loss of ₹ 1,000.

Part (b) [2.5 Marks]:
"Neither profit nor loss" means Total Profit + Total Loss \(= 0\).
Loss incurred on selling 6,400 grey bags \(= 6400 \times (-5) =\) ₹ \(-32,000\)
Let the number of white cement bags to be sold be \(w\).
Profit from white bags \(= w \times 8 = 8w\)
Therefore, \(8w + (-32000) = 0\)
\(8w = 32000\)
\[w = \frac{32000}{8} = 4000\]
Answer: (a) Loss of ₹ 1,000, (b) 4,000 white cement bags
Q13. (a) Sushant reads \(\frac{1}{3}\) part of a book in 1 hour. How much will he read in \(2\frac{1}{5}\) hours?
(b) Express \(5\) cm in metres and kilometres.
[5 Marks]
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Part of the book read in 1 hour \(= \frac{1}{3}\)
Time given \(= 2\frac{1}{5}\) hours. Convert mixed fraction to improper fraction:
\[2\frac{1}{5} = \frac{2 \times 5 + 1}{5} = \frac{11}{5} \text{ hours}\]
Part of the book read in \(\frac{11}{5}\) hours \(= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{11}{5}\)
\[= \frac{1 \times 11}{3 \times 5} = \frac{11}{15}\]
He will read \(\frac{11}{15}\) part of the book.

Part (b) [2 Marks]:
We know that \(100 \text{ cm} = 1 \text{ m}\) and \(1000 \text{ m} = 1 \text{ km}\).
To convert cm to m, divide by 100:
\[5 \text{ cm} = \frac{5}{100} \text{ m} = 0.05 \text{ m}\]
To convert m to km, divide by 1000:
\[0.05 \text{ m} = \frac{0.05}{1000} \text{ km} = 0.00005 \text{ km}\]
Answer: (a) \(\frac{11}{15}\) part, (b) \(0.05\) m and \(0.00005\) km
