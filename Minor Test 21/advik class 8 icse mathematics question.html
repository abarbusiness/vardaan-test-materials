SECTION A (10 Marks) (5 Questions — 2 Marks Each)
Q1.

(A) $15\%$
(B) $20\%$

Q2.
[2 Marks] Factorise the expression completely: $16x^2 - 81y^2$.
(A) $(4x - 9y)(4x + 9y)$
(B) $(4x - 9y)^2$
(C) $(16x - 9y)(x + 9y)$
(D) $(4x + 9y)^2$
Q3.
[2 Marks] A student scores $360$ marks out of a total of $450$ marks in an examination. Calculate his score as a percentage.
(A) $75\%$
(B) $80\%$
(C) $82\%$
(D) $85\%$
Q4.
[2 Marks] The original price of an article increases from $\text{₹}800$ to $\text{₹}960$. Calculate the percentage increase in price.
Q5.
[2 Marks] Factorise the quadratic expression by splitting the middle term: $x^2 - 7x + 12$.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (5 Questions — 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Factorise the quadratic trinomial completely: $$2a^2 + 7a + 3$$
Q7.
[3 Marks] Out of a total monthly salary of $\text{₹}45,000$, Mr. Sharma spends $20\%$ on house rent, $40\%$ on household expenses, and $15\%$ on education. How much money does he save each month?
Q8.
[3 Marks] Factorise the following polynomial expression completely into real algebraic factors: $$x^4 - 16y^4$$
Q9.
[3 Marks] The price of sugar rises by $25\%$. By what percentage must a household reduce its consumption of sugar so that its total expenditure on sugar remains unchanged?
Q10.

SECTION C (15 Marks) (3 Questions — 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] In an annual examination, $85\%$ of the total candidates passed. It was found that $45$ candidates failed the examination.

(a) Find the total number of candidates who appeared in the examination. [3 Marks]
(b) If $60\%$ of the total appeared candidates were boys, calculate the number of girls who appeared in the examination. [2 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] Factorise the following algebraic expressions completely:

(a) $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} - 2 - 5x + \frac{5}{x}$ [2.5 Marks]
(b) $8x^3 + 27y^3$ [2.5 Marks]
Q13.
[5 Marks] [HIGH-ORDER THINKING SKILL (HOTS)]
An electronics factory produced a total batch of electronic components. During quality control inspection, $8\%$ of the total components were found defective. Of the remaining non-defective components, $75\%$ were sold in the domestic market, and the remaining $2,070$ non-defective components were exported abroad.

(a) Determine the total number of electronic components produced by the factory. [2.5 Marks]
(b) The production cost equation polynomial is given by $1000x^3 - 125$. Factorise this polynomial completely into linear and quadratic factors. [1.5 Marks]
(c) If defective items are sold at a $60\%$ discount on cost price while non-defective exported items are sold at a $20\%$ profit, calculate the net profit or loss percentage earned on the sum of these two batches combined. [1 Mark]
