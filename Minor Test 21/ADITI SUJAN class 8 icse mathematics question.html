SECTION A (10 Marks) (5 Questions — 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] Factorise the expression completely using the difference of two squares identity: $25x^2 - 49y^2$.
(A) $(5x - 7y)(5x + 7y)$
(B) $(5x - 7y)^2$
(C) $(25x - 7y)(x + 7y)$
(D) $(5x + 7y)^2$
Q2.
[2 Marks] Find the compound interest on $\text{₹}10,000$ for $2$ years at $10\%$ per annum compounded annually.
(A) $\text{₹}2,000$
(B) $\text{₹}2,100$
(C) $\text{₹}2,200$
(D) $\text{₹}12,100$
Q3.
[2 Marks] Factorise by grouping the terms suitably: $ab - by - ax + xy$.
(A) $(a - x)(b - y)$
(B) $(a - y)(b - x)$
(C) $(a + y)(b - x)$
(D) $(a - y)(b + x)$
Q4.
[2 Marks] The present population of a town is $50,000$. If it grows at a rate of $4\%$ per annum compounded annually, calculate its population after $2$ years.
Q5.
[2 Marks] Factorise completely into linear and quadratic real factors: $x^4 - 81$.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (5 Questions — 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Factorise the quadratic trinomial by splitting the middle term: $$6x^2 + 17x + 12$$
Q7.
[3 Marks] Calculate the final amount and compound interest on $\text{₹}8,000$ for $1\frac{1}{2}$ years at $10\%$ per annum, interest being compounded half-yearly.
Q8.
[3 Marks] Factorise the following algebraic expression completely: $$x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 - 3x - \frac{3}{x}$$
Q9.
[3 Marks] At what rate percent per annum will $\text{₹}4,000$ yield a compound interest of $\text{₹}1,324$ in $3$ years, with interest compounded annually?
Q10.
[3 Marks] Using algebraic factorisation formulas, evaluate without direct multiplication: $$\frac{0.87 \times 0.87 \times 0.87 + 0.13 \times 0.13 \times 0.13}{0.87 \times 0.87 - 0.87 \times 0.13 + 0.13 \times 0.13}$$
SECTION C (15 Marks) (3 Questions — 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] The difference between the simple interest and compound interest on a certain sum of money for $2$ years at $8\%$ per annum compounded annually is $\text{₹}128$.

(a) Find the principal sum of money. [3 Marks]
(b) Find the total compound interest earned on this exact sum over $3$ years at the same annual rate. [2 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] Factorise the following polynomial expressions completely:

(a) $a^4 - 2a^2b^2 + b^4 - c^4$ [2.5 Marks]
(b) $2x^2 + y^2 + 8z^2 - 2\sqrt{2}xy + 4\sqrt{2}yz - 8xz$ [2.5 Marks]
Q13.
[5 Marks] [HIGH-ORDER THINKING SKILL (HOTS)]
A sum of money invested at compound interest, compounded annually, amounts to $\text{₹}18,600$ after $3$ years and to $\text{₹}27,900$ after $6$ years.

(a) Determine the original principal sum $P$ and the growth multiplier for a $3$-year period. [2.5 Marks]
(b) Calculate the total accumulated amount after $9$ years. [1.5 Marks]
(c) If this principal sum $P$ was instead invested in an asset that depreciates at a rate of $20\%$ per annum compounded annually, factorise the polynomial expression $P(1 - r)^3$ and find the depreciated value after $3$ years. [1 Mark]
