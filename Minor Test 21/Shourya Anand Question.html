Minor Test 21
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Geography Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Who discovered North America and after which explorer is it named?
2.
Define the term 'mineral' in your own words.
3.
Name any two major physical divisions of North America.
4.
What are 'ores'? Give one example of a metallic ore.
5.
State the two main types of mining methods used to extract minerals.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Why is the lumbering industry considered important in Canada?
7.
Differentiate between metallic and non-metallic minerals. Give one example of each.
8.
Why is it essential to conserve mineral resources? State any two reasons.
9.
Write a short note on the Canadian Shield highlighting its economic importance.
10.
What are 'fossil fuels'? Name any two widely used fossil fuels.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Describe the location and extent of North America. Mention the oceans that border it on its northern, eastern, and western sides.
12.
Explain the different categories of lumberjacks (high riggers, buckers, skidders, haulers) and the specific work they perform in the lumbering industry.
13.
Answer the following:
(a)
What is the difference between surface mining and underground mining? (3 Marks)
(b)
State any two uses of the mineral Bauxite. (2 Marks)
