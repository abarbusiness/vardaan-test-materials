Minor Test 21: Light Energy & Heat
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Science (Physics) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams where necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define temperature and state its S.I. unit.
2.
What is meant by the reflection of light? Name the two types of reflection.
3.
Convert $45^\circ \text{C}$ to the Kelvin scale.
4.
State two properties of an image formed by a plane mirror.
5.
Define a real image. Can it be obtained on a screen?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
State the two laws of reflection of light.
7.
The normal body temperature of a human is $98.6^\circ \text{F}$. Calculate this temperature on the Celsius scale.
8.
Distinguish between regular reflection and irregular (diffused) reflection by giving two points of difference.
9.
Why does a clinical thermometer have a slight bend (kink) in its capillary tube? Explain briefly.
10.
What is meant by the rectilinear propagation of light? Give one everyday example to support your answer.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Answer the following regarding temperature scales:
(a) Write the mathematical relationship (formula) that connects the Celsius ($^\circ \text{C}$) and Fahrenheit ($^\circ \text{F}$) scales. (2 Marks)
(b) The room temperature on a cold day is recorded as $20^\circ \text{C}$. What is this temperature on the Fahrenheit scale? (2 Marks)
(c) Define heat energy. (1 Mark)
12.
Answer the following regarding light:
(a) With the help of a neatly labeled diagram, demonstrate the reflection of a light ray by a plane mirror. Clearly mark the incident ray, reflected ray, normal, angle of incidence, and angle of reflection. (3 Marks)
(b) If a light ray strikes a plane mirror such that the angle of incidence is $30^\circ$, calculate the angle of reflection and the total angle between the incident ray and the reflected ray. (2 Marks)
13.
Answer the following regarding heat transfer:
(a) Differentiate between the three modes of heat transfer: Conduction, Convection, and Radiation. Explain them briefly. (3 Marks)
(b) Give one example of a good conductor of heat and one example of an insulator. (2 Marks)
