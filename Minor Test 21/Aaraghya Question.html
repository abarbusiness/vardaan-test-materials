Minor Test 23: Chemistry & Geography
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Science & Social Studies Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Keep answers to the point.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define the terms 'sonorous' and 'lustre' in the context of metals.
2.
What is meant by a 'joint sector industry'? Give one prominent example in India.
3.
Name a metal and a non-metal that exist in the liquid state at room temperature.
4.
State two reasons why aluminium is widely preferred for manufacturing cooking utensils.
5.
Briefly explain the term 'cooperative sector industries' with an example.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Differentiate between the terms 'mineral' and 'ore'. Are all minerals considered ores? Justify your answer.
7.
Classify industries on the basis of raw materials used. Provide at least three categories with one example for each.
8.
Write three specific physical properties of non-metals that distinguish them from metals.
9.
What is rust chemically? Write down the two essential conditions required for the rusting of iron.
10.
Explain how government policies and the availability of cheap labor play a crucial role in deciding the location of a new industry.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Define an alloy. (1 Mark)
(b) State the composition (constituent elements) and one primary use of the following alloys: (i) Bronze (ii) Stainless Steel (4 Marks)
12.
Differentiate between large scale industries and small scale industries based on the following parameters: Capital investment, labor employed, volume of production, power usage, and examples. (5 Marks)
13.
(a) Compare metals and non-metals explicitly on the basis of malleability, ductility, and electrical conductivity. (3 Marks)
(b) Give a scientific reason why phosphorus is always stored underwater. (2 Marks)
