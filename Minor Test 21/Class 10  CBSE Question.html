SECTION A (10 Marks) (5 Questions — 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] From a point $Q$, the length of the tangent to a circle is $24\text{ cm}$ and the distance of $Q$ from the centre is $25\text{ cm}$. Find the radius of the circle.
(A) $7\text{ cm}$
(B) $12\text{ cm}$
(C) $15\text{ cm}$
(D) $24.5\text{ cm}$
Q2.
[2 Marks] In the given figure, if $TP$ and $TQ$ are the two tangents to a circle with centre $O$ so that $\angle POQ = 110^\circ$, then find $\angle PTQ$.
(A) $60^\circ$
(B) $70^\circ$
(C) $80^\circ$
(D) $90^\circ$
Q3.
[2 Marks] If tangents $PA$ and $PB$ from a point $P$ to a circle with centre $O$ are inclined to each other at an angle of $80^\circ$, then find $\angle POA$.
(A) $50^\circ$
(B) $60^\circ$
(C) $70^\circ$
(D) $80^\circ$
Q4.
[2 Marks] Two concentric circles are of radii $5\text{ cm}$ and $3\text{ cm}$. Find the length of the chord of the larger circle which touches the smaller circle.
Q5.
[2 Marks] The length of a tangent from a point $A$ at a distance $5\text{ cm}$ from the centre of the circle is $4\text{ cm}$. Find the radius of the circle.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (5 Questions — 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel.
Q7.
[3 Marks] A quadrilateral $ABCD$ is drawn to circumscribe a circle. Prove that: $$AB + CD = AD + BC$$
Q8.
[3 Marks] Prove that the angle between the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle is supplementary to the angle subtended by the line-segment joining the points of contact at the centre.
Q9.
[3 Marks] Prove that the parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus.
Q10.
[3 Marks] Two tangents $TP$ and $TQ$ are drawn to a circle with centre $O$ from an external point $T$. Prove that: $$\angle PTQ = 2\angle OPQ$$
SECTION C (15 Marks) (3 Questions — 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] In the given figure, $XY$ and $X'Y'$ are two parallel tangents to a circle with centre $O$ and another tangent $AB$ with point of contact $C$ intersecting $XY$ at $A$ and $X'Y'$ at $B$. Prove that $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$.
Q12.
[5 Marks] A triangle $ABC$ is drawn to circumscribe a circle of radius $4\text{ cm}$ such that the segments $BD$ and $DC$ into which $BC$ is divided by the point of contact $D$ are of lengths $8\text{ cm}$ and $6\text{ cm}$ respectively. Find the sides $AB$ and $AC$.
Q13.
[5 Marks] [HIGH-ORDER THINKING SKILL (HOTS)]
Two circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ with radii $r_1 = 9\text{ cm}$ and $r_2 = 4\text{ cm}$ touch each other externally at a point $P$. A direct common tangent touches circle $C_1$ at $A$ and circle $C_2$ at $B$. A third smaller circle $C_3$ of radius $r_3$ is drawn in the curvilinear space between $C_1$, $C_2$, and the line segment $AB$ such that $C_3$ touches $C_1$ externally, $C_2$ externally, and line $AB$ tangentially.

(a) Calculate the exact length of the direct common tangent segment $AB$.
(b) Determine the exact radius $r_3$ of the circle $C_3$.
(c) Calculate the area of the curvilinear triangle region bounded between the two larger touching circles $C_1$, $C_2$ and the tangent segment $AB$. (Express answer correct to 2 decimal places).
