SECTION A SOLUTIONS (10 MARKS)

Q1. Factorise completely: $25x^2 - 49y^2$.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (A) $(5x - 7y)(5x + 7y)$
Step-by-Step Solution:
Express each term as a perfect square:
$$25x^2 - 49y^2 = (5x)^2 - (7y)^2$$
Applying the algebraic identity $A^2 - B^2 = (A - B)(A + B)$ where $A = 5x$ and $B = 7y$:
$$(5x)^2 - (7y)^2 = (5x - 7y)(5x + 7y)$$
Q2. Find the compound interest on $\text{₹}10,000$ for $2$ years at $10\%$ per annum compounded annually.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (B) $\text{₹}2,100$
Step-by-Step Solution:
Given: Principal $P = \text{₹}10,000$, Rate $R = 10\%$, Time $n = 2$ years.
$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n$$ $$A = 10000\left(1 + \frac{10}{100}\right)^2 = 10000\left(\frac{11}{10}\right)^2$$ $$A = 10000 \times \frac{121}{100} = \text{₹}12,100$$ $$\text{Compound Interest (CI)} = A - P = 12100 - 10000 = \mathbf{\text{₹}2,100}$$
Q3. Factorise by grouping: $ab - by - ax + xy$.
[2 Marks]
Correct Option: (B) $(a - y)(b - x)$
Step-by-Step Solution:
Group the terms in pairs:
$$ab - by - ax + xy = b(a - y) - x(a - y)$$
Take out the common binomial factor $(a - y)$:
$$= (a - y)(b - x)$$
Q4. Population of a town is $50,000$. Growth rate is $4\%$ p.a. Find population after $2$ years.
[2 Marks]
Final Population $= 54,080$
Step-by-Step Solution:
Given $P_0 = 50,000$, $r = 4\%$, $n = 2$ years.
$$P_2 = P_0\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$ $$P_2 = 50000\left(1 + \frac{4}{100}\right)^2 = 50000\left(\frac{26}{25}\right)^2$$ $$P_2 = 50000 \times \frac{676}{625} = 80 \times 676 = \mathbf{54,080}$$
Q5. Factorise completely: $x^4 - 81$.
[2 Marks]
Factors $= (x - 3)(x + 3)(x^2 + 9)$
Step-by-Step Solution:
Apply difference of squares twice:
$$x^4 - 81 = (x^2)^2 - (9)^2 = (x^2 - 9)(x^2 + 9)$$ $$= (x^2 - 3^2)(x^2 + 9) = \mathbf{(x - 3)(x + 3)(x^2 + 9)}$$

SECTION B SOLUTIONS (15 MARKS)

Q6. Factorise by splitting the middle term: $6x^2 + 17x + 12$.
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:
To factorise $6x^2 + 17x + 12$, find two numbers whose product is $6 \times 12 = 72$ and whose sum is $17$.
The required numbers are $9$ and $8$ ($9 \times 8 = 72$ and $9 + 8 = 17$).
$$6x^2 + 17x + 12 = 6x^2 + 9x + 8x + 12$$ $$= 3x(2x + 3) + 4(2x + 3)$$ $$= \mathbf{(2x + 3)(3x + 4)}$$
Q7. Calculate Amount and CI on $\text{₹}8,000$ for $1\frac{1}{2}$ years at $10\%$ p.a., interest compounded half-yearly.
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:
Given Principal $P = \text{₹}8,000$.
Annual Rate $R = 10\% \implies$ Half-yearly Rate $r = \frac{10}{2} = 5\%$ per half-year.
Time $= 1\frac{1}{2}$ years $= 3$ half-years ($n = 3$).
$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$ $$A = 8000\left(1 + \frac{5}{100}\right)^3 = 8000\left(\frac{21}{20}\right)^3$$ $$A = 8000 \times \frac{9261}{8000} = \mathbf{\text{₹}9,261}$$ $$\text{CI} = A - P = 9261 - 8000 = \mathbf{\text{₹}1,261}$$
Q8. Factorise: $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 - 3x - \frac{3}{x}$.
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:
Observe that $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 = \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2$.
$$x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 - 3x - \frac{3}{x} = \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 - 3\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)$$
Taking out $\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)$ as common factor:
$$= \mathbf{\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)\left(x + \frac{1}{x} - 3\right)}$$
Q9. At what rate % p.a. will $\text{₹}4,000$ yield a CI of $\text{₹}1,324$ in $3$ years compounded annually?
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:
$P = \text{₹}4,000$, $\text{CI} = \text{₹}1,324 \implies A = P + \text{CI} = 4000 + 1324 = \text{₹}5,324$.
$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^3$$ $$5324 = 4000\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^3$$ $$\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^3 = \frac{5324}{4000} = \frac{1331}{1000}$$ $$\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{11}{10}\right)^3$$
Taking cube root on both sides:
$$1 + \frac{R}{100} = \frac{11}{10} \implies \frac{R}{100} = \frac{11}{10} - 1 = \frac{1}{10}$$ $$R = \mathbf{10\% \text{ per annum}}$$
Q10. Evaluate: $\frac{0.87 \times 0.87 \times 0.87 + 0.13 \times 0.13 \times 0.13}{0.87 \times 0.87 - 0.87 \times 0.13 + 0.13 \times 0.13}$
[3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Solution:
Let $a = 0.87$ and $b = 0.13$.
The expression is of the form:
$$\frac{a^3 + b^3}{a^2 - ab + b^2}$$
Using identity $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$:
$$\frac{(a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)}{a^2 - ab + b^2} = a + b$$ $$a + b = 0.87 + 0.13 = \mathbf{1.00}$$

SECTION C SOLUTIONS (15 MARKS)

Q11. Difference between SI and CI for $2$ years at $8\%$ p.a. is $\text{₹}128$. Find sum and total CI for $3$ years.
[5 Marks]
Part (a): Finding Principal $P$ [3 Marks]
Let the Principal be $P$. Rate $R = 8\%$, time $n = 2$ years.
$$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times 8 \times 2}{100} = \frac{16P}{100}$$ $$\text{CI} = P\left[\left(1 + \frac{8}{100}\right)^2 - 1\right] = P\left[\left(\frac{27}{25}\right)^2 - 1\right] = P\left[\frac{729 - 625}{625}\right] = \frac{104P}{625}$$ $$\text{CI} - \text{SI} = \frac{104P}{625} - \frac{16P}{100} = \frac{104P - 100P}{625} = \frac{4P}{625}$$ $$\frac{4P}{625} = 128 \implies P = \frac{128 \times 625}{4} = 32 \times 625 = \mathbf{\text{₹}20,000}$$
Part (b): Compound Interest for 3 Years [2 Marks]
$$A_3 = 20000\left(1 + \frac{8}{100}\right)^3 = 20000\left(\frac{27}{25}\right)^3 = 20000 \times \frac{19683}{15625}$$ $$A_3 = 1.28 \times 19683 = \text{₹}25,194.24$$ $$\text{CI}_3 = 25194.24 - 20000 = \mathbf{\text{₹}5,194.24}$$
Q12. Factorise: (a) $a^4 - 2a^2b^2 + b^4 - c^4$   (b) $2x^2 + y^2 + 8z^2 - 2\sqrt{2}xy + 4\sqrt{2}yz - 8xz$.
[5 Marks]
Part (a) Solution [2.5 Marks]:
Group the first three terms as a perfect square trinomial:
$$a^4 - 2a^2b^2 + b^4 - c^4 = (a^2 - b^2)^2 - (c^2)^2$$ $$= [(a^2 - b^2) - c^2][(a^2 - b^2) + c^2]$$ $$= \mathbf{(a - b)(a + b)(a^2 - b^2 + c^2)}$$
Part (b) Solution [2.5 Marks]:
Express as $(A + B + C)^2 = A^2 + B^2 + C^2 + 2AB + 2BC + 2CA$:
$$2x^2 + y^2 + 8z^2 - 2\sqrt{2}xy + 4\sqrt{2}yz - 8xz$$ $$= (-\sqrt{2}x)^2 + (y)^2 + (2\sqrt{2}z)^2 + 2(-\sqrt{2}x)(y) + 2(y)(2\sqrt{2}z) + 2(2\sqrt{2}z)(-\sqrt{2}x)$$ $$= \mathbf{(-\sqrt{2}x + y + 2\sqrt{2}z)^2} \quad \text{or} \quad \mathbf{(\sqrt{2}x - y - 2\sqrt{2}z)^2}$$
Q13. [HOTS] A sum amounts to $\text{₹}18,600$ in $3$ years and $\text{₹}27,900$ in $6$ years. Find principal, $9$-year amount, and $20\%$ depreciation value.
[5 Marks]
Part (a): Principal Sum and 3-Year Growth Factor [2.5 Marks]
Let principal be $P$ and $3$-year growth multiplier be $k = \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^3$.
$$A_3 = P \cdot k = 18,600 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$ $$A_6 = P \cdot k^2 = 27,900 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$ $$\text{Divide (2) by (1): } k = \frac{27900}{18600} = \mathbf{1.5}$$ $$\text{From (1): } P \times 1.5 = 18600 \implies P = \frac{18600}{1.5} = \mathbf{\text{₹}12,400}$$
Part (b): Accumulated Amount after 9 Years [1.5 Marks]
$$A_9 = A_6 \times k = 27900 \times 1.5 = \mathbf{\text{₹}41,850}$$
Part (c): Depreciation Factorisation & Value [1 Mark]
Factorisation of $P(1 - r)^3$:
$$P(1 - r)^3 = P(1 - r)(1 - r)(1 - r)$$ $$\text{For } P = 12400 \text{ and } r = 0.20 \text{ (20\% depreciation):}$$ $$\text{Depreciated Value} = 12400(1 - 0.20)^3 = 12400 \times (0.8)^3 = 12400 \times 0.512 = \mathbf{\text{₹}6,348.80}$$
