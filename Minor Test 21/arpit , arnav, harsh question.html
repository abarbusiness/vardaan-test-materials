Minor Test 21: Linear Equations & Parallel Lines
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show proper steps and workings. Draw neat diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Solve for $x$: $$ \frac{3x - 2}{2} = 5 $$
2.
Two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal. If one interior angle is $65^\circ$, find the measure of the co-interior angle on the same side of the transversal.
3.
The sum of two consecutive multiples of $3$ is $39$. Form the linear equation representing this situation and find the two numbers.
4.
In the given figure, lines $l$ and $m$ are parallel, and $t$ is a transversal. If $\angle 1 = 50^\circ$, find the value of $\angle x$ and $\angle y$.
5.
Solve the following equation: $$ 2(x - 3) = 3(x - 4) $$
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
In the figure below, ray $AB \parallel$ ray $CD$. A point $O$ is situated between them. If $\angle OAB = 45^\circ$ and $\angle OCD = 35^\circ$, find the measure of reflex $\angle AOC$. (Hint: Draw a line through $O$ parallel to $AB$).
7.
Ten years ago, a father was $3$ times as old as his son. After $10$ years from now, the father will be twice as old as his son. Find their present ages.
8.
The digits of a two-digit number differ by $3$. If the digits are interchanged, and the resulting number is added to the original number, we get $143$. Find the original number.
9.
In the given diagram, line $l \parallel m$ and line $p \parallel q$. If one of the angles formed is $70^\circ$ as shown, find the values of $x, y$, and $z$ giving geometrical reasons.
10.
Solve the linear equation for $x$: $$ \frac{x+2}{6} - \left(\frac{11-x}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\right) = \frac{3x-4}{12} $$
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Two parallel lines $AB$ and $CD$ are intersected by a transversal $XY$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. If the interior angles on the same side of the transversal are in the ratio $2:3$, find the measure of all the eight angles formed. Support your answer with a rough sketch and proper geometrical reasoning for each pair of angles.
12.
(a) A rational number is such that its numerator is $5$ less than its denominator. If $3$ is added to both the numerator and the denominator, the new fraction becomes $\frac{3}{4}$. Find the original rational number. (3 Marks)
(b) Solve for $x$: $$ \frac{2x+5}{3} - \frac{x-1}{4} = 3 $$ (2 Marks)
13.
A motorboat goes downstream on a river and covers a certain distance in $4$ hours. It covers the same distance upstream in $5$ hours. If the speed of the stream is $3 \text{ km/hr}$, find the speed of the motorboat in still water and the total distance covered by the boat in one direction.
