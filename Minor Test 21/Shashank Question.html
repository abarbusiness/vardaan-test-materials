Minor Test 21
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: General Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all working clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Write the number name for \( 4,56,789 \) in the Indian numbering system.
2.
Find the sum of \( 23.45 \) and \( 6.7 \).
3.
Define a prime number and give two basic examples.
4.
Solve for \( x \): \( x + 5 = 12 \).
5.
What is an acute angle? Give one example of an acute angle in degrees.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Simplify the fraction \( \frac{36}{48} \) to its lowest terms.
7.
Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of 12 and 18.
8.
A boy bought a book for ₹120 and sold it for ₹150. Find his profit percentage.
9.
Calculate the perimeter and area of a rectangle whose length is \( 8 \text{ cm} \) and breadth is \( 5 \text{ cm} \).
10.
Evaluate the following using the BODMAS rule:
\( 15 + (12 \div 3) - 5 \times 2 \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Arrange the following fractions in ascending order: \( \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{2}{5} \). [3]
(b) Convert \( \frac{3}{4} \) into a percentage. [2]
12.
The marks obtained by 5 students in a basic math test are 45, 67, 89, 55, and 74.
(a) Find the mean (average) marks. [3]
(b) What is the range of the given marks? [2]
13.
(a) If \( a = 3 \) and \( b = 4 \), find the value of the expression \( 2a^2 + 3b - 5 \). [3]
(b) Write an algebraic expression for the statement: "Five times a number \( y \) decreased by 7". [2]
