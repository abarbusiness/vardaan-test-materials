Minor Test 21: Pressure
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science (Physics) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show proper steps and formulas for numerical questions. Draw neat diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define pressure. What is the SI unit of pressure?
2.
Why do school bags have wide and broad straps?
3.
What happens to the pressure exerted by a liquid as the depth increases?
4.
Define atmospheric pressure. Why are our bodies not crushed by the heavy atmospheric pressure?
5.
A force of $100\text{ N}$ is applied to an area of $2\text{ m}^2$. Calculate the pressure exerted on the object.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Explain scientifically why it is much easier to cut an apple with a sharp knife rather than a blunt knife.
7.
Describe a simple experiment (with reasoning) to show that liquids exert pressure on the walls of the container.
8.
Give scientific reasons for the following observations:
(a) Porters place a round piece of cloth on their heads when they have to carry heavy loads.
(b) Heavy duty trucks and tractors have very broad and wide tyres.
9.
Calculate the pressure produced by a force of $800\text{ N}$ acting on an area of $2.0\text{ m}^2$. What will be the new pressure if the contact area is halved while the force remains the same?
10.
What is a barometer? Name the liquid conventionally used in a standard barometer. Why are astronauts required to wear specially designed pressurized spacesuits?
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) State any three fundamental properties of the pressure exerted by liquids. (3 Marks)
(b) A solid metallic block weighs $250\text{ N}$. When it is placed on a wooden plank, the area of contact is $0.5\text{ m}^2$. Calculate the pressure exerted by the block on the plank. (2 Marks)
12.
(a) With the help of the given diagram, describe an activity to demonstrate that liquids exert equal pressure at the same depth. (3 Marks)
(b) Deep-sea divers wear specially designed suits. Explain why. (2 Marks)
13.
(a) Explain the phenomenon of atmospheric pressure using the example of a rubber sucker sticking to a smooth wall. Support your explanation with the provided diagram. (3 Marks)
(b) Why do some people experience nose bleeding when they travel to very high altitudes? (2 Marks)
