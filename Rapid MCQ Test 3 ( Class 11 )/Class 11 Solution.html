DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Electronic Configuration
1.The general outer electronic configuration of s-block elements is represented as:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: A) \( ns^{1-2} \)
Explanation: s-block elements consist of Group 1 (Alkali metals, \( ns^1 \)) and Group 2 (Alkaline earth metals, \( ns^2 \)). Therefore, their combined general configuration is \( ns^{1-2} \).
2.According to NCERT, the correct general electronic configuration for d-block elements is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) \( (n-1)d^{1-10} ns^{0-2} \)
Explanation: Most d-block elements have \( ns^{1-2} \), but Palladium (Pd) has an exceptional configuration of \( [\text{Kr}] 4d^{10} 5s^0 \). Thus, the correct general NCERT formula includes \( ns^0 \), making it \( ns^{0-2} \).
3.What is the correct electronic configuration of Copper (\( Z=29 \))?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^{10} 4s^1 \)
Explanation: An electron jumps from the 4s orbital to the 3d orbital to achieve a fully filled, highly stable \( d^{10} \) state due to symmetry and maximum exchange energy.
4.Identify the electronic configuration of the \( \text{Cr}^{3+} \) ion (\( Z=24 \)):
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^3 \)
Explanation: Chromium ground state: \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^5 4s^1 \). To form \( \text{Cr}^{3+} \), remove 3 electrons (first from 4s, then two from 3d). Result: \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^3 \).
5.Palladium (\( Z=46 \)) exhibits an exceptional configuration. Which of the following is correct?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: D) \( [\text{Kr}] 4d^{10} 5s^0 \)
Explanation: Palladium transfers both its 5s electrons to the 4d subshell to achieve a completely filled \( 4d^{10} \) configuration, gaining immense stability.
6.Which of the following ions possesses a \( 3d^5 \) outer configuration?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: D) All of the above
Explanation: Mn (\( 3d^5 4s^2 \)) \(\rightarrow \text{Mn}^{2+}\) (\( 3d^5 \)). Fe (\( 3d^6 4s^2 \)) \(\rightarrow \text{Fe}^{3+}\) (\( 3d^5 \)). Cr (\( 3d^5 4s^1 \)) \(\rightarrow \text{Cr}^{+}\) (\( 3d^5 \)).
7.The element with the electronic configuration \( [\text{Rn}] 5f^{14} 6d^1 7s^2 \) belongs to which block?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: D) f-block
Explanation: This is Lawrencium (\( Z=103 \)). According to IUPAC/NCERT, it completes the Actinide series, making it an f-block element, despite the last electron theoretically entering the 6d orbital.
8.An element has the outer electronic configuration \( 3s^2 3p^4 \). Which group does it belong to?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) Group 16
Explanation: For p-block elements, Group = 10 + valence electrons (s + p). Valence electrons = 2 + 4 = 6. Group = 10 + 6 = 16 (Sulfur family).
9.What is the period number of the element with the configuration \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^{10} 4s^2 4p^3 \)?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 4
Explanation: The period number is determined by the highest principal quantum number (n) of the outermost shell. The highest 'n' here is 4.
10.The total number of unpaired electrons in \( \text{Fe}^{2+} \) (\( Z=26 \)) is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: A) 4
Explanation: Fe is \( 3d^6 4s^2 \). \( \text{Fe}^{2+} \) is \( 3d^6 \). Filling 5 d-orbitals with 6 electrons gives 1 paired and 4 unpaired electrons according to Hund's Rule.
11.Which of the following represents the general electronic configuration of Halogens?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) \( ns^2 np^5 \)
Explanation: Halogens (Group 17) have 7 valence electrons, distributed as 2 in the s-subshell and 5 in the p-subshell.
12.What is the atomic number of the element whose outermost configuration is \( 4s^1 \) and lies in Group 1?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 19
Explanation: Full configuration: \( 1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6 4s^1 \). Adding electrons: 2+2+6+2+6+1 = 19 (Potassium).
13.Gadolinium (\( Z=64 \)) shows extra stability. Its anomalous configuration is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) \( [\text{Xe}] 4f^7 5d^1 6s^2 \)
Explanation: To achieve a stable, exactly half-filled f-subshell (\( f^7 \)), one electron enters the 5d orbital instead of the 4f orbital.
14.The element with the configuration \( 1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6 4s^2 \) belongs to the family of:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) Alkaline earth metals
Explanation: Elements with general outer configuration \( ns^2 \) belong to Group 2, the Alkaline Earth Metals (This is Calcium, Z=20).
15.How many electrons are present in the 3d-subshell of a \( \text{Zn}^{2+} \) ion (\( Z=30 \))?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 10
Explanation: Zinc ground state: \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^{10} 4s^2 \). When it forms \( \text{Zn}^{2+} \), it loses the 4s electrons. The 3d subshell retains all 10 electrons.
16.Which of the following elements has the maximum number of unpaired electrons in its ground state?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) Cr (\( Z=24 \))
Explanation: Chromium (\( 3d^5 4s^1 \)) has 5 unpaired in 3d and 1 in 4s, totaling 6 unpaired electrons.
17.Identify the group of isoelectronic species among the following:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) Both A and B
Explanation: Isoelectronic species have the same number of electrons. Group A (\( \text{N}^{3-}, \text{O}^{2-}, \text{F}^- \)) and Group B (\( \text{Na}^+, \text{Mg}^{2+}, \text{Al}^{3+} \)) all have exactly 10 electrons.
18.What is the predicted outer electronic configuration of Unnilennium (\( Z=109 \))?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: A) \( 5f^{14} 6d^7 7s^2 \)
Explanation: Element 109 falls in Group 9 of the d-block. It follows the configuration pattern of that group in the 7th period.
19.The configuration \( [\text{Kr}] 4d^{10} 5s^1 \) represents which element?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) Silver
Explanation: This is the anomalous configuration of Silver (Ag, \( Z=47 \)). An electron shifts from 5s to 4d to achieve a stable fully-filled \( d^{10} \) configuration.
20.An element with the valence shell configuration \( nd^2 ns^2 \) belongs to:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) Group 4
Explanation: For d-block elements, the group number is the sum of (n-1)d and ns electrons. 2+2 = 4 (e.g., Titanium).
21.According to the (n+l) rule, the 4s orbital is filled before the 3d orbital because:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 4s has lower energy than 3d
Explanation: Aufbau principle: For 4s, (n+l) = 4+0 = 4. For 3d, (n+l) = 3+2 = 5. Since 4 < 5, 4s is lower in energy.
22.If an element has atomic number 35, how many electrons does its valence p-orbital contain?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) 5
Explanation: Atomic number 35 is Bromine: \( [\text{Ar}] 3d^{10} 4s^2 4p^5 \). The valence p-orbital (4p) contains 5 electrons.
23.Noble gases (excluding Helium) are characterized by the general outer electronic configuration:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: C) \( ns^2 np^6 \)
Explanation: Noble gases possess a completely filled outermost shell (stable octet), configuration \( ns^2 np^6 \).
24.The element with electronic configuration \( 1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^3 \) is classified generally as a:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) Non-metal
Explanation: Atomic number 15 (Phosphorus). With 5 valence electrons, it falls on the right side of the periodic table, classifying it as a non-metal.
25.Zinc (\( Z=30 \)) is excluded from the family of true transition elements because:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) It has completely filled d-orbitals in its ground state.
Explanation: A transition element must have incompletely filled d-orbitals in its ground state or stable oxidation states. Zinc (\( 3d^{10} \)) does not.
26.What is the atomic number of the element placed immediately below the element with \( Z=15 \) in the periodic table?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 33
Explanation: Z=15 is Period 3. To find the element below it in Period 4, add 18 (due to the d-block elements inserted). 15 + 18 = 33 (Arsenic).
27.Identify the INCORRECT statement regarding electronic configurations:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) Fully filled orbitals have min exchange energy.
Explanation: Fully filled and half-filled orbitals actually have maximum exchange energy, which contributes to their high stability.
28.The outermost electronic configuration of the most electronegative element (Fluorine) is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: A) \( 2s^2 2p^5 \)
Explanation: Fluorine (Z=9) has the configuration \( 1s^2 2s^2 2p^5 \). Its outermost shell (n=2) is \( 2s^2 2p^5 \).
29.How many electrons can be accommodated in the subshell characterized by \( n=4, l=3 \)?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) 14
Explanation: \( l=3 \) is the f-subshell. Number of orbitals = 2l+1 = 7. Each holds 2 electrons. Max electrons = 7 x 2 = 14.
30.The element Oganesson (\( Z=118 \)) is predicted to have the outer electronic configuration:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: B) \( 7s^2 7p^6 \)
Explanation: Oganesson is a noble gas at the end of the 7th period. It completes the 7th shell p-block, yielding the stable \( 7s^2 7p^6 \) configuration.
