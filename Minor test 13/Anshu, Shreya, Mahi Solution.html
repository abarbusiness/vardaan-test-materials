28. Describe the structure and functioning of nephrons in brief. [3 Marks]

Structure: Nephron is the basic filtration unit of the kidney. It consists of a cup-shaped structure called Bowman's capsule containing a cluster of capillaries called Glomerulus. The Bowman's capsule leads into a long tubular structure which eventually joins a collecting duct. 1.5M

Functioning:

  • Filtration: Blood enters the glomerulus under high pressure. Water and small solutes (glucose, amino acids, salts, urea) are filtered into Bowman's capsule.
  • Reabsorption: As the filtrate passes along the tubular part, useful substances like glucose, amino acids, salts, and a major amount of water are selectively reabsorbed into surrounding capillaries.
  • Urine Formation: The remaining liquid containing urea, excess salts, and water forms urine, which enters the collecting duct. 1.5M
29. Explain the mechanism of breathing in humans. [3 Marks]

Breathing involves two steps:

  • Inhalation (Inspiration): When we breathe in, our ribs are lifted up and outwards, and the diaphragm flattens. As a result, the chest cavity becomes larger. This decreases the air pressure inside the lungs, and air is sucked into the lungs, filling the expanded alveoli. 1.5M
  • Exhalation (Expiration): When we breathe out, the ribs move downwards and inwards, and the diaphragm relaxes and arches upwards. The chest cavity becomes smaller. This increases the pressure inside the lungs, and the air is pushed out of the lungs. 1.5M
30. Why is it necessary to separate oxygenated and deoxygenated blood in mammals and birds? [3 Marks]

Mammals and birds are warm-blooded (endothermic) animals, meaning they constantly need energy to maintain their body temperature irrespective of the environment.

Separation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood:

  1. Prevents the mixing of oxygen-rich blood with oxygen-poor blood. 1M
  2. Allows a highly efficient supply of oxygen to the body cells. 1M
  3. This high efficiency of oxygen supply is essential to meet their high energy demands for cellular respiration to generate heat and maintain constant body temperature. 1M

31. (a) Human heart diagram. (b) Double circulation. [5 Marks]

(a) Diagram of Human Heart: (Examiner notes: Award 3 marks for a neat diagram with at least 4 correct labellings such as Left/Right Atrium, Left/Right Ventricle, Aorta, Vena Cava, Pulmonary Artery/Vein, Septum, Valves.) 3M

(b) Double Circulation: Blood passes through the human heart twice during one complete cycle through the body. This is called double circulation. It includes:

  • Systemic Circulation: Oxygenated blood from the left ventricle is pumped to all body parts, and deoxygenated blood returns to the right atrium.
  • Pulmonary Circulation: Deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle is pumped to the lungs for oxygenation, and oxygenated blood returns to the left atrium.

Importance: It ensures strict separation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood, ensuring highly efficient oxygen supply required by warm-blooded animals. 2M
