3 Marks

26. Define Fertilization. Distinguish between Internal and External fertilization.

Fertilization: The fusion of the male gamete (sperm) with the female gamete (egg/ovum) is called fertilization.
Distinction: Internal fertilization occurs inside the female body (e.g., Humans, Cows). External fertilization occurs outside the female body, usually in water (e.g., Fish, Frogs).

3 Marks

27. List three physiological changes that occur in the human body during the onset of puberty.

1. Rapid increase in height: Sudden growth spurt in bones.
2. Voice change: Deepening of voice in boys due to vocal cord growth.
3. Secondary sexual characteristics: Growth of facial/pubic hair, widening of shoulders (boys) or hips (girls).

3 Marks

28. Differentiate between a Zygote and a Foetus.

Zygote: The single-celled structure formed immediately after the fusion of sperm and egg.
Foetus: The stage of the embryo development where all body parts (hands, legs, head) are distinct and identifiable, occurring later in pregnancy.

3 Marks

29. Explain the functions of the following: (a) Testis (b) Ovary (c) Uterus.

(a) Testis: Produces male gametes (sperms) and male hormones like testosterone.
(b) Ovary: Produces female gametes (ova/eggs) and female hormones.
(c) Uterus: The site where the embryo implants and develops into a foetus until birth.

3 Marks

30. Why is reproduction essential for the continuation of a species?

Reproduction is the process by which living organisms produce new individuals of their own kind. Without reproduction, organisms would die, and eventually, their species would go extinct. It ensures the continuity of life on Earth across generations.
