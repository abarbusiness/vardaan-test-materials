SECTION B - Solutions (15 Marks)
26. State any three main differences between Monocotyledons (Monocots) and Dicotyledons (Dicots).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
(1 mark for each correct difference)
Feature Monocotyledons (Monocots) Dicotyledons (Dicots)
Seed Contain only one cotyledon (seed leaf). Contain two cotyledons (seed leaves).
Venation Leaves have parallel venation. Leaves have reticulate (net-like) venation.
Root System Possess a fibrous root system. Possess a tap root system.
27. What are amphibians? Give two common examples of animals belonging to this class.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Amphibians: Amphibians are cold-blooded vertebrates that are adapted to live both on land and in water. They typically breathe through gills in their larval stage (in water) and through lungs and moist skin in their adult stage (on land). (2 Marks for definition)

Examples: Frog, Toad, Salamander (Any two). (1 Mark for examples)
28. Define vertebrates and invertebrates. Provide one example for each category.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Vertebrates: Animals that possess a well-defined vertebral column (backbone) and an internal skeleton are called vertebrates.
Example: Human, Dog, Fish, Bird (Any one). (1.5 Marks)

Invertebrates: Animals that do NOT possess a vertebral column (backbone) are called invertebrates.
Example: Earthworm, Snail, Butterfly, Sponge (Any one). (1.5 Marks)
29. State any three defining characteristics of the Phylum Arthropoda. Name two animals that belong to this phylum.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Characteristics (Any three) - (2 Marks):
  1. They have jointed appendages (legs).
  2. Their body is segmented, typically divided into head, thorax, and abdomen.
  3. They have a hard, outer exoskeleton made of chitin.
  4. They are the largest phylum in the animal kingdom.
Examples - (1 Mark): Butterfly, Spider, Crab, Mosquito, Cockroach (Any two).
30. Differentiate between Gymnosperms and Angiosperms based on any three points.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
(1 mark for each correct difference)
Feature Gymnosperms Angiosperms
Seeds Bear naked seeds (not enclosed inside a fruit). Bear seeds enclosed within a fruit.
Flowers They are non-flowering plants (produce cones instead). They are flowering plants.
Examples Pine, Cycas, Cedar, Fir. Mango, Rose, Wheat, Sunflower.
