SECTION B SOLUTIONS (15 Marks)
26.
Name any three main components of food. Why is roughage (dietary fiber) necessary for our body even though it does not provide nutrients?
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Main components of food (Any three) [1.5 Marks]: Carbohydrates, Proteins, Fats, Vitamins, Minerals.

Importance of Roughage [1.5 Marks]: Roughage (dietary fiber) is essential because it helps our body get rid of undigested food. It adds bulk to the stool, preventing constipation and aiding in the smooth movement of food through the digestive tract.

27.
Differentiate between a taproot and fibrous roots. Give one example of a plant for each type.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
  • Taproot [1 Mark]: In a taproot system, there is one main, thick root that grows straight downwards, and smaller lateral roots branch off from it.
    Example [0.5 Mark]: Carrot, Radish, or Mango.
  • Fibrous Roots [1 Mark]: In a fibrous root system, there is no single main root. Instead, a cluster of similar-sized, thin roots emerges directly from the base of the stem and spreads out in the soil.
    Example [0.5 Mark]: Grass, Wheat, or Maize.
28.
What is a ball and socket joint? Explain briefly how it allows movement, and state where it is found in the human body.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Definition [1 Mark]: A ball and socket joint is a type of freely movable joint where the rounded end (ball) of one bone fits securely into the hollow space (socket) of another bone.

Movement [1 Mark]: Because of this unique structure, it allows for movement in all directions (360 degrees).

Location [1 Mark]: It is found in the shoulder joints and the hip joints of the human body.

29.
List any three major characteristics that separate living organisms from non-living things. Explain one of them briefly.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Characteristics of living organisms (Any three) [1.5 Marks]:

  • They need food (Nutrition).
  • They show growth.
  • They respire (Breathe/Release energy).
  • They respond to stimuli.
  • They reproduce their own kind.

Explanation of one characteristic [1.5 Marks] (Example - Respiration): Respiration is the process by which living organisms take in oxygen to break down food and release the energy needed to survive. Non-living things do not breathe or require energy in this way.

30.
Explain the process of photosynthesis in plants. Write down the names of the essential raw materials required for this process.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Process of Photosynthesis [1.5 Marks]: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their own food. Leaves use sunlight to convert carbon dioxide and water into food (glucose) and release oxygen as a byproduct. The green pigment called chlorophyll helps capture the sunlight energy.

Essential Raw Materials [1.5 Marks]:

  • Sunlight
  • Chlorophyll (present in leaves)
  • Carbon dioxide (from the air)
  • Water (from the soil)
