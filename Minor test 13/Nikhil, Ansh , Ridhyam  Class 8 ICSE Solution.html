SECTION B : SUBJECTIVE QUESTIONS (15 Marks)
26. (a) What is a neuron? (b) Name the three main parts of a neuron and write one function of each.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Neuron: A neuron (or nerve cell) is the basic structural and functional unit of the nervous system. It is specialized to transmit messages (nerve impulses) across the body. [2 Marks]
(b) Three main parts of a neuron and their functions: [3 Marks (1 each)]
  • Cyton (Cell Body): It is the main part of the cell containing the nucleus. Function: It controls the life processes of the cell and directs the impulses towards the axon.
  • Dendrites: Short, highly branched fibres arising from the cyton. Function: They receive nerve impulses from other neurons or receptors and carry them towards the cyton.
  • Axon: A single, long tail-like extension. Function: It conducts nerve impulses away from the cyton to the next neuron or an effector organ (muscle/gland).
27. Differentiate between the following pairs (give at least two differences for each):
(a) Cerebrum and Cerebellum
(b) Sensory nerve and Motor nerve
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Difference between Cerebrum and Cerebellum: [2.5 Marks]
Cerebrum Cerebellum
1. It is the largest part of the brain. 1. It is a smaller part, located at the back of the head under the cerebrum.
2. It controls intelligence, memory, thinking, and voluntary actions. 2. It controls the balance, posture, and coordination of muscular movements.
(b) Difference between Sensory nerve and Motor nerve: [2.5 Marks]
Sensory Nerve Motor Nerve
1. Made up of sensory neurons. 1. Made up of motor neurons.
2. Carries nerve impulses from the sense organs (receptors) to the Central Nervous System (brain/spinal cord). 2. Carries instructions from the Central Nervous System (brain/spinal cord) to the effector organs (muscles/glands).
28. (a) Define 'Reflex Action'. Give any two examples of reflex action from your daily life.
(b) Name the protective coverings of the brain. What fluid is present between them and what is its main function?
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Reflex Action: [1 Mark]
It is a quick, automatic, involuntary, and sudden response of the body to a stimulus, without the involvement of the brain's conscious thought. (It is mostly controlled by the spinal cord).
Examples: [2 Marks]
  1. Immediate withdrawal of the hand when it touches a hot object.
  2. Watering of the mouth on seeing or smelling delicious food.
  3. Blinking of eyes when dust particles enter.
(Any two correct examples are acceptable)
(b) Protective Coverings and Fluid: [2 Marks]
  • Coverings: The brain is protected by three membranous coverings called Meninges.
  • Fluid: The space between the meninges is filled with Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF).
  • Function: It acts as a shock absorber and protects the brain from mechanical shocks and jerks. It also provides nutrients to the brain tissues.
