SECTION B: Short Answer Type (15 Marks)
Q26. (a) Define communicable diseases. (b) Give two examples. (c) State one direct method of disease transmission.
[1+1+1 = 3 Marks]
Ans:
  • (a) Definition (1 Mark): Diseases that can spread from an infected person to a healthy person through air, water, food, or physical contact are called communicable diseases.
  • (b) Examples (1 Mark): Common cold, Cholera, Tuberculosis, Covid-19, Chickenpox (Any two valid examples earn 1/2 mark each).
  • (c) Direct Method (1 Mark): Direct physical contact with an infected person (e.g., shaking hands, hugging) or direct exchange of body fluids.
Q27. Differentiate between living in a clean environment and a polluted environment by giving any three points.
[1×3 = 3 Marks]
Ans: (Any three valid points fetch 1 mark each)
Clean Environment Polluted Environment
1. Fresh air with a good Air Quality Index (AQI). 1. Smog and toxic gases leading to poor AQI.
2. Access to safe, uncontaminated drinking water. 2. Contaminated water sources causing diseases like cholera.
3. Proper waste disposal, reducing breeding of vectors. 3. Garbage accumulation causing mosquito/fly breeding.
4. Promotes better physical and mental health. 4. Increases the risk of respiratory and communicable diseases.
Q28. What is immunity? Explain briefly how vaccination helps our immune system fight off diseases.
[1+2 = 3 Marks]
Ans:

Immunity (1 Mark): It is the natural ability or self-defense mechanism of the body to resist, fight, and overcome infections or diseases caused by pathogens.

Role of Vaccination (2 Marks): A vaccine contains dead or weakened (attenuated) microbes of a specific disease. When introduced into a healthy body, the body's immune system reacts by producing special proteins called antibodies to fight these harmless invaders.

The immune system "remembers" this pathogen. If the actual, dangerous microbe enters the body in the future, the pre-formed antibodies quickly recognize and destroy it before it can cause the disease. This is how vaccination provides acquired immunity.

Q29. (a) Name the pathogen that causes Malaria. (b) What is a disease vector? (c) Name the specific vector responsible for transmitting Malaria.
[1+1+1 = 3 Marks]
Ans:
  • (a) Pathogen (1 Mark): Malaria is caused by a Protozoa (specifically Plasmodium).
  • (b) Vector Definition (1 Mark): A vector is an organism (usually an insect or animal) that acts as a carrier, transmitting disease-causing pathogens from an infected person or environment to a healthy individual.
  • (c) Malaria Vector (1 Mark): The Female Anopheles mosquito.
Q30. List any three general habits of a healthy lifestyle that help in the prevention of diseases.
[1×3 = 3 Marks]
Ans: (Any three valid points fetch 1 mark each)
  • 1. Personal Hygiene: Taking daily baths, washing hands frequently with soap (especially before meals and after using the washroom), and brushing teeth twice a day.
  • 2. Balanced Diet: Eating nutritious, freshly cooked food that contains all essential vitamins and minerals, and avoiding excessive junk food.
  • 3. Safe Drinking Water: Boiling or filtering drinking water to prevent water-borne diseases.
  • 4. Regular Exercise & Rest: Doing daily physical exercise or yoga and ensuring 7-8 hours of sound sleep for proper bodily function and mental peace.
  • 5. Clean Surroundings: Ensuring no stagnant water around the house to prevent mosquito breeding and proper disposal of household garbage.
