VARDAAN LEARNING INSTITUTE

MINOR TEST 13: MOTION IN TWO DIMENSIONS
Class: 11 Subject: Physics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly. Use of a non-programmable calculator is permitted. Take \( g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2 \) unless specified otherwise.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
A boat has a velocity of \( 5\text{ m/s} \) in still water. It crosses a river of width \( 200\text{ m} \) flowing at \( 3\text{ m/s} \) along the shortest possible path. Find the time taken to cross the river.
2.
A bullet is dropped from a certain height. Simultaneously, another bullet is fired horizontally from exactly the same height. Which one will hit the ground first and why? (Neglect air resistance).
3.
Two projectiles are thrown with the same initial velocity at angles \( (45^\circ - \theta) \) and \( (45^\circ + \theta) \) with the horizontal. What is the ratio of their horizontal ranges? Show your reasoning.
4.
Rain is falling vertically downwards with a speed of \( 30\text{ m/s} \). A woman rides a bicycle with a speed of \( 10\text{ m/s} \) in the east to west direction. What is the magnitude and direction of the relative velocity of the rain with respect to the woman?
5.
A ball is thrown at an angle of \( 30^\circ \) to the horizontal. Calculate the ratio of the kinetic energy of the ball at the highest point of its trajectory to its initial kinetic energy.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A man can swim at \( 4\text{ km/h} \) in still water. How long does he take to cross a river \( 1\text{ km} \) wide if the river flows steadily at \( 3\text{ km/h} \) and he makes his strokes strictly normal to the river current? How far down the river does he go when he reaches the other bank?
7.
An airplane is flying horizontally at a height of \( 490\text{ m} \) with a velocity of \( 360\text{ km/h} \). A food packet is released from it. Calculate the time taken by the packet to reach the ground and its horizontal range. (Take \( g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2 \)).
8.
The equation of trajectory of a projectile is given by \( y = \sqrt{3}x - \frac{g x^2}{2} \). Calculate the angle of projection and the initial velocity of the projectile.
9.
An aircraft is flying at a speed of \( 400\text{ km/h} \) due north. A strong wind starts blowing at \( 100\text{ km/h} \) from east to west. Find the magnitude and direction of the resultant velocity of the aircraft.
10.
Mathematically prove that the maximum horizontal range is exactly four times the maximum height attained by a projectile when it is fired at an angle that provides the maximum horizontal range.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A projectile is fired from the top of a \( 40\text{ m} \) high tower with an initial velocity of \( 20\text{ m/s} \) at an angle of \( 30^\circ \) above the horizontal. Find:
(a) The total time taken by the projectile to hit the ground.
(b) The horizontal distance from the base of the tower where it hits the ground.
(c) The magnitude of the velocity with which it strikes the ground.
(Take \( g = 10\text{ m/s}^2 \))
12.
A river is flowing due east with a steady speed of \( 3\text{ m/s} \). A swimmer can swim in still water at a speed of \( 4\text{ m/s} \).
(a) If he wants to cross the river along the shortest possible path, at what angle should he swim with respect to the river flow?
(b) What is his resultant velocity relative to the ground in this case?
(c) If the river is \( 100\text{ m} \) wide, find the time taken to cross the river along the shortest path.
(d) If he decides to cross in the shortest possible time instead, what will be his horizontal drift downstream?
13.
Based on the principles of projectile motion:
(a) Prove that there are two angles of projection for a given initial velocity for which the horizontal range is the same.
(b) A body is projected with a velocity \( u \) such that its horizontal range is \( R \). If \( t_1 \) and \( t_2 \) are the times of flight for the two possible angles of projection to get this exact same range, derive the relation: \( t_1 t_2 = \frac{2R}{g} \).
