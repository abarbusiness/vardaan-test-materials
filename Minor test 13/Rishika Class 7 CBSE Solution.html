SECTION B (Subjective Questions)
26.
What is the function of platelets in the blood? Why is this function important? [2]
Ans:
  • Function (1 Mark): Platelets are responsible for the clotting of blood whenever there is an injury or a cut on the body.
  • Importance (1 Mark): If platelets were absent, bleeding would not stop during an injury, leading to severe blood loss, which can be fatal. Clotting plugs the cut and prevents excess blood loss.
27.
Why is the transport of materials (like food, water, and oxygen) necessary in a plant or an animal? Give two basic reasons. [2]
Ans:
Transport of materials is necessary because:
  1. To provide energy and growth (1 Mark): Every living cell requires a continuous supply of essential substances like food (glucose), water, and oxygen ($O_2$) to survive, perform life functions, and release energy.
  2. To remove toxic waste (1 Mark): Cells constantly produce harmful waste products (like Carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) and urea) during their functioning. A transport system is required to carry these wastes away from the cells to excretory organs for removal.
28.
State three main differences between arteries and veins. [3]
Ans:
(3 Marks - 1 mark for each valid difference):
Parameter Arteries Veins
Direction of Flow Carry blood away from the heart to body parts. Carry blood towards the heart from body parts.
Type of Blood Generally carry oxygen-rich blood (except pulmonary artery). Generally carry carbon dioxide-rich blood (except pulmonary vein).
Wall Thickness & Valves Have thick, elastic walls to handle high pressure. No valves present. Have thin walls as pressure is low. Valves are present to prevent backflow.
29.
Name the four major organs of the human excretory system in their correct sequence. What is the specific role of the kidneys in this system? [3]
Ans:
Sequence of Organs (2 Marks - 0.5 each):
The correct pathway for the flow of urine is:
$$ \text{Kidneys} \xrightarrow{\text{filtered into}} \text{Ureters} \xrightarrow{\text{stored in}} \text{Urinary Bladder} \xrightarrow{\text{excreted via}} \text{Urethra} $$
Role of Kidneys (1 Mark):
Kidneys contain millions of tiny blood capillaries that act as filters. When blood reaches the kidneys, useful substances are absorbed back into the blood, while toxic wastes dissolved in water are filtered out as urine.
30.
(a) What is transpiration? (1 mark)
(b) How does transpiration help tall plants and trees? (2 marks)
(c) Why do plants need root hairs, and how do they assist in the transportation process? (2 marks) [5]
Ans:
(a) Transpiration (1 Mark):
The process by which plants lose water in the form of water vapour through the stomata present on the surface of their leaves is called transpiration.

(b) Helpfulness to Tall Plants (2 Marks):
  • Creates Suction Pull (1 Mark): As water evaporates from the leaves, it creates a vacuum or suction pull (transpirational pull). This force is strong enough to pull water and dissolved minerals upwards from the roots to great heights in tall trees.
  • Cooling Effect (1 Mark): Evaporation of water cools the plant body, protecting it from the extreme heat of the sun.

(c) Role of Root Hairs (2 Marks):
  • Plants need root hairs to vastly increase the surface area (1 Mark) of the roots.
  • Assistance in Transportation (1 Mark): A larger surface area allows roots to absorb maximum amounts of water and dissolved mineral salts from the surrounding soil efficiently, which are then passed into the xylem vessels for upward transport.
