Q26.
Name the organs of the human respiratory system in the correct sequence, starting from the nose.
[3]
Answer:
The organs of the human respiratory system in the correct sequence are:
Nose (Nostrils/Nasal cavity) → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli (Lungs).
(Marking Scheme: 0.5 marks for each correct organ in the sequence.)
Q27.
Why is it advised to breathe through the nose rather than the mouth? Give any two reasons.
[3]
Answer:
It is advised to breathe through the nose because:
1. Filtration: The fine hair and mucus present inside the nasal cavity trap dust particles and germs, preventing them from entering the lungs. [1.5 Marks]
2. Warming and Moistening: The inner lining of the nose warms and moistens the inhaled air, making it suitable for the delicate lungs. Breathing through the mouth bypasses these protective mechanisms. [1.5 Marks]
Q28.
What is the function of the rib cage in the respiratory system? How does it help during breathing?
[3]
Answer:
Function: The primary function of the rib cage is to protect the delicate respiratory organs, specifically the lungs, from physical injury. [1.5 Marks]

Role during breathing: The rib cage expands outwards and upwards during inhalation to increase the space in the chest cavity (allowing lungs to fill with air). During exhalation, it moves inwards and downwards, helping to push the air out. [1.5 Marks]
Q29.
State what happens to the diaphragm during inhalation and exhalation.
[3]
Answer:
  • During Inhalation: The diaphragm muscles contract, and it moves downwards (flattens). This increases the volume of the chest cavity, allowing air to rush into the lungs. [1.5 Marks]
  • During Exhalation: The diaphragm muscles relax, and it moves upwards back to its original dome shape. This decreases the volume of the chest cavity, pushing air out of the lungs. [1.5 Marks]
Q30.
Define cellular respiration. Write the word equation to show what happens during this process.
[3]
Answer:
Definition: Cellular respiration is the chemical process by which food (glucose) is broken down in the cells of the body with the help of oxygen to release energy, along with carbon dioxide and water as by-products. [1.5 Marks]

Word Equation: [1.5 Marks]
$$ \text{Glucose} + \text{Oxygen} \xrightarrow{} \text{Carbon dioxide} + \text{Water} + \text{Energy} $$
