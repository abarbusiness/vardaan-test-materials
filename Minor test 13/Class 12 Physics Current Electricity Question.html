Minor Test 13 - Current Electricity
Class: 12 CBSE Subject: Physics Time: 2 Hours Max. Marks: 50
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A consists of 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B consists of 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C consists of 5 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Use of log tables is permitted. Calculators are strictly prohibited.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
A cylindrical metallic wire of resistance \(R\) is stretched to uniformly double its length. What will be its new resistance and resistivity? Justify your answer.
2.
Plot a graph showing the variation of resistivity (\(\rho\)) with temperature (\(T\)) for (i) a typical metal (like Copper) and (ii) a typical semiconductor (like Silicon).
3.
Define mobility of charge carriers in a conductor. Write its S.I. unit.
4.
A cell of emf '\(E\)' and internal resistance '\(r\)' is connected across a variable external resistance '\(R\)'. Plot a graph showing the variation of terminal potential difference '\(V\)' with '\(R\)'.
5.
State the microscopic form of Ohm's law (vector form) relating current density (\(\vec{J}\)), conductivity (\(\sigma\)), and electric field (\(\vec{E}\)). Show briefly how it connects to the macroscopic form \(V = IR\).
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Define relaxation time of the free electrons drifting in a conductor. How is it related to the drift velocity of free electrons? Deduce the expression for drift velocity \(v_d = -\frac{eE}{m}\tau\).
7.
Two identical cells, whether joined in series or in parallel, give the same current when connected to an external resistance of \(2 \, \Omega\). Find the internal resistance of each cell.
8.
State Kirchhoff's rules for electrical networks. Name the fundamental conservation principle on which each rule is based.
9.
Draw a schematic diagram of a Wheatstone bridge. Using Kirchhoff's laws, derive the condition for a balanced Wheatstone bridge.
10.
A nichrome heating element connected to a \(220 \text{ V}\) supply draws an initial current of \(3.2 \text{ A}\) which settles after a few seconds to a steady value of \(2.8 \text{ A}\). What is the steady temperature of the heating element if the room temperature is \(27^\circ \text{C}\)?
(Temperature coefficient of resistance of nichrome averaged over the temperature range involved is \(1.7 \times 10^{-4} {}^{\circ}\text{C}^{-1}\))
SECTION C (25 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
State Kirchhoff's laws. Use these laws to calculate the current flowing through each branch of the electrical network shown in the figure below.
12.
(a) Establish a relation between electric current '\(I\)' and drift velocity '\(v_d\)' of electrons in a conductor of cross-section '\(A\)'. [3]
(b) How does the drift velocity of electrons in a metallic conductor change if the length of the conductor is doubled while keeping the applied potential difference constant? Justify. [2]
13.
(a) For the network shown below, identify the type of circuit and calculate the equivalent resistance between the terminals A and B. [3]
(b) Calculate the total current drawn by the circuit from the \(9 \text{ V}\) battery. [1]
(c) Why is the Wheatstone bridge method considered a 'null method' and preferred over using a voltmeter/ammeter to measure unknown resistance? [1]
14.
(a) Derive the expression for the equivalent emf (\(E_{eq}\)) and equivalent internal resistance (\(r_{eq}\)) when two cells of emfs \(E_1\) and \(E_2\) and internal resistances \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) are connected in parallel. [3]
(b) Two cells of emfs \(1.5 \text{ V}\) and \(2.0 \text{ V}\) having internal resistances \(0.2 \, \Omega\) and \(0.3 \, \Omega\) respectively are connected in parallel. Calculate the emf and internal resistance of the equivalent cell. [2]
15.
(a) Define electrical conductivity. Write its relationship with resistivity. [1]
(b) Two bulbs are rated \(P_1\) at \(V\) volts and \(P_2\) at \(V\) volts. If they are connected in series across a supply voltage \(V\), prove that the total power consumed \(P\) is given by \(\frac{1}{P} = \frac{1}{P_1} + \frac{1}{P_2}\). [2]
(c) Which bulb will glow brighter in the series combination described in part (b) and why? [2]
