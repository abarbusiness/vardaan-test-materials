SECTION B (15 Marks) - Subjective
Q4. (a) Definitions & (b) Diagram of Malpighian capsule
[2+3 = 5M]
Ans:
(a) Definitions (1 Mark each):
  • (i) Excretion: It is the biological process by which harmful, useless metabolic waste products (like urea, uric acid) are removed from the body.
  • (ii) Osmoregulation: It is the process of regulating the osmotic pressure of the blood and body fluids by maintaining the correct balance of water and dissolved salts.
(b) Diagram & Function (2 Marks for Diagram + 1 Mark for Function):
[Teacher's Note: Students must draw a cup-shaped structure (Bowman's Capsule) enclosing a knot of capillaries (Glomerulus). The wider Afferent arteriole entering and narrower Efferent arteriole leaving must be clearly labelled.]

Function: The Malpighian capsule is the site for Ultrafiltration. Blood flows under high pressure through the glomerulus, forcing liquid (water, glucose, urea, salts) into the Bowman's capsule, creating the glomerular filtrate.
Q5. Differentiate between the following:
[5M]
Ans:
(Marks: 2M for (a), 2M for (b), 1M for (c))
Basis First Entity Second Entity
(a) Function Ureter: It carries urine from the kidneys to the urinary bladder. Urethra: It is a tube that carries urine from the urinary bladder to the outside of the body.
(b) Nitrogenous waste composition Renal Artery: Contains blood with a high concentration of urea (unfiltered blood). Renal Vein: Contains blood with a low concentration of urea (filtered blood).
(c) Location Renal Cortex: The outer, darker region of the kidney. Renal Medulla: The inner, lighter region of the kidney.
Q6. Give biological reasons:
[5M]
Ans:
(Marks: 2M for (a), 1.5M for (b), 1.5M for (c))
  • (a) Urine in summer is thicker (more concentrated): In summer, we lose a large amount of water from the body in the form of sweat (to regulate body temperature). To conserve water and maintain the osmotic balance of the blood (osmoregulation), the kidneys reabsorb more water from the glomerular filtrate. As a result, less water is eliminated, making the urine thicker and hypertonic.
  • (b) Efferent arteriole is narrower than afferent arteriole: The wider afferent arteriole brings more blood into the glomerulus, while the narrower efferent arteriole restricts its easy outflow. This difference in diameter creates a high hydrostatic pressure inside the glomerulus, which is essential for ultrafiltration to occur.
  • (c) Advice for Dialysis: A person is advised to undergo dialysis when they suffer from Renal Failure (Kidney failure). When both kidneys are damaged (due to infection, injury, or restricted blood flow) and cannot filter the blood effectively, toxic nitrogenous wastes like urea begin to accumulate in the body (uremia), which can be fatal. The dialysis machine acts as an artificial kidney to filter these wastes.
