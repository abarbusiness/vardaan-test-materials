SECTION B : SUBJECTIVE QUESTIONS (15 Marks)
26. Define an ecosystem. Name its two major components with one example for each.
[3 Marks]
Answer:
Ecosystem: An ecosystem is a self-sustaining structural and functional unit of the biosphere consisting of a community of living beings and the physical environment, both interacting and exchanging materials with each other.

Major Components:
  1. Biotic (Living) Components: e.g., Plants, Animals, Microorganisms.
  2. Abiotic (Non-living) Components: e.g., Sunlight, Water, Soil, Temperature.
[Marking Scheme: Definition: 1 Mark | Naming components: 1 Mark | Examples: 1 Mark]
27. What is a food web? Briefly explain how a food web is different from a food chain.
[3 Marks]
Answer:
Food Web: A complex network of many interconnected food chains existing in an ecosystem is called a food web.

Difference:
  • Food Chain: It is a simple, straight, linear pathway showing how energy is passed from one organism to another (e.g., Grass \(\rightarrow\) Grasshopper \(\rightarrow\) Frog).
  • Food Web: It consists of multiple interconnecting food chains, showing multiple alternative pathways for energy flow, making the ecosystem more stable.
[Marking Scheme: Definition of Food Web: 1.5 Marks | Clear difference/comparison: 1.5 Marks]
28. Name the primary male and primary female reproductive organs in human beings. What are the respective gametes produced by them?
[3 Marks]
Answer:
Primary Male Reproductive Organ: Testis (plural: Testes).
Male Gamete Produced: Sperm (Spermatozoa).

Primary Female Reproductive Organ: Ovary (plural: Ovaries).
Female Gamete Produced: Egg or Ovum.
[Marking Scheme: Male organ & gamete: 1.5 Marks | Female organ & gamete: 1.5 Marks]
29. Define the term fertilization. What is the product of fertilization called? Where exactly does this process occur in the human female body?
[3 Marks]
Answer:
Fertilization: The biological process of fusion of a male gamete (sperm) with a female gamete (egg/ovum) to form a single cell is called fertilization.
Product of Fertilization: The single fused cell formed is called a Zygote.
Site of Fertilization: In human females, fertilization naturally takes place inside the Fallopian tube (Oviduct).
[Marking Scheme: Definition: 1 Mark | Product (Zygote): 1 Mark | Site (Fallopian tube): 1 Mark]
30. What do you understand by the term 'puberty'? Mention any two physical changes that occur specifically in boys during puberty.
[3 Marks]
Answer:
Puberty: It is the stage in life when the reproductive organs reach maturity, begin to function, and secondary sexual characteristics develop, making the individual capable of reproduction.

Changes in Boys during Puberty (Any two):
  • Sudden increase in height and broadening of shoulders.
  • Deepening or cracking of the voice (due to enlargement of the voice box/Adam's apple).
  • Growth of facial hair (beard and moustaches) and body hair.
  • Development and maturation of reproductive organs (testes begin producing sperm).
[Marking Scheme: Definition of Puberty: 1 Mark | Any two correct changes in boys: 1 Mark each]
