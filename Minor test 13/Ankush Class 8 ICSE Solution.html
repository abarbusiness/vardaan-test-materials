Q1. Define Ecosystem & Components5 Marks

An ecosystem is a biological community of interacting organisms and their physical environment. Components:

  • Biotic: Living parts (e.g., Plants).
  • Abiotic: Non-living parts (e.g., Sunlight).
Q2. Food Chain vs Food Web5 Marks

Food chain: Single linear pathway of energy flow. Food web: Interconnected network of multiple food chains.

Q3. Producers & 10% Law5 Marks

Producers fix solar energy into chemical energy. The 10% law states that only 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next:
$$Energy_{next} = Energy_{previous} \times 0.10$$
