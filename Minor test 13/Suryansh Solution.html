SECTION B - Subjective Solutions (Short Answer)
Q26. What are stomata? Write down their primary function in plants.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
  • Definition: Stomata are tiny pores present mainly on the surface of the leaves of plants. These pores are surrounded by 'guard cells'. [1 Mark]
  • Function: Their primary function is the exchange of gases (taking in Carbon dioxide and releasing Oxygen during photosynthesis) and transpiration (loss of excess water vapour). [1 Mark]

[2 Marks]
Solution:
ArteriesVeins
1. Carry oxygen-rich blood from the heart to all parts of the body.1. Carry carbon dioxide-rich blood from all parts of the body back to the heart.

Q28. Define adolescence. Why is personal hygiene important during this stage?
[2 Marks]
Solution:
  • Adolescence: The period of life, when the body undergoes changes, leading to reproductive maturity, is called adolescence. It roughly begins at age 11 and lasts up to 18 or 19 years of age. [1 Mark]
  • Importance of Hygiene: During adolescence, the increased activity of sweat and sebaceous (oil) glands can make the body smell and lead to acne/pimples. Maintaining personal hygiene prevents bacterial infections and maintains skin health. [1 Mark]
SECTION C - Subjective Solutions (Long Answer)
Q29. Explain the process of photosynthesis. Write the balanced chemical equation representing this process.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Explanation: Photosynthesis is the vital life process by which green plants synthesize their own food (glucose) from carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll (the green pigment in leaves). Oxygen is released as a by-product. [1.5 Marks]

Chemical Equation:

$$ 6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 $$

(Carbon dioxide + Water $\rightarrow$ Carbohydrate/Glucose + Oxygen) [1.5 Marks]

Q30. List out three major physical changes that take place in the body of boys and girls during puberty.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Three major physical changes during puberty are:
  1. Increase in Height: The most conspicuous change during puberty is the sudden increase in height as the bones of the arms and legs elongate. [1 Mark]
  2. Change in Body Shape: In boys, shoulders broaden and the chest widens. In girls, the region below the waist becomes wider. [1 Mark]
  3. Voice Change and Hair Growth: The voice box (larynx) grows, resulting in a deeper voice in boys (Adam's apple becomes prominent), while girls develop a high-pitched voice. Hair grows in new areas like underarms and the pubic region in both sexes. [1 Mark]
Q31. Name the main organs of the human digestive system in the correct sequential order starting from the mouth. Briefly state the function of the stomach.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Main Organs in Order:
The alimentary canal consists of the following parts in sequential order:
Mouth (Buccal cavity) $\rightarrow$ Oesophagus (Food pipe) $\rightarrow$ Stomach $\rightarrow$ Small Intestine $\rightarrow$ Large Intestine $\rightarrow$ Rectum $\rightarrow$ Anus. [2 Marks]

Function of Stomach:
The stomach is a thick-walled, U-shaped bag. Its inner lining secretes mucous, hydrochloric acid, and digestive juices. The acid kills harmful bacteria that enter with food, and the digestive juices break down proteins into simpler substances. [1 Mark]
