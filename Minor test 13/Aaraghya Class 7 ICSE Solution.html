Q26. State three main characteristic features of Kingdom Monera. Give one example.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Characteristic features of Kingdom Monera (Any three):
  1. They are unicellular (single-celled) organisms.
  2. They are prokaryotic, meaning they lack a well-defined true nucleus; nuclear material is scattered in the cytoplasm.
  3. They lack membrane-bound cell organelles like mitochondria, chloroplasts, etc.
  4. Some are autotrophic (like blue-green algae) while most are heterotrophic.
Example: Bacteria (e.g., Lactobacillus) or Blue-green algae.
(Marking Scheme: 0.5 mark for each characteristic = 1.5M, 0.5 mark for prokaryotic term = 0.5M, 1 mark for correct example.)
Q27. Differentiate between Kingdom Plantae and Kingdom Animalia. (Write any three differences).
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Feature Kingdom Plantae Kingdom Animalia
Cell Wall Cells possess a rigid cell wall made of cellulose. Cells lack a cell wall; only a cell membrane is present.
Nutrition They are mostly autotrophic (make their own food via photosynthesis). They are heterotrophic (depend on others for food).
Locomotion They are stationary and rooted in one place. Most animals can move freely from one place to another.
(Marking Scheme: 1 mark for each valid difference correctly contrasting both sides. Total = 3 Marks.)
Q28. Why are Fungi not classified under Kingdom Plantae? State two scientific reasons. Name one example of a fungus.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
Reasons Fungi are excluded from Plantae:
  • Lack of Chlorophyll: Fungi do not possess chlorophyll and cannot perform photosynthesis. Therefore, their mode of nutrition is heterotrophic (saprophytic), whereas plants are autotrophic.
  • Cell Wall Composition: The cell wall of fungi is made of a tough complex sugar called chitin, unlike plants whose cell walls are primarily made of cellulose.
Example: Mushroom / Bread mould (Rhizopus) / Yeast.
(Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for lack of chlorophyll, 1 Mark for Chitin cell wall, 1 Mark for valid example.)
Q29. Name all the five kingdoms of classification in order. Write one distinct characteristic of Kingdom Protista.
[3 Marks]
Ans:
The Five Kingdoms (Proposed by R.H. Whittaker):
  1. Kingdom Monera
  2. Kingdom Protista
  3. Kingdom Fungi
  4. Kingdom Plantae
  5. Kingdom Animalia
Characteristic of Kingdom Protista: They are predominantly unicellular eukaryotic organisms (they have a single cell with a well-defined true nucleus).
(Marking Scheme: 2 Marks for correctly listing all 5 kingdoms, 1 Mark for the correct characteristic of Protista.)
Q30. What are 'Prokaryotes' and 'Eukaryotes'? Give one example of each from the Five Kingdom classification.
[3 Marks]
Ans:

Prokaryotes: These are primitive organisms whose cells do not have a well-defined nucleus. Their genetic material is not enclosed by a nuclear membrane.
Example: Bacteria (belongs to Kingdom Monera).

Eukaryotes: These are organisms that have advanced cells with a well-defined true nucleus enclosed in a nuclear membrane, along with membrane-bound organelles.
Example: Amoeba (Protista) OR Mango tree (Plantae) OR Human (Animalia).

(Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for correct definition of Prokaryote + 0.5 for example. 1 Mark for definition of Eukaryote + 0.5 for example. Total = 3 Marks.)
