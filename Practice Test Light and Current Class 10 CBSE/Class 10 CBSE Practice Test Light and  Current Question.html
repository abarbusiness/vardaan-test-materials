HOTS Major Test - Electricity & Light (Reflection/Refraction)
Class: 10 Subject: Physics Time: 150 Minutes (2.5 Hours) Max. Marks: 80
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • This paper consists of 22 questions focusing heavily on CBSE High Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) and complex numericals.
  • Section A: Questions 1 to 5 are 2 marks each.
  • Section B: Questions 6 to 13 are 3 marks each.
  • Section C: Questions 14 to 17 are 4 marks each.
  • Section D: Questions 18 to 22 are 6 marks each.
  • Show all working formulas, substitute values with proper SI units, and draw neat scientifically accurate diagrams where asked.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Short Answer Type I - 2 Marks Each)
1.
A wire of resistance $R$ is bent into the form of a closed circle. What is the equivalent resistance across any diameter of the circle? Justify your answer.
2.
If the angle of incidence for a light ray incident on a plane mirror is $45^\circ$, and the mirror is rotated by $10^\circ$ keeping the incident ray fixed, what is the new angle of reflection?
3.
An electric iron is rated at $1100 \text{ W}$, $220 \text{ V}$. If it is operated on $110 \text{ V}$, what will be the power consumed by it? (Assume resistance remains constant).
4.
A ray of light starting from air passes through medium A ($n = 1.50$), medium B ($n = 1.33$) and medium C ($n = 2.42$). If this ray emerges out in air from C, then for which pair of media is the bending of light maximum? Explain why.
5.
Two wires of equal length, one of copper and the other of manganin, have the same electrical resistance. Which wire is thicker? (Given: resistivity of manganin $\gt$ resistivity of copper).
SECTION B (24 Marks)(Short Answer Type II - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A copper wire has a diameter of $0.5 \text{ mm}$ and a resistivity of $1.6 \times 10^{-8} \Omega\cdot\text{m}$. What will be the length of this wire to make its resistance $10 \Omega$? How much does the resistance change if the diameter is doubled, keeping length constant?
7.
An object $3 \text{ cm}$ high is placed at a distance of $8 \text{ cm}$ from a concave mirror, which produces a virtual image $4.5 \text{ cm}$ high.
(i) What is the focal length of the mirror?
(ii) What is the position of the image?
8.
Calculate the number of electrons that would flow per second through the cross-section of a wire when $1 \text{ A}$ current flows in it. Also, calculate the work done in moving a charge of $5 \text{ C}$ across two points having a potential difference of $15 \text{ V}$.
9.
A student traces the path of a ray of light through a rectangular glass slab. Tabulating the observations, the student records:
Case 1: $i = 60^\circ$, $r = 40^\circ$
Case 2: $i = 40^\circ$, $r = 28^\circ$
Verify which observation is scientifically correct by calculating the refractive index of glass in both cases. (Given: $\sin 60^\circ = 0.866$, $\sin 40^\circ = 0.642$, $\sin 28^\circ = 0.469$).
10.
Two conducting wires of the same material, equal lengths, and equal diameters are first connected in series and then in parallel in a circuit across the same potential difference. Find the ratio of the heat produced in the series combination to that in the parallel combination.
11.
A concave lens has a focal length of $15 \text{ cm}$. At what distance should the object from the lens be placed so that it forms an image at $10 \text{ cm}$ from the lens? Also, find the magnification produced by the lens.
12.
If the length of a uniform wire is increased by $20\%$ by stretching it, find the percentage change in its resistance and state the change in its resistivity.
13.
A convex mirror used for rear-view on an automobile has a radius of curvature of $3.00 \text{ m}$. If a bus is located at $5.00 \text{ m}$ from this mirror, find the position, nature, and size of the image relative to the object.
SECTION C (16 Marks)(Long Answer Type I - 4 Marks Each)
14.
Examine the circuit diagram given below. Calculate:
(a) The total equivalent resistance of the circuit.
(b) The total current flowing in the circuit.
(c) The potential difference across the $4 \Omega$ resistor.
15.
An object is placed at a distance of $20 \text{ cm}$ from a concave mirror. A real image magnified three times is formed. Find:
(a) The focal length of the mirror.
(b) Where must the object be placed to give a virtual image three times the height of the object?
16.
A motor of $1100 \text{ W}$ runs for 5 hours daily for 6 days.
(a) Find the electrical energy consumed in kWh.
(b) Calculate the cost of the electrical energy consumed if the rate is ₹ 5.00 per unit.
(c) State the commercial unit of electrical energy and define its relation to Joules.
17.
When an object is placed at a distance of $50 \text{ cm}$ from a concave spherical mirror, the magnification produced is $-1/2$. Where should the object be placed to get a magnification of $-1/5$?
SECTION D (30 Marks)(Long Answer Type II - 6 Marks Each)
18.
(a) State Ohm's Law and write the conditions under which it strictly applies.
(b) Three resistors $R_1, R_2, \text{ and } R_3$ are connected in parallel. Derive the mathematical expression for their equivalent resistance.
(c) Explain why an ammeter is always connected in series and a voltmeter in parallel in a circuit. What happens if an ammeter is connected in parallel with the battery?
19.
An object of size $7.0 \text{ cm}$ is placed at $27 \text{ cm}$ in front of a concave mirror of focal length $18 \text{ cm}$.
(a) At what distance from the mirror should a screen be placed so that a sharp focused image can be obtained?
(b) Find the size and the nature of the image.
(c) Draw a ray diagram accurately depicting this image formation, clearly marking C, F, P, object, and image positions.
20.
(a) How many $176 \Omega$ resistors (in parallel) are required to carry $5 \text{ A}$ on a $220 \text{ V}$ line?
(b) Show how you would connect three resistors, each of resistance $6 \Omega$, so that the combination has a resistance of (i) $9 \Omega$ (ii) $4 \Omega$. Support your answer with calculations and schematic diagrams.
21.
(a) Define the absolute refractive index of a medium.
(b) Light enters from air into glass having a refractive index of $1.50$. What is the speed of light in the glass? (Speed of light in vacuum = $3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$).
(c) The refractive index of diamond is $2.42$ and that of water is $1.33$. Find the refractive index of diamond with respect to water. What does this value indicate regarding the speed of light in these media?
22.
(a) Two lamps, one rated $100 \text{ W}$ at $220 \text{ V}$, and the other $60 \text{ W}$ at $220 \text{ V}$, are connected in parallel to electric mains supply. What current is drawn from the line if the supply voltage is $220 \text{ V}$?
(b) Which lamp has a higher resistance? Prove it mathematically.
(c) If these two lamps were connected in series across the same $220 \text{ V}$ supply, which lamp would glow brighter? Give a detailed scientific reason based on power dissipation.
