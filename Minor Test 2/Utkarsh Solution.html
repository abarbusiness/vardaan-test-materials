SECTION A (12 Marks)
Part I: Science (Matter and Pure Substances)
1.
Define matter. Name the three fundamental states of matter.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Anything that occupies space (has volume) and has mass is called matter. (1 Mark)

The three fundamental states of matter are: Solid, Liquid, and Gas. (1 Mark)

2.
Write any two key differences between a pure substance and a mixture.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
  • Composition: A pure substance consists of a single type of particles and has a fixed composition, whereas a mixture consists of two or more types of particles and has a variable composition. (1 Mark)
  • Separation: The constituents of a pure substance cannot be separated by simple physical methods, whereas the constituents of a mixture can be separated easily by physical methods (like filtration, evaporation). (1 Mark)
3.
Why does a desert cooler cool better on a hot, dry day?
[2 Marks]
Solution:

A desert cooler works on the principle of evaporative cooling. On a hot and dry day, the temperature is high and humidity (moisture in the air) is low. These conditions significantly increase the rate of evaporation of water. Because evaporation causes cooling (as it draws latent heat from the surroundings), the rapid evaporation leads to a better cooling effect. (2 Marks)

Part II: Mathematics
4.
Simplify the following expression: $(3 + \sqrt{3})(3 - \sqrt{3})$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

The given expression is in the form of the algebraic identity: $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Here, } a &= 3 \text{ and } b = \sqrt{3} \\ (3 + \sqrt{3})(3 - \sqrt{3}) &= (3)^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2 \\ &= 9 - 3 \\ &= 6 \end{aligned} $$

Final Answer: 6 (2 Marks for applying identity and final result)

5.
Find the value of the polynomial $P(x) = 5x - 4x^2 + 3$ at $x = 2$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Given polynomial: $P(x) = 5x - 4x^2 + 3$

Substitute $x = 2$ into the polynomial:

$$ \begin{aligned} P(2) &= 5(2) - 4(2)^2 + 3 \\ &= 10 - 4(4) + 3 \\ &= 10 - 16 + 3 \\ &= -6 + 3 \\ &= -3 \end{aligned} $$

Final Answer: -3 (2 Marks)

6.
Express the linear equation $2x + 3y = 9.3\overline{5}$ in the standard form $ax + by + c = 0$ and indicate the values of $a, b,$ and $c$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

The standard form of a linear equation in two variables is $ax + by + c = 0$.

Given equation: $2x + 3y = 9.3\overline{5}$

Transposing the constant term to the LHS:

$$ 2x + 3y - 9.3\overline{5} = 0 $$

Comparing this with $ax + by + c = 0$, we get:

$a = 2, \quad b = 3, \quad c = -9.3\overline{5}$ (1 Mark for standard form, 1 Mark for values)

SECTION B (18 Marks)
Part I: Science (Matter and Pure Substances)
7.
State any three characteristics of the particles of matter.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

The three main characteristics of particles of matter are:

  1. Particles of matter have space between them: (Intermolecular spaces exist between particles, which is why salt dissolves evenly in water).
  2. Particles of matter are continuously moving: (They possess kinetic energy. Example: Smell of hot sizzling food reaches us from a distance due to diffusion).
  3. Particles of matter attract each other: (There is an intermolecular force of attraction acting between particles that keeps them together).

(1 Mark for each correct characteristic)

8.
Define the following terms clearly: (a) Melting point, (b) Boiling point, (c) Latent heat of fusion.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Melting point: The specific temperature at which a solid melts to become a liquid at atmospheric pressure is called its melting point. (1 Mark)
(b) Boiling point: The specific temperature at which a liquid starts boiling and changing into vapor at atmospheric pressure is called its boiling point. (1 Mark)
(c) Latent heat of fusion: It is the amount of heat energy required to change 1 kg of a solid into a liquid at atmospheric pressure at its melting point, without any change in temperature. (1 Mark)
9.
How can we successfully separate a mixture containing common salt and ammonium chloride? Briefly explain the principle involved.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Method: Sublimation (1 Mark)

Procedure & Principle:

  • The mixture is placed in a china dish and covered with an inverted glass funnel. The stem of the funnel is plugged with cotton.
  • On heating the china dish, the ammonium chloride undergoes sublimation.
  • Principle: Sublimation is the process where a solid changes directly into vapors on heating, and vapors change directly into solid on cooling, bypassing the liquid state. Ammonium chloride is a sublimable volatile component, while common salt (sodium chloride) is non-sublimable. (1.5 Marks)
  • The ammonium chloride vapors rise and condense on the cooler inner walls of the funnel, forming a solid sublimate. Common salt is left behind in the china dish. (0.5 Marks)
Part II: Mathematics
10.
Show how $\sqrt{5}$ can be represented on the number line. (Draw a neat sketch).
[3 Marks]
Solution:

By Pythagoras Theorem, we can write $5 = 2^2 + 1^2$. Therefore, $\sqrt{5} = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2}$.

Steps of construction:

  1. Draw a horizontal number line and mark a point 'O' representing zero.
  2. Mark point 'A' on the number line such that OA = 2 units.
  3. From point 'A', draw a perpendicular line segment AB of length 1 unit (AB $\perp$ OA).
  4. Join O and B. Now, $\Delta OAB$ is a right-angled triangle at A.
  5. By Pythagoras theorem: $OB^2 = OA^2 + AB^2 \Rightarrow OB = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{4 + 1} = \sqrt{5}$ units.
  6. Taking O as the center and OB as the radius, draw an arc intersecting the number line at a point, say 'P'.
  7. Point P represents $\sqrt{5}$ on the number line.
[Teacher Note: Students should draw a right angled triangle on the number line with base 2 units, perpendicular 1 unit. Hypotenuse will be $\sqrt{5}$. An arc from the hypotenuse tip to the number line represents the exact point.]

(1 Mark for logic/Pythagoras break down, 2 Marks for steps and correct sketch)

11.
Factorize the quadratic polynomial $x^2 + 7x + 10$ using the splitting the middle term method.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Given polynomial: $x^2 + 7x + 10$

We need to find two numbers whose product is $1 \times 10 = 10$ and whose sum is the middle term, $7$.

The numbers are $5$ and $2$ (since $5 \times 2 = 10$ and $5 + 2 = 7$).

Splitting the middle term ($7x$):

$$ \begin{aligned} x^2 + 7x + 10 &= x^2 + 5x + 2x + 10 \\ &= x(x + 5) + 2(x + 5) \quad \text{(Taking common terms)} \\ &= (x + 2)(x + 5) \end{aligned} $$

Final Factors: $(x + 2)(x + 5)$ (1 Mark for correct split, 2 Marks for correct factorization)

12.
Find three different solutions for the linear equation in two variables: $2x + y = 7$.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Given equation: $2x + y = 7$. We can express $y$ in terms of $x$:

$$ y = 7 - 2x $$

Let's find three solutions by substituting arbitrary values for $x$:

  • Let $x = 0$:
    $y = 7 - 2(0) = 7$.
    Solution 1: (0, 7) (1 Mark)
  • Let $x = 1$:
    $y = 7 - 2(1) = 7 - 2 = 5$.
    Solution 2: (1, 5) (1 Mark)
  • Let $x = 2$:
    $y = 7 - 2(2) = 7 - 4 = 3$.
    Solution 3: (2, 3) (1 Mark)

(Note: Any mathematically valid coordinate pair solving the equation is acceptable.)

14.
(a) What is a suspension? State any two distinguishing properties of a suspension. (3 Marks)

(b) Differentiate between homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures with one example of each. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Suspension: A suspension is a heterogeneous mixture in which the solute particles do not dissolve but remain suspended throughout the bulk of the medium. (1 Mark)
Properties (Any Two):
  • It is a heterogeneous mixture.
  • The particles of a suspension can be seen by the naked eye (size > 100 nm).
  • It shows the Tyndall effect (scatters a beam of light passing through it).
  • It is unstable; particles settle down when left undisturbed.
(2 Marks, 1 for each property)
(b) Homogeneous vs Heterogeneous Mixtures:
  • Homogeneous Mixture: A mixture having a uniform composition throughout. The components are indistinguishable.
    Example: Salt dissolved in water, air. (1 Mark)
  • Heterogeneous Mixture: A mixture having a non-uniform composition throughout. Distinct parts or boundaries are visible.
    Example: Sand and iron filings, oil and water. (1 Mark)
15.
(a) Define the process of evaporation. (2 Marks)

(b) List and briefly explain any three factors that affect the rate of evaporation of a liquid. (3 Marks)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Evaporation: The phenomenon of change of a liquid into vapors at any temperature below its boiling point is called evaporation. It is a surface phenomenon where particles at the surface gain enough energy to overcome forces of attraction. (2 Marks)
(b) Factors affecting the rate of evaporation (Any three):
  1. Surface Area: Evaporation is a surface phenomenon. If the surface area increases, the rate of evaporation increases (e.g., spreading clothes to dry).
  2. Temperature: With an increase in temperature, more particles get enough kinetic energy to convert into the vapor state, increasing the rate of evaporation.
  3. Humidity: Humidity is the amount of water vapor present in air. If the humidity in the air is already high, the capacity to hold more water is low, so the rate of evaporation decreases.
  4. Wind Speed: Higher wind speed blows away the water vapor particles, decreasing the local humidity and increasing the rate of evaporation.
(3 Marks, 1 Mark for each factor correctly explained)

16.
Apply the concepts of Number Systems to solve the following:
(a) Rationalize the denominator of $\frac{1}{7 + 3\sqrt{2}}$. (3 Marks)

(b) Express $0.4\overline{7}$ in the form $\frac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are integers and $q \neq 0$. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Rationalize the denominator:

To rationalize $\frac{1}{7 + 3\sqrt{2}}$, multiply the numerator and the denominator by its conjugate, $7 - 3\sqrt{2}$.

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{7 + 3\sqrt{2}} &= \frac{1}{7 + 3\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{7 - 3\sqrt{2}}{7 - 3\sqrt{2}} \\ &= \frac{7 - 3\sqrt{2}}{(7)^2 - (3\sqrt{2})^2} \quad \text{[Using } (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2 \text{]} \\ &= \frac{7 - 3\sqrt{2}}{49 - 18} \\ &= \frac{7 - 3\sqrt{2}}{31} \end{aligned} $$
(3 Marks: 1 for conjugate multiplication, 1 for identity application, 1 for final answer)
(b) Express $0.4\overline{7}$ as $\frac{p}{q}$:

Let $x = 0.4777\dots$ ---- (Equation 1)

Multiply by 10 to shift the decimal past the non-repeating digit:

$10x = 4.777\dots$ ---- (Equation 2)

Multiply by 10 again to shift past one repeating digit:

$100x = 47.777\dots$ ---- (Equation 3)

Subtract Eq 2 from Eq 3:

$$ \begin{aligned} 100x - 10x &= 47.777\dots - 4.777\dots \\ 90x &= 43 \\ x &= \frac{43}{90} \end{aligned} $$
(2 Marks: 1 for correct decimal shifting logic, 1 for final fraction)
17.
Apply algebraic identities and the Factor Theorem:
(a) Factorize the cubic polynomial completely: $x^3 - 2x^2 - x + 2$. (3 Marks)

(b) Evaluate the following using a suitable algebraic identity: $(99)^3$. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Factorize $x^3 - 2x^2 - x + 2$:

Method 1: Grouping terms.

$$ \begin{aligned} x^3 - 2x^2 - x + 2 &= x^2(x - 2) - 1(x - 2) \\ &= (x^2 - 1)(x - 2) \end{aligned} $$

Apply identity $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$ on $(x^2 - 1)$:

$$ = (x - 1)(x + 1)(x - 2) $$
(3 Marks: 1.5 for valid grouping/initial factor, 1.5 for complete factorization)
(b) Evaluate $(99)^3$:

Write $99$ as $(100 - 1)$ and apply the identity $(a - b)^3 = a^3 - b^3 - 3ab(a - b)$.

$$ \begin{aligned} (100 - 1)^3 &= (100)^3 - (1)^3 - 3(100)(1)(100 - 1) \\ &= 1,000,000 - 1 - 300(99) \\ &= 1,000,000 - 1 - 29,700 \\ &= 1,000,000 - 29,701 \\ &= 970,299 \end{aligned} $$
(2 Marks: 1 for identity setup, 1 for correct calculation)
18.
Linear equations geometry:
(a) Draw the graph of the linear equation $x + y = 4$ on a Cartesian plane. (3 Marks)

(b) Check algebraically whether $(2, 2)$ and $(4, 1)$ are solutions of the equation $x + 2y = 6$. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Graph of $x + y = 4$:

First, find at least two coordinates to plot the line:

  • If $x = 0$, $0 + y = 4 \Rightarrow y = 4$. Coordinate: (0, 4)
  • If $y = 0$, $x + 0 = 4 \Rightarrow x = 4$. Coordinate: (4, 0)
  • If $x = 2$, $2 + y = 4 \Rightarrow y = 2$. Coordinate: (2, 2)
[Teacher Note: Students should draw a neat Cartesian Plane. Plot the points (0,4), (4,0), and (2,2). Draw a straight line passing through these points, extending infinitely in both directions, and label the line "$x+y=4$". Axes should be properly labeled with scale.]
(3 Marks: 1 for finding coordinates, 2 for correct graph plotting)
(b) Check solutions for $x + 2y = 6$:

Substitute the coordinates into the LHS of the equation.

For (2, 2): Substitute $x = 2, y = 2$

$$ \text{LHS} = 2 + 2(2) = 2 + 4 = 6 = \text{RHS} $$

Yes, $(2, 2)$ is a solution. (1 Mark)

For (4, 1): Substitute $x = 4, y = 1$

$$ \text{LHS} = 4 + 2(1) = 4 + 2 = 6 = \text{RHS} $$

Yes, $(4, 1)$ is also a solution. (1 Mark)
