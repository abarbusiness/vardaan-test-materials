Class: 7 (ICSE) Subject: Mathematics (Probability & Statistics) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all calculations clearly.
  • Marks for each question are indicated in the respective sections.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define the terms 'Range' and 'Mean' in the context of statistics.
2.
Find the mean of the first five prime numbers.
3.
A fair coin is tossed once. What is the probability of getting a tail?
4.
What is a tally mark? Draw the tally mark representation for the number 13.
5.
The marks obtained by 7 students in a surprise test are: 15, 20, 15, 18, 14, 20, 15. Find the mode of the given data.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find the median of the following set of observations: $24, 36, 46, 17, 18, 25, 35$.
7.
A standard die is rolled once. Find the probability of getting:
(i) an even number.
(ii) a number strictly greater than 4.
8.
A bag contains 4 red balls, 5 blue balls, and 3 green balls. One ball is drawn at random from the bag. Find the probability that the ball drawn is:
(i) red.
(ii) not green.
9.
The mean of 5 observations $x, x+2, x+4, x+6, \text{ and } x+8$ is $11$. Find the value of $x$.
10.
The weights (in kg) of 8 students in a sports club are: $45, 50, 48, 52, 49, 45, 51, 46$. Find the range and the mean weight of the students.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The following data shows the number of children in 20 families of a particular locality:
$2, 1, 3, 1, 2, 3, 2, 2, 4, 1, 2, 1, 4, 3, 2, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1$
(a) Prepare a frequency distribution table for the above data using tally marks. (3 Marks)
(b) Find the mode of the data based on your table. (2 Marks)
12.
A box contains 25 identical cards numbered consecutively from 1 to 25. A card is drawn from the box at random. Find the probability that the number on the drawn card is:
(a) a multiple of 5. (2 Marks)
(b) a prime number. (2 Marks)
(c) a perfect square. (1 Mark)
13.
The runs scored by a batsman in 7 consecutive matches are as follows: $45, 89, 12, 105, 56, 34, 78$.
(a) Calculate the mean runs scored by the batsman. (2 Marks)
(b) Calculate the median runs scored. (2 Marks)
(c) If he scores 50 runs in his 8th match, will his new median score increase or decrease? (1 Mark)
