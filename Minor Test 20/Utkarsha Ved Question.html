MINOR TEST 20
Class: 6 CBSE Subject: Mathematics (Percentage & Its Application) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Read each question carefully.
  • The paper consists of three sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • Write neat calculation steps wherever required. Avoid overwriting.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks] (a) Convert the fraction \(\dfrac{7}{20}\) into a percentage.
(b) Convert \(45\%\) into a fraction in its simplest form.
Q2.
[2 Marks] (a) Express the decimal number \(0.35\) as a percentage.
(b) Convert \(18\%\) into a decimal number.
Q3.
[2 Marks] Find the numerical value of the following quantities:
(a) \(15\%\) of \(\text{₹ } 600\)
(b) \(25\%\) of \(80\text{ kg}\)
Q4.
[2 Marks] In a class of \(40\) students, \(30\) students passed a mathematics test. Find the percentage of students who passed the test.
Q5.
[2 Marks] (a) What percentage is \(15\) out of \(60\)?
(b) If \(20\%\) of a number \(x\) is \(14\), find the value of \(x\).
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks] In an examination, Rohan scored \(420\) marks out of a total of \(500\) marks.
(a) Calculate his percentage score.
(b) If the minimum passing criteria is \(40\%\), did Rohan pass the examination?
Q7.
[3 Marks] A shopkeeper bought a school bag for \(\text{₹ } 400\) and sold it to a customer for \(\text{₹ } 480\). Find his profit amount and his profit percentage.
Q8.
[3 Marks] The population of a small town increased from \(2,000\) to \(2,300\) in one year. Calculate the percentage increase in the population.
Q9.
[3 Marks] In an orchard containing \(250\) trees, \(40\%\) are mango trees, \(30\%\) are apple trees, and the remaining are lemon trees. Calculate the total number of lemon trees in the orchard.
Q10.
[3 Marks] A worker receives a monthly salary of \(\text{₹ } 25,000\). He spends \(65\%\) of his salary on household expenses and saves the rest. Calculate his total monthly savings in rupees.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks] (a) A fruit vendor buys a basket of fresh apples for \(\text{₹ } 800\). Due to overstocking, he sells them at a loss of \(5\%\). Calculate the selling price of the basket of apples. [3 Marks]
(b) Out of \(1,200\) registered voters in a locality, \(85\%\) cast their votes during an election. How many voters did NOT cast their vote? [2 Marks]
Q12.
[5 Marks] In an annual school sports meet, \(60\%\) of the total students in the school participated in various events.
(a) If exactly \(450\) students participated, calculate the total number of students enrolled in the school. [3 Marks]
(b) How many students did NOT participate in the sports meet? [2 Marks]
Q13.
[5 Marks] (a) Ananya scored \(45\) out of \(50\) marks in Test A, and \(72\) out of \(80\) marks in Test B. Calculate her percentage score in both tests and state in which test she performed better. [3 Marks]
(b) During a festive discount sale, the price of a jacket was reduced from \(\text{₹ } 2,000\) to \(\text{₹ } 1,600\). Calculate the percentage discount offered on the jacket. [2 Marks]
