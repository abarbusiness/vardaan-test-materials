MINOR TEST 20 — CLASS VIII CBSE MATHEMATICS
Topic: Basic Mathematics (Rational Numbers, Linear Equations, Quadrilaterals, Square Roots & Algebraic Identities)
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • Answers to this paper must be written neatly on the answer sheet provided separately.
  • First 10 minutes are allotted exclusively for reading the question paper. Do not start writing during this period.
  • The paper consists of Three Sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • All numerical steps and mathematical calculations must be shown clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Objective & Fundamental Recall — 5 Questions x 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks]
Write the additive inverse and multiplicative inverse of the following rational numbers:
(a) $-\frac{7}{19}$ (1 Mark)
(b) $\frac{-5}{8} \times \frac{-3}{7}$ (1 Mark)
Q2.
[2 Marks]
Solve the following linear equation in one variable for $x$:
$$\frac{3x - 5}{2} = \frac{x + 7}{3}$$
Q3.
[2 Marks]
Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram are in the ratio $2 : 3$. Find the measures of all four interior angles of the parallelogram.
Q4.
[2 Marks]
Find the smallest whole number by which $252$ must be multiplied so as to get a perfect square number. Also, find the square root of the square number so obtained.
Q5.
[2 Marks]
Evaluate the product $(103 \times 105)$ using the standard algebraic identity $(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$ without direct multiplication.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer & Conceptual Application Type — 5 Questions x 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks]
Find three rational numbers strictly lying between $\frac{-1}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$. Show all steps of making denominators equal clearly.
Q7.
[3 Marks]
Present ages of Anu and Raj are in the ratio $4 : 5$. Eight years from now, the ratio of their ages will be $5 : 6$. Find their present ages.
Q8.
[3 Marks]
The diagonals of a rhombus $ABCD$ measure $16\text{ cm}$ and $12\text{ cm}$ respectively. Calculate:
(a) The length of each side of the rhombus. (2 Marks)
(b) The perimeter of the rhombus. (1 Mark)
Q9.
[3 Marks]
Find the square root of $5625$ using the long division method. Show all steps of pairing and division clearly.
Q10.
[3 Marks]
Is $68600$ a perfect cube? If not, find the smallest natural number by which $68600$ must be divided so that the quotient becomes a perfect cube. Also, find the cube root of the quotient.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer & Step-wise Problem Solving — 3 Questions x 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks]
The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is $9$. When we interchange the digits, it is found that the resulting new number exceeds the original number by $27$.
(a) Form a linear equation in one variable representing the given conditions. (2 Marks)
(b) Solve the equation to find the original two-digit number. (2 Marks)
(c) Verify your answer by interchanging the digits of the obtained original number. (1 Mark)
Q12.
[5 Marks]
(a) In a convex quadrilateral $PQRS$, the interior angle measures are given as $\angle P = (2x + 10)^\circ$, $\angle Q = (3x - 20)^\circ$, $\angle R = (x + 30)^\circ$, and $\angle S = (2x + 60)^\circ$. Find the value of $x$ and the measure of each interior angle. (3 Marks)
(b) State the angle sum property of a polygon with $n$ sides. Use this formula to calculate the sum of interior angles of a convex polygon having $8$ sides (octagon). (2 Marks)
Q13.
[5 Marks]
(a) Simplify the algebraic expression using identities: (2.5 Marks)
$$(2x + 5y)^2 - (2x - 5y)^2$$
(b) Factorise the following algebraic expressions completely: (2.5 Marks)
(i) $a^2 + 7a + 12$
(ii) $16x^2 - 49y^2$
