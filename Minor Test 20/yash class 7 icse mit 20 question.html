SECTION A (10 Marks) (Objective & Recall Questions — 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
Fill in the blanks with correct scientific terms: [2]
(a) The green pigment present in leaves that absorbs light energy is called ____________.
(b) Extra glucose produced during photosynthesis is stored in plants in the form of ____________.
Q2.
State whether the following statements are True or False: [2]
(a) Oxygen gas is absorbed by green plants from the air during photosynthesis.
(b) Iodine solution turns dark blue-black in the presence of starch.
Q3.
Give one scientific term for each of the following: [2]
(a) The tiny microscopic pores on the leaf surface through which gaseous exchange takes place.
(b) Organisms that can synthesize their own food using simple inorganic substances.
Q4.
Match Column A with Column B: [2]
Column A Column B
(i) Chloroplast (A) Water transportation to leaves
(ii) Xylem (B) Site of photosynthesis in a plant cell
Q5.
Choose the correct option for each question: [2]
(a) Which gas is liberated as a byproduct during photosynthesis?
      i) Nitrogen      ii) Carbon dioxide      iii) Oxygen      iv) Hydrogen
(b) The process of keeping a plant in a dark room for 24–48 hours to remove starch from leaves is called:
      i) Bleaching      ii) De-starching      iii) Transpiration      iv) Respiration
