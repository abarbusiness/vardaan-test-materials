Q1. Evaluate the following using BODMAS rule: $ 45 + 3 \times 5 - 10 $
[2 Marks]
According to BODMAS, we first perform Multiplication, then Addition, and finally Subtraction.
Expression: $ 45 + 3 \times 5 - 10 $
Step 1 (Multiply): $= 45 + 15 - 10 $   (1 Mark)
Step 2 (Add): $= 60 - 10 $
Step 3 (Subtract): $= 50 $   (1 Mark)
Final Answer: 50
Q2. Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of $12$ and $18$.
[2 Marks]
Multiples of $12$: $12, 24, 36, 48, 60, \dots$
Multiples of $18$: $18, 36, 54, 72, \dots$
Alternatively, by prime factorization:
$12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3$
$18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2 \times 3^2$
(1 Mark for correct approach/factors)
$\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36$   (1 Mark)
Final Answer: LCM is 36

Percentage: $\frac{3}{4} \times 100\% = 3 \times 25\% = 75\%$   (1 Mark)

Perimeter $= 2 \times (8 + 5)$
Perimeter $= 2 \times 13 = 26 \text{ cm}$   (1 Mark for correct answer with units)
Q5. Solve the simple equation for $x$: $ 2x + 5 = 15 $
[2 Marks]
Given equation: $2x + 5 = 15$
Subtract 5 from both sides (transpose 5 to RHS):
$2x = 15 - 5$
$2x = 10$   (1 Mark)
Divide both sides by 2:
$x = \frac{10}{2}$
$x = 5$   (1 Mark)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. A shopkeeper bought a bag for ₹$400$ and sold it for ₹$520$. Find his total profit and the profit percentage.
[3 Marks]
Cost Price (C.P.) $= \text{₹}400$
Selling Price (S.P.) $= \text{₹}520$
Since $\text{S.P.} > \text{C.P.}$, there is a profit.
$\text{Profit} = \text{S.P.} - \text{C.P.} = 520 - 400 = \text{₹}120$   (1.5 Marks)
$\text{Profit Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{C.P.}} \right) \times 100\%$
$= \left( \frac{120}{400} \right) \times 100\%$
$= \frac{120}{4} = 30\%$   (1.5 Marks)
Q7. Simplify the fraction $\frac{36}{48}$ to its lowest terms. Also, find an equivalent fraction of $\frac{36}{48}$ with a denominator of $12$.
[3 Marks]
Lowest terms: The Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 48 is 12.
Dividing numerator and denominator by 12:
$\frac{36 \div 12}{48 \div 12} = \frac{3}{4}$   (1.5 Marks)
Equivalent fraction with denominator 12:
We need $\frac{36}{48} = \frac{x}{12}$.
Since $48 \div 4 = 12$, we divide the numerator by 4 as well.
$\frac{36 \div 4}{48 \div 4} = \frac{9}{12}$   (1.5 Marks)
Q8. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. Find the measure of each angle of the triangle.
[3 Marks]
Let the angles of the triangle be $x$, $2x$, and $3x$.   (0.5 Mark)
According to the angle sum property of a triangle, the sum of all interior angles is $180^\circ$.
$x + 2x + 3x = 180^\circ$
$6x = 180^\circ$   (1 Mark)
$x = \frac{180^\circ}{6} = 30^\circ$   (0.5 Mark)
Now, calculate the three angles:
First angle $= x = 30^\circ$
Second angle $= 2x = 2 \times 30^\circ = 60^\circ$
Third angle $= 3x = 3 \times 30^\circ = 90^\circ$   (1 Mark for all three angles)
Q9. A car travels a distance of $240 \text{ km}$ in $4 \text{ hours}$. How much distance will it cover in $7 \text{ hours}$ if it travels at the same constant speed?
[3 Marks]
We will use the Unitary Method.
Distance covered in $4 \text{ hours} = 240 \text{ km}$.
Distance covered in $1 \text{ hour}$ (Speed) $= \frac{240}{4} = 60 \text{ km}$.   (1.5 Marks)
Therefore, Distance covered in $7 \text{ hours} = 60 \times 7 = 420 \text{ km}$.   (1.5 Marks)
Final Answer: 420 km
Q10. The perimeter of a square is $24 \text{ cm}$. Find the length of its side and calculate the area of the square.
[3 Marks]
Formula for Perimeter of a square $= 4 \times \text{side}$.
Given Perimeter $= 24 \text{ cm}$.
$4 \times \text{side} = 24$
$\text{Side} = \frac{24}{4} = 6 \text{ cm}$.   (1.5 Marks)
Formula for Area of a square $= \text{side} \times \text{side}$ (or $\text{side}^2$).
$\text{Area} = 6 \times 6 = 36 \text{ cm}^2$.   (1.5 Marks)

Q12. (a) Simplify using laws of exponents: $\frac{2^5 \times 3^4}{2^3 \times 3^2}$ (3 Marks)
(b) Express $120$ as a product of its prime factors. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
(a) Laws of Exponents:
Using the quotient law: $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$   (1 Mark for stating or applying the law correctly)
$= \left(\frac{2^5}{2^3}\right) \times \left(\frac{3^4}{3^2}\right)$
$= 2^{5-3} \times 3^{4-2}$
$= 2^2 \times 3^2$   (1 Mark)
$= 4 \times 9 = 36$   (1 Mark)
(b) Prime Factorization:
Divide $120$ by the smallest prime number (2):
$120 = 2 \times 60$
$60 = 2 \times 30$
$30 = 2 \times 15$
$15 = 3 \times 5$
$5 = 5 \times 1$   (1 Mark for correct steps)
Therefore, $120 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5$ or $2^3 \times 3 \times 5$.   (1 Mark)
Q13. (a) The sum of two consecutive integers is $27$. Form an equation and find the two integers. (3 Marks)
(b) Find the ratio of $500 \text{ ml}$ to $2 \text{ Litres}$ in its simplest form. (2 Marks)
[5 Marks]
(a) Consecutive Integers:
Let the first integer be $x$.
Then, the next consecutive integer will be $x + 1$.   (1 Mark)
According to the question, their sum is $27$:
$x + (x + 1) = 27$
$2x + 1 = 27$
$2x = 27 - 1 = 26$   (1 Mark)
$x = \frac{26}{2} = 13$
So, the first integer is $13$, and the second integer is $13 + 1 = 14$.
The integers are 13 and 14.   (1 Mark)
(b) Ratios:
To find the ratio, both quantities must have the same units.
Convert $2 \text{ Litres}$ to milliliters ($\text{ml}$).
We know, $1 \text{ Litre} = 1000 \text{ ml}$.
Therefore, $2 \text{ Litres} = 2 \times 1000 = 2000 \text{ ml}$.   (1 Mark)
Now, find the ratio:
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{500 \text{ ml}}{2000 \text{ ml}}$
Divide both by 500:
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{1}{4} = 1:4$   (1 Mark)
