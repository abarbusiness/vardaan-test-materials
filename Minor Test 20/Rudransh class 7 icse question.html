General Instructions:

SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Simplify the following expression using laws of exponents and write the final answer in exponential form:
$(2^3 \times 2^4) \div 2^5$
2.
Convert $35\%$ into a fraction in its simplest form, and also express it as a decimal.
3.
Add the following algebraic expressions:
$3x - 4y + 2z$ and $2x + 5y - z$
4.
In $\triangle ABC$, it is given that $\angle A = 50^\circ$ and $\angle B = 70^\circ$. Find the measure of $\angle C$. Based on the angles, name the type of triangle this forms.
5.
Evaluate the value of the algebraic expression $2a^2 - 3b + 4$ when $a = 2$ and $b = -1$.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Rohan scored $45$ marks out of a total of $60$ marks in a Mathematics test. Calculate his percentage score. If the passing criteria is $40\%$, did Rohan pass the test?
7.
The exterior angle of a triangle measures $110^\circ$, and one of its interior opposite angles is $45^\circ$. Find the measures of the other two interior angles of the triangle.
8.
Simplify the expression below and express the result in power notation with a positive exponent:
$\left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^5 \times \left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{-3}$
9.
Subtract the expression $4p^2q - 3pq + 5pq^2 - 8p + 7q - 10$ from $18 - 3p - 11q + 5pq - 2pq^2 + 5p^2q$.
10.
Is it geometrically possible to construct a triangle with side lengths $3 \text{ cm}$, $6 \text{ cm}$, and $7 \text{ cm}$? Provide a mathematical reason for your answer.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Mr. Sharma earns ₹ $25,000$ per month. He spends $20\%$ of his monthly income on house rent and $40\%$ of his income on food. He saves the remaining amount.
a) Calculate the total amount he spends on rent and food combined.
b) Find his monthly savings.
c) Express his savings as a fraction of his total income in its simplest form.
12.
Consider the algebraic expression: $2x(x - y^2) - 3y(xy + 2x) - xy(x + y)$
a) Simplify the expression by expanding the brackets and grouping like terms.
b) Hence, find the numerical value of the simplified expression when $x = -1$ and $y = 2$.
13.
Answer the following questions based on the properties of triangles:
a) In a right-angled triangle, the two acute angles are in the ratio $2:3$. Find the exact measure of both acute angles.
b) The angles of a different triangle are given as $x^\circ$, $(x - 20)^\circ$, and $(x + 50)^\circ$. Form an equation and find the value of $x$.
