MINOR TEST 20
Class: 6 (ICSE) Subject: Physics (Force Around Us) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Illustrate with examples where necessary.
  • Marks for each question are indicated in the respective sections.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define 'Force'. Give one example of a push and one example of a pull from your daily life.
2.
What do you understand by a non-contact force? Give two examples of non-contact forces.
3.
A rubber band stretches when it is pulled from both ends. Which effect of force is demonstrated here? State one other effect a force can have on an object.
4.
Define 'Frictional force'. Does this force oppose or support the motion of a moving object?
5.
What is electrostatic force? Give a common everyday activity that demonstrates this force.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Differentiate between contact and non-contact forces. Support your answer with one example of each.
7.
Name the specific type of force acting in the following cases:
(i) A ripe apple falling from a tree to the ground.
(ii) A plastic comb, rubbed in dry hair, attracting tiny bits of paper.
(iii) A horse pulling a cart along a road.
8.
Why do we tend to slip easily when we walk on a smooth, wet floor? Explain this phenomenon in terms of force.
9.
List any three major effects a force can produce on a body. Give one practical example for each effect mentioned.
10.
A bar magnet is brought close to a mixture containing iron nails and wooden toothpicks. What will you observe? Which specific force is responsible for this observation?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) What is meant by 'Muscular Force'? Provide two instances where you use muscular force. (2 Marks)
(b) Explain in detail why a football rolling on the ground gradually slows down and finally comes to a halt. Name the force involved and state whether it is a contact or non-contact force. (3 Marks)
12.
Explain the concept of gravitational force in detail. How is gravitational force different from magnetic force? Provide examples to clearly distinguish between the two.
13.
(a) "A applied force can change the state of motion as well as the shape of an object." Justify this statement with one practical example for each. (2 Marks)
(b) Classify the forces acting in the following scenarios as either Contact Force or Non-Contact Force: (3 Marks)
(i) Kicking a football.
(ii) Leaves falling from a branch.
(iii) Two magnets repelling each other.
