SECTION A - DETAILED SOLUTIONS (10 Marks)

Q1. (a) Convert \(\dfrac{7}{20}\) to percentage. (b) Convert \(45\%\) into fraction in simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Fraction to Percentage:

To convert a fraction into a percentage, multiply by \(100\%\):

\[ \dfrac{7}{20} \times 100\% = 7 \times 5\% = 35\% \]

Answer: \(35\%\)

(b) Percentage to Simplest Fraction:

Write percentage as a fraction with denominator \(100\) and simplify:

\[ 45\% = \dfrac{45}{100} \]

Dividing numerator and denominator by HCF (\(5\)):

\[ \dfrac{45 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \dfrac{9}{20} \]

Answer: \(\dfrac{9}{20}\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for correct percentage conversion (\(35\%\)); 1 Mark for fraction in lowest terms (\(9/20\)).
Q2. (a) Express \(0.35\) as percentage. (b) Convert \(18\%\) into decimal.
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Decimal to Percentage:

\[ 0.35 \times 100\% = 35\% \]

Answer: \(35\%\)

(b) Percentage to Decimal:

\[ 18\% = \dfrac{18}{100} = 0.18 \]

Answer: \(0.18\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for converting decimal to percentage (\(35\%\)); 1 Mark for converting percentage to decimal (\(0.18\)).
Q3. (a) Find \(15\%\) of \(\text{₹ } 600\). (b) Find \(25\%\) of \(80\text{ kg}\).
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) \(15\%\) of \(\text{₹ } 600\):

\[ \dfrac{15}{100} \times 600 = 15 \times 6 = 90 \]

Answer: \(\text{₹ } 90\)

(b) \(25\%\) of \(80\text{ kg}\):

\[ \dfrac{25}{100} \times 80 = \dfrac{1}{4} \times 80 = 20\text{ kg} \]

Answer: \(20\text{ kg}\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for part (a) (\(₹ 90\)); 1 Mark for part (b) (\(20\text{ kg}\)).
Q4. Pass percentage when \(30\) out of \(40\) students pass.
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given:

  • Total number of students = \(40\)
  • Number of students who passed = \(30\)
\[ \text{Pass Percentage} = \left( \dfrac{\text{Number of students passed}}{\text{Total students}} \right) \times 100\% \]
\[ = \left( \dfrac{30}{40} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{3}{4} \times 100\% = 75\% \]

Answer: \(75\%\) of the students passed.

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for formula/setup; 1 Mark for final calculation (\(75\%\)).
Q5. (a) Percentage of \(15\) out of \(60\). (b) If \(20\%\) of \(x\) is \(14\), find \(x\).
[2 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Percentage of \(15\) out of \(60\):

\[ \left( \dfrac{15}{60} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{1}{4} \times 100\% = 25\% \]

(b) Find \(x\) when \(20\%\) of \(x = 14\):

\[ \dfrac{20}{100} \times x = 14 \implies \dfrac{1}{5} \times x = 14 \]
\[ x = 14 \times 5 = 70 \]

Answer: (a) \(25\%\), (b) \(x = 70\)

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for part (a) (\(25\%\)); 1 Mark for part (b) (\(x = 70\)).

Q6. Rohan's score percentage (\(420\) out of \(500\)) and pass verification.
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given: Marks scored = \(420\), Total marks = \(500\).

(a) Percentage Score:

\[ \text{Percentage} = \left( \dfrac{420}{500} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{420}{5} = 84\% \]

(b) Pass Verification:

Passing criteria = \(40\%\).

Since Rohan's score (\(84\%\)) is greater than \(40\%\), Yes, Rohan passed the examination comfortably.

Marking Scheme: 2 Marks for calculating score percentage (\(84\%\)); 1 Mark for concluding pass status.
Q7. Profit and Profit Percentage (CP = \(\text{₹ } 400\), SP = \(\text{₹ } 480\)).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given:

  • Cost Price (CP) = \(\text{₹ } 400\)
  • Selling Price (SP) = \(\text{₹ } 480\)

Step 1: Profit Calculation:

\[ \text{Profit} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 480 - 400 = \text{₹ } 80 \]

Step 2: Profit Percentage Calculation:

\[ \text{Profit \%} = \left( \dfrac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100\% \]
\[ = \left( \dfrac{80}{400} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{80}{4} = 20\% \]

Answer: Profit = \(\text{₹ } 80\), Profit Percentage = \(20\%\)

Marking Scheme: 1.5 Marks for calculating profit amount (\(₹ 80\)); 1.5 Marks for calculating profit percentage (\(20\%\)).
Q8. Population increase percentage (\(2,000\) to \(2,300\)).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given:

  • Original Population = \(2,000\)
  • New Population = \(2,300\)

Step 1: Find increase in population:

\[ \text{Increase} = 2,300 - 2,000 = 300 \]

Step 2: Calculate percentage increase:

\[ \text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \dfrac{\text{Increase}}{\text{Original Population}} \right) \times 100\% \]
\[ = \left( \dfrac{300}{2000} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{30}{2} = 15\% \]

Answer: The population increased by \(15\%\).

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for finding increase in population (\(300\)); 2 Marks for calculating percentage increase (\(15\%\)).
Q9. Number of lemon trees out of \(250\) total trees (\(40\%\) mango, \(30\%\) apple).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given: Total number of trees = \(250\)

Percentage of Mango trees = \(40\%\)

Percentage of Apple trees = \(30\%\)

Method 1: By Percentage Remaining:

Percentage of Lemon trees = \(100\% - (40\% + 30\%) = 100\% - 70\% = 30\%\)

\[ \text{Number of Lemon trees} = 30\% \text{ of } 250 \]
\[ = \dfrac{30}{100} \times 250 = 3 \times 25 = 75 \]

Answer: There are \(75\) lemon trees in the orchard.

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for calculating remaining percentage (\(30\%\)); 2 Marks for evaluating quantity (\(75\) trees).
Q10. Calculate monthly savings (Salary = \(\text{₹ } 25,000\), Expenditure = \(65\%\)).
[3 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

Given:

  • Total Salary = \(\text{₹ } 25,000\)
  • Expenditure Percentage = \(65\%\)

Step 1: Calculate Savings Percentage:

\[ \text{Savings Percentage} = 100\% - 65\% = 35\% \]

Step 2: Calculate Savings Amount:

\[ \text{Savings} = 35\% \text{ of } 25,000 \]
\[ = \dfrac{35}{100} \times 25000 = 35 \times 250 = \text{₹ } 8,750 \]

Answer: His monthly savings equal \(\text{₹ } 8,750\).

Marking Scheme: 1 Mark for finding savings percentage (\(35\%\)); 2 Marks for calculating savings in rupees (\(₹ 8,750\)).

SECTION C - DETAILED SOLUTIONS (15 Marks)

Q11. (a) SP of apples with \(5\%\) loss on \(\text{₹ } 800\). (b) Non-voting voters out of \(1,200\) (\(85\%\) voted).
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Selling Price of Apple Basket:

Cost Price (CP) = \(\text{₹ } 800\), Loss Percentage = \(5\%\)

\[ \text{Loss Amount} = 5\% \text{ of } 800 = \dfrac{5}{100} \times 800 = \text{₹ } 40 \]
\[ \text{Selling Price (SP)} = \text{CP} - \text{Loss} = 800 - 40 = \text{₹ } 760 \]

Answer (a): Selling Price = \(\text{₹ } 760\)

(b) Voters who did NOT cast vote:

Total voters = \(1,200\)

Percentage who voted = \(85\%\) \(\implies\) Percentage who did NOT vote = \(100\% - 85\% = 15\%\)

\[ \text{Non-voters} = 15\% \text{ of } 1200 = \dfrac{15}{100} \times 1200 = 15 \times 12 = 180 \]

Answer (b): \(180\) voters did not cast their vote.

Marking Scheme: (a) 1.5 Marks for loss amount (\(₹ 40\)), 1.5 Marks for SP (\(₹ 760\)). (b) 1 Mark for finding non-voting percentage (\(15\%\)), 1 Mark for calculating voters count (\(180\)).
Q12. (a) Total students if \(60\%\) equals \(450\). (b) Non-participating students.
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Calculating Total Strength of School:

Let total number of students in school be \(N\).

Given: \(60\%\) of \(N = 450\)

\[ \dfrac{60}{100} \times N = 450 \]
\[ N = \dfrac{450 \times 100}{60} = \dfrac{4500}{6} = 750 \]

Answer (a): Total students in school = \(750\).

(b) Calculating Non-Participating Students:

\[ \text{Non-participating students} = \text{Total students} - \text{Participating students} \]
\[ = 750 - 450 = 300 \]

(Alternative Method: \(40\%\) of \(750 = \dfrac{40}{100} \times 750 = 300\))

Answer (b): \(300\) students did not participate.

Marking Scheme: (a) 1 Mark for setting equation, 2 Marks for finding total students (\(750\)). (b) 2 Marks for finding non-participating count (\(300\)).
Q13. (a) Score comparison using percentage (Test A vs Test B). (b) Discount percentage.
[5 Marks]
Solution & Explanation:

(a) Performance Comparison:

Test A: Score = \(45\) out of \(50\)

\[ \text{Percentage in Test A} = \left( \dfrac{45}{50} \right) \times 100\% = 45 \times 2\% = 90\% \]

Test B: Score = \(72\) out of \(80\)

\[ \text{Percentage in Test B} = \left( \dfrac{72}{80} \right) \times 100\% = \dfrac{9}{10} \times 100\% = 90\% \]

Conclusion: Ananya scored \(90\%\) in both tests, so her performance was equally outstanding in both tests.

(b) Discount Percentage on Jacket:

Original Price = \(\text{₹ } 2,000\), Reduced Price = \(\text{₹ } 1,600\)

\[ \text{Discount Amount} = 2000 - 1600 = \text{₹ } 400 \]
\[ \text{Discount Percentage} = \left( \dfrac{\text{Discount}}{\text{Original Price}} \right) \times 100\% = \left( \dfrac{400}{2000} \right) \times 100\% = 20\% \]

Answer (b): Discount offered = \(20\%\).

Marking Scheme: (a) 1 Mark for Test A percentage (\(90\%\)), 1 Mark for Test B percentage (\(90\%\)), 1 Mark for comparison conclusion. (b) 1 Mark for discount amount (\(₹ 400\)), 1 Mark for discount percentage (\(20\%\)).
