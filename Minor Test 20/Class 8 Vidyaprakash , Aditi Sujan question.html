SECTION A (10 Marks) (Objective & Fundamental Recall — 5 Questions x 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks]
State the formula to calculate the total Amount ($A$) and Compound Interest ($CI$) on a principal sum $P$ at an annual interest rate $r\%$ per annum for $n$ years when the interest is:
(a) Compounded annually.
(b) Compounded half-yearly.
Q2.
[2 Marks]
Calculate the compound interest on a principal amount of $\text{₹ } 5,000$ for $2$ years at $10\%$ per annum compounded annually.
Q3.
[2 Marks]
Find the difference between Simple Interest ($SI$) and Compound Interest ($CI$) on $\text{₹ } 8,000$ for $1$ year at $5\%$ per annum when the compound interest is compounded half-yearly.
Q4.
[2 Marks]
A sum of $\text{₹ } 10,000$ is invested at $10\%$ per annum for $1$ year. Calculate the final compound amount if the interest is compounded semi-annually.
Q5.
[2 Marks]
The value of a machine costing $\text{₹ } 25,000$ depreciates at the rate of $10\%$ per annum. Calculate its depreciated value after $1$ year.
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer & Application Type — 5 Questions x 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks]
The difference between Simple Interest and Compound Interest on a certain sum of money invested at $10\%$ per annum for $2$ years, compounded annually, is $\text{₹ } 150$. Find the principal sum.
Q7.
[3 Marks]
Find the compound interest on $\text{₹ } 12,500$ at $12\%$ per annum for $1 \frac{1}{2}$ years (1.5 years) when the interest is compounded half-yearly.
Q8.
[3 Marks]
A sum of $\text{₹ } 20,000$ is borrowed for $2$ years at compound interest. If the rate of interest for the first year is $8\%$ per annum and for the second year is $10\%$ per annum, find the total amount and compound interest payable at the end of $2$ years.
Q9.
[3 Marks]
A motorcycle was purchased for $\text{₹ } 80,000$. Its value depreciates at a rate of $10\%$ per annum. Calculate its market value at the end of $2$ years.
Q10.
[3 Marks]
The population of a growing town in the year 2022 was $1,76,400$. If the rate of growth of population is $5\%$ per annum, find:
(a) The estimated population in the year 2024.
(b) The population in the year 2020.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer & Numerical Derivation Type — 3 Questions x 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks]
(a) At what rate percent per annum will a principal sum of $\text{₹ } 4,000$ amount to $\text{₹ } 4,840$ in $2$ years, compounded annually? (2.5 Marks)
(b) In how many years will $\text{₹ } 8,000$ amount to $\text{₹ } 9,261$ at $5\%$ per annum compound interest compounded annually? (2.5 Marks)
Q12.
[5 Marks]
A person deposited $\text{₹ } 16,000$ in a bank for $1 \frac{1}{2}$ years at $10\%$ per annum.
(a) Calculate the compound interest earned if the interest is compounded half-yearly. (2.5 Marks)
(b) Calculate the compound interest earned if the interest is compounded annually. (2 Marks)
(c) Find the extra gain/interest obtained by choosing half-yearly compounding over annual compounding. (0.5 Mark)
Q13.
[5 Marks]
A certain sum of money placed at compound interest (compounded annually) amounts to $\text{₹ } 12,100$ at the end of $2$ years and to $\text{₹ } 13,310$ at the end of $3$ years.
(a) Find the annual rate of interest ($r\%$). (2.5 Marks)
(b) Determine the original principal sum ($P$) invested. (2.5 Marks)
