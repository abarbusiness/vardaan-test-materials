Q7. Transpiration & its two vital benefits in plants.
[3 Marks]
  • (a) Definition of Transpiration: Evaporation of water from leaves and stems into the atmosphere via microscopic stomatal pores. [1 Mark]
  • (b) Two Functions/Benefits: [2 Marks - 1 Mark each]
    1. Suction Pull (Transpiration Pull): Creates a continuous upward pull that draws water and dissolved mineral ions from roots to high leaves.
    2. Cooling Effect: Evaporative loss prevents plant tissue from overheating under bright sunlight.
Q8. Three major physical changes during adolescence.
[3 Marks]
Key Physical Changes (1 Mark each for any 3 points):
  • 1. Increase in Height & Weight: Rapid growth of long bones in limbs resulting in sudden increase in stature.
  • 2. Change in Body Shape: Broader shoulders and chest in boys; widening of pelvic region in girls.
  • 3. Voice Changes & Hair Growth: Enlargement of voice box (Adam's apple) leading to deeper voice in boys; development of facial/body hair.
Q9. Role of Stomata in Photosynthesis and Respiration.
[3 Marks]
  • Definition of Stomata: Microscopic pore openings present on the surface of plant leaves surrounded by two kidney-shaped guard cells. [1 Mark]
  • Role in Photosynthesis: Stomata allow uptake of \(\text{CO}_2\) from surrounding air into leaves and release oxygen gas (\(\text{O}_2\)) as a byproduct. [1 Mark]
  • Role in Respiration: Stomata allow absorption of atmospheric oxygen (\(\text{O}_2\)) for cellular breakdown of glucose and release carbon dioxide (\(\text{CO}_2\)). [1 Mark]
Q10. Nutritional needs of adolescents & two essential minerals.
[3 Marks]
  • (a) Importance of Balanced Diet: Adolescence involves intense growth of muscle, bones, and organs. A balanced meal containing proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals supports rapid bodily transformation and health. [1.5 Marks]
  • (b) Two Essential Minerals: [1.5 Marks]
    • Iron: Essential for blood formation (hemoglobin construction) to meet increased blood volume requirements.
    • Calcium: Crucial for skeletal growth, bone density, and teeth strength.
SECTION C: LONG ANSWER & DETAILED CONCEPTS (15 MARKS)
Q11. Xylem vs Phloem & Photosynthesis Chemical Equation.
[5 Marks]
  • (a) Transport Tissues (Xylem vs Phloem): [3 Marks]
    • Xylem: Consists of hollow dead cells (tracheids, vessels) forming a continuous network from roots to leaves. It conducts water and dissolved mineral salts unidirectionally (upward). [1.5 Marks]
    • Phloem: Made of living vascular cells (sieve tubes, companion cells). It conducts organic food (sucrose/glucose) multidirectionally from green leaves to all storage and growing parts. [1.5 Marks]
  • (b) Photosynthesis Chemical/Word Equation: [2 Marks]
    $$\text{Carbon Dioxide} + \text{Water} \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} \text{Glucose} + \text{Oxygen}$$ $$\text{OR}$$ $$6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2$$
Q12. Endocrine System, Hormones & Dual Role of Pancreas.
[5 Marks]
  • (a) Definition of Hormones: Chemical messengers secreted in tiny amounts directly into blood by ductless endocrine glands to regulate specific body processes. [1 Mark]
  • (b) Endocrine Glands (Any 2 described): [3 Marks - 1.5 Marks each]
    1. Thyroid Gland: Located in neck region. Secretes Thyroxine. Controls body metabolism, energy production, and physical development.
    2. Adrenal Gland: Located above kidney caps. Secretes Adrenaline. Prepares the body to deal with emergency stress situations ("fight or flight" response).
    3. Pancreas: Located near stomach. Secretes Insulin. Regulates blood sugar levels.
  • (c) Dual Function of Pancreas: It acts as an exocrine gland by producing digestive pancreatic juice into ducts, and as an endocrine gland by secreting insulin hormone directly into the bloodstream. [1 Mark]
Q13. Secondary Sexual Characters, Personal Hygiene & Exercise.
[5 Marks]
  • (a) Secondary Sexual Characters: Physical traits that distinguish mature males from females but are not directly involved in reproduction. [1 Mark]
    • In Males: Growth of facial beard/moustache, broadening of chest/shoulders, deepening of voice. [1 Mark]
    • In Females: Enlargement of breasts, broadening of hips/pelvis, onset of menstruation. [1 Mark]
  • (b) Importance of Hygiene & Physical Exercise: [2 Marks - 1 Mark each]
    1. Personal Hygiene: Increased activity of sweat and oil glands during puberty can cause body odor and bacterial skin infections if daily bathing and clean clothing are neglected.
    2. Physical Exercise: Keeping active through walking, running, or outdoor games promotes bone strength, builds healthy muscles, improves blood circulation, and maintains mental health.
