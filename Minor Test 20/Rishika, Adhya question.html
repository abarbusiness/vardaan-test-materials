MINOR TEST 20
Class: 6 & 7 Subject: Basic Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory. Keep your calculations neat and clean.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers step-by-step. Illustrate with examples where necessary.
  • Marks for each question are indicated in the respective sections.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following using BODMAS rule: $$ 45 + 3 \times 5 - 10 $$
2.
Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of $12$ and $18$.
3.
Convert the fraction $\frac{3}{4}$ into a decimal and a percentage.
4.
Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length is $8 \text{ cm}$ and breadth is $5 \text{ cm}$.
5.
Solve the simple equation for $x$: $$ 2x + 5 = 15 $$
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
A shopkeeper bought a bag for ₹$400$ and sold it for ₹$520$. Find his total profit and the profit percentage.
7.
Simplify the fraction $\frac{36}{48}$ to its lowest terms. Also, find an equivalent fraction of $\frac{36}{48}$ with a denominator of $12$.
8.
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. Find the measure of each angle of the triangle.
9.
A car travels a distance of $240 \text{ km}$ in $4 \text{ hours}$. How much distance will it cover in $7 \text{ hours}$ if it travels at the same constant speed?
10.
The perimeter of a square is $24 \text{ cm}$. Find the length of its side and calculate the area of the square.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Solve the following basic operations:
(a) Add the mixed fractions: $3 \frac{1}{2} + 2 \frac{1}{4}$. (2 Marks)
(b) Subtract $14.56$ from $25.3$. (3 Marks)
12.
Answer the following based on exponents and factors:
(a) Simplify the following using the laws of exponents and find its value:
$$ \frac{2^5 \times 3^4}{2^3 \times 3^2} $$ (3 Marks)
(b) Express the number $120$ as a product of its prime factors. (2 Marks)
13.
Word problems based on numbers and ratios:
(a) The sum of two consecutive integers is $27$. Form an equation and find the two integers. (3 Marks)
(b) Find the ratio of $500 \text{ ml}$ to $2 \text{ Litres}$ in its simplest form. (2 Marks)
