MINOR TEST 20 — CLASS VIII ICSE
Syllabus: Atomic Structure | The Language of Chemistry | Chemical Reactions
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Chemistry Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • Answers to this paper must be written neatly on the answer sheet provided separately.
  • You will not be allowed to write during the first 10 minutes. This time is to be spent in reading the question paper.
  • The paper consists of Three Sections: Section A (10 Marks), Section B (15 Marks), and Section C (15 Marks).
  • All questions are compulsory. Show chemical equations and state symbols wherever applicable.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Objective & Basic Conceptual Recall — 5 Questions x 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
[2 Marks]
Define the term 'Valency'. State the variable valency exhibited by Iron in:
(a) $\text{FeCl}_2$
(b) $\text{FeCl}_3$
Q2.
[2 Marks]
An atom of Aluminium has an Atomic Number ($Z$) of $13$ and a Mass Number ($A$) of $27$. Determine:
(a) The number of protons, electrons, and neutrons present in an atom of Aluminium.
(b) Its electronic configuration ($K, L, M$ shell representation).
Q3.
[2 Marks]
Differentiate between a Chemical Symbol and a Radical. Give one example with chemical formula/symbol for each.
Q4.
[2 Marks]
Write the balanced chemical equation with proper state symbols for the action of heat on solid Calcium Carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$). Identify the gaseous product formed.
Q5.
[2 Marks]
Classify the following reactions as Combination, Decomposition, Displacement, or Double Displacement:
(a) $\text{Zn}\,(s) + \text{CuSO}_4\,(aq) \rightarrow \text{ZnSO}_4\,(aq) + \text{Cu}\,(s)$
(b) $2\text{Mg}\,(s) + \text{O}_2\,(g) \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2\text{MgO}\,(s)$
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer & Application Type — 5 Questions x 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
[3 Marks]
Using the Criss-Cross Method, derive the chemical formulas for the following compounds:
(a) Aluminium Sulphate
(b) Calcium Hydroxide
(c) Ammonium Phosphate
Q7.
[3 Marks]
State three main postulates of Dalton's Atomic Theory regarding the nature of matter and atoms. Mention one modern modification to his theory.
Q8.
[3 Marks]
Balance the following chemical equations by step-by-step inspection:
(a) $\text{Fe} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + \text{H}_2$
(b) $\text{KClO}_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{KCl} + \text{O}_2$
(c) $\text{NaOH} + \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
Q9.
[3 Marks]
Give a balanced chemical equation and state your visual observation for each of the following chemical changes:
(a) Dilute Hydrochloric acid ($\text{HCl}$) is added to Zinc granules ($\text{Zn}$).
(b) Barium Chloride solution ($\text{BaCl}_2$) is mixed with Sodium Sulphate solution ($\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$).
(c) Copper Carbonate powder ($\text{CuCO}_3$) is heated strongly in a dry test tube.
Q10.
[3 Marks]
Differentiate between a Combination Reaction and a Decomposition Reaction based on:
(a) Number of reactants vs products.
(b) Energy requirements (Exothermic vs Endothermic nature).
(c) Provide one balanced chemical equation for each type.
SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer & Numerical/Derivation Type — 3 Questions x 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
[5 Marks]
(a) Tabulate the three fundamental subatomic particles—Electron, Proton, and Neutron—with respect to:
  1. Discoverer
  2. Relative Charge
  3. Relative Mass (in amu)
  4. Location in an atom
(3 Marks)
(b) Phosphorus has an Atomic Number $Z = 15$ and Mass Number $A = 31$. Represent its atomic structure schematically showing the nucleus and distribution of electrons in $K, L, M$ shells. (2 Marks)
Q12.
[5 Marks]
(a) State the Law of Conservation of Mass. (1 Mark)
(b) In a laboratory experiment, $10.0\,\text{g}$ of Calcium Carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$) on strong heating completely decomposed to form $5.6\,\text{g}$ of Calcium Oxide ($\text{CaO}$) residue and Carbon Dioxide gas ($\text{CO}_2$).
  1. Calculate the mass of Carbon Dioxide gas released into the atmosphere.
  2. Explain how this result verifies the Law of Conservation of Mass.
(2 Marks)
(c) Explain why a chemical equation must always be balanced in accordance with the Law of Conservation of Mass. (2 Marks)
Q13.
[5 Marks]
Define each of the following types of chemical reactions and write ONE complete balanced chemical equation with state symbols for each:
(a) Thermal Decomposition Reaction (2 Marks)
(b) Neutralization Reaction (1.5 Marks)
(c) Single Displacement Reaction (1.5 Marks)
