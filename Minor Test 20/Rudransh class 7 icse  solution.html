SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Simplify: $(2^3 \times 2^4) \div 2^5$
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Apply the product law of exponents ($a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$) to the numerator.
$(2^3 \times 2^4) = 2^{3+4} = 2^7$
Step 2: Apply the quotient law of exponents ($a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}$).
$2^7 \div 2^5 = 2^{7-5} = 2^2$
Final Answer: $2^2$ (or $4$)
Q2. Convert $35\%$ into a fraction in simplest form, and into a decimal.
[2 Marks]
Fraction Form: To convert a percentage to a fraction, divide by $100$.
$35\% = \frac{35}{100}$
Divide numerator and denominator by $5$: $\frac{35 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \frac{7}{20}$
Decimal Form: $35\% = \frac{35}{100} = 0.35$
Final Answer: Fraction = $\frac{7}{20}$, Decimal = $0.35$
Q3. Add: $3x - 4y + 2z$ and $2x + 5y - z$.
[2 Marks]
Write the expressions together and group the like terms:
$(3x - 4y + 2z) + (2x + 5y - z)$
$= (3x + 2x) + (-4y + 5y) + (2z - z)$
$= 5x + y + z$
Final Answer: $5x + y + z$
Q4. In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle A = 50^\circ$, $\angle B = 70^\circ$. Find $\angle C$. Name the triangle based on its angles.
[2 Marks]
By the Angle Sum Property of a triangle, the sum of interior angles is $180^\circ$.
$\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$
$50^\circ + 70^\circ + \angle C = 180^\circ$
$120^\circ + \angle C = 180^\circ \implies \angle C = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$
Since all three angles ($50^\circ, 70^\circ, 60^\circ$) are less than $90^\circ$, it is an acute-angled triangle.
Final Answer: $\angle C = 60^\circ$; Acute-angled triangle.
Q5. Evaluate $2a^2 - 3b + 4$ when $a = 2, b = -1$.
[2 Marks]
Substitute the given values into the expression:
$= 2(2)^2 - 3(-1) + 4$
Calculate the exponent and multiply:
$= 2(4) + 3 + 4$ (Note: $-3 \times -1 = +3$)
$= 8 + 3 + 4 = 15$
Final Answer: $15$

Q6. Rohan scored $45$ marks out of $60$. Find his percentage. If passing criteria is $40\%$, did he pass?
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Calculate Percentage
$\text{Percentage} = \left(\frac{\text{Marks Obtained}}{\text{Total Marks}}\right) \times 100\%$
$= \left(\frac{45}{60}\right) \times 100\%$
$= \left(\frac{3}{4}\right) \times 100\% = 3 \times 25\% = 75\%$
Step 2: Conclusion
Rohan scored $75\%$. Since $75\%$ is strictly greater than the passing mark of $40\%$, he successfully passed the test.
Final Answer: $75\%$; Yes, he passed.
Q7. Exterior angle is $110^\circ$, one interior opposite angle is $45^\circ$. Find the other two interior angles.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Use Exterior Angle Property
The exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of its two interior opposite angles. Let the unknown interior opposite angle be $x$.
$110^\circ = 45^\circ + x$
$x = 110^\circ - 45^\circ = 65^\circ$
Step 2: Find the third angle
The exterior angle and the adjacent interior angle form a linear pair (sum $= 180^\circ$). Let this angle be $y$.
$110^\circ + y = 180^\circ \implies y = 70^\circ$
(Alternatively, use angle sum property: $180^\circ - (45^\circ + 65^\circ) = 70^\circ$).
Final Answer: The other two angles are $65^\circ$ and $70^\circ$.
Q8. Simplify and express with a positive exponent: $\left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^5 \times \left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{-3}$
[3 Marks]
Use the law of exponents for multiplication with the same base: $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$
Here, the base $a = \frac{-3}{4}$, $m = 5$, and $n = -3$.
$= \left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{5 + (-3)}$
$= \left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{2}$
The exponent $2$ is already positive, so the requirement is satisfied.
Final Answer: $\left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{2}$
Q9. Subtract $4p^2q - 3pq + 5pq^2 - 8p + 7q - 10$ from $18 - 3p - 11q + 5pq - 2pq^2 + 5p^2q$.
[3 Marks]
To subtract, we reverse the signs of all terms in the expression to be subtracted and add:
$= (5p^2q - 2pq^2 + 5pq - 3p - 11q + 18) - (4p^2q + 5pq^2 - 3pq - 8p + 7q - 10)$
Distribute the negative sign:
$= 5p^2q - 2pq^2 + 5pq - 3p - 11q + 18 - 4p^2q - 5pq^2 + 3pq + 8p - 7q + 10$
Group like terms together:
$= (5p^2q - 4p^2q) + (-2pq^2 - 5pq^2) + (5pq + 3pq) + (-3p + 8p) + (-11q - 7q) + (18 + 10)$
$= p^2q - 7pq^2 + 8pq + 5p - 18q + 28$
Final Answer: $p^2q - 7pq^2 + 8pq + 5p - 18q + 28$
Q10. Is it possible to construct a triangle with side lengths $3\text{ cm}$, $6\text{ cm}$, and $7\text{ cm}$? Reason your answer.
[3 Marks]
According to the Triangle Inequality Theorem, the sum of the lengths of any two sides of a triangle must always be strictly greater than the length of the third side.
Let's check all three conditions:
1. $3 + 6 = 9 > 7$ (True)
2. $3 + 7 = 10 > 6$ (True)
3. $6 + 7 = 13 > 3$ (True)
Since all three conditions are satisfied, a triangle with these dimensions can be formed.
Final Answer: Yes, it is possible because the sum of any two sides is greater than the third side.
SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. Mr. Sharma earns ₹ $25,000$ per month. He spends $20\%$ on rent and $40\%$ on food. Find (a) Total spend on rent and food, (b) Monthly savings, (c) Savings as a fraction of total income.
[5 Marks]
a) Total Amount Spent:
Rent = $20\%$ of ₹ $25,000 = \frac{20}{100} \times 25000 = \text{₹ } 5,000$
Food = $40\%$ of ₹ $25,000 = \frac{40}{100} \times 25000 = \text{₹ } 10,000$
Total Spent = $5000 + 10000 = \text{₹ } 15,000$
b) Monthly Savings:
Savings = Total Income $-$ Total Expenditure
$= 25,000 - 15,000 = \text{₹ } 10,000$
c) Fraction of Savings:
$\text{Fraction} = \frac{\text{Savings}}{\text{Total Income}} = \frac{10000}{25000}$
Divide by $5000$ on both numerator and denominator: $\frac{2}{5}$
Final Answers: (a) ₹ $15,000$   (b) ₹ $10,000$   (c) $\frac{2}{5}$
Q12. Simplify: $2x(x - y^2) - 3y(xy + 2x) - xy(x + y)$. Find its value when $x = -1, y = 2$.
[5 Marks]
a) Simplifying the Expression:
Expand each bracket carefully:
$= (2x^2 - 2xy^2) - (3xy^2 + 6xy) - (x^2y + xy^2)$
Remove brackets (be mindful of negative signs):
$= 2x^2 - 2xy^2 - 3xy^2 - 6xy - x^2y - xy^2$
Group the $xy^2$ terms together ($-2 - 3 - 1 = -6$):
$= 2x^2 - x^2y - 6xy^2 - 6xy$
b) Substitution & Evaluation:
Substitute $x = -1$ and $y = 2$ into the simplified equation:
$= 2(-1)^2 - (-1)^2(2) - 6(-1)(2)^2 - 6(-1)(2)$
Calculate exponents first: $(-1)^2 = 1, (2)^2 = 4$
$= 2(1) - (1)(2) - 6(-1)(4) - (-12)$
$= 2 - 2 - (-24) + 12$
$= 2 - 2 + 24 + 12 = 36$
Final Answer: Simplified = $2x^2 - x^2y - 6xy^2 - 6xy$; Value = $36$
Q13. a) In a right-angled triangle, the acute angles are in the ratio $2:3$. Find them. b) Angles of a triangle are $x^\circ, (x-20)^\circ, (x+50)^\circ$. Find $x$.
[5 Marks]
a) Acute Angles of a Right Triangle:
One angle is $90^\circ$. By angle sum property, the sum of the remaining two acute angles is $180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$.
Let the angles be $2a$ and $3a$.
$2a + 3a = 90^\circ \implies 5a = 90^\circ \implies a = 18^\circ$
The angles are: $2 \times 18^\circ = 36^\circ$ and $3 \times 18^\circ = 54^\circ$.
b) Finding x using Angle Sum Property:
The sum of all angles in a triangle is $180^\circ$.
$x + (x - 20) + (x + 50) = 180$
$3x + 30 = 180$
$3x = 180 - 30 = 150$
$x = \frac{150}{3} = 50$
Final Answers: (a) $36^\circ$ and $54^\circ$   (b) $x = 50$
