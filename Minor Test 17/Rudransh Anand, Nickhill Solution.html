SECTION A
Q1. Find the square root of 729 using the prime factorization method.
[2 Marks]
Answer: 27
Step 1: Perform prime factorization of 729.
$729 \div 3 = 243$
$243 \div 3 = 81$
$81 \div 3 = 27$
$27 \div 3 = 9$
$9 \div 3 = 3$
$3 \div 3 = 1$
Step 2: Write 729 as a product of its prime factors:
$729 = \underline{3 \times 3} \times \underline{3 \times 3} \times \underline{3 \times 3}$
Step 3: Pair the factors and take one number from each pair:
$\sqrt{729} = 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$.
Q2. Is 243 a perfect cube? Give mathematical reasoning to support your answer.
[2 Marks]
Answer: No, 243 is not a perfect cube.
Step 1: Find the prime factorization of 243.
$243 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3$
Step 2: Group the factors in triplets.
$243 = (3 \times 3 \times 3) \times 3 \times 3$
Reasoning: After grouping the prime factors in triplets, the prime factor 3 is left over twice (it does not form a complete triplet). Therefore, 243 is not a perfect cube.
Q3. Find the smallest whole number by which 392 must be multiplied so that the product becomes a perfect cube.
[2 Marks]
Answer: 7
Step 1: Find the prime factorization of 392.
$392 = 2 \times 196 = 2 \times 2 \times 98 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 49 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 7 \times 7$
Step 2: Group factors in triplets.
$392 = (2 \times 2 \times 2) \times 7 \times 7$
Step 3: The prime factor 7 does not appear in a group of three. To make it a perfect cube, we need one more 7.
Therefore, 392 must be multiplied by 7.
Q4. Without doing any calculation, explain why the numbers 153 and 257 are surely not perfect squares.
[2 Marks]
Answer: Due to their unit digits (3 and 7).
Reasoning: We know that the square of any integer always ends with the digits 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, or 9 at units place.
A perfect square can never end with 2, 3, 7, or 8.
- The number 153 ends in 3.
- The number 257 ends in 7.
Hence, neither 153 nor 257 can be perfect squares.
Q5. Evaluate: (i) $\sqrt{0.0009}$ (ii) $\sqrt[3]{0.008}$
[2 Marks]
Answer: (i) 0.03   (ii) 0.2
Part (i):
$\sqrt{0.0009} = \sqrt{\frac{9}{10000}} = \frac{\sqrt{9}}{\sqrt{10000}} = \frac{3}{100} = 0.03$

Part (ii):
$\sqrt[3]{0.008} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{8}{1000}} = \frac{\sqrt[3]{8}}{\sqrt[3]{1000}} = \frac{2}{10} = 0.2$

Q6. Find the smallest square number that is perfectly divisible by each of the numbers 4, 9, and 10.
[3 Marks]
Answer: 900
Step 1: Find the LCM of the given numbers to find the smallest number divisible by 4, 9, and 10.
LCM(4, 9, 10) = 180.
Step 2: Check if 180 is a perfect square by prime factorization.
$180 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = (2 \times 2) \times (3 \times 3) \times 5$
Step 3: Since the prime factor 5 is not in a pair, 180 is not a perfect square.
To make it a perfect square, we must multiply 180 by 5.
Smallest square number = $180 \times 5 = 900$.

Q8. Find the smallest whole number by which 9408 must be divided so that the quotient is a perfect square. Also, find the square root of the quotient.
[3 Marks]
Answer: Divide by 3; Square root is 56.
Step 1: Prime factorize 9408.
$9408 = 2 \times 4704 = 2^2 \times 2352 = 2^3 \times 1176 = 2^4 \times 588 = 2^5 \times 294 = 2^6 \times 147 = 2^6 \times 3 \times 49 = 2^6 \times 3 \times 7^2$
$9408 = \underline{2 \times 2} \times \underline{2 \times 2} \times \underline{2 \times 2} \times \underline{7 \times 7} \times 3$
Step 2: The prime factor 3 is without a pair. Thus, 9408 must be divided by 3 to make the quotient a perfect square.
Step 3: Quotient = $9408 \div 3 = 3136$.
Step 4: $\sqrt{3136} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 7 = 8 \times 7 = 56$.
Q9. A gardener has 1000 plants. He wants to plant these such that the number of rows and columns remain the same. Find the minimum number of plants he needs more.
[3 Marks]
Answer: 24 plants
Step 1: Let the number of rows and columns be $x$. Total plants needed = $x \times x = x^2$.
We need to find a perfect square just greater than 1000.
Step 2: Find the square root of 1000 to identify the nearest squares.
$31^2 = 31 \times 31 = 961$. (This is less than 1000, so he will have plants left over, but he wants to ADD plants).
The next whole number is 32.
$32^2 = 32 \times 32 = 1024$.
Step 3: To arrange them in a $32 \times 32$ grid, he needs 1024 plants.
Number of plants he needs more = $1024 - 1000 = 24$.
Q10. Find the value of $\sqrt[3]{-2744}$ and clearly state the property used for negative cube roots.
[3 Marks]
Answer: -14
Property Used: The cube root of a negative number is negative. Mathematically, $\sqrt[3]{-x} = -\sqrt[3]{x}$.
Step 1: We need to find $\sqrt[3]{-2744} = -\sqrt[3]{2744}$.
Step 2: Prime factorize 2744.
$2744 = 2 \times 1372 = 2^2 \times 686 = 2^3 \times 343 = 2^3 \times 7^3$
$2744 = (2 \times 7)^3 = 14^3$.
Step 3: Therefore, $\sqrt[3]{2744} = 14$.
Applying the property: $\sqrt[3]{-2744} = -14$.

Q12. Three numbers are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. If the sum of their cubes is $33957$, find all the three original numbers.
[5 Marks]
Answer: 14, 21, and 28
Step 1: Let the common ratio multiplier be $x$. Therefore, the three numbers are $2x$, $3x$, and $4x$.
Step 2: According to the problem, the sum of their cubes is 33957.
$(2x)^3 + (3x)^3 + (4x)^3 = 33957$
Step 3: Expand the cubes:
$8x^3 + 27x^3 + 64x^3 = 33957$
Step 4: Add the terms:
$(8 + 27 + 64)x^3 = 33957$
$99x^3 = 33957$
Step 5: Solve for $x^3$:
$x^3 = \frac{33957}{99}$
$x^3 = 343$
Step 6: Find the cube root of 343.
$x = \sqrt[3]{343} = 7$ (since $7 \times 7 \times 7 = 343$)
Step 7: Calculate the original numbers:
First number = $2x = 2(7) = 14$
Second number = $3x = 3(7) = 21$
Third number = $4x = 4(7) = 28$
Q13. (a) Find the greatest 4-digit number which is a perfect square.
(b) Find the least number that must be subtracted from 5607 so as to get a perfect square. Also, find the square root of this perfect square.
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) 9801   (b) Subtract 131; Root is 74
Part (a): Greatest 4-digit perfect square
The greatest 4-digit number is 9999.
We find the square root of 9999 by long division:
Grouping: $\overline{99}\overline{99}$
$9 \times 9 = 81$. Remainder = $99 - 81 = 18$.
Bring down 99. New dividend = 1899. Divisor becomes $9 \times 2 = 18$.
Place 9 next to 18 to make 189. $189 \times 9 = 1701$.
Remainder = $1899 - 1701 = 198$.
This means $9999$ is $198$ more than the square of $99$.
Greatest 4-digit perfect square = $9999 - 198 = 9801$. ($99^2 = 9801$).

Part (b): Subtract from 5607
We find the square root of 5607 by long division:
Grouping: $\overline{56}\overline{07}$
$7 \times 7 = 49$. Remainder = $56 - 49 = 7$.
Bring down 07. New dividend = 707. Divisor becomes $7 \times 2 = 14$.
Place 4 next to 14. $144 \times 4 = 576$. (Note: $145 \times 5 = 725$, which is $>707$).
Remainder = $707 - 576 = 131$.
Since the remainder is 131, we must subtract 131 from 5607 to get a perfect square.
Perfect square = $5607 - 131 = 5476$.
The square root of 5476 is the quotient, which is 74.
