Minor Test 17
Class: 6 ICSE Subjects: Physics (Light & Force) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
What is meant by the rectilinear propagation of light? Give one natural phenomenon that occurs due to this property.
2.
Define force. State its S.I. unit.
3.
Differentiate between transparent and translucent materials. Give one example of each.
4.
What are non-contact forces? Name any two non-contact forces.
5.
State any two effects that a force can have on a stationary or moving object.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Why do we slip easily on a smooth wet floor? Which specific contact force is responsible for helping us walk normally? Explain briefly.
7.
How is a shadow formed? State the three essential things required for the formation of a shadow.
8.
Identify the type of force acting in the following cases:
(a) A fruit falling from a branch towards the ground.
(b) A bullock pulling a heavy cart.
(c) Tiny bits of paper getting attracted to a plastic ruler rubbed with dry hair.
9.
Explain the basic principle of a pinhole camera. State the two characteristics of the image formed by it.
10.
A child is pushing a heavy table, but the table does not move. Has the child applied force? Why is the table not moving despite the applied force?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) With the help of a neat and labeled diagram, explain the formation of a Solar Eclipse. (3 marks)
(b) Why are we advised not to look at a solar eclipse directly with naked eyes? (2 marks)
12.
Force cannot be seen, but it can be judged by its effects. Explain any five effects of force on an object. Provide one clear example for each effect.
13.
(a) What is meant by the reflection of light? How does a mirror form an image? (3 marks)
(b) Differentiate between luminous and non-luminous objects, giving one example for each. (2 marks)
