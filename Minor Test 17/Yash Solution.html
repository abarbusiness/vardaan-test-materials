SECTION A

Q1. State the two laws of reflection of light.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

The two laws of reflection are:

  1. The angle of incidence is always equal to the angle of reflection ($\angle i = \angle r$). [1 Mark]
  2. The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal at the point of incidence, all lie in the same plane. [1 Mark]
Q2. A car is moving with a speed of 90 km/h. Convert this speed into meters per second (m/s).
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Given, Speed = $90 \text{ km/h}$

To convert km/h to m/s, we multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$. [1 Mark for concept]

$$ \text{Speed in m/s} = 90 \times \frac{5}{18} = 5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ m/s} $$

Answer: 25 m/s [1 Mark for correct answer]

Q3. Mention any two characteristics of the image formed by a plane mirror.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Characteristics of an image formed by a plane mirror (Any two):

  • The image is virtual and erect.
  • The image is laterally inverted.
  • The size of the image is equal to the size of the object.
  • The image is formed as far behind the mirror as the object is in front of it.

[1 Mark for each correct point]

Q4. A cyclist travels at a constant speed of 15 m/s. Calculate the distance covered by the cyclist in 2 hours (in kilometers).
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Speed = $15 \text{ m/s}$. Time = $2 \text{ hours}$.

First, convert speed into km/h:

$$ \text{Speed} = 15 \times \frac{18}{5} \text{ km/h} = 3 \times 18 = 54 \text{ km/h} $$

[1 Mark for conversion]

Distance = Speed $\times$ Time

$$ \text{Distance} = 54 \text{ km/h} \times 2 \text{ h} = 108 \text{ km} $$

Answer: 108 km [1 Mark for final answer]

Q5. Define the terms: (a) Pole of a spherical mirror (b) Principal axis
[2 Marks]
Solution:
  • (a) Pole: The geometric center of the reflecting surface of a spherical mirror is called its pole. It is usually denoted by 'P'. [1 Mark]
  • (b) Principal Axis: The imaginary straight line passing through the pole and the center of curvature of a spherical mirror is called the principal axis. [1 Mark]

Q7. A boy walks from his home to school at a speed of 3 km/h and returns back at 2 km/h. If he takes 5 hours in total, find the distance between his home and the school.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Let the distance between home and school be $x \text{ km}$.

Time taken to go to school ($t_1$) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{x}{3} \text{ hours}$.

Time taken to return ($t_2$) = $\frac{x}{2} \text{ hours}$. [1 Mark]

Total time taken = $t_1 + t_2 = 5 \text{ hours}$.

$$ \frac{x}{3} + \frac{x}{2} = 5 $$ $$ \frac{2x + 3x}{6} = 5 $$ $$ \frac{5x}{6} = 5 $$

[1 Mark for equation setup]

$$ 5x = 30 \implies x = \frac{30}{5} = 6 \text{ km} $$

Answer: The distance between his home and school is 6 km. [1 Mark]

Q8. A ray of light strikes a plane mirror such that the angle between the incident ray and the surface of the mirror is 30°. Find (a) angle of incidence (b) angle of reflection (c) Draw diagram.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Given, Angle with the mirror surface = $30^\circ$.

(a) Angle of Incidence ($\angle i$): The angle between the normal (which is at $90^\circ$ to the surface) and the incident ray.
$\angle i = 90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$ [1 Mark]

(b) Angle of Reflection ($\angle r$): According to the law of reflection, $\angle i = \angle r$.
Therefore, $\angle r = 60^\circ$ [1 Mark]

(c) Ray Diagram:
(Students should draw a horizontal line for the mirror with shading on one side. A perpendicular dashed line for the normal. An incident ray coming in at $60^\circ$ to the normal, and reflected ray leaving at $60^\circ$ to the normal. Angles should be clearly labeled.) [1 Mark for correct diagram]

Q9. A train 200 meters long crosses a stationary electric pole in 10 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

To cross a stationary pole, the train has to cover a distance equal to its own length.

Distance ($D$) = $200 \text{ m}$
Time ($T$) = $10 \text{ s}$ [1 Mark for conceptual logic]

$$ \text{Speed in m/s} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{200}{10} = 20 \text{ m/s} $$

[1 Mark for speed in m/s]

Converting to km/h:

$$ \text{Speed in km/h} = 20 \times \frac{18}{5} = 4 \times 18 = 72 \text{ km/h} $$

Answer: 72 km/h [1 Mark for final conversion]

Q10. What is meant by 'Lateral Inversion'? Explain with the help of a daily life example why the word 'AMBULANCE' is written backwards.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Lateral Inversion: The phenomenon due to which the left side of an object appears as the right side in its image, and the right side appears as the left side in a plane mirror, is called lateral inversion. [1.5 Marks]

Example (AMBULANCE): The word AMBULANCE is written laterally inverted (backwards) on the front of hospital vans so that when the driver of a vehicle moving ahead looks in their rear-view mirror, the laterally inverted image of the word is inverted again. This makes the word read correctly as 'AMBULANCE', allowing the driver to quickly give way to the emergency vehicle. [1.5 Marks]

SECTION C

Q11. With the help of a neat diagram, explain image formation by a plane mirror. Prove using geometry that the image is at the same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front of it.
[5 Marks]
Solution:

Diagram: [2 Marks for accurate diagram with virtual rays backward]
(Diagram requirement: A point object O in front of plane mirror MM'. Two diverging rays OA and OB strike mirror. Normals drawn at A and B. Reflected rays AC and BD drawn following laws of reflection. Reflected rays produced backward to meet at virtual image I. Perpendicular ON drawn to mirror and extended to I.)

Geometric Proof:

Let ON be perpendicular to the mirror surface from object O, and extended backward to meet the virtual image I.
Let an incident ray strike the mirror at an angle of incidence $i$ and reflect at angle $r$. By the law of reflection, $\angle i = \angle r$.

Consider the triangles formed by the object, the image, the normal, and the point of incidence (say point A).
In $\Delta ONA$ and $\Delta INA$:

$$ \angle ONA = \angle INA = 90^\circ \text{ (ON is perpendicular to mirror)} $$ $$ NA = NA \text{ (Common side)} $$

Because the normal at A is parallel to the normal ON, alternate interior angles are equal: $\angle NOA = \angle i$.
Also, corresponding angles are equal for the backward extension of the reflected ray: $\angle NIA = \angle r$.

Since $\angle i = \angle r$, we get $\angle NOA = \angle NIA$.

Therefore, by Angle-Side-Angle (ASA) congruency criterion, $\Delta ONA \cong \Delta INA$. [2 Marks for mathematical logic]

Since the triangles are congruent, their corresponding parts are equal (CPCTC).
Thus, $ON = IN$. [1 Mark for conclusion]

Conclusion: The distance of the object in front of the mirror ($ON$) is equal to the distance of the image behind the mirror ($IN$).

Q12. (a) Train 150m long, speed 72 km/h crosses 250m bridge. Time? (b) Athlete 400m in 50s. Average speed in km/h?
[5 Marks]
Solution:

Part (a): [2.5 Marks]

Total distance to be covered by the train to cross the bridge = Length of Train + Length of Bridge

$$ \text{Total Distance } (D) = 150 \text{ m} + 250 \text{ m} = 400 \text{ m} $$

Speed of train = $72 \text{ km/h}$. Converting to m/s:

$$ \text{Speed } (S) = 72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ m/s} $$

Time taken ($T$) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$

$$ T = \frac{400}{20} = 20 \text{ seconds} $$

Part (b): [2.5 Marks]

Distance ($D$) = $400 \text{ m}$
Time ($T$) = $50 \text{ s}$

Average speed in m/s:

$$ S = \frac{D}{T} = \frac{400}{50} = 8 \text{ m/s} $$

Converting to km/h:

$$ \text{Speed in km/h} = 8 \times \frac{18}{5} = \frac{144}{5} = 28.8 \text{ km/h} $$

Answers: (a) 20 seconds, (b) 28.8 km/h

Q13. Car goes A to B at 60 km/h, returns at 40 km/h. (a) Average speed? (b) If total time is 5 hrs, find distance A to B.
[5 Marks]
Solution:

Part (a): Average Speed [3 Marks]

Let the distance between City A and City B be $x \text{ km}$.

Time taken from A to B ($t_1$) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{x}{60} \text{ hours}$.

Time taken from B to A ($t_2$) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{x}{40} \text{ hours}$.

Total Distance = $x + x = 2x$.

Total Time = $t_1 + t_2 = \frac{x}{60} + \frac{x}{40}$

$$ \text{Total Time} = \frac{2x + 3x}{120} = \frac{5x}{120} = \frac{x}{24} \text{ hours} $$

Average Speed = $\frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}$

$$ \text{Avg Speed} = \frac{2x}{\frac{x}{24}} = \frac{2x \times 24}{x} = 48 \text{ km/h} $$

(Alternatively, students can use the formula $\frac{2ab}{a+b} = \frac{2 \times 60 \times 40}{60+40} = 48 \text{ km/h}$)

Part (b): Distance between A and B [2 Marks]

We know Average Speed = $48 \text{ km/h}$ and Total Time = $5 \text{ hours}$.

Total Distance covered (round trip) = Average Speed $\times$ Total Time

$$ \text{Total Distance} = 48 \times 5 = 240 \text{ km} $$

Since total distance represents a round trip (A to B and back to A), the exact distance between City A and City B is half of the total distance.

$$ \text{Distance between A & B} = \frac{240}{2} = 120 \text{ km} $$

Answers: (a) 48 km/h, (b) 120 km
