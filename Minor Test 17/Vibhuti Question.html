Minor Test 17: Algebraic Expressions
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Use proper mathematical notation.
SECTION A (10 Marks)
(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the terms and their coefficients for the following algebraic expression: \( 5xyz^2 - 3zy \)
2.
Classify the following polynomials as monomials, binomials, or trinomials:
(i) \( x + y \)
(ii) \( 1000 \)
(iii) \( x + x^2 + x^3 + x^4 \)
(iv) \( 7 + y + 5x \)
3.
Add the following algebraic expressions: \( ab - bc \), \( bc - ca \), and \( ca - ab \).
4.
Subtract \( 4a - 7ab + 3b + 12 \) from \( 12a - 9ab + 5b - 3 \).
5.
Find the product of the following pairs of monomials:
(i) \( 4, 7p \)
(ii) \( -4p, 7pq \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)
(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Simplify the expression \( 3x(4x - 5) + 3 \) and find its value for \( x = 3 \).
7.
Multiply the binomials: \( (2x + 5) \) and \( (4x - 3) \).
8.
Using the suitable standard identity, evaluate: \( (102)^2 \).
9.
Subtract \( 3pq(p - q) \) from \( 2pq(p + q) \).
10.
Using the identity \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \), find the value of \( (99)^2 \).
SECTION C (15 Marks)
(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Simplify the following expression:
\( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \)
(b) Add the following expressions:
\( p(p-q) \), \( q(q-r) \) and \( r(r-p) \)
12.
Using suitable identities, evaluate the following:
(i) \( 71^2 \)
(ii) \( 297 \times 303 \)
13.
Show that:
(i) \( (3x + 7)^2 - 84x = (3x - 7)^2 \)
(ii) \( (a - b)(a + b) + (b - c)(b + c) + (c - a)(c + a) = 0 \)
