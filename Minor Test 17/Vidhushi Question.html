Minor Test 17
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working clearly where required.
  • Use of calculators is not permitted.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the ratio of \( 5 \text{ km} \) to \( 500 \text{ m} \).
2.
Express the ratio \( 45 : 108 \) in its simplest form.
3.
Are the ratios \( 25 \text{ g} : 30 \text{ g} \) and \( 40 \text{ kg} : 48 \text{ kg} \) in proportion? Justify your answer.
4.
Find the value of \( x \) if \( 3 : 4 :: x : 20 \).
5.
Out of 40 students in a class, 16 are girls. Find the ratio of the number of girls to the number of boys in the simplest form.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Divide ₹ 1,500 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio of \( 3 : 2 \).
7.
The lengths of the sides of a triangle are in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 4 \). If the perimeter of the triangle is \( 63 \text{ cm} \), find the length of each side of the triangle.
8.
A car travels \( 165 \text{ km} \) in 3 hours. How far will it travel in 5 hours, assuming the speed remains uniform?
9.
Two numbers are in the ratio \( 5 : 8 \). If the sum of the numbers is \( 182 \), find the two numbers.
10.
A map is given with a scale of \( 2 \text{ cm} = 1000 \text{ km} \). What is the actual distance between two places in kms, if the distance on the map is \( 2.5 \text{ cm} \)?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
In a school library, the ratio of mathematics books to science books is \( 4 : 5 \). If there are 320 mathematics books, find:
(a) The number of science books.
(b) The total number of mathematics and science books.
(c) The new ratio of mathematics to science books if 40 more science books are added to the library.
12.
A certain amount of money is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio \( 2 : 3 : 5 \).
(a) If C gets ₹ 1,500 more than B, what is the total amount distributed?
(b) Find the individual share of A.
13.
Solve the following:
(a) Check if the following quantities are in continued proportion: \( 4, 12, 36 \). (2 Marks)
(b) A machine in a soft drink factory fills 840 bottles in 6 hours. How many bottles will it fill in 5 hours? (3 Marks)
