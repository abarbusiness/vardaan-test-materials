SECTION A (10 Marks)
1. Define Mechanical Advantage. Write its mathematical formula.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Mechanical Advantage (MA) is defined as the ratio of the load lifted (or overcome) by a machine to the effort applied to the machine.

\( \text{Mechanical Advantage (MA)} = \frac{\text{Load (L)}}{\text{Effort (E)}} \)

3. What is a Class II lever? Give one common everyday example of it.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

A Class II lever is a lever in which the Load (L) is situated between the Fulcrum (F) and the Effort (E).

Example: A wheelbarrow, a nutcracker, or a bottle opener. (Any one is correct).

4. According to the kinetic theory of matter, state the properties of intermolecular space and intermolecular force of attraction in a gaseous state.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
  • Intermolecular space: It is very large (maximum compared to solids and liquids) because the particles are far apart.
  • Intermolecular force of attraction: It is negligible (or minimum), which allows the gas particles to move freely in all directions.
5. If the effort arm of a lever is 40 cm and the load arm is 10 cm, calculate its mechanical advantage.
[2 Marks]
Solution:

Given:

  • Effort Arm = 40 cm
  • Load Arm = 10 cm

By the principle of moments for a lever:

\( \text{Mechanical Advantage (MA)} = \frac{\text{Effort Arm}}{\text{Load Arm}} \)
\( \text{MA} = \frac{40 \text{ cm}}{10 \text{ cm}} = 4 \)

Ans: The mechanical advantage is 4.

SECTION B (15 Marks)
6. Explain the process of sublimation with an example. How does this process fundamentally differ from melting?
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Sublimation: It is the process in which a solid changes directly into its gaseous state on heating, without passing through the intermediate liquid state.
Example: Heating of camphor, dry ice (solid CO₂), or naphthalene balls.

Difference from melting: Melting is the process where a solid changes into a liquid on heating. Sublimation skips the liquid phase entirely and directly becomes a gas.

7. A crowbar of length 120 cm has its fulcrum situated at a distance of 20 cm from the load. Calculate the mechanical advantage of the crowbar.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

A crowbar acts as a lever (usually Class I). Let the fulcrum be between the load and effort.

  • Total length of crowbar = 120 cm
  • Load arm (distance from fulcrum to load) = 20 cm

The effort arm will be the remaining length of the crowbar:

\( \text{Effort Arm} = \text{Total Length} - \text{Load Arm} \)
\( \text{Effort Arm} = 120 \text{ cm} - 20 \text{ cm} = 100 \text{ cm} \)
\( \text{MA} = \frac{\text{Effort Arm}}{\text{Load Arm}} = \frac{100}{20} = 5 \)

Ans: The mechanical advantage of the crowbar is 5.

8. Why do solids possess a definite shape and a definite volume while gases do not? Explain this strictly on the basis of the arrangement of particles.
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Solids: In solids, the constituent particles are very closely packed. The intermolecular force of attraction is extremely strong, and the intermolecular space is almost negligible. Because particles cannot move from their fixed positions and can only vibrate, solids retain a definite shape and a definite volume.

Gases: In gases, the particles are very loosely packed. The intermolecular force of attraction is negligible, and the intermolecular spaces are very large. Therefore, particles are free to move in any direction and fill the entire available space, giving gases neither a definite shape nor a definite volume.

10. State the Law of Conservation of Mass. If 4g of carbon reacts completely with 10.6g of oxygen to form carbon dioxide, what will be the total mass of the carbon dioxide formed?
[3 Marks]
Solution:

Law of Conservation of Mass: It states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. In a closed system, the total mass of the reactants is equal to the total mass of the products.

Calculation:

\( \text{Mass of Reactants} = \text{Mass of Carbon} + \text{Mass of Oxygen} \)
\( \text{Mass of Reactants} = 4\text{g} + 10.6\text{g} = 14.6\text{g} \)

According to the law, Mass of Products = Mass of Reactants.

Ans: The total mass of the carbon dioxide formed is 14.6g.

SECTION C (15 Marks)
11. (a) Define an inclined plane and state its primary use in daily life.
(b) A block and tackle system consists of 4 pulleys. If an effort of 200 N is required to lift a load of 600 N, calculate the Mechanical Advantage, Velocity Ratio, and Efficiency of the system.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) An inclined plane is a rigid, flat sloping surface that connects a lower level to a higher level.
Use: It acts as a force multiplier and is used to easily load heavy drums or cargo into trucks by rolling or pushing them up the slope, requiring less effort than lifting them vertically.
(b) Given:
  • Number of pulleys (n) = 4. Hence, Velocity Ratio (\( \text{VR} \)) = 4.
  • Effort (\( \text{E} \)) = 200 N
  • Load (\( \text{L} \)) = 600 N

1. Mechanical Advantage (MA):

\( \text{MA} = \frac{\text{L}}{\text{E}} = \frac{600}{200} = 3 \)

2. Velocity Ratio (VR): For a block and tackle system, VR = total number of pulleys.

\( \text{VR} = 4 \)

3. Efficiency (\( \eta \)):

\( \eta = \left( \frac{\text{MA}}{\text{VR}} \right) \times 100\% = \left( \frac{3}{4} \right) \times 100\% = 75\% \)
12. (a) What is meant by a chemical formula? Write the chemical formulae for Calcium Carbonate and Magnesium Chloride.
(b) Write a balanced chemical equation for the reaction between Hydrogen and Oxygen to form Water.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Chemical Formula: A chemical formula is a symbolic representation of a molecule of an element or a compound. It denotes the exact number and types of atoms present in one molecule of that substance.

Formulae:
  • Calcium Carbonate: \( \text{CaCO}_3 \) (Valency of Ca is +2, CO₃ is -2)
  • Magnesium Chloride: \( \text{MgCl}_2 \) (Valency of Mg is +2, Cl is -1)
(b) Balanced Chemical Equation:
Hydrogen gas (\( \text{H}_2 \)) reacts with oxygen gas (\( \text{O}_2 \)) to form liquid water (\( \text{H}_2\text{O} \)).
\( 2\text{H}_2(g) + \text{O}_2(g) \longrightarrow 2\text{H}_2\text{O}(l) \)
(Balancing requires 2 molecules of Hydrogen reacting with 1 molecule of Oxygen to give 2 molecules of Water.)
13. (a) Classify the following substances into elements, compounds, and mixtures: Iron, Air, Water, 22-carat gold.
(b) Explain how a pair of scissors (Class I lever) differs from a nutcracker (Class II lever) in terms of the relative position of Load, Effort, and Fulcrum. Draw simple line diagrams for both to support your answer.
[5 Marks]
Solution:
(a) Classification:
  • Iron: Element (Made of only Fe atoms).
  • Air: Mixture (Homogeneous mixture of gases like N₂, O₂, CO₂, etc.).
  • Water: Compound (\( \text{H}_2\text{O} \), chemically combined in fixed ratio).
  • 22-carat gold: Mixture (Alloy of Gold with Copper/Silver).
(b) Difference in Levers:

Pair of Scissors (Class I lever): The Fulcrum (F) is located exactly in the middle, between the Load (L) at the blades and the Effort (E) applied at the handles.
Configuration: L --- F --- E

Nutcracker (Class II lever): The Load (L) (the nut) is placed in the middle, between the Fulcrum (F) at the hinge and the Effort (E) applied at the end of the handles.
Configuration: F --- L --- E

(The student should draw a basic straight line indicating F, L, E relative positions and direction of arrows for both classes.)
