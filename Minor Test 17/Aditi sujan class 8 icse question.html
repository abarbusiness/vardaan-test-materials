MINOR TEST 17: FACTORISATION
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C based on ML Aggarwal curriculum.
  • Write your answers neatly and show all steps clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Factorise by taking out the common factor: \( 8xy^3 + 12x^2y^2 \)
2.
Factorise the expression completely by grouping: \( x^2 + xy - x - y \)
3.
Factorise using standard algebraic identities: \( x^2 - 12x + 36 \)
4.
Factorise using the difference of two squares identity: \( 25a^2 - 49b^2 \)
5.
Factorise the simple quadratic trinomial: \( x^2 + 7x + 12 \)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Factorise by grouping terms: \( 5p^2 - 8pq - 10p + 16q \)
7.
Factorise completely using identities: \( 36p^2 - 60pq + 25q^2 \)
8.
Factorise the expression: \( x^2 - y^2 - 6y - 9 \)
9.
Factorise the trinomial by splitting the middle term: \( x^2 - 5x - 24 \)
10.
Factorise: \( a(a - 2b) - 3(a - 2b) \)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Factorise the following expressions completely:
(a) \( 3x^2y - 3xy + 12x - 12 \) (3 Marks)
(b) \( x^2 - \frac{1}{x^2} \) (2 Marks)
12.
Factorise by splitting the middle term completely:
(a) \( 2x^2 + 7x + 3 \) (3 Marks)
(b) \( 3x^2 - 10x - 8 \) (2 Marks)
13.
Factorise the following expressions using grouping and identity methods:
(a) \( a^3 - a^2b - ab^2 + b^3 \) (3 Marks)
(b) Evaluate using factorisation: \( (102)^2 - (98)^2 \) (2 Marks)
