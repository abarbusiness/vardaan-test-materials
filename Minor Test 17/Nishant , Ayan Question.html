Minor Test 17
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Science (Physics & Chemistry) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
  • Use of appropriate scientific units is mandatory in numerical calculations.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define Mechanical Advantage. Write its mathematical formula.
2.
State any two main points of difference between an atom and a molecule.
3.
What is a Class II lever? Give one common everyday example of it.
4.
According to the kinetic theory of matter, state the properties of intermolecular space and intermolecular force of attraction in a gaseous state.
5.
If the effort arm of a lever is 40 cm and the load arm is 10 cm, calculate its mechanical advantage.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Explain the process of sublimation with an example. How does this process fundamentally differ from melting?
7.
A crowbar of length 120 cm has its fulcrum situated at a distance of 20 cm from the load. Calculate the mechanical advantage of the crowbar.
8.
Why do solids possess a definite shape and a definite volume while gases do not? Explain this strictly on the basis of the arrangement of particles.
9.
Draw a neat, labeled diagram of a single fixed pulley. State its theoretical mechanical advantage and velocity ratio.
10.
State the Law of Conservation of Mass. If 4g of carbon reacts completely with 10.6g of oxygen to form carbon dioxide, what will be the total mass of the carbon dioxide formed?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Define an inclined plane and state its primary use in daily life. (2 Marks)
(b) A block and tackle system consists of 4 pulleys. If an effort of 200 N is required to lift a load of 600 N, calculate the Mechanical Advantage, Velocity Ratio, and Efficiency of the system. (3 Marks)
12.
(a) What is meant by a chemical formula? Write the chemical formulae for Calcium Carbonate and Magnesium Chloride. (3 Marks)
(b) Write a balanced chemical equation for the reaction between Hydrogen and Oxygen to form Water. (2 Marks)
13.
(a) Classify the following substances into elements, compounds, and mixtures: Iron, Air, Water, 22-carat gold. (2 Marks)
(b) Explain how a pair of scissors (Class I lever) differs from a nutcracker (Class II lever) in terms of the relative position of Load, Effort, and Fulcrum. Draw simple line diagrams for both to support your answer. (3 Marks)
