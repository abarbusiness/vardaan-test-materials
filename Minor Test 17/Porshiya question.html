Minor Test 17
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Algebra) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all working clearly.
  • Calculators are strictly prohibited.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Identify the terms and write down the numerical coefficient of each term in the following algebraic expression:
\[ 5x^3 - 4x^2y + 7xy^2 - 9 \]
2.
Write the degree of the following polynomials:
(i) \( 4x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x \)
(ii) \( 7a^2b^3 + 5a^4b - 2 \)
3.
Find the value of the expression \( 3p^2q - 2pq^2 + 5 \) when \( p = -2 \) and \( q = 3 \).
4.
Subtract the expression \( 3a^2 - 5ab + 2b^2 \) from \( 5a^2 + 4ab - 3b^2 \).
5.
From the following list of terms, group the like terms together:
\[ 5x^2y, \quad -3xy, \quad 7xy^2, \quad -8yx^2, \quad 4yx \]
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Add the following algebraic expressions:
\[ (4x^2 - 5x + 6), \quad (-3x^2 + 2x - 4), \quad \text{and} \quad (x^2 - 3x + 2) \]
7.
Find the product of the following binomials:
\[ (4a - 5b)(3a + 2b) \]
8.
Divide the given polynomial by the monomial:
\[ (12x^4y^3 - 18x^3y^2 + 24x^2y) \div (6x^2y) \]
9.
What must be subtracted from \( 3x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x - 1 \) to obtain the polynomial \( x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x + 3 \)?
10.
The length and breadth of a rectangle are given by the expressions \( (3x + 4y) \) units and \( (2x - y) \) units respectively. Find the algebraic expression for its area.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Simplify the following expression by removing brackets and collecting like terms:
\[ 3a - [4b - \{3a - 5b - (2a - 3b)\} + 5a] \]
12.
Let the polynomials be defined as follows:
\( P = 7x^2 - 4x + 5 \)
\( Q = 4x^2 - 3x - 2 \)
\( R = -2x^2 + 5x + 3 \)

(i) Find the simplified expression for \( P - Q + R \).
(ii) Evaluate the resulting expression for \( x = -1 \).
13.
(i) Multiply the binomial \( (2x - 3) \) by the trinomial \( (3x^2 - 5x + 4) \).
(ii) Verify your result by substituting \( x = 2 \) into both the original expressions and the final product.
