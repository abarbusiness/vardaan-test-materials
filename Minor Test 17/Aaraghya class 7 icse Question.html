History Minor Test: Turkish Invasions & The Delhi Sultanate
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: History & Civics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Give precise historical terms where required.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Name the two major rulers belonging to the Ghaznavid and Ghurid dynasties who invaded India from the Northwest.
2.
State any two main objectives behind Mahmud of Ghazni’s invasions of India.
3.
Who was the founder of the Slave Dynasty (the first dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate)? In which year was the Delhi Sultanate established?
4.
Mention the famous historical book written by Al-Biruni that provides valuable information about India during the 11th century.
5.
Name the two historical battles fought between Prithviraj Chauhan and Muhammad Ghori, along with their respective outcomes.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Give three reasons for the defeat of the Indian Rajput rulers against the invading Turkish forces.
7.
Write a short note on Razia Sultana. Why was her accession to the throne significant in the history of the Delhi Sultanate?
8.
Explain the term 'Iqta' system introduced during the Delhi Sultanate. Who were called Iqtadars?
9.
Briefly describe any three major economic or administrative reforms introduced by Ala-ud-din Khilji.
10.
Mention three architectural or cultural contributions made by the rulers of the Delhi Sultanate.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Compare the motives and impacts of Mahmud of Ghazni and Muhammad Ghori on India. How did Muhammad Ghori's campaigns permanently alter the political landscape of Northern India?
12.
Discuss the reign and policies of Muhammad-bin-Tughlaq. Explain any two of his ambitious experiments that failed miserably due to poor execution.
13.
Write short notes on the following dynasties of the Delhi Sultanate:
(a) The Khilji Dynasty (Expansion and control)
(b) The Lodi Dynasty (Significance as the final Afghan ruling house)
