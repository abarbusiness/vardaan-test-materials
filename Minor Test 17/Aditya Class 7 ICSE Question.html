Minor Test 17
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show all necessary working.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the given expression based on the rules of integers: \([(-15) \times 4] \div (-6)\).
2.
Find any two rational numbers strictly between \(\frac{1}{4}\) and \(\frac{1}{2}\).
3.
Convert \(12.5\%\) into a fraction in its simplest form.
4.
Subtract the algebraic expression \(3x^2 - 5x + 2\) from \(5x^2 - 3x + 8\).
5.
Solve the following linear equation for \(x\):
\(3(x - 2) = 15\)
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Two numbers are in the ratio \(3 : 5\). If the sum of the numbers is \(96\), find both the numbers.
7.
A car travels \(228 \text{ km}\) in \(3 \text{ hours}\). Using the unitary method, calculate how much time it will take to travel \(912 \text{ km}\) at the same uniform speed.
8.
A shopkeeper bought a chair for \(\text{₹}450\) and sold it for \(\text{₹}540\). Calculate his profit percentage.
9.
Simplify the following algebraic expression by removing the brackets:
\[ 4a - [3b - \{a - (2b - 3a)\}] \]
10.
Simplify the following rational numbers:
\[ \frac{-3}{8} + \frac{5}{12} - \frac{7}{16} \]
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
The length of a rectangular field is twice its breadth. If the perimeter of the field is \(150 \text{ m}\), form a linear equation to find its length and breadth. Also, calculate the area of the field.
12.
An article was sold for \(\text{₹}1200\) at a loss of \(20\%\). Find the cost price of the article. What should have been the selling price to gain \(10\%\)?
13.
Answer the following:
(a)
\(15\) men can complete a piece of work in \(24\) days. In how many days will \(18\) men complete the same work? (3 Marks)
(b)
Express the ratio \(75 \text{ paise}\) to \(\text{₹}3\) in its simplest form. (2 Marks)
