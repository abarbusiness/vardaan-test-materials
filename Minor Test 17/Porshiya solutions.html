Q2. Write the degree of polynomials: (i) \( 4x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x \) (ii) \( 7a^2b^3 + 5a^4b - 2 \)
[2 Marks]
(i) Degree 3   (ii) Degree 5
(i) \( 4x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x \):
The highest power of the variable \( x \) is 3. Therefore, the degree is 3.

(ii) \( 7a^2b^3 + 5a^4b - 2 \):
For polynomials with multiple variables, we add the powers of the variables in each term.
Term 1 (\( 7a^2b^3 \)): power = 2 + 3 = 5
Term 2 (\( 5a^4b \)): power = 4 + 1 = 5
The maximum sum of powers is 5. Therefore, the degree is 5.
Q3. Find the value of \( 3p^2q - 2pq^2 + 5 \) when \( p = -2 \) and \( q = 3 \).
[2 Marks]
77
Substitute \( p = -2 \) and \( q = 3 \) into the expression:
\[ = 3(-2)^2(3) - 2(-2)(3)^2 + 5 \]
Calculate the powers first: \( (-2)^2 = 4 \) and \( (3)^2 = 9 \)
\[ = 3(4)(3) - 2(-2)(9) + 5 \]
\[ = (12 \times 3) - (-4 \times 9) + 5 \]
\[ = 36 - (-36) + 5 \]
\[ = 36 + 36 + 5 \]
\[ = 77 \]
Q4. Subtract \( 3a^2 - 5ab + 2b^2 \) from \( 5a^2 + 4ab - 3b^2 \).
[2 Marks]
\( 2a^2 + 9ab - 5b^2 \)
Set up the subtraction expression (second polynomial minus the first):
\[ (5a^2 + 4ab - 3b^2) - (3a^2 - 5ab + 2b^2) \]
Change the signs of all terms inside the second bracket:
\[ = 5a^2 + 4ab - 3b^2 - 3a^2 + 5ab - 2b^2 \]
Group the like terms:
\[ = (5a^2 - 3a^2) + (4ab + 5ab) + (-3b^2 - 2b^2) \]
\[ = 2a^2 + 9ab - 5b^2 \]
Q5. Group the like terms: \( 5x^2y, \ -3xy, \ 7xy^2, \ -8yx^2, \ 4yx \)
[2 Marks]
Group 1: \( 5x^2y, -8yx^2 \) | Group 2: \( -3xy, 4yx \)
Like terms have exactly the same variables with the exact same powers (order of variables doesn't matter).
  • Group 1 (\( x^2y \) terms): \( 5x^2y \) and \( -8yx^2 \) (since \( yx^2 = x^2y \))
  • Group 2 (\( xy \) terms): \( -3xy \) and \( 4yx \) (since \( yx = xy \))
  • Ungrouped: \( 7xy^2 \) has no other like term in the list.

Q6. Add: \( (4x^2 - 5x + 6), \ (-3x^2 + 2x - 4), \ \text{and} \ (x^2 - 3x + 2) \)
[3 Marks]
\( 2x^2 - 6x + 4 \)
Arrange the expressions vertically or horizontally. We will do it horizontally by grouping like terms.
\[ = (4x^2 - 5x + 6) + (-3x^2 + 2x - 4) + (x^2 - 3x + 2) \]
Group all \( x^2 \) terms, \( x \) terms, and constants together:
\[ = (4x^2 - 3x^2 + x^2) + (-5x - 3x + 2x) + (6 - 4 + 2) \]
Add the coefficients for each group:
\[ = (4 - 3 + 1)x^2 + (-5 - 3 + 2)x + (2 + 2) \]
\[ = (2)x^2 + (-6)x + (4) \]
\[ = 2x^2 - 6x + 4 \]
Q7. Find the product: \( (4a - 5b)(3a + 2b) \)
[3 Marks]
\( 12a^2 - 7ab - 10b^2 \)
Use the distributive property (multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second):
\[ = 4a(3a + 2b) - 5b(3a + 2b) \]
Distribute:
\[ = (4a \times 3a) + (4a \times 2b) - (5b \times 3a) - (5b \times 2b) \]
\[ = 12a^2 + 8ab - 15ab - 10b^2 \]
Combine the like terms in the middle (\( 8ab \) and \( -15ab \)):
\[ = 12a^2 + (8 - 15)ab - 10b^2 \]
\[ = 12a^2 - 7ab - 10b^2 \]
Q8. Divide: \( (12x^4y^3 - 18x^3y^2 + 24x^2y) \div (6x^2y) \)
[3 Marks]
\( 2x^2y^2 - 3xy + 4 \)
Divide each term of the polynomial in the numerator by the monomial denominator:
\[ = \frac{12x^4y^3}{6x^2y} - \frac{18x^3y^2}{6x^2y} + \frac{24x^2y}{6x^2y} \]
Use the laws of exponents (\( a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n} \)):
First term: \( (12/6)x^{4-2}y^{3-1} = 2x^2y^2 \)
Second term: \( -(18/6)x^{3-2}y^{2-1} = -3x^1y^1 = -3xy \)
Third term: \( (24/6)x^{2-2}y^{1-1} = 4x^0y^0 = 4(1)(1) = 4 \)
Putting it all together:
\[ = 2x^2y^2 - 3xy + 4 \]
Q9. What must be subtracted from \( 3x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x - 1 \) to obtain \( x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x + 3 \)?
[3 Marks]
\( 2x^3 - 6x^2 + 7x - 4 \)
Let the required expression be \( E \).
\[ (3x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x - 1) - E = (x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x + 3) \]
To find \( E \), we must subtract the result from the original expression:
\[ E = (3x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x - 1) - (x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x + 3) \]
Open brackets and change signs:
\[ = 3x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x - 1 - x^3 - 4x^2 + 2x - 3 \]
Group like terms:
\[ = (3x^3 - x^3) + (-2x^2 - 4x^2) + (5x + 2x) + (-1 - 3) \]
\[ = 2x^3 - 6x^2 + 7x - 4 \]
Q10. Length = \( (3x + 4y) \), Breadth = \( (2x - y) \). Find Area.
[3 Marks]
\( 6x^2 + 5xy - 4y^2 \) sq. units
Area of a rectangle = Length × Breadth
\[ \text{Area} = (3x + 4y) \times (2x - y) \]
Multiply the binomials:
\[ = 3x(2x - y) + 4y(2x - y) \]
\[ = 6x^2 - 3xy + 8xy - 4y^2 \]
Combine the like terms (\( -3xy \) and \( +8xy \)):
\[ = 6x^2 + 5xy - 4y^2 \]

Q11. Simplify: \( 3a - [4b - \{3a - 5b - (2a - 3b)\} + 5a] \)
[5 Marks]
\( -a - 6b \)
Follow BODMAS/BODMAS rule. Solve innermost brackets (parentheses) first:
\[ = 3a - [4b - \{3a - 5b - 2a + 3b\} + 5a] \]
Simplify inside the curly braces \(\{\}\) by grouping like terms:
\[ = 3a - [4b - \{(3a - 2a) + (-5b + 3b)\} + 5a] \]
\[ = 3a - [4b - \{a - 2b\} + 5a] \]
Now open the curly braces. Negative sign outside will reverse signs inside:
\[ = 3a - [4b - a + 2b + 5a] \]
Simplify inside the square brackets \([]\):
\[ = 3a - [(-a + 5a) + (4b + 2b)] \]
\[ = 3a - [4a + 6b] \]
Open the square bracket:
\[ = 3a - 4a - 6b \]
\[ = -a - 6b \]
Q12. \( P = 7x^2 - 4x + 5 \), \( Q = 4x^2 - 3x - 2 \), \( R = -2x^2 + 5x + 3 \).
(i) Find \( P - Q + R \). (ii) Evaluate for \( x = -1 \).
[5 Marks]
(i) \( x^2 + 4x + 10 \)   (ii) \( 7 \)
Part (i): Find \( P - Q + R \)
Substitute the expressions for P, Q, and R:
\[ = (7x^2 - 4x + 5) - (4x^2 - 3x - 2) + (-2x^2 + 5x + 3) \]
Open the brackets, taking care of the negative sign before Q:
\[ = 7x^2 - 4x + 5 - 4x^2 + 3x + 2 - 2x^2 + 5x + 3 \]
Group like terms together:
\[ = (7x^2 - 4x^2 - 2x^2) + (-4x + 3x + 5x) + (5 + 2 + 3) \]
\[ = (7 - 6)x^2 + (-4 + 8)x + (10) \]
\[ = x^2 + 4x + 10 \]

Part (ii): Evaluate for \( x = -1 \)
Substitute \( x = -1 \) into the simplified expression:
\[ = (-1)^2 + 4(-1) + 10 \]
\[ = 1 - 4 + 10 \]
\[ = -3 + 10 = 7 \]
Q13. (i) Multiply \( (2x - 3) \) by \( (3x^2 - 5x + 4) \).
(ii) Verify for \( x = 2 \).
[5 Marks]
(i) \( 6x^3 - 19x^2 + 23x - 12 \)   (ii) LHS = 2, RHS = 2 (Verified)
Part (i): Multiplication
Multiply each term of the binomial by the trinomial:
\[ = 2x(3x^2 - 5x + 4) - 3(3x^2 - 5x + 4) \]
\[ = (6x^3 - 10x^2 + 8x) - (9x^2 - 15x + 12) \]
Open brackets and combine like terms:
\[ = 6x^3 - 10x^2 - 9x^2 + 8x + 15x - 12 \]
\[ = 6x^3 - 19x^2 + 23x - 12 \]

Part (ii): Verification for \( x = 2 \)
Substitute \( x = 2 \) into the Original Expressions (Left Hand Side):
\[ \text{LHS} = [2(2) - 3] \times [3(2)^2 - 5(2) + 4] \]
\[ \text{LHS} = [4 - 3] \times [3(4) - 10 + 4] \]
\[ \text{LHS} = [1] \times [12 - 10 + 4] \]
\[ \text{LHS} = 1 \times 6 = 6 \]
Substitute \( x = 2 \) into the Product Expression (Right Hand Side):
\[ \text{RHS} = 6(2)^3 - 19(2)^2 + 23(2) - 12 \]
\[ \text{RHS} = 6(8) - 19(4) + 46 - 12 \]
\[ \text{RHS} = 48 - 76 + 46 - 12 \]
\[ \text{RHS} = (48 + 46) - (76 + 12) = 94 - 88 = 6 \]
Since LHS = RHS = 6, the result is verified.
