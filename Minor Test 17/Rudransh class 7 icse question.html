Minor Test 17
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Mathematics (Triangles & Pythagoras Theorem) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
  • Use of calculator is not permitted.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
State the 'Angle Sum Property' and the 'Exterior Angle Property' of a triangle.
2.
Is it possible to construct a triangle with side lengths 5 cm, 8 cm, and 14 cm? Give a mathematical reason to support your answer.
3.
In \(\triangle ABC\), \(\angle A = 50^\circ\) and \(\angle B = 60^\circ\). Find the measure of the exterior angle formed by producing side \(BC\).
4.
State the Pythagoras Theorem. For a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse \(c\) and legs \(a\) and \(b\), write the mathematical equation representing the theorem.
5.
Determine if a triangle with side lengths 9 cm, 12 cm, and 15 cm is a right-angled triangle. Show your working.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
In \(\triangle PQR\), the exterior angle at \(R\) is \(130^\circ\). If the interior opposite angles \(\angle P\) and \(\angle Q\) are in the ratio \(2:3\), find the measure of all three interior angles of the triangle.
7.
The lengths of two sides of a triangle are 12 cm and 18 cm. Between what two distinct positive values must the length of the third side fall? Explain the property you used to find the range.
8.
A ladder 13 m long is placed against a wall such that its foot is at a distance of 5 m from the base of the wall. Find the height at which the ladder reaches the wall.
9.
In \(\triangle LMN\), \(LM = LN\) (isosceles triangle). If the exterior angle formed at vertex \(N\) is \(110^\circ\), find the measures of all the interior angles of \(\triangle LMN\).
10.
A tree breaks due to a storm and the broken part bends so that the top of the tree touches the ground at a distance of 8 m from the base of the tree. If the point where it broke is 6 m above the ground, what was the total original height of the tree before it broke?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Find the value of \(x\) and \(y\) in the given figure. Give geometrical reasons for each step.
12.
Rohan walks 15 km towards North, then turns West and walks 8 km.
  1. Draw a neat labeled sketch representing his path.
  2. Calculate his shortest straight-line distance from the starting point.
  3. State the theorem used to find this distance.
13.
In a rhombus \(ABCD\), the lengths of the diagonals \(AC\) and \(BD\) are 24 cm and 10 cm respectively.
(Hint: The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles).
  1. Draw a rough sketch of the rhombus and mark the intersection point of diagonals as \(O\).
  2. Find the length of one side of the rhombus using Pythagoras Theorem.
  3. Calculate the perimeter of the rhombus.
