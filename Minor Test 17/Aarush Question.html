MINOR TEST 17
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • The question paper strictly covers topics: Light (Shadows & Reflections) and Measurement of Time & Motion.
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Use scientific formatting where necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define the terms 'opaque' and 'transparent' materials. Give one example of each.
2.
What is a simple pendulum? Define the term 'time period' in the context of a simple pendulum.
3.
State the three essential conditions necessary for a shadow to be formed.
4.
Convert a speed of \( 54 \text{ km/h} \) into \( \text{m/s} \). Show your calculation steps clearly.
5.
Differentiate between uniform motion and non-uniform motion with the help of one example for each.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Describe the basic principle behind the working of a pinhole camera. State the two characteristics of the image formed by it.
7.
A simple pendulum takes \( 32 \text{ seconds} \) to complete \( 20 \text{ oscillations} \). What is the time period of the pendulum? Calculate its frequency.
8.
Explain how a shadow of an object differs from the image of the same object formed by a plane mirror. State any three differences.
9.
Salma takes \( 15 \text{ minutes} \) from her house to reach her school on a bicycle. If the bicycle has a steady speed of \( 2 \text{ m/s} \), calculate the distance between her house and the school in kilometers.
10.
What is a speedometer and an odometer? Why is it important to use standard units of measurement (like SI units) instead of using arbitrary units like handspans or footsteps?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Describe a simple activity to prove that light travels in a straight line (rectilinear propagation of light). (3 Marks)
(b) Why can we not see our clear image in a rough piece of cardboard, even though it reflects light? Explain. (2 Marks)
12.
(a) The distance between two railway stations is \( 240 \text{ km} \). A train takes \( 4 \text{ hours} \) to cover this distance. Calculate the speed of the train in km/h. (2 Marks)
(b) If another express train travels the same distance at a constant speed of \( 80 \text{ km/h} \), how much time will it take to complete the journey? (3 Marks)
13.
(a) A car moves with a constant speed of \( 10 \text{ km/min} \). Describe the shape of its distance-time graph. (2 Marks)
(b) Explain how you can determine the speed of an object directly from its distance-time graph. (1 Mark)
(c) What will be the shape of a distance-time graph for a truck that is parked (stationary) at a distance of \( 20 \text{ m} \) from the origin? (2 Marks)
