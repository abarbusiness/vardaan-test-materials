MINOR TEST 17: MENSURATION
Class: 8 Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show proper steps and formulas used. Write your answers neatly.
  • Use of calculator is strictly prohibited.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Find the volume of a cube whose edge length is \( 6 \text{ cm} \).
2.
The dimensions of a cuboidal box are \( 4 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm} \times 2 \text{ cm} \). Find its Total Surface Area (TSA).
3.
The radius of the base of a cylinder is \( 7 \text{ cm} \) and its height is \( 10 \text{ cm} \). Find its Curved Surface Area (CSA). \( \left(\text{Assume } \pi = \frac{22}{7}\right) \)
4.
If each side of a cube is doubled, how many times will its new volume be compared to its original volume?
5.
A cylindrical tank has a base area of \( 154 \text{ cm}^2 \) and a height of \( 5 \text{ cm} \). What is the total volume of the tank?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Find the height of a cylinder whose volume is \( 1.54 \text{ m}^3 \) and the diameter of the base is \( 140 \text{ cm} \).
7.
A closed cylindrical water tank of radius \( 7 \text{ m} \) and height \( 3 \text{ m} \) is to be made from a sheet of metal. How much sheet of metal is required?
8.
A cuboidal room has dimensions \( 5 \text{ m} \times 4 \text{ m} \times 3 \text{ m} \). Find the cost of white-washing the walls of the room and the ceiling at the rate of \( \text{₹} 7.50 \text{ per m}^2 \).
9.
The lateral surface area of a hollow cylinder is \( 4224 \text{ cm}^2 \). It is cut along its height and formed into a rectangular sheet of width \( 33 \text{ cm} \). Find the perimeter of the rectangular sheet.
10.
Three identical solid cubes of side \( 4 \text{ cm} \) each are joined end to end to form a single cuboid. Find the total surface area of the resulting cuboid.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
A godown is in the form of a cuboid measuring \( 60 \text{ m} \times 40 \text{ m} \times 30 \text{ m} \). Find the maximum number of cuboidal boxes that can be stored in it if the volume of one box is \( 0.8 \text{ m}^3 \).
12.
A metallic solid cylinder of radius \( 8 \text{ cm} \) and height \( 2 \text{ cm} \) is melted and completely converted into a new right circular cylinder of radius \( 4 \text{ cm} \).
(a) Find the height of the new cylinder formed.
(b) Find the curved surface area (CSA) of the new cylinder.
13.
Solve the following geometric problems:
(a) A rectangular block has dimensions \( 10 \text{ cm} \times 8 \text{ cm} \times 6 \text{ cm} \). Calculate its volume. (2 Marks)
(b) A solid wooden cube of side 'a' is cut strictly parallel to one of its faces to form two perfectly equal cuboidal halves. Prove mathematically what the ratio will be between the Total Surface Area of the original cube to the Total Surface Area of one of the resulting cuboidal halves. (3 Marks)
