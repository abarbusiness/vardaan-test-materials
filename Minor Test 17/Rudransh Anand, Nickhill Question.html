Minor Test 17
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Show all working steps clearly. Marks will be deducted for direct answers without proper steps.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
SECTION A (10 Marks) (Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.

Find the square root of 729 using the prime factorization method.

2.

Is 243 a perfect cube? Give mathematical reasoning to support your answer.

3.

Find the smallest whole number by which 392 must be multiplied so that the product becomes a perfect cube.

4.

Without doing any calculation, explain why the numbers 153 and 257 are surely not perfect squares.

5.

Evaluate the following:

(i) $\sqrt{0.0009}$
(ii) $\sqrt[3]{0.008}$
SECTION B (15 Marks) (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.

Find the smallest square number that is perfectly divisible by each of the numbers 4, 9, and 10.

7.

Find the cube root of $13824$ by the prime factorization method.

8.

Find the smallest whole number by which 9408 must be divided so that the quotient is a perfect square. Also, find the square root of the newly obtained perfect square.

9.

A gardener has 1000 plants. He wants to plant these in his garden in such a way that the number of rows and the number of columns remain the same. Find the minimum number of plants he needs more for this arrangement.

10.

Find the value of $\sqrt[3]{-2744}$ and clearly state the property used for negative cube roots.

SECTION C (15 Marks) (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.

Using the long division method, find the square root of the following decimal numbers:

(a) 12.25
(b) 42.25
12.

Three numbers are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. If the sum of their cubes is $33957$, find all the three original numbers. Show complete step-by-step algebraic formulation.

13.
(a) Find the greatest 4-digit number which is a perfect square.
(b) Find the least number that must be subtracted from 5607 so as to get a perfect square. Also, find the square root of this perfect square.
