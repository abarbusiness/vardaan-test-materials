SECTION A

Q1. State the laws of reflection of light.
[2 Marks]
Answer: Two Laws of Reflection
1st Law: The angle of incidence is always equal to the angle of reflection (\( \angle i = \angle r \)).
2nd Law: The incident ray, the normal to the mirror at the point of incidence, and the reflected ray all lie in the same plane.
Q2. Define health according to the World Health Organization (WHO).
[2 Marks]
Answer: WHO definition of health
According to WHO, health is defined as a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.
Q3. What is a mixture? Give one example of a solid-liquid mixture.
[2 Marks]
Answer: Definition and Example
Mixture: A substance consisting of two or more elements or compounds not chemically combined together.
Example: Salt dissolved in water (or sand in water).
Q4. Why can we see a non-luminous object from all directions when light falls on it? Name the type of reflection involved.
[2 Marks]
Answer: Diffused Reflection
Reason: Non-luminous objects have rough surfaces that scatter light rays in all directions when hit by light, making them visible from any angle.
Type of Reflection: Irregular or diffused reflection.
Q5. Differentiate briefly between a homogeneous mixture and a heterogeneous mixture with examples.
[2 Marks]
Answer: Homogeneous vs Heterogeneous
Homogeneous Mixture: Has a uniform composition throughout (e.g., sugar solution).
Heterogeneous Mixture: Does not have a uniform composition and parts are easily distinguishable (e.g., oil and water).

SECTION B

Q6. Explain regular reflection and irregular (diffused) reflection with the help of simple descriptions. Do both types obey the laws of reflection?
[3 Marks]
Answer: Regular vs Diffused Reflection
Regular Reflection: Occurs on smooth, polished surfaces (like plane mirrors) where parallel incident rays reflect parallel in one direction.
Diffused Reflection: Occurs on rough surfaces where parallel incident rays are reflected in different directions.
Note: Yes, both regular and diffused reflections strictly obey the laws of reflection.

Q8. Name the technique used to separate a mixture of salt and water. Describe briefly how it is carried out.
[3 Marks]
Answer: Evaporation / Distillation
Technique: Evaporation (or Distillation).
Procedure: The salt solution is heated in a china dish. The water evaporates completely into steam, leaving behind solid salt crystals at the bottom of the dish.
Q9. Calculate the number of images formed when two plane mirrors are placed parallel to each other facing each other. Explain why infinite images are seen.
[3 Marks]
Answer: Infinite Images
Number of Images: Infinite (\( \infty \)).
Explanation: When mirrors are parallel, the angle between them is \( 0^\circ \). Using formula \( n = \frac{360^\circ}{0^\circ} - 1 \), it results in an infinite series of reflections where the image formed in one mirror acts as an object for the other mirror repeatedly.

SECTION C

Q11. Answer the following questions based on light and mirrors: (a) Characteristics of plane mirror image (3M), (b) Numerical distance calculation (2M)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) Characteristics, (b) 30 cm
Part (a) [3 Marks]: Characteristics of plane mirror image:
1. Virtual and erect.
2. Same size as the object.
3. Laterally inverted.
4. Distance of image behind the mirror equals distance of object in front.
Part (b) [2 Marks]: Numerical:
Object distance = \( 15\text{ cm} \). Image distance behind mirror = \( 15\text{ cm} \).
Total distance between object and image = \( 15\text{ cm} + 15\text{ cm} = 30\text{ cm} \).
Q12. Answer the following questions based on health and hygiene: (a) Communicable vs Non-communicable (3M), (b) Importance of mental health (2M)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) Disease differences, (b) Mental health rationale
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Communicable Diseases: Infectious diseases that spread from person to person (e.g., Common cold, Cholera, COVID-19).
Non-communicable Diseases: Diseases that do not spread from one person to another, caused by lifestyle or genetics (e.g., Diabetes, Asthma).
Part (b) [2 Marks]: Mental health is vital because:
1. It affects emotional balance, decision-making, and coping with daily stress.
2. Poor mental health can negatively impact physical health (psychosomatic effects).
Q13. Answer the following questions based on mixtures and separation: (a) Reasons for separation (2M), (b) Separating sand and iron filings (3M)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) Separation reasons, (b) Magnetic separation
Part (a) [2 Marks]: Reasons for separating components:
1. To remove harmful or unwanted impurities.
2. To obtain useful, pure components for daily use.
Part (b) [3 Marks]: Separating sand and iron filings:
Method: Magnetic Separation.
Procedure: Move a bar magnet through the mixture of sand and iron filings. The iron filings, being magnetic, will get attracted and stick to the magnet, leaving behind pure sand in the container.
