MINOR TEST 17
Class: 7 ICSE Subject: Physics & Maths Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
  • Show all working steps for mathematics numericals clearly.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
State the two laws of reflection of light.
2.
A car is moving with a speed of 90 km/h. Convert this speed into meters per second (m/s).
3.
Mention any two characteristics of the image formed by a plane mirror.
4.
A cyclist travels at a constant speed of 15 m/s. Calculate the distance covered by the cyclist in 2 hours (in kilometers).
5.
Define the terms:
(a) Pole of a spherical mirror
(b) Principal axis
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Distinguish between a real image and a virtual image. (Write any three points of difference).
7.
A boy walks from his home to school at a speed of 3 km/h and returns back from school to home at a speed of 2 km/h. If he takes 5 hours in total for the entire journey, find the distance between his home and the school.
8.
A ray of light strikes a plane mirror such that the angle between the incident ray and the surface of the mirror is 30°.
Find:
(a) The angle of incidence
(b) The angle of reflection
(c) Draw a neat ray diagram to illustrate this situation.
9.
A train 200 meters long crosses a stationary electric pole in 10 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
10.
What is meant by 'Lateral Inversion'? Explain with the help of a daily life example why the word 'AMBULANCE' is written backwards on the hospital vans.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
With the help of a neat and labeled ray diagram, explain the formation of the image of a point object by a plane mirror. Prove using geometry that the image formed is at the same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front of it.
12.
Solve the following:
(a)
A train 150 m long is running at a uniform speed of 72 km/h. How much time will it take to completely cross a bridge which is 250 m long? (2.5 Marks)
(b)
An athlete runs a 400 m race in 50 seconds. Find his average speed in km/h. (2.5 Marks)
13.
A car travels from City A to City B at a uniform speed of 60 km/h and returns from City B to City A along the same route at a uniform speed of 40 km/h.
(a)
Calculate the average speed of the car for the entire journey. (3 Marks)
(b)
If the total time taken for the entire round trip is 5 hours, find the exact distance between City A and City B. (2 Marks)
