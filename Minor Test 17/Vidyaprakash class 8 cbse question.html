MINOR TEST 17: SCIENCE (Physics, Chemistry & Biology)
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • Section A contains Objective/Short Answer questions (2 Marks each).
  • Section B contains Short Answer questions (3 Marks each).
  • Section C contains Long Answer questions (5 Marks each).
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
State the laws of reflection of light.
2.
Define health according to the World Health Organization (WHO).
3.
What is a mixture? Give one example of a solid-liquid mixture.
4.
Why can we see a non-luminous object from all directions when light falls on it? Name the type of reflection involved.
5.
Differentiate briefly between a homogeneous mixture and a heterogeneous mixture with examples.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Explain regular reflection and irregular (diffused) reflection with the help of simple descriptions. Do both types obey the laws of reflection?
7.
List three personal habits or lifestyle practices that help maintain good physical and mental health.
8.
Name the technique used to separate a mixture of salt and water. Describe briefly how it is carried out.
9.
Calculate the number of images formed when two plane mirrors are placed parallel to each other facing each other. Explain why infinite images are seen.
10.
What are balanced diet and hygiene? Why are they considered the ultimate treasures for personal wellness?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Answer the following questions based on light and mirrors:
(a) List four important characteristics of the image formed by a plane mirror. (3 Marks)
(b) If an object is placed at a distance of \( 15\text{ cm} \) in front of a plane mirror, what is the distance between the object and its image? (2 Marks)
12.
Answer the following questions based on health and hygiene:
(a) Distinguish clearly between communicable diseases and non-communicable diseases with examples. (3 Marks)
(b) Why is mental health equally as important as physical health? Give two reasons. (2 Marks)
13.
Answer the following questions based on mixtures and separation techniques:
(a) Why do we need to separate different components of a mixture? Give two reasons. (2 Marks)
(b) Describe a method to separate a mixture containing two solid components: sand and iron filings. (3 Marks)
