Minor Test 17: Chemistry - Matter
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Chemistry Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The question paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Read each question carefully and write neat, precise answers.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
Define matter. Give two examples of things that are considered matter and two that are not matter.
Q2.
State the two main characteristics of particles of matter with respect to space between them and their movement.
Q3.
Define inter-molecular spaces and inter-molecular forces of attraction.
Q4.
Why are solids rigid and incompressible, whereas gases can be easily compressed?
Q5.
What is meant by the term 'change of state'? Give an example of a substance that changes directly from solid to gas on heating.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
Differentiate between solids, liquids, and gases based on:
(a) Shape and Volume (1 Mark)
(b) Fluidity (1 Mark)
(c) Compressibility (1 Mark)
Q7.
Explain the phenomenon of Diffusion with a suitable daily life example. Why does diffusion happen faster in gases than in liquids?
Q8.
Explain the effect of heat on the kinetic energy of particles of matter. How does heating a solid lead to its conversion into a liquid (melting)?
Q9.
Define the following terms clearly:
(a) Melting point (1 Mark)
(b) Boiling point (1 Mark)
(c) Condensation (1 Mark)
Q10.
Give scientific reasons for the following:
(a) A gas fills completely the vessel in which it is kept. (1.5 Marks)
(b) Wooden blocks cannot flow like water. (1.5 Marks)
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
Describe the kinetic theory of matter in detail. List any four key postulates of this theory and explain how it helps us understand the three states of matter.
Q12.
Explain the complete cycle of changes of state with the help of a descriptive flow diagram or text description covering melting, vaporization, condensation, freezing, and sublimation.
Q13.
(a) State three activities or observations from daily life that prove matter is made up of tiny particles with spaces between them. (3 Marks)
(b) Why does a solid expand very slightly on heating, while a gas expands to a large extent? (2 Marks)
