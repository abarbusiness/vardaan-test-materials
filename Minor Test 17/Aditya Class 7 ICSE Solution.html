SECTION A

Q1. Evaluate the given expression based on the rules of integers: \([(-15) \times 4] \div (-6)\).
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Solve the expression inside the square bracket first (BODMAS rule).
\((-15) \times 4 = -60\)
Step 2: Divide the result by \(-6\).
\(-60 \div (-6)\)
Since both numbers are negative, the result will be positive.
\(60 \div 6 = 10\)
Answer: 10
Q2. Find any two rational numbers strictly between \(\frac{1}{4}\) and \(\frac{1}{2}\).
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Make the denominators equal. The LCM of 4 and 2 is 4.
\(\frac{1}{4}\) remains \(\frac{1}{4}\).
\(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{1 \times 2}{2 \times 2} = \frac{2}{4}\).
Step 2: To find numbers between them, multiply the numerator and denominator of both by a suitable integer, say 4.
\(\frac{1 \times 4}{4 \times 4} = \frac{4}{16}\)
\(\frac{2 \times 4}{4 \times 4} = \frac{8}{16}\)
Step 3: The rational numbers strictly between \(\frac{4}{16}\) and \(\frac{8}{16}\) are \(\frac{5}{16}, \frac{6}{16}, \frac{7}{16}\).
*(Note: Any valid alternative method and correct rational numbers are accepted).*
Answer: \(\frac{5}{16}\) and \(\frac{6}{16}\) (or \(\frac{3}{8}\))
Q3. Convert \(12.5\%\) into a fraction in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Remove the percentage sign and divide by 100.
\(12.5\% = \frac{12.5}{100}\)
Step 2: Remove the decimal by multiplying numerator and denominator by 10.
\(\frac{125}{1000}\)
Step 3: Simplify the fraction by dividing by their HCF (125).
\(\frac{125 \div 125}{1000 \div 125} = \frac{1}{8}\)
Answer: \(\frac{1}{8}\)
Q4. Subtract the algebraic expression \(3x^2 - 5x + 2\) from \(5x^2 - 3x + 8\).
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Write the expressions in subtraction format. Remember to enclose the expression being subtracted in brackets.
\((5x^2 - 3x + 8) - (3x^2 - 5x + 2)\)
Step 2: Open the brackets, which changes the signs of all terms inside the second bracket.
\(5x^2 - 3x + 8 - 3x^2 + 5x - 2\)
Step 3: Group and simplify the like terms.
\((5x^2 - 3x^2) + (-3x + 5x) + (8 - 2)\)
\(2x^2 + 2x + 6\)
Answer: \(2x^2 + 2x + 6\)
Q5. Solve the following linear equation for \(x\): \(3(x - 2) = 15\)
[2 Marks]
Method 1: Expand the bracket.
\(3x - 6 = 15\)
\(3x = 15 + 6\)
\(3x = 21 \implies x = \frac{21}{3} = 7\)

Method 2: Divide both sides by 3 directly.
\((x - 2) = \frac{15}{3}\)
\(x - 2 = 5 \implies x = 5 + 2 = 7\)
Answer: \(x = 7\)

SECTION B

Q6. Two numbers are in the ratio \(3 : 5\). If the sum of the numbers is \(96\), find both the numbers.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Let the common multiple be \(x\). Therefore, the numbers are \(3x\) and \(5x\).
Step 2: According to the given condition, their sum is 96.
\(3x + 5x = 96\)
\(8x = 96\)
\(x = \frac{96}{8} = 12\)
Step 3: Find the actual numbers by substituting the value of \(x\).
First number \(= 3 \times 12 = 36\)
Second number \(= 5 \times 12 = 60\)
Answer: The numbers are 36 and 60.
Q7. A car travels \(228 \text{ km}\) in \(3 \text{ hours}\). Using the unitary method, calculate how much time it will take to travel \(912 \text{ km}\) at the same uniform speed.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Find the distance traveled in 1 hour (Unit value).
Distance covered in 3 hours = \(228 \text{ km}\)
Distance covered in 1 hour = \(\frac{228}{3} = 76 \text{ km/hr}\) (This is the speed).
Step 2: Calculate the time required to cover the new distance.
Time taken to cover 76 km = \(1 \text{ hour}\)
Time taken to cover 912 km = \(\frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Speed}}\)
Time = \(\frac{912}{76} = 12 \text{ hours}\)
Answer: 12 hours
Q8. A shopkeeper bought a chair for \(\text{₹}450\) and sold it for \(\text{₹}540\). Calculate his profit percentage.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Identify Cost Price (CP) and Selling Price (SP).
\(\text{CP} = \text{₹}450\), \(\text{SP} = \text{₹}540\).
Since \(\text{SP} > \text{CP}\), there is a profit.
Step 2: Calculate the Profit.
\(\text{Profit} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 540 - 450 = \text{₹}90\).
Step 3: Calculate the Profit Percentage.
\(\text{Profit } \% = \left(\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}}\right) \times 100\)
\(\text{Profit } \% = \left(\frac{90}{450}\right) \times 100 = \frac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20\%\)
Answer: 20%
Q9. Simplify: \( 4a - [3b - \{a - (2b - 3a)\}] \)
[3 Marks]
According to BODMAS, open the innermost brackets first.
Step 1: Open the inner parenthesis \(( )\) by distributing the negative sign.
\(= 4a - [3b - \{a - 2b + 3a\}]\)
Step 2: Simplify the terms inside the curly braces \(\{ \}\).
\(= 4a - [3b - \{4a - 2b\}]\)
Step 3: Open the curly braces \(\{ \}\).
\(= 4a - [3b - 4a + 2b]\)
Step 4: Simplify inside the square brackets \([ ]\).
\(= 4a - [5b - 4a]\)
Step 5: Open the square brackets and solve.
\(= 4a - 5b + 4a\)
\(= 8a - 5b\)
Answer: \(8a - 5b\)
Q10. Simplify: \( \frac{-3}{8} + \frac{5}{12} - \frac{7}{16} \)
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Find the LCM of the denominators: 8, 12, and 16.
\(\text{LCM}(8, 12, 16) = 48\)
Step 2: Convert all rational numbers to equivalent fractions with denominator 48.
\(\frac{-3 \times 6}{8 \times 6} = \frac{-18}{48}\)
\(\frac{5 \times 4}{12 \times 4} = \frac{20}{48}\)
\(\frac{-7 \times 3}{16 \times 3} = \frac{-21}{48}\)
Step 3: Perform the addition and subtraction on the numerators.
\(=\frac{-18 + 20 - 21}{48}\)
\(=\frac{2 - 21}{48} = \frac{-19}{48}\)

Answer: \(\frac{-19}{48}\)

SECTION C

Q11. The length of a rectangular field is twice its breadth. If the perimeter of the field is \(150 \text{ m}\), form a linear equation to find its length and breadth. Also, calculate the area of the field.
[5 Marks]
Step 1: Form the linear equation.
Let the breadth of the rectangular field be \(x \text{ meters}\).
Therefore, the length of the field will be \(2x \text{ meters}\).
Formula for Perimeter of a rectangle \(= 2(\text{Length} + \text{Breadth})\)
\(2(2x + x) = 150\) (This is the required linear equation)

Step 2: Solve for dimensions.
\(2(3x) = 150\)
\(6x = 150\)
\(x = \frac{150}{6} = 25 \text{ m}\)
Breadth \((x) = 25 \text{ m}\)
Length \((2x) = 2 \times 25 = 50 \text{ m}\)

Step 3: Calculate the area.
\(\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Breadth}\)
\(\text{Area} = 50 \text{ m} \times 25 \text{ m} = 1250 \text{ m}^2\)
Answer: Length = 50m, Breadth = 25m, Area = 1250 m²
Q12. An article was sold for \(\text{₹}1200\) at a loss of \(20\%\). Find the cost price of the article. What should have been the selling price to gain \(10\%\)?
[5 Marks]
Part 1: Find Cost Price (CP)
Given: \(\text{SP} = \text{₹}1200\), \(\text{Loss } \% = 20\%\).
Formula: \(\text{SP} = \text{CP} \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Loss } \%}{100}\right)\)
Alternatively, if loss is \(20\%\), SP is \(80\%\) of CP.
\(\frac{80}{100} \times \text{CP} = 1200\)
\(\text{CP} = \frac{1200 \times 100}{80}\)
\(\text{CP} = 15 \times 100 = \text{₹}1500\).

Part 2: Find new Selling Price (SP) for 10% gain
New Gain \(\% = 10\%\).
New \(\text{SP} = \text{CP} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Gain } \%}{100}\right)\)
New \(\text{SP} = 1500 \times \left(\frac{110}{100}\right)\)
New \(\text{SP} = 15 \times 110 = \text{₹}1650\).
Answer: CP = ₹1500, New SP = ₹1650
Q13. (a) \(15\) men can complete a piece of work in \(24\) days. In how many days will \(18\) men complete the same work?
(b) Express the ratio \(75 \text{ paise}\) to \(\text{₹}3\) in its simplest form.
[5 Marks]
Part (a) - [3 Marks]
This is a case of inverse proportion. If the number of men increases, the number of days required to complete the work decreases.
Let the required number of days be \(x\).
\(M_1 \times D_1 = M_2 \times D_2\)
\(15 \times 24 = 18 \times x\)
\(x = \frac{15 \times 24}{18}\)
\(x = \frac{15 \times 4}{3}\) (Dividing 24 and 18 by 6)
\(x = 5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ days}\).
Answer (a): 20 days


Part (b) - [2 Marks]
To find the ratio, both quantities must have the same unit.
We know, \(\text{₹}1 = 100 \text{ paise}\).
Therefore, \(\text{₹}3 = 3 \times 100 = 300 \text{ paise}\).
Now, the ratio is \(75 \text{ paise} : 300 \text{ paise}\).
Ratio \(= \frac{75}{300}\)
Dividing numerator and denominator by 25: \(\frac{3}{12}\)
Dividing further by 3: \(\frac{1}{4}\)
Answer (b): \(1 : 4\)
