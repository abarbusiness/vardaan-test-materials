SECTION A

Q1. Find the volume of a cube whose edge length is \( 6 \text{ cm} \).
[2 Marks]
Answer: 216 cm³
Given: Edge of the cube (\( a \)) = \( 6 \text{ cm} \)
Formula: Volume of a cube = \( a^3 \)
Calculation:
Volume = \( 6 \times 6 \times 6 = 216 \text{ cm}^3 \)
Q2. The dimensions of a cuboidal box are \( 4 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm} \times 2 \text{ cm} \). Find its Total Surface Area (TSA).
[2 Marks]
Answer: 52 cm²
Given: Length (\( l \)) = \( 4 \text{ cm} \), Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 3 \text{ cm} \), Height (\( h \)) = \( 2 \text{ cm} \)
Formula: \( \text{TSA of Cuboid} = 2(lb + bh + hl) \)
Calculation:
\begin{align*} \text{TSA} &= 2(4 \times 3 + 3 \times 2 + 2 \times 4) \\ &= 2(12 + 6 + 8) \\ &= 2(26) = 52 \text{ cm}^2 \end{align*}
Q3. The radius of the base of a cylinder is \( 7 \text{ cm} \) and its height is \( 10 \text{ cm} \). Find its Curved Surface Area (CSA).
[2 Marks]
Answer: 440 cm²
Given: Radius (\( r \)) = \( 7 \text{ cm} \), Height (\( h \)) = \( 10 \text{ cm} \)
Formula: \( \text{CSA of Cylinder} = 2\pi rh \)
Calculation:
\begin{align*} \text{CSA} &= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 \\ &= 2 \times 22 \times 10 \\ &= 440 \text{ cm}^2 \end{align*}
Q4. If each side of a cube is doubled, how many times will its new volume be compared to its original volume?
[2 Marks]
Answer: 8 times
Let the original side of the cube be \( s \).
Original Volume (\( V_1 \)) = \( s^3 \)
New side of the cube = \( 2s \)
New Volume (\( V_2 \)) = \( (2s)^3 = 8s^3 \)
Therefore, \( V_2 = 8 \times V_1 \). The new volume will be 8 times the original volume.
Q5. A cylindrical tank has a base area of \( 154 \text{ cm}^2 \) and a height of \( 5 \text{ cm} \). What is the total volume of the tank?
[2 Marks]
Answer: 770 cm³
Given: Base Area (\( \pi r^2 \)) = \( 154 \text{ cm}^2 \), Height (\( h \)) = \( 5 \text{ cm} \)
Concept: Volume of a cylinder = Base Area \( \times \) Height
Calculation:
Volume = \( 154 \times 5 = 770 \text{ cm}^3 \)

SECTION B

Q6. Find the height of a cylinder whose volume is \( 1.54 \text{ m}^3 \) and the diameter of the base is \( 140 \text{ cm} \).
[3 Marks]
Answer: 1 m
Given: Volume (\( V \)) = \( 1.54 \text{ m}^3 \), Diameter = \( 140 \text{ cm} \)
Radius (\( r \)) = \( \frac{140}{2} = 70 \text{ cm} \)
Convert radius to meters: \( r = 0.7 \text{ m} \)
Formula: \( V = \pi r^2 h \)
Calculation:
\begin{align*} 1.54 &= \frac{22}{7} \times (0.7)^2 \times h \\ 1.54 &= \frac{22}{7} \times 0.49 \times h \\ 1.54 &= 22 \times 0.07 \times h \\ 1.54 &= 1.54 \times h \\ h &= 1 \text{ m} \end{align*}
Therefore, the height of the cylinder is 1 m.
Q7. A closed cylindrical water tank of radius \( 7 \text{ m} \) and height \( 3 \text{ m} \) is to be made from a sheet of metal. How much sheet of metal is required?
[3 Marks]
Answer: 440 m²
Given: Radius (\( r \)) = \( 7 \text{ m} \), Height (\( h \)) = \( 3 \text{ m} \)
Since the tank is closed, the metal required is equal to the Total Surface Area (TSA) of the cylinder.
Formula: \( \text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r + h) \)
Calculation:
\begin{align*} \text{TSA} &= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times (7 + 3) \\ &= 44 \times 10 \\ &= 440 \text{ m}^2 \end{align*}
Q8. A cuboidal room has dimensions \( 5 \text{ m} \times 4 \text{ m} \times 3 \text{ m} \). Find the cost of white-washing the walls of the room and the ceiling at the rate of \( \text{₹} 7.50 \text{ per m}^2 \).
[3 Marks]
Answer: ₹ 555
Given: \( l = 5 \text{ m}, b = 4 \text{ m}, h = 3 \text{ m} \)
Area to be white-washed = Area of 4 walls (LSA) + Area of ceiling
Area = \( 2h(l + b) + (l \times b) \)
Calculation:
\begin{align*} \text{Area} &= 2 \times 3(5 + 4) + (5 \times 4) \\ &= 6 \times 9 + 20 \\ &= 54 + 20 = 74 \text{ m}^2 \end{align*}
Cost = Area \( \times \) Rate = \( 74 \times 7.50 \)
Cost = \( \text{₹ } 555 \)
Q9. The lateral surface area of a hollow cylinder is \( 4224 \text{ cm}^2 \). It is cut along its height and formed into a rectangular sheet of width \( 33 \text{ cm} \). Find the perimeter of the rectangular sheet.
[3 Marks]
Answer: 322 cm
When a cylinder is cut along its height, the lateral surface area becomes the area of the rectangular sheet.
Given: Area of rectangle = LSA of cylinder = \( 4224 \text{ cm}^2 \), Width (\( b \)) = \( 33 \text{ cm} \)
To find Length (\( l \)):
\( l \times b = \text{Area} \)
\( l \times 33 = 4224 \)
\( l = \frac{4224}{33} = 128 \text{ cm} \)
Perimeter of the sheet:
\( \text{Perimeter} = 2(l + b) = 2(128 + 33) \)
\( \text{Perimeter} = 2 \times 161 = 322 \text{ cm} \)
Q10. Three identical solid cubes of side \( 4 \text{ cm} \) each are joined end to end to form a single cuboid. Find the total surface area of the resulting cuboid.
[3 Marks]
Answer: 224 cm²
When 3 cubes of side 4 cm are joined end to end, the length increases, but breadth and height remain the same.
Dimensions of resulting cuboid:
Length (\( l \)) = \( 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 \text{ cm} \)
Breadth (\( b \)) = \( 4 \text{ cm} \)
Height (\( h \)) = \( 4 \text{ cm} \)
Calculation for TSA:
\begin{align*} \text{TSA} &= 2(lb + bh + hl) \\ &= 2(12 \times 4 + 4 \times 4 + 4 \times 12) \\ &= 2(48 + 16 + 48) \\ &= 2(112) = 224 \text{ cm}^2 \end{align*}

SECTION C

Q11. A godown is in the form of a cuboid measuring \( 60 \text{ m} \times 40 \text{ m} \times 30 \text{ m} \). Find the maximum number of cuboidal boxes that can be stored in it if the volume of one box is \( 0.8 \text{ m}^3 \).
[5 Marks]
Answer: 90,000 boxes
Step 1: Calculate Total Volume of Godown
Volume of Godown (\( V_g \)) = \( L \times B \times H \)
\( V_g = 60 \times 40 \times 30 = 72,000 \text{ m}^3 \)
Step 2: Note the volume of one box
Volume of one box (\( V_b \)) = \( 0.8 \text{ m}^3 \)
Step 3: Calculate Maximum Number of Boxes
Number of boxes = \( \frac{\text{Volume of Godown}}{\text{Volume of one box}} \)
Number = \( \frac{72000}{0.8} = \frac{720000}{8} \)
Number = \( 90,000 \)
Hence, 90,000 boxes can be stored in the godown.
Q12. A metallic solid cylinder of radius \( 8 \text{ cm} \) and height \( 2 \text{ cm} \) is melted and completely converted into a new right circular cylinder of radius \( 4 \text{ cm} \). Find (a) height and (b) CSA of the new cylinder.
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) Height = 8 cm, (b) CSA = 64π cm² (or ≈ 201.14 cm²)
Concept: When a solid is melted to form a new solid, the Volume remains constant.
Part (a): Finding Height
Original Cylinder: \( r_1 = 8 \text{ cm}, h_1 = 2 \text{ cm} \)
New Cylinder: \( r_2 = 4 \text{ cm}, h_2 = ? \)
\( \text{Volume}_1 = \text{Volume}_2 \)
\( \pi \cdot (r_1)^2 \cdot h_1 = \pi \cdot (r_2)^2 \cdot h_2 \)
\( (8)^2 \cdot 2 = (4)^2 \cdot h_2 \)
\( 64 \times 2 = 16 \times h_2 \)
\( 128 = 16 h_2 \implies h_2 = \frac{128}{16} = 8 \text{ cm} \)
Part (b): Finding Curved Surface Area (CSA)
\( \text{CSA} = 2\pi r_2 h_2 \)
\( \text{CSA} = 2 \times \pi \times 4 \times 8 \)
\( \text{CSA} = 64\pi \text{ cm}^2 \)
(If \( \pi \) is taken as 3.14, CSA = \( 64 \times 3.14 = 200.96 \text{ cm}^2 \))
Q13. Solve: (a) Volume of a \( 10 \times 8 \times 6 \) block. (b) A solid wooden cube of side 'a' is cut strictly parallel to one of its faces to form two perfectly equal cuboidal halves. Find the ratio of TSA of original cube to TSA of one of the cuboids.
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) 480 cm³, (b) Ratio = 3 : 2
Part (a) [2 Marks]: Volume Calculation
Volume of cuboid = \( l \times b \times h = 10 \times 8 \times 6 = 480 \text{ cm}^3 \).
Part (b) [3 Marks]: The Hard Conceptual Differentiator
Step 1: TSA of the Original Cube
Let the side of the original cube be \( a \).
TSA of the original cube = \( 6a^2 \)
Step 2: Dimensions of the New Cuboid
When the cube is cut parallel to a face exactly in half, the resulting 3D figure is a cuboid.
Two dimensions remain unchanged (the cut face dimensions), while the third dimension is halved.
New dimensions: \( \text{Length} = a, \text{ Breadth} = a, \text{ Height} = \frac{a}{2} \)
Step 3: TSA of One Cuboidal Half
\begin{align*} \text{TSA of cuboid} &= 2 \left(l \cdot b + b \cdot h + h \cdot l\right) \\ &= 2 \left( a \cdot a + a \cdot \frac{a}{2} + \frac{a}{2} \cdot a \right) \\ &= 2 \left( a^2 + \frac{a^2}{2} + \frac{a^2}{2} \right) \\ &= 2 \left( a^2 + a^2 \right) \\ &= 2(2a^2) = 4a^2 \end{align*}
Step 4: Finding the Ratio
\( \text{Ratio} = \frac{\text{TSA of Cube}}{\text{TSA of Cuboid}} = \frac{6a^2}{4a^2} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2} \)
Final Answer: The ratio is 3 : 2.
