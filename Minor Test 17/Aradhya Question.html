Minor Test 17
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • The question paper comprises three sections: A, B, and C. All questions are compulsory.
  • Section A: Contains 5 questions of 2 marks each. (Short Answer Type I)
  • Section B: Contains 5 questions of 3 marks each. (Short Answer Type II)
  • Section C: Contains 3 questions of 5 marks each. (Long Answer Type)
  • Write neatly. Use scientific notation and formulas where applicable.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define force. State whether opening a table drawer is a push or a pull.
2.
What is an element? Give any two examples of elements.
3.
What is a contact force? Give one simple example from your daily life.
4.
Define a mixture. Give one example of a mixture.
5.
Give two examples from daily life where applying force changes the shape of an object.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
(a) What is muscular force?
(b) Why is muscular force called a contact force?
7.
Classify the following substances into elements, compounds, and mixtures:
(i) Gold \(\quad\) (ii) Sugar \(\quad\) (iii) Air
8.
What is frictional force? In which direction does friction act on a moving object?
9.
Write three simple differences between an element and a compound.
10.
What is gravitational force? Give one example to show the effect of gravity.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) What is a non-contact force? (1)
(b) Name any two non-contact forces and explain them briefly with one simple example each. (4)
12.
(a) What is a compound? (2)
(b) Is water considered an element, a mixture, or a compound? Give two simple reasons to support your answer. (3)
13.
(a) Can force change the state of motion of an object? Explain with a simple example. (2.5)
(b) Can force change the direction of a moving object? Explain with a simple example. (2.5)
