Minor Test 17: Science - Soil
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The question paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Read each question carefully and write neat, precise answers.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q1.
Define soil based on its natural occurrence and composition.
Q2.
State what term is used for the intermediate particles of soil that are neither very rough nor too smooth. Give one main characteristic feature of this soil type.
Q3.
Define the term 'Cohesion' in relation to soil properties.
Q4.
Why is earthworm known as 'nature's ploughman'?
Q5.
Name any two main causes of soil pollution.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
State the meaning of the term 'soil'. Explain why top soil is considered so valuable and delicate.
Q7.
List the four main agents of physical weathering responsible for soil formation.
Q8.
Give one significant difference between each of the following:
(a) Sand and Silt (1.5 Marks)
(b) Infiltration rate and Percolation rate (1.5 Marks)
Q9.
Explain why coconut trees thrive in coastal regions where the soil present is mainly sandy.
Q10.
What is soil erosion? Mention any two ways by which soil erosion can be avoided.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
Classify and explain the categories of soil on the basis of 'texture of the soil'. Name the soil type that can retain the highest amount of water.
Q12.
Discuss the overall composition of soil in detail. Explain what components make up a healthy soil mix.
Q13.
(a) Explain why the type of crop grown in a particular field depends on the type of soil with examples. (3 Marks)
(b) Why is it said that 'plasticity' and 'cohesion' play an important role in the workability of a soil? (2 Marks)
