Minor Test 17
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Physics (Force & Matter) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Show working for numericals.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define matter. Give one example of a substance that exists naturally in all three states of matter.
2.
What is meant by a 'non-contact force'? State any two examples of non-contact forces.
3.
Numerical: Two teams are playing a game of tug-of-war. Team A applies a force of 150 N towards the East, and Team B applies a force of 120 N towards the West. Calculate the net (resultant) force and state the direction in which the rope will move.
4.
Why do liquids have the ability to flow, whereas solids do not? Explain on the basis of intermolecular forces.
5.
State any two practical methods used to reduce friction between the moving parts of a machine.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Distinguish between solids, liquids, and gases on the basis of the following properties:
(a)
Compressibility
(b)
Intermolecular space
(c)
Rigidity
7.
Define the terms 'mass' and 'weight'. State any two distinct differences between mass and weight.
8.
A force can produce various effects on an object. State any three distinct effects a force can have, giving one everyday example for each effect.
9.
Give scientific reasons for the following:
(a)
It is dangerous and easy to slip on a banana peel thrown on the road.
(b)
Deep treads (grooves) are made on the tyres of vehicles.
10.
A piece of sponge can be compressed easily when squeezed with our hands, yet it is classified as a solid state of matter. Give scientific reasons to justify this statement.
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a)
Define friction. Is friction considered a contact force or a non-contact force? (2 Marks)
(b)
Describe a simple activity or example from your daily life to demonstrate that a force can change the direction of a moving object. (3 Marks)
12.
(a)
Describe a simple experiment to prove that matter (using the example of air) occupies space. (3 Marks)
(b)
According to the kinetic theory of matter, what happens to the intermolecular space and intermolecular force when a solid is heated and melts into a liquid? (2 Marks)
13.
(a)
Differentiate between muscular force and magnetic force giving the basic definition of each. (2 Marks)
(b)
Numerical: Three friends are pushing a heavy wooden box. Rahul and Amit push the box towards the right with forces of 30 N and 25 N respectively. Sumit pushes the box from the opposite side towards the left with a force of 40 N. Calculate the net (resultant) force acting on the box and state the direction in which the box will move. (3 Marks)
