DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Factorisation (Minor Test 17) - Class 8 ICSE

SECTION A

Q1. Factorise by taking out the common factor: \( 8xy^3 + 12x^2y^2 \)
[2 Marks]
Answer: 4xy²(2y + 3x)
Step 1: Find the HCF of the numerical coefficients (\( 8 \) and \( 12 \)), which is \( 4 \).
Step 2: Find the HCF of the literal coefficients (\( x \cdot y^3 \) and \( x^2 \cdot y^2 \)), which is \( xy^2 \).
Step 3: Divide each term by the HCF (\( 4xy^2 \)):
\( 8xy^3 \div 4xy^2 = 2y \)
\( 12x^2y^2 \div 4xy^2 = 3x \)
Final Expression: \( 4xy^2(2y + 3x) \)
Q2. Factorise the expression completely by grouping: \( x^2 + xy - x - y \)
[2 Marks]
Answer: (x + y)(x - 1)
Step 1: Group terms with common factors: \( (x^2 + xy) - (x + y) \)
Step 2: Factor out common terms from each group:
\( x(x + y) - 1(x + y) \)
Step 3: Take out the common binomial factor \( (x + y) \):
\( (x + y)(x - 1) \)
Q3. Factorise using standard algebraic identities: \( x^2 - 12x + 36 \)
[2 Marks]
Answer: (x - 6)²
Step 1: Identify the form matching \( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = (a - b)^2 \).
Here, \( a^2 = x^2 \implies a = x \) and \( b^2 = 36 \implies b = 6 \).
Step 2: Check the middle term: \( 2ab = 2 \cdot x \cdot 6 = 12x \) (matches).
Step 3: Apply the formula: \( (x - 6)^2 \)
Q4. Factorise using the difference of two squares identity: \( 25a^2 - 49b^2 \)
[2 Marks]
Answer: (5a + 7b)(5a - 7b)
Step 1: Express both terms as squares: \( (5a)^2 - (7b)^2 \)
Step 2: Apply the identity \( a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b) \).
Final Expression: \( (5a + 7b)(5a - 7b) \)
Q5. Factorise the simple quadratic trinomial: \( x^2 + 7x + 12 \)
[2 Marks]
Answer: (x + 3)(x + 4)
Step 1: Find two numbers whose product is \( 12 \) and sum is \( 7 \).
The numbers are \( 3 \) and \( 4 \) (since \( 3 \times 4 = 12 \) and \( 3 + 4 = 7 \)).
Step 2: Rewrite the middle term: \( x^2 + 3x + 4x + 12 \)
Step 3: Group and factorise: \( x(x + 3) + 4(x + 3) = (x + 3)(x + 4) \)

SECTION B

Q6. Factorise by grouping terms: \( 5p^2 - 8pq - 10p + 16q \)
[3 Marks]
Answer: (p - 2)(5p - 8q)
Step 1: Rearrange terms to make convenient pairs: \( (5p^2 - 10p) - (8pq - 16q) \)
Step 2: Factor out common terms from each pair:
\( 5p(p - 2) - 8q(p - 2) \)
Step 3: Take out the common binomial factor \( (p - 2) \):
\( (p - 2)(5p - 8q) \)
Q7. Factorise completely using identities: \( 36p^2 - 60pq + 25q^2 \)
[3 Marks]
Answer: (6p - 5q)²
Step 1: Express the expression in standard square form:
\( (6p)^2 - 2 \cdot (6p) \cdot (5q) + (5q)^2 \)
Step 2: Identify the identity \( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = (a - b)^2 \) where \( a = 6p \) and \( b = 5q \).
Final Expression: \( (6p - 5q)^2 \)
Q8. Factorise the expression: \( x^2 - y^2 - 6y - 9 \)
[3 Marks]
Answer: (x + y + 3)(x - y - 3)
Step 1: Group the last three terms by taking out a minus sign:
\( x^2 - (y^2 + 6y + 9) \)
Step 2: Recognise the trinomial as a perfect square: \( y^2 + 6y + 9 = (y + 3)^2 \)
Step 3: Rewrite as: \( x^2 - (y + 3)^2 \)
Step 4: Apply the difference of squares identity \( a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b) \):
\( [x + (y + 3)][x - (y + 3)] = (x + y + 3)(x - y - 3) \)
Q9. Factorise the trinomial by splitting the middle term: \( x^2 - 5x - 24 \)
[3 Marks]
Answer: (x - 8)(x + 3)
Step 1: Find two numbers whose product is \( -24 \) and sum is \( -5 \).
The numbers are \( -8 \) and \( +3 \) (since \( -8 \times 3 = -24 \) and \( -8 + 3 = -5 \)).
Step 2: Split the middle term: \( x^2 - 8x + 3x - 24 \)
Step 3: Group and factorise:
\( x(x - 8) + 3(x - 8) = (x - 8)(x + 3) \)
Q10. Factorise: \( a(a - 2b) - 3(a - 2b) \)
[3 Marks]
Answer: (a - 2b)(a - 3)
Step 1: Notice that \( (a - 2b) \) is a common binomial factor in both terms.
Step 2: Factor out \( (a - 2b) \):
\( (a - 2b)(a - 3) \)

SECTION C

Q11. Factorise completely: (a) \( 3x^2y - 3xy + 12x - 12 \) (b) \( x^2 - \frac{1}{x^2} \)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) 3(x - 1)(xy + 4), (b) (x + 1/x)(x - 1/x)
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Step 1: Take out common factor \( 3 \) from all terms: \( 3(x^2y - xy + 4x - 4) \)
Step 2: Group terms inside the bracket: \( 3[(x^2y - xy) + (4x - 4)] \)
Step 3: Factor out common elements: \( 3[xy(x - 1) + 4(x - 1)] \)
Step 4: Take out binomial factor \( (x - 1) \): \( 3(x - 1)(xy + 4) \)
Part (b) [2 Marks]:
Step 1: Express as difference of squares: \( (x)^2 - \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 \)
Step 2: Apply \( a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b) \):
\( \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right) \)
Q12. Factorise completely: (a) \( 2x^2 + 7x + 3 \) (b) \( 3x^2 - 10x - 8 \)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) (2x + 1)(x + 3), (b) (3x + 2)(x - 4)
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Step 1: Product of coefficient of \( x^2 \) and constant term = \( 2 \times 3 = 6 \). Sum = \( 7 \).
Numbers are \( 6 \) and \( 1 \).
Step 2: Split middle term: \( 2x^2 + 6x + x + 3 \)
Step 3: Group and factorise: \( 2x(x + 3) + 1(x + 3) = (2x + 1)(x + 3) \)
Part (b) [2 Marks]:
Step 1: Product = \( 3 \times (-8) = -24 \). Sum = \( -10 \). Numbers are \( -12 \) and \( +2 \).
Step 2: Split middle term: \( 3x^2 - 12x + 2x - 8 \)
Step 3: Group and factorise: \( 3x(x - 4) + 2(x - 4) = (3x + 2)(x - 4) \)
Q13. (a) Factorise: \( a^3 - a^2b - ab^2 + b^3 \) (b) Evaluate: \( (102)^2 - (98)^2 \)
[5 Marks]
Answer: (a) (a - b)²(a + b), (b) 800
Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Step 1: Group terms in pairs: \( (a^3 - a^2b) - (ab^2 - b^3) \)
Step 2: Factor out common elements: \( a^2(a - b) - b^2(a - b) \)
Step 3: Take out common binomial factor \( (a - b) \): \( (a - b)(a^2 - b^2) \)
Step 4: Further expand \( (a^2 - b^2) \) using identity: \( (a - b)(a - b)(a + b) = (a - b)^2(a + b) \)
Part (b) [2 Marks]:
Step 1: Apply difference of squares identity \( a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b) \):
\( (102 + 98)(102 - 98) \)
Step 2: Simplify the terms: \( (200) \times (4) \)
Final Answer: \( 800 \)
