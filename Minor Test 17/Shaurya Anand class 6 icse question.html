MINOR TEST 17: RATIO AND PROPORTION
Class: 6 ICSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The question paper comprises 17 questions divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write necessary steps clearly. Use of calculators is not permitted.
SECTION A (Objective & Short Answer Type - 2 Marks Each = 10 Marks)
Q1.
Express the ratio \(45\) minutes to \(2\) hours in its simplest form.
Q2.
Find the two numbers in the ratio \(3 : 5\) whose sum is \(64\).
Q3.
Check whether the numbers \(15, 20, 24,\) and \(32\) are in proportion.
Q4.
Find the fourth proportional to \(6\), \(8\), and \(15\).
Q5.
If the cost of \(7\) notebooks is ₹ \(252\), find the cost of \(12\) such notebooks.
SECTION B (Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each = 15 Marks)
Q6.
Two numbers are in the ratio \(7 : 9\). If \(12\) is subtracted from each, the new ratio becomes \(3 : 4\). Find the numbers.
Q7.
An amount of ₹ \(7,200\) is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio \(2 : 3 : 4\). How much money does each get?
Q8.
Find the third proportional to \(12\) and \(18\).
Q9.
A car travels \(210\text{ km}\) in \(3\) hours.
(i) How much distance will it cover in \(5\) hours at the same speed? [1.5 Marks]
(ii) In how much time will it cover \(350\text{ km}\)? [1.5 Marks]
Q10.
Find the mean proportional between \(9\) and \(16\).
SECTION C (Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each = 15 Marks)
Q11.
A rectangular field has length \(120\text{ m}\) and breadth \(75\text{ m}\). Find the ratio of:
(i) Length to the breadth of the field. [1 Mark]
(ii) Breadth to the perimeter of the field. [2 Marks]
(iii) Length to the perimeter of the field. [2 Marks]
Q12.
The income of a family is ₹ \(45,000\) per month and their savings are ₹ \(9,000\) per month. Find the ratio of:
(i) Income to savings. [1 Mark]
(ii) Income to expenditure. [2 Marks]
(iii) Expenditure to savings. [2 Marks]
Q13.
Four numbers \(a, 15, 20,\) and \(25\) are in proportion. Also, find the value of \(x\) if \(x, 12, 15\) are in continued proportion. (Solve both parts in detail).
