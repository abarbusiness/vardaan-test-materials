SECTION A SOLUTIONS
1. Define the terms 'opaque' and 'transparent' materials. Give one example of each.
[2 Marks]
Opaque Materials (1 Mark): Materials that do not allow light to pass through them at all are called opaque materials. We cannot see through them.
Example: Wood, stone, or iron.

Transparent Materials (1 Mark): Materials that allow light to pass through them completely are called transparent materials. We can see through them clearly.
Example: Clear glass, pure water, or air.
2. What is a simple pendulum? Define the term 'time period' in the context of a simple pendulum.
[2 Marks]
Simple Pendulum (1 Mark): A simple pendulum consists of a small metallic ball (called a bob) suspended by a long thread from a rigid stand.

Time Period (1 Mark): The time taken by the pendulum to complete one full oscillation (one complete to-and-fro motion) is called its time period.
3. State the three essential conditions necessary for a shadow to be formed.
[2 Marks]
To form a shadow, the following three conditions are strictly required (Partial marking applies):
  • A source of light.
  • An opaque object to block the path of light.
  • A screen (or surface) behind the object on which the shadow can be formed.
4. Convert a speed of 54 km/h into m/s. Show your calculation steps clearly.
[2 Marks]
Formula/Concept: To convert km/h to m/s, we multiply the value by \( \frac{5}{18} \) (since \( 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \) and \( 1 \text{ hour} = 3600 \text{ s} \)).

\( \text{Speed in m/s} = 54 \times \frac{5}{18} \)

\( = 3 \times 5 = 15 \text{ m/s} \)

Answer: The speed is 15 m/s. (1 Mark for formula/substitution, 1 Mark for correct final answer with unit).
5. Differentiate between uniform motion and non-uniform motion with the help of one example for each.
[2 Marks]
Uniform Motion (1 Mark): An object moving along a straight line with a constant speed is said to be in uniform motion. It covers equal distances in equal intervals of time.
Example: A car moving on a straight, clear highway at a steady speed of 60 km/h.

Non-Uniform Motion (1 Mark): If the speed of an object moving along a straight line keeps changing, its motion is said to be non-uniform. It covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time.
Example: A train approaching a station (slowing down) or a car in crowded city traffic.
SECTION B SOLUTIONS
6. Describe the basic principle behind the working of a pinhole camera. State the two characteristics of the image formed by it.
[3 Marks]
Principle (1 Mark): A pinhole camera works on the principle of the rectilinear propagation of light, which means light travels in a straight line.

Characteristics of the Image (2 Marks):
  1. The image formed is inverted (upside down) because light rays traveling in straight lines cross each other at the pinhole.
  2. The image is usually smaller than the object (diminished) and maintains the color of the object (unlike a shadow).
7. A simple pendulum takes 32 seconds to complete 20 oscillations. What is the time period of the pendulum? Calculate its frequency.
[3 Marks]
Given:
Time taken (\( t \)) = 32 seconds
Number of oscillations (\( n \)) = 20

(a) Time Period (T): (1.5 Marks)
\( \text{Time Period } (T) = \frac{\text{Total time taken}}{\text{Number of oscillations}} \)

\( T = \frac{32}{20} = 1.6 \text{ seconds} \)

(b) Frequency (f): (1.5 Marks)
Frequency is the number of oscillations per second.
\( f = \frac{\text{Number of oscillations}}{\text{Total time taken}} \) OR \( f = \frac{1}{T} \)

\( f = \frac{20}{32} = 0.625 \text{ Hz (oscillations per second)} \)

9. Salma takes 15 minutes from her house to reach her school on a bicycle. If the bicycle has a steady speed of 2 m/s, calculate the distance between her house and the school in kilometers.
[3 Marks]
Given:
Time taken (\( t \)) = \( 15 \text{ minutes} \)
Speed (\( v \)) = \( 2 \text{ m/s} \)

Step 1: Convert time into seconds (1 Mark)
\( t = 15 \times 60 \text{ seconds} = 900 \text{ seconds} \)

Step 2: Calculate Distance in meters (1 Mark)
\( \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} \)
\( \text{Distance} = 2 \text{ m/s} \times 900 \text{ s} = 1800 \text{ meters} \)

Step 3: Convert Distance to Kilometers (1 Mark)
Since \( 1000 \text{ m} = 1 \text{ km} \),
\( \text{Distance} = \frac{1800}{1000} \text{ km} = 1.8 \text{ km} \)

Answer: The distance between her house and the school is 1.8 km.
10. What is a speedometer and an odometer? Why is it important to use standard units of measurement (like SI units) instead of using arbitrary units like handspans or footsteps?
[3 Marks]
Speedometer (0.5 Mark): An instrument on a vehicle's dashboard that records and displays the speed directly in km/h.
Odometer (0.5 Mark): An instrument that measures the distance moved by the vehicle in kilometers.

Importance of Standard Units (2 Marks):
Arbitrary units like handspans, cubits, or footsteps vary from person to person because the size of body parts is different for everyone. This leads to confusion and inaccurate measurements. To ensure consistency, accuracy, and uniformity in measurements globally (whether in trade, science, or daily life), standard units (like SI units) are strictly required as they remain constant everywhere and for everyone.
SECTION C SOLUTIONS
11. (a) Describe a simple activity to prove that light travels in a straight line.
(b) Why can we not see our clear image in a rough piece of cardboard, even though it reflects light? Explain.
[5 Marks]
(a) Rectilinear Propagation Activity (3 Marks):
  • Setup: Take a lighted candle and fix it on a table. Take a long, straight, hollow rubber or plastic tube.
  • Observation 1: Look at the candle flame through the straight tube. The flame is clearly visible.
  • Observation 2: Now, bend the tube slightly and try to look at the flame again. This time, the flame is not visible.
  • Conclusion: Since the light from the candle could not bend along with the tube, this activity proves that light strictly travels in a straight line.
(b) Reflection from Cardboard (2 Marks):
We cannot see our image in a rough piece of cardboard due to irregular (diffused) reflection. Even though the cardboard reflects light, its rough surface causes parallel incident rays of light to reflect in many different directions. Because the reflected rays scatter and do not remain parallel, a clear image cannot be formed. (A smooth mirror causes regular reflection, preserving the parallel nature of rays to form an image).
12. (a) The distance between two railway stations is 240 km. A train takes 4 hours to cover this distance. Calculate the speed of the train in km/h.
(b) If another express train travels the same distance at a constant speed of 80 km/h, how much time will it take to complete the journey?
[5 Marks]
(a) Speed of the first train (2 Marks):
Given: Distance = 240 km, Time = 4 hours.
\( \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} \)
\( \text{Speed} = \frac{240 \text{ km}}{4 \text{ hours}} = 60 \text{ km/h} \)

(b) Time taken by the express train (3 Marks):
Given: Distance = 240 km, New Speed = 80 km/h.
\( \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} \)
\( \text{Time} = \frac{240 \text{ km}}{80 \text{ km/h}} = 3 \text{ hours} \)

Final Statement: The speed of the first train is 60 km/h. The express train will take 3 hours to cover the same distance.
13. (a) A car moves with a constant speed of 10 km/min. Describe the shape of its distance-time graph.
(b) Explain how you can determine the speed of an object directly from its distance-time graph.
(c) What will be the shape of a distance-time graph for a truck that is parked (stationary) at a distance of 20 m from the origin?
[5 Marks]
(a) Shape for Constant Speed (2 Marks):
Since the car is moving with a constant speed (uniform motion), it covers equal distances in equal time intervals. Therefore, the distance-time graph will be a straight line slanting upwards (inclined to the time axis), passing through the origin (if it starts from zero distance at zero time).

(b) Finding Speed from Graph (1 Mark):
The speed of an object can be determined by finding the slope (steepness) of its distance-time graph. The steeper the line, the higher the speed. (Mathematically: Speed = \(\Delta y / \Delta x\)).

(c) Shape for Stationary Object (2 Marks):
If a truck is parked at a distance of 20 m, its distance from the origin does not change as time passes. Therefore, the distance-time graph will be a straight line parallel to the time-axis (X-axis), drawn at the 20 m mark on the distance axis (Y-axis).
