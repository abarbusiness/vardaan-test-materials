MINOR TEST 17
Class: 7 CBSE Subject: Mathematics (Exponents & Powers) Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and show necessary working steps.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Evaluate the following:
(i) \( 2^6 \)
(ii) \( 7^3 \)
2.
Express \( 343 \) as a power of \( 7 \).
3.
Simplify the expression: \( 5^2 \times 10^3 \).
4.
Find the value of: \( (3^0 \times 4^0) + 5^0 \).
5.
Write the number \( 4,560,000 \) in standard form.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
Express \( 324 \) as a product of powers of its prime factors.
7.
Simplify and express the result in exponential form:
\[ \frac{(2^3)^4 \times 2^5}{2^7} \]
8.
Find the value of \( x \) if: \( 7^{2x+1} \div 7^3 = 7^4 \).
9.
(i) Compare the numbers \( 2.7 \times 10^{12} \) and \( 1.5 \times 10^{8} \).
(ii) State which is larger between \( 4 \times 10^{14} \) and \( 3 \times 10^{17} \). Provide a reason for your answer.
10.
Simplify the following expression using laws of exponents:
\[ \frac{2^5 \times 5^2 \times y^8}{10^3 \times y^4} \]
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Simplify and find the value: \[ \frac{12^4 \times 9^3 \times 4}{6^3 \times 8^2 \times 27} \]
(b) Which is greater: \( 2^8 \) or \( 8^2 \)? Justify your answer.
12.
(a) Express \( 2,806,196 \) in expanded form using exponents.
(b) Simplify the expression and write the answer in exponential form: \[ \frac{3^5 \times 10^5 \times 25}{5^7 \times 6^5} \]
13.
(a) The average distance between the Earth and the Moon is \( 384,000,000 \) m. Express this distance in standard form.
(b) The diameter of the Sun is \( 1.4 \times 10^9 \) m and the diameter of the Earth is \( 1.2756 \times 10^7 \) m. Compare them and estimate roughly how many times the Sun's diameter is larger than the Earth's diameter.
