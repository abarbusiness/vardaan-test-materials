SECTION A

1. Find the ratio of 5 km to 500 m.
[2 Marks]

To find the ratio, both quantities must be in the same unit. Let's convert kilometers to meters.

\[ 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \] \[ 5 \text{ km} = 5 \times 1000 \text{ m} = 5000 \text{ m} \]

Now, find the ratio of 5000 m to 500 m:

\[ \text{Ratio} = \frac{5000}{500} = \frac{50}{5} = \frac{10}{1} \]

Answer: The ratio is 10 : 1.

2. Express the ratio 45 : 108 in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]

To simplify, we divide both the numerator and denominator by their Highest Common Factor (HCF).

Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45
Factors of 108: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, ...
The HCF of 45 and 108 is 9.

\[ \frac{45}{108} = \frac{45 \div 9}{108 \div 9} = \frac{5}{12} \]

Answer: The simplest form is 5 : 12.

3. Are the ratios 25 g : 30 g and 40 kg : 48 kg in proportion? Justify your answer.
[2 Marks]

Two ratios are in proportion if their simplified forms are equal.

First ratio: \( \frac{25}{30} \)

\[ \frac{25 \div 5}{30 \div 5} = \frac{5}{6} \implies 5 : 6 \]

Second ratio: \( \frac{40}{48} \)

\[ \frac{40 \div 8}{48 \div 8} = \frac{5}{6} \implies 5 : 6 \]

Answer: Since both ratios simplify to 5 : 6, Yes, they are in proportion.

4. Find the value of x if 3 : 4 :: x : 20.
[2 Marks]

Since the quantities are in proportion, the product of extremes equals the product of means.

\[ 3 : 4 = x : 20 \implies \frac{3}{4} = \frac{x}{20} \]

By cross-multiplying:

\[ 4 \times x = 3 \times 20 \] \[ 4x = 60 \] \[ x = \frac{60}{4} = 15 \]

Answer: The value of x is 15.

5. Out of 40 students in a class, 16 are girls. Find the ratio of the number of girls to the number of boys in the simplest form.
[2 Marks]

Total students = 40

Number of girls = 16

Number of boys = Total students - Number of girls

\[ \text{Boys} = 40 - 16 = 24 \]

Ratio of girls to boys = 16 : 24

\[ \frac{16}{24} = \frac{16 \div 8}{24 \div 8} = \frac{2}{3} \]

Answer: The ratio of girls to boys is 2 : 3.

SECTION B

6. Divide ₹ 1,500 between Amit and Sumit in the ratio of 3 : 2.
[3 Marks]

Total ratio parts = \( 3 + 2 = 5 \) parts.

Total amount = ₹ 1,500.

Value of one part:

\[ \frac{1500}{5} = 300 \]

Amit's share (3 parts):

\[ 3 \times 300 = \text{₹ } 900 \]

Sumit's share (2 parts):

\[ 2 \times 300 = \text{₹ } 600 \]

Answer: Amit gets ₹ 900 and Sumit gets ₹ 600.

7. The lengths of the sides of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. If the perimeter of the triangle is 63 cm, find the length of each side.
[3 Marks]

Let the lengths of the sides be \( 2x, 3x, \) and \( 4x \).

The perimeter is the sum of all sides.

\[ 2x + 3x + 4x = 63 \] \[ 9x = 63 \] \[ x = \frac{63}{9} = 7 \text{ cm} \]

Now, calculate each side:

  • First side = \( 2x = 2 \times 7 = 14 \text{ cm} \)
  • Second side = \( 3x = 3 \times 7 = 21 \text{ cm} \)
  • Third side = \( 4x = 4 \times 7 = 28 \text{ cm} \)

Answer: The sides are 14 cm, 21 cm, and 28 cm.

8. A car travels 165 km in 3 hours. How far will it travel in 5 hours, assuming the speed remains uniform?
[3 Marks]

This problem can be solved using the unitary method or direct proportion.

Method (Unitary):

Distance traveled in 3 hours = 165 km.

Distance traveled in 1 hour (Speed):

\[ \frac{165}{3} = 55 \text{ km/hr} \]

Distance traveled in 5 hours:

\[ 55 \text{ km/hr} \times 5 \text{ hr} = 275 \text{ km} \]

Answer: The car will travel 275 km in 5 hours.

9. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 8. If the sum of the numbers is 182, find the two numbers.
[3 Marks]

Let the two numbers be \( 5x \) and \( 8x \).

According to the question, their sum is 182.

\[ 5x + 8x = 182 \] \[ 13x = 182 \] \[ x = \frac{182}{13} = 14 \]

Substitute the value of x back to find the numbers:

  • First number = \( 5x = 5 \times 14 = 70 \)
  • Second number = \( 8x = 8 \times 14 = 112 \)

Answer: The two numbers are 70 and 112.

10. A map is given with a scale of 2 cm = 1000 km. What is the actual distance between two places in kms, if the distance on the map is 2.5 cm?
[3 Marks]

This is a case of direct proportion. Let the actual distance be \( x \) km.

\[ \frac{\text{Map Distance}_1}{\text{Actual Distance}_1} = \frac{\text{Map Distance}_2}{\text{Actual Distance}_2} \]
\[ \frac{2}{1000} = \frac{2.5}{x} \]

Cross-multiplying:

\[ 2x = 1000 \times 2.5 \] \[ 2x = 2500 \] \[ x = \frac{2500}{2} = 1250 \text{ km} \]

Answer: The actual distance is 1250 km.

SECTION C

11. In a school library, the ratio of math books to science books is 4 : 5. If there are 320 math books, find (a) number of science books, (b) total books, (c) new ratio if 40 more science books are added.
[5 Marks]

Let the number of math books be \( 4x \) and science books be \( 5x \).

We are given that math books = 320.

\[ 4x = 320 \implies x = \frac{320}{4} = 80 \]

(a) Number of science books:

\[ 5x = 5 \times 80 = 400 \text{ books} \]

(b) Total number of math and science books:

\[ \text{Total} = 320 \text{ (Math)} + 400 \text{ (Science)} = 720 \text{ books} \]

(c) New ratio:

If 40 more science books are added, the new number of science books = \( 400 + 40 = 440 \).

The number of math books remains 320.

\[ \text{New Ratio} = \frac{320}{440} = \frac{32}{44} = \frac{8}{11} \]

Answers: (a) 400, (b) 720, (c) 8 : 11.

12. A certain amount is divided among A, B, and C in ratio 2:3:5. (a) If C gets ₹ 1,500 more than B, what is the total amount? (b) Find the share of A.
[5 Marks]

Let the shares of A, B, and C be \( 2x \), \( 3x \), and \( 5x \) respectively.

According to the condition, C's share is ₹ 1,500 more than B's share.

\[ 5x - 3x = 1500 \] \[ 2x = 1500 \] \[ x = \frac{1500}{2} = 750 \]

(a) Total amount distributed:

Total amount = \( 2x + 3x + 5x = 10x \)

\[ \text{Total} = 10 \times 750 = \text{₹ } 7,500 \]

(b) Share of A:

A's share = \( 2x \)

\[ \text{A's share} = 2 \times 750 = \text{₹ } 1,500 \]

Answers: (a) Total amount is ₹ 7,500. (b) A's share is ₹ 1,500.

13. (a) Check if 4, 12, 36 are in continued proportion. (b) A machine fills 840 bottles in 6 hours. How many bottles in 5 hours?
[5 Marks]

Part (a) [2 Marks]: Continued Proportion

Three numbers a, b, c are in continued proportion if \( a : b :: b : c \), which means \( b^2 = ac \).

Here, a = 4, b = 12, c = 36.

\[ b^2 = 12^2 = 144 \] \[ a \times c = 4 \times 36 = 144 \]

Since \( b^2 = ac \), the numbers 4, 12, and 36 are in continued proportion.

Part (b) [3 Marks]: Unitary Method

Number of bottles filled in 6 hours = 840

Number of bottles filled in 1 hour:

\[ \frac{840}{6} = 140 \text{ bottles} \]

Number of bottles filled in 5 hours:

\[ 140 \times 5 = 700 \text{ bottles} \]

Answer: The machine will fill 700 bottles in 5 hours.
