SECTION A (10 Marks)
Q1. Identify the numerical coefficients... [2 Marks]
Identify the numerical coefficients of terms (other than constants) in the following expressions:
(i) \( 5 - 3t^2 \)     (ii) \( 2x - 3y + 5xyz \)
Solution:

(i) \( 5 - 3t^2 \) :
Terms other than constant: \( -3t^2 \)
Numerical Coefficient = \( -3 \) [1 Mark]

(ii) \( 2x - 3y + 5xyz \) :
Term: \( 2x \) → Coefficient = \( 2 \)
Term: \( -3y \) → Coefficient = \( -3 \)
Term: \( 5xyz \) → Coefficient = \( 5 \) [1 Mark]

Q2. Like or Unlike terms... [2 Marks]
State whether the given pairs of terms are like or unlike terms:
(i) \( 14xy \) and \( 42yx \)     (ii) \( 4m^2p \) and \( 4mp^2 \)
Solution:

(i) \( 14xy \) and \( 42yx \):
The algebraic factors are \( xy \) and \( yx \) (which is the same as \( xy \)). Since factors are identical, they are Like Terms. [1 Mark]

(ii) \( 4m^2p \) and \( 4mp^2 \):
The algebraic factors are \( m^2p \) and \( mp^2 \). Since the powers of the variables are different, they are Unlike Terms. [1 Mark]

Q3. Classify expressions... [2 Marks]
Classify the following expressions as monomials, binomials, or trinomials:
(i) \( y^2 \)     (ii) \( 1 + x + x^2 \)
Solution:

(i) \( y^2 \): Contains only one term. Therefore, it is a Monomial. [1 Mark]

(ii) \( 1 + x + x^2 \): Contains three unlike terms. Therefore, it is a Trinomial. [1 Mark]

Q4. Add algebraic expressions... [2 Marks]
Add the following algebraic expressions: \( 3mn, -5mn, 8mn, -4mn \).
Solution:

These are all like terms (all contain \( mn \)). We simply add their numerical coefficients:

\begin{aligned} \text{Sum} &= 3mn + (-5mn) + 8mn + (-4mn) \\ &= (3 - 5 + 8 - 4)mn \\ &= (11 - 9)mn \\ &= 2mn \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( 2mn \) [2 Marks]

Q5. Value of expression... [2 Marks]
If \( m = 2 \), find the value of the expression \( 9 - 5m \).
Solution:

Substitute the value of \( m = 2 \) into the expression:

\begin{aligned} 9 - 5m &= 9 - 5(2) \\ &= 9 - 10 \\ &= -1 \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( -1 \) [2 Marks]

SECTION B (15 Marks)
Q6. Simplify by combining like terms... [3 Marks]
Simplify the following expression by combining like terms: \( p - (p - q) - q - (q - p) \)
Solution:

First, open the brackets. Be careful with the negative signs outside the brackets:

\begin{aligned} &= p - p + q - q - q + p \\ \end{aligned}

Now, group the like terms together:

\begin{aligned} &= (p - p + p) + (q - q - q) \\ &= (1 - 1 + 1)p + (1 - 1 - 1)q \\ &= (1)p + (-1)q \\ &= p - q \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( p - q \) [3 Marks]

Q7. Subtract expressions... [3 Marks]
Subtract \( 24ab - 10b - 18a \) from \( 30ab + 12b + 14a \).
Solution:

Set up the subtraction carefully: (Second Expression) - (First Expression)

\begin{aligned} &= (30ab + 12b + 14a) - (24ab - 10b - 18a) \end{aligned}

Distribute the negative sign to all terms in the second bracket:

\begin{aligned} &= 30ab + 12b + 14a - 24ab + 10b + 18a \end{aligned}

Rearrange and combine like terms:

\begin{aligned} &= (30ab - 24ab) + (12b + 10b) + (14a + 18a) \\ &= 6ab + 22b + 32a \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( 6ab + 22b + 32a \) [3 Marks]

Q8. What should be added... [3 Marks]
What should be added to \( x^2 + xy + y^2 \) to obtain \( 2x^2 + 3xy \)?
Solution:

Let the required expression to be added be \( P \). Then,

\begin{aligned} (x^2 + xy + y^2) + P &= 2x^2 + 3xy \end{aligned}

To find \( P \), subtract \( (x^2 + xy + y^2) \) from \( (2x^2 + 3xy) \):

\begin{aligned} P &= (2x^2 + 3xy) - (x^2 + xy + y^2) \\ P &= 2x^2 + 3xy - x^2 - xy - y^2 \\ P &= (2x^2 - x^2) + (3xy - xy) - y^2 \\ P &= x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( x^2 + 2xy - y^2 \) should be added. [3 Marks]

Q9. Find the value... [3 Marks]
If \( a = 2 \) and \( b = -2 \), find the value of the algebraic expression: \( a^2 + ab + b^2 \)
Solution:

Substitute the given values into the expression:

\begin{aligned} a^2 + ab + b^2 &= (2)^2 + (2)(-2) + (-2)^2 \\ \end{aligned}

Calculate each term:

\begin{aligned} &= 4 + (-4) + 4 \\ &= 4 - 4 + 4 \\ &= 4 \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( 4 \) [3 Marks]

Q10. Perimeter of Triangle... [3 Marks]
The sides of a triangle are represented by the expressions \( 2y + 3x \), \( x - y \), and \( 4x - 5y \). Find the perimeter of the triangle.
Solution:

The perimeter of a triangle is the sum of the lengths of its three sides.

\begin{aligned} \text{Perimeter} &= (2y + 3x) + (x - y) + (4x - 5y) \end{aligned}

Group the like terms (\( x \) terms and \( y \) terms) together:

\begin{aligned} &= (3x + x + 4x) + (2y - y - 5y) \\ &= (3 + 1 + 4)x + (2 - 1 - 5)y \\ &= 8x + (1 - 5)y \\ &= 8x - 4y \end{aligned}

Final Answer: The perimeter is \( 8x - 4y \). [3 Marks]

SECTION C (15 Marks)
Q11. Simplify and Evaluate... [5 Marks]
Simplify the given expression and find its value when \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \):
\( 2(x^2 - xy) + 3 - xy + 2y \)
Solution:

Step 1: Simplify the expression (2.5 Marks)

Open the brackets by distributing the 2:

\begin{aligned} &= 2x^2 - 2xy + 3 - xy + 2y \end{aligned}

Group and combine the like terms (the \( xy \) terms):

\begin{aligned} &= 2x^2 + (-2xy - xy) + 2y + 3 \\ &= 2x^2 - 3xy + 2y + 3 \end{aligned}

Step 2: Find the value (2.5 Marks)

Substitute \( x = 2 \) and \( y = -1 \) into the simplified expression:

\begin{aligned} &= 2(2)^2 - 3(2)(-1) + 2(-1) + 3 \\ &= 2(4) - (-6) - 2 + 3 \\ &= 8 + 6 - 2 + 3 \\ &= 14 - 2 + 3 \\ &= 12 + 3 \\ &= 15 \end{aligned}

Final Answer: Simplified = \( 2x^2 - 3xy + 2y + 3 \), Value = \( 15 \) [5 Marks]

Q12. What should be taken away & Evaluation... [5 Marks]
(a) What should be taken away from \( 3x^2 - 4y^2 + 5xy + 20 \) to obtain \( -x^2 - y^2 + 6xy + 20 \)?
(b) Find the value of the expression \( n^3 + 5n^2 + 5n - 2 \) when \( n = -2 \).
Solution:

Part (a) [3 Marks]:
Let the expression to be taken away be \( M \).
\( (3x^2 - 4y^2 + 5xy + 20) - M = -x^2 - y^2 + 6xy + 20 \)

Therefore, \( M = (3x^2 - 4y^2 + 5xy + 20) - (-x^2 - y^2 + 6xy + 20) \)

\begin{aligned} M &= 3x^2 - 4y^2 + 5xy + 20 + x^2 + y^2 - 6xy - 20 \\ &= (3x^2 + x^2) + (-4y^2 + y^2) + (5xy - 6xy) + (20 - 20) \\ &= 4x^2 - 3y^2 - xy + 0 \end{aligned}

Answer (a): \( 4x^2 - 3y^2 - xy \) should be taken away.

Part (b) [2 Marks]:
Substitute \( n = -2 \) into the expression \( n^3 + 5n^2 + 5n - 2 \):

\begin{aligned} &= (-2)^3 + 5(-2)^2 + 5(-2) - 2 \\ &= -8 + 5(4) - 10 - 2 \\ &= -8 + 20 - 10 - 2 \\ &= 12 - 10 - 2 \\ &= 2 - 2 \\ &= 0 \end{aligned}

Answer (b): The value is 0.

Q13. Sum and Subtraction of expressions... [5 Marks]
From the sum of \( 2y^2 + 3yz \), \(-y^2 - yz - z^2\), and \( yz + 2z^2 \), subtract the sum of \( 3y^2 - z^2 \) and \( -y^2 + yz + z^2 \).
Solution:

Step 1: Find the first sum (Sum 1) [2 Marks]

\begin{aligned} \text{Sum 1} &= (2y^2 + 3yz) + (-y^2 - yz - z^2) + (yz + 2z^2) \\ &= (2y^2 - y^2) + (3yz - yz + yz) + (-z^2 + 2z^2) \\ &= y^2 + 3yz + z^2 \end{aligned}

Step 2: Find the second sum (Sum 2) [1.5 Marks]

\begin{aligned} \text{Sum 2} &= (3y^2 - z^2) + (-y^2 + yz + z^2) \\ &= (3y^2 - y^2) + yz + (-z^2 + z^2) \\ &= 2y^2 + yz + 0 \\ &= 2y^2 + yz \end{aligned}

Step 3: Subtract Sum 2 from Sum 1 [1.5 Marks]

We need to evaluate: \( (\text{Sum 1}) - (\text{Sum 2}) \)

\begin{aligned} &= (y^2 + 3yz + z^2) - (2y^2 + yz) \\ &= y^2 + 3yz + z^2 - 2y^2 - yz \\ &= (y^2 - 2y^2) + (3yz - yz) + z^2 \\ &= -y^2 + 2yz + z^2 \end{aligned}

Final Answer: \( -y^2 + 2yz + z^2 \) [5 Marks]
