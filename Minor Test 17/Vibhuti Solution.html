Q1. Identify the terms and their coefficients for the following algebraic expression: \( 5xyz^2 - 3zy \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
The expression is \( 5xyz^2 - 3zy \). It has two terms.
1. First Term: \( 5xyz^2 \)
Coefficient: \( 5 \) (1 Mark)
2. Second Term: \( -3zy \)
Coefficient: \( -3 \) (1 Mark)
Note: The negative sign must be included with the coefficient.
Q2. Classify the following polynomials as monomials, binomials, or trinomials: (i) \( x + y \) (ii) \( 1000 \) (iii) \( x + x^2 + x^3 + x^4 \) (iv) \( 7 + y + 5x \)
[2 Marks]
Solution:
(i) \( x + y \) : It has 2 terms. Hence, it is a Binomial. (0.5 Mark)
(ii) \( 1000 \) : It has 1 term. Hence, it is a Monomial. (0.5 Mark)
(iii) \( x + x^2 + x^3 + x^4 \) : It has 4 terms. It does not fit in the three categories. It is simply a polynomial (or multinomial). (0.5 Mark)
(iv) \( 7 + y + 5x \) : It has 3 terms. Hence, it is a Trinomial. (0.5 Mark)
Q3. Add the following algebraic expressions: \( ab - bc \), \( bc - ca \), and \( ca - ab \).
[2 Marks]
Solution:
We need to add: \( (ab - bc) + (bc - ca) + (ca - ab) \)
Grouping the like terms together:
\( = (ab - ab) + (-bc + bc) + (-ca + ca) \) (1 Mark)
\( = 0 + 0 + 0 \)
Ans: 0 (1 Mark)
Q4. Subtract \( 4a - 7ab + 3b + 12 \) from \( 12a - 9ab + 5b - 3 \).
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Writing the expressions, reversing the signs of the polynomial to be subtracted:
\( = (12a - 9ab + 5b - 3) - (4a - 7ab + 3b + 12) \)
\( = 12a - 9ab + 5b - 3 - 4a + 7ab - 3b - 12 \) (1 Mark)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (12a - 4a) + (-9ab + 7ab) + (5b - 3b) + (-3 - 12) \)
\( = 8a - 2ab + 2b - 15 \) (1 Mark)

SECTION B

Q6. Simplify the expression \( 3x(4x - 5) + 3 \) and find its value for \( x = 3 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Step 1: Simplify the expression
\( = 3x(4x - 5) + 3 \)
\( = (3x \times 4x) - (3x \times 5) + 3 \)
\( = 12x^2 - 15x + 3 \) (1.5 Marks)
Step 2: Substitute \( x = 3 \)
\( = 12(3)^2 - 15(3) + 3 \)
\( = 12(9) - 45 + 3 \)
\( = 108 - 45 + 3 \)
\( = 111 - 45 \) \( = 66 \) (1.5 Marks)
Q7. Multiply the binomials: \( (2x + 5) \) and \( (4x - 3) \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Using distributive property:
\( = (2x + 5) \times (4x - 3) \)
\( = 2x(4x - 3) + 5(4x - 3) \) (1 Mark)
\( = (2x \times 4x) - (2x \times 3) + (5 \times 4x) - (5 \times 3) \)
\( = 8x^2 - 6x + 20x - 15 \) (1 Mark)
Combining like terms:
\( = 8x^2 + 14x - 15 \) (1 Mark)
Q8. Using the suitable standard identity, evaluate: \( (102)^2 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can write \( 102 \) as \( (100 + 2) \).
Therefore, \( (102)^2 = (100 + 2)^2 \)
Using the identity: \( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \) (1 Mark)
Here, \( a = 100 \), \( b = 2 \)
\( = (100)^2 + 2(100)(2) + (2)^2 \) (1 Mark)
\( = 10000 + 400 + 4 \)
\( = 10404 \) (1 Mark)
Q9. Subtract \( 3pq(p - q) \) from \( 2pq(p + q) \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
First, simplify both expressions:
Expression 1: \( 3pq(p - q) = 3p^2q - 3pq^2 \) (0.5 Mark)
Expression 2: \( 2pq(p + q) = 2p^2q + 2pq^2 \) (0.5 Mark)
Now subtract Expression 1 from Expression 2:
\( = (2p^2q + 2pq^2) - (3p^2q - 3pq^2) \)
\( = 2p^2q + 2pq^2 - 3p^2q + 3pq^2 \) (1 Mark)
Grouping like terms:
\( = (2p^2q - 3p^2q) + (2pq^2 + 3pq^2) \)
\( = -p^2q + 5pq^2 \) (1 Mark)
Q10. Using the identity \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \), find the value of \( (99)^2 \).
[3 Marks]
Solution:
We can write \( 99 \) as \( (100 - 1) \).
Therefore, \( (99)^2 = (100 - 1)^2 \)
Using the given identity: \( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \)
Here, \( a = 100 \), \( b = 1 \)
\( = (100)^2 - 2(100)(1) + (1)^2 \) (1.5 Marks)
\( = 10000 - 200 + 1 \)
\( = 9800 + 1 \)
\( = 9801 \) (1.5 Marks)

SECTION C

Q11. (a) Simplify: \( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \)
(b) Add: \( p(p-q) \), \( q(q-r) \) and \( r(r-p) \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (a) - Simplify (3 Marks)
Expression: \( (a + b)(2a - 3b + c) - (2a - 3b)c \)
Expand the first part:
\( = a(2a - 3b + c) + b(2a - 3b + c) - [c(2a) - c(3b)] \)
\( = 2a^2 - 3ab + ac + 2ab - 3b^2 + bc - (2ac - 3bc) \) (1.5 Marks)
Open the bracket and combine like terms:
\( = 2a^2 - ab + ac - 3b^2 + bc - 2ac + 3bc \)
\( = 2a^2 - 3b^2 - ab - ac + 4bc \) (1.5 Marks)
Part (b) - Add (2 Marks)
Simplify the expressions first:
\( p(p-q) = p^2 - pq \)
\( q(q-r) = q^2 - qr \)
\( r(r-p) = r^2 - rp \) (1 Mark for simplifications)
Now add them:
\( = (p^2 - pq) + (q^2 - qr) + (r^2 - rp) \)
\( = p^2 + q^2 + r^2 - pq - qr - rp \) (1 Mark)
Q12. Using suitable identities, evaluate: (i) \( 71^2 \) (ii) \( 297 \times 303 \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i) Evaluate \( 71^2 \) (2.5 Marks)
\( 71^2 = (70 + 1)^2 \)
Using identity \( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \), where \( a=70, b=1 \): (0.5 Mark)
\( = (70)^2 + 2(70)(1) + (1)^2 \) (1 Mark)
\( = 4900 + 140 + 1 \)
\( = 5041 \) (1 Mark)
Part (ii) Evaluate \( 297 \times 303 \) (2.5 Marks)
We can write \( 297 = (300 - 3) \) and \( 303 = (300 + 3) \).
\( \therefore 297 \times 303 = (300 - 3)(300 + 3) \)
Using identity \( (a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2 \), where \( a=300, b=3 \): (0.5 Mark)
\( = (300)^2 - (3)^2 \) (1 Mark)
\( = 90000 - 9 \)
\( = 89991 \) (1 Mark)
Q13. Show that:
(i) \( (3x + 7)^2 - 84x = (3x - 7)^2 \)
(ii) \( (a - b)(a + b) + (b - c)(b + c) + (c - a)(c + a) = 0 \)
[5 Marks]
Solution:
Part (i) Prove: \( (3x + 7)^2 - 84x = (3x - 7)^2 \) (2.5 Marks)
Solving LHS:
\( \text{LHS} = (3x + 7)^2 - 84x \)
Using \( (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \):
\( = [(3x)^2 + 2(3x)(7) + (7)^2] - 84x \)
\( = 9x^2 + 42x + 49 - 84x \) (1 Mark)
\( = 9x^2 - 42x + 49 \) (0.5 Mark)
Now solving RHS:
\( \text{RHS} = (3x - 7)^2 \)
Using \( (a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \):
\( = (3x)^2 - 2(3x)(7) + (7)^2 \)
\( = 9x^2 - 42x + 49 \) (0.5 Mark)
Since LHS = RHS, Hence Proved. (0.5 Mark)
Part (ii) Prove: \( (a - b)(a + b) + (b - c)(b + c) + (c - a)(c + a) = 0 \) (2.5 Marks)
Using the identity \( (x - y)(x + y) = x^2 - y^2 \) for each term: (1 Mark)
Term 1: \( (a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2 \)
Term 2: \( (b - c)(b + c) = b^2 - c^2 \)
Term 3: \( (c - a)(c + a) = c^2 - a^2 \) (0.5 Mark)
Substituting these into the LHS expression:
\( \text{LHS} = (a^2 - b^2) + (b^2 - c^2) + (c^2 - a^2) \)
\( = a^2 - a^2 - b^2 + b^2 - c^2 + c^2 \) (0.5 Mark)
\( = 0 \)
Since LHS = RHS = 0, Hence Proved. (0.5 Mark)
