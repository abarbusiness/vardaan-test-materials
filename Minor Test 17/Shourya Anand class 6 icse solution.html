DETAILED SOLUTIONS: MINOR TEST 17 - RATIO AND PROPORTION
Class: 6 ICSE | Subject: Mathematics | Max. Marks: 40
Q1. Express the ratio \(45\) minutes to \(2\) hours in its simplest form.
[2 Marks]
To find the ratio, both quantities must be in the same unit.
\(1 \text{ hour} = 60 \text{ minutes}\)
\(\therefore 2 \text{ hours} = 2 \times 60 = 120 \text{ minutes}\)
Ratio = \(\frac{45 \text{ minutes}}{120 \text{ minutes}}\) = \(\frac{45}{120}\)
Dividing numerator and denominator by their HCF (\(15\)):
Ratio = 3 : 8
Q2. Find the two numbers in the ratio \(3 : 5\) whose sum is \(64\).
[2 Marks]
Let the common multiplier be \(x\).
The two numbers are \(3x\) and \(5x\).
According to the question: \(3x + 5x = 64\)
\(8x = 64 \implies x = \frac{64}{8} = 8\)
First number = \(3 \times 8 = 24\)
Second number = \(5 \times 8 = 40\)
The numbers are 24 and 40.
Q3. Check whether the numbers \(15, 20, 24,\) and \(32\) are in proportion.
[2 Marks]
Four numbers \(a, b, c, d\) are in proportion if \(a \times d = b \times c\)
Product of extremes = \(15 \times 32 = 480\)
Product of means = \(20 \times 24 = 480\)
Since Product of extremes = Product of means (<(480 = 480)>),
Yes, the numbers are in proportion.
Q4. Find the fourth proportional to \(6\), \(8\), and \(15\).
[2 Marks]
Let the fourth proportional be \(x\). Then \(6 : 8 :: 15 : x\)
Product of extremes = Product of means
\(6 \times x = 8 \times 15\)
\(6x = 120 \implies x = \frac{120}{6} = 20\)
Fourth proportional = 20
Q5. If the cost of \(7\) notebooks is ₹ \(252\), find the cost of \(12\) such notebooks.
[2 Marks]
Cost of \(7\) notebooks = ₹ \(252\)
Cost of \(1\) notebook = \(\frac{252}{7} = \text{₹ } 36\)
Cost of \(12\) notebooks = \(36 \times 12 = \text{₹ } 432\)
Cost of 12 notebooks = ₹ 432
Q6. Two numbers are in the ratio \(7 : 9\). If \(12\) is subtracted from each, the new ratio becomes \(3 : 4\). Find the numbers.
[3 Marks]
Let the numbers be \(7x\) and \(9x\).
According to condition: \(\frac{7x - 12}{9x - 12} = \frac{3}{4}\)
Cross multiplying: \(4(7x - 12) = 3(9x - 12)\)
\(28x - 48 = 27x - 36\)
\(28x - 27x = 48 - 36 \implies x = 12\)
First number = \(7 \times 12 = 84\)
Second number = \(9 \times 12 = 108\)
The numbers are 84 and 108.
Q7. An amount of ₹ \(7,200\) is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio \(2 : 3 : 4\). How much money does each get?
[3 Marks]
Sum of ratio terms = \(2 + 3 + 4 = 9\)
Total amount = ₹ \(7,200\)
A's share = \(\frac{2}{9} \times 7,200 = 2 \times 800 = \text{₹ } 1,600\)
B's share = \(\frac{3}{9} \times 7,200 = 3 \times 800 = \text{₹ } 2,400\)
C's share = \(\frac{4}{9} \times 7,200 = 4 \times 800 = \text{₹ } 3,200\)
A gets ₹ 1,600, B gets ₹ 2,400, and C gets ₹ 3,200.
Q8. Find the third proportional to \(12\) and \(18\).
[3 Marks]
Let the third proportional be \(x\). Then \(12, 18, x\) are in continued proportion, meaning \(12 : 18 :: 18 : x\)
Product of extremes = Product of means
\(12 \times x = 18 \times 18\)
\(12x = 324 \implies x = \frac{324}{12} = 27\)
Third proportional = 27
Q9. A car travels \(210\text{ km}\) in \(3\) hours. (i) Distance in \(5\) hours? (ii) Time for \(350\text{ km}\)?
[3 Marks]
Speed of the car = \(\frac{210 \text{ km}}{3 \text{ hours}} = 70 \text{ km/h}\)
(i) Distance covered in \(5\) hours = \(\text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 70 \times 5 = 350 \text{ km}\).
(ii) Time taken to cover \(350 \text{ km}\) = \(\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{350}{70} = 5 \text{ hours}\).
(i) 350 km, (ii) 5 hours
Q10. Find the mean proportional between \(9\) and \(16\).
[3 Marks]
Let the mean proportional be \(x\). Then \(9, x, 16\) are in proportion.
\(9 : x :: x : 16 \implies x^2 = 9 \times 16\)
\(x^2 = 144 \implies x = \sqrt{144} = 12\)
Mean proportional = 12
Q11. Rectangular field length \(120\text{ m}\), breadth \(75\text{ m}\). Find ratios of dimensions and perimeter.
[5 Marks]
Length (\(l\)) = \(120\text{ m}\), Breadth (\(b\)) = \(75\text{ m}\)
Perimeter (\(P\)) = \(2(l + b) = 2(120 + 75) = 2(195) = 390\text{ m}\)
(i) Length to breadth = \(\frac{120}{75} = \frac{8}{5}\) = 8 : 5 [1 Mark]
(ii) Breadth to perimeter = \(\frac{75}{390} = \frac{5}{26}\) = 5 : 26 [2 Marks]
(iii) Length to perimeter = \(\frac{120}{390} = \frac{4}{13}\) = 4 : 13 [2 Marks]
(i) 8 : 5, (ii) 5 : 26, (iii) 4 : 13
Q12. Family income ₹ \(45,000\), savings ₹ \(9,000\). Find ratios of income, savings, and expenditure.
[5 Marks]
Income = ₹ \(45,000\), Savings = ₹ \(9,000\)
Expenditure = \(\text{Income} - \text{Savings} = 45,000 - 9,000 = \text{₹ } 36,000\)
(i) Income to savings = \(\frac{45,000}{9,000} = \frac{5}{1}\) = 5 : 1 [1 Mark]
(ii) Income to expenditure = \(\frac{45,000}{36,000} = \frac{5}{4}\) = 5 : 4 [2 Marks]
(iii) Expenditure to savings = \(\frac{36,000}{9,000} = \frac{4}{1}\) = 4 : 1 [2 Marks]
(i) 5 : 1, (ii) 5 : 4, (iii) 4 : 1
Q13. If \(a, 15, 20, 25\) are in proportion, find \(a\). Also find \(x\) if \(x, 12, 15\) are in continued proportion.
[5 Marks]
Part 1: Since \(a, 15, 20, 25\) are in proportion:
\(\frac{a}{15} = \frac{20}{25} \implies \frac{a}{15} = \frac{4}{5}\)
\(a = \frac{4 \times 15}{5} = 4 \times 3 = 12\) [2.5 Marks]

Part 2: Since \(x, 12, 15\) are in continued proportion:
\(x : 12 :: 12 : 15 \implies \frac{x}{12} = \frac{12}{15}\)
\(x = \frac{12 \times 12}{15} = \frac{144}{15} = \frac{48}{5} = 9.6\) [2.5 Marks]
a = 12, x = 9.6 (or 48/5)
