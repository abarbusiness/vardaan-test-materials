Minor Test 22: Combustion and Flame
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Section A consists of 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B consists of 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C consists of 3 questions of 5 marks each.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define a combustible substance. Classify the following into combustible and non-combustible substances: Wood, Glass, Iron, Kerosene.
2.
What is meant by the 'ignition temperature' of a substance? Why doesn't a piece of wood catch fire on its own at room temperature?
3.
Define the calorific value of a fuel. Name the fuel that has the highest calorific value among common fuels and state its value.
4.
Do all burning substances produce a flame? Name one substance that produces a flame during combustion and one that does not.
5.
Why is carbon dioxide (\(CO_2\)) considered the best extinguisher for fires involving electrical equipment and inflammable materials like petrol?
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
What is the 'Fire Triangle'? List the three essential conditions represented in this triangle that must be met simultaneously for a fire to start.
7.
Differentiate between Rapid Combustion and Spontaneous Combustion. Give one suitable example of each from your everyday observation or chemical studies.
8.
Give scientific reasons for the following statements:
(a) Water should not be used to extinguish oil or petrol fires.
(b) LPG is considered a much better domestic fuel than traditional wood.
9.
What is global warming? Name the gas whose increased concentration in the atmosphere traps heat, and mention two of its environmental consequences.
10.
How does the burning of coal and diesel contribute to the formation of 'acid rain'? What harmful impacts does acid rain have on the environment?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
Describe the physical structure of a candle flame in detail. Explain the characteristics (colour, level of combustion, and temperature) of its outermost, middle, and innermost zones.
12.
The increasing global consumption and burning of fossil fuels directly threatens the health of humans and the environment. Elaborate on any five harmful effects of burning fuels.
13.
(a) A goldsmith uses a metallic blowpipe to direct a specific zone of a flame to melt gold and silver. Which zone is used and why? (2 Marks)
(b) Why is it considered highly fatal to sleep in a closed room with a burning coal fire inside? Explain the chemical reason behind it. (3 Marks)
