MINOR TEST 22
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Science & Mathematics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Use proper units where necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
1.
Define pressure. Write its mathematical formula and SI unit.
2.
Find the simplest ratio of \( 5 \text{ m} \) to \( 10 \text{ km} \).
3.
Give a scientific reason: Why do school bags have wide straps?
4.
Are the ratios \( 1:2 \) and \( 2:3 \) equivalent? Justify your answer.
5.
State any two common applications or effects of atmospheric pressure in our daily life.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
6.
If 15 workers can build a wall in 48 hours, how many workers will be required to do the same work in 30 hours? Identify the type of proportion used.
7.
A force of \( 100 \text{ N} \) is applied perpendicular to an area of \( 2 \text{ m}^2 \). Calculate the pressure exerted on the surface.
8.
Rohan has Rs 3000. He decides to divide this amount among his three children A, B, and C in the ratio \( 2:3:5 \). Find the amount received by each child.
9.
Describe a simple activity to demonstrate that liquids exert pressure on the walls of their container.
10.
A car travels \( 432 \text{ km} \) on \( 48 \text{ litres} \) of petrol. How far would it travel on \( 20 \text{ litres} \) of petrol assuming the consumption rate is uniform?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
11.
(a) Explain why a sharp knife cuts objects more easily than a blunt knife. (2 marks)
(b) A wooden block of mass \( 5 \text{ kg} \) is kept on a tabletop. The dimensions of the wooden block are \( 40 \text{ cm} \times 20 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} \). Calculate the pressure exerted by the wooden block on the tabletop if it is made to lie with its dimensions:
(i) \( 20 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} \)
(ii) \( 40 \text{ cm} \times 20 \text{ cm} \)
(Take \( g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2 \)) (3 marks)
12.
A train is moving at a uniform speed of \( 75 \text{ km/hr} \). Assuming it maintains this speed:
(i) How far will it travel in \( 20 \text{ minutes} \)? (2.5 marks)
(ii) Find the time required to cover a distance of \( 250 \text{ km} \). Express your answer in hours and minutes. (2.5 marks)
13.
The scale of a geographical map is given as \( 1 : 30,000,000 \).
(a) If two cities are \( 4 \text{ cm} \) apart on the map, find the actual distance between them in kilometers. (3 marks)
(b) If the actual distance between two other cities is \( 1500 \text{ km} \), how far apart will they be placed on the same map? (2 marks)
