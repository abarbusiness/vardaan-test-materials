Minor Test: Our Environment
Class: 10 CBSE Subject: Science Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly.
  • Section A contains 5 questions of 2 marks each.
  • Section B contains 5 questions of 3 marks each.
  • Section C contains 3 questions of 5 marks each.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Attempt all questions - 2 Marks Each)
Q.1
Define the term "ecosystem". Name the two main constituents of an ecosystem.
Q.2
What are trophic levels? Give one example of a food chain comprising four trophic levels.
Q.3
Why are green plants and certain bacteria called "producers"?
Q.4
State the chemical equations to show what happens when higher energy ultraviolet (UV) radiations act on an oxygen \(\left(O_2\right)\) molecule at the higher levels of the atmosphere.
Q.5
Differentiate between biodegradable and non-biodegradable substances. Give one example of each.
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q.6
What is biological magnification? Explain why the maximum concentration of harmful agricultural chemicals is found in human beings.
Q.7
"The flow of energy in an ecosystem is unidirectional." Justify this statement with valid reasons.
Q.8
What is ozone? How does it affect any ecosystem? Mention the primary reason why the amount of ozone in the atmosphere began to drop sharply in the 1980s.
Q.9
Food chains generally consist of only three or four steps. Give reasons to justify this statement based on the flow of energy.
Q.10
What is the role of decomposers in the ecosystem? What would happen if decomposers are completely removed from an ecosystem?
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q.11
(a) Draw a labeled diagram showing the flow of energy in an ecosystem.
(b) In a terrestrial ecosystem, green plants capture \(10,000 \text{ J}\) of solar energy that falls on their leaves. Calculate the amount of energy that will be available to the secondary consumer in the following food chain:
\(\text{Sun} \rightarrow \text{Producer} \rightarrow \text{Primary Consumer} \rightarrow \text{Secondary Consumer}\)
Q.12
(a) List any three problems caused by the non-biodegradable wastes that we generate.
(b) Suggest any two methods you can adopt in your daily life to help in reducing the problem of waste disposal.
Q.13
(a) Will the impact of removing all the organisms in a trophic level be different for different trophic levels? Can the organisms of any trophic level be removed without causing any damage to the ecosystem?
(b) Construct a simple food web consisting of at least four interdependent food chains operating in nature.
