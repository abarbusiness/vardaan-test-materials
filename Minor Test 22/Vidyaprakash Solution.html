DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Minor Test 22 (Class 8 Science & Maths)

SECTION A

1. Define pressure. Write its mathematical formula and SI unit.
[2 Marks]
Definition: The force acting per unit area of a surface is called pressure.
Formula: \( \text{Pressure } (P) = \frac{\text{Force } (F)}{\text{Area } (A)} \)
SI Unit: The SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa) or \( \text{N/m}^2 \).
2. Find the simplest ratio of \( 5 \text{ m} \) to \( 10 \text{ km} \).
[2 Marks]
To find the ratio, the units must be the same.
We know, \( 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \).
So, \( 10 \text{ km} = 10 \times 1000 = 10000 \text{ m} \).
\( \text{Ratio} = \frac{5 \text{ m}}{10000 \text{ m}} = \frac{1}{2000} \)
Final Ratio = 1 : 2000
3. Give a scientific reason: Why do school bags have wide straps?
[2 Marks]
School bags have wide straps to increase the surface area in contact with the shoulders.
Since pressure is inversely proportional to area (\( P = \frac{F}{A} \)), a larger area decreases the pressure exerted by the weight of the bag on the shoulders, making it more comfortable for students to carry.
4. Are the ratios \( 1:2 \) and \( 2:3 \) equivalent? Justify your answer.
[2 Marks]
Expressing the ratios as fractions: \( \frac{1}{2} \) and \( \frac{2}{3} \).
To compare them, make their denominators equal by taking the LCM of 2 and 3, which is 6.
\( \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1 \times 3}{2 \times 3} = \frac{3}{6} \)

\( \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{4}{6} \)
Since \( \frac{3}{6} \neq \frac{4}{6} \), the ratios are not equivalent.
No, they are not equivalent.
5. State any two common applications or effects of atmospheric pressure in our daily life.
[2 Marks]
1. Drinking a liquid through a straw. (Sucking creates low pressure inside, and atmospheric pressure pushes the liquid up).
2. Filling liquid inside a medical syringe or an ink dropper.
3. The sticking of a rubber sucker to a smooth surface. (Any two valid points are acceptable).

SECTION B

6. If 15 workers can build a wall in 48 hours, how many workers will be required to do the same work in 30 hours? Identify the type of proportion.
[3 Marks]
Let the number of workers required be \( x \).
Fewer hours mean more workers are needed. Therefore, this is a case of Inverse Proportion.
In inverse proportion: \( x_1 \times y_1 = x_2 \times y_2 \)
\( 15 \times 48 = x \times 30 \)
\( x = \frac{15 \times 48}{30} \)
\( x = \frac{48}{2} = 24 \)
24 workers will be required.
7. A force of \( 100 \text{ N} \) is applied perpendicular to an area of \( 2 \text{ m}^2 \). Calculate the pressure exerted.
[3 Marks]
Given:
Force (\( F \)) = \( 100 \text{ N} \)
Area (\( A \)) = \( 2 \text{ m}^2 \)
We know that, \( P = \frac{F}{A} \)
\( P = \frac{100}{2} = 50 \)
Pressure exerted = \( 50 \text{ Pa} \) (or \( 50 \text{ N/m}^2 \))
8. Rohan has Rs 3000. He decides to divide this among his three children A, B, and C in the ratio \( 2:3:5 \). Find the amount received by each.
[3 Marks]
Total amount = Rs 3000
Ratio of division = \( 2 : 3 : 5 \)
Total number of parts = \( 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 \)
Amount received by A = \( \frac{2}{10} \times 3000 = 2 \times 300 = \text{Rs } 600 \)

Amount received by B = \( \frac{3}{10} \times 3000 = 3 \times 300 = \text{Rs } 900 \)

Amount received by C = \( \frac{5}{10} \times 3000 = 5 \times 300 = \text{Rs } 1500 \)
A: Rs 600, B: Rs 900, C: Rs 1500
9. Describe a simple activity to demonstrate that liquids exert pressure on the walls of their container.
[3 Marks]
Activity:
1. Take a plastic water bottle.
2. Drill 3 or 4 tiny holes of the same size at equal heights near the bottom of the bottle.
3. Fill the bottle with water.
Observation: The water squirts out of all the holes with equal force, falling at the same distance from the bottle.
Conclusion: The liquid is pushing outwards against the container walls, thus proving that liquids exert pressure on the walls of their container.
10. A car travels \( 432 \text{ km} \) on \( 48 \text{ litres} \) of petrol. How far would it travel on \( 20 \text{ litres} \)?
[3 Marks]
Let the distance travelled on 20 litres be \( x \) km.
Since less petrol means less distance covered, this is a case of Direct Proportion.
\( \frac{432}{48} = \frac{x}{20} \)

\( x = \frac{432 \times 20}{48} \)
Simplifying: \( \frac{432}{48} = 9 \) (since \( 48 \times 9 = 432 \)).
\( x = 9 \times 20 = 180 \)
The car will travel 180 km.

SECTION C

11. (a) Sharp vs Blunt knife. (b) Pressure of a 5 kg wooden block lying on two different faces.
[5 Marks]
(a) A sharp knife has a very thin edge, which means its surface area is very small. When force is applied, this small area produces a very large pressure (\( P = \frac{F}{A} \)), cutting objects easily. A blunt knife has a larger surface area, yielding less pressure for the same force. [2 Marks]
(b) Given:
Mass of block (\( m \)) = \( 5 \text{ kg} \)
Force/Weight (\( F \)) = \( m \times g = 5 \times 10 = 50 \text{ N} \).
Case (i): Dimensions \( 20 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} \)
Area (\( A_1 \)) = \( 20 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} = 200 \text{ cm}^2 \)
Converting to \( \text{m}^2 \): \( A_1 = \frac{200}{10000} = 0.02 \text{ m}^2 \)
Pressure (\( P_1 \)) = \( \frac{F}{A_1} = \frac{50}{0.02} = \frac{5000}{2} = 2500 \text{ Pa} \)
Case (ii): Dimensions \( 40 \text{ cm} \times 20 \text{ cm} \)
Area (\( A_2 \)) = \( 40 \text{ cm} \times 20 \text{ cm} = 800 \text{ cm}^2 \)
Converting to \( \text{m}^2 \): \( A_2 = \frac{800}{10000} = 0.08 \text{ m}^2 \)
Pressure (\( P_2 \)) = \( \frac{F}{A_2} = \frac{50}{0.08} = \frac{5000}{8} = 625 \text{ Pa} \)
(i) 2500 Pa, (ii) 625 Pa
12. Train moving at 75 km/hr. Find (i) Distance in 20 min, (ii) Time for 250 km.
[5 Marks]
Given: Speed of the train = \( 75 \text{ km/hr} \).
(i) Distance travelled in 20 minutes:
First, convert time into hours: \( 20 \text{ mins} = \frac{20}{60} \text{ hours} = \frac{1}{3} \text{ hours} \).
\( \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} \)
\( \text{Distance} = 75 \times \frac{1}{3} = 25 \text{ km} \).
(i) 25 km
[2.5 Marks]
(ii) Time required to cover 250 km:
\( \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} \)
\( \text{Time} = \frac{250}{75} \text{ hours} = \frac{10}{3} \text{ hours} \)
Converting \( \frac{10}{3} \text{ hours} \) to hours and minutes:
\( \frac{10}{3} = 3 \frac{1}{3} \text{ hours} \)
\( 3 \text{ hours} + \left(\frac{1}{3} \times 60 \text{ mins}\right) = 3 \text{ hours 20 minutes} \).
(ii) 3 hours 20 minutes
[2.5 Marks]
13. Map scale 1 : 30,000,000. Find (a) actual distance for 4 cm on map, (b) map distance for 1500 km actual.
[5 Marks]
The scale \( 1 : 30,000,000 \) means \( 1 \text{ cm} \) on the map represents \( 30,000,000 \text{ cm} \) in reality.
(a) Actual distance for 4 cm on map:
\( \text{Actual Distance} = 4 \times 30,000,000 \text{ cm} = 120,000,000 \text{ cm} \)
Converting cm to km (Since \( 1 \text{ km} = 100,000 \text{ cm} \)):
\( \frac{120,000,000}{100,000} = 1200 \text{ km} \)
(a) 1200 km
[3 Marks]
(b) Map distance for 1500 km actual distance:
First, convert actual distance to cm:
\( 1500 \text{ km} = 1500 \times 100,000 \text{ cm} = 150,000,000 \text{ cm} \)
Using the scale to find map distance:
\( \text{Map Distance} = \frac{150,000,000}{30,000,000} = 5 \text{ cm} \)
(b) 5 cm
[2 Marks]
